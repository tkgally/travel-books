# Chapter I.

[[subtitle]] The Start—A Wet Morning—The Carrier's Cart

W<sc>e</sc> left the town at six o'clock, in a rain that the landlord called 'a little damp.'[[fnref *]] The carrier had promised nothing; he was a man of few words & fewer teeth.

"Shall we arrive to-night?" I asked.

"'Tis as may be," said he; and that was all he said for an hour, while

[[fn *]] He used the same words of the Flood, I was told.
