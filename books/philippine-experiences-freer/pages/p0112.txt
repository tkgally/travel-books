[[cont]]such as the savages’ examination of the sergeant’s gun, which goes off unexpectedly, creating great consternation among them and almost killing one. The entertainment is concluded by a realistic war dance and sham battle, fought with spears and shields.

In such manner do the Filipinos periodically relieve the tedium of life, which for them contains much of poverty and sickness and but little of real satisfaction and joy.
