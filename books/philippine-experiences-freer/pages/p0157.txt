More than a year after my final departure I received a letter from a citizen of Solano, a patron of the school and a student of English in the adults’ class. It said that, in response to petition and as evidence of the appreciation in which my services were held, the municipal council, by enactment, had paid me the compliment of naming one of the principal streets of the town for me. So appreciative are the Filipinos.
