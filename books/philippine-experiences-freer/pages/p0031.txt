This counsel was emphasized by the supposed presence of the Virgin, symbolized by her image bearing in its arms a living infant. What the belief of the people was respecting the infant—whether a re-incarnation or merely a symbol of the Christ Child—I did not learn. But they were awed by the miraculous voice, and with solemn mien stealthily went their several ways to do as they were bid, talking with each other meanwhile of the “Black Jesus.”

This was the outfit that the captain at Pagsanjan had captured a few days before; and he pulled out a wooden chest and showed us the dismembered portions of the wooden images, the well-made wax heads and hands, and the richly embroidered robes which are so impressive to the mind of the untutored <i>tao</i>. By this capture was one source of revenue cut off from the native adventurers of the Province of La Laguna who had been posing as patriots; for the real patriots had surrendered with General Cailles several weeks before.
