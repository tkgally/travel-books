Lake Bombon, containing an islet from which rises the active volcano of Taal, is but a day’s journey from either Manila or Batangas. The most terrible convulsions have taken place here in times past, the accounts of which, written for the most part by the friars, form very interesting reading.[[fnref *]]

From Batangas to Tanauan I travelled in crazy <i>carromatas</i> drawn by ill-broken and balky ponies. From Tanauan I walked to Bañadero, at the edge of the lake, and then went by <i>baroto</i> the short distance to Ambulong, where I made arrangements with natives to cross to the islet the following morning; and here, as usual, I slept with the family on the floor.
