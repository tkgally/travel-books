If your simian be a female, gratify its maternal instinct by giving it a kitten to hug and fondle; if you attempt presently to take the kitten away the foster-mother will scold you severely, at the same time attempting with one hand to drag it into the tree. Upon more mature acquaintance the kitten will become fond of the monkey, and they will lie down and play together. Such was our experience with these pets. Besides them I owned, for a time, a pretty pony, which I kept under the rear portion of the house and used in my journeys; and also a fine fawn presented to me by a friend, which lived on <i>camote</i> leaves and bounded up the stairs and into the house like a rubber ball when it became frightened. So that, altogether, our dwelling was well filled.
