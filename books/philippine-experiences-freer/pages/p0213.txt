In all the time I was there the number of stores that I saw in the provinces owned and conducted by natives could be counted on the fingers of the hands; and along with his other merchandise, John imported opium into the Islands and taught the natives its use.[[fnref *]] But, before we condemn him too severely, let us recall the history of the opium traffic and the part the English bore in it in China.

Caramoan is the town of which a tale is told that, after the establishment of civil government, as an American official, the deputy provincial treasurer, approached unexpectedly one day on official duty, a company of the men was engaged in military drill togged out in <i>insurrecto</i> uniforms. The deputy did not see them himself, but they espied him and immediately scattered to the four winds; and a few moments later the <i>presidente,</i> who was commander of the organization, met and greeted the deputy without having had time or opportunity to change his attire.
