[[fn *]] “I have heretofore in this report referred to the incompetency and ignorance of many of the justices-of-the-peace. Instead of <i>many</i> I could have said <i>most,</i> and might even have employed a stronger term. . . . Sometimes, and only too frequently, in addition to their ignorance, these justices are found to be either grossly dishonest or else under the influence of a sharp and tricky secretary or <i>consultor,</i> as he is sometimes styled, who, from his superior education and intelligence, dominates the justice-of-the-peace, exercises a strong influence, and preys upon the wretched inhabitants of the community.
