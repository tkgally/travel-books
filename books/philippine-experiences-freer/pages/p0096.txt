# Chapter VI
[[subtitle]] The Town Fiesta
[[subtitle]] Social Recreation—Preparations for the <i>Fiesta</i>—Another <i>Baile</i>—The Procession—Mass—Two Dinners—The <i>Moro-Moro</i>—Serenades—The Cock-pit—A Surprise Party—The Wild East Show.

T<sc>here</sc> is so little opportunity for social recreation among the Filipinos of the provinces that when an occasion does present itself it is entered into with the greatest zest. Ordinarily, the Sunday mass, the market and the cock-pit afford the only opportunities for the gratification of the social instinct in the small and remote towns. But every town has its patron saint, and annually on the saint’s day there are general festivities, sometimes extending over several days, during which every one keeps open house and the people from the surrounding country flock in to enjoy what is to go forward, very much as our own country- and towns-people enjoy their annual county fairs.
