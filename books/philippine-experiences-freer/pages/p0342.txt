Early each morning the <i>tao</i> might be seen climbing the tall trees with hands and feet, monkey fashion, making good use of his almost prehensile great toes; emptying the bamboo vessels suspended under the cut flower stems into a larger vessel hanging at his back; walking from tree to tree on the bamboo poles placed between until the receptacle was filled, and then clambering to the ground again. In early morning the <i>tuba</i> is a refreshing drink, much like fresh apple cider; but as the day advances it ferments rapidly. After collecting the product each morning it was taken to the distillery half way to town, where it was converted into <i>vino.</i> The wife and mother grew betel leaves in the lot next the house, picking them from the trellised vines every alternate morning and taking them to sell in the market.

One day Pedro and his neighbors were disturbed by the news which came to them that the government required them to pay a yearly tax on their land—something that they had never before heard of.
