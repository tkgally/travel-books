After dinner, before leaving for school, I asked for the bride to bid her <i>adios,</i> when the captain came up and whispered, “I should strongly advise you not to attempt to speak to the bride unless you want to be knocked down by the groom.” I believe he exaggerated the danger; however that may be, I was obliged to be content to take leave of the girl’s father in lieu of the bridal couple, whom I had not seen.
