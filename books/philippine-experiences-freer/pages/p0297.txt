But when I arrived at the place I found the beasts to be very ordinary ones, without the gorgeous trappings I had pictured. However there remained His Majesty himself, now sojourning, if my informant was correct, in a dwelling which had nothing to distinguish it particularly from the others in the neighborhood, except that it was somewhat larger. “Do you think I could see the Sultan?” I inquired. “I don’t know,” with a shake of the head, was the response; “you can go in and ask.” So I went through the opening in the bamboo wickerwork with which the lower part of the house was enclosed, and into a bare basement with a floor of hardened earth, from which a ladder led to an opening in the bamboo-stripped floor above.
