Excellent rice is produced in Japan, but nearly all of it is exported to China, as it is too valuable a crop for the poor peasants themselves to consume. They subsist principally on millet, barley and beans, and are able to afford rice only when they are ill. Thus the Filipinos, who in normal times have plenty of rice all the year round, little realize how fortunate they are, especially when compared with the Japanese peasantry.
