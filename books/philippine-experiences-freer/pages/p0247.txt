# Chapter XVI
[[subtitle]] Among the Camarines Tagalogs
[[subtitle]] The Daet and Indan Roads—Hardships of Travel—Supervisory Duties—A Twelve-hour Day—One Christmas Eve—A Chagrined <i>Tao</i>—Affairs in Matango—By Water to Paracale—The Old Ferryman—The Paracale Mines—Journey to Mambulao—An Iguana—Events in Mambulao—A Voyage by <i>Banca</i> on San Miguel Bay.

W<sc>hile</sc> Ambos Camarines is known as a Bicol province, there are six towns of Tagalogs in Camarines Norte. The Bicol race flows over the geographical boundary between the former provinces and meets the Tagalog in Daet and Talisay, where the tribes mingle and both dialects are spoken interchangeably. Reference has already been made, in a preceding chapter, to the wide-awake town of Daet and its schools.
