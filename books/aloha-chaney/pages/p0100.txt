But she undertook the difficult task of soothing the savage breast before the savage ear had been attuned to concord of sweet sounds. Her scanty store of musical learning, brought from a New England singing-school, seemed little capital to begin with. But she made the most of it. She taught them their “fa-sol-la,” until they caught the secret, and once learned, they took the greatest delight in their new accomplishment. Every new musical instrument that came to the island was like the visit of an angel to them. They were eager to master each manipulation. A bass-viol, which arrived one day in a whaling-ship, was an object of intense interest and desire. She never played a viol in her life, but she taught them to play. Since then, they have learned to use many different instruments.
