But how base a thing is fear; how doubly base, when one is travelling in the same ship with majesty! We look for our prince. A camp-stool from the cabin serves him as a provisional throne, and around this precarious seat upon the after-deck is grouped as servile a company of courtiers as any heir-apparent could collect. The same breeze which wafted the smoke of his Highness’s cigar in our direction brought snatches of conversation, in which there was a suffocating odor of obsequious flattery. To the credit of the native complexion be it said, this servility was more marked in the manner and speech of foreigners than of Hawaiians. There was a respectful desire to please, and the natural devotion of followers to their master, in the conduct of the native suite. But there was all the profusion of insincerity in the address of the white courtiers.
