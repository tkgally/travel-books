Where is the enthusiastic lover of the Alps who would just at that moment exchange the field of damask for the field of snow, the glasses for <i>séracs</i>, the plates for slabs of rock, and the waiter with his burden for a guide with an ice-axe? Not to speak of the pleasant variety which a large, lofty bedroom, with a comfortable spring bed and a well-cushioned sofa, affords to those who have been roughing it for some time on couches of chaff and seats of wood in the high Alps.
