# Chapter X.

[[subtitle]] <i>Hints to Lady Pedestrians.</i>

T<sc>he</sc> first thing in order, if not in importance, which must be thought of by a lady intending to make long Alpine expeditions, is the dress which she is to wear when on the mountain. This is a more serious consideration than might at first be supposed, as not only should she aim at presenting as much as possible a tidy appearance on all occasions, but also must she endeavour to have clothes which will protect her effectually from extremes of heat and cold, and which will not impede her movements more than can be helped, in situations where great exertion and activity are required. A great deal of her comfort and enjoyment will depend on her costume being suitable and well selected.
