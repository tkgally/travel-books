Only let us hope that we get profit as well as pleasure from the mountain scenes to which we go, that we move among those wonderful works of God with our eyes open to read the lessons which they teach, and that though they have ‘neither speech nor language’ we yet hear their voices around, and catch with our listening ears some strains of the grand chorus of their ever-ascending hymn of praise.
