# Chapter VII
[[subtitle]] By Steamer from Khanka to the Amoor

T<sc>he</sc> clouds which I had seen gathering on the horizon brought a change of weather. A strong south wind began to blow, and by its warmth and vehemence, as it swept over the surface of Lake Khanka, speedily broke up the ice, and drove it in piled-up masses northward, leaving the southern half of that great sheet of water clear. As soon as the course was open, a steamer came over from the other side; and as several people had been waiting for it in the village for days and weeks, in a few hours it had booked its full complement of passengers, and was ready to start again.

We found the steamer waiting for us at a roughly-constructed wooden jetty a few miles from the village.
