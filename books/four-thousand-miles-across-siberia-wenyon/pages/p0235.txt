The bait was successful. The Tartars felt themselves so flattered by this mark of the Czar’s favour, that the whole tribe applied for admission to the Church.

As we proceeded northward the June days lengthened until, when we had passed the latitude of 60°, there was no night at all. The sun just dipped below the horizon, but the sunset glory never faded, and as banners waving above hedgerows indicate the progress of an unseen procession, so the golden splendours of the evening followed the sun’s movement round the north until again they brightened into day.
