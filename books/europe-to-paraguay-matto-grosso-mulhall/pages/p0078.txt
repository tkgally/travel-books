# XII.

[[subtitle]] River Paraguay to Asuncion

The island of Cerrito is at the confluence of the Paraná and Paraguay, formerly belonging to Paraguay and now ceded to the Argentine Republic. As we pass this island we see numerous crosses erected on the banks over the graves of distinguished officers who fell on the side of the Allies in the Paraguayan war. Every inch of ground was valiantly disputed by Lopez and his people, during 5 years, until the overpowering forces of Brazil and [[corr Buénos|Buenos]] Ayres triumphed in 1870.
