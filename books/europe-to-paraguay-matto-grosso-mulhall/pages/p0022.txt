We left Bahia at 6 p.m. The beauty of the surrounding tropical scenery was enhanced by the glorious tints of sunset mellowed by the evening vapors of the tropics. As we sailed from port we heard the dear and familiar strains of ‘God save the Queen’ from H. M. S. Narcissus.

Note—There is now (1876) a Steam-lift at Bahia which obviates the use of palanquins, taking passengers in a few seconds from the Custom-house to the upper town.
