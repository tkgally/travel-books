# Chapter XI.

[[image: decorative initial T with a man climbing a rocky slope with a staff]]

THERE was no hurry to be up and off on Sunday morning, June 8th. When I awoke we were still lying at anchor in front of the glacier, and I was told by my fellow-passengers that an immense slice of the wall had yielded to the pressure and jumped over-board during the night. And when I came on deck I saw this must be so, for a bright, new green surface to the ice front was presented, entirely free from the snowdrifts which I had seen there yesterday, and there were many more floating bergs than when I had retired.
