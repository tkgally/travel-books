The morning was cloudy and chilly, with occasional showers, very much in keeping with the few dreary streets and abandoned huts which go to make up this old western fortress of the Czar. The fort itself or stockade was an utter wreck; in fact I would not have known of its existence if left to discover it for myself, so I hurried on, picking my way as best I could through the muddy thoroughfares to get a view of my first totem pole.
