John Treadwell, a San Francisco builder, bought this property in 1881 from a prospector, of whom a fair estimate may be formed by the fact that he was known only as “French Pete.” The price was $400, and for some time subsequently it was operated as a placer mine, the character of ore visible being, I am told, what is scientifically known as decomposed quartz. Treadwell sold part of his interest to others at a large advance; and to-day this quarry of gold produces an income of half a million of dollars every year.
