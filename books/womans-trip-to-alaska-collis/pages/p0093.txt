It is a shame to be disturbed at such a moment and in the midst of such an ever-changing panorama by the sound of the breakfast bell, but the meals on the good ship <i>Queen</i> are always so excellent, so hot, and of such variety that I must go down. I sometimes wished they were quite bad, that I might feel it no hardship to skip a few of them, but, like every thing else on this glorious trip, they are above criticism.
