I could not but contrast the joyous, healthful hour, which was ours here at the ending, with the dungeon-life of serfdom over there where it all began. “Yes, Madame,” said Judge Calkins, when with some enthusiasm I gave him the benefit of this idea one evening at Tacoma, “the tail is now wagging the dog.”
