<verse>
“All human history attests
That happiness of man (the hungry sinner),
Since Eve ate apples, much depends on dinner!”
</verse>

You are right, Lord Byron! but when you wrote “the mountains look on Marathon, and Marathon looks on the sea,” I doubt very much while “musing there awhile,” you would have tolerated an invitation to a dinner even at the “Poodle Dog”; yet let me assure you that if you had sent a regret you would have made the mistake of your life.

The “Poodle Dog” is presided over by M. Marbœuf, who is a cook of excellent merit, as I can testify, being quite a <i>cordon-bleu</i> myself. We were shown to a little apartment in rear of the store, which seemed devoted principally to the ice-cream eaters, and found a table with covers for three, prettily decorated with radishes, olives, and shrimps, and a few bright roses.
