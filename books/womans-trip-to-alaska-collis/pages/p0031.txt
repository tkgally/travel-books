Next day we made the tour of the chain of lakes which, with the surrounding land, constitute the people’s pleasure-ground, or public park. Lakes Harriet and Calhoun are exquisite sheets of water bordered by wide and well-made boulevards and groves of trees; in fact, only just a sufficient contribution of art to preserve the beautiful gifts of nature, which is a great comfort in a world so given to strained efforts to compete with the Creator; yet where the handicraft and taste of man are essential, as in the construction of their massive public buildings, the people of Minneapolis are unsurpassed. The churches, libraries, banks, city buildings, office buildings, and newspaper offices have a most assured fire-proof and age-proof effect.
