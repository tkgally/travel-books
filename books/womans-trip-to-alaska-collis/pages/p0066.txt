[[cont]]selves! I heed not the whisper which tells me you tower nearly 15,000 feet above the sea, any more than I would listen to the cynic who analyzed the features or the figure of an ideal woman. I can look at you with the raptures of Shasta, and Washington, Mount Blanc, and the Jungfrau still tingling in my nature, but I yield to you the palm; I care not how tall or how broad you are, or whether your deep shadows and high lights are forests, or rocks, or glaciers; to me you are peerless and unrivalled, like the Venus de Milo, without prototype or antitype, absolutely unique.
