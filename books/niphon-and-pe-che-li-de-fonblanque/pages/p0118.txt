That functionary shakes his head solemnly, ominously, as if the alternative were too horrible to mention; but Captain Schott declares that if the Russian flag were insulted (what the Russian flag has to do in the matter does not clearly appear) his guns should open fire upon the town.

A few minutes later, the funeral procession moved off; and, for the third time[[fnref *]] in about six months, a Japanese Governor, with his suite, were seen to assist in paying the last honours over the graves of murdered Europeans.
