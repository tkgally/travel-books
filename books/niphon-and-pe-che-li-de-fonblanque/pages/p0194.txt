[[break]]

Partly to escape the prevailing head-winds, partly to gratify my curiosity and extend my experiences of Japan, I had arranged to proceed to Nagasaki (where my duties required that I should touch on my way to China), by the great inland sea of Sowonada (I refer the reader to a sketch of it); and, having obtained the services of an experienced Japanese pilot, we steamed away in the <i>Berenice</i> on the 22nd October.
