Europeans are still novelties in Pekin. A few grandees whom we met in chairs, and some mandarins, numerously attended, on horseback, carefully averted their gaze from the offensive foreign devils as we passed; but the shopkeepers came to their doors, and stared at us with mild curiosity, and the mob, and more particularly its juvenile portion, followed us in crowds, and occasionally saluted us with expressions more familiar than polite. Upon the whole, however, we had no reason to complain of the conduct of the people; the race of <i>gamins</i> is much the same all the world over.

[[fn *]] I understand that time and money, backed by good taste, have done more than I gave them credit for, and that the Legation is both handsomely and comfortably fitted up.
