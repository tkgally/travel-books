From this day, the number of beggars in Tien-tsin increased tenfold.

A more judicious measure was set on foot by Dr. Lamprey, an army surgeon, who established a Chinese Hospital, and devoted himself, with most praiseworthy zeal, to alleviating the misery of the poor wretches, who eagerly sought his aid. The means at his disposal were necessarily limited, but he had the satisfaction of being enabled to afford relief to thousands of sufferers, and of restoring to a large number the blessing of sight, to which they had long been strangers.[[fnref *]]

[[fn *]] Neglected ophthalmia, with want of cleanliness, is one of the principal causes of the blindness which prevails so greatly among the poor in China.
