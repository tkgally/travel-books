Further, he was expected to have a sound knowledge of astronomy, of the light literature most popular in the year 2, of the principles of gunnery, anatomy, and mensuration, and of the secret history of the eunuchs at the Court of Pekin from the beginning of the world, down to the accession of Hien Fung.[[fnref *]] Lastly, each candidate was required to write a short and succinct account of the late war with England and France, describing its principal features and its results.
