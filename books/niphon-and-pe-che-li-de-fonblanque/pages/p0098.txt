One of the many difficulties with which I had to contend, in the management of my stables, was getting the horses exercised. None but a two-sworded man is allowed to ride in Japan, and my bettos, or grooms, disapproved of leading horses for a walk, and when compelled to do so, were no sooner out of my sight than they tied their beasts up at a tea-house door, and passed their afternoons in the quiet and undisturbed enjoyment of their <i>dolce far niente.</i>

Occasionally, I attempted to remedy this, by placing myself at the head of my “Irregular Horse,” and leading them out into the country, where there were no Yaconins to notice my innocent infraction of the law.
