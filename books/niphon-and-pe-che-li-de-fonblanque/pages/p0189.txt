In no case, whether escorted or alone, did we meet with a single instance of rudeness or incivility on the part of the people; nor did we, during the whole course of our journey, meet either a beggar or a drunken man.[[fnref *]] The general appearance of the populace is one of great prosperity and contentment; their houses are remarkably clean and in good repair; their patches of garden well cultivated, and never without regard to ornament; and if they are not overburdened with clothing, it is evident that their will and not their poverty consents to forego this luxury. Would to Heaven we might travel as far in European countries—nay, even in our own favoured land, without meeting more misery!
