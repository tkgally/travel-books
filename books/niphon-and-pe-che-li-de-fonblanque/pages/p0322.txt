<i>The principal Agricultural Crops, Vegetables, &c., &c., are—</i>

Rice—one species, grown on irrigated, and two on dry, ground.

Millet—one species grown in fields similar to corn-fields, three to four feet high; and two species, transplanted in single lines, five to six feet high.

Sweet Potato (Dioscorea Balatas).

Egg Plant (Solanum Esculentum).

Indian Corn.

Beans, several kinds of, dwarf and running.

Peas, a dwarf kind, growing in fields.

Carrots, turnips, onions, pumpkins, &c., &c., grown by all cottagers.

Tobacco and cotton—in small quantities only in these districts.

## <i>Fruits.</i>

Cherries, chestnuts, figs, grapes, oranges, pears, peaches, plums, walnuts, and two or three kinds of melons.

With exception of the grapes, which are delicious, none of these have as fine a flavour as European fruits.
