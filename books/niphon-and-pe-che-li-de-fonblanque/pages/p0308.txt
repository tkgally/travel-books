At the same time, he was instrumental in raising the price of grain, and thus incurred the odium of the populace. He was sentenced to decapitation, and underwent his fate with due submission. Borne in his chair to a square in the centre of one of the main streets of Pekin, he stepped out and was greeted with the yells and howls of the assembled multitude. A high officer of justice then approached him, read the sentence, made the mark of a red cross upon his forehead, and handed him over to the executioners. One of them drew his neck forward by means of his pigtail; another drew his body backward; and the head fell at one blow.
