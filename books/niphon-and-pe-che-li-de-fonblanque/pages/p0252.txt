Passing over the fine stone bridge of Palichou, which bears unmistakable marks of the French cannon, and where a craven mandarin, smarting from a wound, ordered poor Brabazon to be decapitated, we came upon the broad paved road leading into Pekin. This was in such wretched repair, that we preferred a path running parallel with it, and which, after a weary dusty ride of three or four hours, brought us to the Chan-yung gate of the Celestial capital.
