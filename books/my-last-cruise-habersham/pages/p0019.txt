# Contents.

[[center]] Chapter I.

I<sc>ntroduces</sc> the reader to the Expedition, and carries him to the Cape of Good Hope; after which he is taken back to the Cape De Verde Islands, and from thence again to the Cape of Good Hope <pref>13</pref>

[[center]] Chapter II.

We receive a visit which is not intended to inquire after our health, and make the acquaintance of “Lieutenant Paget, R.N.;” after which the purser and myself “smell a rat” and fire at a catbird, to the infinite terror of some Hottentot women <pref>23</pref>

[[center]] Chapter III.

We make up a party to visit Cape Town, and take our seats in a chariot—We read poetry in four languages, and think that it must be a fine thing to be a Mormon—We make the acquaintance of an eager gentleman, and conceive a high idea of the hospitality of Cape Town <pref>37</pref>

[[center]] Chapter IV.

We leave Simon’s Town and sail for Batavia, where we receive a visit that <i>does</i> relate to our health, and see a remarkable gun; after which we listen to several astonishing accounts from an English resident, and again put to sea <pref>54</pref>
