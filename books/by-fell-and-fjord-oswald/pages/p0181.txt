The parsonage is situated among grassy hillocks near a burn with little falls and secluded pools. I went to sketch a noble waterfall, Skogarfoss, whose roar sounded like a sustained chord on the pedals of an organ, behind all the farm noises, and the shrill shouts of the boys and women who screamed against it, on the chance that Sira Kjartan might hear what they said. Has this waterfall, perhaps, deafened Sira Kjartan by its never-ceasing din? The approach to the falls is striking, for they are invisible, till on fording the river you come round the face of a high crag and see them close before you, and from the best point of view.
