The Dean was at Borg on a visit to the priest; and the following day being a Sacrament Sunday, the little church was packed to overflowing. I sat among several women in full costume. The old ones wore dark cloth or velvet bodices, set with silver clasps, and laced with silver chains. They had stiff black collars round their necks, embroidered in gold or silver, and short Zouave jackets, also embroidered, and high black head-dresses, rather like an ancient Greek helmet. An average old woman becomes quite an imposing personage when thus accoutred.

The priest’s handsome young daughters wore the dress in a modernised form. The embroidered jacket was fitted to the figure, and was finished off by a silver belt. The head-dress was the white helmet or <i>faldr</i>, ornamented by a sort of circlet on the brow, and softened by an ample white lace veil.
