A picnic and a dance is rather a favourite summer amusement at Reykjavik, and seems to create great hilarity, ending with a gallop home in the evening, and sometimes, as now, the entertaining spectacle of sailors on horseback. We were glad in the evening to find ourselves comfortably at home in the house of our guide and his wife.
