# Chapter XXII.

[[subtitle]] Faröes—To Akureyrei—Steinstadr—Over Oxnadal Heath—Miklabæ and its Ghost—Sorcerers—Hidden Treasures—The Spectre Lover.

<verse>
“W<sc>hat</sc> is the wondrous form
Far in the North,
Round her the circling storm
Whirls the snow forth?
Maiden with veil of snow
Swathed round her icy brow,
All her heart fire below,
Far in the North.

What is the glorious song,
Valiant and sweet,
Loved of the loyal throng,
Reared at her feet?
Maiden with veil of snow,
Why dost thou haunt me so—
Longing thy song to know,
Valiant and sweet?

Sæmund and Snorri, these
Seers of the past,
Send down the centuries
Words that shall last.
Now in the ages grey,
Long themselves passed away,
Still they fire heart and lay—
Seers of the past.

Iceland, thou island maid
Loved of my heart,
Fire-gemmed and snow-arrayed,
Stern as thou art.
</verse>
