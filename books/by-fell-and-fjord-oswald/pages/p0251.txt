Professor Maurer, while attributing to this story an independent origin from that of the German “Leonore” legend, says that it shows how the popular beliefs of the same race are cast in the same moulds while differing so greatly in local colouring and details; and in Iceland the folk-lore is the more remarkable as being still alive,—not yet, as in Germany, obliterated by the crowded uniformity of modern life.
