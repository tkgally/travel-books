[[cont]]till we reached the flat country and stopped by the edge of the Kallirà or Cold water—a mysterious river which, after a brawling course, disappears suddenly into a cleft in the lava-crust, and finds some unknown way to the distant sea. The people of a neighbouring little farm brought out fragrant coffee and cream, and set the tray before us on the grass, and we loitered there till the twilight warned us to go on to Hafnarfjord. There it became quite dark, but as the road was excellent it did not matter. Fireworks disputed the empire of the sky with the Northern Lights—the former the farewell salute of the departing French frigate, the latter the first greeting of winter, for August was nearly gone. As we rode by an inland creek, the rockets and the merry dancers looked beautiful reflected in the water, and cheered all the dark way to Reykjavik.
