# Uganda for a Holiday

# Chapter I
[[subtitle]] The Lyons Mail

T<sc>here</sc> is a happy vagueness in the minds of many as to Uganda. With such it is one of the countries of that mystic geography of Robinson Crusoe and Gulliver where around a name are gathered some sort of a coast, a palm beach with canoes, appropriate huts, and people wearing feathers. It may with the less precise be merely a place without form and void, where ‘things’ come from, and from whence zoological items are supplied. These indefinite lands, whether attached to such names as Senegambia, Uganda, Galapagos or Kuria Muria are a labour-saving possession for those who prefer the unexacting guide of the imagination to the fatiguing statements of a gazetteer. To these geographers Uganda is but a land of Punt whither go the extremists in travel and where are to be seen exceptional forms of life.
