Some of the Andorobo incline to the Bushman type, while others are distinctly Hamitic, and few exhibit the ugly mouth which is characteristic of their aristocratic neighbours. Some excellent photographs, both of the Masai and of the Andorobo, will be found in Sir Harry Johnston’s interesting work, ‘The Uganda Protectorate.’
