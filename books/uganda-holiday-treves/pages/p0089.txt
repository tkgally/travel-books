This dance of Hectic was kept up, it would seem, till the dawn of the next day. It was an infinite relief at the end of the first phase of the performance to break from the suffocating ring, from its glare, its noise and its heat, and stand once more in the open night with the sense that a dew was falling and be soothed by the sound of rustling palms and the far-off boom of the sea.
