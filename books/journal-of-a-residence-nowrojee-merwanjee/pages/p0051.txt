There was only a slight breeze, and the captain took the precaution to give her the best possible reception he could. Accordingly, the poop ladders were removed, and the deck cleared for action. We had only six guns on board, which were kept in readiness—the gunner was called upon to do his best in case of action. The captain, in the meantime, kept watching her movements by the aid of his glass.

A dead calm now succeeded the wind; and as we and many others were looking at our suspected enemy from the poop, and forming all sorts of conjectures about her, one of the midshipmen cried out, “she is shortening the sails.” “What?” exclaimed the captain. “She is shortening the sails, sir,” was the answer. He looked through the glass, and found that it was the fact; and our suspicions were the more enhanced that she was really a pirate ship, and there were many opinions expressed about her superiority over us in force, and how ill-prepared we were to meet her. However, there was a great deal of anxiety and activity on board..
