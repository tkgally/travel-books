It was with these intentions that we were determined to take a tour to these places, in the month of April 1840, which is generally the best time of the year, the days being fourteen hours long, and the weather very mild; and while we were thinking to do so, orders were forwarded to Chatham to launch the frigate Mæander of 46 guns; this being the first launch since our entering the yard, it was of importance for us to witness the preparations of fitting the launching apparatus, and to note down every detail and minutiæ of it; we therefore gave up the idea till after the launch which took place on the 5th of May, and the account of which will be found in another part of this work.
