# Chapter XXXIV.

[[subtitle]] Climate of England.

Some of the Customs of England, of every day’s occurrence, appeared to us very odd; when two intimate friends meet in the street (particularly in the country) they say to each other without stopping, but nodding to each other, “How do!” “how do you do,” “pretty well,” “fine day,” “looks like rain,” “good bye,” “good bye.” This we saw repeated constantly, very much to our amusement, with, of course, the variation of “very warm,” “quite oppressive,” or “very gloomy,” all these remarks of course are brought about by the variableness of the climate, and it is indeed perpetually changing.
