The inhabitants are though few yet very respectable and honest, and we know from our having seen other places, that Egham, from its retired situation, is free from many of the vices and corruptions to which large towns and sea-ports are subject.

We forgot to mention that we had our tutor’s brother, Mr. Joseph Hopkins, living with us, during the time we were under his superintendence. He is an excellent penman, and by following his directions we improved our hand-writing very much, and by constantly conversing with him we made a more rapid progress in the English language than we otherwise might have done, in addition to which he was a most agreeable companion.
