# Chapter XVII.

[[subtitle]] Public Buildings, &c.

<sc>St. Paul’s Cathedral.</sc>—We should have previously mentioned St. Paul’s Cathedral, there has been three edifices upon the same spot burned down, the last was in 1666. The present was built from designs by Sir Christopher Wren, and the first stone was laid in 1675 it is the principal ornament of London and stands between Cheapside and Ludgate Hill close to the new Post office.
