We were from youth told or rather taught to regard him as a most attached friend to the natives of Bombay, and more particularly so to our own family, and it has been our good fortune to know by our visit to England that his kindness and zeal for the natives of the east <i>was not fully communicated to us, as we found and saw him</i> much more devoted to their cause than the idea that was conveyed to us of him <i>by words</i>. For our own selves whenever we wanted advice he kindly gave it, whenever we asked for information it was immediately afforded us, and whilst life and reason continue with us, we shall recollect him and all his acts of patronage, friendship and kindness. At Sir Charles's we often met our good and warm friend Mr. John Forbes, and how shall we tell our grief, that during our stay in England, he was taken away from earth? Death laid his cold hand upon him and his heart ceased to beat, and oh! what a heart was that!!! yes, we felt that we had indeed lost a <i>friend</i>.
