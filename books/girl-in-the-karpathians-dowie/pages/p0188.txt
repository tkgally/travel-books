[[cont]]At the same moment Hryc began shouting for the painter, and the word “bear” came in frequently. The bears too appreciate these little drops of scarlet nectar, and we had come upon the tracks, not over an hour old, of one of them.

Three steady hours followed of a breakneck scramble down the mountain side. Every now and then the horses went sliding forward down the sheer rock, or stopped altogether, and refused to be pushed or dragged a step further. Then it was a case of lifting up the whole trembling leg, and heaving it from one insecure foothold to another. It was slow and very tiring work. <i>We</i> could have got down like goats; but the horses had a terrible trial, which, since they were tired and hungry already, came the more hardly upon them. Three hours saw an end of it; and then came a winding way by a river, with the accustomed fording, and plenty of rock, and then an ascent through woods, where I saw and had under my stick a little hissing viper of the size of whipcord.
