Passers-by brought up infallibly, like machine-moved figures, at my side, and by the time the horses came I could have taken a fairly correct census of the neighbourhood within a mile-wide circle.

The delay made me impatient, for the evening deepened in the unknown hills, and twilight shadows came slipping out of the woods where they had tarried all day, to brood along the valley; I knew it for one of those occasions on which one can look back with pleasure, but which at the time presents principally its irritating rather than its artistic side.

Sitting upon a chair in the lamplight, that mass of perfectly clad people would come before my eyes, and I should feast upon impressions which my senses would, upon their own responsibility, have stored for me.
