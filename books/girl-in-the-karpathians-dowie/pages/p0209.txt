From the difficulty I have had in getting words verified, even by Polish, Ruthene, and Russian scholars, I am inclined to think that the Huculs have had an effect on the language they use, and that it is by no means pure Ruthene. Throughout this book the Ruthenian spelling has been given when the word has been traced to be Ruthene, but Polish words, which I certainly came across, are also given. I have forborne to Anglicise and translate, even when I could easily do so; an approximate pronunciation has been given where possible, but, for my part, find the arbitrary Anglicising of every foreign word inartistic and unsatisfactory, taking much from the effect of the text.
