[[image: Sketch of a peasant in a broad-brimmed hat and embroidered cloak, sitting on a rock beside a small fir tree, holding a stick]]

A strikingly handsome peasant singled himself out to help me. His manner was very lively and funny, with the frequent gesture of scorn at the ignorance of his fellows, and the “st, st, st,” when the straps would go wrong. The head-gear slipped easily over the <i>Doppelmäuser's</i> knowing little head, and slipped off again even more easily; but, after much shortening, the Pelham was wrestled in between the obstinate brown lips, and, in the whole shouting, yelling, pushing crowd, the <i>Doppelmäuser</i> was by far the quietest person. I was elbowed ruthlessly even by my handsome peasant, who, when I pointed out a mistake in his arrangements, shook his hands behind his ears, with the gesture of a person whose calm head is driven dizzy by the popular idiocy, and retired to the extreme verges of the crowd.
