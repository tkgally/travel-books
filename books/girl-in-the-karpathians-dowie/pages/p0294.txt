[[cont]]an afternoon “on a low-backed car, upon a truss of hay,” behind a Jew, and the poor beast that he burns to ill-use, though you have no one to speak to—particularly <i>because</i> you have no one to speak to perhaps:—there is the curious infinitude of the white road to be wondered over, the voices of a million parched frogs—what long, soft, melancholy notes are theirs sometimes—in the place where the marshes ought to be, the coy winking of the little star that heralds Venus, to such as notice it, the “warm moon-birth,” and the “long evening end.”

As ten o'clock went over, and still we did not come into Kolomyja, I was happy; for all these enumerated things and many others, sweeter and smaller still, were with me.
