It was more amusing to sit under the oleanders and discourse fustian with the doctor, while consuming the relays of dainties exceeding delicate sent out by the doctor’s sister. A mining engineer joined the party, and placed his horse—a beautiful bay Hucul mare—at my disposal, and the manager of a small weaving factory called in. The proposal that I should inspect this factory, and see how it tallied with the great and marvellous factories of my country, met with my approval, and we sallied forth adown the curious street. That little factory is a fitting finish to my chronicle of the mountains, and what obtains among them.
