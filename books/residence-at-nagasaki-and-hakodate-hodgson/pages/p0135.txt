The country round about us seems very fertile; on every side we are surrounded by high “coteaux,” almost meriting the name of mountains, if they were not cultivated to their very summits.

The little houses look like babies’ toys, so pretty are they, but yet so little. I have not yet been to see the town, which every one who has seen pronounces to be uninteresting. I am not quite sure what effect I shall produce on these men and women, who are described to me as demi-savages, and must wait awhile before venturing into their streets, which, however, are reported to be of an astonishing cleanliness.

Scarcely had we anchored, when the governor of the town sent a high functionary to pay us a visit, and to assure us of the pleasure our arrival in the port gave him. He was accompanied by other authorities and many attendants, most of whom had two swords in their belts, while a few young boys, carrying emblems of rank, followed upon his heels. These diminutive officers had only one sword, but they wore it proudly.
