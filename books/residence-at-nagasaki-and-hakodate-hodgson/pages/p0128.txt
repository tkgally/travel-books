The band played in the Temple garden; but all around, through the fences, under the fences and over them, in the trees and on the roof, there was a crowd, such as no prima donna ever saw at the moment of her greatest triumph. The great “Bougnio” sat in state, the Vice-governor, and all the grand officials were sitting or standing around him, and the usual army of two-sworded myrmidons were within call; but not even the presence of His Excellency or the sight of his Yakonins could scare away this music-loving people, or prevent them breaking down the sacred hedges of Bonze Sama’s holy garden.

This interview lasted several hours and was very satisfactory, as the Governor seemed to be desirous of doing all he could; so, after fixing an hour for business on the next day, we took our leave of His Excellency and went on board as we had landed.
