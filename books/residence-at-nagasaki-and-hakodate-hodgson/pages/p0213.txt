Time will soon show if he merited such distinction and honour, and if he had that great influence; for, although he may have played his game with oriental violence; although he may not have scrupled to shed blood on his way to empire; although he may have been an impediment to our success, and however much foreign diplomatists may have had reason to dislike him; still he was a Japanese, and, if not a patriot, undoubtedly a man of capacity, talent, and principle—whether for good or bad, that depends on the judge.
