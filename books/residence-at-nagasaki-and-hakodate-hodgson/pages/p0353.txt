Future naturalists may discover much more that is valuable, and which I have neglected to mention; and my feeble attempt at briefly describing a few of the geological specimens and products of this beautiful island may be corroborated by them.

To revert to the woods and forests of Yezo in a general and summary manner; for in my four excursions I have mentioned all the trees and flowers I could name, and Sir William Hooker has most generously embellished my narrative with an authentic list of Japanese plants received by him from the different amateurs and collectors in these islands.
