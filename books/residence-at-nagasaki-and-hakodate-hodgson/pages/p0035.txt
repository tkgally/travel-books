More than half Japan (nearly all the Daimios and nobles) were and are opposed to the Treaties. They are ever at hand to press the Government to expel the hated stranger from their shores. They are said to direct the murders which have taken place at Yukohama, in order that the foreigner may attempt revenge and demand redress from the Government, and then—let me not think of it!—either Japan ceases to exist, or the Treaties will be waste paper.
