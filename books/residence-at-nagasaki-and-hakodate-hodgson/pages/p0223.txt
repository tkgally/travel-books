These things are all true; but to whom the credit? Is it due to the actors in the scenes I have enumerated? Is it not rather due to the brave and high-minded Japanese, who bore all, suffered all, endured all patiently, because they felt their treaties bound them?

The Tycoon may, however, against his will, desire to honour the signature of his imperial predecessor. The Gōrrŏgĭō may support him; but the barons of Japan, as the barons of England have done, can force him to the water’s edge, and make “Kăwăsātĭ” as celebrated as Runnymede.

What do we dare ask of France or of Russia? Nothing but what we are expected in return to give an equivalent for. What do we demand, with big words and loud menaces, of Japan? Everything: and they want nothing.
