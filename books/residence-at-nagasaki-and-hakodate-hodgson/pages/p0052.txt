But I must say adieu to monetary affairs, as this is not my field. Suffice to say, that there are many “Fabii Cunctatores” in Japan, and when I reach Hakodate, I shall have occasion to speak more of them.

I am free to confess, that, with all their “ruses” and all their diplomacy (if I so may designate the transparent game they so feebly play), I have a regard for my Nagasaki antagonists. Talent they have, speech they have, a love of their country they have, but they did not love foreigners, and are too glad when a powerful anti-foreign ministry at Yedo sends them the “mot d’ordre” to be severe. They will respect the Treaty until forced to oppose it.
