This might have been the case, but I do not say it was. Whether it were or not, the fact soon became apparent, that, if any trade was to be done, it could be done only through the Custom-house; and to make assurance doubly sure, the Custom-house was made the Treasury also.

If the native “akindos”[[fnref *]] were compelled to pass all their goods through the Custom-house, their profits would naturally not be expected to be considerably augmented, and to recover their former prices, they would demand from the price given a proportionate increase; or decline selling: and so I believe it happened.
