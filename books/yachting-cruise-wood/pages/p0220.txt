But Pleasant Island, from which they came, has about the worst name of any of the Pacific islands, owing to the presence of runaway convicts.

Such men as these, capable of lending themselves to any villainy, should be wiped off the face of the earth. When they told me they came from Pleasant Island, I took down a book from my shelf, and read them the following extracts from the log of a vessel that visited that island: “This island is infested by Europeans, who are either runaway convicts, expirees, or deserters from whalers, and are for the most part men of the worst description. They live in a manner easily to be imagined from men of this class—without either law, religion, or education to control them, with an unlimited quantity of ardent spirits which they obtain from distilling the toddy that exudes from the cocoa-nut tree.
