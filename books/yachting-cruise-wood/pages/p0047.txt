So I fancy they were the creation of Cou Moala’s brain: your travelled South Sea Islander is always a liar—even if he were not, he would always get the credit of being one. I have mentioned above, that formerly the Rotumans were possessed of large canoes; an old chief told me that many of these canoes used to sail away loaded with men and were never more heard of.

In those days a young chief, fired with a desire to see the world, would fit out his canoe, and call upon his followers to attend him, who dared not refuse; there was always great weeping and wailing at their departure, and they sailed away often never to return.

We know that some canoes from Rotumah arrived on the northern coasts of Vanua Levu in Fiji, for their half-breed descendants may be seen to this day.
