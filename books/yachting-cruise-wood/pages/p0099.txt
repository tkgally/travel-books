# Chapter IV.

ON Wednesday, August 13th, I left Rotumah, bound for the New Hebrides, and at 2 <sc>a. m.</sc> on Sunday, land was sighted. None of us having ever visited these islands before, we waited for daylight, and then discovered we were off Traitor’s Head, on the east side of the island of Erromango. This headland received its name from Captain Cook, on account of the treacherous character of the natives. The island of Tanna, for which we were bound, was visible to the southwards. For this latter we steered, but the wind fell and we did not get into Port Resolution till next morning.
