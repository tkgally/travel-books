If they were taught that habits of industry were a necessary adjunct to the Christian religion, they would not so perish before our eyes.

It is written, “The Lord God took the man, and put him into the Garden of Eden <i>to dress it and to keep it.</i>”

The marvellously rich soil of these islands was never intended to be idle. If the present race are proved incapable of turning it to account, they will fade away before another and more energetic race, that must in course of time occupy the whole of these Pacific Islands.
