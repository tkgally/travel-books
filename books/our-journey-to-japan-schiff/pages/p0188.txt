In this northern country darkness at this time of the year sets in very late, and the evening being short, before we are aware of it the hour to retire has arrived.

<i>Monday, June 4th.</i>—This is a quiet, uneventful day. We are traversing the wide, unbroken, and to a great extent yet uncultivated prairies of Western Canada, in the forenoon the Province of Alberta, and Manitoba in the afternoon. It is worth while to observe the many new hamlets and villages springing up along the line of the railroad and which so pointedly mark the march of empire and civilization. To me in particular this development is fascinating.
