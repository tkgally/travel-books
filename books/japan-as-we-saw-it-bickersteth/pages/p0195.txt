We spent a quiet evening with our respective hosts, and my father afterwards wrote: “On Tuesday he—(Archdeacon Warren)—asked me to take their family prayers, and I had chosen Ps. xci. and said a few words on our home in God and its security and blessedness.” He little thought when choosing that Psalm how appropriate its words would be to the events of the next twelve hours.
