We are very glad that you have come to see the work of the Church in this country, in which your son is working as our Bishop, and we heartily thank God for sending you here. We had heard long before of your name as Bishop of Exeter, as the father of our dear Bishop, as one who takes a very warm interest in the C.M.S., and also of your fame as an author and poet; but hitherto we have had no opportunity of seeing you face to face, but now our hearts are filled with joy to meet you in this room and listen to your words.

“We understand you have already spent some weeks in the country, and, therefore, you have no doubt seen some of the beauties of nature here, such as Mount Fuji, Lake Biwa, etc.; and we hope you have also noticed the progress made in civilisation, and more especially the great progress in education as shown by the statistics of schools.
