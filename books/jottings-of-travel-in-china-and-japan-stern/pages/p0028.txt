[[cont]]Lake, we came upon a stretch of country showing the results of Mormon thrift. Here we saw grassy meadows, blossoming fruit trees, grazing herds of cattle and flocks of sheep. Salt Lake City, as seen from the station, was rather disappointing. The new Temple and the Tabernacle were pointed out to us; the other buildings seemed insignificant, and the town (or city) had a straggling, scattered look. Leaving Salt Lake City and passing along the border of the Great Salt Lake, we soon reached Ogden, where we changed to the cars of the Central Pacific R. R.

When we awoke on Sunday, we were in Nevada. That whole day was a journey through the desert, with mountains, bluffs and buttes in view all the time.
