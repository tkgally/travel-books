[[cont]]my life. My first sea voyage, so to say, for I had never before that been at sea more than a day and a night. I thoroughly enjoyed it, for I was so fortunate as not to have one moment of sea-sickness. I attempted to read, but read very little; I smoked, lounged about, chatted with my fellow-passengers, bored myself at whist, or joined in the few games on deck for the sake of the exercise they afforded. It was a never-ending delight to watch the ocean, at one time “deeply, darkly, beautifully blue,” at another gray, then green, then of a dull leaden color, almost black; now almost as smooth as a mill-pond, except for the long swell with which our vessel would slowly rise and then as gently sink again, and at other seasons playful or angry by turns.
