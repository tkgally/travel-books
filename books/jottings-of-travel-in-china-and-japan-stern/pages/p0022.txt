[[cont]]the next day, April 11th. While still on the train I noticed in one of the suburbs the following sign, which I thought worth remembering:

<verse>
<i>Billy Rogers Wabash House.</i>
<i>Oysters & Meals at all Hours.</i>
<i>The Resort of Sports and the Hungry,</i>
<i>The Dry and the Tired.</i>
</verse>

While walking the busy streets of bustling Chicago, I chanced across Theodore Thomas, who intended to leave that night for Omaha, there to join the National Opera Company. As this was my first visit to Chicago, I found much to interest me in its huge business buildings and the active, energetic ways of its people. If I were to put the impression it made upon me into words, I could best describe it as a giant that has not done growing.
