# XVII. Foreigners in China—A Jewish Synagogue, etc.

Concerning the social habits of the foreigners resident here, there is not much to be said. They are very hospitable, and the attentions shown a stranger usually come in the form of invitations to tiffin or to dinner. The dining hour is 7 P.M. in winter and 7.30 P.M. to 8 P.M. in summer. There is but little visiting in the evenings, and social calls are usually paid between 4 P.M. and 7 P.M.
