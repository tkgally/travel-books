When he was in the depths of the “blues” he heard steps on the veranda, and some one near by whistling “Marching through Georgia.” A moment later, a young Japanese entered and, introducing himself, said, “I am——, and have been assigned as your secretary and interpreter.”

After exchanging greetings, the Doctor’s first words were: “I heard some one whistling, a moment ago. Can you tell me who it was?”

The young man stammered an apology, saying, “It was I; I didn’t know you were so near by. Please excuse me.”

“Don’t apologize,” answered the Doctor. “I am really much obliged to you.”
