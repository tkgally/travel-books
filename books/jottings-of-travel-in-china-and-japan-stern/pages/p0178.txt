There is no general tax levied by the imperial government, and falling alike on all subjects; indeed, there is no general system of finance.

This accounts for the high rate of interest paid on such loans as the government has effected. The lowest I know of bears 5½ per cent. There are others made within the last few years paying 7, 8, and 9 per cent. respectively, although the receipts from the imperial customs are pledged as security.
