# XV. Street Scenes, etc.

The streets in the English settlement are named after Chinese provinces and cities, the word “road” being substituted for “street.” The finest shops are on the Nanking Road and are situated in the first three or four blocks after leaving the Bund. The majority of these establishments, and some of them are quite extensive, belong to foreigners, although there are a few that are conducted by Chinese. The best of these is devoted to the sale of Canton goods of various kinds, such as gold and silver ware, carvings in ivory and sandal wood, embroideries, porcelains, etc.
