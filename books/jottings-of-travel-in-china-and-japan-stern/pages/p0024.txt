There was nothing in the shape of scenery to interest one. It was too early to see the beautiful green carpet that in the course of the next few weeks will cover those vast tracts. It was after nightfall when we crossed the Mississippi River at Burlington, Iowa, where we changed to the Burlington & Missouri River R. R. We saw very little of the Iowa portion of our route, because it was not until morning that we reached Red Oak; somewhat later, we crossed the Missouri River at a place called Pacific Junction, and thus entered the State of Nebraska.
