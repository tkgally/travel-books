# IV. Yokohama.

[[right]] <i>Yokohama, May 17, 1887.</i>

* * * * In the two days that have passed since my arrival I have seen much of Yokohama, and my appetite for sight-seeing seems to grow with every hour.

Our friends have gone to the races. This is a sort of holiday with the foreign population of Yokohama. As it is an English or American, rather than a Japanese institution, I shall, instead of going to the grounds, devote this afternoon to letter writing.
