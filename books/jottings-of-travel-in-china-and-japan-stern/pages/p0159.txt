The houses of the foreigners are, as a rule, large; the rooms, on account of the summer heat, have high ceilings, and the furnishing and appointments suggest a blending of Western comfort with Eastern luxury. Public entertainments are few and far between, and, because of their rarity, are fairly well patronized, even when indifferent as to quality. There is an amateur orchestra, a choral society and the thriving Shanghai Literary and Debating Society. For out-door sports there are cricket, lawn tennis and, of late, base ball.
