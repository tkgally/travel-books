# VII. A Fire in Yokohama.

[[right]] <i>Grand Hotel,</i>

[[right]] <i>Yokohama, May 24th, 1887.</i>

This is a general holiday among the Europeans and Americans residing here. Business of nearly all kinds is suspended, for it is Queen Victoria’s birthday. Although it is more especially an English holiday, foreigners of all nationalities seem to take part in its observance.

The weather is perfect, the sky is cloudless, except near the opposite shore of lovely Yokohama Bay; the air is soft and balmy, hardly stirring the waters into a ripple.
