Then, too, there is the shampooer or rubber, usually a blind man, who wanders the streets at night seeking employment, and obtaining it, too. He carries a little fife or flute, on which he sounds two notes, an ascending fifth, so that those who are weary and yet unable to sleep may know of his coming. His fees are ridiculously low when compared with those of our artists in <i>massage</i>, although I am told his work is equally as good as theirs.

But, for that matter, the wages of native laborers are said to range between 20 and 30 <i>sen</i> per day. The <i>sen</i> equals about four-fifths of an American cent.
