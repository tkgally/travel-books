On the 15th I attended the “smoking concert” of the Shanghai Literary and Debating Society, at the Lyceum Theatre, where several hundred gentlemen were assembled. I went there as the guest of Consul-General J. S. Kennedy, the President of the Society, who has been very obliging to me during my stay here. The performance consisted of songs, instrumental solos, readings and recitations, by members of the Society, and some of the numbers were very cleverly done.

[[right]]<i>Shanghai, June 26th, 1887.</i>

After all, it rained on the 21st, and the illumination was postponed until yesterday, the 25th. There were showers at intervals through the day, and just now it looks as if it never would stop raining.
