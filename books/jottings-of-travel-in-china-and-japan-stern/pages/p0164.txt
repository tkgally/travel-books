Three of these gentlemen, Messrs. Chu Pow Fay, Kit Foo and Tong, are stationed at Shanghai, in government employ, in the Department of Telegraphs. As a matter of course, they speak English with ease, and they are bright and clever men. I have been much in their society, and owe them thanks for their unremitting kindly attention during my stay here.
