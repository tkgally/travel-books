It is easy enough to understand how the European or American is constantly longing to return to the home he has left, and quite as easy to comprehend how, when, after several years spent here, he goes back to that home, he is all impatient to return to Shanghai, where, paradoxical as it may seem, he feels that life if narrower is yet freer, and where he knows and is known by every man he meets.

And now, although it is about 10 A.M. in Philadelphia, I shall say good-night to you.
