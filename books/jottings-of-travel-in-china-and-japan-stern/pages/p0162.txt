He told me to wait a moment, ran into the house, and soon returned in company with a man about forty years of age, who informed me that the Synagogue was upstairs, and kindly offered to show it to me. On inquiring, I found that I was addressing the <i>hazan</i> of the congregation and, in all probability, its <i>schamass.</i> He told me that he came from Jerusalem. His command of English was such that I found it easier to converse with him in German, which he spoke with an unmistakable Polish accent.
