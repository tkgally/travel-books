[[cont]]ingly. At a few minutes before four o’clock the gang plank was drawn ashore and we started on our long voyage. A moment later the surface of the water in the dock was covered with bits of paper of divers colors and sizes, which had been thrown overboard by our Chinese steerage passengers, as offerings to propitiate the water-god. What is to be seen while sailing down the beautiful Bay of San Francisco and through the Golden Gate is so happily described in the little book with which we all made ourselves so familiar while I was yet at home, that I need not write a word about it. The broad waters beneath us; craft of many kinds, large and small, around and about us; with the verdure clad hills encircling the Bay, for a background: formed a picture that I shall long remember.
