In every instance, they told me that, while they prefer “European” music, they are nevertheless swayed by the spell of their own national melodies and the words to which they are set. I can easily believe that part of it, but how they can enjoy both kinds passes my understanding.

But, sooner or later, all things must end, even this long letter. I have nearly got to the end of this sheet and shall not begin another to-night.

Our day had been a tolerably busy one, as you will probably conclude by the time you have read thus far.
