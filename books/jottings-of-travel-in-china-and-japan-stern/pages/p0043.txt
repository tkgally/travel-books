This was the first trip made by the <i>San Pablo</i> after being thus refitted.

We passed several ships on the day after we left port. After that, not a sail was seen until, on the morning of May 15th, we were steaming up the Bay of Yokohama.

During the long voyage we were favored with pleasant weather until the last day. We took the course along the 37th parallel of north latitude until nearing the coast of Japan. Our sails, however, were of no use, as, during the greater part of the time, we had head winds. The steamers that cross the Pacific are not to be hurried, and the Captains are expected to be sparing of the coal used. The <i>San Pablo’s</i> allowance was about forty tons per day.
