I did not mind the discomfort, but gave up almost the entire day to watching the huge waves that would now and then dash over the roof of the saloon and the smoking-room. I was gratified at beholding the storm and at being in it, and yet my curiosity in that direction was so fully satisfied by that day's experience, that, instead of wishing for a worse, I trust I may never again be in so violent a one. I was not afraid, except as realizing that one is always in danger while on the water. While I knew that, for the time being, our risk was increased, I had enough confidence in the ship's officers not to worry.
