The harbor of Nagasaki is almost landlocked, the town lying at the foot and along the sides of the green hills that encircle it like an amphitheatre. It looks prettier and more inviting from the ship than it proves on shore. It has all the usual features of a busy maritime town. Jack-tars of various nationalities abound, and in the foreign quarter there is quite a number of queer looking taverns that seem to have been intended for the special entertainment of sailors.

There are five Russian men-of-war lying in the harbor. This morning we heard the band on the Admiral’s ship playing the Russian national anthem, and it was well played, too.
