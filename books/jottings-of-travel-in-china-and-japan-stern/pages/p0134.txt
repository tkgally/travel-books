They prefer being under the jurisdiction of the Municipal Council to subjecting themselves to the capricious and at times exacting “squeezes” of the native officials of Shanghai proper. Some of them have fine shops in the best quarters and are regarded as honorable merchants; that they are very shrewd at driving a bargain, I am, from my own experience, quite willing to believe.

The American quarter is known as Hongkew. In this are situated the Astor House, the American Consulate, the German Consulate, the offices and wharves of the Japanese Steamer Company, the docks and ship-yards of Farnham & Co., and factories of various kinds.
