On the 24th a small steamer arrived, but declined to take any passengers because she had been ordered to sail in quest of the missing <i>Pautah.</i> On the 25th there was another departure, but the captain politely informed me that he had cabin accommodations for only two passengers and that he had been “bespoke” by the <i>taotai</i> of Cheefoo and his Secretary, the clever Mr. Wong-Kai-Ka. Finally, during the afternoon of that day, the S. S. <i>Tung Chow,</i> Captain Shaw, arrived, and I was informed that she would sail the next morning. I lost no time in securing passage, and, before retiring for the night, had sent my trunks on board.
