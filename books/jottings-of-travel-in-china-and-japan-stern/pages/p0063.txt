At the present writing there are, I understand, some four hundred miles of railroads in the Empire, with numerous additional lines projected or already under way. The Tokio-Yokohama line was built at government expense, which may in part explain why it cost so much. There is no need of one’s travelling so far from home to find similar instances of a lavish use of public funds. The present policy of the government is to encourage the forming of joint stock companies for such purposes, leaving private persons to take the initiative; thus rendering it tolerably certain that future undertakings will be based on the actual needs of the community and will be conducted with proper economy.
