EARLY in February, 1887, my friend W. B. called at my office one day and, drawing me aside, said that he wanted me to go to China for him, to represent him in certain business matters in which he was interested. He thought that the trip would take about five months; told me that I would have one or two travelling companions, and that the rest of the party would sail from New York, <i>via</i> Suez, and join us at Shanghai.
