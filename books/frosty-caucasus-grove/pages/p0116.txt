For naked grandeur of mountain form the Tcherek valley is probably unequalled in Europe. In the far recesses of the Himalayas or Andes, perhaps in the Caucasus itself, though it is little likely, some vale may be found of yet more sublime and commanding beauty; but in the Alps there is nothing so great to behold. We were none of us anxious to leave the worship of our youth, or hastily to run after new gods. I shall have to say later on, that in many ways the Northern Caucasus is below the beauty of Switzerland; but no fond memory of early admiration, no sympathetic prejudice in favour of the land where we had wandered so long, could blind us to the greater splendour of this Tcherek valley. Half admiring, half reluctant, we felt the presence of a mountain glory beyond any we had known.

The huge peaks and ridges which are seen rise around the valley, with the exception of Dych-Tau, which shows admirably from some points, but is at a considerable distance to the north-west.
