# Chapter XII.

[[subtitle]] The Nakhar Pass and the Valleys of the Klütch and the Kodor.—The Last March.

D<sc>uring</sc> the first part of the night an ominous cloud covered the col of the Nakhar pass, and as it was certain that the Utchkulan men would not make their way over the snow if the work was in any degree more difficult than usual, I watched the sky with considerable anxiety for some time.
