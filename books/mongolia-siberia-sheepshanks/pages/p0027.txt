Towards evening the weather seemed to be clearing up a little, and the camels were packed. There were now nine of them, beside the one that drew the cart. No sooner were we under weigh than the rain recommenced with increased violence. To my dismay I found that the door and the little window on the windward side leaked considerably, and gutters of water began to flow down upon my clothes and what stock of food I had stowed inside the cart. One of the most exciting and exasperating of the minor evils of life is to live in a hut that leaks, and by hut I mean a house containing but one room, so that there is no possibility of escaping the falling drops by fleeing to another apartment.
