I left my tarantass to my travelling companion, asking him to sell it if he could and forward me the money; but I was not greatly surprised that I heard no more of the matter.

We sailed pleasantly down the Kama to its junction with the Volga, then up the Volga to Nijni-Novgorod, thence on to Moscow, delightful city! where I spent some time. Thence by St. Petersburg through Germany to England, where I arrived before the end of November 1867, not unthankful, I trust, for my preservation and countless mercies, and happy in the thought that I was again at home.
