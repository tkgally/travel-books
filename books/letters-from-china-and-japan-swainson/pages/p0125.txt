# Letter XXVI.

[[right]] <i>March 5th.</i>

For the last few evenings a number of people have been going about in processions, carrying lanterns and beating gongs—the former made in the shape of fish, cocks, hens, and some very large ones illuminated by numerous lights, representing a dragon. This amusement is called “manœuvering the dragon,” which is carried on poles by many bearers, so that the <i>animal</i> wriggles about like a great sea-serpent.
