# Preface.

I<sc>t</sc> is with much diffidence that I venture to present the following pages to the Public. Had I seriously contemplated the possibility of being induced to <i>write a book,</i> while we were yet resident in China, I should certainly have taken more care in collecting materials to make it an interesting one. It was, however, only on returning to this country that I fully realised how little is known by the world at large of daily life in the far East; and the oft-repeated question—“Why don’t you write a book?”—resulted in the publication of the Letters for whose appearance I am now apologising.

They are simply those written to my relatives at home, divested of all personal matter not absolutely necessary for carrying on the narrative.
