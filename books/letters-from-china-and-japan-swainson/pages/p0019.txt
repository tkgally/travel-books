# Letter IV.

[[right]] P. <sc>and</sc> O. S<sc>teamer</sc>, <i>March 20th.</i>

The voyage begins to be very tiresome, but two days hence, the longest part of it, being that between Aden and Point-de-Galle, is expected to come to an end.

At Aden most of the passengers went on shore, and I was to have accompanied the E.’s, had such a torrent of rain not come on, that Mrs S. and I decided to remain on board, which we afterwards regretted, as it was a lovely afternoon and quite cool. We were, however, much amused in watching the natives chattering and diving round the steamer.
