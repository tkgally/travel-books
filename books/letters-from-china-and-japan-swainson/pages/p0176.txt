In the evening we had a <i>rechauffé</i> of the previous day’s dinner-party, with two additions, one of whom I found had come to the East by the same steamer that I did.

<i>Oct. 6th.</i>—To church in the morning—congregation only thirteen in number. There was no music except two hymns—the clergyman acting as precentor, and “setting up” the tunes. At four o’clock we sailed again for Shanghai. The scenery on the river is rather pretty,—green valleys bounded by distant hills till we got near the entrance, where it is of a rougher, wilder type of beauty,—two high hills on each side, one of them defended by a fort, and in the middle of the channel a great rock on which there is a lighthouse.
