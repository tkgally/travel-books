[[cont]]supply. Miss Laurence has now entered on the sixth year of her labour of love, and is most anxious to find a suitable helper in her work. Will no daughter of the Church of England follow her example, and, forsaking all for the sake of Him who will abundantly reward her, go out at once to share the work, and enable Miss Laurence, as soon as her health may require it, to recruit her strength without having to leave the work to suffer in her absence?

What can be more useful than the training from early childhood of the future mothers of a nation where female education has so long been neglected?
