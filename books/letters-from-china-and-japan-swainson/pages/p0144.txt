# Letter XXXIII.

[[right]] <i>November 8th,</i> 187-

We were present yesterday at a very curious scene—the thanksgiving service held by the crew of the training ship, in honour of her safe return.

The invitation was sent us on a large sheet of bright red paper, and H. said it was most amusing to see how pleased the men were when they heard we should be present, and how eager to have everything “<i>all proper,</i>” which in their acceptation of the expression, means a liberal supply of “<i>chow-chow.</i>” He had to explain to them that if “<i>Missisy</i>” went their acting must really be all proper, and of course there was some little difficulty in defining our meaning of the word. The result proved that he had been successful, and that they had attended to his behest, for Chinese plays are, generally speaking, too coarse for female eyes.
