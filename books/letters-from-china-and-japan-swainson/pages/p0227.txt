## Bernard (Bayle).

SAMUEL LOVER, THE LIFE AND UNPUBLISHED WORKS OF. In 2 vols. Post 8vo. With a Steel Portrait. 21<i>s.</i>

Samuel Lover was at once Poet, Novelist, Dramatist, Painter, Etcher, and Composer, and in addition to the story of his life, Mr. Bernard has here presented some of his most interesting unpublished papers.

## Blanc (Henry), M.D.

CHOLERA: HOW TO AVOID AND TREAT IT. Popular and Practical Notes. Crown 8vo. 4<i>s.</i> 6<i>d.</i>

The author refers in these practical notes to well-ascertained facts, and avoids all theories and hypotheses. He has applied to Indian authors for most of the facts on which alone a knowledge of cholera can be based, and has added to them the results of his own experience.

<blockquote>
“A very practical manual, based on experience and careful observation, full of excellent hints on a most dangerous disease.”—<i>Standard.</i>
</blockquote>

## Blume (Major William).

THE OPERATIONS OF THE GERMAN ARMIES IN FRANCE, from Sedan to the end of the war of 1870-71. With Map. From the Journals of the Head-quarters Staff, by Major William Blume. Transl. by the late E. M. Jones, Maj. 20th Foot, Prof. of Mil. Hist., Sandhurst. Demy 8vo. 9<i>s.</i>

The purpose of this work is to give a sketch of the events of the late war before Sedan, correct in all material points. The translation is a literal one, each narrative requiring as nearly as possible the <i>ipsissima verba.</i>

<blockquote>
“The work of Major von Blume in its English dress forms the most valuable addition to our stock of works upon the war that our press has put forth. Our space forbids our doing more than commending it earnestly as the most authentic and instructive narrative of the second section of the war that has yet appeared.”—<i>Saturday Review.</i>
</blockquote>
