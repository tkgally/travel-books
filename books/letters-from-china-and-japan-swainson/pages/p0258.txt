## Jackson (T. G.)

MODERN GOTHIC ARCHITECTURE. Crown 8vo. 5<i>s.</i>

The author desires to induce some to pause in the midst of the revival of Gothic architecture, which is being so assiduously promoted, and to consider candidly what useful end the revival is to serve, and what solid advantage society has hitherto obtained from it.

“The reader will find some of the most important doctrines of eminent art teachers practically applied in this little book, which is well written and popular in style.”—<i>Manchester Examiner.</i>

This thoughtful little book is worthy of the perusal of all interested in art or architecture.”—<i>Standard.</i>
