Those of my friends who regarded my departure for Foo-chow as one to a different world, will be surprised to hear that gaiety can exist in what to them, seemed such an unknown region of the earth. Yet so it is! and a few evenings ago, we found it quite possible to dance in the “unknown region” till somewhere about three in the morning.

The ball did not take place here, but at the town, which is twelve miles farther up the river, and where we went to stay with H.’s friends, the Consul and his wife.

There being a regatta here last Wednesday, and consequently a holiday at the Arsenal, we took advantage of it, that we might have two whole days for our visit, which we preferred to being present at the boat races. The Baron de M. kindly sent his house-boat for us, which is a species of vessel I have mentioned once or twice without describing, so I had better now enlighten your mind on the subject, it being an all-important conveyance in this roadless land.
