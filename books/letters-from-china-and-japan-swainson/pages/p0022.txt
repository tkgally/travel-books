22nd. Rather an uncomfortable rolling going on this morning, most of the ladies ill. Last night it was charming, and I quite enjoyed the little breeze when walking up and down with Mr S. It is not, however, favourable to writing, and the Punkah boys are working so energetically, that my paper is being blown about in a most aggravating manner. I think, on the whole, Punkahs are a <i>bore</i>, they make a draft, give one cold, and spoil one's temper by carrying off, paper, work, and all other light property.

The prospect of reaching Ceylon this afternoon is most cheering. We shall spend all to-morrow on shore and intend to enjoy ourselves thoroughly.
