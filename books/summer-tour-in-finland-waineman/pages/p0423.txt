## The Little Guides.

With many Illustrations by E. H. N<sc>ew</sc> and other artists, and from photographs.

<i>Small Pott</i> 8<i>vo, cloth,</i> 2<i>s.</i> 6<i>d. net; leather,</i> 3<i>s.</i> 6<i>d. net, each volume.</i>

The main features of these Guides are (1) a handy and charming form; (2) illustrations from photographs and by well-known artists; (3) good plans and maps; (4) an adequate but compact presentation of everything that is interesting in the natural features, history, archæology, and architecture of [[corr tbe|the]] town or district treated.
