I left Torneå in sunshine, but I saw the hand of its dreaded neighbour, which so soon would shower millions and millions of snowflakes upon those peaceful homesteads. The light and sun, that the coming darkness of the long polar night would so mercilessly extinguish, seemed a cruel mockery. The thought of that endless winter gave me a sensation of horror. Then my eyes chanced to fall on the walls of the ancient churchyard, and I remembered those nameless graves where the brave soldiers of Finland are resting. The soft snow would cover them in a warm pall of white and hide their poverty. Winter brings its blessings also. My dream town became again cloudless and serene.
