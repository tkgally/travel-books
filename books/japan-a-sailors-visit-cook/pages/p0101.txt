Now they are as expert in their manufacture as any other nation, having extensive mills in which they use immense quantities of rice straw, making a tough, flinty paper. When combined with linen it makes the finest paper for books and writing material. The paper used for their bank bills is not excelled by the paper made for the same purpose in the United States.

In decorative art they are true artists, and their groups of well chosen, delicate flowers, their life-like birds and their own gorgeously arrayed figures cannot fail to please the most exacting.
