In bringing my fragmentary notes to a close, I must say that to my mind the picture of Japan is a bright one. Up to the present time the people have shown great aptitude in adapting themselves to a higher civilization, and in very many things they compare favorably with the nations of the West. What their rapid advancement may lead to will be known in the near future. A people so amiable and clever can have only the best wishes of all who hail the advance of Christianity with a love that is second to none other.
