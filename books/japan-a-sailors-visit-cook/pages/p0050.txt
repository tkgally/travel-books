After a cup of tea at the station tea house, the whistle of the engine caused us to gather up our packages and hasten to take a seat in the rolling carriage that was to carry us home. On our way near the station Totska, which we passed, are some artificial caves, the walls being covered with representations of various real and imaginary creatures.

It is said they are well worth a visit, but time was short, the train in a hurry, so on we went leaving them behind. Shortly after we swept into Yokohama, and although so far from our native land, rejoiced that we had arrived at our temporary home.
