# Japan.

# Chapter I.

[[subtitle]] Approaching Japan—The Sacred Mountain—Island of Osima—Arrival at Yokohama—Sampans—On Shore.

A<sc>lways</sc> having had a desire to visit the East, it was a pleasant surprise, when I was called by business in that direction, but when the Japanese Empire was announced as my destination I felt as one favored by the gods. The following narrative of my travels is the result of my visit there. The notes I commenced as the land was neared, so as to convey some idea of the outlying islands and coast from the time we passed the volcano group, the southernmost of which lies a few hundred miles south of Japan, where we began anxiously to count the days and hours which must expire before we could arrive at our destined port.

Our ship drove forward through mist and fog towards the land of the “Rising Sun,” that land which in the past had been visited only in dreams, in which it was pictured as a fairy land, and the people, in imagination, the elves and fairies who peopled it.
