In the front of Monolith Lavatory (so called), we were shown a large granite urn, with a mouth over two feet across, gradually growing less until at the depth of three feet the excavation ended. This was partly full of water, and we were told by our guide (in all sincerity) that it was full twice in twenty-four hours, and that the miracle was caused by the prayer of an old priest who wished a token from Buddha of his acceptance into the divine paradise.

I wished to examine the boulder on which it sat to see where the pipe which helped perform the miracle entered the vase, but was gently reminded by the priest that it was time to be on the move.
