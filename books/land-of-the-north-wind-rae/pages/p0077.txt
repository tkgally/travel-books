The extremity of it is Torneå. Eastward it reaches to the White Lake: towards the North, comprehending diverse provinces, it extends itself beyond all knowledge. On the West towards Iseland it joins to part of Norway, tis bounded with Swedland, Finland, and both the Bothnia’s. But,’ says my old chronicler with triumph, ‘thus part of Lapland would lie in Finland or Ostrobothnia, and part in Westrobothnia, which everyone knows is false, and the very Vulgar can tell so much that the Bothnick sea comes not anywhere within eighteen or twenty miles of Lapland.
