Above all the Pooles is Enâratraesk, in which the Hills and Islands are innumerable.’

This Enâra Traesk is a great lake, close to Buolgai Suolo, on the northern coast of Lapland—the funny little place I visited three years ago: not far either from Mr. Dahll’s gold river, the Tâna, where the Russians are working. We are fairly in the heart of Lapland now, the Lapland sung by Regnard:

<verse>
<i>Tombeau de la nature, effroyables rivages,</i>
<i>Que l’ours dispute encore à l’homme sauvage.</i>
</verse>

If the Doctor finds the bear disputing anything with savage man, he will not wait to see the result: indeed, if he sees a bear without the savage man, and not disputing with anybody, he will not stay.
