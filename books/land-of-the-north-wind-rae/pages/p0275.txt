Behind us was the turbid river, in front a quagmire: to decide was the work of a moment: I leapt into the mud, which rose half-way to my knees, and floundered to the bank, while loud laughter came pealing from the boat.

In two hours more there appeared our boatmen, seven Samoyedî—that is, gentlemen—and one Samoyedka—that is, a lady—in procession, with ten sledges and forty reindeer.
