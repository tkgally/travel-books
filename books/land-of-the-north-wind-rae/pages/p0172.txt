On the whole we felt that we should require considerable means of transport, and were glad to hear that our steamer was not likely to be full.

Our tent deserves more than a passing notice. It was constructed, after protracted studies, with two jointed upright posts—four feet six inches in height, with brass ferrules and spikes—planted in the ground six feet and a half apart.
