As it is, whether Jones’ ancestors were Cyclops, Pygmies, or Troglodytes I am ignorant, and the world will remain in darkness. If it were not impertinent to advance an opinion founded upon mere personal observation, and only of value accordingly, I should be inclined to set Jones down as a Troglodyte.

Of the climate of Lapland John Schefferus, Professor at Upsal, says: ‘It is so extreme cold here in the Winter, that tis not to be endured but by those that have bin brought up in it.
