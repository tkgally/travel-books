We saw the famous Codex and Luther’s Bible, with that tough old Christian’s handwriting all over the margins, and Gustav Wasa’s Bible, and Charles XII.’s Bible, and the Devil’s Bible. We did not place much confidence in the two latter books, as the pugnacious Karl XII. was unlikely to have much time to spend in Bible-reading, and we thought the ownership of the second was not satisfactorily established. Thanking the good-natured scholar who had humoured the aspirations of two young travellers, we returned to the hotel to write. At eight o’clock we left for Upsala.
