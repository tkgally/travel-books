We carried a mosquito curtain of gauze dyed brown, with which to close the opening in front of the tent. Then we laughed in our moustaches, and said we should be too many for the mosquitoes. In Lapland we made the mistake of carrying white mosquito veils, which were both conspicuous and seductive to the mosquitoes: this year a sober brown proved more becoming and unobtrusive.

Eventually we decided to make the voyage to Archangel direct by sea, as being the least fatiguing mode of travelling. An obliging firm of shipping agents in London furnished us with particulars of steamers sailing from time to time, and we set our choice upon the steamer Norden, sailing from London on the 15th of June for Archangel. It was a pleasant surprise to us to learn that she would call for a few hours at Granton, in the Frith of Forth, and that we might avoid two days of dreary voyage by joining her there.

We left home accordingly with light hearts on the 17th of June, and reached Granton towards midnight—going directly on board of the Norden, which had arrived and would sail towards daybreak.
