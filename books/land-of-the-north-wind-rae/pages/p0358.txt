Then follows the declaration by the above parties to serve on board the said ship on a voyage from Archangel to Dundee or any ports in the Baltic, White Sea, and adjacent continent of Europe, for a probable period of three months: to conduct themselves in an honest, orderly, and sober manner: to love, honour, and obey the captain: to submit themselves to the lawful commands of the superior officers: to consent, in event of their having succeeded in embezzling or wilfully destroying any of the ship’s cargo or stores, to make good the loss out of the salary due to them: to submit in case of incompetency to a proportionate reduction in their wages. No wines or spirits were to be allowed, and no cash to be advanced in a foreign port.

We saw some good furs in Archangel, a beautiful glutton skin among others, costing four roubles. A sable’s skin will cost perhaps ten times as much, a sea otter’s thirty times, a fine black fox-skin perhaps a hundred times. We did not buy any black fox-skins, as we had become poor, very poor, before leaving Archangel. Captain Wright and Mr. Birse dined with us at the club, and we played pyramids afterwards.
