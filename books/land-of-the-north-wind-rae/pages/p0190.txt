Their utensils, weapons, dresses, idols, even their skeletons, would be valuable: and a word from the Governor would bring them—all but the skeletons perhaps—for the Northern savages, Samoyédes and Ostjaks, are very superstitious.

We drove again to Solômbola and called upon Mr. Schmitt, who very kindly volunteered to lend us a pillow and two little mattrasses for the journey.
