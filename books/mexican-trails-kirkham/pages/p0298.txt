Should you follow them, you might have a peep into the houses: the best room on exhibition with its innumerable chairs, arranged always with precision along the walls, and its many large vases of artificial flowers. The fashion is stereotyped and time-honoured. Throughout the length and breadth of Mexico are the selfsame chairs, set with equal rigour, the identical vases.

Peradventure you would encounter more than one love-lorn youth, standing beneath a window,—as near as he may approach in his shy and distant courtship. He is “playing bear,” as they say in Mexico. Thus the course of true love runs here. It may, perhaps, be smooth as long as there are iron bars between the lovers. How should they do otherwise in Morelia—or in Seville? Shall not tradition have an abiding-place here and there? and may there not be a nook and corner in the world where good old customs still obtain, for the benefit of painters and writers and chance travellers
