Lying at the base of the volcano, the town some day may meet the fate of Herculaneum. Savants will explore and discover. There will be a museum of the utensils and ornaments of the people of Tonila, who were destroyed in a single day by the great eruption of the year——, a collection containing many horse pistols, but no forks.

Beyond Tonila the road, which has skirted the base of the volcano all the way from Tuxpan, gently descends into the beautiful valley in which lies the town of Colima. The fire-peak is a perfect cone. From its crater now ascends only a faint wreath of vapour, but occasionally there are explosions and discharges of ashes and stones. The last great eruption was in 1903.
