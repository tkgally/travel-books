Its low, massive style, rectangular and geometric, without an arch or curve anywhere save in the columns, is full of dignity. Huge monoliths surmount the square-cut doorways, while the walls within and without are enriched with mosaics in intricate geometric patterns, giving a peculiarly refined and delicate appearance to a structure in effect massive as a fortress. Like an antique gem it is set here in the solemn landscape amid the cacti.

There is a present Mitla, built on the site perhaps of the ancient city, a village of cane huts hidden behind their cactus walls. Incapable of temples, the moderns project nothing more lasting than a mud house. Their old men dream no dreams and their young men see no visions. The villagers speak Tzapoteco, an uncouth and barbaric tongue, and look curiously at the stranger within their gates, as upon some new and undesirable species of man.
