# Chapter XIX

[[subtitle]] A Week on the Isthmus

IT is a far cry from Mexico City to the Isthmus of Tehuantepec of irreproachable immorality—from the oil-stove to the hammock. The “Tierra Caliente” is another and distinct Mexico, as different from the great plateau as is Egypt from Italy.

I had thought of making the trip on horseback from Oaxaca, but found it was a matter of six days, among a peculiarly inhospitable folk. Moreover, there were no good horses to be had.
