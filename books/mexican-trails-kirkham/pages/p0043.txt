Appreciative folk, who have long imbibed the spirit of the land, talk much of the “Mexican habit.” Already I am coming to realise what it means. One may be laying the foundations for it while sitting in the Zocalo, loitering in the cathedral, or sauntering under the ahuehuetls of Chapultepec and in the by-roads of Coyoacan. Far from being the inertia of the tropics, it is an infatuation pure and simple. One might have a Japanese, an Italian, or possibly a French habit, but none of them would quite resemble this.
