# Chapter III

[[subtitle]] In Tacubaya

WHATEVER cosmopolitan airs the City of Mexico may wear, they are left behind when one goes out to Tacubaya—that is to say, the Tacubaya of the people. Here are, to be sure, large estates, of which vistas may be caught at the porter’s gate, but they affect not at all the life and character of the village streets and lie concealed and guarded within their high walls. Now, Tacubaya is just beyond Chapultepec—the Hill of the Grasshopper.
