It was the full of the moon and Iguala lay dreaming under its tamarinds in the glorious tropical night. We were quartered in one room in the little hotel. It was a large room, but not large enough, and we slept ill.

Wherefore, next morning, three very sleepy men got into the saddle. We were to stay at an hacienda, and our host had ridden down the day before to meet us. Iguala lies south of Cuernavaca, while Tasco is northwest of Iguala, so that a semi-circle of a hundred miles is described in reaching it. We were soon following up the long cañon through grim defiles. On approaching, there is not the least evidence of a place of the importance of Tasco, which is the oldest mining-camp in Mexico.
