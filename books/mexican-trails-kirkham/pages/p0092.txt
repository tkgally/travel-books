What innumerable pictures on these never to be forgotten roads: burros lost to view beneath their loads of hay; others laden with pottery, with charcoal, with pigskins of pulque and barrels of spirits. Lo, the poor donkey! Ever toiling, ever patient, he carries the burdens of Mexico on his unresisting back. These are all dream-pictures and naturally fit into the dream-life one leads; so that to leave the cloistered seclusion of the patio, the friendly and musical voice of the little fountain, is not to come into rude contact with any hard world of facts but rather to go forth in sleep, as it were, and to encounter dream-people.

A Mexican cannot fail to be picturesque when he wears his hat. He supports it with an air always, an abandon that is good to see.
