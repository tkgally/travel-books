# Chapter XVI

[[subtitle]] A Tavern of Puebla

IN the days of the Conquest, Cholula was a great capital, while Puebla had not been founded. To-day Cholula has dwindled to a squalid village, whereas Puebla is one of the most thriving cities of the republic, a place of commerce rather than of antiquarian interest. Its one attraction lies in the tiled domes of its churches. These are of all colours, and the tiles give to Puebla such individuality as it may possess. They were, perhaps, a legacy from the Moor; for the tile, like the dome, long ago came out of the East.
