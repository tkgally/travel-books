At last Sanchez relented in so far as to let go of one leg and listen to the fervent appeal in the prisoner’s behalf. The majesty of the law was to be upheld however, and it was only when tired of holding his captive—no easy task, to be sure—that he consented to relinquish him, authority having been vindicated, the populace impressed.

Don Sanchez pulled down his pea-jacket, readjusted his pistol, and resumed his beat.

The Calle Matamoros is the “Main Street” of the town, and one sees over the doors of the shops some good old Spanish names. Francisco Mendoza, of aristocratic name if not lineage, has the only show-window. Here he displays several pieces of cloth, some pairs of yellow congress gaiters with very pointed toes, and a few silk neckerchiefs, pea-green and lemon-yellow.
