In true Mexican fashion I embraced the Pioneer, and the little cavalcade trotted through the deserted streets into the silent country.

The route to Los Reyes lies over the Sierra, which, in Michoacan, is covered with odorous pine woods. All the world seemed fair and life was good. Throughout that day we followed dim forest aisles, hearing only the woodpecker. The road wound in and out between grim volcanic peaks; it was the lonely heart of the Sierra where the light is ever subdued. We had crossed the divide and were riding over a little plateau, when the trail made a turn, and there in the west loomed the volcano of Colima. It seemed near at hand, but was in reality eight days’ ride. No smoke issued from the crater; apparently Colima was at rest.
