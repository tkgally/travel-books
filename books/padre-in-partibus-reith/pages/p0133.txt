I have wandered far away from Jokjakarta, but I gave my readers fair warning that I should be discursive. In my next letter, I shall give an account of my expedition to Mendoet and Boro Boedoer, while in a future letter there will probably be more to say about Jokjakarta.

H<sc>otel</sc> T<sc>oegoe</sc>, J<sc>okjakarta</sc>,

<i>April 7th, 1896.</i>
