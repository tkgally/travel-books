## P<sc>ostscript</sc>.

For the information of any Singaporeans who may decide to make the Bromo trip during their next holiday, I append a note of the expenses, which are not very heavy considering the length of the journey and the time occupied in making it. It should be done leisurely—a night at Poespoh on the way up, and another on the way down, and two nights at Tosari. I was told in Sourabaya that a well known Singapore gentleman left the town a few weeks ago with the intention of achieving the double journey in two days. He has not since been heard of.

| Railway fare, Sourabaya to Pasoeroean and back first class | f.8.00 |
| Dogcart from Pasoeroean to Paserpan and back | 5.00 |
| Dogcart from Paserpan to Poespoh and back | 6.00 |
| Hotel at Poespoh (everything included) two days | 20.00 |
| Pony to Tosari and back | 5.00 |
| Two coolies to carry baggage to Tosari and back | 3.00 |
| Pony to the Bromo and back | 4.50 |
| Guide | 1.50 |
| Hotel at Tosari, two days | 14.00 |
| | f.67.00 |

A few extras will have to be added,—the inevitable <i>per sen</i> to boys, coolies and syces, and the price of liquors, &c., at Tosari, but the former will not run to more than two guilders unless the traveller scatters his tips like a lord, and the latter may be much or little according to the traveller's tastes.
