There, I was told, two cherubs were fondly kissing, though to my eyes I confess they looked more like a pair of sheep’s heads; and so finding new absurdities every moment, we came to the great gateway; drove between the huge red rocks, 250 feet high; and turned to see the view. It surpasses everything I have yet seen.

The great rocks were of a warm salmon colour, with green pines growing in their crevices, bringing out the richness of their colouring; and between them, as if set in a glowing frame, shone Pike’s Peak, covered with snow, as a centre to the picture, with Cameron’s Cone and the foot-hills, all blue, white, and pink, three or four miles off.

I wish every one at home could see this view. No descriptions or photographs can do it justice; and as for drawing it—who can do that?
