## Decisions
- Cover: no readable title on the cloth; `cover.jpg` is the title page. Frontispiece p0008-1 set in book.json; the IA notice leaf p0006 waived.
- The second half-title (p0019) is JSON only; the Camões motto with its translation (p0020) is a reading division “Motto”; the head title above the Introduction is JSON only.
- Plate captions with their printed page references (“P. 389.”), as printed; the caption of p0378 (cut off in this scan) read from the Oxford copy (fairlusitania00jackgoog n381), said in `notes_on_text`.
- Running heads “Contents.” typed as headings on the contents' later pages (by the corrections pass, agreed by the judge) removed.
- The appendix's music is four pictures; the words under it on p. 406 are a verse block.
- The printed contents give chapter summaries that the chapters do not print; the contents of this edition use the chapter titles (assumed).
