[[cont]]First came a dish of <i>fesinjun</i>,—a fat capon, partly grilled and then stewed in a mixture of pomegranate juice and powdered walnut. This was the <i>pièce de resistance</i> of the meal, and the only other thing I can remember as being worthy of notice on account of its distinctly Persian characteristic was the bread, a peculiar black variety, warm and spongy.

After dinner the Archbishop smoked a Kalian.

Now, the Kalian is such an important feature in Persian life that I think it must have a little chapter to itself.
