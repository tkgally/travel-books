## Decisions
- The half-title and Stevenson motto (p0005–p0006) stand before the title page: JSON only. Frontispiece p0008-1 set in book.json. Tissue p0009–p0010 waived (not transcribed).
- Cover: the front cover's title is dim on black cloth with a library label; `cover.jpg` is the title page.
- Captions as on the plates (capitals and small capitals); the list of illustrations in small capitals of one height (`<sc>` lower case).
- The Greek inscriptions are typed as verse (one printed line a line), their translations as quotations; `css_extra` lets their long unbroken lines wrap at phone width (the rendered look found sideways scrolling).
- Long s in quotations typed s; a real f stays (p0200 “chafe”).
