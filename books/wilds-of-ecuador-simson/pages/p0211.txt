One such assault took place quite shortly before our descent of the river, as related to us by the <i>padre.</i> As that missionary was ascending the Napo in his canoe, he suddenly observed a number of savages, which he estimated at seventy or eighty, on two large rafts, just in the act of crossing the river towards its right bank, a little below the Aguarico; and before he could come up with them, they had landed and escaped into the forest. Upon arriving at the Aguarico, the Piojés informed him that the band he had seen had attacked them, wounding some—whom he saw—and carrying off all their iron implements and valuables. On other occasions they had also carried off some of the women.
