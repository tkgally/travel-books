This ordeal completed, Llore’s mother-in-law, who was originally one of their horde and related to some of them, gave them a most energetic exhortation, in which she told them that they must treat us and her son-in-law well, and on no account abandon us on the way, or in any way fail to look after our safety and welfare on the journey. For, she threatened them, if they did not behave well and bring him safely back, they need never come to her again for <i>lienzo</i>, knives, poison, or any other commodity.

She spoke with great vehemence, and they evidently listened to her with attention and respect, appearing like schoolboys under a rating for misconduct. She was the most intelligent of their horde, in fact remarkably so; and having married a trader, was often able to assist them and supply their greatest wants. They held her in great consideration, and looked up to her, allowing her great influence over them.
