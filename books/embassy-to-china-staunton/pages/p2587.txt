The first word or character of the first series or column of ten words, joined to the first word of the second series or column of twelve, marks the first year of the cycle; and so on until the first series is exhausted; when the eleventh word of the second series combined with the first of the first series, marks the eleventh year of the cycle; and the twelfth or last of the second series joined with the second of the first series, serves for denoting the twelfth year. The third of the first series becomes united in regular progression with the first of the second series, to mark the thirteenth year; and proceeding by this rule, the first character in the first and in the second series cannot come again together for sixty years, or until the first year of the second cycle. The Christian year 1797, answers to the fifty-fourth year of the sixty-eighth Chinese cycle, which ascertains its commencement to have been two thousand two hundred and seventy-seven years before the birth of Christ; unless it be supposed that the official records and public annals of the empire, which bear testimony to it, should all be falsified; and that the cycle when first established should have been antidated; which is indeed as little probable, as that the period, for example, of the Olympiads, should be asserted to have commenced many ages prior to the first Olympic games.
