Some of them entering into the great cabin of the Lion, where the Embassador had a portrait of the Emperor of China, immediately recognized it, and prostrating themselves before it, kissed the ground several times with great devotion; on rising, they appeared to feel a sort of gratitude towards the foreigner who had the attention to place the portrait of their sovereign in his apartment. Tho the ship's crew, at length, suffered many of them to range unnoticed and unaccompanied thro every part of the vessel, this indulgence was not abused by the commission of any act of impropriety. Among them few betrayed that awkwardness or rudeness of manners, or apparent vacancy of mind, so frequently observable among other people in the lower classes of life.

The Clarence in going to Chu-san, worked with the tide up Duffield's passage, where she was obliged to anchor when the ebb began. This passage is formed between the large island Lowang on the east, and a smaller, on the west, and is not above three miles wide; yet the depth of water is from one hundred to one hundred and twenty fathoms, and in the midst are several rocks, and two or three islets.
