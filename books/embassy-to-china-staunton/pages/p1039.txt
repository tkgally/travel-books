XLIV. The Scoop-wheel of China, for lifting water upon the banks of rivers for agricultural purposes. These wheels, which are very common in the southern provinces, are made entirely of bamboo, are put together without a nail, and are from fifteen to forty feet in diameter. They come nearest to the Persian or bucket-wheel, but are materially different in the principle and construction. A wheel thirty feet in diameter will lift, in the course of twenty-four hours, near seventy thousand gallons of water.
