So great a rise from so small an origin, may perhaps seem singular to men accustomed to the intermediate orders and regular gradations of a mixed government; but it is not uncommon either in countries, the monarch of which can indulge his fancy or his will without any apprehension of control; or in distracted states, where shining qualities or extraordinary exertions lead rapidly to eminence. It frequently happens in the former case, that the sovereign, having made once his choice, waves for the most part the exercise of his own authority; leading a life of indolence, or indulging in sensual gratifications; but the Emperor of China still continued in an indefatigable attention to the administration of public affairs; having rather divided with his Vizier, than conferred upon him, the whole care of his vast empire.
