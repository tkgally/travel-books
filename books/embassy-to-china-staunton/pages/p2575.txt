The few lapidaries who cut diamonds at Canton, used for that purpose adamantine spar, which being mixed in small proportions with grey granite, the mass was imagined to contain nothing else, and excited a doubt, whether it could be real diamond, which pure granite could affect. The Canton artists are uncommonly expert in imitating European works. They mend, and even make watches, copy paintings, and colour drawings, with great success. They supply strangers with coarse silk stockings, knit or woven at Canton, tho none of the natives wear such, unless it be some young Chinese, who are privately fond of following the fashions of Europeans. The toys made at Canton, and known under the name of balancers and tumblers, are partly filled, and their equilibrium sustained by quicksilver. That metal is used sometimes in the same complaints where it is applied in Europe, as a specific; but a prejudice prevails among the common Chinese, that its use is apt to destroy the powers of one sex, and to occasion barrenness in the other. The extreme populousness of the country, seems to prove that such a medicine is not frequently administered, or that those effects are attributed to it erroneously.
