[[caption]] Section <i>and</i> Elevation <i>of a</i> Wheel <i>used by the</i> Chinese <i>for raising</i> Water. References. A.B. <i>The two rims or bamboo fellies of the Wheel,</i> A <i>being about one foot or eighteen inches in diameter less than</i> B. C. <i>The Nave or Axis. Near the two extremities of this axis are inserted sixteen or eighteen spokes of bamboo,</i> D <i>which cross each other at</i> F. <i>where they are bound together and strengthened by a ring of split & twisted bamboo</i> G <i>concentric with the rims or circumference of the Wheel. The spokes are continued from hence to the two peripheries of the wheel to which they are firmly bound with a strong cordage made of split bamboo.</i> References. <i>Between the spokes is woven the basket work</i> H. <i>which is made of the same material, and serves for the floats or ladle-boards of the wheel.</i> L. <i>Scoops or tubes of bamboo, fixed to the rims of the wheel, one end of which</i> M. <i>is open: These scoops are fixed at a certain angle with the axis that the water may be retained in them till it ascends to the vertex of the wheel.</i> O. <i>A long trough supported between the upright posts</i> R <i>from this proceed two tubes</i> P. <i>to convey the water from thence to the reservoir on the bank.</i> S. <i>Posts to support the transoms on which the wheel turns.</i>
