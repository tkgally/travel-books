But by the intelligence the Embassador received at this time, through an unquestionable channel, it appeared that their ancient policy, of endeavouring to exclude all other foreigners from China, still continued in its full force; and his Excellency had, at least, the early advantage of being thus apprized, that he was to depend chiefly upon the impression which his own conduct and that of his suite would make upon the Chinese, for removing any prejudices against the nation he went to represent, as well as for overcoming the difficulties that might be thrown in his way, during his residence in the country.

Soon after the return of the brigs from Macao, on the twenty-third of June the squadron weighed anchor from Chook-tchoo, and proceeded with a favourable gale towards the straits which divide the continent of China from the great island of Formosa, as it is generally termed by Europeans, but which among the natives, as well as the Chinese, is called Tai-wan. The same day the squadron passed between two small islands, one called Asses' Ears, from its forked aspect, the other termed Lema, both of them barren and uncultivated, and surrounded by large rocks, appearing above the surface of the sea.
