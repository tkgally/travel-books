Before he vomited the last, he felt a sensation as if his stomach were twisting together, and which motion, he supposed, produced the hemorrhage. Had the blood proceeded from the lungs, he judged, it would have been spumous, or mixed with air bubbles. He felt constantly a nausea in his mouth; his salivary glands swelled, and the saliva became thickened and vitiated. His mind grew indifferent to all things, either past or future, and even to his existence. Regret and hope were equally extinct within his breast. His head felt light and sore, and as if its sutures were separated from each other. It likewise ached; and he had alternate sensations of violent heat, and chilling cold.
