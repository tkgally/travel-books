I am a Frenchman and a doctor and I understand women. I tell you the priests rule the women and through them the family, and therefore they have the upper hand in Spain.”

Having delivered himself of these pithy remarks he changed the subject and asked how we liked the Spaniards. When we replied, “In general very much,” he assented, and looking at his assistant said, “A fair sort of people, not clever like the French but good-natured and worthy, is it not so, <i>amigo?</i>” Looking a trifle more crestfallen, if possible, than before, <i>amigo</i> replied that it was.
