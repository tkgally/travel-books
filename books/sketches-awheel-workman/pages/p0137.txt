Had wood been used in place of stucco, and glass where it should not be, and the mystic softness of age-tempered walls covered with a tapestry of paint and mosaic garish as a modern rug, as in the Alcazar of Seville, then might the traveller forbear to go to Granada.

It is said the Moors liked bright colours, and perhaps they did, but should by any chance the shade of Yusuf I. find itself in the Sevillian palace to-day, it would doubtless flee at once to the more subdued and æsthetic halls of the Alhambra.
