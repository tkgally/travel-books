On our departure the sacristan received the mantilla together with a <i>propina</i> with evident satisfaction. Probably this custom, which we observed nowhere else in Spain, will be strictly adhered to in the future, at least by that sacristan.

Poets have sung of “Valencia del Cid” and its <i>huerta</i>, but they might find a more appropriate subject or at least a more poetic one in Jativa and its surroundings. Here one finds the Arcadian plain of the Moor with its miles of orange, olive, ilex, and carouba trees surmounted not by a half-modern Spanish city only, but by a grand castle of quaintest Moorish outline.
