# Chapter XIV.

[[subtitle]] The Fonda of Tarancon—An Autocrat of the Dinner Table.

THE road from Toledo to Aranjuez was merely a track in the soft sand, entirely unfit for any wheeled vehicle. As it ran near the railway we decided to put into execution if possible a plan we had once or twice previously thought of, viz., riding on the side of the roadbed of the latter. Such an idea would not have been entertained for a moment in any Continental country except Spain, but here where individual freedom, or perhaps one might say licence, more nearly approaches that in the United States, it seemed worth while to make the attempt.
