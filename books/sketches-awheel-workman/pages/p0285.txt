Without the walls stand the beautiful churches of San Pedro and San Vicente strong rivals of the cathedral, and one wonders that so much of art should have been placed without the walls at a time these were necessary for its protection. The entrances of several of the churches are very striking. The finest of these is the western portal of San Vicente, which is a marvel of elaborate yet tasteful transitional work quite surpassing any of the entrances of the beautiful cathedral.

Like Burgos, Avila is rich in splendid tombs. The cathedral has a number mostly by unknown artists of the thirteenth century, which are finely wrought. One of the best is that of the Bishop of Segundo in a small church outside the city.
