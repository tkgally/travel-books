But best of all was the sauce of good-will that garnished all that was set before us. We found the Andalucians of all classes more courteous and hospitable than those of the eastern coast or of the north, and while in every part we received much kindness, the Andalucians appeared to possess to a special degree a spirit of cordiality and friendliness.

On a cool day the last of May we entered Seville, and as we were unacquainted with the intricate streets, the escort of two policemen, who kindly offered to take us to our hotel, was accepted. From the moment one enters Seville a different impression is made on the mind from that made by any other Spanish city.
