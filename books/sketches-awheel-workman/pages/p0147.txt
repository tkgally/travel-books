We asked a road repairer if the road down was good. Looking doubtfully at our wheels he answered, “Yes, it is good, but <i>muy pendiente.</i>” We had ten miles to ride and only an hour of twilight as we started on the descent, which proved to be very sharp in places. We were obliged to ride with caution, dismounting several times as the road wound around curves dangerously near the edge of precipices. The plain was reached in safety as darkness was closing in.

Malaga is mentioned as a desirable winter resort for invalids. While the climate is doubtless favourable, he must be a strong-nerved invalid who can endure the pandemonium of indescribable noises that renders night hideous in this city.
