When it did appear, it had been relieved of a new revolver, which one of the party had left in it, perhaps with a feminine desire to avoid carrying a weapon till really on the road in Spain. A new one was easily procured and we set out on a lovely April morning for one of the wonders of Spain, the pride of Catalonia, Monserrat.
