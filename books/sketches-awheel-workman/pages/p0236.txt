These exquisite church tapestries, in which crimson, mauve, and <i>azulejos</i> blue are blended, as only the softening effects of time can blend, are exposed to the public gaze during the forenoon of Corpus Christi. Some are in a state of perfect preservation, others, among them many of the finest, representing the tutelar San Ildefonso or gallant <i>campeadores,</i> have been rent and worn and are carefully darned. Following the narrow streets that surround the church on three sides we made the circuit of this extraordinary out-of-door gallery.

Within it the unexpressed thoughts of a thousand kneeling figures were finding utterance in a flood of orchestral and organ melody. How the music and environment of the Spanish cathedral spoil one for oratorio music as ordinarily rendered! In these temples the soul of music as well as of mysticism has its home.
