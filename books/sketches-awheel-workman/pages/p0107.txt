Owing to a number of mountain passes to be crossed, we were not able to reach Alicante in one day from Jativa, but had to put up for the night at the most out-of-the-way little place imaginable. The route lay through a desolate mountain region. The only town of any importance, Alcoy, was reached at noon when it was too early to stop off. Had we wished to stay there we could not have done so, as no room was to be had at the <i>posada.</i> The whole place was in an uproar with side shows and merry-go-rounds in full operation, the people being in that state of ebullition that characterises a Spanish country <i>fête.</i> As was usual when we arrived on the scene on such occasions, they worked off their superfluous spirits on us, pressing around to see us and our wheels and accompanying us until we left the town, which we speedily did, trusting to luck and some mountain <i>venta</i> for luncheon.
