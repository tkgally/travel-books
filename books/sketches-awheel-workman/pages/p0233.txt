Long before this the newspapers, having got wind of our presence in Spain and of our intended trip through the country, had been heralding our movements and arrival at different cities, garnishing the notices with various bits of information suggested by the imaginations of their editors regarding us and our bicycles, the most common of which were that we were a “matrimonio ingles” or English married pair from London, and that we were mounted on wonderfully fine bicycles. Hence the remark of the <i>patron.</i>[[fnref 1]]

The guide-books state that Toledo has no <i>fonda</i> fit to stay in, and as a consequence this city is usually visited in one day from Madrid, travellers in Spain as elsewhere having a great horror of inferior accommodations.
