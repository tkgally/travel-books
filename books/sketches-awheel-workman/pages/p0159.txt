# Chapter X.

[[subtitle]] Tetuan, a White City of Morocco, Home of the Descendants of the Exiles of Granada.

ON a breezy day about the middle of May we sailed from Algeciras to Tangier, not so much to see the latter city, which, since it has become the tourist show city of Morocco, has lost the charm of originality and has become a paradise for guides, interpreters, and hotel porters, in this respect rivalling Ronda the show picturesque city of Spain, but more especially to make the trip to Tetuan, which like Fez offers an accurate picture of Oriental life.

Sitting near us was one of the wise-acres generally to be found on coasting steamers, who was instructing his neighbours about Tangier. We asked him if he knew Tetuan.
