# Chapter IX.

[[subtitle]] Over the Malaga Sierras to the Queen of the Shimmering Sea—Ronda, a Jaunt to the Cueva del Gato and the Zumidero.

THE approach to Granada is guarded on every side by chains of sierras and one must be prepared to grapple with steep grades in passing these. Inquiry about the road to Malaga only elicited the information that it was “muy pendiente” (very steep). South Spaniards are apt to call mountain grades <i>muy pendiente</i>, and they are often correct. In Spain as well as in Algeria we found it difficult to obtain any accurate idea of the character of a given road even from those who had been over it.
