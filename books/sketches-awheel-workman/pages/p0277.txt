It was the last week in June, but La Granja was as dead as in winter. The <i>patron</i> of the hotel, who was in bed when we arrived at two in the afternoon, told us at dinner they were only beginning to put the hotel in order for guests, the season not opening till the fifteenth of July. The palace of San Ildefonso is occupied for two months in summer by the infantas and a part of the court, but the Queen Regent seldom goes there, preferring the sea and her palace at San Sebastian. The Rambouillet palace, filled with comfortable, cheerful rooms, is without interest, as would be the gardens with fountains laid out in French style were it not for the peculiar contrast they form with the wild barren mountains overhanging them.
