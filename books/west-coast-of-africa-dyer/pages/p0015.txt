# Chapter I.

ON the 25th of September, 1871, I left Southampton in the Cape Mail Steamer <i>Cambrian,</i> to take command of H.M.S. <i>Torch</i> on the West African Station. Our voyage was one of incident and interest, and far from being altogether unpleasant, although few longer ones are on record. Mr. Boyle, in his book “<i>To the Cape for Diamonds,</i>” has given an account of it, which I will not repeat here; sufficient to say that we did, eventually, on the 8th November, of the same year, reach Table Bay!
