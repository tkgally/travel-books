In front and rear of the huts were slight railings, attached to which in leather collars, and tied by long strips of leather, were rows of large white dogs for winter sleighing. The sleighs, of a very light construction, were outside each hut, and, together with the bows and arrows, were protected from the weather by the projecting roofs.

These people had communication with either the Manchu Tartars or Russian settlements on the Amùr, as they possessed iron points for their arrows, hunting-knives, spear-heads, and strike-alights apparently of tolerable fabrication: and a few iron chains were on the dog collars.
