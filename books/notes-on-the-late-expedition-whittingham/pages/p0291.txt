Of the utility of the fort in its present state much cannot be said: it certainly keeps the sea-breeze out,—a friendly visitant who should, if possible, be admitted to the homes of the Europeans, whose pale cheeks and nerveless motions speak of the slow and steady effects of a residence in a climate generally thought favourably of.

Just before it became dark, we threaded the narrow, short, and intricate passage to sea, and with almost the certainty of a chronometrical arrangement, we were borne along ten or eleven knots an hour for two days. Hot tropical weather made the early morning stroll on deck most agreeable, and we were commencing our walk before sunrise, when a Lascar, sent up to the maintop to repair something, hailed that “a man was overboard.”
