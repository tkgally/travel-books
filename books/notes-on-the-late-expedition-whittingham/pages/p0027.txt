The pleasure of leaving the tropics, the delight of being soon employed on active service, and the certain distinction to be gained under their popular commander, was visible in the radiant countenances and zealous activity of the officers and men of the Sibylle,—every omen seemed auspicious, and, with a fresh breeze, the frigate and her consorts soon made a recall impossible.

The winter was nearly over, and what a dry, equable, and temperate winter it is! comparable to the same season in Egypt or Malaga. In six months we had rain only on six days, and that in partial non-tropical showers: now every day the sudden change of temperature caused by the conquest of the south-west monsoon over the strong breezes from the North was expected; but we seemed for some days to be on the neutral ground, and lay nearly becalmed, close to the bold romantic shores of the island of Formosa—the Beautiful—so well named by the Portuguese.
