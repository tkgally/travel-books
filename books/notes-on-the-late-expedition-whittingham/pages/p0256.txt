He spoke English perfectly, and gave us an intimation that the Dutch negotiations were drawing to a close, and that there was less caution exercised by the provincial authorities to cut off intercourse between the Dutch and natives, as the former now landed and walked about the country, easily shaking off the police escorts ordered to accompany their parties. He also inquired with eagerness about news from Europe, as he had been many weeks already in the harbour, and asked how the siege of Sevastopol progressed. His stay on board was brief; and as we passed his vessel the colours were once more dipped, her marines presented arms, and her officers uncovered their heads. The first compliment was returned as quickly as possible,—to my apprehension a less noisy, simpler, and more flattering compliment than the discharge of powder from the muzzles of a dozen guns, and perhaps more worthy of civilised nations than the present custom.
