We landed, and saw a large storehouse, and a pier belonging to the Russian Fur Company, as two Americans living in it informed us. We strolled through the small settlement, consisting of six separate wooden houses in a row, with a small Greek church at the end of the village; and sentiments of pity for the women and children who had been driven by fear from this rude settlement into the adjoining forests were the predominant feelings of all. The disposition and the judgment of the Commodore, I presume, alike led him to promise the strictest adherence to the proclamation inviting the return of the inhabitants, issued by the commander of the first squadron which visited the port: the houses unfortunately had in the interim been ruthlessly rifled by the crews of two whale ships, the names of which are known; and I trust the honour of an adventurous and daring profession will lead those engaged in it to take the proper steps
