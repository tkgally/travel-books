The coaling of the steamer was completed in about twelve hours, and we then resumed our voyage westwards. After a favourable passage of four days and a few hours, we saw the light on Europa Point, and were shortly again at anchor. We were enabled to get ashore for two or three hours the following morning, to ramble over the Rock, whose summit was shrouded in the heavy dark mists with which a Levanter invariably enwraps it.
