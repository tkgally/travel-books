On some topics their flow of conversation was most agreeable, and in none more so than when its current ran through the details of Russian private life, or touched the noble patriotism which the mention of the name of their late Czar immediately raised. Indeed, the enthusiastic admiration felt for Nicholas I. was for the magnificent impersonation of Russia’s might and majesty. “L’état c’est moi,” with him, was no boast, and still, amongst the younger well-born officers, his name is mentioned with reverential respect.
