The bulletin announcing the Emperor Nicholas’ death had been received at Aian in forty-eight days: the quickest expresses, during good travelling weather, which includes the whole year with the exception of two months before the short summer and a month at its termination, generally require about fifty days. Ordinarily the mail leaves St. Petersburg for Irkutsk twice a week; between the latter and Yakutsk the communication is by the Lena twice a month; and from thence, once a month, the forests and swamps extending for 800 miles, between Yakutsk and Aian, are crossed on horseback.
