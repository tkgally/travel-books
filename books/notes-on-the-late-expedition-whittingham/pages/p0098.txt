We climbed up the notched log that served as a staircase, and entered one of the least ruinous of the huts, and found fishing and household implements, nets, baits, traps, baskets of birch bark, wooden bowls, dishes and spoons, bows and arrows, a large iron nail let into walrus bone, and two European knife blades, tied to hard wood by strips of hide. We afterwards glanced at two or three of the other houses which looked most habitable, and saw the same signs of late occupation. Dogs and sleighs were searched for, and we found something like a portion of a sleigh, and the skull of a dog.
