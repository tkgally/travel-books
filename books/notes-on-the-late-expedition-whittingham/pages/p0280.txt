This establishment is young, but promises to afford the facilities which a depôt at such a distance should be capable of offering. The buildings and wharves are substantial and well built, and are good evidence of the creditable manner in which this useful and flourishing company is served at remote stations.

A drive across a morass along good roads and through the suburbs gave me an insight into the different races living in the settlements.

The industrious trades are in the hands of the Chinese, of whom a very large proportion of the population consists: they are found most useful settlers, and great numbers are constantly arriving, as the richer Chinese are permitted to acquire large portions of the interior of the island, and to plant colonies of their countrymen on them, which are brought often from the interior of the south-west part of China.
