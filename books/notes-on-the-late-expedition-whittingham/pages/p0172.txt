The soil immediately near the numerous streams appears good: want of summer and sun will prevent any crops ripening until a general colonisation of these countries has improved the climate, and even then I fear there would be a difficulty in raising sufficient food. Wild rhubarb grows in large quantities, and we relished rhubarb tarts exceedingly, as vegetables and fruit were scarce commodities; whilst for the men, who were beginning to feel the effects of the unusually long cruise, its efficacy as an antiscorbutic was valuable indeed.
