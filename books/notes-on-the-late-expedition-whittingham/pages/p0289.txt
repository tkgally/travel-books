[[cont]]growing difficulty in the government of Ceylon would also seem to point to the obvious advantages of a larger legislative assembly. In the successful assertion of the claims of party there is more chance of benefit to a colony than in gratifying the ambition of the few individuals who form the council; and the tone of the local press and of public opinion would be improved by the more thorough discussion of colonial interests by a large number of representatives, if many years’ experience in different colonies teaches me aright.
