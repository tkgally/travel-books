At the head of the third flight of stone stairs <i>à la Maltaise</i> there was a small square, upon one side of which the verandah of a large single-storied house opened: this small square was also lined by troops, and passing them on either hand, we ascended the wooden steps of the verandah: some apparently higher officials met us as we entered, and heralded us along dark matted passages into a small low chamber, divided from its neighbouring compartments by high wooden partitions: at the back of this apartment chairs were ranged, on which we were invited to sit.
