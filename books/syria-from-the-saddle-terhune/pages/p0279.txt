A mound with a collection of stones and broken pottery under the surface is all that remains of ancient Jericho. Near the mound is a stone-lined pool, called the Fountain of Elisha. This and a hut or two in the neighborhood are the only signs of human workmanship in what was once the greatest city in all the plain.
