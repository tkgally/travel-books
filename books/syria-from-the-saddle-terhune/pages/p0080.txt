My own attire was far less brilliant and becoming. I had adopted the silk kafieh, which I have found for all weathers the easiest, most comfortable headdress imaginable, a flannel hunting-shirt, riding-trousers, and high boots. To these I had added, by David’s advice, a pistol belt. Fully equipped, I looked like something
