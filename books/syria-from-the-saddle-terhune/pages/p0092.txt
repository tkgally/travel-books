Here in the East, the sight of the old flag was like a message from home. I have heard other Americans speak of this, and I believe the reason we love to see our flag in far lands is not because the sight stirs up our patriotism or our pride in our country, as a country, but because it brings to us thoughts of home, home and nation being two decidedly different things.
