It will readily be observed that in some parts of the volume considerable space is occupied with accounts of certain Missions of the Methodist Church, especially among the Indians of the Upper Saskatchewan region. As the main object of my tour was to inspect these missions, and to prospect with reference to the religious needs of the country, extended reference to Mission work was unavoidable; but it is believed that this kind of information will not be unacceptable to those who have the best interests of the country at heart.
