[[cont]]that the country is “timbered” in the sense in which we would use that term in Ontario or Quebec, but timber in sufficient quantities for building and fencing can easily be obtained, while a good portable saw-mill could find ample employment, and, I doubt not, a ready market for its lumber, for many a year to come. In the course of our ride we passed a number of small fields which have been enclosed and cultivated by the Indians. Some of these are clean and well kept, and give promise of a good harvest in due time. Several cabins have also been erected, one being two stories high and neatly built. I am persuaded that if these Stoneys receive reasonable instruction and encouragement they will soon be able to provide for their own wants, irrespective of what they may obtain by hunting.
