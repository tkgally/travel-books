[[cont]]Company’s post, which we reached before one o’clock, and pitched our tent in a grove of poplars, a few hundred yards from the Fort. During the afternoon, Mr. McKay, the officer in charge, sent out to the “guard” for a band of horses, from which we selected two that we thought would suit our purpose; but this consumed most of the afternoon, and we resolved to remain in camp till Monday. Heavy showers fell at intervals; the wind increased to a gale; and it became so cold that we expected a fall of snow before morning, During the night the wind at times approached a hurricane in violence; but as our tent had been pitched in the timber, we were sheltered from the worst of the storm.
