# Prefatory.

MANY eyes, from many lands, are looking toward the prairies of the great North-West. That this grand Territory is destined to be the home of prosperous millions in the near future, is now all but universally conceded, and hence every item of reliable information in regard to its capabilities and resources is eagerly welcomed. The causes of this wide-spread interest are various.
