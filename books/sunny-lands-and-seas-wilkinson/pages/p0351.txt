A few minutes afterwards we stop at Inspiration Point. We know it is Inspiration Point, for our considerate American cousins have had it painted up for us on a board, in large letters. From here we can see nearly the entire valley. It would seem more correct to call it a chasm; for masses of nearly white granite, nearly 4,000 feet, tower above it almost precipitously on all sides. It is only six miles long, and from half a mile to a mile in width; but it looks much less than this, owing to the precipices which hem it in on all sides.
