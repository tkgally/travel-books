[[right]] Hongkong,

[[right]] March 1, 1882.

W<sc>e</sc> arrived here on the 24th of February at 8 a.m., after a most miserable, cold and stormy voyage of over three days, which is officially reported as follows: ‘The British steamer <i>Ceylon</i> reports encountered heavy N.E. monsoon, with high sea throughout.’ This sea is said to be nearly the worst sea in the world, and the voyage from Manila one of the most trying one can undergo.
