Now another awful smell meets us, which makes us hold our breath to suffocation-point. They are so frequent, that it makes one feel quite faint. What must they be during the hotter weather? and yet they say there is no fever! Beggars there are many of in these crowded ways, and they are importunate beyond all conception; there are also many lepers. Here comes an old Chinaman with the hugest imaginable spectacles, not with invisible rims, but good wholesome stout ones of tortoise-shell, half an inch wide; these soon become so common as to cease to create wonder.
