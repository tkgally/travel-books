The weather each day is perfectly glorious, and I have not been bitten once since I left England by mosquito or any other living thing. This is rather extraordinary, for I am awfully punished by gnats and midges at home.

These natives seem to do nearly everything out of doors. We see them in the early morning sitting on their heels ‘brushing’ their teeth with their bare fingers, and some of them cooking.
