# Chapter XVII.

[[right]] Honolulu, April 12th, 1882.

[[right]] 10 hr. 32 min. behind our home time.

D<sc>isappointment</sc> and disgust are weak words with which to express our feelings after having first <i>read</i> of and then having <i>seen</i> this overrated and priest-ridden spot. After fifteen days at sea, one can easily understand that almost any land would be welcomed with delight, and any beauties that it might possess would be magnified—perhaps a little pardonably.
