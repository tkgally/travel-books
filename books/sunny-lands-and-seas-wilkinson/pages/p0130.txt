The day ended happily for man and beast, and nothing was shot this day bigger than a linnet, which, sitting on a bough fifteen yards away, fell to the deadly accuracy and long range of the choke-bore. This was the bag, and though small, was better than having it filled, <i>and a little to spare,</i> at one shot, as one of Dickens’ great sporting characters came so near accomplishing.

[[right]] Feb. 7, 1882.

We got in yesterday at 3 p.m., the approach to the harbour being through clusters of low-lying islands covered with all the tropical vegetation we had seen at Penang; the trees not only growing down to the water’s edge, but actually growing out of the water for a long distance from the shore; these are the celebrated Mangrove Swamps. Before being anchored at the wharf, three miles from the town, we had the home letters brought us by a man in the pilot boat, and introduced by a friend as, ‘Here’s a policeman wants you.’ The fright was soon got over as we saw the letters, which were the only registered ones sent on board, the bag for the <i>Ceylon</i> arriving soon after.
