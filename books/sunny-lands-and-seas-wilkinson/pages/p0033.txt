[[cont]]* * * *, and about the same distance apart as our Great Bear stars are from one another. A word about our ship. She belonged formerly to the P. and O. Company, but was sold by them on account of her not being able to carry freight, having been constructed only for the accommodation of passengers. She is a splendid sea boat; is very strongly built; has high bulwarks like an old frigate, and is barque-rigged.

[[break]]

We both intend travelling across India instead of going round with the ship. We shall miss seeing Ceylon, but then we shall have more than three weeks in India, and shall see Jeypore, Delhi, Agra, Lucknow and Benares, and try to get up to Darjeeling, from which we shall see the Himalayas and peaks over 29,000 feet high, the highest land in the world.
