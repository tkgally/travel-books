## IV.

Arriving as we did in Canton at the break of day, we had the best of chances to witness the religious devotions of the countless river swarm, consisting in the discharge of fire-crackers from each separate boat, to scare away the devils. Never before had we seen on so impressive a scale the practical application of the maxim, “Fight the devil with fire!” and the spectacle inevitably led to certain profound speculations on the relation between business and religion. To supply the needful missals and breviaries for the morning devotions of such millions, the manufacture of fire-crackers in China must be on an absolutely colossal scale. Imagination refuses to grasp the numbers of powder and paper mills thus literally “rooted and grounded in the faith.” Should foreign missionaries convert the millions of their customers to a creed prescribing a less noisy and more inward form of morning worship, total financial ruin would at once stare no end of manufacturers and workmen in the face.
