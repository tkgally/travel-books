In contrast with this, beings there are whose whole response to the impact of love or sorrow seems but as the petty click of a spoon on the rim of a teacup. Lingering resonance is there none. The momentary click, and all is by; while these deep ones, like the great bells, at each touch of renewing memory hum and reverberate in every spiritual molecule. Devout idolater does one become, as he breathes the prayer, “Oh! for the capacity of long, rich reverberation like thine! Without it, how shallow, how fleeting all human experience.” So at least is it murmured in the ear by the deep-hearted bells of Japan.
