[[cont]]“Tribune” that Jay Gould, weary of the long fret of the finite involved in wrecking railroads, had thrown up his millions and started out, a naked mendicant, to devote what remained of his life to absorption in the Absolute. Bloomingdale Asylum! would have been the exclamation on every lip. And yet, in any higher sense of the word, which of the two were the saner seems hardly open to question,—certainly it would not be in India.
