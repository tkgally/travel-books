Yet once this overwhelming temple stood in direct connection by a broad sphinx-lined avenue of more than a mile with the vast Luxor temple, while equally over across the Nile, past the colossal Memnon statues, and on and on to the temple of Kurnah, the Ramesseum, and Medinet Habu, great sphinx-lined avenues brought it into like connection with these stupendous structures. Now first one begins to feel Thebes in its day of glory. All other ruins seem the ruins of little children at their child-play sport of building sand castles on the beach.
