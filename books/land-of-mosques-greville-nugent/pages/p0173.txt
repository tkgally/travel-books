The great sheikhs, whose word is to the tribes still law, camping out at starlight in the silent desert beyond Biskra, with their flocks and falcons, may sometimes look at the old swords, sometimes think on their grandfathers slain by the battalions of Louis Philippe, and some day perhaps will rise up and say, “Do not Allah and His Prophet bid us revenge the wrongs which His people have suffered?” It may be long in coming, but—<i>ces choses là ne s’oublient pas!</i>
