But to-day all is smiling. The shops in the Bab-el-Oued and the Bab-Azoun are smartly decked; the mandoline players are tinkling away amongst the little outdoor tables of the Café Apollon; the golden date-sticks quiver on the palm trees; the flower stalls beneath them give forth sweetness; the toilettes are ravishing; and the square looks as gay as a little bit of Nice.
