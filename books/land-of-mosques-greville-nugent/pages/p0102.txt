At last, getting rather “riled” at the persistent contrariness of fate, I insisted on being rowed to shore, and sent Mahommed up to the Pêcheries to buy bait, after which we essayed our luck, only to be baffled once more. For whereas on other occasions the boat had been quickly peopled by the delicious little fish of the bay (name unknown, but peace to their bones, for I have eaten many a good dishful of them!), on this day not one could we hook. Eventually we returned to shore, very much disgusted with our chapter of accidents; and as I landed, I said, “Īa Mahommed, thou seest that Friday is not a fortunate day for the Christians.” He cordially assented, seeming duly impressed with the fact that the Christ must have been a very great Marabout,[[fnref *]] and one thing is certain, namely, that he never suggested to us to go for a trip with him on a Friday again.

And whose is this little voice at my feet crying “Madame, donne un sou”? Ah, is it you, my little Fatma, my child-friend? and did you think I had forgotten you?
