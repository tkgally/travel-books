He was a holy man, and renowned in the working of miracles; and may Allah grant us participation in the favours vouchsafed to him, and place us with him in the Day of the Resurrection. Amin!

Now and again you may see a water-carrier bearing on his back a wild-boar’s skin, full to bursting of the precious fluid, and the bristles, wetted by escaping drops, glisten in the sunshine. A sorcerer sits in his narrow den who would be delighted to show one a little serpent-charming, only it is Ramadan time (an excuse which is very useful when they want to get out of doing anything), but who will tattoo you artistically—should you desire it—for a trifling consideration. But we must now retrace our steps to the Jewish quarter, for we have been invited to witness a Hebrew wedding, which, from the peculiar gorgeousness of the Tunisian Jewesses’ costumes, is a most important sight; and it is time to start for the synagogue.
