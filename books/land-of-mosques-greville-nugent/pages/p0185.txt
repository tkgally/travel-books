Before hailing a boat to take us back to <i>déjeuner</i> on the ship we paused before a French café on the quay to take an <i>apéritif</i>, which was served by a clean, smiling, white-aproned waiter. What a wonderful animal is your French <i>garçon de café!</i> Here he is, exiled with a mere handful of his compatriots in an African town, far from home and friends, and yet at ten a.m. he will don his dress-coat and white tablier, and smilingly range his tables and chairs on the pavement, and serve your <i>vermout de Turin</i> as briskly as though he were at home in his beloved Paris. Our change was tendered here in the pretty little Tunisian coins, whereby we woke to the fact that we had that morning for the first time entered the dominions of his Highness the Bey of Tunis.
