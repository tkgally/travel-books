# Chapter V.

[[subtitle]] Japan pleading with England to be reconciled to God.

[[subtitle]] A S<sc>ermon preached by a</sc> J<sc>apanese</sc> S<sc>tudent at</sc> C<sc>ambridge.</sc>

<blockquote>
“At that time ye were without Christ.”—Eph. ii. 12.
“Look unto me, and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth.”—Isa. xlv. 22.
</blockquote>

I CANNOT find words to express what my heart feels, on this, to me, most solemn, as well as very pleasing occasion, when, for the first time in my life, I am permitted to stand up before you, my fellow Christians, to preach Christ, to testify what he has done for the soul of a poor and helpless sinner, who, for these many years, was walking according to the course of this world, without the Saviour, without the Comforter, without the Life, without the Lord our God to serve; in a word, without Him who is all in all to us sinners; even Jesus Christ.
