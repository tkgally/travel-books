[[cont]]I could take that part, but not with brethren.[[fnref *]] But in preaching the gospel, or the study of the Word with others, I can say, “<i>My God helpeth me.</i>”

[[break]]

There is great cry for union, and I long and pant for <i>true</i> unity; but experience has taught me that true unity is not brought about until each believer dares to be first a unit—alone before God. Much of what is accepted as unity is only kept together by what “Anna Shipton” calls “<i>the honey of Nature</i>”; and we know well, though Nature’s honey is sweet and adhesive, that it cannot stand the storm and rain; and much of what keeps Christians together is some attraction or advantage of N<sc>ature</sc>.
