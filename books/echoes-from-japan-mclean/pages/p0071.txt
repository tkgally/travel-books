[[cont]]self-denial. Christ gave Himself for us. What would you give to Him? Oh, be steadfast and immovable in your faith; forget not for a moment, what a grand and glorious thing it is to be called the children of God. “At that time ye were without Christ.” You cannot do without Christ. Stand for Him, live for Him, work for Him, suffer for Him, and if need be, die for Him. Never forsake Him.

God forbid that it should be said of a single soul among you assembled here this evening, “At that time ye were without Christ.”

“Look unto Me, and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth.” So says our Christ. Oh, be you saved, be saved, by all means!

Now to God the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, the God of Holiness and Love, the God of the Bible, be honour and glory, for ever and ever. Amen.
