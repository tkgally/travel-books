## Extracts.

FLASHES OF THOUGHT; being one Thousand Choice Extracts from the Works of C. H. S<sc>purgeon</sc>. Alphabetically arranged, and with a copious Index. Price 5s.

“A thousand extracts, bright with the light of heaven, sparkling with wit, rich in imagery, beautiful in their setting, forcible in style, and devoutly stimulating in tone, make up a volume of unique merit.”—<i>General Baptist Magazine.</i>

SPURGEON’S GEMS: being Brilliant Passages Selected from the Discourses of C. H. S<sc>purgeon</sc>. Large Type, 4s.

GLEANINGS AMONG THE SHEAVES. By C. H. S<sc>purgeon</sc>. Cloth, price 1s.

“These extracts are quite Spurgeonic—racy, rich, and rare, both as to style and matter—full of exquisite consolation—faithful advice—clear analogies—poetic touches—and glorious old gospel.”—<i>Weekly Review.</i>

SPURGEON’S BIRTHDAY BOOK. Cloth, 2s. 6d., Calf or Morocco, 5s., Russia, with Photograph, 10s. 6d.

“A metaphor, simile, allegory, or illustration for every day in the year, compiled from the works of C. H. S<sc>purgeon</sc>. For thirty pence our readers may possess a book which is as useful as it is handsomely got up.”—<i>Christian Age.</i>

## Devotional.

MORNING BY MORNING; or, Daily Readings for the Family or the Closet. By C. H. S<sc>purgeon</sc>. 3s. 6d. Morocco, 7s. 6d.

“Those who have learned the value of morning devotion, will highly prize these helps. All who love a full-orbed gospel, vigorous, varied thought, and a racy style, will appreciate this volume.”—<i>Rev. J. Angus, D.D.</i>

EVENING BY EVENING; or, Readings at Eventide for the Family or the Closet. By C. H. S<sc>purgeon</sc>. 3s. 6d. Morocco, 7s. 6d.

“On learning that ‘Evening by Evening’ was published, how gladly I bade it welcome! And I can humbly commend it in no higher terms than by simply saying that it will be found a fit companion, every way, for its forerunner of the morning.”—<i>Charles J. Brown, D.D., Edinburgh.</i>

THE CHEQUE BOOK OF THE BANK OF FAITH; Being Precious Promises arranged for Daily Use; with Brief Experimental Comments. 3s. 6d. Persian Morocco, red and gilt edges, 7s. 6d.

“‘The Cheque Book of the Bank of Faith’ is suitable for family use, or for solitary prayer. It consists of a series of texts from the Bible, both from the Old and New Testaments, with a short commentary upon them. There is one of these texts for each day in the year. Each of them contains a promise of God, which, as the writer remarks, may be compared to a cheque payable to order, to be endorsed by faith, and presented in due time. There is much that is comforting in the volume, which will, doubtless, be largely used in households.”

## For Students.

LECTURES TO MY STUDENTS; A Selection from Addresses delivered to the Students of the Pastors’ College, Metropolitan Tabernacle. By C. H. S<sc>purgeon</sc>, President. First and Second Series. Price 2s. 6d. each.

“We have read this work with a feeling very nearly approaching to delight. Nothing that Mr. Spurgeon has printed has so thoroughly pleased us, and few of his works are calculated to be of greater practical service. It abounds in words of wisdom; it is rich in humour, but richer in human and spiritual experience.”—<i>Nonconformist</i>
