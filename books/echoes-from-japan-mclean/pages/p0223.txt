## In Harmony

with what Mr. Eugene Stock says in his excellent remarks on Christian educators (in the <i>Christian</i>, of May 24, 1889), I should like to say that I met, and now hear from, lay workers in Japan, who went there with no other object than that they should bring the Japanese to the feet of Jesus. A gentleman from Edinburgh, who arrived in Japan in 1880, has been engaged as a teacher of English in different parts of the country; and directly, and indirectly, he planted the Word of God as the source of that knowledge so all-important in all lands—even the knowledge of the living and true God and Jesus Christ, whom He hath sent. This young Christian teacher reached Japan with absolutely nothing but the living God and His unfailing promise to lean on. And surely this was enough! “It is enough for the servant that he be as his Lord.”
