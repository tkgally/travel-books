In a short time, a young Samurai named Hirano, came to me privately and said, “I have found Jesus. My heart is changed. I am filled with joy. It is wonderful, so unlike anything I have heretofore experienced.” His face beamed as he went on thus for an hour, telling me, with tears, of his new-found treasure, and also of the compassion he had for his countrymen. “Once,” said he, “I cared to come to your class to learn English only, but now my desire is to learn the Bible thoroughly, that I may go and teach it to my countrymen.” I was greatly in need of a helper in my work, and he proved most efficient. I at once invited him to come and stay with me. All his spare time was devoted to Bible reading, and it seemed to me to be the dawn of better things for Japan, as I watched him daily poring over its contents.
