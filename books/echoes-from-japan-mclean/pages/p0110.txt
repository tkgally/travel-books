To love one believer more than another, because he belongs to one’s church party, or holds the same views, is un-Christ-like. “We are members <i>one of another</i>,” and are united to Christ our Head.

4. As a home or foreign worker, I believe I ought to enter by every door, which I recognise the hand of God to have thrown open, and carry out the Scriptural principle: “<i>Whatsoever</i> thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might.”

5. Although we entered Japan with clean hands, yet I must say that civilisation, as a <i>pioneer</i> of the Gospel, has rendered that country less the virgin soil for apostolic methods than the world was for the heralds of the Cross in the first century. In short, I can now only say what I have repeatedly uttered, viz.:—That the thirst for learning among the Japanese, is one instrument with which to meet them wisely, and be of service to them, for the great aim and end of establishing the Gospel among them.
