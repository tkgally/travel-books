I need not say that a pair of chopsticks were put at my disposal, so that I had no difficulty in keeping my friends company, although not quite as <i>au fait</i> in the use of them as they were perhaps. ## Ex-Daimio Mizuno at Home.

The son of ex-daimio Mizuno and his Chief Vassal come to me every afternoon for private instruction. Both are as persevering as they are intelligent. They do not get tired of repeating the same lesson for an hour or more, so determined are they to pronounce English correctly. They are quite willing to sit an hour longer than is expected of me for teaching English, in order to read the somewhat easy first chapter of St. John’s Gospel, and they come every Sunday afternoon regularly, to go over the same ground. I feel that the soil of their minds is very sterile, but as was often the case in China, so it is here. The lines come seasonably to my memory:—

<verse>
“<i>Sow, though the rock repel thee,</i>
<i>In its cold and sterile pride.</i>”
</verse>
