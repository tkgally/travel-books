## Share the Blessing.

[[center]] “Keep none of it till the morning.”

“Here is a story from Japan which should do us all good.

“A poor man who is a Christian has to work all day away from home. He has put up the following notice on the door of his house—

“‘<i>I am a Christian, and if any one likes to go in and read my Good Book while I am out they may. The Buddhist Priests need not come here. I do not want them any more.</i>’

“People go into his house and read his Bible.

“Would that <i>we</i> had more of that natural child-like desire to have others share in the blessings which we enjoy that this poor Japanese has!”—<i>Extract from</i> “<i>C. M. S. Gleaner.</i>”
