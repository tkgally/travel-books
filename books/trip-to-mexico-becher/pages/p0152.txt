But a few years since and the burial service of the Protestant Church could not be read over their dead; and woe to that man who, while the <i>Host</i> was being carried past, did not throw himself on his knees before it!

The change has been great indeed, and extraordinary in this, that the bulk of the population, it might be said nearly all who possessed any religion, were and are still Roman Catholics.

And so it is not surprising although all this has come to pass, and although the priest is never seen in the streets or public places in cassock or robe, that the Church has still great influence in the country.

The first blow practically struck at church property fell within a hundred yards of this hotel. The street on which it fronts runs now from the plaza past the alameda without interruption, and is one of the principal thoroughfares of Mexico; but, in the time of Comonfort and before, it did not; the grand church and convent of San Francisco, or rather the masses of building connected with them, blocked the way.
