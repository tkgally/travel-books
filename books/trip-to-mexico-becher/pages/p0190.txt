Jalapa, like Cordoba, is very Spanish. Balconies are numerous, and the latticed iron cage outside the windows as common here as there. Both these, by-the-way, have a good deal to do with Mexican courtships and marriages—in the towns at all events. The various ways and means used in England, Canada, &c., of man’s paying his addresses to woman, the “courting,” “sweet-hearting,” “sparking,” “keeping company,” &c., would not be tolerated. When a gentleman has serious intentions towards a lady, he shows himself in the street near her balcony or window, casting continual and most respectful glances at it; if he can, <i>looking</i> love. This is unheeded for a time, but, often repeated, it generally softens the <i>senorita’s</i> heart, and she appears at balcony or window for a moment; the lover, thus encouraged, comes again and again, each time <i>drawing</i> the lady, who lingers longer and longer, that he may gaze at her, and she see more of him.
