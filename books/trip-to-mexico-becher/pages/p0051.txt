The Aztecs founded the City of Tenochtitlan or Mexico, in the year 1325, inspired in the selection of its site by seeing an eagle on the shore of the Lake Tezcuco perched on a cactus, holding a serpent in his talons, his wings spread to the rising sun. They took this as pointing to where their future capital should be. The legend is preserved in the Arms of Mexico to the present day.

The Aztecs remained, and were the possessors of the country, under the rule of their king, Montezuma, when Cortes invaded it in 1519; their government ceased with his conquest, but they have ever since occupied the country, and are now, and have been since its separation from Spain, the governing race.
