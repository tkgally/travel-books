<verse>
“These mountains piercing to the sky
With their eternal cones of ice,
Change not, but still remain as ever,
Unwasting, deathless and sublime.”
</verse>

[[right]] —<i>Albert Pike.</i>

<verse>
“There is a wakening on the mighty hills,
A kindling with the spirit of the morn!
Bright gleams are scattered from the thousand rills,
And a soft, visionary hue is born.”
</verse>

[[right]] —<i>Mrs. Hemans.</i>

MY last letter was finished as we were about to leave the City of Mexico. The diary will tell you what we have done and seen since.

[[right]] 8th March.

We leave the Iturbide this morning at six o’clock, driving to the station in the dark. We had seen the two great volcanoes daily in all their changes of light, shade and colour, but never as we were about to see them.
