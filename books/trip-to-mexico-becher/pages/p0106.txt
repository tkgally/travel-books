This the Indian did, but he made no impression on the mind of the bishop. Again the Virgin appeared to the Indian in the same spot, making the same intimation to him, and telling him to inform the bishop of it, which he does, but the bishop is still incredulous. Three times more the Virgin appeared to Juan Diego, always in the same spot, and at their last meeting she cures his uncle of a serious malady by the waters of the chalybeate spring, which suddenly bursts up from under her feet. She then tells the Indian to go up the mountain nearly to its summit and gather some roses, and that he must take them to the bishop as a proof that the message she now sends by him comes from her; and the message is, that she desires the bishop to “build a church on this mountain, and worship her there as the Virgin of Guadalupe.”
