We are now out of the torrid, and well within the temperate zone, not by latitude, but by <i>altitude;</i> look across the deep valley on our left: on the mountain side above it, is a forest of pines, reminding us of the land of our home. Flowers are in abundance on either side of us, and we breast the ascent so slowly, and are so close to them frequently, as to see their full forms and many shades of colour distinctly as we pass. I note here that the Fairleigh locomotive is a wonder of power and fitness for its work; without it they could not work this railway, its gradients are so steep, its curves so sharp. Soon we stop at, and pass, Alta Luz—<i>but look, oh look!</i> there at last is Orizaba—immense, calm, majestic; in shape, reminding us of Etna!
