[[right]]T<sc>hursday</sc>, 21st Feb.

We left our anchorage about seven last evening, and this morning at eight are anchored off Tuxpan. We passed on our left the “Havana,” a fine steamer of this line (the Alexandre of N. York), lying fast on a coral reef, on which she ran and became a wreck last July.

The land is high behind and about Tuxpan, and we see hills, seemingly of volcanic origin far inland. Tuxpan is visible across the bar, a few short miles up the river, but we cannot land, for the “Norther” may come yet, and thus these two Mexican ports are lost to us, and become annoyances, for we <i>must</i> stop for freight, and so lose much time.
