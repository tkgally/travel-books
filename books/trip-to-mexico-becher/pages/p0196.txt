I should like to telegraph to Canada by way of New Orleans, but the line is out of order, again the monkeys have broken the wires.

By noon we are clear of hotel and custom-house, and taking a boat, are soon with all our impedimenta again on board the good steam-ship <i>City of Mexico</i>, and re-possessed of our old state rooms.

We sail to-morrow for New Orleans.
