# Letter No. 3.

[[right]] H<sc>otel</sc> I<sc>turbide</sc>, C<sc>ity of</sc> M<sc>exico</sc>,

[[right]] 25th February.

WE got here last night, and as my last letter was from Orizaba, I give you the diary—much of which is addressed to you, shewing our journey here.

[[right]] 24th Feb.

We leave Orizaba this morning at half-past six. The most interesting and beautiful parts of this wonderful railway are between Orizaba and Boca Del Monte, before arriving at which place the stations of Encinal, Maltrata, Bota, and Alta Luz are passed. We run through the valley of Encinal, which is much higher than that of Orizaba, and enter the gorges of the Infiernillo (little hell) and Second Infiernillo; emerging from thence, the train passes into the valley and among the beautiful mountains of Maltrata, and then up and up, round and round, but always ascending, till we look down upon peaks and clouds.
