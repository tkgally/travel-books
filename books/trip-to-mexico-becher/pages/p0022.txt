On Friday morning at 8 o’clock we awoke close to New Orleans: it was summer; on either side of the railway brush palms, magnolias, and live oak were growing in abundance; and, arrived at the St. Charles Hotel, a bouquet of roses and many flowers graced our breakfast table. What a change of scene and temperature (seventy degrees of Fahrenheit and more), between Canada and Monday, and New Orleans and Friday! and, but for loitering on the way, we might have been here on Wednesday.

We were told an opera troupe was going to Vera Cruz, and that every berth in the ship was engaged; but three of the best state rooms we afterwards found were to be had, and we quickly secured them, having half an hour before almost given up all hope of going at all; for there was no other ship for three weeks, and that delay would have brought us to Vera Cruz on our return, at a much later time than was prudent to be there.
