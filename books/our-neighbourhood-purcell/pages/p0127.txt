However that may be, it is well known that a rich relation paying a round sum receives from the priest a piece of linen which has been carefully scraped thin in the middle, so that it gives way at once, whilst the poverty-stricken survivor, alas! must drag his chain of suspense through the coarsest canvas. But he has, in consequence perhaps, the sympathy of every passer-by, on whom the mute appeal to drop a ladleful of water, for the love of heaven, is never lost; for whatever faults there may be found amongst the Japanese a want of kindliness and sympathy can never be included in them.
