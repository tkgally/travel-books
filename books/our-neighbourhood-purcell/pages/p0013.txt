# Preface.

T<sc>hese</sc> little sketches, originally published in the “<i>Japan Weekly Mail</i>” and here collected together, have been in a measure revised and, indeed, in some instances rewritten. Mere outlines at the best, if, here and there, a touch of shading or a dash of colour can be detected, the author has gratefully to acknowledge the hand of one or other of three friendly artists, to whose kindly help the pictures owe whatever claim they may be found to have to public favour.
