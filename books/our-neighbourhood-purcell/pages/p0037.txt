# The <i>Saké</i> Shop.

The <i>saké</i> shop is, without doubt, the house of most pretensions in “Our Neighbourhood.” Not that there is only one <i>saké</i> shop, for there are several; but the big one at the corner, wherein resides the Worshipful the Mayor, exceeds so much in size and solidity all the other wine-shops, that in comparison with it they seem mere pretenders, contemptible outsiders, little pettifogging retailers of drams to be drunk on the premises—shadowy nonentities on whom the definite article would sit as incongruously as a new hat upon the head of a beggar. The <i>saké</i> shop, on the other hand, built round a fire-and robber-proof godown, which towers substantial-looking in the middle of the less solid but more ornamental looking circumstructure, is like a goodly apple, sound from core to rind.
