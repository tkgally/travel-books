“He was out of his mind for many days before, bewitched by the fox, it is thought, and the doctor says he has been exactly seventy-three days and a-half under water. Well! well! who’d have thought it?” All the time occupied by this colloquy he’s been staring at the speaker, with great interest depicted upon his face, whilst his hands are busy with the customer’s ear; yet, helix, anti-helix, tragus, lobule, he has traversed them all safely with his razor, and has actually commenced to trim an eyebrow, by the time his friend says goodbye. He has a word for everybody in or out of season. “How hot the weather is” says a sleepy horse-boy, yawning whilst waiting his turn to be shaved. “I’m quite exhausted this morning, having travelled three <i>ri</i> since daylight, and slept never a wink last night.
