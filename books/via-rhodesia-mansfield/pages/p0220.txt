Natives here have no love of the beautiful as we understand beauty. They value only those vegetables and trees that yield food. They plant no flowers near their huts. At first my boys were surprised to see me gather flowers, and they seemed to imagine I liked them for their colour, and so, to please me, began to gather scarlet or yellow flowers if any grew in our pathway, and presented them to me without stems! I shall never forget the first time I was offered a flower on a stalk. I was walking slowly one morning, for I had just seen a herd of zebras grazing near and I wanted to see more of their quaint stripes and pretty heads before they scented the presence of strangers and trotted off.
