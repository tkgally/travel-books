In fact they cannot lay down a trunk of a tree or a piece of cloth in a straight line. But stick a spear in the ground, tie to the spear a piece of string with a scrap of iron attached to it, and run round marking the earth as you go, and you have the perfect ground plan of a hut marked out. These huts are about six feet in height and are built of wattle and daub, the wattle being usually not reeds but strong poles. Neither windows nor chimneys are made, so the atmosphere when a huge fire is burning may be imagined.
