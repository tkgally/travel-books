At one time this district was much troubled by the Angoni tribe making raids and stealing both slaves and cattle, but now peace reigns. Here, too, the Arabs established a slave centre, but in 1889 they were routed out, their stockade being captured and destroyed.

Certainly North Nyasaland is far more picturesque than the greater part of Rhodesia, but the very points which lend to its beauty detract from its utility from an agricultural point of view, for the hills and valleys seem to be too broken and precipitous to afford good vantage ground for crops.

Game in Nyasaland is very plentiful, including (in the southern portion) elephants, rhinoceroses, and buffaloes.
