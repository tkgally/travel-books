True, they did not skin the buck on the spot where it fell, but carried it into camp and skinned it there before I had fetched my camera from the tent—also the skin disappeared, and only the skull and horns of the exterior fell to my portion. That is how I knew it was a pookoo, because by showing the horns afterwards I was told so by a white man—no, not even in a book of travel will I claim a knowledge I do not possess—and in zoology I am not a bit learned.

The next morning I went boating on the river. It was Sunday, and I smiled to myself as I contrasted my dugout with a launch on the Thames, and thought how amusing it would be to appear suddenly with my boys at Boulter's Lock! I should like to have had some good photographs of the boating-party, but I had to trust to my head-man to take the photo, and I fear the result is not too good.
