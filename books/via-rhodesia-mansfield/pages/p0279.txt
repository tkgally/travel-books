That it is possible to live in Rhodesia on very little money while yet observing all the rules of cleanliness, comfort, and hospitality is shown by these teaching Fathers. The buildings were spotlessly clean, the table was loaded with good things to eat and home-made wines to drink, and the gardens were filled with vegetables, and yet each Father has an allowance of only twenty-five pounds a year to live upon!

I begged the Fathers, if they were in the habit of holding afternoon service, not to give it up on my account. It seemed that they usually had a service at two o’clock, but in order that I might have a little rest, and then attend the service, the bell was ordered to be rung to summon the natives at three o’clock.
