[[cont]]All traces of the town were soon left behind, and we were in the open country.

Jacob’s conversation certainly could not compare in quantity or quality with a London bus driver’s; to all my inquiries he had but one reply, “Yes, missus.” In fact, he must have been the human descendant of the Raven of poetical fame. Occasionally he made strange noises, which, however, the mules seemed thoroughly to understand, and he rarely, very rarely, used a prehistoric whip, the handle of which reminded one of a worm that has been ruthlessly cut by man and joined by nature.
