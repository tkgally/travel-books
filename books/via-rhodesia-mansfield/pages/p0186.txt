[[cont]]and later, in dense darkness, we arrived at Broken Hill.

That night I made my first acquaintance with a hut, for a very comfortable one was given to me to sleep in. This hut, I learn, is hereafter to be called Mansfield Lodge in honour of my visit.

I remained three days in Broken Hill, and during that time received much consideration from all the officials. And what a jolly party they were! I used to take my meals at their united mess, and one day they proclaimed a half-day's holiday so that I might tell all their fortunes. What a picture they made, seated round the table, so eagerly watching the cards!
