[[cont]]bye to my “boys,” gallant giants, ignorant, yes, as we count knowledge, but how rich in the contentment nature gives and the man of means rarely knows! good-bye to my one and only journey, which started with faith, travelled through danger, and ended in love.

And good-bye to my book, in whose pages I have lived one year over again, and through whose voice I wish with all my heart to help those emigrants who are longing for life in a new world. If to them I give perchance a little hope and encouragement, then my work will not have been in vain, for courage can conquer every obstacle, even self.
