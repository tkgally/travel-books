Mr. and Mrs. West Sheene received me with the usual kindness I had hitherto experienced. The news that I was on the road had travelled, and a comfortable room had been prepared for me. Mrs. West Sheene informed me that all the servants were very interested, they never before having seen a “white donna without a white bwama.”

After a wash and change of garments I went for a drive with that wonderful pony, Mrs. West Sheene taking the rhino reins.

Many were the interesting stories I heard here. Perhaps by degrees my memory will unfold, but I think my store-room got so full of impressions that I can only let out a few at a time now; the rest remain a concrete block, which only with time will dissolve into ink.
