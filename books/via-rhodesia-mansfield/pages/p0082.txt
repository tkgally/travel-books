Travelling from Bulawayo towards Salisbury, you have to change at Gwelo, about six hours’ journey from Bulawayo, whence a branch line runs to Selukwe (where is situated the Wanderer Mine), and from there to Zimbabwe, via Victoria, is a coach journey of ninety-seven miles.

The ruins are about seventeen miles from Victoria, which was the first township established after the occupation of Mashonaland.
