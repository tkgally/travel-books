Divorce is not easy, but separation can be arranged. Very few widows exist, as a widow is theoretically the wife of the next heir, who is the elder brother. Nor is a man a widower for long. If his wife dies, her sister or nearest relation must take her place. Should the sister be too young, the father-in-law provides another

The widower places beer on his wife’s grave, then walks in the garden with his new wife, who, on entering his hut, sits down on a mat, taking the man on her knees (as in the “Bwinga” ceremonious marriage), to show that she is henceforth his, and the people dance round, thus acknowledging the new wife.
