# Chapter XII.
[[subtitle]] A Pilgrimage—A Black Saint—Cerro Redondo—Pascual—Arpino—A Tortilla—The Road-Runner—Swallow-Tailed Fly-Catcher—Cuajiniquilapa—Esclavos—Azagualpa—Jutiapa—Organ-Cactus—Agua Blanca—San Antonio—Volcano of Suchitan—Santa Catarina—El Rio—Amatillo—Silver Mines of Alotepeque.

THE town of Esquipulas is situated only a short distance from the frontier of Honduras, and about five days’ journey from the city of Guatemala.
