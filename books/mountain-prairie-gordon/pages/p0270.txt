Any stores thus left for a traveller in these regions though placed, as in this case, in an unlocked hut, or even though fastened to the branches of a tree, are as safe from all disturbance by the hand of man as though they were guarded by a regiment. The wolverine may sometimes help himself to them, and it requires thoughtful arrangement to secure them against his cunning, but every Indian, or other traveller, holds it a matter of sacred faith to leave them untouched, and passes them as if they were not. The letter and provisions were both in due time found in perfect safety by Dr. Dawson.

After I had completed these postal arrangements my boatmen were attracted by the sight of an Indian lodge near the river, and, recognizing some friends, they went ashore. After they had been gone for some time I found them comfortably engaged at tea in the lodge, and on my appearance the Indian woman at once asked me to join them. As the tepee was hung round with dried moose and beaver-tails, I ventured, with the assistance of one of the men who acted as interpreter, to express admiration of this abundant store of provisions.
