A Canadian clergyman on one occasion visited Cariboo, and hearing occasional profanity, he attempted gently to remonstrate with the offenders. The miners could stand a good lecture on Sunday, but they did not relish reproofs of this kind through the week for what, after all, appeared to them little more than emphatic language; so they undertook to astonish his reverence. By pre-arrangement some of them, when within ear-shot of the Doctor, dropped into conversation, and interlarded their talk with such profanity as even they themselves had never heard before. No wonder that the good man was horrified and gave the miners of Cariboo a bad name, although, had he been behind the scenes, he would hardly have taken this as a specimen of their common conversation.
