## Decisions
- Cover picture: the title page (the cloth cover has a vignette but no readable title).
- Frontispiece p0006 → title page; the map p0020 bound before Chapter I → `frontispiece` list, so it opens Chapter I (lesson from *Clark*).
- Captions: the credit line printed above each plate's title made one caption, title first (“Fort Edmonton. From a Photo. by Dr. G. M. Dawson.”), names in upper and lower case (said in notes_on_text). Alt texts without “engraving, printed sideways”. All plates but p0049 were printed sideways; crops turned upright.
- Contents: the printed chapter titles; the summaries stay at the chapter heads. p0323's title made plain like the others.
- Judge: p0240 “McConnell” (broken l in print), p0260 title stop kept, p0296 credit joined into the caption. The verifier removed [[cont]] on p0012 and p0221: both pages start flush left mid-paragraph; restored.
- p0025: the paragraph runs on after a verse quotation; the markup cannot continue it (new paragraph; known issue). p0347–p0349: a stray space before a note mark removed.
- keep_hyphens: worn-out (p0028), twenty-five (p0142), prairie-chicken (p0200).
- Spot-read (Opus subagent; p0271 seen by the session): printed misprints marked — subtantial (p0153), ourneys (p0168), Stipendary (p0315), probabably (p0271), and four missing stops (p0091, p0139 — the draft had added it silently —, p0210 twice); p0261 “prisner” is the spelling of a quoted inscription, left; Pére (p0229, p0238) as printed; p0018 the page of Chapter XI 281 (a broken 8); p0024 a semicolon; p0224 a note's title in roman; contents titles IV–VII in small capitals like the rest; title page “BY THE” on its own line. The chapter X summary's missing stops are as printed and left.
- Rendered look (Opus subagent): no faults; the preface folio “V” → “v”. The fourth map stays where bound, facing the last page of Chapter IX.
