You would smile at the former, though it seems fine to us here—a freight car painted white, with a bench down the center, around which are some chairs. The messes served are something terrible. We confine our orders, perforce, to beefsteak, which is well cooked. The tea and bread are good, and we drink the milk, but have long since ceased to discuss as to what kind of animal it comes from—an oil tank, I should say, from the taste. The thermometer can not rise very high if the wind keeps up. One can not see fifty yards into the desolation that surrounds him, and does not desire to. I do not believe that without shelter human life could endure for a day. There is no water in all the distance from Merve to the Oxsus. In our whole land, from the Elano Estacado to the Bad Lands, from Maine to California, we have nothing so terrible as this Black Desert.
