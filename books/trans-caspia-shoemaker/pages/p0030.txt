Yet this comfortable official looked us calmly in the eyes, knowing that he was delivering himself of nonsense, and knowing that we were fully aware of that fact, and assured us that the minister of war would be most happy to ask the Governor-General of Turkistan for our permission, but that it must be done by numerous telegrams to that distant region, all of which would take some days, and they had only been at work on it “four.” He would be happy to do all he could, but we must await the pleasure of those rulers of the Far East.[[fnref *]] So we bowed deeply and departed.

I know of no more dreary place for tourists than St. Petersburg in bad weather, when the rain is coming down in torrents. After one has done the sights of the town, there is nothing for it but to retire to one’s hotel, which is far from good, but good as Russian hotels go.
