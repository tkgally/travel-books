The shooting down of another handful of Afghans; perhaps the moving his soldiers rapidly from point to point of that desolate land, and the sending of high-sounding reports to the great white Czar, and receiving therefor further honors—greater rank? Very likely. In the meantime, what is Russia doing for the great cause of civilization and enlightenment in these provinces of Central Asia? And is it done for the benefit of the people, or the further glory of one man?

It is about twenty-five years since she laid her hand on this land of Turkistan, and it is now entirely possible to travel from end to end of it in safety, both as to life and property.
