The people must say nothing and do nothing, otherwise the <i>Schlüsselburg</i>, Peter and Paul, the mines, and Siberia, death having long since ceased to be a terror in Russia. Yet it struck me upon my entrance at Wirballan on Saturday, that the espionage and restrictions were not nearly so great as a few years since. My luggage was scarcely examined at all, and I noticed that it was likewise with crowds of the most suspicious-looking characters. I could have smuggled no end of Nihilist literature and other contraband articles, and perhaps the very fact that less attention is paid to such things makes their authors less anxious to introduce them.
