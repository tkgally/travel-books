Of Russia as a great power, I have not much idea. I do not think she is strong. True, she possesses a great army, but it is, in proportion to her needs and her country to be defended, no larger than ours, of which we certainly do not boast. To guard Russia proper from Turkey, from Germany, and from England, would alone take all her army, leaving the whole of Turkistan open to invasion from England on the Indian side, from China to the eastward, and to internal rebellion, rapine, and murder. As for her Siberian possessions, she could not possibly protect them; but she is probably aware that their best protection lies in their terrible climate and in the fact that no one would have them as a gift. A war would also mean revolution internal in Russia, and what the result would be for her is plainly to be seen. She has sowed the wind; she will most certainly reap the whirlwind.
