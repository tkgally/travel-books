[[cont]]lazarettos are comfortable frame houses, containing two rooms, each about fifteen feet square. The one we enter certainly is very clean, and, with comfortable beds, would not be a bad abiding place if one were quarantined here; but it gives us an uncomfortable sensation to smell iodoform and chloride of lime over all, and to notice that even the grass has been burned. There has been no epidemic this year—only some sporadic cases, of which there are one or two here now. Viewed from these windows, our ship floats a very emblem of liberty, a very portal of Heaven. After all, the fumigation is much of a farce, so far as we are concerned. I most certainly could carry away no end of bacilli. The ceremony over, I wander to the cliff’s side and watch our boatmen in the waters below. What superb forms! They put to shame the Apollo. How entirely impossible such evenly perfect development is with artificial training! They make me wish I had been raised more like a duck or a water-dog. I doubt if I would have been obliged to turn back from the Chinese desert
