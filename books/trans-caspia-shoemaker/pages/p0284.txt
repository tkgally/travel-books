Then I awoke at Jisak, and the morning of my last day's ride in a tarantass.

Later in the day, the road became so rough that twice the tarantass broke down. Finally, when I thought, on seeing the last post before Samarkand, come into view, that our woes were over, I found such a crowd of waiting tarantasses that all hope of progress for at least twenty-four hours was gone. It was simply impossible to stay there. There was not even a dirty spot on which to sit down, and absolutely no chance of any thing to eat.
