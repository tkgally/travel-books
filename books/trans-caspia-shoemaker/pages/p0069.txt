Russia does not neglect the religious welfare of her troops. Daily attendance at mass is required, and to-day her priests, gorgeous in green and gold and purple, hold high celebration of the sacred rite. Then one and all are blessed, and with a fanfare of trumpets the troops move off to their barracks, and we retire to pack up, as we leave at noon for Baku.
