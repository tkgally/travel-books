I learned in Osh that the black plague always exists in many of the cities of Western China, which rather lessens my desire for oriental investigation at those points. It would seem that no epidemic could long exist in the glorious air that sweeps around this ship to-day.

The Bosphorus is almost in sight, but we must stop an hour for medical inspection. It means nothing, but the news that may reach us means much. Those in security have no idea what a quarantine at Constantinople would be. One would not only be subjected to it there, but wherever one might happen to go thereafter, until Northern Europe be reached. That might take two months and more. I might go on in this ship and risk quarantine at Marseilles, but I think I should prefer to land here.
