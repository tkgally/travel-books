One can not but feel that all that had and still has something to do with the present filth of Jisak; and I do not altogether object to these clouds of dust, as they shut out my last view of the filthy place. The native tribes of these eastern countries are dirty enough, but plant amongst them a low type of our much-vaunted civilization, and it will soon outdo the sons of the soil in the matter of filth.
