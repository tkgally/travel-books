So it will be in Turkistan. We are not given a passport or any sort of credentials to that land, but simply notified that we are permitted to travel therein, the notification coming in the shape of an informal note to our legation, and we have been asked by what route we desire to move. Within twenty-four hours of the granting of the permission every point in that vast and lonely land, lonely to us because of our isolation amongst thousands of natives, has been advised and is looking for our coming. Therefore, when one travels the unbeaten paths of farther Asia, it is well to go with the benediction and under the eye of the Czar.
