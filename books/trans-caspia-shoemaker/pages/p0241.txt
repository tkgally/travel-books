Now we are moving backward toward the high, cold valley of the Alai. Of course it is best. Onward certainly meant death to me, and I have other work to do before I can consent to that. I can never speak of Rachmed's kindness and sympathy, when things were at their worst, without deep feeling. To-day should have been one of great heat, and therefore of intense distress for me, but during the night a high wind arose and is still blowing. It has lifted, carried far above us, and filled the sky with a gray veil of sand, which has shaded the sun and made the day bearable.
