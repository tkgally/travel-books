Still, the cities of Kashgar, Yarkand, and Leh, and the tremendous Killian and Karakoram Passes, eighteen thousand feet up, repay all that, and I reach Cashmere, the goal of my ambitions for years, in time to see something of it. <i>Via</i> the Pamirs, I can simply pass through that romantic vale and hurry southward, as the journey will occupy a month longer than the Kashgar route. However, this is a question I shall not have to decide until we reach Osh.
