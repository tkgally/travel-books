Russia is endeavoring to destroy Samarkand’s reputation as being the birth and eternal abiding place of the scourge. She insists that the cisterns, which are the great source of that terrible scourge, shall be cleaned every few years, instead of once in three hundred years.

Our stay in Samarkand draws to a close, and I can not depart without a word more for Madam Metzler and her very comfortable hotel of the same name. Madam is French, as I have remarked, and has brought to this oasis in the desert many of those charming French ways that one remembers in the little hotel at Blois, where the pet magpie had no tail. You know the place. The Madam there had a daughter; the Madam here has none, and so we are in a measure all her children.
