[[cont]]that, when he came in, after having absented himself from his post for eight hours, he was loaded with whiskey or beer; not absolutely drunk—he could have been put to bed had he been that—but quarrelsome in the extreme and very impertinent. The next morning, after the tarantass was packed and we were about to start for Tashkend, he informed us that: “We speak cross to him. Pay him little money. He no go.” So we drove off without him. Of course, under the circumstances, he received no wages, and had to pay his way home. He must have greatly changed since Mr. Littledale employed him. His bills were always larger than ours, and we have since discovered that he was not exactly honest. When a servant consumes five bottles of beer in an afternoon, as he did, his usefulness is over. We were sorry on many accounts, especially as he was a handy fellow, and, more weighty still, because Rachmed speaks but little that we can understand.
