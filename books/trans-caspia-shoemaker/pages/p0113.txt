For centuries Bokhara has been hidden as in the depths of midnight, coming distinctly into view only now and then as though in a blaze of lightning, during which has been seen the shadowy forms of Jenghiz Kahn and his savage hordes appearing on the horizon, passing with sword and flame over the ancient city, and vanishing, phantom-like, in the desert to the westward; then the city again has sunk into darkness, mysterious, romantic, and impenetrable.
