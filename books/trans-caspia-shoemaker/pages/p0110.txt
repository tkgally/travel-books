The “invention of the devil” has ceased to alarm, has greatly decreased the power of the Amir, and increased that of Russia. The Amir is prevented by dignity from gratifying his curiosity, which is as great as the lowest in his realm.

Bokhara maintains a semblance of independence and is allowed to do so through the policy of Russia, whose representative, Baron Wrevfsky, the governor-general at Tashkend, told us that he never had and never could visit Bokhara until Russia assumed in name the control thereof.
