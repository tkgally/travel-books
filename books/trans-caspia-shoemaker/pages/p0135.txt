Samarkand was old and Samarkand was beautiful before the advent of our Christ. Some say that she vies in antiquity with ancient Egypt. Be that as it may, she stands peerless to-day in this heart of Central Asia, and all through the blessing of the waters of the Zarafshan—true gold, verily, in this land of the sun. Forty-three great canals, whose combined length is over six hundred miles, flow from that river in a network over all the land, and from these a thousand branches complete a perfect system of irrigation.
