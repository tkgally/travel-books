These Sarts are a cunning lot, and will take advantage wherever they can. They are spending at least an hour fussing over their horses this morning, on the plea that the extra duration of yesterday’s journey renders it necessary. It makes no difference to me, as I shall insist upon just so many versts, no matter how late we are in getting off. There is no use becoming impatient. It simply starts them in a torrent of words, all of which are Greek to you, and also merely delays their progress. You will have your way in the end; and if you have any serious trouble, simply threaten them with a report to the Russian governor of the province, and they will come down from their high positions at once.
