My return passage of the “Taldek” is much more interesting than was the first, the scenery as viewed from the south being more rugged and bolder in outline. Of course, it in no way equals the view of the Trans-Ali, but you see none of that in your passage southward until the Taldek is far behind you. I am referring now simply to the scenery that pertains to that pass, and it is certainly much more interesting as viewed coming north. In addition, marvelous to relate, it does not in many places seem familiar, though my former passage was just ten days ago. So strange to me do portions of it appear, that I question Rachmed as to whether we have not come another route; but that could not be, as we are on the one military road in this section. The panorama is very varied and grand all day long, and the tempest of wind which sweeps from the north makes the day, which would otherwise be intensely hot, very pleasant.
