# Chapter V.

RAILROADS here are conducted in a somewhat unusual manner. It is always necessary to go to the station at least half an hour before the time of starting, and even then you will be hurried at the last. I fear Abbas is little better, if any, than old Thomas, my Indian servant. At least, he completely loses his wits in the station at Tiflis, and I am forced literally to shake him once or twice as he insists upon running after every one’s luggage save ours. The confusion is enormous, and “worse confounded” every moment, while the waiting-rooms look like a city camping out. We are just one hour getting booked and seated, having had in that time the usual number of rows with people who tried to impose upon us.
