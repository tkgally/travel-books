A large immigration of Chinese has, of late years, taken place to the Malay Peninsula, and their conflicts with the natives have complicated the position of affairs. The country appears to be rich in mines, which are a great inducement to English capitalists to settle in it. In 1867 the management of these colonies was transferred from the Indian Government to the Colonial Secretary. Till 1873 the Colonial Office wisely strove to prevent interference with the troubled politics of the native States.
