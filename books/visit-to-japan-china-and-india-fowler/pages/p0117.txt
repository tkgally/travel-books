It is an Indian rather than a Chinese question, and for the present I must merely allude to the results in regard to China. A great authority, with whom I conversed on the subject, pleaded that the taste for opium did not originate with its introduction by the East India Company, but must have existed in China long before we traded there, as we then knew nothing, and now know little, of the interior provinces. For the honour of England, I hope this view may be true, as it would be a comfort to think that, though we have undoubtedly encouraged, we did not introduce this vice; but I can but fear there is no evidence in support of the theory. Opium is now so general that people cannot conceive that a century since it was unknown. My friend believed that the growth in China is large and increasing, and has existed for a long course of years. This seems the general view, and I can but fear that the evil has grown to such an extent that it is no longer in our power to put a stop to it.
