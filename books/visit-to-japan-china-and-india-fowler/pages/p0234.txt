The Committee of the House of Commons which sat from 1870 to 1873, owing to the dissolution of Parliament made no report to the House. What the report of that Committee might have been, had they been permitted to make one, may in many points be very doubtful. From what I know of the sentiments of my colleagues, I believe that, in the question of the Home Charges, they would by a large majority, perhaps unanimously, with the exception of the official members—those who were, who had been, or who expected to be connected with the India Office—have recommended that many of these charges should be transferred to the English Exchequer.
