At home elaborate preparations and accessories are required for serpentine dances, whilst here we had three little Japanese maidens, dancing on an ordinary matted floor to the dreariest of accompaniments, yet holding their audience enthralled by their graceful gyrations, as they flung the long white strips over and around them with amazing rapidity. It was late in the evening before we left the Maple Club, where we had spent two hours and a half in a very delightful manner.
