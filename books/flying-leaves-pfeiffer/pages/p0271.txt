In crossing this spur of the “Rockies” we have before us now, at near two o’clock, the whole of the grand range bounding the plain to the right of the line, and our spirits revive with the imposing spectacle. The prairie since first seen at sunrise has been covered with the short, sweet tussucks of the buffalo grass, and now in the middle distance the surface is for the first time dotted with trees. We have had our lunch on a table spread in our “section” of the car, and with it a cup of tea, which has refreshed our poor battered bodies. We have read a most impressive sermon of the Rev. Myron W. Reade, reprinted in the <i>Denver Daily News</i>, and that has refreshed our spirits. The art of sermonizing, occasionally to effective purpose, would not seem to be extinct in the new world. The sun is shining; the world is in progress; the end is still hopeful if God is its Guide.
