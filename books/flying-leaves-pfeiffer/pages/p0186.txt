If the public prints of the States, and the openly expressed belief of well-informed persons are to be trusted, fœticide obtains throughout all classes of the Union, in a measure highly subversive of the interests of a growing nation, if for no other reason than that the accretions from without can hardly be expected to be of such quality as those which might legitimately be looked for from within. The Mormon people pointing in triumph to their overflowing nurseries, denounces the monogamic system which it charges with these barren results. In effect they are doubtless to be looked for elsewhere; but that is for the present beside the mark.

There can surely be no heart that would not hail the hope of any mitigation of the most festering ulcer of our overgrown cities—the shame of which is shared by every woman however placed, from the queen upon her throne to the sempstress who is sewing, as with one thread, a “shirt and a shroud;” many a thoughtful mind must also be exercised with that problem of the future, and in many places of the present: that glut of population which is already the plethora of industrial centres, and which threatens in a time not quite vaguely distant, to over-burthen all the green and pleasant places of the earth.
