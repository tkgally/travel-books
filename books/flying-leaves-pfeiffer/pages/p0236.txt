[[cont]]in the rear. In the front and to either side are magnificent views of the rocky walls of the valley, and rich, and now as we first see it in the slanting beams of the sun, glowing combinations of woodland and river.

Here we are glad to find rest and shelter for a time, and are fain to be content with such food as we can find to repair the waste of our much-enduring frames.
