When near Brush Creek, our camping ground, we came on a herd of seven Rocky Mountain sheep feeding by a small pool. Getting as near as the ground permitted, we both fired and dropped one, while the rest of the herd, scampering off, climbed up the face of what seemed to us a perpendicular precipice. On reaching the top they turned round and stared at us, standing with all four legs drawn close together on the points of the highest rocks. As darkness was setting in we could not pursue them, but made the best of our way to camp, and found that the other hunters had made a good bag of antelope, deer, and hares.
