After another night of misery, on Tuesday morning we reached Argenti, and joyfully bade farewell to the horrible stage waggons in which Messrs. Wells, Fargo and Co. had conveyed us unfortunate passengers the distance of nearly seven hundred miles between the terminus of the Union Pacific and that of the Central Pacific Railway.

Argenti is merely a station in the middle of a huge plain, through which runs the Humboldt River, and is about three hundred and thirty miles east of Sacramento. The station is surrounded by half a dozen drinking and gambling-houses, and one of our fellow-passengers, having ventured into a gambling tent, was very soon robbed of the sum of five hundred dollars in gold.
