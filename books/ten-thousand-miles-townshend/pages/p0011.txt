Happily, owing to the kindness of Generals Sherman and Augur, in allowing us to accompany a party sent on the scout across the plains of Nebraska and Kansas, we were not only enabled to enjoy our novel sport with comparative security, but had an excellent opportunity of becoming acquainted with the life of the American soldier in the military outposts of the Far West. We were received at these distant stations, as everywhere in America, with the greatest kindness and hospitality by the officers, who invariably pressed us to spend some time with them, promising to show us the best sport to be obtained in their neighbourhood.
