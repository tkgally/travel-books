[[cont]]two former classes have lately been a good deal thinned by the exertions of that secret tribunal known and dreaded as the Vigilantes, or Vigilance Committee.

I was told that most of the respectable storekeepers were members of this society, which exercises the functions of judge, jury, and executioner on all such murderers, or horse-thieves—whose crime is considered much worse even than that of taking the life of a fellow-creature—as they can catch. The sentence is carried out at night, and the very morning of our arrival at Laramie, the bodies of six horse-thieves were found hanging to the timbers of a frame-house in course of construction in the town, and four more dangled from the telegraph poles along the railroad; while underneath was posted a notice, signed, “The Vigilance Committee,” to the effect that, unless Bill Smith or Joe Brown (naming some suspected thieves) cleared out of Laramie at once, they would be served in the same manner.
