Just out of Tupper’s Lake the river was deeply fringed with water-lilies, on which rested the most extraordinary number of huge bull-frogs, who, with their bright black eyes, appeared to watch us intently, disappearing in the water with a croak as our paddles made play about their heads. Passing through Racquet Pond we killed several wild fowl, and a large snake which was crawling along the bank. From this point we had a carry of a couple of miles to Little Wolf Pond, which we crossed, and then had another carry of half a mile to Big Wolf Pond, where we had determined to take up our quarters, as the most likely place to find a good supply of game. Coasting round the lake we soon hit on a good site for a camping ground.
