We made one charming excursion to a village at the head waters of the Hudson. The distance was about twelve miles, over a mountain road running most of the way through a dense forest, with occasional clearings where log-huts and small fields sown with buckwheat and Indian corn, occasionally appeared. The fields are surrounded by the wooden zig-zag or snake fences, or by huge roots of trees stuck up on end, which effectually keep off the inroads of any cattle whose tastes lead them to prefer the green shoots of the Indian corn to the scanty herbage found in the forest.
