[[cont]]Eddystone Lighthouse, it was not till we were some miles past it, and the driving mist cleared for a minute, that our captain was able to make out its position, or where we were. From the pilot, who came on board in the Sound, we learnt that no communication had taken place between the lighthouse and the mainland for eight weeks, and that the weather in the Channel had been fearful. Having landed mails and passengers, we again steamed out into the gale, and early on Monday morning, 28th December, anchored off Netley Hospital, Southampton.
