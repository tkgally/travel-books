In the valley of the San Joachim river is found that marvel of vegetable life the Mammoth Grove of Calaveros, the <i>Washingtonia</i> of the Americans, known in Europe as the <i>Wellingtonia gigantea.</i> This grove consists of over ninety trees, many of which are from three to four hundred feet in height, and from eighty to a hundred feet in circumference. Though this species of tree nowhere attains such a gigantic size as in the Grove of Calaveros, numerous specimens of smaller size are met with in many of the Californian valleys. In fact, the natural tendency of the vegetable productions of California, whether they be trees, roots, plants, or fruit, seems to be to attain to a size unequalled in any other part of the world.
