[[cont]]direction. The left lateral gallery was stopped at midnight July 22nd. The right lateral gallery, being two hundred and thirty-eight feet long, was stopped at 6 <sc>p.m.</sc> July 23rd. The mine could have been charged and exploded at this time. The men were employed from that time in draining, timbering, and placing eight magazines in position, and having received the order to charge the mine on the 27th of July, the powder was commenced to be placed in at 4 <sc>p.m.</sc>, and finished at 10 <sc>p.m.</sc> The tamping was then begun, and completed by 6 <sc>p.m.</sc> the 28th. The charge consisted of three hundred and twenty kegs of powder, each containing twenty-five pounds—eight thousand pounds in all. The size of the crater formed by the explosion was at least two hundred feet long, fifty feet wide, and twenty-five feet deep.”

Of the terrible loss of life caused by the explosion one may yet judge to a certain extent by the numerous human remains still visible, notwithstanding the immense number of bodies which have been removed and buried.
