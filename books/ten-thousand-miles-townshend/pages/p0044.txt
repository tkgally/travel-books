The evening of our arrival, there was a ball in the immense saloon—I should say one of the largest rooms in the world—of the hotel called Congress Hall. The room was well filled with dancers. Some of the gentlemen were in evening dress, though the larger proportion wore frock coats, and the ladies appeared in the most gorgeous toilettes of the latest Paris fashions.

How the American ladies managed to travel about with the enormous quantity of luggage necessary to carry the number of different dresses they appeared in every day, was always a matter of astonishment to me. Paris, of course, is the model of fashion adopted by them, though in some respects they appeared rather to overdo it. The ridiculous manner of walking, called the Grecian bend, for example, was a mere parody on the Parisian fashion.
