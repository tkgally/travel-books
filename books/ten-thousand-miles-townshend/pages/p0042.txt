On Monday, 24th August, we started by rail for Saratoga, a journey of about twelve hours, <i>viâ</i> Fitchburgh and Rutland. The road runs through the picturesque mountain and valley scenery of the green mountains. The forest trees that cover the surrounding country have suffered severely from the ravages of some insect which invests them with a sort of web, and after destroying all the leaves, eventually kills the tree.

On our arrival at Saratoga we put up at the Clarendon Hotel, in which, though the season was nearly over, some five hundred people were staying. The neighbourhood of Saratoga is extremely pretty; but the village itself is not the least remarkable for beauty of situation.
