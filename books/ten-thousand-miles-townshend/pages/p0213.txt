He must possess the greatest tact and executive ability, as his power is supreme and unhesitatingly obeyed, though he is now only head of the church, and no longer governor of the territory, both of which offices he formerly held.

After visiting the President, we drove to the sulphur springs, about a mile outside the town, where the water bubbles out of the side of the mountain at a temperature of 110 degrees Fahr., and is conducted into a large swimming-bath, into which, notwithstanding the horrible smell of sulphur, we at once plunged. We enjoyed the warm sulphurous water immensely. The aches and rheumatics contracted in the waggon journey of the last three days were considerably relieved by the bath.
