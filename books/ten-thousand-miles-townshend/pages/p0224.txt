The advance of the Union Pacific Railway has enormously increased the number of Gentile residents and visitors to Salt Lake, and many of the principal stores are now held by them.

An anti-Mormon newspaper has been established by Yankee unbelievers at Salt Lake city. The editor informed us that no active steps had been taken by the Mormon leaders against him—the fate of Joseph Smith, after repressing the anti-Mormon paper at Nauvoo, no doubt being a warning to Brigham Young. The present number of Mormons is supposed to be about two hundred thousand.
