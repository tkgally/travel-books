## Birds.

The wild turkey (<i>Meleagris gallopavo</i>), shot in the creeks intersecting the plains west of Missouri river.

The prairie hen (<i>Cupidonia cupido</i>), shot on the prairies of Iowa.

The spruce partridge (<i>Tetrao canadensis</i>), shot in the forests of the Adirondack region.

Sharp-tailed grouse (<i>Pedioccetes phasianellus</i>), shot in the valleys intersecting the plains west of Missouri river.

Sage hens (<i>Centrocircus mophasianus?</i>), shot in the Rocky Mountains.

Grey mountain grouse (<i>Bonasa umbellus</i>), shot in the valleys of the Rocky Mountains.

English snipe (<i>Gallinago Wilsonii</i>), shot on the marshes near Missouri river, Iowa.

Brent goose (<i>Bernilla Brenta</i>), shot on the marshes near Missouri river, Iowa.

The mallard (<i>Anas boschas</i>), shot near Missouri river, Iowa; on the plains and Rocky Mountains.

The pintail (<i>Dufila acuta</i>), shot near Missouri river, and on the plains and Rocky Mountains.
