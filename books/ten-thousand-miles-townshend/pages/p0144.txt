# Chapter IX.

[[subtitle]] Encounter with a Buffalo—Pawnee Bob—Vindictive Cruelty of Indian Squaws—A Medicine Robe—Medicine Fork—Tame Magpies—Cañons—Black-tailed Deer—Fort Macpherson—Construction of the Forts—Life of Soldiers in Outposts—The Chevington Massacre—Irish Soldiers—Anti-British Songs—Fenian Anthem.

F<sc>riday</sc>, 23<sc>rd</sc>.—Our course lay north-west, over the usual plains and ravines. A large herd of antelopes suddenly appeared on our flank, before the sun had cleared away the morning mist. Sending a volley into their midst, we killed two, but dared not follow the herd, for fear of being lost in the fog.

In the afternoon the sun shone out brilliantly, and as we approached the bluffs over the valley of the Republican River, we saw eight buffaloes grazing on a hill about two miles on our right.
