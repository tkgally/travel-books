Just off Port Royal we had to stop until the medical officer came on board, before we were allowed to enter the bay.

Port Royal consists of a few white houses and a fort. It is situated at the end of the long sandy peninsula which separates the Bay of Kingston from the open sea. The houses are built on ground hardly at all above the level of the sea, and yellow fever is said to be more prevalent there than in any other part of the island. Under a grove of palm trees close by were white tents, where some soldiers were encamped. Having shown a clean bill of health we steamed on up the bay, and at 11 <sc>a.m.</sc> arrived off the Company’s coal wharf at Kingston.
