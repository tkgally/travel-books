# Chapter XIV.

[[subtitle]] Start for San Francisco—The Shores of Salt Lake—Stockton—A Fair Mormon—Stockton Lake—The Great American Desert—Character of the Country, Inhabitants and Climate—Deep Creek—Meteoric Shower—Ruby Valley—Diamond Mountain—White Pine—Austin—A Rush to the Silver Mines of Nevada—Curious Atmospherical Illusion—Terminus of Central Pacific Railroad—Roughs in the Cars—Sunrise in the Sierra Nevada Mountains.

T<sc>hursday</sc>, 12<sc>th</sc>. Seven <sc>a.m.</sc> found eight of us packed once more into the odious mud-waggon bound for California, a journey of about five hundred miles by waggon, and five hundred more by rail or steamboat down the Sacramento River to San Francisco.
