# Chapter XV.

[[subtitle]] Arrival at Sacramento—River Scenery—San Francisco—The Overland Route—California—Climate and Temperature—Productions—Fruit—Wines—The Earthquake of October 21st—The New Docks—Monopoly—Woollen Mills—Chinese—The Cliff House—Sea-lions—Theatre and Club.

As we continued our journey we passed several villages inhabited entirely by Chinese, and after crossing a great level plain, resembling a prairie, but with groups of large trees scattered over it, we shortly before midday reached Sacramento, the capital of the State of California.

Sacramento is situated at the junction of the Sacramento and American rivers, and lies low, surrounded by plains and the great swamp which extends down to San Francisco.
