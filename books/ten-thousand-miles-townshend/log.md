## Decisions
- Cover picture: the title page (the cloth cover has no readable title).
- Frontispiece p0007 (sideways; turned upright) set as `frontispiece`: shown on the title page after the title-page vignette.
- Contents: the chapters have only numbers and summaries; each entry is “Chapter N.” and two or three topics from the summary, in its words (said in notes_on_text).
- Advertisements p0294–p0311: one division; their `#` headings made `##`. Blotted letters in three advertisements supplied from the context ([[supplied]]: desideratum, out, women, better, as).
- Judge: 2 spots, both in the advertisements (p0303 the address line restored from the draft, as the judge read it).
- Spot-read (Opus subagent): p0089 “vià” → “viâ” as printed; three missing stops printed (p0085, p0155, p0221) marked `[[corr]]`; “canôns” and “cañons” both printed; “cock-fight” keeps its hyphen (p0263, as the next line prints it); rules before Fish and Reptiles added. The contents and the chapter heads differ in a few words in print (coyotes/CAYOTES, LOSE/LOOSE SAILS …); each is transcribed as printed.
- Rendered look (Opus subagent): no faults but the appendix's opening line, which made a long contents entry: typed as a centred line (it is centred under the heading in print), not a subtitle.
