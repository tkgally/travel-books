To me at least the Lapps are by far the most interesting race of people in Europe, and I longed to see with my own eyes what sort of people they were; and the strongest inducement I could have had to spend a much longer time in Finmark was the study of this interesting people in their remote mountain homes, where I might hope to see a nearer approach to the ancient habits, customs, and superstitions than is possible on the sea-coast, where a closer contact with the Gothic race must have modernized these to a certain extent.
