Travelers on carriole routes in Norway should always start with a good supply of provisions, for they are very liable to need them before they get through, and they should be well provided with water-proofs, for they may be frequently exposed to rains, though fortunately we escaped them entirely. The sun shone brightly all day, and it was quite warm enough for comfort.

The next route was about seven miles, and brought us to Austbjerg. At Bjerkager one of the ladies changed places with Mr. Richards, in order to have some experience of carriole riding; she led us a merry dance over the road, and, I imagine, pretended not to understand the post-boy when he hinted that she would get through at last at a little slower pace.
