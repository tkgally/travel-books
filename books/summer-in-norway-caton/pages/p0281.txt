Their whole intercourse seemed to recognize this physical capacity of the women, and probably anything which would suggest any lack in this regard would have been regarded, by one of those strapping girls, as an affront to her womanhood, and would have required an apology from the unlucky swain chargeable with the offense.

These women are good-looking, but not handsome according to our standard of beauty.
