At the mouth they catch them with their infernal nets by the cart-load, and quite as many as they ever do. I can’t explain it. I can’t understand it. I have studied these fish for twenty years and more, and think I know all their ways, but this is one for which I can conceive of no reason. When I came the water was high, and I thought that might be the reason; but here I have been after them faithfully every day till now it is low, and yet not a single rise. This unaccountable habit of the fish is not confined to this river, but from the reports I get, it is universal throughout Norway, even clear around North Cape to the Russian line, and probably beyond. The same thing has occurred a few times before, but it is very exceptional. That the fish are abundant, and in fine condition, is shown by those taken at the mouths of the rivers, where the nets are allowed to be stretched across them three days in the week.
