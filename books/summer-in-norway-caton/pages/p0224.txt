The gardens, too, around the fisher-huts, seemed now completed, and the snow-banks which had rested against the inclosing walls, were now gone or much diminished. Nature seemed to have awakened from her long sleep, and to have aroused herself to gigantic efforts to complete her work if possible in the short summer allotted there. The scenery was less dark and forbidding than it had seemed before. If then the scene seemed to wear a forbidding frown, it smiled a welcome now.
