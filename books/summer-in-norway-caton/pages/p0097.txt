# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] C<sc>oast towns and people; local travel and traffic; no baggage smashing;</sc> T<sc>orghatten, the natural tunnel; the seven sisters; entering the snow-area; “the horseman’s” island; legend of the horseman and the tunnel; the midnight sun; strange sensations produced; a school for myths.</sc>

OUR stops were so frequent that our real progress was slow, though the little steamer was smart when under way. These frequent stops furnished an excellent opportunity to observe the people, and many of their habits and customs. Here the waters are the public highways, and so their carriages must be water-craft. They go visiting, to church, and to business, in their boats. A young lady does not hesitate to jump into her light shallup, and row off half-a-dozen miles to call on her associates and have a chat about parties, picnics and beaux.
