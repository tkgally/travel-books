A single day showed a wonderful development of flowers, and indeed all vegetation. Ten flowers were found now where one was met the day before, and many new varieties which were not then observed, so that we had no trouble in making a fine collection.

On the top of the upper table an extensive peat bog was found, though apparently of limited depth. Several excavations showed where peat, though of an inferior quality, had been taken for fuel, and drains were cut through it in various directions.

Many cows were pasturing upon it, though the grass seemed coarser and less abundant than beyond it.
