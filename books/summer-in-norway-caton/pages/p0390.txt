Many have supposed that Norway was a mere appendage to and a dependence upon Sweden. This is no more true than that Sweden is appended to and dependent upon Norway. The truth is, each is quite independent of the other so far as all internal affairs are concerned. In their foreign relations they are united, but upon equal terms. It is true that the principal residence of the king is in Stockholm, simply because it is a more eligible place for the royal residence. It is a larger city and in a more southern latitude than Christiana.
