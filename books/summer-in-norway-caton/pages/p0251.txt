Of all the deer family the female reindeer alone is provided with antlers, which, like those of the males of all, are shed and renewed every year. This is true, not only of the European reindeer, but of both the American species as well. Indeed, the little barren-ground caribou, whose range is substantially within the Arctic Circle of America, has much the largest antlers of any of the reindeer, as well the female as the male. However, the antlers of the female in all the species and varieties of the reindeer are much smaller than on the male, and she carries them later in the season before she sheds them.
