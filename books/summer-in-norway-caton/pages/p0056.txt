The truth is, extravagance in Norway does not run to beds or bedsteads. Now, one would suppose that in so cold a country, double beds would have been found comfortable, and become popular, especially during their long, dark winters; but I imagine, in the hotels at least, they never tried it, and probably would consider it very unbecoming. I understand that in private houses they frequently have extension bedsteads, which may be drawn out and double beds made upon them, but we met none of these at the hotels. On nearly all beds in Norway we found a very thick spread, made of down, almost enough for a bed of itself, and indeed we used it as such.
