When the storm abated and the fog lifted, he saw to the west a level wooded coast, but not observing the snow-clad mountains characteristic of Greenland he refused to land, and bore away to the northeast. After a few days’ sail he again discovered a similar coast, and again after a few days more similar land was seen on his left, which, for the same reason, he passed by, and, pressed forward by a strong southwest wind, sighted the snow mountains of Greenland, which told him it was the land of his destination.
