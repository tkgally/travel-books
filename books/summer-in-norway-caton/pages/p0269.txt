Do what we could to darken the windows to keep out the light, still it was not night as nature makes it, and which the habit of a lifetime had rendered necessary to sound repose. Artificial darkness, especially when incomplete, is as far from night as artificial light is from day. What we now desired was, a good long, sound sleep in a natural night, and not the less perfect rest in the sombre shade of curtained windows. We therefore went on board the Trondhjem, which would take us below the Arctic Circle and beyond the confines of perpetual day, without regret.
