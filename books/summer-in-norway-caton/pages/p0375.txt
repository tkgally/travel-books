LILLEHAMMER is situated on two eminences overlooking the lake, with a view up the valley of the Laagan. In the estuary at the mouth of the river, the floating timber we had seen drifting down the river was collected to an immense amount, and is thence transported to different points on the lake to be cut up into lumber.

We drove on directly through that part of the town situated on the first eminence, across the valley, which is well built up, to the Hammer hotel, which is built on the southern eminence. Here we found comfortable rooms, in which we were soon established and ordered dinner.
