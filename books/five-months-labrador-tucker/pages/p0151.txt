Cape Breton, from its situation, forming the eastern barrier of the Gulf of St. Lawrence, and commanding the access from the Atlantic, from the earliest date was regarded as the key to the Canadas. Any naval power in possession of it, provided as it is with good harbors, will be arbiter of the commerce of Canada, and the coasts bordering the St. Lawrence Gulf. The whole circuit of the island of Cape Breton is 275 miles. The principal harbors are those of Sydney, Louisbourg, St. Anne’s, and the Great Bras d’Or. As we sailed along the coast, we noticed the buildings and improvements at Sydney, which is regarded as one of the finest harbors in the British provinces.
