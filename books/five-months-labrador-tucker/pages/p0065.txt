[[cont]]we heard a rushing among the bushes, and cracking of sticks, as of some wild animal, hovering on our path, to take advantage of our want of deadly preparation. We now began to fear foul play—and to concert plans of operation—for there was evidently some enemy near, whom it was our duty to circumvent if possible. Take him, we could not, if he should happen to prove one of the brown bears of the island, for they are good fighters, having an instinctive dislike to the biped race; and unless better skilled than we then were in the use of the knife, he would probably have mastered a couple such as we were. My comrade grew pale with affright, and stepping close up to me, and asking in a low voice, “if we had not better <i>scratch gravel?</i>” he actually took to his heels, like one bereft of his wits, before I had time to reply to his ludicrous question.
