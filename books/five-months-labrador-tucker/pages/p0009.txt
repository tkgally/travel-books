# Preface.

W<sc>hen</sc> a new publication is offered to the reading community, the inquiry naturally arises, who is the author? and, what are his motives in writing? Both these inquiries I will endeavor to answer as briefly as possible.

In the spring of 1834, agreeably to previous impressions of duty, I entered upon a course of study, preparatory to the work of the gospel ministry. Having no friends, whose worldly circumstances enabled them to afford me the means of pursuing an education, I was thrown entirely upon my own resources.
