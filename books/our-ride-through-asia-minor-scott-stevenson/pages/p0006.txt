# Dedication.

[[center]] I Dedicate this my Second Book,

[[center]] <sc>as i dedicated my first,</sc>

[[center]] TO MY HUSBAND,

[[center]] CAPTAIN ANDREW SCOTT-STEVENSON,

[[center]] <i>Forty-Second Royal Highlanders</i> (“<i>The Black Watch</i>”),

[[center]] CIVIL COMMISSIONER OF KYRENIA, CYPRUS,

[[center]] <sc>as a tribute to his love and care.</sc>

[[center]] <sc>he shared in every scene described in its pages; and without his courage, tact, knowledge, and determination, i could not have accomplished a journey which has occupied three months of the happiest time of my life.</sc>
