M<sc>r.</sc> H<sc>enderson’s</sc> dragoman and cavasses having arrived, we devoted the morning to looking through the bazaars. We were much struck with the excellent repair of the pavement as well as the cleanliness of the streets—things quite unlooked-for in a Turkish town.
