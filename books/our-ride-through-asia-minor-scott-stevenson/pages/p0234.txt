One man offered to drive us by way of Nem Shehr to Koniah. He said that from the former town we could get down to Nigdeh and Eregli, and thence across the great plain by Karabunar to the Turkish capital. We agreed to pay him five pounds for the journey and the bargain was struck. He promised to appear at our house by sunrise next day. In the meantime, as it was Holy Friday and the shops shut as before, we amused ourselves by walking about and re-examining the ruins we had seen on the previous day.
