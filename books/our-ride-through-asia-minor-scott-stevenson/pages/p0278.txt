[[cont]]broken one. These Tartars are fond of money; so in spite of his sufferings our friend was determined not to forfeit the good price we were paying him for the journey. Thus he succeeded in getting the original sum, and in keeping the present we had made him for the loss of his araba besides something in addition. For his first words of greeting were “Backsheesh;” and really Andrew was so pleased with his pluck that he cheerfully made him a present of a new narghili.
