[[subtitle]] The Deserted Village.

W<sc>hen</sc> next day, May 8th, we left Eregli early, I confess that, for my part, it was in fear and trembling. Nahli had told us the Circassians had been asking the arabaji, who we were, and making particular inquiries as to our route. I am not a bit afraid of Turks, Armenians or Greeks, but I must say I don’t like the Circassians.
