Indeed, it looked as if its owner had only lately left it, the condition was so good. The town was not large, but possessed many curiosity shops, some of which contained great quantities of armour, bows, arrows, and spears, that had formerly belonged to the castle, and been sold when the owner had retired into private life. Some of the suits were beautifully made of steel, inlaid with gold; some of them made of chain, and some of small pieces of bamboo, which they said would turn a sword cut. The helmets were fitted with curious iron masks to fit the face exactly, and which gave a most diabolical expression to the countenance, as they were made with a long aquiline nose and strongly marked features, with a sneering sinister look. There was enough common armour, spears, bows, and arrows to arm a regiment, and to be purchased at a very cheap rate.
