Nearly every vessel dragged their anchors. Although we had three anchors down and were steaming as well, the lower yards and topmasts being down on deck and everything else sent from aloft, yet we managed to drag our anchors till we got close under the bow of the “Princess Charlotte;” so close, that it appeared as if her great cutwater must strike our stern as she pitched to the waves. Fortunately we had not veered quite all our cable; we therefore watched our opportunity, and gave her full speed with the engines, the helm being placed hard over to port at the same time, and then veered on the cables quickly. The ship answered her helm and sheered clear of the “Princess Charlotte,” bringing up safely under her stern. The houses in the town were much damaged, and the sea-wall was washed away by the waves for a considerable distance.
