The Japanese General received them courteously, but stated that he could not leave without orders from Japan; indeed he had no transports to go in if he wished to. Before the negotiations terminated, a gale came on, driving the gunboats away, and compelling the commissioners to return by land, a very fatiguing journey. Before leaving the town of Liang-kiou, they had sent for the chiefs of the neighbouring tribes, and induced those that came to acknowledge allegiance to China, and sent them away with presents. They also issued the following Proclamation.
