The drive round by the race-course is very good, and much frequented with carriages of an afternoon. The temples and tombs of the Tycoons at Yeddo are very beautiful from their carving, gilding, and quantity of valuable bronze figures. Each temple is surrounded by large trees and gardens, which are always laid out with good taste. In front of the temples there are numbers of large lanterns of stone and bronze. The temples themselves are of very simple construction, being built with enormous beams, and having high roofs of wood.

The Japanese theatre at Yeddo was a large building, with stage, pit and gallery, and the acting was going on all day. The afternoon I visited it, they were acting some historical piece; the dresses were fine and rich, and there was a great deal of fighting on the stage, the ladies in the play taking an active part.
