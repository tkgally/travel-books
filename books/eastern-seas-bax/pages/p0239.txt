The wind often blows with great violence down the Kii Channel, while at the same time inside the Isumi Nada, or that part of the Inland Sea it leads into, and which is only a short distance from it, there may be very little wind at all. Many of these sudden changes are due to the high temperature of the Japan current that runs along the coast.

On the 5th the Admiral left Kobé with the squadron to go to Nagasaki, while the “Dwarf” was ordered to remain to carry out the duty of senior officer’s ship in the Inland Sea. We were all sorry to part with those whom we had travelled with so far and for so long a time.
