On our return to Foo-Chow we luckily came in for a fête given by the director of the arsenal, who kindly asked the officers of the ship. The entertainment consisted of a lunch to sixty-four people, then dancing in the afternoon for those that wished, while the others were at liberty to examine the arsenal. It wound up with a dinner in the evening and some capital theatricals at the French theatre attached to the establishment, the performers being the French employés belonging to the arsenal. Everything went off very well, great good taste and management being shown by the host and all the officials.

On the 14th of January H.M. ship “Curlew” arrived with five shipwrecked men from the Loo-Choo Islands, who stated that they had been well fed, clothed, and kindly treated by the natives, who had even given them a feast on Christmas and New Year’s Day, having found out that these were feast days with Englishmen.
