The “Dwarf” stuck by the flagship and we got into Yokohama together: the other two gun-vessels arrived a few hours after. All the vessels proved themselves to be excellent sea-boats, even in the very irregular sea we had experienced.

The cruise to the Russian possessions during the summer restored our ship’s company to a fine state of health, after having been much debilitated by the previous two summers in China.
