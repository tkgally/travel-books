The slopes of Fusi-yama, and the neighbouring hills and plains, are covered with a rich black soil, which is very similar to that near Mount Vesuvius, and from the luxuriant manner in which the wild grapes grew all about, I believe that the vine might be brought to as high a state of cultivation as in Europe, the climate being very much like that of Italy. The grapes that are brought into Yokohama for sale are fine, but require more cultivation before they can become valuable for making wine; however, it shows what could be done if skilled labour were employed from the vineyards of Europe, and the best varieties of vine imported. There were many miles of beautiful rich plain in the neighbourhood of Fusi-yama mountain, quite uncultivated and covered with long grass, a few farms being scattered about here and there.
