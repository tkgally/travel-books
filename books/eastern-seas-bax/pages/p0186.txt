The Russian government have established schools here for the children. One morning I noticed the girls’ school out for a walk on the road near the beach, while I was on board the ship. The order they marched in was most accurate; first walked the mistress with an umbrella, then all the girls in pairs, the smallest first, and tallest last, in the shape of a wedge; the girls wore large sun bonnets, but carried no umbrellas like the mistress. The effect was very comical.
