# Chapter VI.

[[subtitle]] Voyage to the Russian Possessions in Tartary and Eastern Siberia—The Island of Yesso.

O<sc>n</sc> the 26th July, 1873, the “Iron Duke,” “Dwarf” and “Mosquito” left Nagasaki for a cruise up the gulf of Tartary, and to visit the Russian possessions as far north as the river Amur.

We first called at the island of Tsu-sima, which belongs to Japan, and lies half-way between that country and the Corea. The harbour is large and safe, and it is perfectly land-locked. The island is hilly and covered with trees. Although Tsu-sima gives the title of Prince to one of the Daimios, the inhabitants are very poor and few in number, living by the fish they catch and a little cultivation of rice, sweet potato, and hemp. The vicinity to the Corea is its chief importance, in consequence of its making a good place for the Japanese to assemble their fleet and forces at in the event of any future invasions of the Corea by them.
