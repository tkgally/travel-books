Its structure is somewhat complicated. A series of vertical shafts, of a number depending upon the area to be irrigated, are sunk until water is reached. These are then connected with each other by underground tunnels about six feet in height. The whole system of shafts and tunnels, which may extend to over a mile in length, communicates with a main tunnel which, in its turn, conducts the water to the <i>segias</i> of the oasis which it is intended to irrigate. Wells on a similar principle are found, I believe, in Baluchistan and Italy.
