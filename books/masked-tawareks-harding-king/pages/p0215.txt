[[cont]]After arranging for our breakfast, hearing that I was still asleep, he had left a message of farewell for me, and gone off with a couple of men to meet his father, who was returning that day to his home from a visit to some <i>sheykh</i> in the outlying part of his district.

The old <i>Khalifa</i>, leaning on a stick, hospitable to the last, insisted on accompanying us to the outskirts of the oasis before taking leave of us.
