There are comfortable inns in the village here, where a tourist may well put up for a week or two, and where he even may obtain splendid fish, cooked in any fashion, beefsteak, omelettes, and chicken with salad.

The <i>torii</i> of the grand temple, rebuilt and occupied in times of old by the grim conqueror of Corea, Hideyoshi, stands in the blue sea, and so do the foundations of the entire edifice, which contains the sacred fire, and a great many famous paintings, antiquities, and other curiosities. It is dedicated to a famous Shinto goddess, named Susa-no-o, who was the mother of three goddesses, all sacred beyond conception. With the exception of the shrines of Ise, there is nothing more holy, more sacred, more beautiful, in the eyes of the Japanese than Miyajima, the island of the gods.

Sad is the hour of departure, and long will it be before I forget the beauties of the blue sea and the lovely green of the valleys of this Island of Eden.
