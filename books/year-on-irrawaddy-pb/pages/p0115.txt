While the game is in progress a steamer passes down stream. To an inexperienced eye there is nothing visible but the mast head light and the side lights on the flats, but the Skipper recognises her. “That’s the “Pekin,” he says, “Good Heavens! I wonder what the man’s made of. Eighteen hours a day is quite enough for me. But I’ll catch him up yet—the swab!”

However, there is special reason for this extra work, as we learn when we reach Rangoon.
