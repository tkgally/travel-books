The deck was so crowded with passengers that it was almost impossible to walk along it without stepping on a sleeping-mat, or stumbling amongst someone’s pots and pans. It seemed as if all the inhabitants of a Burmese village had transplanted themselves on to the deck of the steamer, and were going on living their ordinary life uninterruptedly.

Men were smoking, or chatting, or sitting in dreamy silence chewing betel-nut. Mothers were attending to the needs of their little ones. Children were sleeping, or trotting about, or sprawling on the deck amusing themselves in the way children do all the world over.
