# Chapter XI.

[[subtitle]] The End of the Trip.

By this time we are amidst the delta scenery again, where the brimming river sweeps round by thickly-wooded islands and jungle-covered banks. As the day grows hotter, one longs to leave the ship and rest in the shade of the trees where everything looks deliciously cool and quiet. But we slide by the banks so quickly that there is little time available to appreciate the scenery. We pass again the stranded steamer looking more than ever like a “faked” photograph. Surely, the sea roaring round St. Pauls would not look more incongruous than cabbage plots around a ship!

At Donabyu we drop the last pilot and proceed without one for the rest of the journey. Yandoon is the next large village that we pass—Yandoon where the “ngapee” comes from.
