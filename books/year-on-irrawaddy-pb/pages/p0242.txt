It was an interesting experience to become acquainted with several totally new and unheard-of beliefs in the course of one day. Truly, we live and learn!

I once met an earnest and devout <i>Agapemonite</i>,—but again I am tempted to digress.

We went ashore only once during the downward journey, and that was at a little village above Pagan, where we bought some curious and cleverly-made lacquer-ware.

For the Skipper the chief interest of the trip lay in watching how another man worked his ship, while I, on the other hand, found the various people on board an absorbing study.
