One moment there is sunlight, glaring and merciless, the river a brazen, blinding mass of molten metal; gold-topped pagodas sheeted in flame; dazzling white spires and vivid foliage hazy and pulsing in an overwhelming superfluity of light. Another moment, and the hurtful glitter, the keen, spiky radiance have gone. All is now suffused in a rosy glow, the water is pink, the thatch on the river-side houses is flushed with soft colour, the sky is flecked with crimson. But, before the details are realised, a change has come; land water and sky are bathed in a purple flood in which there is every shade from helitrope to violet, from slate to plum colour, from mauve to the richest royal blue.
