No intellectual advance can ever be made by magnifying differences, for “all progress lies, through opposites to their reconciliation.”

Christianity and Buddhism differ widely in many respects, but, where they agree they both converge on truth, and where they differ it is not in matters of paramount importance. They agree as to the necessity for the subduing of the baser instincts and the cultivation of the higher faculties; but they appear to vary in their explanations of “the problem of our being here,” and the problem of our going hence.

One religion asserts one thing and bases its claim to verity on ‘insight,’ the other asserts another thing and gives as its authority ‘revelation’ or ‘inspiration,’ but it is not essential to salvation that we believe in either the Story of Genesis or the Law of Dependent Origination; the resurrection of the body, or the re-incarnation of Karma; the everlasting delights of Heaven. or the eternal peace of Nirvana.
