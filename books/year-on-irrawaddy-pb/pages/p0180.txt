Whenever the ship was fogged it meant an enforced holiday for the Skipper, and he usually availed himself of it to make up arrears of sleep, but, occasionally, he found himself at a loss for something to do.

One morning he was standing idly gazing over the rail, waiting for the mist to clear away, when, suddenly, his attention was caught by two water-snakes swimming across the river.

“Come here! come quickly!” he sang out to me.

I ran forward and saw the snakes, about fifty yards ahead of us, passing across our bows.

“Now’s your chance for a shot!” said the Skipper.

I rushed for my small Winchester rifle which lay handy, and took aim at the nearer one.

<i>Bang!</i>
