More music followed, and, as was only natural when most of the men there had served their time in wind-jammers, they soon fell to singing old sailor chanties.

The Skipper led the way with,

<verse>
“Sally Brown, she’s a gay old lady.
Way, hey, heave and go!
Oh! Sally Brown is a gay old lady,
Spend my money on Sally Brown.”
</verse>

[[cont]]and all the others put as much vigour into the chorus as if they were hoisting the topsail yard, homeward bound from ’Frisco.

Then someone else sang,

<verse>
“Poor old Storm is dead and gone”
</verse>

[[cont]]a mournful chanty in a minor key, and the high, scarred cliffs of Nyounghla threw back over the quiet waters the echoes of that slow, sad chorus,

<verse>
Ay! ay! ay! boys we’ll storm a-long!
</verse>
