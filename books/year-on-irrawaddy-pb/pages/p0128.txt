So limited is the space that it seems necessary to calculate to the turn of a float on the paddle wheels how much speed to give her. It is a case of

Dead slow!

Stop her!

Back her easy!

Stop her!

Easy ahead! until all is finished. This little piece of work is recorded in the log as “Speeds various—fifteen minutes.”

Having got clear of the Pegu River, we proceed towards Rangoon with our one flat tow, and the searchlight is requisitioned in order to pick up the buoys which mark out the channel. As we near the Hastings a native can be seen waiting on a cargo flat, ready to put off in a sampan and bring the letters across to the ship.
