The stairway that led upwards was roofed over and refreshingly cool and dark, so we left the terraces and proceeded to the top of the building. Here we found the only image that was not broken or dilapidated. We saw no living person about the place, but fresh flowers were lying in front of the shrine, and the little room was swept and clean; in fact, the broom that had been recently used was in a corner, leaning up against the wall.

Little unseen bells were tinkling sweetly in the breeze outside, and, from the topmost terrace, a beautiful scene was visible. The pagoda itself, slanting outwards from beneath our feet, glistened like polished marble, till its white radiance was hurtful to the eyes.
