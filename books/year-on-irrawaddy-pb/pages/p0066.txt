All afternoon there are people bathing in the river. Wherever there is a village or town along the banks, there, there is sure to be a crowd of children, with a few girls and older women, disporting in the water. A Burmese woman’s clothing consists of a rather tight little bust bodice, a straight skirt and a little loose jacket widening at the waist and lower end of the sleeves. For bathing purposes, the bodice and jacket are discarded and the skirt is pulled up to the armpits and fastened there, making a simple covering from shoulder to knee. The wearer wades into the stream and proceeds to splash the water over her arms and shoulders, vigorously slapping and rubbing them at the same time.
