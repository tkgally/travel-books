Theirs is one ot the most responsible positions on board. In their hands, at many times, lie the reputation of the Captain and the safety of the ship. Wilful misinterpretation of an order—the turn of a spoke more or less—might mean the wreck of the ship and the ruin of its commander.

A Secunnie has three stages of existence. Formerly he was a purri-wallah who rang the telegraph, acted as messenger boy, and followed the Captain about, like a shadow, ready to execute any command. In the future he aspires to be a Serang, the proud possessor of a silver whistle, with which to direct the actions of the rest of the crew.

These men, with the donkey-wallah and the Serang of the Ag-wallahs, (firemen) are all that show any sign of intelligence. The others are, apparently, just brute beasts, who are hustled and driven about in herds, doing always the most stupid things that dawn on their vacant minds. Watch them holystoning the decks.
