It is fortunate that he does so, because, during the night, the sand cuts out, and just after dawn, a cargo steamer passes down stream. She steams past us and proffers help, but it is not needed, because her wash lifts us up off the bank, and a timely turn on the engines with a simultaneous heave on the anchor does all that is necessary. It is the work of a moment. Three orders rapped out in quick succession,

“<i>Avis Karo!</i>”

“Easy ahead!”

“<i>Dina Jow</i>,” and we are afloat in the channel again.

We meet with no further mishaps before reaching Nyounghla, where the empty flats are cast off to be filled with oil. There are other flats ready to bring down, but we have orders to go on a little further and load ground-nuts.
