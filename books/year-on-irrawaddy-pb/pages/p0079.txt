Then instantly, she disappears,—vanishes completely in less than a moment of time. It is unbelievable. She simply <i>was</i> and <i>is not.</i> In utter bewilderment I turn to the Skipper for information.

He laughs, “I forgot that you would not understand that. It is merely our shadow thrown on to the cliff by the searchlight of the steamer astern. See, there it is! Now it is gone again!”

It was undoubtedly the most solid, impenetrable, unwavering shadow I ever saw. It looked as if a good heavy hatchet would be needed to chip blocks out of it. Instinctively I had drawn back, awaiting the shock as we should crash into it. The idea of meeting a phantom ship never before seemed so terrible to me as that of seeing the ghost of a human being, but now I can understand, that, seen on the lonely seas at night, it is an Unutterable Horror.
