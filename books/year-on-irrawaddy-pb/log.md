## Decisions
- No title on the cloth cover: `cover.jpg` is the title page (a library stamp in its left margin stays).
- Divisions: contents, list of illustrations (with the note “An edition of this book is issued in cloth.”), the corrigenda slip and the book's title above Part I in the JSON only. Parts I and II nest their chapters.
- The frontispiece is bound after the corrigenda (p0016): `frontispiece` puts it at the head of Part I.
- Corrigenda: all 13 entries marked `[[corr]]` in the text (p. 125 “hold”: an *s* added by hand in this copy; p. 212 “fifty” struck out by hand: the print is followed, with the corrigenda's reading as the correction). Said in `notes_on_text`.
- Other printer's errors marked: 12 (thc, experieneed, torest, successfuly, spash, possiblity, stright, a doubled “of”, stray and missing quotation marks …). “Part. I.” left as printed.
- Plate captions with the photographer's credit in one `[[caption]]` line. Menus (p0055, p0093) and the flats list (p0121) as `<verse>` (one item a line; as blockquote paragraphs they were indented unevenly: rendered look).
- `keep_hyphens` p0165 (jungle-wallahs), p0211 (five-tiered), p0243 (deck-space).
- Assumed: period “c. 1907–10” (the preface, March 1911, speaks of three or four years on the river; no year is given).
