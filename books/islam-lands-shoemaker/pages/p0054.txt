Some few years since two foreigners left their boat for a stroll after dinner and—never came back. So to-night when two of the maids belonging to passengers on this boat started out to visit the German boat just in and but a few hundred yards below us, the captain sent a guard after them in post haste. He said it would be dangerous to visit yonder temple not one eighth of a mile away unless we went in a party, but as the moonlight is not strong to-night it is not worth the walk.
