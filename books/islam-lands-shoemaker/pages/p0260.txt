[[center]] “Gather your roses while you may.”

If you are interested in so doing, you may easily discover the ancient ports of Carthage. We passed their sites as we entered by Goletta from the sea. What funny little places they would appear to our modern eyes,—the <i>Mauretania</i> would cover them both and have room to spare for a few Roman ships on her deck. They were artificial basins and to one of our day present the appearance of a railway round-house with their amphitheatre-like structure into the cells of which the galleys—little if any larger than a locomotive—were backed for the night. Two hundred and twenty could be accommodated while above were naval store-houses.

The admiral lived on the island in the centre (see illustration) from whence were trumpeted his orders.
