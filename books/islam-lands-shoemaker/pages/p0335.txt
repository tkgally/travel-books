On our return to Batna we pause a moment at the ruins of the great Roman camp, but after Timgad they do not hold us long and we are soon off and away, turning for a last glimpse at the stately city where she crowns her hill and is guarded by her solemn mountains. Are those Roman legions or shadows moving down her grand avenue? Doubtless the place is haunted.

Between the cold rushing air and the great interests of the day we are both tired and sleepy when we reach our hotel in Constantine, but such a day was worth being both, a day to be remembered when one becomes too old for aught save dreams.
