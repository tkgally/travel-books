“Is not this the head of your uncle the unbeliever?”

“What of it? A brave soldier who fell at his post; happy is he to have fallen, his sufferings are over.”

It is said the head was fixed in a tree in Omdurman where the people stoned it.

You will look in vain for any tree there now.

It is stated that the Mahdi was very wroth at the death of Gordon, whom he really admired and respected, and that he had given orders that he was not to be harmed in any way.
