On the whole, from an Arab’s point of view, yonder Sir Rudolph does not appear to have had a very bad time, though to a European the best of lives in such a place as Omdurman must have been torture. But to return to the Mahdi.

When the time came for prayers all his gorgeousness was laid aside, and clad in humble stuff, anything but clean, he marched off, prayed and preached, then returned to the delights of the harem. As the result of his life he grew very fat and died on June 2, 1885, of fatty degeneration of the heart, surviving his victim Gordon but a few months.
