If you are interested in the reptile and insect life of the desert, it is here in bottles all around the entrance hall, where a comfortable cat will make you welcome. Across the street, under the shadow of a wall and beneath some trees, are a few benches where I pass the drowsy hours and am writing now, but the light fades and night descends, and with it the wind, which has been moaning around us all day, drops to rest.

To-morrow we leave Biskra, and while I have been disappointed in some things I am glad to have seen it, though I do not think it will ever be a great resort, because of its howling winds.
