Slatin Pasha was put in irons once or twice, was forced to run barefooted in the hot sun after the swift Arab horse of his master, was forced to adore the prophet five times a day, but all the time the Khalifa assured him that he loved him. Certainly his fate was heavenly when compared to many prisoners in Omdurman. At no time was he in danger of the tortures which would have befallen him had he been captured by our American Indians or the Chinese of to-day. They, the latter, may dig a hole in your side and sweetening the end of a hollow rod insert it in the wound and after binding you leave you to be devoured alive by ants.

The next Khalifa should be educated in China, then he would know how to properly fill his station. Apparently Slatin Pasha stands to-day nothing the worse for his experience, though Capt. A. told me last night that he thought it was beginning to tell upon him. Let us hope not.
