In the old palace there was a vine trellis where now the gates stand, and through and over this the savages swarmed in countless hundreds, crowding the court and pouring forward and up the staircase in the west wing at the top of which they encountered Gordon and Orphali.

Let the latter tell the story; it certainly proves that both fought to the end and that Gordon’s death was very different from the accounts usually given to the world, which have always struck me as theatrical and foreign to the character of Gordon, whose bravery has never been doubted and who would certainly sell his life as dearly as possible, knowing how important that life was to his soldiers and the people of the town.
