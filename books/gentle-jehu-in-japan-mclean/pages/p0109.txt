Namikawa, the most famous maker of cloisonné, has a delightful garden back of his shop and is himself worthy a visit. Unfortunately he was out of town when we called and we missed seeing the old man.

Seifu, on Gojo Saka, near Spectacle Bridge, is rapidly coming to the fore as a maker of pottery and beautiful in color and form is everything on his shelves.

Whether or not you care for Satsuma you must go to Kinkozan’s where you may see the whole process from beginning to end (you had best purchase <i>something</i> before asking for the privilege) and you are sure to come away with a lighter pocketbook.

He who does not care for bamboo had best look closely before putting himself on record, or, like myself, he may be forced to admire against his will and to his surprise find many old friends whose origin he had never suspected. Bamboo, like many other things of Japanese manufacture, is too often estimated by the rubbish seen in America, which is entirely made up for foreign trade.
