The trip down from Portland was very interesting, particularly after we crossed the California border. We had a wonderful view of Mt. Shasta covered with snow, 14, 872 feet high, and the Black Crater, 9,000 feet high, close by, made a splendid foil.

Many of the fruit orchards were in blossom and everything looked Springy. It was noon by the time we reached the Oakland ferry so we went direct to the St. Francis where they gave us a nice suite and a good lunch.

We found telegrams, books and letters waiting for us and a big pile more of the latter at Cook's office. As we had not had any, or even the possibility of getting any, for ten days, it was a treat. We did a little shopping and after walking till we were tired (which didn't take long with the sudden change to summer weather) we rode up hill and down on trolleys and finally out to Golden Gate Park, arriving just in time for a glorious sunset.
