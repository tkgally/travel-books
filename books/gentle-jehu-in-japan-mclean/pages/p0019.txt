# A Gentle Jehu in Japan

# I

[[subtitle]] Across the Continent

[[right]] <i>February ninth.</i>

IT MUST have been very foolish—everybody told us it was—with a long journey across country ahead of us and so many trains to choose from, to allow only thirty-five minutes for our connection at Montreal and that at a time when even the trains between New York and Buffalo were running four to five hours behind schedule.

The fact that by so doing we went in and out of the same depot was very tempting—and why did they claim that the C. P. R. Express would wait half an hour for us if it was not a safe gamble.

We had not been an hour out of New York when we had a hot-box. From there on one kind of a delay followed another until we began to think of the advantages of a night in Montreal and to count the number of friends who could claim to have advised us.
