Saturday morning we planned to devote to our camera and more especially to the cherry-blossoms which were quite at their best and which we wished to take home in the most realistic way possible. Unfortunately the weather-man disapproved of our project and we waked to a down-pour of rain and were forced to compromise on a Shinto Shrine, a large museum close by mostly filled with implements of war and relics of the late conflict with Russia, and a fruitless quest of “Chin” dogs.

In the afternoon we had the pleasure of lunching and attending the famous “No-dance” with a charming Japanese lady who had attended college in America and therefore spoke our language perfectly, and who very amiably acted as interpreter—no easy task, as the peculiar singing which accompanies this ancient and classical drama is mostly in an obsolete form of Japanese.
