[[cont]]new sensations, we reached the Sannomiya station which is nearer the foreign settlement than Kobe proper. The Tor is very new, has a bathroom with every bedroom, but a rather poor table. It is beautifully located at the top of a long hill and is making a wonderful Japanese garden. The hill is no disadvantage because the distances in Kobe are not great and ’rikshas cheap—a few sen an hour or one yen, fifty sen a day—seventy-five cents in our money. Single rooms with bath each, were eight yen a day, maid six yen.
