We are getting out of the regions of swamp into the regions of sand now; white banks and stretches of sand glare like snow in the fierce afternoon sun. The fine sand contributes liberally to the dust that flies into the car, with the sunbeams and the smoke from the engine, until all about us that should be white is black, and all that should be black is white.

‘Daisy darling, where have you put the pistols?’ inquires the young man, who is evidently on his honeymoon.

‘Here they are, dear, along with the quinine, and the brandy, and the camphor,’ replies the bride, lifting the lid of a basket whose contents cause us to glance at one another with some dismay.
