# The Falls of Niagara.

E<sc>veryone</sc> warned us to prepare to be disappointed in our first view of Niagara. Even those who were most enthusiastic about its wonders and beauties told the same tale—we were to be disappointed at first sight. Now Niagara to us was to be the realization of a life’s dream; and between our own thrilling anticipations, and the warnings of friends and strangers not to allow those anticipations of the first view to soar too high, we drew near our goal with our eagerness for that ‘first view’ lashed to white heat.

The crowded car bumped and rattled along the little local line that leads from the town of Niagara to the falls. It was hot, it was dusty; but we recked neither of dust nor heat.
