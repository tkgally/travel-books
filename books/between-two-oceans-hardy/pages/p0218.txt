We put up at a queer little adobe hotel, with walls about a yard thick, and not a drop of water fit to drink. A little way outside the town, a magnificent new hotel, surrounded by extensive and beautifully wooded grounds, was nearly completed. It was opened soon after our visit to Monterey, and is a brilliant success: a result surely to be anticipated, as good accommodation was really all that the picturesque old Spanish town on the shore of the Pacific Ocean needed to make it a most enjoyable and popular resort.
