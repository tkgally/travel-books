I do not know how we should have killed time that evening by the light of two dim oil-lamps, hung so high in the roof of the car that we could neither see to read nor write, had not the Judge produced a pack of cards, the beauty of the car a box of dominoes, and Tommy’s mother contributed the priceless treasure of the fifteen-puzzle. The General and the Judge got up a whist-party, and a bachelor quartette at the end of the car enjoyed an uproarious game of Poker. The rest of the gentlemen disappeared, including the British trio. I think there was a saloon in the town, for they did not return till late, warbling the melodious strains of <i>Upidee-idee-ida!</i> which idiotic refrain appeared to be popular.

The porter pulled the sliding shelves from under our seats, which make the two seats into what is called a ‘double lower berth,’ let down the upper berths, hooked up the curtains, and soon the car was a dormitory.
