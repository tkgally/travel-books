‘How fine and upright the column of spray is to-night!’ observed a strange voice at our elbow.

That broke the illusion. But yet it seemed impossible that our ghost should be only a pillar of rising and falling spray. We saw it again daily and nightly, but seldom again like that. We saw it blown along in clouds; we saw it like a great veil hiding the whole face of the fall; we saw it one evening at sunset leaping like a fountain of liquid gold: but only once again did we see it rise up in that shape—the still and ghostly guardian of the night.

No mortal eye has ever beheld the base of the great Horseshoe Fall; it is for ever veiled and lost in a wild, white chaos of foam tossed up by the fury of its headlong plunge, and hiding its depths in mystery.
