The Bill is unconstitutional—true; the Republic of Washington’s foundation, according to its original constitution, was free and open to all peaceable and law-abiding persons to enter as to leave. But the Bill has passed both houses; and the President’s veto was not maintained. California is too strong a State to defy or ignore.

I remember one day I entered a street-car which was crowded, amongst the passengers being two Chinamen. The conductor looked along the car, and, seeing there was no seat for me, reached out a long, strong arm, took the nearest Chinaman by the collar, and coolly dragged him out of the car without a word. The Chinaman accepted this without resistance; his companion as quietly followed him. Nobody in the car appeared to take the slightest notice of the proceeding; it was evidently regarded as merely the natural order of things.
