It is well that law and order in this city of San Francisco are strong—stronger than either or all of the parties that are struggling for supremacy. For there are turbulent and violent elements here; and the conflict is not over yet.

## IV.—San Francisco Life.

S<sc>an</sc> F<sc>rancisco</sc> is not a city to hurry away from. We lingered there week after week, and month after month; and as the vividness of first impressions wore off, and the city ceased to be a dream and a wonder to us, it only became more home-like and more dear.
