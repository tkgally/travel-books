One comfort is that the intense heat seldom lasts long unbroken in New York; it comes and goes in spells, and one grows to a certain extent acclimatised and habituated to it.

## II.—The City in Sorrow.

T<sc>he</sc> sunny September days melted by, and there came that not-to-be-forgotten day when the bells tolled out the death of President Garfield, and the message flashed across the sea that clothed two hemispheres in mourning. Was there a home in all America over which a gloom was not cast that day? There was but one thought of sorrow in the land; the heart of the nation beat with one regret; the word on every lip was, ‘The President is dead.’
