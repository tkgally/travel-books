# Between Two Oceans:

[[subtitle]] Or, Sketches of American Travel.

# How We Cross the Atlantic Now-a-Days.

O<sc>ur</sc> big ship stretched its lazy length sleepily along the sluggish, dark waters of the Mersey, as our little snorting tender came fussily up, like a small, pug-nosed puppy to a great, sleeping mastiff. The dingy little tender was crowded, our close ranks packed together as tightly as if we were preserved goods ‘canned’ for exportation.
