On this homeward drive we had a beautiful panoramic view of the town of Atlanta. From this valley road we saw the city spreading itself up the hillside—a rising sea of houses, gardens, clumps of trees, and shining roofs, climbing up to the hill-tops, its broad red roads running in parallels up the slope, the whole scene bathed in the golden light of sunset. All the roads around here are of a rich red earth; all the banks and vales, wherever unclad in green, all the barren hills, are of the same deep ruddy hue.

<verse>
‘The red old hills of Georgia,
So bold and bare and bleak!
They have no robe of verdure,
Stript naked to the blast.’
</verse>

These lines well describe the Georgian hills.
