# Sketches in and Around New York.

## I.—Ninety-five Degrees in the Shade.

N<sc>inety-five</sc> degrees in the shade! was the tale told by the thermometer. All day Lake Ontario lay like a sheet of blinding blue radiance, against which the little boats stood out in silhouette, so sharp as almost to hurt the eye; the sands on the shore sparkled like grains of ‘the red, red gold;’ the path of sunlight that pointed across the dancing ripples was one dazzle of diamonds. All day the sky was without a fleck of cloud, and the sun’s fierce rays fell like flame on whatever they touched. All day our life was one long endeavour to escape ‘anywhere, anywhere out of the’ heat!
