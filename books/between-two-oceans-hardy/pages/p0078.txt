However, it turned out he had lived twelve years in America, which satisfactorily accounted for it. But for his providential appearance, I believe we might have been wandering about that bewildering Buffalo dépot until now—certainly we should never have found the rightful car wherein we now arranged ourselves contentedly behind the curtains of our little boudoir of a ‘section,’ to pass the night as comfortably as is possible at ninety degrees Fahrenheit with all the windows shut.

Is there room for yet another word on the vexed question of the comparative comforts and discomforts of the English and American railway-cars?
