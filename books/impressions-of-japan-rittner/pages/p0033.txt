The gratitude she showered on us, when we reached our destination, for accompanying her down the river was touching; a prince could not have received greater homage than we received. To be of service to a Japanese is a supreme pleasure.

For three weeks we travelled inland, with no one but a Japanese coolie to accompany us; he acted as spokesman and interpreter, wherever his deficient knowledge of the English language permitted of such a thing. He was also luggage man in chief, and carried the stores for three weeks, whilst at the rate of a shilling a day he hired the services of any coolie he could find to shoulder the small amount of baggage we took with us. The wage this man received was seventy-five sen per diem, equivalent to about one shilling and sixpence, and, after being paid two evenings in succession, he asked whether we minded keeping the rest for him until the end of the three weeks.
