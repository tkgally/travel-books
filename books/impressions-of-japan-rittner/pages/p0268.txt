The legend says that Ama-terasu, when she plunged the earth in darkness by hiding in the cave, caught an image of her beauty in a mirror which her enticers held before her, and being enraptured at the sight of her own face, came out again.

The Temple of Ise is extremely plain in architecture, and an example of the pure Shinto type of building. Plain white wood unadorned with carving and paint.

Amida is the next god to attract one. Seated with his hands clasped and lying in his lap, he looks supremely pensive, with a smile on his lips. In the centre of his forehead he has a spot showing where wisdom lies. His feet are crossed, the soles turned up, and the whole figure surrounded by a halo of gold.
