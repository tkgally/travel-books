The Europeans came over to Japan and found there a country devoted to agriculture, no man rich, in our modern sense of the word, every man practically his own master to do what he wanted; a simple-minded, moral nation.

Practically, every nation that is uncivilised is moral, because they have not been taught to distinguish between morality and immorality. Civilisation opens their eyes, Europeans teach them, and missionaries attempt to suppress what they term immorality, but which in reality is only innocence.

The first settlers found their harvest in Japan, they lent money to the nation, who did not even understand the relative value of silver and gold, plundered the inhabitants, took advantage of their ignorance, and the Japanese were helpless, because they were too uneducated to see the follies they were committing.
