In Europe no man, woman, or child is allowed—except in theory—to do as he likes, and should he transgress and become unconventional, his neighbours are shocked, his friends and relations turn against him as impossible.

How different in the East, where conventionality was unheard of in the broader sense of the word. There every man thought, said, and did what he liked, dressed as he chose, and went where he wanted, no man to forbid him or call him eccentric, no formulæ upon which, if he wished to conform to the rules of society, he was forced to act. By conventionality I mean the conformability to such rules or doctrines as are consistent with the modern ideas of modern society.

By attempting to civilise a nation you destroy every spark of originality that country formerly possessed. The change may be slow, but provided that country conforms to the modernisation, the destruction is at length bound to be complete.
