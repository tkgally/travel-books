# Chapter VIII

[[subtitle]] Civilisation

As soon as a country is discovered, and in the case of Japan the discovery dates back as far as 1542, the religious question is the first that is considered. Through the medium of religion nations hope to effect the civilisation they wish to establish.

I do not mean to suggest that Japan was a land of savages, who, through the influence of Europeanism, became transformed into a nation more analogous to our own. By civilisation I mean the development a nation undergoes, whether it entails an improvement or not—the importation of new ideas and methods.
