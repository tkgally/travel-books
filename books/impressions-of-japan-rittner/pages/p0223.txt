The gain to France may never come, but yet she fears a refusal might have the effect of making her an enemy of Russia. Germany, to her shame, joined her “friends”; she, too, felt it safer to have Russia as a friend than as a foe, though I will give her credit for having another deeper reason than cowardice. Germany cast longing eyes on Kiao-Chou. She saw her chance, realised that it would be useful for coaling purposes, and thought (I believe she saw she was doing a dirty trick) Russian influence would help her.
