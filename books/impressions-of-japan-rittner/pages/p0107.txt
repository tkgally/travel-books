The <i>shizoku</i> include all, from the middle upper class to the <i>samurai</i> and <i>daimyos</i> of former times. The <i>daimyos</i> were the old feudal lords, and not by any means the only aristocracy in Japan. Apart from them the emperor had his own followers, members of the royal house. Originally these <i>daimyos</i> were soldiers, and held most of the land in the country. They were extremely proud of the position they held, carried two swords, and walked something like a German infantry soldier. Gradually they became extinct, and gave place to the <i>samurai</i>, who were also military men and the squires of Japan. From the <i>samurai</i> the <i>shizoku</i> took their rise, and now all the better class men are reckoned under that denomination.
