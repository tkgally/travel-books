# Chapter XII.

[[subtitle]] The Kumi-ai, or Associated, Churches.

I <sc>have</sc> already given an account of the formation of the first Christian church in Japan. The first two churches connected with the mission of the American Board were organized about two years later, in the spring of 1874. One of these was in Kōbe, and consisted of eleven members; the other, in Ōsaka, had seven members. Their creed was the nine articles of the Evangelical Alliance; their church rules of the simplest nature; their name, “The Church of Christ.” To this they affixed the local name, and had no thought of adding anything further.
