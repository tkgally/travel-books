<i>Christianity alone brings true repentance.</i> If the old religions bring no adequate sense of sin, how can they produce a genuine repentance? The fear of punishment is general, and often clearly defined, but a deep personal loathing of sin is practically unknown. Often repentance is the strangest and strongest element of the believer’s experience.

<i>Christianity alone awakens a true spirit of prayer.</i> If no sense of sin and no deep repentance, true prayer by sinful man is impossible. “Before I became a Christian I prayed for temporal blessings only, for health, for children, for business prosperity. I never prayed for spiritual blessings. I did not pray for others.”

<i>Christianity alone brings the knowledge that God is our Father.</i> Some sects of Buddhists teach that all prayer is useless; practically, the followers of both Buddhism and Shintoism worship many beings. Among scholars the idea of a Supreme Ruler, though not his worship, is common. Christianity alone teaches that this “Supreme Ruler” is our Father, and that we are his children.
