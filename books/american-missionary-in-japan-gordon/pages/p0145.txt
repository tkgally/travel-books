# Chapter X.

[[subtitle]] By Their Fruits.

T<sc>his</sc> is the divine test for all things,—a test ordained not only by the words of our Lord, but also by the reason divinely implanted in every soul of man. That grapes do not grow upon thorn-bushes, that fruits declare the nature of the trees that bore them, is recognized by every sane mind. Nor does the presence of tares among the wheat disprove the existence of true wheat, or deny that it grows only from good seed-wheat. What has been true of Christianity everywhere, and in all ages, is markedly true of its history in Japan; the divine characteristics of the fruit prove the divine nature of the tree.
