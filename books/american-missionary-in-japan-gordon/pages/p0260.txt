The time has now come to speak of the aggregate results of missionary effort. The latest available statistics are given in Appendix D.

These statistics, though not complete for all the missions, are sufficiently accurate to show, so far as a tabular statement can, the results and present condition of missionary effort in Japan. We have there more than five hundred foreign missionaries, and a considerably larger native force, living and preaching in five hundred different places. There are now thirty-three thousand Christians gathered into three hundred and forty-four churches, one evidence of whose faith is the gift of $72,000 in 1891 for Christian and philanthropic purposes. More than seven thousand youth are under instruction in the various institutions of learning; seventeen thousand pupils being enrolled in the Sunday-schools.
