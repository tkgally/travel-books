And of a second meeting:—

“I got there a little before six <sc>p. m.</sc> A few minutes later several young men, mostly teachers, came in, and after the usual polite salutations were ended, one of them took two or three books from his bosom and went for ‘that hairy foreigner’ with questions of every conceivable kind till after eight o’clock, when the preaching began. He had evidently been hearing objections to Christianity, and we had first a rapid examination of the first chapter of Genesis, including the creation of light before the sun, the plural form <i>Elohim</i> and its possible relation to the doctrine of the Trinity.
