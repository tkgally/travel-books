“Love your enemies.”

<i>Christianity’s doctrine of this world is superior.</i> Buddhism makes this a world of misery to be escaped from. Strictly speaking only those are Buddhists who have “abandoned the world” and become monks or nuns. Christianity makes this world the sphere of duty to both clergy and laity, and so impels to progress and reform as Buddhism does not.

<i>Christianity’s doctrine of a future life is more rational and consistent than that of other religions.</i> Neither Shintoism nor Confucianism has any clear doctrine of a future life. The oldest Buddhist books deny it altogether; the later books tell of an inconceivably sensuous heaven.

<i>Christianity alone has a Sunday; a fixed day for physical rest and worship.</i> “Sunday is a day for spiritual culture, for cleansing our hearts by meditation, Bible reading, and prayer.”
