Dr. Neesima was not so much of an orator naturally as many others of our preachers were, but his sermons were characterized by a modesty, simplicity, and tenderness which, together with his remarkable history and nobility of life, always gave them great power.

It would be a great pleasure to give in detail the characteristics of our leading preachers, not only of the older and better-known men, such as Miyagawa, Matsuyama, Ebina, Kozaki, Yokoi, and others, many of whom have already been referred to, but also of the younger men, like Hori, Osada, Abe, Homma, Murata, Harada, and others, who, with perhaps a less striking experience, are not less gifted, not less zealous, not less successful; but time will not allow of this.
