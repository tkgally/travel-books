[[cont]]for a suitable man. The request finally came to Mr. Tomeoka, the pastor of this mountain church. After deliberation and consultation he accepted the call, and in May last entered upon his duties there. Every Sunday afternoon the three thousand prisoners are marched in, and he addresses them upon Christian morality; and on Sunday mornings he has a Bible-class which between three and four hundred of the prisoners voluntarily attend. Several persons connected with the prisons have already been baptized. So highly appreciated is his work that in another large prison two other Christian “moral instructors” have since been appointed.
