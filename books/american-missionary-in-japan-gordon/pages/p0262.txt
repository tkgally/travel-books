But what shall we say of the influence of this considerable body of Japanese Christians?

As already intimated, it is a large estimate which puts the present number of Christians in Japan at forty thousand, or one in one thousand of its forty millions of people. This fact shows among other things that the thorough evangelization of the nation is still a great way off. Taken in connection with another fact, it has special significance.
