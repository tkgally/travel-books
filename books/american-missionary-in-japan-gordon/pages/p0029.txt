# An American Missionary in Japan.

# Chapter I.

[[subtitle]] Missionary Life in Japan.

“Is this ‘Japan’ on your trunk the <i>Chinese</i> Japan?”

“Yes.”

“Are you going there?”

“Yes.”

A pause.

“Going out in government employ?”

“No.”

Another pause.

“Are you a tea-merchant?”

“I am not a tea-merchant.”

A still longer pause.

“Going out in a missionary capacity, perhaps?”

“Yes.”

“Oh, then you don’t care for money.”
