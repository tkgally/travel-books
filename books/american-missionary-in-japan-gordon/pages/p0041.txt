He who takes this subordinate position willingly and gracefully on all occasions, ever believing, hoping, and enduring all things, is not likely to be troubled by any surplus consecration. If, however, he can stand this severest of tests, he will find his work and life such a continual joy that he will be able to say of it what Bishop Phillips Brooks has said of the work of the ministry in general: “In a world where there are a great many good and happy things to do, God has given us the best and happiest.”
