He, however, was still in bed, and was not likely to be up for several hours. It was very trying to be forced to remain inactive, knowing as I did that the other party could not be many hours behind me. Early in the afternoon their baggage-train did arrive, and, to my great relief, a few minutes later, the acting governor walked into my tent. He was a deadly pale young man, with dull sunken eyes, and his head wrapped up in a shepherd’s-plaid shawl. He was very pleased to see me in Akabah, he said; to which I replied with great truth that I was very glad to see him.
