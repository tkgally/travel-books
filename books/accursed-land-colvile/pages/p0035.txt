The more timid ones would prologue the agony by confidences about their prospects at Bunkumpore; but the result was always the same. I found that giving the conversation some such turn as this generally concentrated the inquirer’s thoughts inwards:—

<i>Passenger:</i> “Good morning.”

<i>Author:</i> “Morning.”

<i>Passenger:</i> “Nice breeze here.”

<i>Author:</i> “Yes.”

<i>Passenger:</i> “It will be different when we get the other side.”

<i>Author:</i> “Yes.”

<i>Passenger:</i> “You are going on, I suppose?”

<i>Author:</i> “No, I am not.”

<i>Passenger:</i> “Indeed! <i>I</i>’m going to Wakpurnuggar.”

<i>Author:</i> “Wakpurnuggar! are you? Ever been there before?”

<i>Passenger:</i> “No.”
