First of all, there is the pencil to write down the observations with, and such a thing as a pencil is not to be got in Akabah. Of course I gave him one. He was profuse in his thanks, and walked off assuring me that, until I returned, his one thought would be the care of the instrument. Just as he was getting out of the tent-door, an idea seemed to strike him.

“Ya khawajah,” (Oh, gentleman) he said, “supposing I was to drop the pencil in the sand, I might not be able to find it, and then what should I do? Also I might make a mistake in the figures which I write down, and as I should like them to be well and clearly written, I should want to write them over again, and alas! I have no paper like that which you have given me.”
