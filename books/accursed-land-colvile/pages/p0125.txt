# Chapter VI.

[[subtitle]] Description of the Wady el Arabah.

As I have said, Hammid Effendi having no objection to my going into the country as long as I left my cook and my tents at Akabah, Hammid Jad lent me three of his own dromedaries, and at daylight on the morning of December 1st I started off on my first exploration of the Wady el Arabah.

Now, having travelled hard for three weeks in order to see this place, I suppose that, having got there, I shall be expected to describe it, and I think the most satisfactory way of doing so is to run the description straight off the reel, and have done with it; begging the more frivolous of my readers (may their shadows never grow less!) to pass on to the next chapter.
