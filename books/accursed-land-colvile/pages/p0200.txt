On no point in connection with this valley has there been greater difference of opinion than on the course and destination of the Wady Ghurundel, nor is the reason of this far to seek. Looking at the valley itself from any point of view, one would unhesitatingly say that it flowed towards the north, emerging as it does from a deep ravine running nearly south-east and north-west. Standing at the foot of Rishi, on the other hand, and seeing the barriers which apparently run across the valley both to the north and south, one would at once conclude that the waters had no escape. While any one standing at the mouth of the Wady Byân after a heavy thunderstorm, and seeing the waters rushing past him towards El Ta’abah, would take it for granted that the waters of the Ghurundel escaped to the south.
