A very few minutes had elapsed before “offer accepted” was whizzing through the wires.

A few days afterwards I attended a meeting of the board of directors, and, for the first time, heard some details about the scheme in which I had so suddenly become interested.

In the year 1855 the late Captain William Allen, R.N., published a book called “The Dead Sea, a New Route to India;” and laid before the government a project for a ship-canal through the Jordan Valley. The project was not accepted, nor, as far as I can gather, even seriously entertained, and for some twenty-five years no thought appears to have been given to the subject.
