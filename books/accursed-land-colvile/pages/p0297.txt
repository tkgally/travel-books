For the first time since leaving Suez I found myself mounted on a really good dromedary of my own—a great lanky brute with a villainous temper, but with a great turn of speed, and as keen to go as a hunter after a long frost. It was quite a novel sensation to me to find myself on a dromedary which actually pulled, but I did not give him much chance of doing that, and after the midday halt I generally started off at a swinging trot, and soon left the rest of the party behind. It was a very pleasant sensation, to find oneself bowling along across this broad desert without another human being in sight.
