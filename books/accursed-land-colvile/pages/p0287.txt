This very irregularity formed a pleasing contrast to the otherwise perfect symmetry of her face and figure, and set off those lovely lips in such a way, that the holy prophet himself would have thought of sin on looking at that mouth. Many a better man than Imri had been bewitched by it.

It had been a very bad season; Imri had killed only two pilgrims, and they had proved to be people of no means, and he had not the wherewithal to go to Suez to buy provisions for the summer. His old trick of accepting the hospitality of a tribe and then stealing their camels, had long since been played out, and if he wanted to steal from his own people now, he had to sneak about the outskirts of their camp, and lie in wait for stray camels or goats.
