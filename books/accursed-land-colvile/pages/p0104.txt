In the course of the afternoon we passed some picturesque ruins of a castle situated on the little island of Kuryeh, about 200 yards from the shore. This must have been a very strong place in its day, but I could not see a sign of vegetation on it, and the Bedwin could not tell me whether it had any water supply; it certainly did not look as if it had. Near the island I was puzzled for a long time by a strange black object about half a mile out to sea.
