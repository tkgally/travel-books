# Chapter VIII.

[[subtitle]] Tawarah <i>versus</i> Alawin—A bad start—Quite an accident—Off at last—The first watershed—A bad day for the instruments—Taking it easy—El Ta’abah—Heavy walking—The Gulf of Galilee—Ain Ghudiân—Bad water—Interview with the aborigines—A pleasant ride—“Do you see Richard?”—Making pictures—A bad day for Mohammedans.

A<sc>mong</sc> all the new faces and the excitements of Akabah I had almost forgotten my old friends, Sheikhs Ali and Aïd, and their gentle, orderly Tawarah. Having conducted me safely to Akabah, they handed me over to the tender mercies of Hammid Jad, who promptly gave them a hint that the sooner they cleared out of his territory the better it would be for them.
