# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] Plan of action—Start up the wady—Sheikh Salaam—His weak point—Mekreh Hadîd—A bad look-out—Not as bad as it looked—A dromedary-race—Where is Anthonōs?—Finding the time—Hammid Jad in a hurry—Anthonōs <i>redivivus</i>—“Something to remember you by”—The Bedwin character.

N<sc>ot</sc> knowing when I might be stopped, I thought that the first thing to be done was to make sure of getting <i>some</i> information, and to push on at all hazards, and as quickly as possible, to the watershed. Once there I could take my time, and work carefully down the valley till I was stopped by an emissary of the Sublime Porte.
