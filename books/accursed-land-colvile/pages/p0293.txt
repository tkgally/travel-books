Then Imri, jumping off, began to belabour her with his pointed <i>syâl</i> stick, but he only hit her once. As the stroke descended on her head she raised herself from her knees, and for a moment Imri thought he had mastered her, but he was soon undeceived. Scarcely had she got on to her legs, when with one vicious grab she seized him by the arm, nor let go her hold till she had taken out a great slice of flesh. Maddened by her success, she rushed at Imri, who now ran for his life, and how bitterly he now cursed the very speed which had tempted him to steal her. Imri was a good runner, twice he avoided her teeth, then he felt a sharp pang and fell upon his face—she had caught his uplifted foot as he ran away from her, and bitten it clean off at the ankle; then, apparently satisfied with her revenge, she strode away towards her master’s tent at Kerak.
