Hammid Jad, I knew, never took less than a handful out of a cardboard box, and according to all traditions these two wild fellows, finding me alone and unarmed in the middle of this desert, ought at least to have annexed the whole box. They did not do anything of the sort, however, but taking one cigarette apiece, they thanked me, politely handed me back the box, and saluted, saluting and thanking me again when I gave them a light. Then I sat down, and as they remained standing, I asked them to follow my example, which they did and after smoking for a few minutes, got up, wished me good-bye, saluted again, and disappeared among the sand-hills.
