It is probably, in a great measure, to the influence of this man that the faults of his subjects are to be attributed. Imagine a London rough with a Hammid Jad as a tutor. Here is an example of behaviour of the man who rules the Alawîn.

Although the sheikh takes a large percentage of the money paid for the hire of camels, only a small proportion of animals actually belong to him. All the Bedwin are owners of camels, or, at all events, part owners of one. It is, therefore, of course, the interest both of the sheikh and his people to make the traveller take as many animals as possible.
