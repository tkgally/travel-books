The strata on the western range of the Arabah, besides dipping backward to the west, also incline to the north—the Tih being a segment of a dome—and this northerly dip, which in conjunction with the accumulation of <i>débris</i> has in turn buried the granite and the sandstone, also causes the limestone to disappear soon after Rishi is passed, leaving only the chalk upon the surface, and it is through this chalk that the bed of the Lehyanah, Jerafeh, and Jeraideh runs.
