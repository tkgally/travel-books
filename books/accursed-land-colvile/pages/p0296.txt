Our camp on the 26th was pitched under a long limestone ridge called Jabal Rishar, chiefly remarkable for the extraordinary distortion of the strata of which it is composed. From this point we passed nothing noticeable till the afternoon of the following day, when we reached the Bir Abasseyeh, a large stone tank, nearly 100 yards square, which was constructed by Abas Pasha to collect water for the pilgrims on this route. Not far from it is the Ain Tamai, which is, I believe, the only natural spring upon the route.
