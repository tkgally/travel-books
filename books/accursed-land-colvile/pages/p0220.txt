I do not know how the quarrel originated, but it seems that some years ago Hammid im Jelli, sheikh of the Beni Hammidi, killed Hammid Jad’s brother, and announced at the same time his intention of clearing off the whole of his crew. According to all the laws of Arabian etiquette it was now Hammid Jad’s duty to kill Hammid im Jelli, but thinking discretion the better part of valour, he has on the contrary always kept carefully out of his way. Judging by the conduct of his followers on this and a subsequent occasion, his course of action appears to have met with the cordial approval of his tribe.

After their severe exertions during the night, Sheikh Salaam declared that his men must have a day’s rest, and as no amount of persuasion on my part would make him change his mind, I had to accept his decision.
