“This people like the beasts, sar,” he answered; “he not pray for the God; he not pray for nothing.”

“Then what did he go down on his face for just now?”

Najm laughed.

“He dropped half-dollar in the sand.”

So it was poor old Mammon to whom Salaam was prostrating himself after all.

Najm is a man who has travelled much and had many strange adventures. For some years he carried the mail from Bagdad to Damascus, a thirteen-days’ ride (I think he said) across the desert. Once he started to go to America, but when he arrived at Liverpool, thought that he had had enough of the sea and turned back. Like Anthonōs and Yosîf he is a Maronite Christian.
