[[cont]]Western Tih. Sheikh Louefeh told me that it emptied itself into the Jerafeh.

Under the bank of one of the numerous water-courses which intersect the district I had noticed a largish heap of stones. It was in a curious position for such a cairn, which is almost always placed on a hill-top, or in some conspicuous position. On approaching it the Bedwin one after another ran at it and began pelting it with stones, and insulting it in various ways which, not being the late lamented Captain Gulliver, I should probably be called over the coals for mentioning, shouting at the same time “Yallah anhou” (God damn him). “Kool yallah anhou, khawajah” (say God damn him, gentleman), they all shouted as I came up to the cairn, but before offering up this little prayer I thought that I might as well know for whom I was praying, and why I should wish him to be damned.
