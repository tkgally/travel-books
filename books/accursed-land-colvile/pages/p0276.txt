After leaving El Kantera, the road passes through a very narrow limestone gorge, and then winds up the mountain-side by a steep but even ascent, from the top of which one obtains one’s last view of the Gulf of Akabah and the Arabah; below us, to the right, lies the Wady el Afteh, separating the limestone from the underlying sandstone, on to which formation the road presently descends in the wady.

A three-hours march brought us on to the edge of the Tih, a dreary flat of stones broken only here and there by low limestone ridges and an occasional granite peak. A collection of graves marks here the first halting-place of the pilgrims on the road from Akabah to Suez.
