Great stress has been laid by different writers on the hospitality of the Bedwin, and no doubt, as I have already said, for the sake of common convenience it does exist among themselves. The present occasion was one, I should have imagined, on which any grain of hospitality in any man’s nature would have been likely to come to the front. Here were we four people, all cold, hungry, and thirsty, and, as I afterwards heard, the two Bedwin in constant dread of being attacked and murdered, and very thankful for the protection which an imaginary revolver, which was supposed to be concealed under my coat, afforded.
