It may be urged that the same causes which have led to the filling up of the gulf would work in the same direction on a canal, and be a great annual source of expense. I grant that this would be the case were the original course of the gulf to be followed, and were the canal to be carried through the narrow Strait of Rishi, and past the mouth of the Wady el Houar. I believe this wady to be the sole cause of the silting up of the Arabah; as long as the Strait of Rishi was open the evaporation of the great inland sea which stretched from Mount Hor to the Lebanon would cause a constant current from the Red Sea, and the muddy waters from the hills, instead of depositing their burdens in the Arabah would be kept in agitation till they found rest in the deep lake which covered the northern Ghor.
