So unobtrusive had these people been, that they had left but little impression on me, and until the morning of the final start up the valley I had hardly given them a thought. A glance round, however, as I came out of my tent on the morning of the 3rd, soon showed how greatly things had changed for the worse. With the Tawarah everything went like clockwork. Quiet old Sheikh Aïd gave an order, and it was obeyed at once, without murmur and without fuss; every man knew what he had to do and did it; and the camels, accustomed to being laden, knelt down quietly till their loads were packed, then got up and walked solemnly after their leader. Now everything was reversed. Najm was swearing at the sheikhs, and the sheikhs were swearing at Najm, and at each other, and at their men, and the men were swearing at the sheikhs and at each other.
