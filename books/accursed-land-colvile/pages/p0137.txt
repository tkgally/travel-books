At a distance of thirty miles from Akabah a large wady, El Aheimeh, opening on to the east side of the valley produces again a noticeable <i>talus</i> which stretches nearly across the valley and almost joins the smaller <i>talus</i> of the Wady Musaib on the opposite side, thus leaving a very narrow channel for the outflow of the water, which is here collected in considerable quantities, as not only do a number of large water-courses open into the Arabah immediately to the north of these two wadys, but a great portion of the torrent from the Aheimeh itself flows to the northward, parts of the ground in that direction being below the level of the channel between the wadys Aheimeh and Musaib. The result is an accumulation of water, and a consequent deposit of silt, which during the rainy season is almost impassable.
