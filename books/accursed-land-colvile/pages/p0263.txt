# Chapter XII.

[[subtitle]] An economical executioner—The gulf again—The Admiralty chart—Old friends—A modest governor—Troubled in his mind—A bad day—A painful leave-taking—A little geology—El Kantera—The postman—A warning to travellers—The Houiât—Sheikh Louefeh—Abumazarîk—The cairn.

N<sc>ext</sc> morning, as I saw the men preparing to strike the camp, I took it for granted that the little misunderstanding of the previous evening had been forgotten, and thought it better to ask no questions; but just as I was starting off the sheikhs came to me to ask whether I would administer justice myself or leave it to the governor, adding that they would prefer that I should do it.
