It was not till we were about abreast of Jabal Esseik, and had entered the Wady Lamarah that we began to make any considerable rise. From this point the road made a gradual, but pretty steady, ascent, and at Narkeyeh el Fûl, a narrow and abrupt pass leading down into the Wady Ghurundel, the aneroids showed a height of 3490 feet. It was nearly dark before we reached this place, not a very nice road for camels even by daylight, and loud were the cries of <i>hôti, hôti</i>, with which the drivers warn their beasts, as the cumbrous brutes felt their way from rock to rock. I must say for them that they never made a false step, which would hardly have been the case even with the best mountain ponies.
