[[cont]]Rishar in pursuit of a hare or gazelle was more than he could stand. Off would fly Salman on these occasions, levelling-staff and all, and operations had to be suspended till the hunt was over. I only once saw Mohammoud yield to a temptation of this sort. Rishar was coursing a rock-rabbit, who, rather hard pressed, foolishly took refuge in a shallow hole within twenty yards of Mohammoud. This was more than Bedwin nature could stand, and off he dashed, and after a little scraping with his hands pulled the unfortunate cony out by his hind legs, and brought it to me alive triumphantly. Not knowing what tortures he might put it to, I made him cut its throat at once. In justice to the Bedwin, however, I must say I do not think that he is given to needless cruelty.
