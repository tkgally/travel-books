Of course a certain number of people had to be let into the secret, and very well they kept it, for which I hope they will accept my best thanks.

A few days before I started I saw in the papers that a geological expedition, under Professor Hull, had started for the Arabah, and were to be accompanied by an Egyptian escort. After the distinct refusal which the Porte had given to the request of the Syndicate, this seemed strange news, but it was none the less good. If Professor Hull could go up the Arabah, I felt quite certain that I could do the same, and if Professor Hull were prevented from going, I hoped that in the excitement which his large party would cause I might slip by unobserved.
