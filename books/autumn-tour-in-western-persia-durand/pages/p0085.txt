It was a lovely day when we started, and tired as I had been when we arrived at Ispahan I was glad to be in the saddle again. We rode out escorted by a deputation from the Prince Governor, and by a considerable number of the English community. The “Vezír Mukhtar” was again mounted on one of His Royal Highness’s horses. The Prince is a great lover of horses, and collects them from all parts of the country. Some of his Arabs and Bakhtiaris are very good.

The Bakhtiari horses, by the way, are often beyond price. Of pure Arab race, many of them, but bred in the mountains and accustomed to the roughest ground from their birth, they are as hardy as they are beautiful, and it is quite extraordinary to see the way they will carry their riders over rocks and stones. They scarcely ever make a mistake, and their legs seem to be as hard as steel.
