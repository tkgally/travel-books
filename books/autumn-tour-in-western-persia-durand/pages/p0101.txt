It seems that on the death of the late headman or Ilkhani, who died just before we left Ispahan, his sons and the sons of his brother, who had been chief before him, agreed not to dispute about the succession to the chiefship. It was unselfish and wise of them, for the tribe has suffered much in past times from family feuds. When we were there the eldest nephew of the late chief, by name Isfendiar Khan, was the head of his tribe, but his eldest cousin, the Sipahdar, or Commander-in-Chief, seemed to be nearly on an equality with him. Each had several brothers, who joined in the family councils, but mostly sat on the floor while their elders had chairs. The confirmation by His Majesty the Shah of the succession of Isfendiar Khan was, I believe, awaited.
