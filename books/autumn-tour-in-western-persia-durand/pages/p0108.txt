He was a very tall powerful man, and a great climber and sportsman, and he is much admired by the Bakhtiaris. Towards sunset we reached the top of a tremendous gorge where the work on the new road began.

So far the trade route seemed good enough for mule caravans without improvement. Now we were to see the heart of the Bakhtiari hills, and our introduction to it was certainly rough. When the Khans said goodbye to us we plunged into a gorge such as I have rarely seen. To our left was the Kuh i Sabz, a mountain topped by immense craggy precipices, many hundreds of feet in height, below which were oak forests and cliffs overhanging a rushing river, the Karun.
