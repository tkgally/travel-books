[[cont]]smaller lanterns according to rank, until at the bottom of the official ladder the lantern was about the size of a small concertina. The servants were very particular about this.

The lanterns were made of waxed muslin and wire, with metal top and bottom. A candle was stuck in the bottom piece, and the top had a metal handle which a servant slung over his arm. The light resisted much wind and rain.
