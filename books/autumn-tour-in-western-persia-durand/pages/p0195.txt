His supporter and cousin, Mir Mata Khan, was equally black in appearance, but walked instead of riding, and his eyes and teeth were even merrier.

Each had a good Martini rifle slung over his broad back, and a row of cartridges round his waist.

At Balarud these chiefs came to us and asked us to get “their blood” from a man who had lately killed one of their clansmen, and taken refuge with a neighbouring chief. “Khun i má bigirid” they said—“Take our blood.” They told us that they were very anxious about our safety, and were going to take great care of us. The Governor they said had warned them that if anything went wrong with us they would be flayed alive. Evidently they did not regard this as an empty threat.
