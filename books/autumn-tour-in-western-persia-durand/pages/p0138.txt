My husband found him lying in a state of collapse, and dosed him on the spot. I don’t know what the medicine was, but it was conveyed in a teacupful of old Scotch whisky from Dalgairns’ of Dundee, and was greatly appreciated. The man said he felt better at once, and next day he was well enough to march.

We often noticed that a large quantity of whisky seemed to be required for the bruises on the horses’ legs. No other medicine was regarded by our servants as nearly so efficacious.
