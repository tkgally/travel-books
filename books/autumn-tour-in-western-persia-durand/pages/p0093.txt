We diverged at the village of Kharaji from the route followed by Mrs. Bishop and General Schindler, striking out to the westward between the Jehan Bin and the Kuh i Shekh, a precipitous isolated hill which is kept as a game preserve by the Bakhtiari Khans.

By the way it is very curious to hear the Persians of the north pronouncing their long vowels. I remember being asked in England what we called the capital of Persia. I said “Tehrán.” The answer was, “Oh, that is all very well in Persia, but you don’t call it that in England.” I said, “Why, what do you call it in England?” “Oh, we call it Teheeran.”
