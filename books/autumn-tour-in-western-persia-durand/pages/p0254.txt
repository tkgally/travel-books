Some years ago, when the Shah’s brother, the Zil es Sultan, held the Governorship of Irak, he broke the power of this turbulent priest, who fled to Tehran, and the land had rest. But afterwards he found means to make his peace with the Persian Government, and was allowed to return to his home. “And now,” the villagers said, “we cannot sleep at nights because of him.” Doubtless since that time he has been again reduced to order; and doubtless the story told by the villagers did not suffer in the telling.
