Mounted mostly on well-bred Bakhtiari mares, with very blood heads and powerful quarters, these tribesmen would gallop wildly along the stony slopes to right and left of us, firing with their Martini carbines at any prominent mark they could fix upon. If nothing else offered, one of them would throw down his flat-topped felt cap, the typical Bakhtiari head-dress, and his friends would gallop past and fire at it. I am bound to say the cap rarely suffered much except from the fall, for the shooting was erratic. Sometimes they would shoot across the line of march, and once Mr. Rennie was splashed with mud from a wandering bullet, which struck the ground close to his horse. But it was all very picturesque and charming.
