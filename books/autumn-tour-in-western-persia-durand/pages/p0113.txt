# Chapter VII

WE left Dopulán on the 26th of October, and had a long march to our next halting-place, Sarkhun. It was a difficult march, up and down very steep hills, where Messrs. Lynch had tried hard with their limited means to make a passable mule track. They have, I believe, only five or six thousand pounds to make a road a hundred and fifty miles long. Now and then we came to a bit of soft ground, with open forest glades, and here one could have driven a coach and four, but as a rule it was very rough travelling; quite practicable for mule caravans, but steep and rough.
