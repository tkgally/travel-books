[[cont]]meant to make us laugh, and made me more inclined to cry. It ended:

<verse>
If you do not send an agent
To take over this here pageant,
You will find I am a goner,
In this sad and dismal corner.
</verse>

Mr. Rennie left with him a good filter, to give him drinkable water and tea, but we could do nothing else for him, and it made us unhappy to leave a solitary Englishman in such a place. I suppose British trade needs such “pageants.” He was selling iron and sugar and candles, and “upholding our commercial supremacy.”
