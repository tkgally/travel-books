The next day’s march was toilsome, as we had to cross two ranges by the Lesser Kialan and Dalich passes. As we were scrambling over the former, Mata Khan said to us, “We Lurs call it the Bukan, but in the world it is known as the Bacha i Kialan,” or Child of the Kialan. In the afternoon we got into our camping place at Ab i Sard, or Cold Water—a place fitly named. It was raining hard when we marched in, and it continued to rain the whole evening and night, a real deluge which turned the camping place into a swamp, inches deep in mud and water.
