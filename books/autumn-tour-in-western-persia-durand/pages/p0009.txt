# Chapter I

ON the afternoon of Tuesday, the 26th of September, 1899, we rode out of the picturesque gateway of the British Legation at Tehran for a long tour on horseback in Western Persia.

We had arranged to start the day before; but, as is often the case in Persia, the muleteers or “chárvadars,” who were to supply mules for our baggage, did not appear, and we had to wait for them. However, they were gradually hunted up from the bazaars, where they were enjoying themselves with the money we had advanced them, and we managed to make a start within twenty-four hours of the time fixed.
