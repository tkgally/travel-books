Somewhere about here is the boundary between the highlands and lowlands, between the Lur country and the Persian Governorship of Burujird. But there are many Lur villages in Burujird.

We had a long climb down to the valley, and then crossing the river we came to the village of Chulan Chulan, where very comfortable quarters had been prepared for us by the land owner, a pleasant and hospitable gentleman. Though it froze at night the climate seemed delightful to us after the cold of the mountains, and we revelled in the luxury of potatoes for dinner.
