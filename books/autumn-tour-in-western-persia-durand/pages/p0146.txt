My husband and Mr. Rennie went off in the evening to call on the Khan. Poor man, he was very depressed. The locusts had eaten everything in the valley for six years in succession, and his revenues were gone and his people suffering. He talked about Layard, as every one still does here, nearly sixty years after Layard’s visit to the country, but he was not the descendant of Layard’s old friend Taki Khan. I wonder if any one reads now the story of the Bakhtiari chief and his brave beautiful wife. It is a very touching story.

How it rained that night! Storm after storm, until we were almost deafened with the noise of the rain on our tents and the thunder on the great Mangasht range to the north. In the morning it was still raining, and before we started my husband sat in the tent and made a pencil sketch, in our copy of Layard’s book, of the fort as it seemed to us.
