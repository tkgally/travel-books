“They say the Standard Oil Company is trying to get control of the field already,” comments Frothingham.

“Well, oil is here all right,” asserts the host. “The Pitch Lake people have shipped one tank-steamer full and are building sixteen big thirty-five-thousand-barrel tanks. And they don’t usually spend any money foolishly—except what they give for revolutions in Venezuela.”

“We aren’t like Venezuela,” says the Major virtuously.
