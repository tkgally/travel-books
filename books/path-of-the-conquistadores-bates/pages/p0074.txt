Against the party that desired to carve up this vast territory into a number of small sovereign States loosely confederated, Bolivar threw the whole weight of his vast influence. He pleaded before the Congress: “I have been obliged to beg you to adopt centralization and the union of all the States in a Republic one and indivisible.”

The Congress wavered and then sided with Bolivar. There was decreed a unified Republic, including what are now the Republics of Venezuela, Colombia, and Ecuador. Of this Empire, named Greater Colombia, Bolivar was chosen the first President.
