It was taking a risk, but a necessary risk, and one that was completely justified by the result. For Barriero, the Spanish general, conceiving that he must fight for the defence of Tunja, gave Bolivar battle at Boyaea and was utterly routed. Barriero broke his sword across his knee and surrendered, with many officers and some sixteen hundred men. The Patriot army had to mourn the loss of only thirteen killed and fifty-three wounded.

Everywhere now Bolivar was victorious. He marched to Bogota, from which Samano, the Spanish Viceroy, fled.
