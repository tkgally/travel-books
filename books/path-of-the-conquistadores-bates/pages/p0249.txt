The city of Bolivar looks far less weird in the daylight than it did in the night. A tree-shaded walk along the bank where the band plays in the afternoon stretches in front of the Calle de Orinoco, the main business street.

The river sweeps by below with a rapid current, for the shores converge sharply here, giving the town its former name of “Angostura”—the Narrows. A big rounded rock breasts the current in mid-stream.
