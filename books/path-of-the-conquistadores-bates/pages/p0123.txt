We pass a tall figure of a man with ample robes and a caste mark on his forehead who does not deign to notice us—a Hindu priest. Coolie women, their faces half covered with silken shawls and their arms laden with silver bangles, hammered out from the English shillings which represent the savings of the family, glide gracefully by. What a contrast are their lithe slender figures, in gracefully draped robes, to those of the negro women, in cheap ready-made skirts and bodices, who, shapelessly bundled together, waddle clumsily along! Some of the coolie girls are really beautiful, though they invariably spoil the effect by a nose-ring.
