# VI

[[subtitle]] On the Llanos

SEÑOR ALVARADO accompanies you to the hotel to work out the expedition. He is the traditional Spanish gentleman, grave, dignified, soft-spoken, of punctilious courtesy. He explains in fuller detail the expedition which El Presidente has arranged. You are to go to his estate at San José, some seventy miles southward on the Garapo River, in the heart of the plains. There you will hunt and be shown the life of the Venezuelan ranch-owners. Food is to be carried packed on a burro for the stretches where no supplies are obtainable.

“But most of the people along the road are relatives of mine,” adds Señor Alvarado.
