Mattey shouts lustily into the darkness of the night, calling certain names.

The camp consists of a dozen shelters of palm thatch, each built between two trees and having a hammock stretched underneath. Fires are alight in three or four places to drive away the mosquitoes. The head of a huge fish is roasting on a framework of saplings. We sample a piece of it, and also the red berries lying in a gourd on the ground.
