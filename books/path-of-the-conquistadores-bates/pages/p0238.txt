We inspect next day the falls of the Caroni, set in the tropic forest, one of the most beautiful sights possible—“that wonderful breach of waters,” Raleigh described it. We take the Commandante and his guitar along and the Spanish-German youth.

On the way we break a mirror, and return to find that our gasolene will not take us to Bolivar and that the reserve supply expected on the returning “Delta” has not come. A telegram says it is on the way in a sailing vessel. Five days’ dead loss, waiting at San Felix, is the significance of this.
