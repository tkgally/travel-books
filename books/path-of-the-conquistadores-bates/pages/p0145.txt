“Beastly country, just the same,” insists Robertson, the merchant. “They have an extra customs tax of 30 per cent. on all goods which come from Trinidad. Castro put it on and Gomez does not take it off.”

“You can never get your guns in, anyway,” cautions the cocoa-grower. “The Minister of the Interior is the only man who has the right to issue permits for firearms, and he always refuses to do so. They are so afraid of revolutions.”

Evidently Venezuela is an interesting country. Also, all this advice is worth considering. You sit back and ponder as the critics one and all leave the hotel. Mr. Jefferson turns as he goes: “Over there is a man who can tell you enough about the Orinoco. He is just back from Ciudad Bolivar.”
