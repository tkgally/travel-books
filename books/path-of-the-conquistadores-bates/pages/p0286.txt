“At Los Castillos, the commander of the fort was going to fire on the ‘Apure.’ But the American captain trained his cannon and sent word that on the first shot he would blow the fort into little pieces. So Castro’s men did not fire. And we got food.”

“Et ça goûtait bien, parbleu!” adds Señor Vicentini.

The Presidente has sent a request for you to come at four o’clock to his house. A tall, very dark Venezuelan gentleman with a thin eagle nose and a full beard is there—Señor Ygnacio Alvarado. General Telleria introduces you. Then he announces the purport of his summons.
