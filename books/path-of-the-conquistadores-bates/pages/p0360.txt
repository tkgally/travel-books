[[cont]]and the boxes are not full. At Trinidad they cost 10 cents, and that is dear enough. But can you start a factory here? Not much! because a distinguished official in Caracas owns stock in the match factory which makes these <i>fósforos</i>, whose heads fly off and burn your clothes. Salt sells at Bolivar with the largest official discount, which, by the way, you do not get, for a cent and a fifth a pound. At Curaçoa it is quoted at three-tenths of a cent. But can you sell salt? Not much! It is a Government monopoly. The distinguished official in Caracas owns three-fourths of the stock in this steamer company, which has the concession for selling salt. And this Compania Fluvial has the sole right to navigate the Macareo passage. Any competing line has to go up the Pedernales passage and spend twelve hours longer getting to Bolivar, if, indeed, it does not run onto the bar and stick there like the ‘Apure.’”
