## The Jessie Willcox Smith Edition

## ‘Twas the Night Before Christmas

[[image: circular vignette of two small children in nightclothes sitting on Santa Claus’s lap, inside a holly-cornered border]]

Every one will welcome with delight this exquisite new edition of the poem so dear to all hearts.

It is too familiar to all of us to need any description or quotation. Every one has seen it, read it, and owned it, in one of the many attractive editions of it which have been issued from year to year.

But no one has ever possessed it in so delectable a guise as this present one. Miss Jessie Willcox Smith, always felicitous in her portrayals of child life, has never been quite so happy in picturing children as in the illustrations which she has made for this edition. Young and old will welcome it.
