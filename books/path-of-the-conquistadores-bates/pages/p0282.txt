The Cuban friend of Mattey’s is giving some lurid details of the United States intervention there. “Why, the whole trouble was started for five thousand dollars,” he says; “I know, for I got some of it. Cuba had twenty million dollars in her treasury and the Yankees wanted that. They spent the money on roads, framing up deals with the contractors, and then they evacuated the country. It was all nonsense about the negroes or the Liberals stirring up the row.
