“Something must be wrong,” says Fitzgerald. Nobody contradicts him. “I think it is the spark-plug,” he adds. He unscrews the spark-plug. Nothing seems to be wrong there. Joe turns the wheel some more.

“Charlie, you come and turn the wheel!” shouts Fitzgerald. Charlie is about seventeen years old, a mixture of Chinese, negro and white in an unknown ratio. His arms are skinny, and he is far less strong than Joe, who is an able-bodied wharf-rat. Charlie’s performance at the wheel is not a success. Joe has to try again.
