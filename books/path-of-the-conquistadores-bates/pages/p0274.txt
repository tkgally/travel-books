“Now only a week ago some peons on an estate of ours found a cave with a doorway to it, bricked up. They started to break the door down, but got frightened of ghosts. I have planned to go there and enter. We may find nothing—we may find a treasure. People don’t go to the trouble of bricking up a doorway for nothing. I am afraid of snakes, but not of ghosts.”

“The mention of your ghosts,” says the Venezuelan, “reminds me of a veracious tale about a peon near our estate who met a veiled figure on a lonely road.

“‘Who are you?’ said the peon tremblingly.
