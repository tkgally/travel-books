[[cont]]But it is all part of the show. The handkerchief descends intact in a little parachute from the ceiling. Immense relief and thunderous applause from the rather nervous audience. Bows from the Professor and music by the orchestra.

A long <i>entr’acte</i> follows, during which the row of piccaninnies look with open mouth at the ceiling whence the parachute fell. The Professor is not crowding attractions. He opens the curtain a little and beckons to Fitzgerald, who goes in behind the scenes. The captain is soon back grinning. “The Professor says there have been paid only two pesos. These people have sneaked in from behind.”

Fitzgerald makes himself a collecting agent, and by the help of a dollar of yours gets the pot up to five pesos. The landlord with an improvised bar is doing a thriving trade, meanwhile.
