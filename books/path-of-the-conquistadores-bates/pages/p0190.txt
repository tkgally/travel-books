You crawl beneath the mosquito bar, dead tired, and fall asleep despite the bites. It is not for long, however; in three or four hours you wake. The net is full of the pests, who have either found the meshes passable or have located an entrance underneath. Your hands and even your body, covered by the thick khaki cloth, are raw with their stings. Only the utter exhaustion of the last two days enabled you to sleep at all. Fitzgerald is already up and seated by a smudge. Haggard in the grey of the morning, with bleeding face and hands, he looks as one newly carried from the torture-chamber.
