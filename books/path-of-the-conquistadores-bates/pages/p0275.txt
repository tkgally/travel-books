“‘I am the devil,’ a voice answered in sepulchral tones. The peon walked up and held out his hand.

“‘Embrace me, <i>amigo.</i> I married your sister.’”

Señor Palazzi smiles broadly; Señora Palazzi is a little piqued.

“A disgracefully ungallant story,” she says. “I will tell you a better one, and true, too. It is about a young girl, a Caraceña, sixteen years old, who lived with her grandmother. I will not give the real names, though you will know them. We will call the girl Señorita Dolores Blanco. The grandmother belonged to an old Caracas family which had some fine rare port, dating from the time of the Spaniards. The good dame was stingy and would let no one partake of the closely guarded treasure.
