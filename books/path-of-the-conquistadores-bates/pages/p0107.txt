“He never had to unravel a lawsuit between two time-expired East Indians,” says Major Bridges.

“He never tried to make cocoa-planting pay with negro labour,” grumbles Frothingham. “Those negroes are not worth a penny. If it weren’t for the coolies there would not be a white planter in Trinidad. It is bad enough as it is.”

“Cocoa-men are always grumbling,” says the host. “How would you like to have had sugar and to have seen your values wiped out by foreign beet-root subsidies? Why, you cocoa people and the coco-nut growers are all capitalists!”
