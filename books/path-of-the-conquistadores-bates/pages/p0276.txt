“At once a sickening nausea came over her. Her lips blanched, her eyes became glazed, and her face took on an ashen hue. The grandmother, who had come in, snatched up the empty bottle. It was marked ‘Death to Vermin’!

“Dolores was carried hastily to bed and a doctor was summoned. Only with great efforts he was able to save her life.

“When at last she was out of danger, the family group around her bed plied her with questions.

“‘Why did you drink the poison,’ they asked; ‘did you want to die?’
