At length the “Delta” reappears from across the river and toots her whistle. Most of the townsfolk have come to watch her off. They stream down across the naked earthen bank of the river to the water-line. Friends are out in force to speed the parting guests. M. Mattey is escorting his Cuban confrère, who leaves by this boat. Señor Aquatella has a large shipment of balatá. The American Consul is anxious to get some cigars sent up from Trinidad, and has to commission the Scotch engineer to bring them for him.
