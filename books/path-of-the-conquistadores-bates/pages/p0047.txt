“Every mountain, every stone in the forests of the Orinoco shines like the precious metals. If it be not gold, it is the mother of gold.”

Meanwhile word was constantly carried to Europe of the riches of Guiana. Francis Sparry, left behind on Sir Walter’s expedition, captured by Spaniards and taken through much of Guiana, drifted back to England.

“In the province of Guiana,” he testified, “is much natural and fine gold, which runneth between the stones like veines. Of which gold I had some store, but now the Spaniard is the better for it.
