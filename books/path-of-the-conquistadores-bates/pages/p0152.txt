“You have forgotten the wives of the Commandantes and the Jefes,” suggests Scott.

“Good! I am glad you reminded me,” says Fitzgerald. “Add candy in jars for them. Now put down two dozen bottles of rum for the minor Custom House people and the boatmen; they can’t get along without rum.”

This completes the bill, and you put the list away. Fitzgerald gives a most improper wink and sighs luxuriously, for dinner has been completed and we are sitting on the hotel piazza sipping bad coffee and smoking good cigars. Across the road are the telephone lines of the city.

“Did any one ever tell you how the first telephone in Trinidad came to be put up?” asks Fitzgerald meditatively.

You have not heard, and neither has Scott.
