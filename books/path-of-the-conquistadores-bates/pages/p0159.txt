There is nothing for it, however. Fuel must be provided and gasolene must be carried. It is passed aboard while you stand aghast. The whole floor of the launch, save a small space beside the engines, is piled as high as the seats with gasolene tins and other goods. The Custom House authorities will not let gasolene be loaded even from the dock. The launch has become a very floating powder-magazine.

With many misgivings, you climb in and perch on the cargo. The two boys that compose the crew let go the moorings and you are off. “Be careful in the Serpent’s Mouth,” calls Captain Hunt, of the Customs. He shakes his head and goes back into his office on the dock.
