The old General offered me a commission in the Regulars if I would go to the Philippines with him. But a tornado had struck Porto Rico, and there was a lot of contract work to be done on the island, so I resigned; I wish sometimes I had stayed in the service.” Being a little downcast, he gets out the mouth-organ again.

In due time we are off Cedros Point, that long, narrow neck of land which pointed to the Conquistadores the way to the Orinoco and El Dorado. Venezuela is not in sight; we pass the point and enter the Serpent’s Mouth. The tide-race of which Columbus wrote to the King of Spain is marked only by ripples.
