“Some of the chiefs get their appointments; and at once their enemies flee to Trinidad to escape alive. The new officials take their goods. The men driven out are crazily angry and desperate, and ready to join the next revolution.

“The slick lawyers get to the new President, and say such and such a one is not fit to be Commandante of the promised port. ‘He is a good fighter,’ they allow, ‘a <i>guapo</i>, but he is a rough neck. He cannot fill that job—put in such another.’ So the President tells his officer who had the pledge to wait a little while, or he appoints some one and says it is only temporary, or he offers something else.
