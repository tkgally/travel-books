The hotel regime is printed on a notice-board. Coffee is at seven, breakfast at eleven, tea at four, and dinner at seven. In effect, you are put on a two-meal basis, staving off mid-afternoon pangs with tea and toast. As breakfast is over at twelve, which hour is already rapidly nearing, it seems desirable to indulge now, calling the meal lunch, to justify eating at this time. So you go out on the veranda, which serves as a dining-room.
