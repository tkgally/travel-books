“I took a sergeant and two men and started for Bolivar. A mile out the sergeant told the Turk to get down and tighten his saddle. Then he shot him through the head. One of the soldiers had a shovel, so we buried him and went back. That is what is meant by ‘shoot him if he tries to escape.’ They were content in Bolivar and promoted me.” He takes a gulp of the warm beer.
