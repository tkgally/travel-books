“My father was a Spanish merchant,

The resourceful lover promptly asks if she would refuse him if he offered his hand. She answers “No, sir,” and they all live happy ever afterwards.

Fitzgerald is entertaining. He doubtless feels twinges from a conscience somewhat battered by ten years’ knocking around South America, for he exerts himself to make you forget the troubles of starting and the overloaded powder-magazine on which you are reclining and smoking Jamaica “Tropicals.” Helped out by a ball-bearing imagination and a few drinks, his memoirs become truly worth their cost.
