“It is candy, of course,” says Fitzgerald indignantly. “Everybody will understand.”

The launch plugs away into the night, and at length you fall into an uneasy sleep on the cushions. Shortly before dawn you wake. There is a sound of voices. Joe is explaining something in an insolent drawl and Fitzgerald is swearing in an eminently capable manner. Land is nowhere to be seen. Fitzgerald turns indignantly to you. “This damn fool boy has steered us into the middle of the Gulf of Paria instead of going south along the coast. We ought to be at Cedros Point now, and Heaven knows where we are.”
