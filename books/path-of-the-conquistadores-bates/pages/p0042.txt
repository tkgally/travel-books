Ten secular ecclesiastics and twelve Observantin monks joined the adventurer. The cacique of Marequita, which bordered the Orinoco near where San Felix now stands, came to Cumana at about the same time with a mass of golden images to trade. This event and the story of one Juan Martinez, who said that he had been captured by Indians on the expedition of Ordez and had been taken from town to town until he had actually reached “the Imperial and Golden City of Manoa” and had seen the “Inca of Guiana,” inflamed the party to the highest point. De Berrio’s expedition started from Marequita southward into Paragua.
