## Pilgrim Life in the Middle Ages

[[center]] By SIDNEY HEATH

[[center]] <i>Author of “The South Devon and Dorset Coast,” “Homeland Churches,” etc.</i>

This book is designed to serve and entertain the general reader who is interested in the religious pilgrimages of olden days, of which so many memorials remain throughout England. After general remarks on relics and shrines, hermits, anchorets and recluses, flagellants and dancers, there are chapters on pilgrim’s costumes, tokens, badges and itineraries, holy wells, the shrine of Becket, pilgrim inns, Winchester to Canterbury, etc. The book is fully illustrated from old prints and from sketches by the author, and attractively bound in purple cloth.

## Through the Heart of Africa

[[center]] By FRANK H. MELLAND and EDWARD H. CHOLMELEY

This is an important book upon the Dark Continent, written by two British explorers of more than ordinary ability and courage. The story of their adventures makes fascinating reading, while the results of their explorations and investigations throw important light upon many geographical, ethnological, and political problems. With 80 illustrations from photographs.

[[center]] $4.00 <i>net.</i> Postage 12 cents.
