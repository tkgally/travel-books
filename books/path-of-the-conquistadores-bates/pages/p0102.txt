We leave the Savanna drive and its promenaders and turn to the left into the Maraval road, past a straggling negro settlement and into a wooded valley under the hills. The road runs between huge clumps of bamboos, in many places shading the way like a tunnel. Humming-birds flit here and there, the sacred “Iere” of the now extinct Carib Indians who welcomed the old Conquistadores.

A delightful coolness fills the air, scented with the odour of a multitude of flowers. The contrast to the blaze of midday is luxuriously appreciated.
