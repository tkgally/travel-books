## Around the Clock in Europe

[[center]] By CHARLES F. HOWELL

[[image: night scene of a crowded open-air café on a city boulevard, with an advertising kiosk and street lamps]]

This is an original and cleverly planned volume, and should prove one of the leading travel gift-books of the year. It is made up of a series of impressionistic sketches of European capitals at the most characteristic times of day, as follows:—

| 1 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 2 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Edinburgh. |
| 2 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 3 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Antwerp. |
| 3 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 4 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Rome. |
| 4 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 5 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Prague. |
| 5 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 6 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Scheveningen. |
| 6 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 7 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Berlin. |
| 7 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 8 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | London. |
| 8 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 9 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Naples. |
| 9 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 10 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Heidelberg. |
| 10 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | 11 <sc>p. m.</sc>, | Interlaken. |
| 11 <sc>p. m.</sc> to | Midnight, | Venice. |
| Midnight to | 1 <sc>a. m.</sc>, | Paris. |

The book is illustrated with line drawings, head pieces, full pages, etc., and issued in attractive holiday style.
