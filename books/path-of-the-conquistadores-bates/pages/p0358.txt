“And graft, graft!” he continues. “Mining machinery is free of duty by the Code, which is ideal on paper. But you have to pay the Customs first and then get the money back. A miner up here a while ago paid four hundred dollars in duties, and before he got it back he had expended one hundred dollars for stamps and fees. Why, up at Callao we send telegrams instead of letters, because the dispatch fees go to the salary of the local Commandante, and he has to be taken care of. It is all right so long as you spend money in Venezuela, but God help you if you make any!”
