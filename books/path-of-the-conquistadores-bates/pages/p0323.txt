Down by the kitchen to the left a sow, followed by half a dozen pink pigs, is rooting beneath a lime-tree. A flock of blackbirds wheels in the sky and passes. Guinea-fowl and chickens wander up and down the piazza. A tiger-dog comes and pokes his nose into your hand. A white turkey gobbles in emulation of the peacock.

On the adobe railing in front of you is a row of saddles, while bridles, surcingles and straps are hanging on the posts. The wall behind is pasted up with advertising lithographs of girls’ heads, amid which is a cartoon of a man who sold on credit when he should have sold for cash.
