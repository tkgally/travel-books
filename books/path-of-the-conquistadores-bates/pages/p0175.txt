“It is lucky that blighted engine has not balked again,” you remark. “We would be on the rocks in ten minutes if it did.” Hardly are the words spoken before the engine gives a couple of gasps, starts convulsively again, gives a last dull explosion, and—stops.

One does a lot of quick thinking at such a time. If the boat goes to pieces on those reefs to leeward, can we swim athwart the current to Soldado, or will we be swept past it and have to swim the six miles to Venezuela?
