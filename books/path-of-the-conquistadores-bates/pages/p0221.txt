“Der Schweinhund!” says Herr Dude. “Never mind, he can’t steal my donkey-engine. The cook will give him plenty bananas and cassava. I take him.”

Fairly late the party breaks up. Joe is left like Dido on the bank. The captain is able to navigate the “Geraldo” to Mattey’s lumber-camp, a mile up stream on the right bank.
