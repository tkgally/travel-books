## Egyptian Days

[[center]] By PHILIP S. MARDEN

[[center]] <i>Author of “Travels in Spain,” “Greece and the Ægean Islands,” etc.</i>

An indispensable volume for the intending traveler, rich in entertainment and humor. Mr. Marden’s aim has been to provide a book containing the indispensable information, and at the same time so to guide the traveler that his further reading may be carried on to the best advantage.

The book is illustrated from more than two score excellent, freshly taken photographs of Egyptian scenes, and with a number of helpful maps. It has a strikingly beautiful cover.

[[center]] Boxed. $3.00 <i>net.</i> Postage extra.

## The Path of the Conquistadores

[[center]] By LINDON BATES, JR.

[[center]] <i>Author of “The Russian Road to China,” etc.</i>

Mr. Bates’s book deals with life in Great Britain’s tropical colony of Trinidad, with Latin American society, sport, and adventure in Venezuelan Guiana, and touches upon the political and commercial conditions of the region about which the celebrated boundary dispute of some years ago took place.

[[center]] Illustrated.
