Here a Corsican from San Felix breaks in with the statement: “I know Mannoni’s family in Corsica, and all the men were hunters and soldiers. So the revolver must have come from his great-grandfather.”

“The Germans had the first cartridges with their needle-guns; that is why they beat us in 1870,” says M. Mattey.

The argument is still going on in detached fragments when the divers merchants leave the table to go down to their business.

An envoy from General Navarro, a little, weazened Venezuelan official, arrives somewhat later in the afternoon.
