At length Mannoni’s Hotel is reached. The brother of the proprietor, who has recently finished his time in the French Army, and threatens to go back because, he says, “There are not enough pretty girls in Bolivar,” leads the way to a perfectly clean room looking out on an airy palm-planted courtyard. Three shower-baths are just around the corner.
