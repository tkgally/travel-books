Almost no new blood has been brought in since, and as no care has been taken in breeding, here, where the grass is not particularly good, the cows are rangy and thin, and give but little milk.

A wild bull is to be slaughtered in your honour for fresh meat. Early in the morning, after he has been driven near the house and cornered, a peon has ridden up with the raw-hide lasso fast to the horse’s tail and has caught the victim. He stands now lashed to a tree by the many turns of the raw hide around his horns. A savage look is in his eyes.
