“Ah, so monsieur is going on the ‘Delta’?”

“But no, upon the launch of Monsieur Fitzgerald!”

The Baron’s face goes pale. “That launch! Why, it is only of two tons; you do not know what it is to cross the Straits, the Serpent’s Mouth—it is to die.”

The Venezuelan exile, Carrera, comes up the hotel steps.

“He is going up the Orinoco on Fitzgerald’s little launch,” appeals the Baron. “C’est se suicider—let him ask Vicetella, of the Navigation Company.”

Carrera tactfully shrugs his shoulders and says nothing. But a moment later he draws you to one side.
