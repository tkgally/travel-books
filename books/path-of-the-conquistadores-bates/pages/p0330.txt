Bats fly around in the house and hang from the thatch. Rats run about under the end room where the stores of rice and beans and the Doctor’s medicine chests are kept. Parrots and blackbirds swarm in the trees outside, and here nest the little birds that give warning of serpents.

Near the house is a hill of those strangely civilized communities, the parasol ants. For 200 feet you trace their line of march, one rank going to the nest carrying fragments of leaves, which fall back over their bodies as if the creatures were shading themselves from the sun.
