On the excuse that the eight missing sailors had been murdered by the Spaniards Raleigh surprised Port of Spain and slaughtered its garrison. Then, sending Captain Colfield with sixty men and following himself with forty, he marched to St. Joseph, stormed it, and captured the Governor, de Berrio.

The latter was carried up the Orinoco in the hope that he might be able to supply information. This first expedition of Raleigh’s was, however, an utter failure. The falls of the Caroni prevented a passage up its stream. The tropic jungle was impenetrable. Raleigh returned to Trinidad, released de Berrio, and sailed sadly home.
