“Qué maravilla!” exclaims the Commandante.

“Es posible?” asks El Capitan.

“Yo le aseguro à Usted que es la verdad, palabra de caballero,” says Fitzgerald without the quiver of an eyelid—“on his faith as a cavalier!”
