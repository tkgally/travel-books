“They are <i>blagueurs</i>,” he says; “last month they showed a picture of the Calle Babilonia here in Bolivar with the rebels of Matos’s revolution, and labelled it ‘Mexican insurgents at Juarez.’”

“Did you ever hear of how Palazzi saved us from starvation in that Revolution?” asks M. Mattey.

“Was that Señor Santos Palazzi?” you ask.

“Si, si; Santos Palazzi, whom you know,” says Señor Vicentini.
