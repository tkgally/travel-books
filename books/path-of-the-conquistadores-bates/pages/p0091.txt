Sleepily a half-breed official pokes around in the bags. If one admits having fire-arms they go into bond until a licence is secured. All is over in five minutes, and you are free of Trinidad.

The gateway from the custom-house is blocked by a disorderly mass of riotously vociferating negro hackmen, strangely clad in raiments ranging from antique liveries to brown overalls, with battered top-hats or straw sombreros perched indifferently on their heads. From them you are rescued by a neatly-uniformed half-breed chauffeur. Your luggage is crowded onto his machine, which gradually works clear of the dock and into Marine Square, simmering beneath the morning sun.
