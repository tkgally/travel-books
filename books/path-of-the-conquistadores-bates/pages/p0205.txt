In good time El Capitan wakes and we introduce ourselves. The process is like the old nursery rhyme about the kitty:—

<verse>
“You pet her and stroke her and feed her with food,
And kitty will love you because you are good.”
</verse>

“Will El Capitan sample some Trinidad beer?”

El Capitan will “con mucho gusto.”

El Capitan finds the beer drinkable and the cigars smokable. He accompanies his <i>amigos</i> up to El Commandante. El Commandante finds the beer drinkable, the cigars good, and the clearance papers in perfect order. He returns with us to the war-vessel for dinner.
