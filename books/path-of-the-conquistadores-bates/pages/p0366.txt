“There is another,” adds the promoter.

“The lame one?”

“Yes, the lame señor. The most popular man in Venezuela. I have heard from the inside that he is to be President when there is the next trouble. There will be an intervention by the United States and an entirely free election, with inspectors, and he will be elected, and he is an absolutely honest and patriotic man. Twice before he could have been President, by treachery, and he would not. I should like to see the Presidente of the State of Bolivar go higher. He is most diplomatic. He says, ‘The roads are my monument.’”
