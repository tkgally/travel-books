The <i>señor</i> has been so good as to invite you to his estate at San José, so that you may see something of the interior of Guiana. He is leaving in a day or so. Would you like to go?

You are rather taken aback. The Presidente has manifestly issued to this grave <i>señor</i> a royal request that you be invited. Of course, however, you will be delighted to accept the kindness. It is not a chance to miss.

The Presidente asks about your outfit—a poncho, a mosquito bar, a hammock?

“Give me the pleasure of being allowed to attend to the equipment and to the provisions for the road,” says Señor Alvarado in quiet dignity.

“How about a horse?” asks Telleria.

“Perhaps for the comfort of the trip a mule is better,” says Alvarado. “I can get one from Montez.”
