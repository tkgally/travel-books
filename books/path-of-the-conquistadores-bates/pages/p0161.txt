“Is there a chart?” you ask.

“Yes, yes; I have one,” he says.

But a lengthy search fails to produce it. It has gone overboard or been left, or is buried hopelessly in the inextricable mound of luggage.

Now the engine stops, a mile from land, and we toss about in the trough of the waves.

“Joe, come back and turn this fly-wheel,” orders Fitzgerald.
