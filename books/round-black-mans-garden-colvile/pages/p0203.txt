## III.

Although we were now in the Hova province, and within a forced march of the capital, the forest-track, so far from having improved, was the worst we had yet met with. The succession of ups and downs seemed to be endless, huge fallen trees blocked the path at every few hundred yards, and the slipperiness of the track was too much even for my porters, who once or twice came down on their knees, without actually, however, giving me a fall. The belt fortunately was of no great width, and by eleven o’clock we reached Ankèramadinika, a village on its edge, and the frontier military post of Imèrina proper.

The character of the country now completely changed.
