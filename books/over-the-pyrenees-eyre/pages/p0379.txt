Yet I will not close these pages without again saying that I have seen much that will never be forgotten, and that no scenery I ever beheld equals the romantic wild grandeur of the Spanish Sierras. Every artist ought to visit Spain, for it offers a combination of scenery and grouping, and colouring, and peculiar effects of light and shade, which would be to him a mine of inexhaustible novelty and wealth; and though I should not care greatly to see any of the cities, no, not even Sevilla, la maraviglia; if I had father or husband to protect me, I know nothing I should like better than to ride on mules along all those wild Sierras, from one end of Spain to the other.
