# Over the Pyrenees into Spain.

# Chapter I.

[[subtitle]] Through France to the Eastern Pyrenees.

T<sc>he route</sc> usually taken to the Pyrenees is not the most interesting or varied in scenery. As I hope this book may serve as a guide to future travellers, I must therefore be permitted to say a few words about my journey through France.

Dieppe <i>viâ</i> Newhaven, across a sea of azure blue varied by shades of white and lapis lazuli; not a soul ill on board, and the day so calm that the English and French coasts were both visible at once, which the sailors told us is not often the case. I slept at Dieppe, and went to Rouen the next morning; but ah! I went too late.
