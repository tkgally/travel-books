You, oh, oaks! when big enough, will be cut down to form ships, in which men may traverse the ocean. Your sides, oh, pines! will be pierced that your sap may flow out, and afterwards you will be cut down and burnt for firewood. So it will be with the other trees; and the willows are lopped every few years that baskets may be made of their branches. As to me, in a few months I shall be rudely despoiled of my fruit, and my boughs torn and broken by the vintage gatherers; but I mourn not, for my fruit will be made into rich wine to strengthen the heart of man.
