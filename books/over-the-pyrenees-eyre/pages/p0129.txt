In the filthy little inns between the Hospitalet and Andorre, I noticed the beams of the ceilings, and the outer frame round cupboards, were thus decorated. It is equally customary in way-side <i>ventas</i> in Spain.[[fnref *]]

Fortunately for me, a French schoolmaster, sent by Government to learn Castilian or <i>patois</i>, was lodging also in the inn. He civilly became my medium for making my wants known, and was also obliging in accompanying me in the only two country walks I took while in Andorre. I wished to see the church, about a stone’s-throw from my <i>fonda</i>, or inn, and thought I might venture so far alone. No such thing; I soon had a crowd of ragamuffin children bellowing and screaming after me, and was obliged to return to my quarters.
