How loaded the air was with the scent of all those meadow flowers, literally heavy with honeyed fragrance; it was like breathing honey instead of eating it; and how reviving the scent of the rain-moistened earth and the trees!

Every now and then, as I walked along, the thunder rolled like a peal of artillery amid the mountain-tops; now and then there was a flash of sheet-lightning; twice, the lightning played above my head like an aureole, and all round my hat shone flashes of blue and pink flame; but as I felt I was unhurt, I was not frightened. The Romans had a belief that, when lightning thus played on a person's head, or struck a house without doing harm, it was an omen of good fortune; but what good fortune can come now to me?
