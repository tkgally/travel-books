# Dedication.

I<sc>n my</sc> P<sc>reface</sc> I thanked the public for their favourable reception of my last book. I dedicate this new one to the F<sc>riends</sc> to whose kind interest much of its success was owing. By far the greater number of these were the old friends—many the playfellows of my childhood, as well as the friends of my maturer years; but to both I owe a deep debt of gratitude, and wishing to express my thanks equally to all, I dedicate this book to them in a Round Robin.

[[image: A round robin: a circle divided into segments radiating from the centre, each segment bearing the name of one of the author's friends]]

From one who will never forget the kindness and the help she received from them in her direst need.

[[right]] MARY EYRE.
