I went back to the driver. ‘Had he been to the Fonda del Oriente?’ ‘No, señora, al bagno.’ The mischievous guide whom I had not employed, had sent me to the wrong omnibus,[[fnref *]] and I should not be able to start that day for Madrid, and should perhaps lose my luggage. Back I raced to the Fonda, called the valets who were lounging in the court, explaining what they knew, that I could not explain myself in Spanish; and had lost the omnibus, and wanted one of them to help me to find it. No one stirred—I had left the Fonda. At last I saw the secretary; he accompanied me to the Rambla, and, racing as fast as we could, we caught the omnibus on the point of starting. One place was vacant, into which I jumped with Keeper, and we started. I presume the object was to cause me to lose my place, and be obliged to remain at the Fonda del Oriente; but what short-sighted policy!
