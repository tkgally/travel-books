Altogether, it was a very neat, pretty costume. I was told the first came from Galicia; where these last girls came from, I could not learn. Everyone I asked, replied, ‘Oh! somewhere in the mountains!’ It is astonishing how often the inquiring traveller is disappointed in learning a thing by the <i>insouciance</i> of the natives of a country! How often, both in France and Spain, I asked in vain for the name of a mountain or river of everyone I met!
