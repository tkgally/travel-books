# Chapter XI.

[[subtitle]] Madrid to Granada.

I <sc>left</sc> Madrid by the eight o’clock train for Venta di Cardenas, where, since the railroad goes no further, though the line is gradually advancing towards completion, one has to take the diligence to Granada. We arrived here by daylight, and there I realised the full horrors of Spanish travelling. There is no station house built as yet, though there is a station, and no accommodation for travellers after a night’s journey, but a wretched <i>venta</i>, or inn, built of wood, where one may indeed get bad coffee, and something to eat; but where one cannot get a clean towel to wash one’s face with, or any other necessary comfort.
