He had not even stood near me when I took my ticket; and I took no money but what the ‘Jefé’ gave me into my own hand the second time, and he claimed no three francs until I returned from the ticket-office the second time. I suppose the ‘chief’s’ imposition suggested his; and the chief durst do no other than support his allegations, lest he should betray him. It was well for me he claimed no more, for I had to pay it to avoid trouble. ‘It is a shame,’ said I to the interpreter, who had so kindly helped me. ‘It is a theft, no doubt,’ answered he, but the <i>gend’arme</i> was obliged to force you to pay, as the lad swore you had taken his money; and it was better to do so than to have a delay; he saw you were a respectable person, and was as civil as he could be’—as indeed he was. ‘Yes,’ said I, indignantly, ‘but how travellers are cheated in Spain!
