I do believe he had got them on purpose for me, but after what I had seen,[[fnref *]] I could not have swallowed them.

‘You will not eat in my house,’ he exclaimed.

‘No,’ said I, ‘I am not hungry, but I will drink of your wine, for I am thirsty;’ for I did not wish to offend him or pain him. ‘You know I ate nothing yesterday until night,’ and I filled my flask and drank. I told him of the ring I had left behind me at the <i>venta</i> where I had slept, and he wrote down that the ring was to be sent to Señora Eyre, care of the English Consul at Barcelona, to whom I had brought a letter of introduction.
