But the real truth is, the Spaniards are inhospitable by nature, and hate strangers.

A young Englishman of high family, an intimate friend of my friend, Mrs. Brotherton, the well-known writer for ‘Temple Bar’ and most of the popular magazines of the day; and the authoress of the clever and successful novel entitled ‘Respectable Sinners,’ travelled also in Spain some years ago. How did they treat him? Exactly as they treated me!

He was a very clever, a very elegant, and a very handsome young man; but he was much out of health, and out of spirits. In Madrid the people in the streets used to hoot him, and they even followed him to his hotel, shouting and yelling; and calling him ‘the knight of the rueful countenance.’

To return to the Alhambra. I can compare the carvings on the walls to nothing but the finest point lace. If actual point lace were laid upon a flat wall, the effect would be somewhat similar, only that the patterns would want the depth and shadow given by the chisel.
