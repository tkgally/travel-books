# Chapter XII.

[[subtitle]] Granada and the Alhambra.

M<sc>y</sc> inn was in a line with the Prado or public walk. I first went there. I can hardly convey to my readers even a faint impression of its exceeding beauty. For some distance it is merely a long walk, shaded by trees with seats beneath them, and flanked by buildings on either side; a little higher up, the trees are larger, and full grown; elegant marble fountains decorate the spaces between them; and gardens full of lovely rose-coloured oleanders and other gay flowers replace the rows of houses on either side.
