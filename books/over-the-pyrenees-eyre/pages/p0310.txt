In Spain you can’t cry ‘Waiter, bring me a cup of coffee, or a plate of grapes; I am unwell, and cannot eat meat.’ You must wait till it pleases that waiter to bring it; and as fruit was invariably served last, at all these <i>ventas</i>, I had no chance of a meal; for scarcely had it made its appearance, when the <i>mayoral</i> called us to resume our places in the diligence; and then, I was charged more than my companions who had dined or supped, for the dinner or supper I had not eaten. I suppose I was mulcted as punishment for scorning Spanish fare.

About three <sc>a.m.</sc> we reached Granada, and my heart beat quicker;—the dream of my childhood was about to be realised—I should see the Alhambra!
