It is the fashion in Spain to call people by their Christian rather than their surnames, and even his men never called him anything but ‘El Capitan’ or Julian. And here, although I know all my men critics will fall foul of me for so doing, I must record my inmost conviction that men are not at all grateful for an industrious, hard-working, active, managing wife. They rather prefer a doll, whose sole vocation it is to smile and look pretty, and whom they can pet and protect, and dress out, and check like a child, and who never takes the liberty of thinking for herself.

Alicante looked very pretty from the sea—a white, glittering city, backed by verdure and girdled in by mountains. Indeed, the whole coast of Spain that we sailed past was beautiful, with its long wall-like range of mountains, whose bases sweep down nearly to the shore. Spain is, in fact, one of the loveliest countries on the globe.
