But, on reflection, he finds that God has so overruled the nations that, by the influence of a variety of motives, they are all kept away from it, and prophecy is fulfilled—the land is desolate and overthrown by strangers. When Russia, some years ago, would have blotted the Turkish Empire out of existence, England and France interfered. England will not suffer France, and France is not content for England, to hold Palestine. For a long period the Ottoman Empire has owed its continued existence, not to its own means of resisting either external or internal foes, but to the mutual jealousy of England, Russia, France, and Austria, who keep the decrepit state alive, because they cannot agree what to do upon its death. It was one of the principal grounds of that long-continued opposition which Mr. Cobden maintained against Lord Palmerston, that his lordship was disposed uniformly to uphold the Turks.
