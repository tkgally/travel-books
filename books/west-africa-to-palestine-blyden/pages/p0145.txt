Abeih, one of the central stations of the Syrian mission of the American board, lies upon the western slope of Lebanon, at an elevation of 2,300 feet above the sea. It commands a most splendid view. Beirût is constantly in sight, and, on clear days, Mount Carmel and Sidon on the south, and the island of Cyprus on the west, can be seen. Dido lived in these parts, and she spoke of some of these places to Æneas,—

<verse>
“Teucrum memini <i>Sidona</i> venire
——Genitor tum Belus
Vastabat opimam <i>Cyprum</i>.”[[fnref *]]
</verse>
