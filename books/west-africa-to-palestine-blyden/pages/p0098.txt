All these things crowded into my mind; and everything in the scenery through which we passed seemed to call up incidents in Sacred Writ.

In the vegetation which presents itself there is a pleasing and curious variety. There are palms, oranges, lemons, mulberry, pomegranate, olive, lime, grape, cactus, sycamores, gorgeous roses, and gigantic oleanders. I was so deeply interested by the novelty of everything, and at the same time its seeming familiarity—as if I had lived for years in the land—that the five hours between Alexandria and Cairo passed away almost imperceptibly.

But there was one drawback to my enjoyment—the incessant cigar-smoking kept up by my fellow passengers in the carriage.
