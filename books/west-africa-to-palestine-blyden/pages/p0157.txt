Not a few sink under the intense and unremitting labour from which, once in the field, they find it difficult to restrain themselves. The operations of this mission extend from Sidon to Tripoli, and includes the whole of the Lebanon. The principal stations are Beirût, Abeih, Sidon, and Tripoli, with twenty-one out-stations. They have nine ordained foreign missionaries and sixteen native helpers, not including some twenty-five teachers. The great work accomplished by this mission has been the translation of the whole Bible into Arabic. The translation was commenced by Dr. Eli Smith, some twenty years ago, and completed after his death by Dr. Van Dyck. It is said to be admired even by Moslems for the purity of the style of the Arabic.

A college has recently been established by the members of this mission, to be under the control of American and English Christians. Rev. Dr. Bliss is the President.
