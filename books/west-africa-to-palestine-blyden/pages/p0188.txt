<i>The Leader begins</i>—On account of the palace which is now deserted:

<i>The People reply</i>—We sit here lonely and weep.

<i>Leader.</i>—On account of the temple which has been destroyed:

<i>People.</i>—We sit here lonely and weep.

<i>L.</i>—Because of the walls which have been destroyed:

<i>P.</i>—We sit, &c.

<i>L.</i>—Because of our glory which has departed:

<i>P.</i>—We sit, &c.

<i>L.</i>—Because of our great men who are mingled with the dust:

<i>P.</i>—We sit, &c.

<i>L.</i>—Because of the costly buildings which have been burned:

<i>P.</i>—We sit, &c.

<i>L.</i>—Because of our priests who have gone astray:

<i>P.</i>—We sit, &c.

<i>L.</i>—Because of our kings whom God has despised:

<i>P.</i>—We sit here lonely and weep.
