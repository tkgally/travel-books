“This remarkable rock, occupying the greater part of the inner area of the mosque, is irregular in its form, and measures about sixty feet in one direction, and fifty in the other. It projects about five feet above the marble pavement of the mosque, which is itself twelve feet above the general level of the enclosure. At the south-east corner of this rock is a descent, by a flight of steps, to an excavated chamber, irregular in form; its superficial area being about six hundred feet, the average height seven. In the centre of its rocky pavement is a circular slab of marble, which, being struck, returns a hollow sound, clearly showing that there is a well or excavation beneath.”[[fnref *]]
