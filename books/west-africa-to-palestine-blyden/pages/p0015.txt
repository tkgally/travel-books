# From West Africa to Palestine.

[[image: Ornamental rule]]

# Chapter I.

To one who, living on the West Coast of Africa, has formed the purpose of visiting Egypt and the Holy Land, one of two ways in which he may accomplish his objects presents itself. There being no direct opportunity by sea, he must either make his way across the continent <i>via</i> Soudan, Abyssinia and Nubia, or he must proceed to some seaport in another country from which there are vessels leaving for the Mediterranean.

Having made up my mind to visit the East, I long pondered the question of <i>route.</i> After mature deliberation I chose the sea.
