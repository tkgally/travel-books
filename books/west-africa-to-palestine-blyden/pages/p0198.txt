# Chapter XII.

A<sc>las</sc>! for the country of kings and prophets—the birth-place of Emmanuel, the Prince of Peace!

<verse>
“Land of fair Palestine, where Jesus trod,
Thy ruins and thy relics tell of God;
Thine everlasting hills with awe proclaim
The holy records of Jehovah's name;
Thy fallen cities, crumbled into dust,
Pronounce the judgment of Jehovah just.”
</verse>

When one is at a distance from Palestine, unless he makes the present condition of the country a subject of careful and special study, it seems but a small matter what political power holds possession of it.
