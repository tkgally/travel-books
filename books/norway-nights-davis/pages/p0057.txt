# Chapter III.

[[subtitle]] The Gudbransdal.

BEFORE leaving Christiania we planned our chart of travel with the aid of Mr. Bennett, an Englishman long resident there, who is a sort of Cook's Agency amplified and improved. He rents vehicles for posting, marks out routes in accordance with the time and taste of the inquirer, furnishes guide-books, has a large assortment of antiquities for sale, and is withal so friendly and obliging that he is very popular. Our time was limited, for we had also Sweden and Russia before us, and an engagement at Bayreuth at the end of two months.
