[[cont]]flasks were offered by the excited spectators, as well as sundry disapproving remarks to the reckless boy, which received no reply except “I must have looked jolly comical stuck in that icehole!” He was too plucky to admit that he was half dead with exhaustion; but as soon as the midnight coffee was swallowed he and his faithful dog disappeared to the cabin for many hours. When there was a general promenade over a glacier, the following day, the officer in attendance ordered two sailors to follow that young man and not lose sight of him for a moment. By the laws of the steamer company the captain is required to wait two days for a passenger detained by casualty; but this is an inconvenience to be avoided if possible.
