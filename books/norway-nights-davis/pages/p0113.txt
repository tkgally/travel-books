And yet in this awful solitude a few human beings live and move and preserve their being. We saw two cabins on different mountains, almost overhanging the ledge like eagles’ nests. The small grassy plateaux on which they stand are at least two thousand feet from the black flood below; an apparently perpendicular path and a tiny boat forming the only means of communication with the world. A cow that grazes on the mountain-top, and perhaps a couple of goats, furnish milk. Goats and babies are tethered to the threshold or the rocks, but adults have occasionally by a fatal misstep been precipitated,
