This superb church may well be considered the supreme effort of modern architecture; unlike all other great cathedrals, which were the crystallization of centuries, it was the work of only forty years, under command of the Emperor Nicholas, who did more than all his predecessors to beautify his capital. In order to secure the safety of the foundation, forests of piles were driven into the marshy ground on which it stands, followed by great blocks of granite: and yet it gradually sinks.

Through the kindness of the lady whose acquaintance we made on the <i>Constantin</i>, we obtained privileged places within the choir, one Sunday morning, during an important ceremonial.
