Two hundred years ago this river was unknown except to Finnish huntsmen in the untrodden forests through which it flows from Lake Onega to the bay of Finland. It is as broad as the Rhine at Cologne; its clear, blue waters are not only a beautiful feature, but they supply the wants of the city. At the same time they are a perpetual menace, for, though guarded by massive granite embankments, they are only two feet below the level of the streets; and when in the spring the north wind blows a gale, from the narrow part of the bay the waters of the Neva are forced back, and, if the ice happens to be breaking up at the same time, inundations are inevitable.
