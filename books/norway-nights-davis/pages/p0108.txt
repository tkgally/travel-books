At last the tardy steamer, with little to do and a great deal of time to do it in, puffed around the promontory, and we were soon ensconced for the next ten hours in a favorable position for studying the fiord and its confluents. The views were very varied, of green meadows with background of mountains traversed by the inevitable waterfalls, uplying farms, tiny red hamlets on the shore, quaint-looking people, and fishermen in quiet nooks, with their log-houses perched on ledges of the cliffs. We crossed and recrossed continually, taking on and putting off cargo, and parties of peasants who were a study in manners and dress.
