It costs from four or five rubles to fifty a pound (a ruble being generally equivalent to seventy-five cents or three shillings sterling, but in the present state of Russian funds a third less). <i>Tchai slamonum</i> is made from an inferior quality of leaf; but we found it very palatable—a harmless beverage, as universal as beer in Germany and <i>vin ordinaire</i> in France.

## The Cathedral.

The world-renowned Cathedral of St. Isaac’s stands conspicuously in a large open square, surrounded by several of the finest edifices and monuments in the city. A few words will recall the many descriptions given of it by clever writers.
