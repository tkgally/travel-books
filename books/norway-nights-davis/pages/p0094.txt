The whole household inspected as the work went on, and suggested [[corr snndry|sundry]] additions behind the foreground and beyond the linear perspective which if followed out would have compelled me to tear down a wall and make the drawing from the outside.

After the road leaves Stueflatten it descends by a series of rapid zigzags, and within a distance of seven miles there is a marvellous succession of beautiful waterfalls, some of them tumbling between perpendicular walls of basalt rocks until they culminate in the grand Slettefos.
