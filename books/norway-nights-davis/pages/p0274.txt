Our suite of rooms had a balcony which overlooked a square thoroughly Oriental, save for the lack of turbans and burnouses.

This part of Moscow is the <i>Kitai Gorod</i>, or Chinese City, so called because within its walls merchants formerly sought shelter for the treasures they brought from China and other foreign sources. The Slavianski Bazaar was probably a prominent point of traffic.
