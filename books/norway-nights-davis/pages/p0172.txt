Our party returned from their scramble up and down the Cape, wet, tired, and in high spirits, having dreamed no dreams, save of white bears, of icebergs, and of all the bristling ramparts from which the Polar Sea defies science and curiosity. Let us hope that this one secret may remain a secret—“One, by Itself, singly, everlastingly <i>Alone!</i>”—lest man should sit down and weep that not another inch is left for him to conquer.
