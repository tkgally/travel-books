We arrived Sunday, the morning of the 27th, at Hammerfest, an unimaginably quaint place and the most northerly town in the world, although tiny settlements have tentatively crept still nearer to the mystic Pole. The wayward streets straggle around the harbor, which is crowded with unfamiliar craft manned by Danish, Norse, and Russian sailors, brawny and sometimes picturesque, lolling on piles of lumber on shore, pipe in mouth, or taking on cargo. The nature of the cargo reveals itself—the ubiquitous Cod is master of the field; he is manufactured into oil, he hangs on lines along the harbor, is packed in tumuli on the shore, and is a Smell forever, near and from afar.
