Add to this the ornate signs above the shops and the same gay simulacra as at St. Petersburg of fruits, vegetables, wines, etc. The shrines for holy images with perpetual lamps before them are more profuse, and the genuflections and signs of the cross more noticeable, perhaps because the streets are not so wide and the crowd is larger.
