We landed the first day on an island named Torgnet, which is surmounted by a lofty, precipitous rock called Torghætten, from its fancied likeness to a colossal hat or hood. The rock is about a thousand feet high, and perforated in the centre by a natural tunnel or cavern through which appears, as a picture in a gigantic frame, the sky and a group of islands on the other side. This remarkable orifice is five hundred feet up the rock, and one of the amusements of the voyage is to scramble up and look through it. After a few hundred yards over a flower-sprent, marshy meadow, the ascent is rough and steep, up and over great stones and <i>débris</i>, and through pools of water.
