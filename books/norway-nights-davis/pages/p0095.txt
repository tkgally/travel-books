As we stood there, a tiny hand holding a bunch of wild-flowers was suddenly held before us—only a hand and nothing more, till we turned and saw an elfin creature not more than six years old, who had scrambled down from a grassy knoll among the trees. Imagine a life bounded by the cabin above and the torrent below!

But we must pursue our road. Again and again, fringes of silvery threads all adown the stately hills!
