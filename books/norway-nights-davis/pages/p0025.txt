Here we first heard the language of Denmark and Norway, rather blunt and unmusical, and with so slight analogy to any other that memory retains it with difficulty. A few nouns and phrases for travelling purposes are of course desirable, and well worth the trouble of acquiring. In fact, it is better to take a good deal of trouble rather than endure the humiliating sense of helpless idiocy in a foreign land.
