Among the regalia now used at coronations is a girdle of large diamonds; a sceptre containing the great diamond Lazaref, one of the largest known; the emperor’s crown, made entirely of diamonds, a row of immense pearls, and an uncut ruby an inch long. The empress’s crown is equally rich, but smaller, and is fastened on with diamond hair-pins. A curious relic is the chain of Michael, the first of the Romanoffs, composed of ninety-nine rings, each of which is engraved with one of his titles, accompanied by a short prayer.

The beauty of workmanship in all these baubles is as remarkable as their value.
