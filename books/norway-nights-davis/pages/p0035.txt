The prettiest-mannered little thing in feathers hopped on the back of a chair close by our table, cocked his head on one side inquiringly, but without even the chirp of a petition: evidently a reconnoitring party sent out to scan the resources and disposition of the enemy; for when an amicable volley of crumbs was projected towards him he flew away, but presently returned with half a dozen of his tiny brothers.
