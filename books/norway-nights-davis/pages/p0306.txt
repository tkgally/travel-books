It found its way hither from Kief in the twelfth century, is believed to have forced the flight of Tamerlane from Russia, and its jewels, estimated at £45,000 (one emerald alone being worth £10,000), gleam in the twilight of the edifice with an almost supernatural light.

At the approach of the French army all the most precious articles from the churches were secreted by the priests; but nevertheless the soldiers carried off from this one alone five tons of silver and five hundred pounds in gold.
