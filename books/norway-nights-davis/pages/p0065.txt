Why even <i>That</i> should desire to hurry through picturesque Norway is past comprehension. It is hard upon the poor farmers, who are obliged by the government to furnish horses to travellers in lieu of certain taxes and at fixed and rather low prices, and particularly disadvantageous on some of the by-roads, or “slow stations” as they are called, when in the harvest season the horses are urgently needed in the fields. Along the principal arteries of travel the farmers club together and establish regular “fast,” or fixed, stations, where they always keep in reserve a sufficient number.
