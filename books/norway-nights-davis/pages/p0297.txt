Very interesting is the <i>Terem</i>, or suite of rooms in the upper stories set apart for the wives and children of the Tsars, to which the ascent is by a narrow twisted stairway with carved stone balustrade; the rooms are small and vaulted, ceilings and walls overlaid with ornate and elaborate arabesques; red predominates in one room, blue in another, green in a third; frescoes intermingled present sacred subjects, and the narrow painted windows repeat the mural colors. The light as it came through them was dim and cloistered even that summer day, and must have been lugubrious indeed to the royal ladies who were once restricted to these narrow limits.
