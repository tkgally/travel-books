It pleased us better, with perhaps an instinct of the kind embodied in the proverb—<i>Reculer pour mieux sauter</i>—to first turn southward. Thus, after receiving a benediction in Cologne Cathedral, we glided slowly up the river which is so vain of its castles and clamorous of its legends, to gay, complacent Frankfort; thence, turning northward again, we paused at Cassel, which redeems its commonplace streets by its Auegarten, one of the most beautiful public parks in Germany, dignified by superb oaks and a winding river,—and which also boasts of an admirable picture-gallery, and of the castle and park of Wilhelmshöhe where Napoleon III. was a reluctant “guest” after his fatal defeat at Sedan.
