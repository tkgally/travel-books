Their tiny cribs were ranged in two long rows down the centre of the rooms; and in front of them stood two rows of nurses, wearing short white sleeves, many strings of beads on their bare necks, and high white caps from which hung streamers of red or blue ribbons. Russian nurses wear blue when the baby in charge is a boy, and red when it is a girl. These peasant-women were in <i>grande tenue</i> that day, as it was Sunday, when visitors are expected (the every-day dress is doubtless less coquettish), but their irregular Calmuck features were stolid and unresponsive.
