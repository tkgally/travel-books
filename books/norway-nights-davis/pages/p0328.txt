The familiar details of the tragedy are recalled by the traveller with painful realism.

We returned to St. Petersburg for a day, and then took the rail for Berlin. As far as the German frontier at Eydkuhnen, twenty-four hours, the carriages, the stations, and rail afford entire comfort, though the scenery has no interest; the remaining twelve hours the railway is very rough.
