We were told that the family relations of the Lapps are very kindly, and that they are a happy, contented race, though inferior to their neighbors, the Finns, in mind and appearance. I noticed that one ot them who was fishing near our steamer was not allowed to come on board to receive a proffered gratuity from a passenger. It was handed him from the gangway, no doubt from aversion to closer contact. So much for our narrow prejudice in favor of soap and water! On the whole, our visit to the Lapps was one of the most interesting incidents of the voyage, and we came away with considerable respect for a people who, imprisoned two thirds of the year in darkness and storm, and browbeaten by desolate mountains, preserve the graces of contentment and good-nature.
