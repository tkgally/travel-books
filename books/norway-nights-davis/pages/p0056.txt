Even ships “may rise on stepping-stones of former selves.” Perhaps a thousand years hence the skeleton of one of our ocean steamers may survive—a barbaric toy, when the now-anticipated glories of electric motors shall have had their day, and some more powerful agent may offer summer excursions straight to the Pole itself.
