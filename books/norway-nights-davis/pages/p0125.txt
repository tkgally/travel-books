This delightful trait includes honesty of speech and of deed, and is the nearest approach to the window in the heart which the satirist of Olympus declared wanting in Vulcan’s primeval man. One remembers with indignation the extortions formerly practised upon some of these unsophisticated emigrants in the great port of New York; happily they are now much better protected: but in a country where peculations and falsehoods fly like thistle-down through the air, eternal vigilance is the price of justice.
