Prominent among them was a book of gospels presented by the mother of Peter the Great, in binding of solid gold studded with precious stones, valued at £50,000; it weighs one hundred pounds, and requires two men to lift it. We were carried back to the days of the Emperor Constantine when we looked at the great gold cross incrusted with emeralds, rubies, etc., which belonged to him, and pageants of all earthly pomp were figured in the coronation-crowns and gorgeous vestments of patriarchs and bishops.
