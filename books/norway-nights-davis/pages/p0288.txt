Its crenelated walls, pierced by five great archways of entrance, embrace a triangular space of two square miles, and are flanked by enormous towers of every conceivable shape and size, round and square, light and graceful as minarets, or solid as bastions, surmounted by steep scaly roofs, brilliant as the hues of a tropical serpent. Below the ramparts lie verdant terraces, around the foot of which winds the lovely Moskva River. Formerly, watchmen on these battlements were constantly on the look-out, and when they saw clouds of dust sweeping over the flat plains of the south they knew that the Tartar hordes of the Crimea were at hand with devastating purpose. Then the great bell of the Ivan tower sounded the call of warning, and every one fled for safety to the fortified monasteries or the palace, at whose gates the wild horsemen battered in vain.
