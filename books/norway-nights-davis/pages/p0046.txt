This coarse and horrible mode of settling a grievance was known as “the duel of the girdle.” It is said that as these duels were almost certain to be fatal to one or both parties, every man’s wife used to carry a winding-sheet to banquets where quarrels were likely to arise from jealousy or intoxication.

In the picturesque market-place stands the oldest church, the interest of which is all on the outside, looking down upon the vendors of domestic wares, scanty vegetables and fruits, the best of which at this season are the fragrant little wild strawberries.
