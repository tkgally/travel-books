Again the good captain came to our aid and gave us his own cabin, where with wraps and rugs we had a retrospect of the Nord fiord, increased in interest by his traditions of the rocks and stories of the people, recounted in fair English. The mate was equally entertaining, and very proud of his three visits to the United States. “O yes,” he said in reply to some encouraging remark, “I like New York; I lived in a fine street there—Pearl Street. You know Pearl Street? And I have been to Chicago, too; I was there when the great fire was. I want to go back again.
