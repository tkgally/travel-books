Nothing could be more severely simple than the “Cathedral” (one of the rarely-seen Catholic churches of Norway), the bank, the school-houses, and the red-roofed dwellings. We went into several shops of furs of various sorts, in rugs, overcoats, and ladies’ cloaks, insupportably heavy; also white fur shoes trimmed with red cloth made by the Lapps. Several of these queer little nomads were bargaining for exchange of products, and regarded us with more good-humor than curiosity, for they are accustomed to the sight of summer visitors. We strayed into a small museum and examined a natural-history department of fishes and animals peculiar to that region, but all of which we had seen in other collections.
