At the table we were seated next the captain, and our ears had the inestimable privilege of hearing four unknown languages at the same time from our opposite neighbors—a Russian general, courtly and decisive; a member of the Swedish parliament, ponderous and solemn; a professor from Upsala University, with flowing locks and a fair, sprightly wife; and, as the fourth in this bewildering fugue, a Dane, whose incessant chatter never ceased during the entire voyage.
