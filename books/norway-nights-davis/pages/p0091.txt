By the calendar that morning it was the 15th of June: by the wind that blew down from the snows of the Dovre Fjeld it was the 15th of December; and the numerous warm wraps packed for use in the Arctic Circle were all put in requisition. In fact, we never again found them so essential. But as we gradually descended, the cold winds remained above, the mercury rose to its seasonable altitude, and the air was most exhilarating.
