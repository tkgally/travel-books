The Tsars are as rich as were the Pharaohs in unlimited quarries of marble.

Ponderous sculptured bronze doors lead to the interior, where floor, walls, and supporting pillars are of polished marbles, verde antique, Sienna yellow, porphyry, and jasper. Gilded angels of various sizes, pictures of Christ, the Virgin, and saints, ensconced in gilding and jewelled mosaics, gleam through a mystic twilight. Sculptures are forbidden by the Greek Church, but the command against graven images is not supposed to extend to flat surfaces or bas-reliefs.
