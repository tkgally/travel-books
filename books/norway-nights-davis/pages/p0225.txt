Previous to our journey hither many suggestions had been offered that winter is <i>par excellence</i> the time to visit this hyperborean capital; but the fact is that, like the duplicate shield which was gold on one side and silver on the other, St. Petersburg has two totally different aspects. Most alluring we found it in summer, with its verdant parks, blue waters, green and golden domes, and soft, diaphanous nights; and with such generosity of space and air that in even the warmth of July one is not oppressed as in other cities.
