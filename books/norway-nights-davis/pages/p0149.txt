The birch trees of Tromsöe are rather fine for that latitude; wild-flowers, such as pale violets, heather, and buttercups, make a pathetic effort to represent summer, and pots of geraniums appear in every window. Fruits and vegetables of course refuse to grow. The first officer told us that he had spent a winter there, and although they could dispense with lamps only five or six hours of the twenty-four, the nights were enlivened by dances, music, and theatricals, and altogether it was social and agreeable.
