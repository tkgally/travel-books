The entrance-hall was hung with white bear-skins and the pretty white fox-skins, the expressive heads undetached; while above stretched the arms of fine branching antlers. The sitting-room had a very homelike aspect, with readable books and comfortable sofas. The air was pure and bracing, and we were very glad to repose in this pleasant place for two nights. Herr Dombaas owns one of the best <i>Sæters</i>, or mountain dairies, in Norway. In these sæters one may see life reduced to its primitive elements. They are rude log-cabins with the usual tiny roof, on mountain plateaux, sometimes three thousand feet high, to which in summer the farmers send two or three of their daughters with the cows and goats.
