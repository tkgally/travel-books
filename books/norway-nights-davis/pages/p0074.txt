Pancakes form the usual sweet dish, and are so delicate that we soon learned to ask for them at every substantial meal. They are generally accompanied by stewed currants or raspberries and whipped cream. The butter is tasteless and untempting, and there are no vegetables except potatoes and a species of sorrel. Necessarily the quality of the cooking varies; we came across stations where the <i>cordon bleu</i> was decidedly a <i>cordon vert</i>. The prices are very low: never more than forty cents, American currency, for dinner or supper; twenty cents for morning coffee, eggs, and bread.
