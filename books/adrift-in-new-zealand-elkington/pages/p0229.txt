[[cont]]facial expression. Some tribes have the flat-nosed, thick-lipped, dark-skinned propensity of savages in full, whilst others are much lighter in colour and have quite English features. Some of the half-castes are beauties in the largest sense or the word, and if brought to England they would cause a sensation during any season. They are strong and big, and have perfectly natural figures; in fact, they are awfully nice—some of them.
