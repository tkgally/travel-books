[[cont]]I made my way down to it, and before I got there I distinctly heard a man talking. It was a queer hut, but its surroundings were very beautiful; great trees and palms and towering kauris formed its background, whilst a little babbling creek that ran through a perfect garden of ferns and moss-covered rocks passed the foot of a small patch of kitchen garden, where pumpkins, cabbages, and melons were growing. Two sleepy black cats were lying by the door, and when I came up to them one arched his back and hissed at me as if I had been a terrier.

“Pompey! Pompey!” I heard a voice from the shanty, “why this language?” The next instant a most curious-looking individual came to the door. “Come in, sir, come in. I take it you’ve lost your way, or you would not be in this neighbourhood,” he said.

The moment he spoke two facts were obvious—the first, that he was mad, and the second, that he was a gentleman.
