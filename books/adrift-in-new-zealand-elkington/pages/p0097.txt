He had been approached by certain clergymen to start a Church paper, and, feeling that he did not look like an editor of such an organ, he chose me because I was, as he termed me, “more of a clerical-looking cuss than he.” The man, however, who really did the editing was a tremendously big fellow that could drink more beer than any one man I have ever met. I remember reading a paragraph in one of the local papers cracking up an hotel, and in order to let the world know its exact position the paragraph explained that there was a well-worn track leading from the office of our Church paper right to the bar-parlour door.
