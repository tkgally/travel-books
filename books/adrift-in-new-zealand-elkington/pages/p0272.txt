Nelson is further famous for the fact that there are about three girls to every man. I think that is why Bones was so keen on it; he generally went there when he was low-spirited and some one had been trifling with his affections—it is strange how some men’s hearts guide their feet. As soon as he had gone Beans and I packed up our belongings and took tickets for Christchurch.

Now if there is one spot in the whole of New Zealand where a good holiday can be spent it is Christchurch, especially if the man who is taking it is an Englishman; it is the English town, and everything in it reminds you of home. Some say it is extremely like Ipswich, but as I have not been there I can’t say; if it is, then Ipswich must be a delightful spot. The first time I went to Christchurch it was winter, and the trip down the coast was magnificent.
