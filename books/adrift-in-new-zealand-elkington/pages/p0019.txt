# Adrift in New Zealand

# Chapter I
[[subtitle]] I arrive with threepence—My first impressions—A hint to hard-up travellers, and my first job—The shops and people—My stay at a farm, advice to workers, and my opinion of the colonial girl.

“W<sc>ell</sc>, young man, what do you think of it?”

I was leaning over the side of the boat and gazing at a rugged line of rocks, when I was suddenly asked this question. My interlocutor was an elderly man, with a grey beard and a broad-brimmed hat.

“Not much, so far,” I replied; “but I suppose there are some trees further inland?”

“A few,” he answered, with a superior sort of smile. “What do you think of doing when you arrive?”

“Doing! Well, to tell you the truth, I haven’t thought of it yet. What do most fellows like myself do?”
