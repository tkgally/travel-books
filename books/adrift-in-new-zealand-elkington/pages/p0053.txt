It was a very different afternoon from what I had expected, but my disappointment, if I had any, soon disappeared amid my pleasant surroundings, and my last spasm of regret at the change in events was suddenly dispersed by the entry of two laughing girls, one being the girl at whose feet I had grovelled on my first day in New Zealand.

In a moment I had left my seat; the next instant I was shaking hands with the beautiful Miss Frazer. (Her name was not really Frazer, but it matters very little; she is just as nice under any name.) She and her friend had been shopping, and were loaded up with small parcels, of which I naturally relieved them, and also naturally I conducted them to the table at which I had been sitting.
