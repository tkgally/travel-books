I only remember a horse being gored once, and I was on it. It was my fault, for I thought I knew better what to do than my horse. Through my inexperience I had got in between two cows, and their respective calves were muddled up; I began slashing away right and left at them to try to get them right, when both the angry mothers came for me, one on each side. My horse wanted to make a dash for it and let them collide with each other and fight it out between them, but I wanted to get them into the stock-yard; so I pulled my gee round and then struck one beast full across the face with my whip.
