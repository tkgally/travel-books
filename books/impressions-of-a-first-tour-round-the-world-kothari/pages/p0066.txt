We drove round this beautiful city, across a handsome bridge, some distance along the river, and then returned to the station, where, after having dinner, we got into the train for Bingen, arriving there at midnight. Slept at the White House Hotel, and after breakfast went on board the “Humbold,”

<i>June</i> 23, a paddle-steamer, for a trip up the Rhine as far as Cologne.

This was a delightful trip. The scenery around us was all the way of the loveliest. The ruined castles built on the tips of the rocks on the mountains which hang over the river change the scenery to that of the quiet villages below. The Drachenfels is the grandest of these sights on the Rhine; also the Lorelei, a huge black-looking rock overhangs the river and gives a sombre effect to the scenery around.

The boat we were on was very nice and comfortable, and the dinner they gave us was particularly good. We reached Coblentz at about 3 p.m., Bonn at about 4.30 p.m., and finally arrived in Cologne at 6.30 p.m. Stayed at Hotel Disch.
