# Impressions of a First Tour Round the World,

[[subtitle]] I<sc>n</sc> 1883 <sc>and</sc> 1884.

1883, <i>May</i> 1. It was on a fine May evening that I started, at 5 p.m., per the British India Steam Navigation steam ship “Oriental,” Captain Lewins, on my travels to the New World. The weather we experienced on the first night was not encouraging, as the sea was very rough; indeed, so much so, that even “old sailors” amongst my fellow-passengers found themselves compelled, with pale faces, to enter their cabins, and get through the night the best way they could. The next morning we had a better sea, and all of us were on deck, happy and bright as possible.
