The scenery on the banks of the middle lake is simply charming, the flower-beds and other trees are thickly planted, and the sight I saw was magnificent, and shall never be forgotten. It is really a source of great pity that up to this time no small steamers are allowed to run on any of these lakes. After going as far as the Gap of Dunloe, we discharged our cab, and took a boat and rowed round the upper and middle lake, and thus spent a day in great enjoyment. Although the Lakes of Killarney are of less extent, and devoid of that sublimity attaching to the lochs of Scotland, they possess some remarkable features, such as the dense woods that surround them, the imposing contours of the mountains, the numberless islands, and their luxuriant vegetation. The striking scenery is of the plant arbutus, which freely grows here; its fresh green tints contrast so well with the grey rocks among which it flourishes. These celebrated lakes of Ireland are divided into three parts—the Upper Lake, the Muckross Torc, or Middle Lake, and Lough Leane, or the Lower Lake.
