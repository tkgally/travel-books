The height of the bridge, from high water mark to the floor of the bridge, in the centre, is one hundred and thirty-five feet. The work was commenced in the year 1871 by Mr. J. A. Roebling, a Government engineer, who was afterwards killed by an accident on the same bridge, but the gigantic work was completed by his son in May last (1883), at a cost of fourteen million dollars, which equals thirty-five million rupees. The railway is worked on the bridge across the river by means of wire ropes.

After spending nearly half a day in inspecting this splendid bridge, I went and saw the Greenwood Cemetery. I was told that this cemetery is considered the most beautiful in the world; but, for my part, I did not find it any way better or equal to the one I saw at Genoa.
