We now go to the Belvedere Terrace. This is a large promenade built on the highest point of Dresden, from which we can overlook the town and the river Elbe. The view is very good, and under the shady trees of the Belvedere restaurant we can enjoy the beautiful cool air, together with the music of a first-class band, and the sight of the <i>beau monde</i> of Dresden, who flock here every evening.

We now go to the Opera, the piece being the “Barbier de Seville.” What a magnificent house! This is the most beautiful one I have seen as yet. The Opera House in London is not nearly so fine; it may be somewhat larger, but certainly not so well arranged or decorated. This comic opera is very amusing, and the music pleasing. The principal singer, Fraulien von Weber, has a beautiful voice. I must say I was quite astonished to find in so small a town so splendid a building and so excellent a performance.

<i>July</i> 16. We make our way early to the Picture Gallery. This is one of the most renowned picture galleries in the world, in which the works of some of the best of the old masters are exhibited.
