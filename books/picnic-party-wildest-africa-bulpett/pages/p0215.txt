While in camp at Digira our most energetic efforts were spent in trying to get buffalo, and though we saw plenty of fresh traces of them we came across none. I suppose the noise of our porters and the turmoil of our camp had frightened them out of the district. Nor did we come across any elephants but there was plenty of other game, which, however, we did not molest more than was necessary for supplying the camp with food. The natives told us that the week before our arrival there had been plenty of elephants, but that our shooting had frightened them all away.
