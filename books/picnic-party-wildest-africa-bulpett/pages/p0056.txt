The natives dread the crocodiles up to a certain point, but they will always enter the water fearlessly no matter how many of them there may be around, if there is a firing-party at hand to frighten the beasts away with the noise of their rifles. However, one is never actually safe, and it does not do to run risks, for the ways of crocodiles are curious. Numbers and noise of course usually frighten them; but then when all seems safe and you are wading a stream in apparent security, a man may be suddenly snatched away from the midst of his comrades. <i>Verbum sap.</i> Never run unnecessary risks with crocodiles.
