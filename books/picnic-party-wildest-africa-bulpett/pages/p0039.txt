K<sc>hartoum</sc> has a glorious climate during the winter months. The desert air has a crispness which must be experienced to be appreciated; and though the rays of the mid-day sun are very powerful, the freshness of the early mornings in winter is delightful.

“The city of Khartoum is laid out in fine broad streets emanating from an open square like the stripes of the Union Jack, making perfect defence against any hostile attack possible from the square itself. The buildings are good, in architecture appropriate to the climate, but, save for the private gardens on the river front, there is as yet no shade in other places, the recently planted trees being as yet only a few feet high (1904).
