“‘His Excellence’ and the ‘Baby’ made a march on foot after a giraffe, but although they saw no game, they report most lovely country, and forests full of beautiful flowers. They brought back some sprays of purple orchids, the flowers very like the large purple orchids one buys in London, only much smaller flowers, and a fine white feathery but not very pretty one, and some wild plums which were like greengages, but very bitter.”
