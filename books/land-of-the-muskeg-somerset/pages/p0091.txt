[[caption]] Twelvefoot Davis

At Dunvegan we met another old man of nearly eighty years of age—one Twelvefoot Davis—a white man famous throughout the country. He had been a gold miner for many years, and had made a large sum of money on a neglected claim twelve feet square. This had happened in the old days of the great Cariboo mines, and his fame and nickname had spread far and wide.
