[[cont]]long afterwards she had a son, who was Estas, though she did not know it. The child grew at a great pace, and as soon as he was able to walk commenced to move the vessel in which his grandfather kept the water. At last, when he was a grown man, he rushed out of the lodge with the water and gave it to men, sprinkling it over the earth, and making lakes, rivers, and seas.
