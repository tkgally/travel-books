[[image: two carved Indian gambling sticks, one with a notch in the middle]]
[[caption]] Gambling Sticks

All Indians are great gamblers. We had heard the boats’ crews beating the tom-tom and shouting round their fire at the Athabaska Landing, but had never until now had a chance to watch their game. The process is simple. They squat in two lines opposite each other, each man facing his adversary. The players have two sticks of equal length, one of which has a small notch in the middle.
