## Recent Publications.

MY PARIS NOTE-BOOK. By the Author of “An Englishman in Paris.” In One Volume, demy 8vo. Price 14<i>s.</i>

<i>The Daily Telegraph.</i>—“One of those exceptionally delightful books the manifold fascinations of which it is difficult to exemplify by quotation.”

<i>Galignani’s Messenger.</i>—“Want of space forbids us to make further quotations from the good things in which the book abounds.”

EDMUND AND JULES DE GONCOURT. Letters and Leaves from their Journals. Selected. In Two Volumes, 8vo. With Eight Portraits, 32s.

<i>The Realm.</i>—“It is impossible to indicate the immense variety of entertaining and often profoundly interesting matter which these volumes contain.”

MEMOIRS (VIEUX SOUVENIRS) OF THE PRINCE DE JOINVILLE. Translated from the French by Lady M<sc>ary</sc> L<sc>oyd</sc>. With 78 Illustrations from drawings by the Author. In One Volume, demy 8vo, 15s. net.

<i>The Times.</i>—“They are written in the breezy style of a sailor.”

<i>The St. James’s Gazette.</i>—“This is one of the most entertaining volumes of memoirs that have appeared within recent years.”

<i>The Glasgow Herald.</i>—“A very storehouse of anecdotes and incidents that carry the reader along, and have all the charm of a bright and sparkling conversation.”

NAPOLEON AND THE FAIR SEX. (Napoleon et les Femmes). From the French of F<sc>rédéric</sc> M<sc>asson</sc>. In One Volume, demy 8vo. With Ten Portraits, 15s. net.

<i>The Daily Chronicle.</i>—“The author shows that this side of Napoleon’s life must be understood by those who would realize the manner of man he was.”

THE STORY OF A THRONE. Catherine II. of Russia. From the French of K. W<sc>aliszewski</sc>, Author of “The Romance of an Empress.” With a Portrait. In Two Volumes, demy 8vo, 28s.

<i>The World.</i>—“No novel that ever was written could compete with this historical monograph in absorbing interest.”

THE ROMANCE OF AN EMPRESS. Catherine II. of Russia. By K. W<sc>aliszewski</sc>. Translated from the French. Second Edition. In One Volume, 8vo. With Portrait. Price 7<i>s.</i> 6<i>d.</i>

<i>The Times.</i>—“This book is based on the confessions of the Empress herself; it gives striking pictures of the condition of the contemporary Russia which she did so much to mould as well as to expand.... Few stories in history are more romantic than that of Catherine II. of Russia, with its mysterious incidents and thrilling episodes; few characters present more curious problems.”
