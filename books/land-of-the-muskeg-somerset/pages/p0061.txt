[[cont]]this there ensued a fiery discussion on the subject of pails, so that the Belgians nearly came to blows. However, they decided to lay their case before La Riviere, and allow him to decide whether the pail was clean or no. Now La Riviere was sitting in his tent talking to a venerable Catholic missionary, who happened to be going over the road in his caravan, when they entered, bucket in hand.

But it was all to no purpose. Before either had stated his case a paroxysm of fury seized both young men, and they began to fight, using the prostrate bodies of the free trader and the missionary as their battle-ground. The tent was small, and the view of the interior during the next few moments suggested a human kaleidoscope.
