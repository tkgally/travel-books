Finally, it became evident that the bear was moving himself, so we remounted, and beat a large piece of bush, galloping for about a mile through the underbrush as fast as we could, in hopes that we should cut him off and come to quarters; but we saw nothing, and only got very much scratched by the thorns. Round indeed declared that he had heard the bear moving, but we could find no trace of him, so we returned to camp, feeling rather low. Daukhan had done some wonderful stalking, and it was only by chance that we had missed the animal. He was, however, very disconsolate at his failure.
