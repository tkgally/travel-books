Daukhan said that there was a trail from the river to the lake, and that if only he could find the place where five years before he had killed two young moose, he would be able to set us on it. For two days Daukhan was unable to move, but on the third, after a very hard day’s work amongst fallen timber and swamps, we descended into a little valley and found the whitened poles of an old camp. Daukhan said that this was the place where he ‘ate the moose,’ and very soon we found a blade-bone and other relics of this long-past meal.
