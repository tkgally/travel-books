I fancy, too, that the exertion of the morning’s march and the long ride had set his rheumatism at work again. We made our fire for supper in silence; and while John boiled the kettle and made bread, I toiled down the steep bank to the lake, some hundred feet below us, to bathe. On my return I found supper ready, and with food our spirits revived, and in a rather better humour we lit our pipes and sat on the crest of the hill to watch the fading glories of the sunset.
