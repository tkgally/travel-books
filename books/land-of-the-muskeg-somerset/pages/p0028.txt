The Athabaska Landing is the gate of the great North. It is from here that all the stores go out that supply the Hudson’s Bay Company’s forts from Hudson’s Hope to the mouth of the Mackenzie. A steamer built on the spot plies up the river to the mouth of the Slave River, and down to where the rapids make the Athabaska no longer navigable, where the stores are transhipped to York boats. Beyond the warehouses, offices, and Mr. Wood’s residence there are no buildings, although most of the year there is a large Indian encampment near by. It is, too, the last outpost of the Government, and a couple of the Canadian police were on duty to stop the importation of strong liquor.
