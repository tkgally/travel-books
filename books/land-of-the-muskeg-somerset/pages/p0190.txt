As it was now well on in the afternoon, we moved forward a little way into the timber and there camped. It was the wildest spot I have ever seen—an ideal virgin forest. The ground was very swampy, and covered with a deep carpet of moss. The trees bent one against the other in melancholy decay, covered with long grey lichen and huge fungus. Overhead the matted branches seemed to rot upon the trunks, grey with mould. Huge dead logs strewed the ground, crumbling at the touch of a foot.
