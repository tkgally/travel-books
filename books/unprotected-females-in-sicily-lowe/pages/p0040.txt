The upper stories along the street are beautiful and grand; the lower, rows of hovels, stalls, and shabby shops; in a few <i>caffè</i>, sit noblemen who are too fine to walk, too poor to drive, and whisper <i>Inglese</i>, as we pass; their rich relations rattle in gaudy carriages through the triumphal arch to the Marina, a drive along the sea-shore worthy of Neptune’s car; a young Bacchus, seated on a barrel, and preceded by beating drums, proclaims a ship laden with good wine has reached the harbour; all is miscellaneous, rapid movement. We return home stirred by the animation, and who would say they were not in a romantic land, where Spanish blood and chivalry course together through the people’s veins, on finding a beautiful little note with the following words lying on their table?
