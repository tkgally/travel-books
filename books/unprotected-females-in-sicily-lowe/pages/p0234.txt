# Chapter XIX.

[[subtitle]] Reggio.

[[subtitle]] A Visit from Three Honest Men—Earthquaky State of the Town—Two Kinds of Popular Jealousy—Orange-Groves—Bergamot—Silkworms—A Romantic Afternoon—Some National Traits.

“E<sc>ccellenza</sc>! Signora! ecco tre galant’ uomini che la domandono” (three honest men are asking for you).

“March their honesties up, Padrone; there is room on the balcony for them all.”

Two were from the Cavaliere Monsolini, a great man in these parts, to whom our Messina friends had written; he, having a fit of the gout, had sent flying outposts to announce his future appearance. The third was a young merchant, on his own account, proposing a drive along the “Coda di Volpe,” or Fox’s Brush, as that part of the coast is called which sweeps out insinuatingly to the south. We closed with him, the Cavaliere’s envoys fixing the following day (gout permitting,) for visiting the bergamot and silkworm establishments.
