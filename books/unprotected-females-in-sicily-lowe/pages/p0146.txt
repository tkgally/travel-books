Nicolosi had once been overwhelmed by lava; the present village is built in view of the ruins of the former, as the survivors felt sure the stream would not come the same way for a long, long time again. The last eruption in 1854, a very fine one, made the crater called “Antenario,” on the eastern side of the mountain; people were able to read by its light on the western side. They quizzed an English family who happened to be ascending at the time it broke out, and who, though on quite a safe part of the mountain, hurried away dreadfully frightened, from the scene all were hastening to see, and not content with descending pell-mell, immediately drove away from Catania also; pale, haggard, and trembling. “<i>Ha! non orano volcani al pare di noi!</i>” They were not volcanic, like the Sicilians.
