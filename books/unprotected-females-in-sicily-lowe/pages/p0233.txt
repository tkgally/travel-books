There is another of these simple places on the quay; thus a large party might divide at night and unite at meals, which were admirably served (for Calabrian style), with delicious varieties of fish. In the saucer of each coffee-cup was a pretty sonnet, on rose-coloured paper, wishing a happy New Year.
