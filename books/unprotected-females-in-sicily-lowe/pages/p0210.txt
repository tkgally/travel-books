To speak of the scene, O, had I but the magician's language of joy, where the consonants dance, the vowels sing, the grammar is a feast!—now in front stands Taormina; her slopes still yield Hyblean honey, her amphitheatre gives the last view of Ætna, her position perfects the loveliness around . . . . . . . . At this point, O coast of Sicily,—Venus of Nature! claim the apple—you must surpass all else in earthly beauty! Calabria may frown in dark hilly jealousy opposite—take the prize, your charms are doubled by your ethereal veil of blue!
