# Chapter III.
[[subtitle]] Palermo’s Ways.
[[subtitle]] Interiors of the Houses—Way of Living—Valuable Paintings and Old Lace—Drawing Lottery Prizes—The Mirage—English Service—The Gardens—Fair Ladies and Their Cavaliers—The Royal Villa—Monte Pellegrino—The Catacombs.

F<sc>uture</sc> young ladies who visit Sicily, and may be disposed to look favourably on propositions for remaining there (perhaps better than going to New Zealand), might wish to know what the interiors of the houses of the upper classes are like. Of course they are all <i>Palazzi</i> (palaces), nothing under; and the one next door to the hotel, belonging to Prince Trabbia, is a type of the rest. They must enter it by the terrace, or the horrors of the courtyard and outer staircase may prevent them getting any farther.
