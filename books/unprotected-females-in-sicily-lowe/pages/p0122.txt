The gentleman’s daughters came very pleasantly forward, and spoke English well, one having just passed her honeymoon in London; the mother was merely visible in the distance in a wrapper, evidently bustling about the dinner; the son was a dandy, and not very willing to enter into details as to how mules, guides, &c. could be got for Ætna; so we inwardly decided to go to the silk merchant for the “practicals.” The young ladies presented us with sweet little mandarine oranges, the first of the season, and exquisitely luscious; an inferior kind are known as Maltese oranges in England.
