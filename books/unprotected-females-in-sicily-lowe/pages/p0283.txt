# Chapter XXIII.

[[subtitle]] A Rest at Naples and Portici.

[[subtitle]] Confusion of Naples—Indifferent Hotels and Landlords—Flight to Portici—Trips into the Capital—Equipages of the Shopkeepers—Poncinello—Neapolitan Girls—Anecdotes of the King and the English—A Tragedy of Real Life.

N<sc>aples</sc>, kind reader, is a place you must touch at, see its bay, views, and museum, then fly off. Poetical heroine as she has always been, laughing and glowing from her circling sapphire shores through many a line of verse, the shock of seeing her in her own streets is too great, like visiting some bright Italian beauty in her scrambling morning hours.

The scene of contention upon your landing is but a commencement of the strife which will last during your stay; and the half-clad <i>facchini</i>, who tear your luggage asunder, are not more rapacious than the landlords awaiting your piastres. Even to clear our small bags through the custom-house, required the Swiss guard’s bayonet, the Italian sentinel being busy begging from us.
