But as you hurry up the steps between the two pictures of the death of the good and bad man, and the stanza on dissolution, be not too severe in condemning the Sicilians: all nations have their barbarous customs; and some very civilised people, if consulted, would rather be tortured dead than alive.

One day afterwards we were waiting near a barrack, formerly a monastery, and looking by chance down an old grating in the yard, saw all the defunct monks standing in gown, cowl, and rosary, with here and there some white beard left among them.
