We found in future the best way to manage was either to make an arrangement over the side of the steamer, or to lay down what we thought fit, and walk off without listening to nonsense about tariffs: the same men were sure to come up grinning the next morning, and offer to take us a sail, saying, in good English, “Ave a boat, Mammy?” and remaining at our service during our stay. The traveller should get a notion how much things ought to be charged from some native, then name his own price to whoever he is dealing with; after a few expostulating “Ha Excellenza’s,” with a sly look lurking in their merry eyes, they will take it cheerfully, be civil, then grateful, for a small “buona mano” extra. The exchange upon England has been depressed for some years; the proper value of one pound is sixty taris, but it only passes for fifty-seven. The coins are the same as at Naples, the carlino of ten grains being called a tari; the grano, a bajocco; the piastres, pezzi of twelve tari: but accounts are kept in ducati of ten tari, and oncie of three ducati.
