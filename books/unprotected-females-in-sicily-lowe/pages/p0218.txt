<blockquote>
“M<sc>aria</sc> V<sc>irgo</sc>, Joachim filia, Dei Humilissima Serva, Jesu Christi crucifixi Mater, ex Tribu Juda, Stirpe David. Messinensibus omnibus salutem, et Dei Patris Omnipotentis Benedictionem.

“Vos omnes fide magnâ Legatos, et Nuntios, per publicum documentum ad nos misisse constat. Filium nostrum Dei genitum, Deum et hominem esse fatemini, et in Cœlum post suam Resurectionem ascendisse, Pauli Electi praedicatione mediante, viam veritatis agnoscentes. Ob quod Vos, et ipsam civitatem benedicimus, cujus Perpetua Protectrix nos esse volumus.

“Anno Filij nostri 42. Indictione I., III. Nonas Junij Lunæ 26. Feria 5. ex Hierosolymis. Maria Virgo quæ supra confirmat praesens Chirographum manu propriâ.”
</blockquote>

It was kindly translated for me, thus:—

<blockquote>
“M<sc>aria the</sc> V<sc>irgin</sc>, daughter of Joachim, most humble servant of God, mother of Christ Jesus crucified, of the tribe of Judah and of the seed of David, to all inhabitants of Messina greeting, and the blessing of God the Father Almighty.

“It appears by public document that you all with great faith have sent to us Legates and Messengers. You confess that our Son is born of God and is both God and Man, and that after his Resurrection he ascended up into Heaven; acknowledging also the way of truth, through the preaching of Paul the Elect. On which account we do bless both you and the state itself, whose Eternal Protectress we purpose to be.

“In the Year of our Son 42. Indiction I., III. Nones of the Month of June 26. 5th Sunday, from Jerusalem. Maria the Virgin, here present, confirms over and above this document with her own hand.”
</blockquote>
