One palace alone has an avenue, at the end of which we were put down, escaping the awful town we had the fatal curiosity to visit afterwards, and found there really were more wretched things in the world than our philosophy had yet dreamt of. Prince Palagonia’s father had a taste for ornamental monsters, of which the son has removed the most remarkable, leaving a circle of mitigated ones to amuse the curious. The rooms of the house are also magnificently eccentric, with their walls and ceilings of looking-glass, portraits in variegated marbles, and embroideries of gold and coral.
