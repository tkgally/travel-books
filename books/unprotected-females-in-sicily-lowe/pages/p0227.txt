Kind commiserating friends, of whom we had made several, in a truly English spirit brought us paquets of consoling tea; one came with a canister in each pocket; we had already received another from a nice girl, his niece, who had charmed us by her music the whole of the former evening at her uncle’s and aunt’s house.
