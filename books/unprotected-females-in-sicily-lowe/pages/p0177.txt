# Chapter XIII.

[[subtitle]] The Journey to Syracuse.

[[subtitle]] Hanging About for Thirty-six Hours—The Fashionable Caffè—Modern Inferno and Purgatorio—Farewell to the Monk—A Nymph Turned into Styx—The Tomb of Archimedes—Eureka—An Heiress—Accommodation.

T<sc>he</sc> thirty-six hours we waited in Catania for the “Posta” were much alleviated by discovering an establishment called “Caffè Siciliano,” situated on the large square opposite the lava elephant bearing a car on his back, and which makes a most curious sketch, requiring such patience, that one forgets the post’s delay when engrossed in giving a right twist to the trunk, and bend to the tusks. As the Caffè proprietors keep a veteran retired beggar on purpose to beat off the rising generation of mendicants, who are reduced to looking through the windows, the comfort of the retreat is very great.
