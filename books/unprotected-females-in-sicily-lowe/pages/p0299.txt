<verse>
[[cont]]Together with your earliest friend,
Pale Snowdrop, and her graceful bend;
There’s little peeping Primrose, too,
An old acquaintance would renew;
Whilst underneath a fresh-turned sod
Sweet Violet gives her downcast nod;
This morn we walked the fields around,
And here and there a Daisy found,
Quick—for fear of any loss
Bound them with the dewy moss,
As sportive lambkins seemed to say,
“Don’t pluck those dainty heads away.”
With what deep int’rest Nature’s fraught!
One cannot have a vacant thought;
The very stones upon the shore
Wild waves are daily dashing o’er,
We e’en ’mid them may find a flower![[fnref *]]
</verse>

[[center]] THE END.

[[center]] R. CLAY, PRINTER, BREAD STREET HILL.

[[fn *]] The Sea-Anemone.
