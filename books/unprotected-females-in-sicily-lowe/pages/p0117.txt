[[cont]]stands revealed in one glowing mass, the crowning vapour e’en a roseate streak across the sky, a scene almost too great, too bright, for mortal sense. . . . Oh! mountain, mountain, speak thou to me, and I will answer thee! . . . How I feel thy presence! My heart beats to thine . . . All the grosser part of human nature I lay at thy feet, and catch a fore-glimpse of Paradise in contemplating thee—O most beautiful of the wonderful works of God!

Through lava walls we rolled down into Catania.
