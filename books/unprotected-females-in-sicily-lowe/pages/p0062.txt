# Chapter V.

[[subtitle]] Travelling in Sicily.

[[subtitle]] The Conveyances and Other Travellers—The First Country Inn—Monks, Peasants, and Animals—Villages and Casinos—Adventure—Guardian Bandits—Music of the Mandoline—A Quarrel—The Madonna Appears—The Pasha and His Slave.

A <sc>vetturino</sc>, called Antonino, of the Casa Madonna, Strada Toledo, travels weekly between Palermo and Caltanisetta, the chief town of a province half-way to Girgenti. A Sicilian gentleman and his wife had secured two of the places in the carriage, which was new and good, with large glass windows, and drawn by three horses. The postal courier leaves the passengers at Santa Catarina, from whence they would be dependent on mules, carts, or walking, for the ten miles to Caltanisetta, to which another post carriage goes direct every Wednesday, travelling all night.
