# Chapter XII.
[[subtitle]] Ascent of Mount Ætna.

<verse>
“And climbed with heart the adverse steep.”
</verse>

[[right]] <sc>Dante</sc>.

A <sc>soft</sc> tinkling of bells beneath the window said “Rise!” and though the young moon was alone in the sky with one pale star, the orange dawn lay as a broad belt on the dark Ionian sea. We boundingly dress, and spring on the mules with palpitating pleasure, for is not the fulfilment of our dream, our <i>object,</i>—and you of ardent temperament know all that word contains,—near its fulfilment?
