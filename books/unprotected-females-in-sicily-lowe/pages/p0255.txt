# Chapter XXI.

[[subtitle]] Cosenza.

[[subtitle]] Alaric’s Grave—Lying in State of a Great Man—Adoration of the Magi—Sale of Pigs—Eligible Young Calabrians—Artistic Scenes—Heroes—A Present—Donna Bella—A Silk Manufactory—Tales of the Jesuits and Ministers—Prisoners—The Primitive Opera—National Idea of England—Disinterested Innkeepers—Our Private Equipage.

W<sc>hat</sc> do you think, reader, was the prospect in front of the house? The site of the burial-place of Alaric!

Immediately under the two windows met the Busento and Crati, the two rivers which were stopped in their course until the Last of the Goths was interred beneath the passage of their returning waters.
