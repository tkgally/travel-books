Mingling with a flock of goats brought to taste the salt waters, we were enjoying the almost forgotten pleasure of picking up shells and being caught in the spray; were looking up at both coasts, not knowing which to admire most,—Calabria tipped with snow above, rich red and blue beneath; Sicily with rainbow vapours ’midst her thousand peaks,—and were trying to eye the vulture’s wing as it whirled round frittering rocks—when at each moment that wing flapped lower and lower—we saw the hideous crimson crest, an odour of horror filled the air ... within a few paces lay two flayed horses! Here, on this Arcadian coast, is the chosen spot for casting dead animals away; and from the gay carriages on the daily drive, other corpses can be seen which the insulted wave refuses to receive. O Italians! why are you such an unclean race? ... is your dignity, as well as moral sense, never hurt by this and other odious scenes? with all your feeling of the beautiful can you mar—nay, outrage—the voiceless scenes of Nature thus? ....
