In the University are trophies of the exploring taste of our young countrymen: two of whom, some years ago, discovered ancient bas-reliefs of great value at Selinuntium; one representing Perseus, another Hercules, a third a triumphal car.

Now let us take one of the carriages whose driver has kept us in sight the whole day, and a few minutes will bring us to the <i>Ziza</i>, dwelling of the last Emir who lingered behind his Moorish brethren.
