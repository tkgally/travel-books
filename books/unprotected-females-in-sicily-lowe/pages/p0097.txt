Our departure from Girgenti must be delayed to see a sulphur mine, and the young men have introduced the stoutest Don Cesare as the proprietor of one near the town, that his portly presence may completely dispel any idea of ill effects to the complexion, or health, from descending a few hundred yards into volcanic earth.
