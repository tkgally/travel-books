The sun is intensely scorching, the wind miserably cold, and the more penetrating from being unsuspectedly still as air. The best houses for strangers, and the large hotel, look to the north, and are never visited by a stray sunbeam in front. Some apartments in the Belmonte Palace, under Monte Pellegrino, have the rising rays from the east; but I earnestly recommend invalids to pause before they leave their comfortable homes for marble-halls without fireplaces, where a pan of charcoal gives but the choice of asphixiation, or a chronic affection of chattering teeth from open doors.
