The road then sunk between the mountains, and darkness covered all.

“Are you leaning out of the carriage from affright, Signora?”

“No, Don Raffaelle, but to take in the full majesty of the hour.”

“While I have my sword, you need not dread the fate of that young lady, who, a few years ago, was taken out of a travelling carriage, her father and two brothers being first bound hand and foot, and never has been heard of since.”
