As the starting period changes every evening, and very few Sicilians have John Bull’s punctuality, or a good view of the sinking sun, the watches are not all set at the same moment, and the church clocks do not even agree. I was quizzing a good-natured native about this system one day; he replied, “Of course it would not answer in a country like yours, where one never sees the sun!”
