Their next attack was upon a lady whose husband had left her all his fortune. They declared his will a forgery; and as she immediately got the better of them at the local tribunals, they carried the cause to Naples. There the lady’s advocate told her the contest was all but vain, unless one experiment succeeded. He placed bank notes for 15,000 ducats in a portfolio, asked to see the Minister of Justice, and putting it on his table, said: “Here are the arguments in favour of my client,” then ran off as quickly as he could. The minister took up the portfolio, and finding what it contained, rushed after him saying: “Carry this away!
