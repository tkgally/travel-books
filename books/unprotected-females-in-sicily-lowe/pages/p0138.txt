[[cont]]Despite the assurances that the sky would clear immediately, an impudent drizzle began; we returned indoors, and understood what blankness was . . . Did you ever hear of such a disappointment? Kind reader, do sympathise with us!

After sitting in a state of vacancy till daybreak,—when we felt so angry with the dull foggy dawn that we took to bed again not to see it,—we awoke in somewhat a better frame of mind, and I may as well tell you the proper preparations for this expedition,—when one is not disappointed. Ladies should take a guide and a mule each; the price of the former is one piastre and a half, of the latter, one piastre, including the man who attends the animals; so that for us two, the expense was to have been seven piastres, including the guides’ mules, which they always require, but do not expect a buona mano, unless they give extra satisfaction.
