## Decisions
- Masked corners (p0253–p0297, ten leaves): lost letters `[[supplied]]` from the context (Opus subagent, each checked
  against the next page's first words); one place name on p. 263 `[[gap]]`; the draft had silently filled one word,
  left two truncated and invented a hyphen (“ingenui-”): all corrected.
- Plates in colour as the scan has them (tinted); p0088's caption cut away by the scan edge (left out, said in
  `notes_on_text`); `leaf_order` puts the Agrigentum plate after the page it faces (p0091).
- Printer's errors marked: voeations, Thf (contents), simliar, Grea, witb. Left as printed: the Italian as printed
  (“abboccamentto”, “affeto”…), “nel” p0175 (a mark over the e, uncertain), “Resurectionem” (printed with a bar over
  the r). Elided Italian apostrophes closed up (modern spacing). Chapter XVIII's “Scylla and Charybdis.” a subtitle.
- `keep_hyphens` p0026 double-headed, p0195 break-neck; `[[cont]]` p0061 added, p0079 and p0176 removed (indents and
  tercets on the scan).
- The publisher's 32-page catalogue at the end (p0303–p0334) is not transcribed (assumed: easily added in a later
  version; said in `notes_on_text`).
