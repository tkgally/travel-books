From this point we made our way slowly round the island, returning to Kingston in time to avoid the May “seasons,” ominous symptoms of which had already begun to shew themselves. And from thence one fine morning in spring, as beautiful as that on which we had first seen “the Queen of the Antilles,” we embarked for England, carrying with us none but pleasant recollections of our sojourn in Jamaica.
