[[cont]]negro settlement is either a Comfort or a Content, a Friendship or a Providence, a Prospect or a Retirement. Happy Huts, Pleasant Groves, Cheerful Gardens, Harmony Penns, Golden Vales, Fruitful Mounts, Unity Valleys, and Cherry Banks, abound; and the more wild and desolate the situation, the more jocund and cheery its name.
