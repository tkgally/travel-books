<i>W.</i>—So I says to Henry Thomas—“Henry Thomas, my friend, you hol’ my bag and make I get a drink to put in my bottle and take home to the missus.” Thomas says, “Very well.” So I heave down my bag off my shoulder and give it to him to hold.

<i>C. P. S.</i>—But what did the prisoner say to you?

<i>W.</i>—Please let I make you understand good.

<i>First Magistrate</i> (impatiently)—Yes! yes! But what did the prisoner say?

<i>W.</i> (sullenly, having been interrupted in his narrative)—He say nothing to me, my worship!

<i>Second Magistrate</i>—What did he call you, then?

<i>W.</i>—He called me a thief, my worship!

<i>First Magistrate</i>—Well, tell us about that.

<i>W.</i>—Well, my worship! I says to Henry Thomas, “Thomas, my friend, you hol’ my bag—”

<i>C. P. S.</i>—You’ve told us all that before.

<i>W.</i> (with an injured air)—Hi! massa! you no let I make you understand.

<i>C. P. S.</i> (testily)—Just tell me what the prisoner said to you.

<i>W.</i>—He said I was a thief.

<i>Prisoner</i>—I deny the question!

<i>C. P. S.</i>—Now give the exact words. Be careful, for I have to take them down.

<i>W.</i>—He told me I was a thief, my worship.

<i>C. P. S.</i> (writing)—“Prisoner said to me, you are a thief.” Anything else?
