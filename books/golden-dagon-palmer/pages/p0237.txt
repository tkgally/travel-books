They rummaged our bunks, and without a blush made merry over the mysteries of gun-room penetralia. But, alas! not one of them had ever fainted in her life—they were as ignorant of sal-volatile as of the Sacrament.

One strong-minded puss—who, from her unterrified study of our costume, must have been a tailoress, or a transmigrated Bloomer—managed, with the aid of a midshipman, to get into a pair of my drawers—red flannel ones, left from Cape Horn.
