[[cont]]next instant I was in the water again. I, too, had partaken of the alarm, but, not understanding its nature, had rushed down the bank, and into the river, and was striking out once more for the very shore whence I had just been driven. I swam across and clambered up the bank.

Is there anything picturesquely frightful in story—anything in German legend of solitary men beset by fiends, and beasts of hideous aspect and horrible cries—that will do to describe this by? Think of the knight in Undine, as he rides through the haunted forest, waylaid by Calibanic shapes; or take the lady in Comus, put a pea-jacket and regulation-trowsers on her, and you have me.

Everything proper to a safe man’s mind had left me; nothing remained but cunning and insensible courage. I had never once thought of praying, of pleading for mercy; I had never once asked forgiveness for my sins; I had not given a thought to my family or friends; I was busy only with the damnable inventions, the hellish traps, set for me, and I remember the Bothwell-like resistance I felt—I feel it
