Poor little Mayouk! her father owed a hundred and fifty rupees; his goods had all gone in the fire that swept away the old town when we applied our rocket-torches to its tinder houses; his creditor was stern, and the law was plain; so they were trying to raise the money on little Mayouk, who had nothing at all to do with the negotiations, save to make the most of her prettiness, and wait at home for the coming man. Goods like her were not a drug in town, nor were buyers, such as I, plentiful and competing. I had at once the rupees and the Quixotic sentiment—my comrades were rarely possessed of the two together. So we were not long in concluding “an operation.”
