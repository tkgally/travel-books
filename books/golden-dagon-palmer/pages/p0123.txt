Balls patter in the stream thickly around his head, but spare him always, as though there were indeed some virtue in his charm, his prayers, his incantations; for, as often as he reaches his floating superstition, he hugs it to his heaving breast—and for a space, seemingly forgetful of the danger, he pauses and his lips move rapidly. Again, rising waist-high in the water, and tossing his brown arms aloft, he throws his shell across the stream, and dives, and dives again; and when presently he comes panting to the surface, he dashes the water from his straining eyes, utters a cry of joy as he catches sight of his senseless toy, and strikes out lustily.
