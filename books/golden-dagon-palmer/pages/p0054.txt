From that time till the close of 1851, a few traders from Calcutta, Bombay, Madras, Moulmein, Singapore, and even Hong-Kong—all British subjects, under the protection of the Honorable Company, whether native-born, half-caste, Parsees, Armenians, or Chittagonians—attempted, with various fortune, the establishment of a safe and regular commerce with those difficult people.
