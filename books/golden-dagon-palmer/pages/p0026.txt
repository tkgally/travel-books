“Mi no savee that pigeon; mi too muchee seeckee insidee; spose you likee more ploppa, can ketchee my seesta; he savee that pigeon all ploppa—can do.”

But even Ay-Chung was but as curry to the dry rice of the Hong-Kong fare, and even her spiciness could not long continue to make its insipidities appetizing. So I withdrew within my cheroot, and entertained myself with wondering what would come next.

The <i>North Star</i>, which had brought me hither from California, by way of the Hawaiian Islands, had gone to Macao to “tea,” and would not return until after many days.
