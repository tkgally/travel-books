Sometimes, in the evening, as we sat in the stern, smoking cheroots on the bull-ring and looking down among the shadows of young Ingeboo’s boat, toward where the petroleum taper glimmered behind the Turkey-red curtain that veiled his inner temple, we could spy shadows that were like young bosoms, and catch sounds that were like the laughter of romping girls; and as generous conquerors should, we rejoiced that our prisoners were not pining.

Once a shadow stepped out into the foreground and stood on the edge of a stream of moonlight; next morning I gave the lord of that shadow two bottles of Heidsick champagne, and begged that he would keep it for the entertainment of the vision.
