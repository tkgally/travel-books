And all this while the original sufferers, with an exemplary exhibition of the largest faith, were waiting—waiting for Commodore Lambert to make up his mind whether his heathen friend was the most atrocious cut-throat or just the best fellow in the world—waiting in some instances with their limbs in fetters, in all with their losses unrepaired, their insults unredressed.

Especially had our American missionaries, laboring in the field of Judson, tasted of the cruelty with which all alike had been entertained, and the brave and admirable Kincaid can this day bear witness, even with scars, to the success of the foreign policy of Burmah.
