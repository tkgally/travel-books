As for the others, they tittered, and giggled, and simpered, and smirked, and pouted, and bridled in the good old way, the same in Yangeenchinyah as in Yankee land. When we pointed to the guns, and made faces, and said “boom!” they screamed little screams, and fluttered off a yard or two, and then returned, did-you-evering and no-I-nevering as plainly as mouths, and eye-brows, and hands could do it. And when we pointed to the white rolls of “stowed” hammocks in the nettings, and said “boom!” to them also, they played over again their little foolish trepidations as naturally as before, and as prettily.
