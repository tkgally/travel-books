The <i>Phlegethon</i> had dropped anchor, and the guns were being run out for the enemy, when the boats arrived opposite the houses. At once, nearly two hundred dacoits, headed by the very men who had hailed us, rushed from between the thicket and the houses, and, running to the edge of the bank, fired a volley into the thickest of the boats—and again, and again, before the steamer, now lying quite across the stream, could answer. Many were wounded and not a few killed, and the uproar and consternation were at their height.
