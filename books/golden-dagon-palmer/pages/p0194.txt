They so crowded the ship that it was only with indefatigable watchfulness and pains-taking, lest many of them should be killed, that she could be steered. And in spite of all our care, more than a score of boats were crushed against the sides, or caught in the wheels and swamped—the people in them being rescued not without some severe hurts.
