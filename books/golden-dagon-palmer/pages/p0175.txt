A devoted missionary, Mr. Malcolm, said of Boodhism: “In almost every respect it seems to be the best religion which man has ever invented.” And when, at Doonoobyoo, I asked a poonghee: “What is Boodh?” he answered: “Boodh is you, and I, and all men; when you are I, and I am you, and both are at rest, that is Boodh.” I think I understood him.
