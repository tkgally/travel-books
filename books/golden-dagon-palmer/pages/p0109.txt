As soon as the ships’ firing ceased, the <i>Phlegethon’s</i> cutters were sent to destroy their boats along shore and pick up such arms as could be found. Among the latter were some venerable United States muskets from the Harper’s Ferry Armory. How came they there?

One pretty incident occurred in the midst of this affair, which is worth narrating. Just in the hottest of the fire, and when every shot was telling on the stockade, two war-boats emerged from a narrow creek, behind the upper angle of the works, filled with armed men—perhaps sixty in each—and commanded by a distinguished-looking chief, very handsomely attired, and wearing a <i>dhar</i> (or sword, curved like a scimetar, but not, like that weapon, terminating in a broad point), with golden hilt, and scabbard of chased gold.
