And rockets, wheels, and fountains were stuck thickly on the sides and backs of elephants, oxen, horses, and bears, of wood, paper, and glue.

Then they laid the golden mummy in its golden coffin, and hoisted it, elephant and all, atop of the staging under the white umbrellas; and they mounted the whole upon wheels, and dragged it forth outside the town, to an open place they had cleared in the jungle with fire, banging their gongs the while, beating their drums, blowing their horns, waving their flags—the priests howling, and the boys singing, and the demi-nude girls dancing, the young men flourishing their paddles and dhars, and the old women scolding for alms.
