They salaamed to the passing steamers, the mothers bouncing their little ones and laughing, the girls waving white cloths and tossing flowers in the air, and the young men and little naked boys running along the bank to point out to us the safe way to Prome, by the creek. And this under the very eyes and guns of a fierce, unsparing savage, at whose mercy they would be even before we were out of sight.

But so it was everywhere. In the lower provinces, which we had just left, the inhabitants were flocking by thousands to take refuge under the British flag, imploring and finding protection and aid. Wherever the people had been allowed the use of their eyes and ears, they had been quick to perceive the advantage of an alliance with the enemies of their cruel, crushing government, and had hailed our advent with a thousand welcomes.

It was now late, and the current was torrent-like. We pushed on under full power till the moonlight left us, and then anchored in the dark.
