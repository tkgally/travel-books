It was a dangerous mistake. Unmeaning, even absurd as it may appear to you, that posture is everything to a Burman; for him it has grave significance in religion and social intercourse. But I did so stoop, and one of them—I remember their faces perfectly—was a stupid-looking fellow, with a rather good-natured expression, if he had any at all; the other was a dark, scarred, scowling ruffian, who looked altogether dacoit-like—a practiced brigand, and a born cut-throat.

Now, mind you, this is the impression they left upon my memory, or my imagination: I don't mean to insist that they did really look so; for, likely enough, if I could see them now, I should be ashamed of the injustice I have done them both.
