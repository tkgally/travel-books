Of such traditions came the inspirations and aspirations of the Maha Bandoola. The old man smiled complacently at the end of his story, as much as to say, “So Burmah, also, hath her heroes and her history, you perceive.” So too, thought I, has the Choctaw Nation; but neither the breech-cloth nor the scalp-lock, neither the tattoo nor the war-paint, neither the dhar nor the tomahawk, has left marks in mounds or caverns which the slate-pencil of a Yankee schoolmaster shall not scratch out.
