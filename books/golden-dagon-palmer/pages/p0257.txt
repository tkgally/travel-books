# Chapter XXX.

[[subtitle]] My Adventure at Pegu.

O<sc>ne</sc> day a message came down from the Myosugi, or head man of the friendly people who inhabited the interior town of Pegu, stating that they had been attacked by a superior force of Burmese, driven from their homes, and compelled to take refuge in the jungle.

The Peguans are naturally a brave race, entertaining a bitter hereditary hatred for the aggressive Burmese, always armed against them, always warring with them on continually recurring pretexts, and always disposed to join with any force hostile to them. They, therefore, informed the General, that if he would send a small party of British troops to their aid, they would attack and retake the place. Meantime their families were in the jungle suffering great privation and exposure, their households scattered, and their property destroyed.
