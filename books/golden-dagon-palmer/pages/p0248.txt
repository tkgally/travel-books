They have their spinnings and their knittings and their darnings, their cuttings-out and bastings and back-stitchings; instead of crochet they have gold-flower embroidery; the latest stories of Umerapoora or Prome, brought by some neighbor in the up-river trade who went up with cotton goods and came back with petroleum, serves them for the last new novel; and they have got as far as sandal powder on their way to rouge. They have their love-affairs and engagements and breakings-off, their small jealousies and heart-burnings—the vanities for the girls, and the household cares for the matrons. So that, in these respects, our freest western society has no advantages to offer them.
