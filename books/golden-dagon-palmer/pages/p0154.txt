And under the awning were two brass bowls; an earthen pot, in which was a handful of cold rice; a roll of filthy mats; a filthier garment that once was red, and might have been a woman’s; a broken-hearted duck; a desperate cock that fought like Horatius; the bleeding body of a white-haired man, far gone beyond his three-score-and-ten, whose temple had been pierced by a ball the devil must have guided; and a creature with elf-locks whiter than his, whose portion in womankind was scarcely distinguishable—partly paralyzed and wholly idiotic—the forlornest wretchedness I ever saw.

We took her on board, and tried to make atonement with tenderness, but all kindness was lost upon her wandering wits. So we comforted the poor creature as best we could, and committed her, with ample provision for her present support, to the charge of some kind-hearted people at the first village we came to.
