Her Surgeon, returning from a dinner party on board a Peninsular and Oriental Company's Steamer, late on a dark night, had slipped overboard and was drowned. Captain Neblitt had a large and sickly crew, the worse for a protracted season of hard work and [[corr privation|privation.]] His orders from the Admiral were imperative, not to proceed without a surgeon. Although there were several English men-of-war in port, the sanitary condition of the squadron required the constant attention of all its medical officers, consequently none could be spared to the <i>Phlegethon.</i> Wherefore, the Captain had been beating the town in the hope of finding some starving ’poticary, willing to be a Company's servant for the nonce, for the ecstasy of being his own master thereafter—or some erratic Sawbones, like myself, with a turn for traveling and no care for the morrow.
