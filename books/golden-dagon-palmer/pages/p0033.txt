At dusk Captain Neblitt returned, bringing my appointment to his ship; the Admiral dispensed with preliminary forms. Before nine o’clock I had reported myself on board and gone the sick rounds. After that I returned to the town, and climbed the back streets in the moonlight alone.

I hope Ay-Chung slept.
