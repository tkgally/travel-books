He then sculled his boat back to the others—the Burmese, meantime, having suspended their fire in the direction of the hospital-boat. You must not forget the boy, whom <i>I</i> had quite forgotten, and did not again remember until I saw him, some days later. When we reached the other boats, I was lifted into the large launch of the <i>Fox</i> under the awning (all the boats were now covered), and laid in the bottom, with my head on an ammunition-box.

The midshipman in command of the party hastened to assure me that I was safe; and, finding me in a state of great mental excitement, endeavored to soothe me. While he was thus kindly employed, I became insensible for a time. These intervals of unconsciousness must have been comparatively brief, as the whole affair occupied only the middle of a day.
