Shortly afterward the spire fell in a thunder-storm, and about the same time accounts were received at court, of the arrival of the British expedition; upon which the architect was sent for from prison, taken to the place of execution, and forthwith decapitated. This, although upon a small scale, is a fair example both of the despotism and superstition by which this people are borne down.

“On another occasion, the king, for a very slight offense, had forty of his highest officers laid on their faces in the public street, before the palace wall—kept for hours in a broiling sun with a beam extended across their bodies.”
