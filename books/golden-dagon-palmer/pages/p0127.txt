We left him “hyfalutin,” and, in half an hour, came upon a remarkably strong stockade. On the bank, between it and the river, a party of nearly naked fellows, commanded by a busy-body in a red helmet, were training a monstrous black gun on us, which they had mounted upon some logs of teak. The thing certainly looked formidable, and we had reason to apprehend that a well-directed shot from it would cut us clean in two. Before we could bring our forward gun to bear—the channel being too narrow, and the current too strong, to enable us to place the ship in position, encumbered as we were with the <i>Serpent</i>—they fired.

Although they seemed to have got nearly the proper bearings and elevation, their shot flew wide—we could not discover whither. Crowding on steam, we pressed forward to reply, withholding our fire for a short range
