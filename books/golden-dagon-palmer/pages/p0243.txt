# Chapter XXVIII.

[[subtitle]] The Oath and Imprecations—Maidens, Wives, Concubines and Prostitutes.

A<sc>t</sc> Rangoon I attended the Police Court to see Captain Latter, the Provisional Commissioner, administer the famous Burmese oath to some fellows on trial for dacoitee. This oath, as adapted to Boodhist consciences, is so ingeniously terrible that even a Burman dacoit shrinks from taking it in vain. It is inscribed on leaves of palm, bound like other Burmese books, and held over the head of the witness with certain foolish rites, such as the chewing of pickled tea, sticking out the tongue, etc. In the excellent translation of the Padre Sangermano, from the Book of Imprecations, it is almost literally as follows:
