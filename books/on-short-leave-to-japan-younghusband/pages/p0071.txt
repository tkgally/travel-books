Our guide took us to the main joss-house, or temple, and showed us ‘the first three man, now plenty full men.’ They were made of burnished brass, and were supposed to be the first three people created, since which, as the guide truly remarked, the world has got ‘plenty full’ of men. He also took us to a curious old tea-garden right in the heart of the city, where miniature rockeries of quaint forms were festooned about amidst a few bushes. At one corner stood the tea-house. We asked who it belonged to, and our guide answered, ‘First belong big mandarin, he go bad and have his head cut off, now belong Shanghai Bank.’ Let us hope the tenure of the Shanghai Bank will not have an equally tragic ending.
