It began with a sneaking admiration for an ancient daimio’s costume, and ended in the course of a week in our buying out the shop. Now that the fever is off, and the last cent has departed, not the least sense of shame or regret comes over us. We should probably do the same again next week. The old costumes are wonderfully handsome and picturesque, and everything, down to elaborate wigs, swords, fans, and tobacco-pouch, is complete. Next a ‘sword’ era set in, and we are now in possession of sufficient swords to arm a small squadron. Then we got on to cloissonne and satsuma ware; and here it may be remarked in parentheses that a very large cheque goes a very short way where the best of these wares are concerned. A little coolness now set in, and we descended to cheap and effective things in the doll, fan, and umbrella line.
