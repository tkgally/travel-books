Until recently the Cape of Good Hope was not connected by telegraph with England, which gave an opening for the following clever stratagem on the part of Russia. One of the periodical war scares was then in full swing, and a collision between England and Russia seemed inevitable. A strict watch was kept on the Russian fleet, and to every one’s surprise it went off on a seemingly aimless cruise to Monte Video, of all places in the world. But the cruise was far from aimless, for Monte Video is connected by telegraph with St. Petersburg, and is much nearer to the Cape of Good Hope than England is. A glance at the map of the world will demonstrate the strength of the position.
