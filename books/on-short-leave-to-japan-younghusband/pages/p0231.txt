There is not an hotel in the land in which board and lodging cannot be obtained for Rs.7, or 9s. 4d., <i>per diem.</i> This includes breakfast, lunch, dinner, early morning tea-and-toast and afternoon tea-and-toast, hot water, lighting, attendance—in fact, everything.

At Singapore we come to the region of dollars and a slight improvement in accommodation at about Indian prices. Chinese servants supplant the atrocities of an Indian hotel, and we take a step up in the empire of cleanliness.

It is not till we reach Hong Kong that we come across anything approaching Western civilisation in the matter of hotels. Here we find the immense structures and civilised comforts of large hotels in Europe. Electric lights and bells, lifts, carpets, good furniture, good attendance, and a good table.
