In all the hotels, and in many of the houses at Ikao, hot mineral-water baths form part of the establishment. The water is 115° Fahrenheit, and at our hotel flowed into square baths, their tops flush with the floor, measuring 8 ft. × 3 ft. × 2 ft. These baths are divided one from another, and the natural water flows through them day and night. At any moment one can drop in, and find everything ready. Sulphate of soda and iron are the minerals, but very slightly diluted. The water is supposed to be specially efficacious in cases of rheumatism. The beds of the streams in this region become bright yellow from the action of this water, and the people of the place drive a thriving trade in cotton goods dyed a natural yellow by merely pegging them down in the stream.
