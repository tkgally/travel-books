A very lame description this, believe me, of a very beautiful cluster of buildings. Let me ask those who really appreciate Japanese art to borrow, or buy, some book where an expert describes these things. Murray’s book, amongst others, is excellent.

Great heavens! I have forgotten the cat. The cat, which every one goes to see in this temple enclosure. It is called the sleeping cat, and is by a famous Japanese artist.
