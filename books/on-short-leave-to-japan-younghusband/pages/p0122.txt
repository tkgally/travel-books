Takumi-no-Kami, the feudal lord of these forty-seven, being insulted by another great lord, attempted to slay him. But, failing in his purpose, and being taken in the act, he was ordered by the Council to perform <i>hara-kiri</i>, which he accordingly did. To avenge their master’s death, the forty-seven ronins determined to kill the great lord, his enemy. After countless stratagems and months of waiting, they at last found their opportunity. On a dark, snowy night, with the wind blowing furiously, they had their last supper together, knowing well that, whether they were successful or no, their own fate was sealed. With bold intrepidity they attacked the great man’s castle, and after severe fighting slew his guard, and, seizing their enemy, beheaded him.
