The cost of reaching Japan naturally varies with the distance of the starting-point. From London to Yokohama and back, <i>viâ</i> the Suez Canal and Hong Kong, will cost £115 first-class and £65 second-class, travelling by P. and O. The other way round—that is, from London to Yokohama, <i>viâ</i> Canada or America—will cost about £80 first-class and £55 second-class. From London to London, making a complete circuit of the world, and calling in at Japan and other places, costs only £125 first-class.

From India the cheapest and most direct route is from Calcutta, <i>viâ</i> Singapore and Hong Kong. A <i>return</i> ticket to Yokohama is priced at Rs.580,[[fnref 1]] one of the opium-boats of Messrs. Jardine or Messrs. Apcar taking the traveller as far as Hong Kong, and the P. and O., Messageries Maritimes, or Canadian Pacific lines carrying one on.
