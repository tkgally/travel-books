These points his unerring military genius detected as being dominant positions in any important dispute. Two hundred and fifty years demonstrated the commanding value of these positions; and their inviolability was further secured by the provisions of a law which till quite recently made it a capital offence to enter any property of the Shogun without leave. Death by crucifixion was the penalty exacted. Ieyasu was born <sc>a.d.</sc> 1552, and died <sc>a.d.</sc> 1616. And now to his tomb and mausoleum.
