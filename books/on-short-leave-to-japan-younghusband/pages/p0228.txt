Those days we elected to spend at the waters of Takaradzuka (but what is in a name?), in the green country on the banks of an enticing trout-stream. Here, amidst the simple countryfolks, we passed our last days in Japan, and left with the kindly farewell in our ears, ‘Sayona,[[fnref 1]] please come again.’ I think we shall.
