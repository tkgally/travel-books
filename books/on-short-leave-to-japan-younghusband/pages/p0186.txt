# Chapter XIII
[[subtitle]] The Aboriginal Inn

As this chapter will contain our chief experiences of Japanese inns pure and simple, it is hereby dedicated to them. Our way lay along the hoary old paved road, called the Tokaido, which runs from Tokyo to Kyoto—from the capital of the grand old military dictators to the capital of their liege lord, the Mikado. Year by year, for centuries, these old kings of the sword wended their stately way over the abominable paving-stones, a distance of 329 miles each way.
