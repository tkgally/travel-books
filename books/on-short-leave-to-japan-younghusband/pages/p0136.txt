A little history to start with. It has before been explained that a Shogun was a great military ruler nominally subservient to the Mikado, but in reality ruling him and his people with a rod of iron, or, perhaps more accurately, with a sword of the best steel. The greatest of these was the founder of the last dynasty of Shoguns.
