A hundred and eighty miles south of Hong Kong the engines broke down; but luckily, though we were in the direct track of typhoons, it was perfectly calm for the ten hours we lay helpless. During the whole voyage from Calcutta, with the exception of the first two days, the sea and weather were all that could be desired.

On June 12th, passing through a thirty-mile labyrinth of islands, we turned a sharp corner, and found ourselves face to face with the beautiful town of Hong Kong. Here we had to leave the good ship <i>Catherine Apcar</i> and our kind friend Captain Olifent. It is impossible to help comparing the kindness and attention one receives on these non-professional passenger ships with the want of either often experienced on the large regular liners.
