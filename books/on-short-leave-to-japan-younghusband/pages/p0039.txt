Finally, the original and rightful owner of the large basket, the fisherman, comes along and catches the big fish, and eats <i>him</i>. A submarine tragedy in three acts; with the whole company, in a condensed form, assisting at the theatrical supper afterwards.

Two more bright days, with the sea like glass, brought us by way of the Straits of Malacca to Singapore. So far, exclusive of four days’ detention owing to the cyclone, our voyage had occupied eight days, of which six and a half were at sea.
