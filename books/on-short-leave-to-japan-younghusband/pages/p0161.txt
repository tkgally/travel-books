One of the chief products of Nikko is fur slippers. Here may be found otter, beaver, badger, monkey, wild dog, marten, rabbit, wild cat, and white beaver slippers of every size and shape at a moderate price. For a good otter-skin eight dollars were asked; but probably an all-round price of five dollars a piece would purchase any quantity. Beaver and badger were about equal value, and were priced somewhat lower than otter. The other furs were cheaper, some indeed costing as low as 50 cents for a pair of slippers.

Our handmaid, ever obliging, cut out and helped to make up a Japanese dressing-gown. Completed, the garment cost about $1.25; and very pretty and effective it was. Perhaps it would not be out of place to recommend travellers who want the real Japanese article to do the same.
