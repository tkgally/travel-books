The purchase of one of the existing journals, or the establishment of a thoroughly friendly daily paper well served with foreign telegrams, would be an investment which, I am convinced, would repay us a hundredfold in the years to come. For a British Minister to impress upon the Mikado’s counsellors the friendly feeling of the British nation, in face of the bitter hostility of papers published at his very door, is a task beyond the powers of the most silver-tongued diplomatist.
