With the <i>kaishaku</i> on his left hand, Taki Zenzaburô advanced slowly towards the Japanese witnesses, and the two bowed before them; then, drawing near to the foreigners, they saluted us in the same way, perhaps even with more deference: in each case the salutation was ceremoniously returned. Slowly and with great dignity the condemned man mounted on to the raised floor, prostrated himself before the high altar twice, and seated[[fnref 1]] himself on the felt carpet with his back to the high altar, the <i>kaishaku</i> crouching on his left-hand side.
