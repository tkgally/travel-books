Our host of the Arai had written him a short note asking him to give us a helping-hand, and in response he immediately and decisively took us in charge. He cleared our baggage, procured rickshaws, drove a mile with us to the tram terminus, took our tickets, engaged a special car, impressed upon the conductor many parting injunctions, and with profuse bows backed himself away, without even allowing us to pay his rickshaw fare.

Into a special tramcar, which only costs $2.50, you can take all your baggage, and as many people as can be squeezed in. A rickshaw carries from 80lbs. to 100lbs. of baggage. Very wearily we arrived at Ikao, at about 8 <sc>p.m.</sc>; for a short journey which takes a long time always seems infinitely more tiring than a long journey which occupies the same time.
