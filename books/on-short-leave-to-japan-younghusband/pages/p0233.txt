The Fujuja Hotel at Mujanoshita is more pretentious, and larger, than these latter, and there the charge again rises to $3.50 a-head <i>per diem.</i> On Hakoné Lake are a couple of Japanese inns where foreign food is supplied; the charge at these is $5 a day for a married couple, and $2.50 for a single person.

In the purely Japanese inns, where no foreign comforts in the shape of chairs and tables are provided, or anything but Japanese food supplied, we were generally charged from $1 to $1.50 a-head. A Japanese traveller would get identically the same accommodation for 50 cents. Here we have a great grievance against some of the authorities on Japan.
