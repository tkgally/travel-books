B. made us a list of the prices current both in Upper and Lower Egypt, of a number of things in common use—such as eggs, chickens, mutton, and turkeys; and he counselled us to make the most of this information, and where it failed, to follow Ibrahim’s example, and to act as if it was all-sufficient. The advice was good, and we took it thankfully.
