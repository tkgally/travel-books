In the afternoon, a pretty, nice-looking fellaheen woman came to fill her water-jar at our side. C. asked her on board, the better to buy a handsome silver bracelet she had on, and we found her full of fun, ready with her questions, and free with comments on all she saw and heard. Equally at home in her wit and her language, she had the best of our encounter, and her parting shot was a strong recommendation to me that I should increase my “family,” and an assurance to C. that she would never be dull if she only had a sister.
