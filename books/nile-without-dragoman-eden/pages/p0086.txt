It was, therefore, without much surprise that, awakened about three in the morning by an unusual noise, we listened to the taking up of the transverse planks or bars, by which the hold was covered in. Some heavy bale or barrel was then moved about, and finally the bars were replaced, and we went again to sleep.

On Ibrahim’s coming to me in the morning, I inquired the meaning of the unusual and untimely disturbance. Of course he denied that there had been any noise at all. “But,” I said, “why was the hold opened?” “<i>Il banco apre!</i>” said Ibrahim, with astonished emphasis; “it had never been touched!” As I would not accept this assertion, he soon remembered that it had been opened, but that he had slept through the whole operation. On this, I pointed out that, as his bed lay on the very planks that were moved, he must be a hard sleeper. “Ah,” said Ibrahim, nothing abashed, “<i>el hawager sai tutto</i>.
