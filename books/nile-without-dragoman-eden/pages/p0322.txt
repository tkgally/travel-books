It may be as well, too, to add as proof, that Nile travelling is within the reach of men of moderate means; that the whole cost of our expedition from Cairo to Wadi Halfeh and back was only £60 per month. This sum included £40 for the hire of the <i>Lotus,</i> the wages of our servants, the cost of the provisions sent from England, backshish given to the crew, and every incidental expense. The amount was ridiculously small as compared with the ordinary rate of expenditure in a dahabeah; but then we ventured on “the Nile without a Dragoman.”
