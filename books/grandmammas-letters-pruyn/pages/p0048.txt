It made me so happy the other day to have one of our little girls tell me that Eddie had said, they must pray aloud in their rooms with each other; that he had begun, and they were to take their turn each morning.

In my lot, in the Albany Cemetery, is a little monument, on which is engraved the words “Eddie and Annie.” Is it not a little singular that the two first heathen children committed to my care bear these names in the same order? My own precious ones

<verse>
“Have gone into that school
Where they no longer
Need my poor protection,
And Christ himself doth rule.”
</verse>
