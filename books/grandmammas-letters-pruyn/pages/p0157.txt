I will tell you how one little girl helped us. She lives in Albany, and often sends me letters and gifts. But at last she had a Fair in her papa’s garden, and her little brother and sister helped her, and they not only had a pleasant time, but she raised sixteen dollars, which she sent me “to use for the children.”
