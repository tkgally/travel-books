The people here are very anxious to learn our language, and they will do anything to get such knowledge; and though they do not yet care about our religion, they are very willing to read in the Bible when they are here. This makes us all so glad, for we know that God’s Word contains the true wisdom, and if they read it, they must learn something of our God, and how good and loving He is; and then we feel sure they will not want to worship any longer their ugly wooden and stone gods.

It seems very strange that any people can believe that such hideous old images as their gods are, can do anything for them.
