I cannot write you any more from Japan, but I hope the letters I have written, and all you have heard about this country and our school, have been carefully remembered, and that you will continue to feel an interest in the work here.

You must not think because grandmamma leaves these dear children, you can forget them, or cease to do all you can for their education.

This Home and school are now fairly established, and God has most wonderfully blessed all our labor here; but if this work is to go on, as it surely ought, and grow larger and more useful every year, then all the dear friends in America must continue to help it.

I only send these few lines to tell you that I shall expect you to love and work for Japan as long as there is anything you can do.
