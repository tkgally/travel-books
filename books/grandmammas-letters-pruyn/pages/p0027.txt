# II.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, July 2, 1871.

M<sc>y dear</sc> M<sc>ary</sc>:

This is Sabbath morning with us, but in Albany it is only Saturday evening, for you know we are in the East, though I came west to reach here, which seems very strange,—does it not? Some time I will explain this to you; or you can ask your papa to tell you how it is. But, any way, I cannot think of you now as getting ready to go to Sunday school, as you would be, I hope, on the precious Sabbath morning. When our evening comes, and our time for going to bed arrives, then I will think you are in Sunday school, and learning something more
