Then, all along the front eaves of the houses they hang a deep fringe, made of straw, which also waves with every breath of wind, and looks odd and handsome. Another device is so very peculiar, and so much a part of the superstitious character of the people, that I must try and tell you about it.

They place near the front of the house (and you know all their houses are made entirely open along the front, only closed at night, and in great storms, by sliding doors) a large pile, or pyramid, of cooked rice; on the top of this they put some fine straw, then some bitter oranges, next a crab, and, on top of all, a piece of dried fish-skin.
