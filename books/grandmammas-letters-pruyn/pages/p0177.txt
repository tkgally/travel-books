T<sc>o the</sc> S<sc>unday</sc> S<sc>chool</sc> C<sc>hildren in</sc> A<sc>merica</sc>:

My dear young friends, an utter stranger to you, I still feel that it is my privilege to assume that we are friends; and so I am going to write you a good long letter, and tell you something about this Home, and the work connected with it, in far-off Japan.
