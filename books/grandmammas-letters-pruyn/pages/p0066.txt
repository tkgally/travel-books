# VIII.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, May 15, 1872.

M<sc>y dear</sc> M<sc>ary</sc>:

This is the anniversary of my leaving you in dear old Albany, and I might feel very sad, as I think of the great distance between my home and dear ones, and the long year that has passed since I saw them, but God has been so good, and so many things have happened during this past year to make me glad and thankful, that I will not allow myself to be one bit sad to-day.
