After breakfast we prepared to visit a mountain, about twelve miles distant, called Karnozan. This is a very celebrated place, both on account of the beautiful village and grand old temples right on the top of the mountain, and for the wonderful view there is from one particular spot, where we looked down upon ninety-nine valleys. All over among those valleys were scattered little villages and farms, which made the scene most charming.
