# XXVII.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, June 27, 1875.

M<sc>y dear</sc> K<sc>ittie</sc>:

I have some very sad news for you, which I am sure you will be sorry to hear.

The dear little baby, which I named after you, died two days ago. She was very sick a long time, and I fear her mother was too ignorant about babies to take good care of her. I tried to do all I could for the little thing, but all the houses in which our servants live are some way off on our large grounds, and I could not prevent the poor foolish mother from doing many things that I know were very hurtful to the child.
