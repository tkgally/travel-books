# XXV.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, February 6, 1875.

M<sc>y dear</sc> M<sc>ary</sc>:

I have been reading in a book, published by an English gentleman, what is called “The Fabulous History of Japan,” and as I read, I thought I would try and write it, in a very simple and plain way, for my little folks at home, so that you could understand what strange ideas these people have concerning the creation and their own origin.
