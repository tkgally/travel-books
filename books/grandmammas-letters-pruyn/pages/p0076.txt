I was almost sorry we had brought our own food, for the gentleman was so polite, and anxious to do everything he could for us; but, although it seemed rude in us, it was really necessary, for, excepting eggs, chickens, fish, and potatoes, there is nothing we can eat now; perhaps by and by we may learn to like their strange ways of cooking.

But my letter has grown so long that I think I must not try to tell you about the rest of our visit this time. When I write again, perhaps I will finish the story; for many things happened which I thought very pleasant and interesting.

Now I will only say, “God bless all my dear little ones in America.”
