[[cont]]Now, I dare say you think that must be very nice for the children,—and so it is, when, like these little folks, they do not choose very bad things; but I should not like to try that plan with any other but Japanese children, for I am sure there are no others who are naturally so good as they are.

But there are some very evil things in their life, and some of them come from that very want of government and care. They are a dirty, sore-eyed, sore-headed, crook-backed, miserable, and diseased-looking set of little creatures, and neither they nor their parents know of any better life. They throng about one every step one goes in the streets, and it takes a great deal of patience and pity to get along with them.
