He feels so thankful, too, because he has such a good home and kind friends, and says he never could let a baby come before, for he had neither time nor money to care for one; and the next day after the baby was born, he came to my room, and got down on his face, as the Japanese always do when they want to be very respectful, and expressed his gratitude over and over again, for the baby, and then he said I must give her a name.
