The large room, of which you see the side, is the school-room proper, and in it not only our school assembles, but all the services of the native Christian church are held, except one preaching service on Sabbath morning, which is held at another place. Here, too, our dear Christian girls hold their precious prayer-meetings; and our family worship on Sunday morning—which is really quite a little service, with an audience, when all our household are assembled, of over sixty souls—is also observed in this room.
