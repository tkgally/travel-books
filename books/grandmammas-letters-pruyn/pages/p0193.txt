And now I want you to call this your Japanese baby, and I want you to pray for her. You know God hears your prayers, for He sent you a dear little sister when you asked for her; and so you can pray for this little Japanese child, believing that God will answer you. This is all you can do now, but if she lives to be large enough to come into our school, then perhaps you can help support her.

God is very good to me in giving me so many dear little children to love, not only in my own old home, but here in this new one; and I know, too, they love me. If you were to see how they come <i>close</i> to me, and try to see how many can get within the circle of my arms, you would be amused, I think.
