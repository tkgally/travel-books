“Yes,” she said; “and I’ll tell him he must love Jesus, or he can’t go.” And then, after a moment, she said, “Last time, when I went to my papa’s house, I told him he must pray to Jesus, and he said he would.”

“Did you sing for him?” I asked.

“O yes,” she said, “I sing and I pray, and I make my papa pray too.”

Can this not be the secret of the change the doctor spoke of? I thought. Is not the influence of this dear child the cord by which God is lifting this ungodly father up to a purer and better life? I do hope it may prove so, and we will all rejoice and praise God.
