But I have been going too fast. I must go back and tell you what a merry time some of the girls and younger children had, “popping” corn and stringing it for the tree. It was great fun for them, for many of them had never seen such a thing before. The best of that “pop-corn” to me was, that it grew on our own farm. General Capron gave me some seed, and I, thinking all the time of the pleasure it would give, had a good deal planted, and now we have enjoyed it just as I knew we would. Then we made large bags of mosquito-netting, and filled them with pop-corn and Japanese candy, and hung them on the tree, one for every child.
