As I looked at the wretched creatures, I could hardly think they were men. It was the most dreadful exhibition of heathen idolatry I have ever seen; and O, how it made my heart ache for the poor, ignorant creatures that can call that worship!

But there is a bright side to my visit up on those beautiful mountains; and so I must tell you that the true light of the gospel is beginning to shine up there.
