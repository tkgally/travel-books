# XXVIII.

[[right]] K<sc>atasi</sc>, J<sc>apan</sc>, August 2, 1875.

D<sc>ear</sc> C<sc>hildren</sc>:

Shut up this rainy morning in a little Japanese house, with a group of restless but playful children about me, it is not very easy to write; yet I think I must try and have some talk with a few of the dear little ones far away in America, who are so much interested in our Home and children. I can tell them how a part of our family are spending a portion of the vacation, and what there is here for little people to enjoy in a cheap and quiet way.
