There are only three classes in the large room, each sitting in a circle around the teacher; but if you could see the eager, pleasant faces of the scholars turned towards that teacher, and hear the intelligent questions asked and answered by them, you would think the hour spent there a delightful one for both teachers and scholars.

When the hour is ended, no one seems tired, or in a hurry to get away; but punctuality is our rule; and then we like to have them hungry when they stop studying the Bible, for if so, they will be more likely to come the next Sunday.
