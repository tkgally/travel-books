# XVI.

[[right]] S<sc>hidzooka</sc>, May 9, 1873.

M<sc>y dear</sc> C<sc>hildren</sc>:

As you will see from this date, I am writing from a new place. This is Mr. C——’s home; and as I am a sort of mother to him now, I came here to see how nice he lives, and enjoy the pleasure of travelling in Japan. Well, pleasure it is; though if I could tell you all the discomforts by the way, you would wonder how I could say so; but I am so desirous of seeing the country, and getting acquainted with all the customs and habits of the people, that I am quite willing to bear some inconveniences.
