You all know how I love the boys, and you can imagine that little Charlie and Eddie were especially dear to me, for they seemed to come a little into the place of those who were once “my very own;” but we had found by trial that it was not wise to have boys and girls together, unless they can have separate rooms at all times. This we could not arrange; and after talking a great deal over it, and praying for a long time that God would show us the best way, we decided that, on a certain day, their friends must come and take them all away.
