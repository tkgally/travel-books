Yesterday the heathen priest went to the house of the <i>belto</i>, and carried a picture of a god, which he put up against the side of the room; and then he told the father and mother they must pray to it, and put before it some rice and tea, and then the spirit of little Kittie would very soon go to a happy place.

After he had gone out of the house, the <i>belto</i> took the picture down and put it in the fire, and said he “never could pray to such a thing again.”
