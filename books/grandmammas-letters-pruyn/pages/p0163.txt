# XXI.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, March 18, 1874.

T<sc>o the dear</sc> T<sc>eachers and</sc> S<sc>cholars of the</sc> I<sc>ndustrial</sc> S<sc>chools of</sc> A<sc>lbany</sc>:

I did not need the contribution of your “mite boxes” to assure me of your kind remembrance of me and my work, but it awakened my gratitude, and prompts me to tell you some of the blessings our dear Lord Jesus sends to us in these far away “Islands of the Sea.”
