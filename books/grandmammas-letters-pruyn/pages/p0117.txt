Then I went back to the table; but very soon a gentleman called with a workman, to see about repairing the ceilings that had been shaken down by a very hard earthquake. This gentleman was very kind, and wanted to help me; and so he had come to talk with the Japanese mason, and show him how to do the work. The ceiling in the room where Annie slept was one that was broken, and we had to go there to look at it. When we entered the room, there was the dear child on her knees beside her little bed, alone, saying her morning prayers. She did not jump up afraid, or hasten to finish, but, very quietly, with eyes closed, and her little hands clasped, she softly repeated her usual prayer.
