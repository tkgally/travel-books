You may think it funny for women to do this, but you do not know that the women in Japan are not taught to work, or spend their time in any useful way. There are no schools for girls, and so they grow up without learning anything that can fill their minds with good thoughts, or help them to pass their time pleasantly. The poor Japanese women have a very aimless life, and I think playing with dolls is about as harmless a way for them to get pleasure as they could find.
