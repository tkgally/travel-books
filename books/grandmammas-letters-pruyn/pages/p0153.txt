# XIX.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, January 4, 1874.

D<sc>ear</sc> C<sc>hildren</sc>:

While everything connected with our pleasant Christmas is fresh in my mind, I want to have a chat with the kind friends, especially the “little ones” who sent all the pretty things for our bazaar a year ago. I suppose it seems a long time to them, and perhaps they thought they would never hear any more of their work. Yet I have a good deal to tell them of the happiness their gifts conferred upon the dear children and young girls in our school at Christmas eve.
