Then we started for home; but the flowers made Mr. H——’s <i>jin-ri-ki-sha</i> even more an object of attraction than I was, and the poor fellow was so completely worried, or scared, I do not exactly know which, by all the excitement and commotion of our excursion, that when we reached our home he was as pale as a ghost, and for two or three days was really quite unwell.
