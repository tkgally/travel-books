God could do all this work without money, and He could make these Japanese people good and happy Christians through the labors of other persons than the people and children of America; but I think He has been very kind and condescending, that He has chosen to give us the privilege, and that He will be pleased to use the money which even a little child may send from our Sunday schools, to convert and bless the Japanese. And the reason why I think this, is, because there is no country in the world where it is so pleasant to live and work for Jesus, and there are no people in the world who desire so earnestly to learn about Jesus.
