When the people go to the temple to pray, they carry with them some small pieces of paper, which they get from the priests, and on which are written a few characters. When they come near these images, they chew one of these papers up into a small ball, and throw it at the “guardian.” If it sticks fast, then they believe the gods in the temple will hear their prayers, and they go in; and after putting a few cash, or pennies, into the box near the door, they go up to the altar and pray before the gods.
