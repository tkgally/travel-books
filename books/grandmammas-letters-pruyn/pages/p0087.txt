# X.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, August 6, 1872.

D<sc>ear</sc> M<sc>ary</sc>, B<sc>ertie</sc>, <sc>and</sc> K<sc>ittie</sc>:

How I would like to see you walking about this house to-day! and how I would put my arms close, O, so close, around you, and kiss you so many, many times! Well, I cannot see you here, but you do not know how much I think about you, and how many things I see and hear that I would like to tell you of. O dear! if it did not take so long to write it all, how much you would hear from Japan!
