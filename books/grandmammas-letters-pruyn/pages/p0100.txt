The people here are not allowed by the government to do this; and if they are detected in doing so, they can be taken by the officers and put in prison. This is the reason why none of the missionaries have tried before to have family worship. We thought, however, it was our duty to make the trial, and somehow I felt very sure it would please God, and He would help us. So, when it was proposed to Shonoski,—for that is the young man’s name,—he said he was not afraid to worship God, and he would do the best he could.
