I saw a boy one day, who had very sore eyes, go into a temple, which you know is their church, and go up to a great, hideous idol, or image, as high as the top of your room, all painted red and black and white, with its mouth wide open, and its tongue hanging out, and looking, O, so very ugly! and he began to rub his hands all over the feet and legs of the god, and then rubbed his own eyes.
