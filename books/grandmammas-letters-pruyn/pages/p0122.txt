They put a rough board covering on the top; over that they lay a thick coat of soft, black mud, and then, in the mud, they lay the hard, stone tiles, that you will see in the pictures, and which make the roofs look so pretty. After some time the mud gets very dry, and becomes just like dust, and then if an earthquake comes and shakes the house hard, the whole roof will slide right off down upon the ground.

Not only this, but often the chimneys tumble down, and the plaster falls off the sides of the house; the fences are thrown down, and in many ways a great deal of damage is done. Sometimes we cannot help feeling a little discouraged.
