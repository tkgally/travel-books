## Decisions
- Cover picture: the front cover (title readable, dark cloth).
- Frontispiece p0009 (sideways; crop turned upright) set as `frontispiece`: shown on the title page.
- Contents: the letters have only numbers; each entry is “Letter N.”, the first topic the printed contents lists (for XXI and XXIII the topic that names the subject; IV shortened), and the letter's date (said in notes_on_text). The printed contents' lines made one layout (p0015–p0016 had the topics centred). Small-capital place names made alike (Y<sc>okohama</sc>).
- p0184: the picture splits “Jap-|anese”; the draft set it after the paragraph, the word whole.
- The Sonnet pass changed markup only (6) and left no spots for the judge.
- Spot-read (Opus subagent): all 29 letter headings, contents, preface, verse, footnote right; p0225 salutation's small capitals made like the others; “thunder-storm” keeps its hyphen (p0107, printed so mid-line); “Katasi” and the out-of-order date of Letter XI (July 26, 1872) are as printed.
- Rendered look (Opus subagent): no faults. Letter XII's entry “Our Visit to Sakuma, continued” (printed contents) → “Our Visit to Sakuma”: no earlier part of the visit is in the book.
