(5) <i>Railways and British Capital.</i>—Whitaker (1910) gives 1,300 miles of railways, £150,000,000 of British money invested in them, and £500,000,000 of British money invested in Argentina altogether.

(6) <i>Weights and Measures and Coinage.</i>

(i.) The gold dollar is worth about 4s. The paper dollar has been, since 1899, fixed at about 1s. 9⁸⁄₂₅d.

(ii.) In the provinces of Buenos Aires, Santa Fé, and Entre Rios, the old league (still lingering on) is such that—

| 1 square league | = 1,600 “square squares.” |
| | = 2,699·8 hectares. |
| | = 10·425 square miles. |
| | = 6,671·66 acres. |
| So 1 square square | = 4·17 acres (about). |

In the sale of Government land the “metric league” is used; and it is such that—

| 1 metric square league | = 2,500 hectares. |
| | = 9·653 square miles. |
| | = 6,177·85 acres. |
| So 1 hectare | = 2·47 acres (about). |

[I may mention also that “8 kilometres = 5 miles” is accurate to about 1 in 200.]

(iii.) As regards <i>weight</i>, there would appear to be some ambiguity about the <i>quintal</i>. In the <i>S. Y.B.</i> it is given as 101·40 English lbs., or 50 kilos exactly (I imagine).
