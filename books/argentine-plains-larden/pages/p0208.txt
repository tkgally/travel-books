I saw, alas! no more of the beautiful <i>spoonbills</i> and <i>egrets</i> that I used to see in 1888–9; and I could no longer visit the larger lagunas to search for the <i>black-necked swans</i> and other interesting water-birds. All was “trespass” now, and I had to keep to the dusty roads. But the spoonbills, egrets, and various other birds had really gone; everywhere the wild life was disappearing from these regions.

Of the numerous species of <i>ducks</i>, I will only say that I heard from them whistling sounds, not quacking.
