[[cont]]Tupungato. Such an unpicturesque hump it looked! Returning, we reached our camp late; it was <i>very</i> cold, and we ran short of fuel.

Next day (February 27th) we started back for Puente del Inca, and, pressing the pace a good deal, trotting over flat river beds and cantering over sand when we had such easier terrain to traverse, we managed the whole distance in two long days, sleeping only at our old camping-ground by the mouth of the Rio del Chorillo.

So ended my first and last expedition into the heart of the Andes.
