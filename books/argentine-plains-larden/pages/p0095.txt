Even as late as my visit of 1888 I should say that this state of things obtained at estancias of the older school; and I well remember the gap that yawned between us and the peons; I was warned not to try to learn Spanish by talking with them. So it was that during my first visit I received the impression the gaucho class, as represented by the camp peons, was of a low type and not intelligent or adaptable enough to be of use in helping on the development of the country.
