Being quite unprejudiced in the matter ourselves we were ready to agree unhesitatingly; but when they began to speak slightingly of the dragon trees, and to cut down their age from the traditional thousands of years to mere hundreds, we would listen no longer; we have sent home dragon seeds to be sown, and I shall be deeply disappointed if the gardener has to move them out of their thumb-pots under a century.
