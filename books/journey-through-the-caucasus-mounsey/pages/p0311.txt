[[cont]]night at sunset much powder was blown away in saluting the coming new year, and during the day many rockets were despatched aloft, apparently for the gratification afforded by the noise of their reports. The astrologers, who had been busy with their calculations for some time, announced that the sun would enter Aries at half-past 4 <sc>a.m.</sc> on the 21st of the month, and it was fully expected that in accordance with all precedent, the Shah would, like a good orthodox Shah-in-Shah, at that moment admit his Ministers and the functionaries of his Court to the usual private salaam. The earliness of the hour, however, being inconvenient, his Majesty sent for the wise men, and assured them they were out in their reckoning, and that it was quite impossible that the year could commence before he had had his customary amount of sleep. Whether they were originally wrong in their calculations, or adapted them, subsequently to this interview, to the wishes of their master, does not appear; but it was not until half-past seven o’clock that the year 1283 of the Hejira began.

His Majesty received us with the usual ceremony and announced his approaching departure from the capital on a visit to the province of Khorassan.
