# Appendix.

[[subtitle]] Routes.

## I. Poti to Tiflis.

Total distance, 360 versts (240 miles).

Principal Stations:—

Maran.

Kutäis.

Quiril.

Belagor.

Suram.

Gori.

Railway is opened to Quiril and will soon be completed to Tiflis.
