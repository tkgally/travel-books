In its origin and insipidity it thus strongly resembles the food which was provided for the children of Israel in the desert, and of which they so soon tired. Worked up into round cakes, it is much relished in the harems. The trade of Ispahan is very trifling. Its gold brocades and printed cottons have a certain reputation, but as hand-dies are the only implements used in printing, the quantity of the latter must necessarily be very limited.
