Tabreez is a very ancient city. Persians tell us it was founded by Zobeida, the wife of Haroun el Reschid, who delighted in its shady and well-watered gardens, and made it his favourite residence. European geographers, however, place its origin at a much more remote period, and suppose it to be identical with Gansaca or Gaza, the capital of Atropatene—a part of Media, so called from Atropates, who after the death of Alexander made himself its independent ruler. For ages it has been a place of great importance, both commercially and politically. Under Hoolaloo Khan it became the capital of Persia.
