Nor is it in contradiction with our Book of Genesis. It is there stated that “the Ark rested on the Mountains of Ararat.” But this word Ararat is used indiscriminately with Armenia to denote a country—not a mountain; and the country so denoted included the Cardu Range. A further argument in its favour may be drawn from the fact that in the region around Mount Ararat the climate is now too rigorous for the olive-tree, and there is no reason to believe that it was milder in former days.

If, then, this tradition be the correct one, the two cradles of mankind were in close proximity, almost in sight of each other, and I confess that the version of the story which thus places them in juxtaposition has a charm for the imagination.
