In addition to the work carried on in the college and schools, and over and above her labours as principal of the girls’ school, Mrs. Hewlett pursues the system of Zenana work, visiting the Hindoo women in their own homes. These things may even yet, as Mr. Hewlett with pathetic patience and courage hopes, bring about a great awakening in the Hindoo mind. In the meanwhile the benefits of the educational system administered by the missionaries is unmistakable, and cannot fail to bear fruit in increasing measure.

The general position of Christianity in India as affected by mission labour is grimly told in the official statistics of 1871, the latest available. These show that of the 240 millions then peopling British India, only 718,000 were Christians, considerably less than half per cent. of the population.
