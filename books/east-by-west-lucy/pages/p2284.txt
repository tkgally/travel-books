Even beyond these in beauty are the screens, each one carved out of a solid slab in marble and looking like delicate lace-work. Sometimes a whole window is thus wrought, giving glimpses of the Jumna which washes the walls of the fort, and of the green fields that lie beyond. Often it is an open screen over a doorway, designed to promote the circulation of air which is one of the chief ends of the house-builders in India. Wherever the screens appear they are beautiful beyond possibility of reproduction by modern art, and it is well that so many remain undamaged.

In the Diwan-i-khas, or private audience-hall, is another throne of Abkar’s, a slab of black marble six feet square. Like his couch, it is cracked right across. At intervals on the line of the crack are two smudged red spots, whereby hangs a tale.
