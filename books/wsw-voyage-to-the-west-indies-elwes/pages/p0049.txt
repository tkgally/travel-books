The captain, an Englishman, showed us over everything. We then bid farewell to the Bishop and Mrs. Hills, returned on board our steamer, and went back by moonlight to Panama. The boat of the tender landed us near the Consul’s house, and we walked back to the hotel. I must here mention the kindness of the officials connected with the railway and with the steamers. We had nothing to do but to ask leave, and they allowed us to go out in their tenders, or anywhere we pleased.
