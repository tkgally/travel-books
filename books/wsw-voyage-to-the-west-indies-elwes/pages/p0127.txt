Coal fires were burning on the wharf and on the planks, in iron frames, like great cressets, and by the red lurid light the women appeared to the eye and ear like so many demons. A gang of Seedies coaling at Singapore is bad enough; but they are men and not women; and although they were niggers that makes a great deal of difference Two of our friends took Mrs. E. between them, and we got on board through the crowd easily enough. We went to our cabin at once, and before morning we had left the beautiful Grenada far behind us.
