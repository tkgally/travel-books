# Preface.

T<sc>here</sc> have been numerous books already written about the West Indies, but they are chiefly either statistical works, or adventures of naval officers; and although a voyage to those islands is a most agreeable trip for the winter months, but few people undertake it for pleasure. When I wished to go there, I could find no book, with the exception of A. Trollope’s amusing work, that could give me any information on the subject. People usually profess to have a dislike to Guide-books, but in many countries I have often found the want of them; and unless a traveller studies and “reads up” a country before he goes there, he must often be liable to pass by objects worthy of notice without knowing it till afterwards.
