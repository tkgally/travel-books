# Chapter XV.
[[subtitle]] Visit to the Pitch Lake.—The Lapp of Trinidad.—The Vanilla Plant.—Steamboat Excursion.

O<sc>n</sc> Friday, April 1, we agreed to go and see another great lion of Trinidad; viz., the Pitch Lake, and, as several other gentlemen were going too, we made a large party. The steamer which trades along the coast, sailed at seven <sc>a.m.</sc>, and stopped at a place called La Brea, five or six hours from Port of Spain, and there we were to land, as the Pitch Lake was only a mile distant from it. We touched at several places on the coast, and landed and took in passengers; but the country looked flat and uninteresting. At last we arrived at La Brea, and landed. We had heard that horses had been ordered for us, but there had been some mistake, and there were only horses for some gentlemen, who were going to see, and buy, I believe, a neighbouring estate. However, some one lent a horse to Mrs. E., and we all started off together, some riding and others walking, and we soon had enough of pitch.
