The effect on the Rajah’s mind of his visit to Europe may be summed up thus—he became more attached to the English and more alive to their good qualities. Without losing a particle of affection for his own country and people, he saw more clearly what was lacking in them, and determined, as far as in him lay, to labour for the improvement of both. It may be added that he enjoyed his visit so much that he fully determined to repeat it.
