[[cont]]Passed through a beautiful country. Left Dover at 11 <sc>a.m.</sc> and sailed to Ostend, where we arrived a little after 3 <sc>p.m.</sc> I was very sea-sick. The sea was very rough. Stayed in an hotel at Ostend till 5.45 <sc>p.m.</sc> and took our luncheon there. Left at 6.30, and arrived at Brussels at 9.30 <sc>p.m.</sc> Drove to the “Hotel de Flandre,” which is a large and fine building. I felt leaving England.

2<i>nd.</i>—Mr. J. Saville Lumley, English Ambassador at the Belgian Court, called on me at 12.30. In the afternoon drove through the principal parts of the city, and then went to the Town Hall, which is a large fine carved stone building. There are many rooms in which beautiful tapestry is hung up, and some fine pictures of the Kings of Belgium are hung up also. There is a large fine carved room in which the Municipal Council is held, and there is a room for civil marriages also. Then we drove to the Court of Justice, in which two beautiful pictures are hung up—one in which Charles V., Emperor of Germany, Belgium, Netherlands, &c., gave up the crown, and the other in which a document agreed to by the noblemen was signed by one of them on behalf of Philip II.[[fnref *]] After taking a drive to the City, went to the cathedral, which is a beautiful large carved building.
