After the reception had taken place his Excellency Sir Bartle Frere addressed his Highness the Rajah of Kolhapoor, in English, as follows:—

R<sc>ajah</sc> R<sc>am</sc> C<sc>huttraputtee</sc> M<sc>aharaj</sc>,—

I <sc>cordially</sc> welcome your Highness to Poona, and I regard your visit as a great consolation for the grief with which the Government heard of the death of his Highness the late Rajah. Your Highness has succeeded, as his son, to a great and onerous inheritance.
