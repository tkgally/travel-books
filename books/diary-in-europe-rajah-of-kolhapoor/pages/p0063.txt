<i>14th.</i>—Went to a dancing-master to have some lessons in quadrilles. Went then to German Reed’s entertainment, where several small pieces were played, among which I liked that in which pictures became alive and spoke with each other. An actress called Fanny Holland acted very well. At 4 <sc>p.m.</sc> drove to the House of Lords to hear the debate on the University Tests Bill. I liked the debate very much, and the speeches of both parties.

<i>15th.</i>—Called on Lord Lawrence, who was one of the Governor-Generals in India, and who is one of the great statesmen. Then called on M. de Lesseps, who was in the country. At 3 <sc>p.m.</sc> drove to St. James’s Hall to see the assault of arms. I liked the several feats and the sword dance. The men of the 1st Life Guards and the Royal Guards showed different sword feats. This hall is very large, and it belongs to the public. At intervals the band of both Regiments were playing.
