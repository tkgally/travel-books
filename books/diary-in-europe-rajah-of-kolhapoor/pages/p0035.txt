[[cont]]Nyayadish (Chief Judge) and the Chiefs of Boura and Inchulkurrunjee called at 11 <sc>a.m.</sc>

After taking our meals twice we left the Palace at about 3.30 <sc>p.m.</sc> Drove first to Mira Bagh, where we ate some fruit and enjoyed ourselves, and thence returned to the Palace. Returned to the bungalow at 7 <sc>p.m.</sc>[[fnref *]]

1<i>st Feb., Tuesday.</i>—At 3 <sc>p.m.</sc> drove to Captain West’s to see the criminal and civil work, and thence went to the town to see the ladies. Returned at 7 <sc>p.m.</sc>

Left the bungalow at 6 <sc>a.m.</sc> and rode to Pryag to bathe. My cousins and brother-in-law came with me. Returned at 9 <sc>a.m.</sc>

My priest at about 6 <sc>p.m.</sc>

2<i>nd, Wednesday.</i>—Left the bungalow at about a quarter to 5 <sc>p.m.</sc> and drove to the racecourse to see the review of the 17th Regiment. My cousins, uncle, and brother accompanied me. At the racecourse General Adams and Captain Frazer were introduced to me by Captain West. Returned at about 6.30 <sc>p.m.</sc>
