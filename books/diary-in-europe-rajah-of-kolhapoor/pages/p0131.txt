The Viceroy also telegraphed his condolences, which were duly conveyed to the family in Durbar. Nothing, however, gave so much gratification as the following letter, written to the Dowager Ranee, the late Rajah's adoptive mother, by the Queen's gracious command:—

<blockquote>
[[right]] India Office, 5th January, 1871.
<sc>My Esteemed Friend</sc>,—
I <sc>have</sc> received her Majesty's commands to communicate to your Highness the deep regret and concern with which the Queen has been made acquainted with the lamentable event which has deprived you of your son, his Highness the Rajah of Kolhapoor.
