[[cont]]Mrs. M—— gave me very good entertainment. Both of them were very kind to me.

3<i>rd.</i>—At 2.30 went and left cards on Lord Salisbury. Afterwards drove to Mrs. S——’s party. Mrs. S—— introduced some ladies and gentlemen who were her guests to me. Mr. S—— has a beautiful croquet-ground. We played croquet and enjoyed it much. We won the game. After taking a walk through the garden, which is pretty good, went to supper. Afterwards we had some music. Mr. and Mrs. S—— were kind and very polite to me. I enjoyed this evening immensely.

<i>4th.</i>—In the afternoon, called on Lord Kimberley, Mr. Cardwell, the Speaker and Lord Chancellor. No one of them was in the house. Took a little drive through the Park. In the evening went to the Prince of Wales’s Theatre. This is a very gay theatre, though it is a small one. We liked all the three acts, but especially <i>Dearest Mamma</i> and <i>M. P.</i> Miss Marie Wilton and one who acted the Uncle, acted remarkably well. He was laughing in such a way, that he made all the people burst laughing. The Prince and Princess of Wales were present in the theatre. Our coachman having got drunk, I was obliged to return home in a cab.
