# Appendix.

## I. The Durbar at Poona in 1866.

[[center]] (<i>From the Official Account.</i>)

H<sc>is</sc> E<sc>xcellency</sc> S<sc>ir</sc> H<sc>enry</sc> B<sc>artle</sc> E<sc>dward</sc> F<sc>rere</sc>, G.C.S.I. and K.C.B., Governor of Bombay, held a Durbar at Poona on the 29th October, 1866, for the reception of his Highness the Rajah of Kolhapoor, the Rajah of Jowar, the Sirdars and Chiefs of the Deccan and Southern Mahratta country, and other native gentlemen.

On the arrival of his Highness the Rajah of Kolhapoor, the guard of honour presented arms, and he was received by the Political Secretary to Government and the Political Agent, Kolhapoor and Southern Mahratta country, and was taken to his seat.
