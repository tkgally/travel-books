<i>4th, Friday.</i>—Left the bungalow at 3 <sc>p.m.</sc> and drove to Captain West’s to see the civil and public work, and then took a ride through the camp and <i>viâ</i> Aditwar Bridge.

<i>5th.</i>—At 4 <sc>p.m.</sc> went to the town to hear Daji Bora’s Katha.[[fnref *]] Returned at about 8 <sc>p.m.</sc>

<i>6th, Sunday.</i>—At 3 <sc>p.m.</sc> went to the town, and thence, at 5 <sc>p.m.</sc>, with my mother and grandmother and Ebba Sahib, went to the Kale’s house for the celebration of Munj.[[fnref †]] Returned at about 7 <sc>p.m.</sc>

After his accession to the throne of Kolhapoor, the Rajah, as was natural, spent most of his time in his native place, and was but little heard of in consequence; but on two or three occasions he came prominently before the public.
