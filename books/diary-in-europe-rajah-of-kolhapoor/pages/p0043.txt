15<i>th.</i>—Took a drive in Hyde Park and Regent's Park, and through Regent Street in the afternoon. Hyde Park and Regent's Park are large and beautifully green. These are very good parks. Many people ride, drive, and walk in them.

16<i>th.</i>—Went to see Madame Tussaud's Exhibition. The statues which are here are made of wax, and are very life-like. No one thinks at first thought that they are statues and not real persons. I liked these statues very much. They are of English and European kings and queens and celebrated men. Then took a drive through Hyde Park. At 11 <sc>p.m.</sc> drove to Sir Robert M——, to an evening party. Sir Robert introduced several ladies and gentlemen to me. He is a very good and polite man. It rained much to-night.

17<i>th.</i>—Drove by Trafalgar Square and Whitehall to the India Office, where I first saw Mr. Kaye the Political Secretary, and then called on the Duke of Argyll, and afterwards on Mr. Grant Duff, Sir Bartle Frere, Sir Robert Montgomery, and Sir Henry Anderson. Then took a drive through Hyde Park to Kensington Park.
