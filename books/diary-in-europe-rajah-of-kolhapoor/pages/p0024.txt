Kolhapoor and Sattara thus became the seats of two kindred, but separate dynasties. The former is still an independent territory, governed by the descendants of its old Princes; but the latter lapsed to the British Government in 1849, in consequence of the failure of direct heirs to the last Rajah.

The State of Kolhapoor thus constituted did not play a prominent part on the stage of Indian history. In the year 1760 the direct line of Sivajee became extinct, but an heir from a collateral branch of the family was adopted, who, according to Hindoo ideas, carried on the succession as perfectly as if he had been born of the direct lineage.
