# Diary of the Rajah of Kolhapoor.

S<sc>unday</sc>, June 12<i>th</i>, 1870.—Arrived at the railway-station at Paris, at about 8 <sc>a.m.</sc> Went to the Hôtel de Lille et d’Albion.
