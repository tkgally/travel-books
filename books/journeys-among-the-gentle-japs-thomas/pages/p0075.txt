On another occasion, his enemies having decoyed him into a poisonous bath which induced leprosy, his mistress—more faithful than Samson’s—wheeled him in a barrow all the way to the famous sulphur springs of Yunomine, in Kishu, where a week’s bathing restored him to health and strength.

Leaving Fujisawa at nine, I arrived at Yokohama a little before ten, having completed in one day an excursion to which two days are usually devoted.
