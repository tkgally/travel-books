Englishmen may reflect with pride on the fact that the most distinguished member of the staff of the University is a countryman, Mr. Basil Hall Chamberlain, who is professor of Japanese and philology, and is reputed to know more of Japanese literature than any native scholar. He is one of the authors of that indispensable work, ‘Murray’s Handbook.’
