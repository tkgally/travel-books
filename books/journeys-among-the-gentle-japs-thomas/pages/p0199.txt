But that he has a genius for horticulture cannot be denied. Not only does he with great success train up his children in the way they should go, but also his trees. Giant trees as well as dwarf trees are made to grow the way he would have them. The monarch of the forest no less than the young sapling has to bend to his will. Grafting is practised much more than in this country. Canon Tristram instances a full-grown maple tree with seven large branches, each having foliage of a different hue, varying from dark copper to pink and greenish white.

Two or three hours’ ride brought me to Onomichi, one of the most prosperous towns in Western Japan, and a flourishing seaport. It also possesses a few fine temples, one of which—the Senkoji—is situated on the slope of a very steep hill, and is approached by a long flight of granite steps.
