[[cont]]at the other end, consisting of four young army officers and three <i>geisha</i> girls. The invitation was urged with such insistence that I had no choice but to submit. One officer came forward after another and would take no refusal, though the first sight of the company disposed me to put as much of the floor between us as possible. I was rather glad than otherwise afterwards that I had yielded to the pressure and joined the circle, as, notwithstanding a number of bottles of wine and <i>sakè</i> lying about, they did not seem to be much under the influence of drink, while I had an opportunity which cost me nothing of being present for the first time at a genuine <i>geisha</i> party.
