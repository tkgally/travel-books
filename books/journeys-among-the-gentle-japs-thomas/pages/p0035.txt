The typhoon season seems to be indicated by a rhyming verse which is well known in the Far East. It is as follows:—

<verse>
“June, too soon.
July, stand by.
August, you must.
September, remember.
October, all over.
</verse>

Though one hears variations of it, and sometimes its correct version is a subject of animated disputes among the passengers, the above form is given on the authority of Miss Scidmore, whose little book, ‘Westward to the Far East,’ besides being one of the most readable little handbooks for the journey, is also one of the most reliable.
