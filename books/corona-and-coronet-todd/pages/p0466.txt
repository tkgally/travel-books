Halfway across to Oakland a last sight of her gleaming sides was caught between two islands. The ensign and both signals dipped again, three times; her owner and his guests stood with uncovered heads as the little brass cannon spoke a last good-by; and then a certain mistiness, not wholly of the sea, enshrouded her, the white sides grew bluish in morning haze, the big ferry-boat ploughed on, and the Japan cruise of the Coronet was a thing of the past.
