In these cold regions Japanese seem hardly like themselves. Palms and bamboo belong to the national expression. But a scarlet sun was setting in a gray sea as we entered La Perouse Strait; and far in the north loomed low the shores of Saghalien, now a Russian penal island settlement, but formerly owned by Japan. Wakkanai, just below Cape Soya, was now our next anchorage, and by sunrise—Esashi.
