# Chapter VII

[[subtitle]] Hawaiian Volcanoes

<verse>
The reticent volcano keeps
His never-slumbering plan;
Confided are his projects pink
To no precarious man.
Admonished by his buckled lips
Let every babbler be;
The only secret people keep
Is Immortality.
</verse>

[[right]] E<sc>mily</sc> D<sc>ickinson</sc>.

R<sc>emoteness</sc> of the Hawaiian islands from one another is hardly appreciated by those who have never visited our new possession. Honolulu and the island group are synonymous to most persons. Usual maps, too, give suggestion that channels at most separate the islands, which may lie an hour or two’s sail apart. Local steamers, however, require two days and a night for the trip from Honolulu southward to ports on Hawaii, the largest island.
