One morning the whole sea was alive with exquisite spots of radiant blue fire, both on the surface and far down into the water. For two days this remarkable sight continued, though no one was able to identify the startling little creatures so royally arrayed. They were evidently crustaceans, their color thrilling, iridescent, phosphorescent, flame-like.

Bottles, tightly corked, containing each a record of date and exact latitude and longitude, were thrown overboard on alternate days, that by their drift and possible subsequent landing additional data might be secured for the Hydrographic Office regarding the direction and velocity of ocean currents.

Sealskin coats and sea-rugs were much in evidence on this northern voyage, with brisk walks, and afternoon tea by the open fire; while, dinner over, Beethoven and Bach and Chopin filled the evening hour.
