T<sc>he</sc> northern voyagers had made no sign for many days, except an occasional telegram as to progress in the novel journey. But just as I was starting for Esashi, and the travelers in the south were about to embark upon the Inland Sea, and the experiences related in the last chapter, a journal arrived, in which Chief had minutely chronicled, for our edification, an account of the daily adventures of these scientific gentlemen.
