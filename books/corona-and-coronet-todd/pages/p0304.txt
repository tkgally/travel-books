“Anchoring off Tubi, a sampan took us to see the rushing current of Naruto Channel,—less a sight than anticipated, probably because the tide was setting in the wrong direction. Landing after a hard pull, we scrambled over boulders like the New England coast, and up a steep hill, where a fine view was met, of islands, strait, and far blue sea. After this an ideal cruise back to Kobe, where home messages again annihilated space and time.”
