Odds and ends of pleasant sight-seeing or business were finished; and suddenly out of the intense heat one cool evening descended, suggestive of approaching autumn, and farewell.

Mr. Christie departed for England eastward on the Empress of China; Professor Deslandres went to Nikko, waiting for cooler days to begin his homeward trip by way of India and Suez; L’Alger swung loose from her buoy promptly to the moment of Captain Boutet’s intention, steaming impressively away through the breakwater and bound for Nagasaki, while farewells waved from her bridge and quarterdeck so long as figures could be distinguished.
