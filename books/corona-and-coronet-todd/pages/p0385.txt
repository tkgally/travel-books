He not only speaks their isolated language, but is personally acquainted with every individual of that nation in the vicinity; and as he kindly volunteered to take me to all the houses within riding distance, here was a solution of the vexed question of personal approach to these shy people,—perhaps also a solution of Professor Morse’s problem, the collection of Ainu relics. When our kindly host, and his servants with lanterns, conducted us back to the schoolhouse camp, visions of eclipses and Ainu, telescopes and weaving outfits, horseback rides and treasure boxes mingled in new association invitingly in the future, and to the rhythmic beat of the surf dreams continued the picture.
