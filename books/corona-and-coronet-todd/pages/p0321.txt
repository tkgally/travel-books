W<sc>hile</sc> the expedition was thus setting up its apparatus, writing journals for comrades in the main island, and preparing for the eclipse at Esashi—while the Inland Sea party was still exploring the remote bays and straits of those fairy waters, and studying the native character under new conditions, I was hastening northward from one to the other.

In looking about for a guide or interpreter to accompany me to the far wilds of Yezo, I had been fortunate in meeting a young Japanese, formerly a student at the Doshisha in Kyoto, who speaks excellent English, and is a good French and German scholar as well.
