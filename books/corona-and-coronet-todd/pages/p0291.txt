Summer rain descended softly during most of our days at Nara,—increasing occasionally to heaviest tropical intensity, and on the way to Kobe the whole country was practically under water. Rice-fields and gardens were submerged, little houses made islands of themselves, and small boys, arrayed with simplicity impossible to excel, paddled about ecstatically among trees and over fences. We afterward found that the entire island was seriously flooded, and railway travel everywhere interrupted.
