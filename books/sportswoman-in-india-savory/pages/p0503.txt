[[cont]]I could no longer hide telling you that I am attached, or rather I love you, both as an energetic officer of the Commission and as an exemplary model of a thorough Gentleman.

“I should have been exceedingly happy to remain some time longer in your service, had I not been forced to retire by a domestic affliction which overtook me all of a sudden, and which was too strong for me in my old age to bear.

“I am therefore resolved to seek consolation in devoting the remainder of my days to the happy contemplation of the mysteries of the Most High, free from all cares and trouble,” etc., etc.
