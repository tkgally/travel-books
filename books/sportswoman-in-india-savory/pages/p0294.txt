[[cont]]acceptable. But S. paid the coolies first, which was always a trying business.

Once more the noisy circle was arranged. We stood over them with sticks; each man produced his ticket, and as this had been a long day, received four annas in return; we seldom gave them more than two, but their pay was doubled for every bear. At last the hundred and fifty were got rid of, the camp was once more a scene of quiet and order, and we sat down to eat in peace.

I must reserve for the next chapter a beat which was full—too full—of incident.
