BACK again in Mian Mir. It is itself a hideous station and a most unhealthy one. Most of it was originally an old Sikh burying-ground, and it is now known as the “Graveyard of India.” For this reason it has not been made as much use of, as a military station, as was at first intended. It possesses a fine church, close to the General’s house, where we were staying, and an indifferent polo-ground.
