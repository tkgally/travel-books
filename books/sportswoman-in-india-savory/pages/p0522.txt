I go to the very ends of the earth; and behold, my skeleton steps out of its cup-board and confronts me there. I am as pessimistic as ever, for the last thing I can lose is myself; and though I may tramp to the back of beyond, that grim shadow must always pursue me.

After all is said, only a small percentage of English men and women have either the leisure or the means to travel. That the nation is a great gainer through their experiences, that on their own parts they benefit themselves thereby, is certain. But with all its far-reaching delights, travel need not blind our eyes. The “light that never was on land or sea” is—

<verse>
<i>Always shining</i>; if but mortal eyes
Had strength of vision for realities
That lie beyond the things that seem to be!
</verse>
