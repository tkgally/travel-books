The ox-cart of San Miguel has immense wheels with spokes, and is drawn by one ox between two shafts. The women wear a peculiar capote, and the men a <i>carapuça</i>, or broadcloth cap with huge visor, and a deep havelock-like cape depending from it behind.

The largest crater, that of the <i>Sete Cidades</i>, or Seven Cities, is at the north-western extremity of the island. Its longest diameter is three miles. In the bottom of this vast basin are two great lakes: one named <i>Lagoa Azul</i> from its blue color; the other, <i>Lagoa Verde</i>, being as green as the first is blue.

It is, however, in the crater known as the valley of the Furnas, that one finds more that is novel and attractive than anywhere else in the Azores. This valley is twenty-seven miles distant from Ponta Delgada, at the eastern end of the island. It takes its name from its hot springs and geysers of mineral-water, which render it a resort for invalids.
