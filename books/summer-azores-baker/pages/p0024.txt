So he and his gang went forrard, and hauled out the stowaways, and put ’em aboard their boat, and pulled ashore, appearin’ to be satisfied.

“As soon as they were gone, ——, half crazy, says to me, ‘My God! it’s all up with me! What can I do? They’ll come back for me, but I’ll never be taken alive!’

“I knew he meant what he said; for the priest had told me he’d tried to commit suicide, and, if he couldn’t escape, had determined to kill himself. I calmed him down; told him to go below, and keep out of sight, and I’d try to think up something: but says I, ‘You sha’n’t be taken as long as I can stand by you.’

“I knew very well that as soon as they got ashore those ticket-of-leave men would blow on him; and I really didn’t know what to do. Things looked black.

“By this time it got to be dark, and I sat down by myself to think. Then I remembered a kind of locker under the stairs, where the steward sometimes kep’ the dishes he wasn’t usin’.
