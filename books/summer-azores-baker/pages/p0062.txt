There are pictures everywhere, Murillos or Millets to one’s taste; Madonnas on every door-step; nut-brown maids “winding off their soft woolly task with the spindles;” beautiful child-faces half-shyly peering from the square hole that serves for a window in the peak of the roof; groups of half-naked boys playing cards on the walls.

The interiors are bare and poor: one room, rafters visible above; a floor of earth; “woven work of willow-boughs” sometimes partitioning off one end of the room as a bedroom; a loft above it reached by a ladder, and on the floor a pallet of straw.
