The sensation to a lady rider on a small, slippery leather saddle, with a seat insecure under the best of circumstances, can be better imagined than described. The angle of the road was such, that my knee on the saddle-horn pressed against my body, and every upward leap of the horse threw me back, and threatened to unseat me. It was terribly trying to nerve and muscle.

So, by a series of jerks and jumps, we reached the Lavada, and wound in and up till we came out on the edge of the terrible gorge which terminates higher up in what is called the Little Corral. Our plan was to ascend on one side of this gorge to a height opposite the church, descend into the gorge, wind up to “Our Lady” on the other side, and coast down on one of the famous coasting-sleds of Madeira.
