# A Summer in the Azores.

# The Start.

[[center]] <i>Tuesday, June 12.</i>

THE vague idea has become a reality. Our staterooms are engaged. Retracing our steps from the office, an added dignity attends us. We feel as if all we meet must know that we are about to visit foreign shores.

Later, painful rumors reach us of the unseaworthiness of the steamer: her date of sailing is postponed; and the bark, which heretofore we have scorned, begins to loom up as a possibility. “A sailing-voyage would be such an experience!” cry our young folks.
