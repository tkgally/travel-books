# Fayal and Its Port.

[[center]] <i>Saturday, July 12.</i>

WITH fair winds, the run between Flores and Fayal can be made in twenty-four hours. On one occasion a Boston bark was eight days becalmed between the two islands. Fortune favored us, however; and thirty-six hours after leaving Flores we drifted slowly with the tide into the harbor of Horta, the chief town and seaport of Fayal.

A general description will answer equally well for Fayal and for the other islands of the group. The outline of all, as we see them from the water, is a long ridge of conical hills,—I ought to say mountains,—each with a depression at the top. Long straggling villages of white houses on the slopes.
