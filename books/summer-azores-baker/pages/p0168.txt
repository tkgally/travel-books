On the centre-table was a superb antique Chinese plaque, filled with black-edged visiting-cards, which our host exhibited with much pride, as testimonials of the sympathy of his many friends!

We finish our last shopping in Madeira that Sunday afternoon, and at four o’clock weigh anchor, bound again for the Azores.
