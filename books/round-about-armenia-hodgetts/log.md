## Decisions
- Divisions: half-title (front), title page (frontispiece moved to it), preface, contents, the book's title above Chapter I
  (front, as in Grove), 14 chapters with the title as `[[subtitle]]`, the map as a reading division, the index (JSON only).
  The folded map leaf p0315 is `[[blank]]` (the opened map p0316 is the picture).
- Contents: the printed contents (chapter titles only); nothing added.
- The Sassun list (pp. 111–112): printed in two columns read downwards; entries rearranged to run across the rows, two to a
  row, so that they read in order (said in `notes_on_text`).
- Spot-read (Opus subagent, line pitch and scan): p0151, p0155, p0260 were small type typed as body text → `<blockquote>`;
  p0112 “ycung” → `[[corr ycung|young]]`; p0204 “ot” → “of” (printed “of”); p0130 “1895–96” (en dash, as printed, against
  the judge); p0268 folio misprinted “214” for 244. Left as printed: p0052 “tereskés” (elsewhere “teskerés”), Stoïloff and
  Stoiloff, Péra and Pera, depôt and depot.
- keep_hyphens: p0053 hardy-looking, p0085 cross-examination.
