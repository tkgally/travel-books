I was simply awaiting the removal of my own box from the hearse, and certainly intended no heathen rites to supplement the Christian service already held. But getting suspicious finally that “something was up,” I stole by the priests and went silently up to the altar.

There, sure enough, was <i>my</i> box, with Sam’s body in it, for it had the same bunch of flowers and bamboo reed upon it, which had been placed on the lid. My first impulse was to stop the service; for, without my knowing it, they had brought the body in, while I was in the graveyard, and had commenced their heathen rites as usual. As I afterwards learned, the <i>sinjo</i>, or present of money, which I had previously given the priests, made them polite and particularly anxious to do the thing up well.
