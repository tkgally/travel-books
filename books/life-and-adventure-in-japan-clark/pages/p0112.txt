Not succeeding in securing a hawk hunt with the Tycoon, I started off on a grand boar hunt in a wild mountainous section of the country, where no foreigner had ever penetrated before. I left the students behind this time, as they could not stand the fatigue, and took my most reliable guard with me. The governors of the province gave me guides, and ordered nearly two hundred armed men of that section to go with me and beat up the game.

When I reached the neighborhood of the hunting ground, I found myself at the head of a small army of wild-looking Japanese farmers, who carried old-fashioned guns which they fired with a slow match. They had never seen a foreigner, and gazed at me with as much curiosity as I did at them and their queer guns.
