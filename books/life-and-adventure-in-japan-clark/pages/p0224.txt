The chief officers of the Kunaisho, or Household Department, sat on the opposite side of the room from his majesty. Tokudaigi, the lord chamberlain, and several other high officers were in attendance on his majesty; and every thing passed off in a very pleasant and social manner, there being nothing stiff or formal, though there was a subdued stillness in the room.

At the outset dissolving views were exhibited of Windsor Castle, Sandringham Hall, the Parliament Houses, and other English and Scottish places of interest, during which the band played “God save the Queen.”
