[[cont]]below. At early dawn the steamer was sailing down the harbor, bound for Shanghai, China. The Japanese sun-flag floated at the stern, for the vessel belonged to the new Japanese line of the “Mitsu-Bishi” Company.

I looked out of the cabin window at the murky waters of the Yellow Sea, and realized at last that I was face to face with all the dread uncertainties of a long and lonely journey around the world.
