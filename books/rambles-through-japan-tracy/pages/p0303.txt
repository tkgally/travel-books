It seems to me that, guided by prudence, Japan has taken the wiser and safer course, of waiting for the awakening conscience of England to recognize the justice of her claim. Some day it will come; and therein one hope always remains to her. “For there is in England,” said General Grant, “a widespread desire for justice and fair play, to which Japan need never appeal in vain.” Let us trust he was right.
