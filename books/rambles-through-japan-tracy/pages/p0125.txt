Went out for a walk through the village, and on returning found a party of travellers in the other room. The afternoon was very warm; there was little or no wind stirring, and the landlady by signs requested that I permit the screens to be removed so as to allow of as much circulation of air as possible. I did so, and the result was that I occupied one-half of a large room during the rest of my stay.

A little comedy was enacted in the street opposite my door. A naked boy, seven or eight years old, refused to be scrubbed in the stream flowing through the village, and resisted with tooth and nail. A crowd, largely of youthful sympathizers, gathered about him. The women, however, berate him soundly, and his mother administers most emphatic condemnation of his obstinacy. It ended in his being by main force duly washed, in spite of constant screaming and much ill-directed effort.

<i>August</i> 15.—Where yesterday ended and to-day's record began is somewhat uncertain in my mind. I have had a night's experience in a poor inn; it is good enough for a single occasion, but one does not desire frequent repetitions.
