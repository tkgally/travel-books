Transit by sea to Brazil and the River Plate is carried on by a crowd of shipping companies. Thirty-five years ago the only line to Rio was the Royal Mail, which despatched one vessel a month. Those were the palmy days when 9<i>l.</i> or 10<i>l.</i> a ton was paid for freight. At the present time there are 450 steamers yearly from European ports to Brazil, and ships are glad to get freights at 1<i>l.</i> a ton. At Santos on our return we were offered a cargo of coffee to be delivered in London at 10<i>s.</i> a ton. As the Royal Mail discharges at Southampton and as the charges from Southampton to London are 7<i>s.</i> 6<i>d.</i> a ton, it is hardly necessary to say that the coffee was not shipped.
