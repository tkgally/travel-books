The Bahia and San Francisco railway is a line which probably would never have been built but for the Government guarantee granted to gain favour with the province. It goes through much barren country and has cost 22,500<i>l.</i> a mile. The dividends are paid out of the guarantee. It seems probable, however, that if the staff at unimportant stations were reduced and the line worked on thoroughly economical principles, it would pay a small dividend, perhaps one or two per cent., independent of the guarantee. It is better, however, for Brazil to build railways that yield no return upon the capital sunk in making them than squander her resources upon useless ships of war, plenty of which may be seen rotting in the bay of Rio.
