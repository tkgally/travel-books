# Chapter XIV.

[[subtitle]] Salto.

L<sc>ike</sc> other towns and cities of the River Plate, Salto, in its street architecture, bears much resemblance to an Italian city. The reason of this is that large numbers of the working population are Italian immigrants, who naturally build and decorate their houses in a style similar to that which they are acquainted with in their own country. At a little distance from the town, the flat roofs and the absence of chimneys cause the houses to look like a number of large packing-cases, the only fires required being the smokeless charcoal stoves used for cooking purposes.
