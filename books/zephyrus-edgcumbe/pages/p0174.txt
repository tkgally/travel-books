[[cont]]beyond Santa Rosa, the Cuareim is yet a large river, being about three times the width of the Thames at Westminster Bridge. The river, however, is very shallow during most of the year, and even when in full flood the water does not come down with a rush, being mainly flood water from the Uruguay River. The padre of Santa Rosa told us he remembered the Cuareim River entirely drying up, and that the people of Santa Rosa went out and had a feast in the bed of the stream, which is of solid rock, to celebrate the event. I have never seen more beautifully coloured crystal pebbles than those which lie in multitudes along the bed of the Cuareim River. I filled my pockets with as many as a due regard for the feelings of my horse would allow me to carry.
