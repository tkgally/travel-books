The Dom Pedro Secundo (an extension of the same system), built and worked by the Government, is understood to yield very satisfactory returns; as also the Leopoldina line; but after making these exceptions, the railways of Brazil, taken on their own merits, and without having regard to the Government guarantees, do not offer great encouragement to investors. As they have all been built either by the Government or by the aid of a Government guarantee, there is every reason why they should have been laid out upon an intelligent plan. This unfortunately has not been the case.

When a Government grants concessions accompanied by valuable guarantees, thereby assuring the building of the line guaranteed, irrespective of its real need to the community, every place puts in its claim for a line of railway.
