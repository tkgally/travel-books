# Chapter XVI.
[[subtitle]] The Land of Tongues.

A<sc>fter</sc> leaving Salto, we paid a visit to Concordia on the opposite bank of the river, in the province of Entre Rios, in the Argentine Republic. Thence we dropped down the river to Paysandù, passing on our way, a little above Paysandù, the dangerous Corralitos reef, so named from the rocks lying in a circle in the bed of the river. We next passed the entrance to the Arroyo Mala, in the neighbourhood of which is Las Delicias and several other fine estancias, and a little later the bold bluff of Mesa de Artigas, so called because in the war of independence (1814) General Artigas rolled his prisoners of war, sewn up in ox-hides, down the cliff side into the river below.
