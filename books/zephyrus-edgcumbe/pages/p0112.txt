After our first morning’s two hours’ airing, we were taken off to our cells. My wife and I were fortunate in getting a small room to ourselves; if the first-class passengers had been more numerous, we should have been most certainly separated, and packed away anyhow, as the second-class passengers were, with people of all nationalities, perhaps compelled to share a bed as well as a room, with other horrors of all descriptions. Luckily, we were spared this experience, and only learned how these things were, from an unfortunate English couple, who had to endure these additional miseries as best they could in the second-class corridor.
