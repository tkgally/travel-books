It lies wedged in between the two giant rivers, the Paraná and Uruguay, which from their width operate as barriers to communication between the province and the outer world. This, no doubt, has kept the province back, although it lies almost within hail of Buenos Ayres. But the population, though not numerous, is extremely well-to-do, and there can be little doubt that Entre Rios has a very fine future before it.
