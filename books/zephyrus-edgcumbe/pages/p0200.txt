The Plaza Victoria at Buenos Ayres extends over eight acres, and is surrounded by many effective buildings—the Cathedral, Bishop’s Palace, Government House, Opera House, Post Office, and Custom House. Buenos Ayres has about a dozen plazas of from eight to twelve acres in size. The largest is the Plaza Constitucion (16 acres), formerly the great wool market, and now chiefly remarkable for being fronted on one side by the terminal station of the Great Southern of Buenos Ayres Railway.

This station would probably astonish some of the shareholders if they saw it. Its marble halls and staircases, with magnificent lions couchant, are laid out on truly magnificent lines. It would put Euston or Paddington quite into the shade. I went over the station barracas (stores) for wool, hides, grain, etc., and they seemed to be excellently arranged.
