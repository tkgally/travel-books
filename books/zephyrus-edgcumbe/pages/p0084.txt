# Chapter VIII.

[[subtitle]] Slavery.

B<sc>razil</sc> is half as large again as Russia in Europe, with a population only one-seventh of that of Russia. Continental in point of size, her population, mostly black, numbers only 12,000,000. This population is distributed in a broad fringe along the coast, dwelling thickly about the harbours, and becoming sparser as one advances into the heart of the country. Three hundred miles from the coast, Brazil is still ‘trackless Brazil,’ the abode of scattered tribes of native Indians, and for the most part utterly unknown to the Brazilians themselves.
