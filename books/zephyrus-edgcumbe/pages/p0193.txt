When lassoing a bullock, two men fling lassos simultaneously, and gallop off in different directions; the lassos come taut with a sudden jerk, flinging the bullock on the ground and trying girths, horses, and riders to the utmost. Small boys spend hours practising with the lasso at a horned skull of an ox laid on the ground. It is fortunate that horses are so cheap, as it enables everyone to ride. Workmen going to their work ride; the milkman comes to your door on horseback; the beggars would ride if there were any, but luckily there are none, save here and there a cripple incapable of bestriding a horse, who is licensed to solicit alms. The milkman makes his butter on his rounds to his customers.
