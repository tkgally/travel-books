[[cont]]Engine-drivers, however, are held responsible if they run over anyone; this is somewhat hard upon them, for as a rule they are not to blame.

One day when leaving Buenos Ayres by train, we suddenly experienced a jerking and jolting, and the train was abruptly brought to a full-stop; the guard announced that we had knocked over a cart and had killed both horse and driver. In spite of the line being in full sight for some way, and also regardless of the engine-driver’s warning whistle, the driver of the cart had kept calmly on his way, crossing the line under the nose of the engine, the natural result of which was that the cart was smashed up and the horse killed, while the driver fortunately escaped with a few bruises. A petition was at once got up and signed by all the passengers, to testify that in their opinion it was no fault of the engine-driver; it being his only chance of avoiding imprisonment for reckless driving.
