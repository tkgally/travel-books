In England we convey land by documents of formal shape; in the River Plate countries they convey horses thus. In order to facilitate the documentary identification of horses every horse has a conspicuous mark branded on his hind quarters. These marks are carefully described in the government documents of title which all owners of horses possess, and without which no transfer of a horse is valid.
