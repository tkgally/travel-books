For more than a year before the revolution actually broke out, subscriptions were being collected, arms provided, and all other necessary preparations made. Drilling had been going on in Buenos Ayres and elsewhere on the Argentine side daily for three months before the revolutionists crossed the River Uruguay. The outbreak of hostilities was for months so likely to occur at any moment that all along the river trade was utterly paralysed. No one would make any purchases, and stores of all kinds were depleted to the utmost, through fear of military requisitions.
