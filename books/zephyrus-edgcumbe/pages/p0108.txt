# Chapter XI.

[[subtitle]] The Lazaretto.

W<sc>e</sc> cast anchor off the Isla de Flores, fifteen miles from Monte Video, at seven o’clock on a Tuesday morning. My wife and I were the only two first-class passengers who had come from Rio. All the others had come through from ports beyond, and so might quarantine on board the ship in the River Plate—irksome, but nothing more. Our only chance of avoiding quarantine on the island was that the lazaretto might be so full that we could not be taken in.
