At the terminus of the ‘bond’ we fell in with some lace-makers and made some satisfactory purchases. On our way back to Maceio we came upon a funeral just making ready to start, and found that people were even buried by means of the ‘bond,’

Our next place of call was Bahia, grandly situated, as all the old Jesuit cities are, on a long low bluff, looking across the wide placid harbour to the deep blue distant mountains.
