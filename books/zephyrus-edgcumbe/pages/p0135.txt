# Chapter XIII.

[[subtitle]] Revolution.

W<sc>e</sc> reached Salto about ten days after leaving Monte Video, having been delayed at Buenos Ayres for a week, owing to the interruption of the river traffic, due to the revolution in progress on the north-west frontier of Uruguay. We took our passage in the ‘Cosmos,’ and, owing to the local disturbances, we had this fine vessel almost entirely to ourselves. We reached Salto at midday on a Friday, and took up our quarters at the Hotel Concordia, a large modern hotel built in the centre of the city, where they made us very comfortable.
