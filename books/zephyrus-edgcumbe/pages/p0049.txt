# Chapter IV.

[[subtitle]] An Old Felon.

O<sc>ur</sc> abode at Tijuca was White’s Hotel, a delightful rambling place with a curious history. Some 40 years ago an old Frenchman took up his residence here, and built for his home the oldest part of what is now White’s Hotel. He lived a studiously retired life, never going down to Rio, and from his unsociable nature came to be regarded as a queer character by the few neighbours round about. Rio was not then so easily reached as now, there being only rough mule tracks to the city. After living a lonely life for some twelve years one of the old Frenchman’s slaves ran away.
