The situation is no doubt a difficult one, yet one cannot help feeling that, having made the error of allowing landowners to straightway possess themselves of gigantic estates, the State will sooner or later have to face courageously the difficulty of applying a remedy to this state of things.

The true strength and stability of a country lies in an industrious well-to-do resident population. But to assist the growth of small landowners good means of communication are essential. Small proprietors cannot horse and coach themselves over scores of miles of muddy rut-ploughed tracks in a land where no regular conveyances run.
