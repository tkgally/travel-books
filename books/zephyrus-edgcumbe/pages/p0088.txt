Cruelty to slaves takes many forms, not the least being that of feeding them upon insufficient food of the coarsest description. Slaves are generally fed like animals, on boiled barley meal or beans, just tipped into a trough, the fastest eater getting the largest share. A new manager from the United States recently came to a coffee plantation near Rio. He considered that the rations of the slaves were insufficient in quantity and he obtained leave to double them. Finding that they were still underfed for their work, he with some difficulty obtained leave to double their rations a second time. It was not until they had thus obtained four times their former amount of sustenance that they were sufficiently fed.
