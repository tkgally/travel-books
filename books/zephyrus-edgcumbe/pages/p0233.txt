With some curiosity I inquired why these heavy iron gratings were placed over the tanks. The reply was that the gauchos, when these tanks were first erected with open tops, came from all the country round, with soap and towels, and washed themselves in the water, the result of which was that the boilers of the engines primed badly, and had constantly to be sent to the workshops for repairs.

The steady progress of the River Plate is largely due to the uninterrupted influx of Italian immigrants. Although the language of the country is Spanish, seventy per cent. of the immigrants are Italians, who leave their native country (though far from over-populated) to escape the scourge of conscription.
