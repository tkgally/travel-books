# Chapter XIX.
[[subtitle]] Along the Paraná.

R<sc>osario</sc>, though not the capital, is the largest city in the province of Santa Fé. It is the Liverpool, or perhaps, considering its rapid growth, it might be more aptly termed the Winnipeg of the Argentine Republic. Many of its streets are still mere tracks of dust or mud, according to the season, but the authorities are making fair headway, and considering that their forerunners of a generation ago, and even less, were the owl, the hawk, and the wild horse, none of them specially given to city improvements, there is not much to fairly complain about.
