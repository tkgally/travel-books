Its excesses and vices are subversive of everything good and pure. If, however, it is permitted as a popular recreation, then why not confine it strictly to that, and place it within bounds which will prevent the moral and physical injuries which result from its present observance. It is a form of recreation which endangers health through dangerous exposures to the heat of a tropical summer, and which deadens moral perceptions through the open display of vice.’
