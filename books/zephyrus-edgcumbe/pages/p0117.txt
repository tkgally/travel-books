The only satisfactory extras we indulged in were poached eggs at 10<i>d.</i> a piece. There were a few geese running on the island, and we asked whether we might have one as an ‘extra.’ ‘No,’ was the reply, ‘they are kept for the invalids.’ Poor invalids—what a prospect!

On the third day of our quarantine matters in the food line brightened a little, for some kind friends sent us some potted meats, jams, wine, and grapes from Monte Video. The officials thought the grapes too good for us; so they appropriated them. However the pâté de foie gras and the preserved peaches reached us, and though they were not exactly the sustenance we most required, they did duty fairly well.
