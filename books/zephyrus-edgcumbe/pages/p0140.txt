They were further informed that the Argentine Government would send troops to compel them to retire into Corrientes, which is the next province up the river, and lies opposite to the Brazilian frontier. This notification brought matters to a crisis. To have withdrawn into Corrientes would have been fatal to all hope of success. It would have left the revolutionists with only Santa Rosa to make a descent upon, for the rapids below this point render it impossible to drop further down stream for purposes of effecting a landing elsewhere. Moreover, in the upper reaches of the river above Ceibo, there is very little shipping of any kind obtainable, either by payment or by force. It, therefore, became urgently necessary to commence the revolution forthwith, or abandon it ignominiously.
