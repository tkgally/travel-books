This was only one of the many occasions when the innate chivalry of the Spaniard prompted him to walk a considerable distance to point out some place or object we wished to find.

In another moment we were inside the cathedral remarkable in Gothic architecture as possessing the widest nave of any church in Europe. It seems rather extraordinary that little Gerona should have a church with a nave seventy-three feet wide, wider by twenty-nine feet than those of the Cologne and Canterbury cathedrals, while that of the cathedral of Toulouse, which was considered a wonder in this respect can only show a width of sixty-three feet.
