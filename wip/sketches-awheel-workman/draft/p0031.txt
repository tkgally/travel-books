The train was advertised to leave in one hour. Duly impressed by the kindly reception we retired to the side of the room and waited impatiently for half an hour, in the course of which time the affairs of the other passengers were all disposed of. Then seeing nothing of our trunk and no evidence of the usual formalities incident to making a deposit on the bicycles, we again sought the <i>Jefe</i>.
