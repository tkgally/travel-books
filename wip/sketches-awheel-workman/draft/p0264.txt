We mailed from Granada seven small articles, such as are constantly intrusted to the post in other countries, of which only one was afterwards heard from.

At our second visit to Madrid, which was one of some length, we came to the conclusion presumably reached by other tourists, that besides the interesting Armoury the Museum of the Prado is the great attraction of this lively but colourless capital. In a half-hearted way one rides in the Prado and the Retiro, walks in the broad boulevards, and stands in the Puerta del Sol hoping to see Mazantini in <i>vestido de fiesta</i> pass into a <i>café;</i> as he does not, one moves on, suppressing a yawn thinking how much better <i>grande ville</i> allurements are understood in Paris.
