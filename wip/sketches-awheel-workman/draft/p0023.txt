Spain is a large country, and no one term is descriptive of its roads as a whole. It has some that may be called excellent and many that are good, being macadamized and well constructed, with a hard, fairly smooth surface. Many more, though rideable, are rough, badly made and poorly kept up. Still others, and these a not inconsiderable portion in some sections, can only be spoken of as abominable, being now, if they ever were tolerable, thoroughly worn out, or merely tracks in the sand or clay soil.
