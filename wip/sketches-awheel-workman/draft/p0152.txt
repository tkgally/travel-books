What would the Spaniard do without his tobacco? It is his constant solace in all the activities of life. He uses the cigarette as a condiment to his food at meals. At the theatre he cannot enjoy what is being given on the stage unless viewed through a film of smoke. The chambermaid does not disdain the weed as he puts one’s room in order at the hotel. The conductor on the street car takes the fare cheered by its fragrant aroma. The officer on parade issues his orders with a cigar between his lips. Its fumes even take the place of incense on occasion with the clergyman as he performs the last rites to the departed. A present of a cigar makes a Spaniard your friend, and the traveller’s path is often smoothed by the timely exhibition of tobacco.
