From Tarifa to San Fernando the road is good and runs level nearly the whole distance through a well cultivated farming country dotted with farm houses of stone and thatch and well-to-do villages, the gardens of which were brilliant with red geraniums and lilies. Here as at the entrance to Andalucia the fields were carpeted with many-coloured wild flowers and fringed with flowering bushes. Scarlet poppies, deep yellow daisies and light yellow ones with deep yellow centres, red clover with long glistening heads, pink oleanders, and purple rhododendrons grew in profusion. In place of fences and stone walls the land boundaries were marked by strong hedges of vigorous century plant, the blossom stalks of which shot up twenty to twenty-five feet in the air like lines of telegraph poles.
