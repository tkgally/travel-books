After witnessing <i>corridas</i> at various places including Seville and Madrid, we should judge from the attendance and the intense enthusiasm of the audiences that this cruel sport still has a very strong hold upon the public. One <i>corrida</i> at Seville reached its acme of excitement when by demand of the people <i>banderillas</i> loaded with detonating fireworks were thrust into the last bull. These discharged their fiery streams upon the shoulders of the poor beast, which, tortured by the explosions, tore about the arena in a frenzy of pain and terror, much to the delight of the audience.

At a <i>corrida</i> given at Madrid under the patronage of the Queen Regent for the families of those who went down with the war vessel <i>Reina Regente</i>, ten bulls and over twenty horses were sacrificed. Tickets were sold by speculators at high prices and from noon on nothing but the <i>corrida</i> was of any account in Madrid.
