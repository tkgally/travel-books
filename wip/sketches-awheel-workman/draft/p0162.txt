We consulted together and, although we feared that a man with such a glowing reference might prove an imposition, we decided to take him. Before giving him our answer, however, we asked, “Can you show us the interiors of some of the houses in Tetuan occupied by the descendants of the Moors of Granada?”

“Indeed I can. That is another reason I hope you will go with me, for I am the only guide from Tangier who knows these gentlemen.”

Upon this assurance the agreement was made, and after he had shown us the few sights of Tangier, an hour was spent in trying saddles on the beach. Salem, for so we will call the pasha-like guide, produced a most elaborate side-saddle with back and arms covered with red velvet, worthy of the Queen of Sheba in appearance.
