If in 1829 Irving could say the Alhambra had been so often described that little remained to be said, how much more applicable is this remark to-day? Hence one is not called upon to repeat the legend in regard to the cabalistic signs of the hand and key over the Gate of Justice, but may content oneself with admiring its massive form and orange-red colour, which fancy delights to picture as the result of centuries of Andalucian sunsets. Neither does one thirst on the sweeps of La Mancha nor cross half a dozen ranges of Sierras to resuscitate for the fiftieth time the doubtful tale of the blood spilling of the Abencerrages.
