Some of them were in relations with the court at Fez and as we learned afterwards had been intrusted by the Sultan with missions to the governments of Europe. One or two spoke French, but these were exceptions. We next called upon the Spanish consul, who said he expected invitations for us by two o’clock. A visit to the walls and the picturesque cemetery followed. The latter occupies the side of a hill without the walls, and its tombs and monuments are larger and more elaborate than those of any cemetery we have seen in other Mohammedan countries. It seems to be a favourite resort of the inhabitants, many of whom sat around in groups eating luncheon or engaged in conversation. We then returned for <i>déjeûner</i> and Salem went to take his siesta.
