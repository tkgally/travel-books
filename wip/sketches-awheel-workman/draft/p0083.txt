Upon these the three men seated themselves to watch proceedings. Keeping one eye on the men, who all looked equally brigandish, and the other on the wheel, the repair was soon made.

Meanwhile a fourth and rather better looking individual found his way into the loft and spoke to us in French, saying he heard we were passing through the town, and he would like to talk to us in our native tongue. We seldom stopped in a town, however small, where the man who spoke French, if only a few words, did not appear anxious to assist us by the use of this accomplishment. The country people almost invariably took us to be French, never dreaming that the representatives of any more distant nation could be travelling among them.
