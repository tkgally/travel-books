[[cont]]traveller is expected to go out and forage for his own supplies. In an interview with the hostess on this subject we asked:

“Have you any meat?”

“No, meat is not sold in Jijona after twelve o'clock noon.”

“Fish?”

The landlady smiled a withered smile. “I have not seen fish in many a day.”

“Well, then, eggs?”

She reflected a moment. “Yes, I think I can get some eggs.”

“Good, give us eggs—a great many.”

“How many, four?”

“What else have you?”

“Nothing but bread.”

“Then ten eggs if you please.”

With a surprised expression at the number called for by such <i>gourmands</i> she left us. It was growing dark and, not finding any candle, we went down to the room which served as hall, kitchen, and sitting-room for the family, to inquire for one, when they told us they had none. Hoping candles were sold after twelve o'clock in the town, we went out in search of a shop where we succeeded in securing two.
