# Sketches Awheel in Modern Iberia.

# Chapter I.

[[subtitle]] Passing the Frontier.

W<sc>e</sc> had intended to enter Spain over the turnpike running from Perpignan across the Pyrenees to Figueras, but having learned that luggage sent from France into Spain by rail unaccompanied by the owner is liable to detention at the frontier, which would seriously derange our plans, we concluded to forward our trunk containing extra clothing and supplies by <i>grande vitesse</i> to the Spanish custom-house at Port Bou and meet it there.
