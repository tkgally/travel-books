# Preface.

T<sc>he</sc> following pages are based upon observations and experiences of the authors while on a tour through Spain in the spring and summer of 1895. The tour was made on bicycles, not to satisfy the spirit of adventure commonly ascribed to Americans, though something of adventure must be expected in a country like Spain, nor because there was anything novel to us in this mode of travel—the novelty had long since worn off—but as being the means of conveyance best adapted to our purpose, enabling us in entire independence of the usual hindrances of the traveller to pass through the country at leisure, stopping where and when we pleased.
