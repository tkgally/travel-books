It may be only after several visits that he discovers why his artistic sense and imagination are so powerfully affected by this wonderful structure, and finds the reason in the symmetrical lines of the grand thirteenth century model embellished by the profusion of harmonious fifteenth and sixteenth century ornamentation designed and executed in the most tasteful and careful manner.

Had the original framework been a production of Herrera and the decorations from the hands of the lesser sixteenth and seventeenth century artists, the marvel of Burgos would probably have been only a blot on the architectural landscape instead of standing forth as a shining object in the panorama of Spanish art. With a few exceptions only the best work of the best artists of the four centuries that contributed to its completion was allowed to grace its walls. The mass of sculpture, were it not carried out in such perfection, would certainly confirm the first transient impression of an overloaded interior.
