We asked to see the room, whereupon he assuming the rôle of host led us up stairs followed by the slender man, the <i>patron</i> and his wife, none of whom had anything to say in the presence of the jovial grey-headed Frenchman, for such he was, who was clearly commander in that <i>fonda</i>.

The rooms of the <i>fonda</i> available for guests were three in number, one good-sized room and a small one in front, and a small inner room without windows opening out of the first. As we entered the large room a glance revealed the calling of the occupant.
