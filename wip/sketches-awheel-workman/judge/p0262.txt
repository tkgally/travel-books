This inn was not situated in the dull mediæval part of the town but in a wide street in the modern suburb which was lighted by electricity, but electricity in Spain is not synonymous with comfort.

On the afternoon of our arrival we received a visit from a not infrequent disturber of our peace, an interviewer. As was our custom with these gentlemen we gave him scanty information, revealing nothing of our future plans, and not permitting him to see that our knowledge of “la hermosa lengua española” was as extensive as it really was.
