The fear of the <i>toros</i> was still uppermost in the minds of Don A. and his friends when we said we must leave Avila, and they advised us to wait till the fair was over. We smiled at their fears, bade them adieu, and passing the barrier of animals outside the walls in safety, set out for Salamanca.
