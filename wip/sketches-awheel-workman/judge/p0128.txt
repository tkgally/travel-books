Once the mountains are passed the prodigal fields of Andalucia stretch southward, losing themselves in a wide perspective bounded by gold-shot, undulating hills. Again the contrasts of Spain are felt. Four days ago we left the palm land of Murcia, yesterday we wheeled for hours over the unvarying, toneless plains of La Mancha, and to-day nature spreads before us fields of buttercups and daisies yellow as Bavarian mustard, long slopes of flaming poppies, and gardens of blooming wild roses, in which extremes of colour blend perfectly, as in the great Nibelungen harmonies. Before reaching Granada a night was spent at Bailen and a day at Jaen. From the latter town three mountain ranges had to be crossed before approaching the snow-crowned Sierra Nevada, but the journey was interesting, affording a varied combination of the fertile and the sublime.
