The two or three private houses mentioned as deserving a visit are not of much importance, and even the famous <i>ajimez</i> windows very numerous here have the reputation of being a good deal modernised. These attractive openings, made to admit the sunlight, so Arab in style and name, become a kind of architectural sugar-plum to the tourist, who in the Mediterranean towns as well as in those of Andalucia expects to come upon them in his daily wanderings as surely as he does to eat his salted almonds at table d’hôte.

When he has learned to admire the delicate arches and marble shafts topped by sculptured capitals, it is truly depressing to learn that a noted authority while acknowledging their charm says that, owing to their frequency and similarity of style, he is forced to admit the possibility of their having been made in one place and sent about to different towns as called for.
