A receipt without any figure was then given us, stating that we had paid the entrance duty. Our eyes were opened to one of the peculiarities of Spanish official administration. As the event proved, however, the man did us a real favour, as we were later obliged to dispose of these bicycles, and the larger deposit demanded by law would in this case have been forfeited.

With mutual expressions of admiration and regard we took leave of the courteous official and entered the train for Figueras.
