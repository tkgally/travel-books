Shortly after leaving Pamplona we met with a last instance of disinterested Spanish courtesy. A peasant was driving a mule laden with flasks of wine up a long hill which we were also obliged to ascend. He seemed impressed by our having to push our bicycles, and said he was better off than we as the mule did his work for him. Adding we must be thirsty he offered us wine from the flasks, and upon our declining the offer, urged the matter until we told him we never drank wine <i>en route</i>.

Another incident shows a bright side even to Spanish beggar life. A traveller stopping in Madrid had been in the habit of giving a few <i>centimos</i> daily to a little girl on the street. One morning as he passed the corner where she stood he gave her as he supposed the usual sum. Presently he heard some one calling him, and looking around saw her running after him.
