Often riding was impossible and we were obliged to perform the arduous task of pushing our loaded machines over the soft and uneven mule track, walking ourselves along the ridge on either side. On meeting with teams, which never moved out of their course for us, the inconvenience of getting out of the track and getting into it again after they had passed can be imagined. Still worse was it when we were obliged to pass them, as we had to hurry by on the heavy obstructed roadside in order to mount again ahead.

Another class of roads which caused us much trouble and delay were those which were being repaired. Often places existed where for several kilometres the whole available roadbed was covered thickly with broken stone left to be trodden in and consolidated by travel. Here again, nothing remained but to push ahead on foot till the end was reached.
