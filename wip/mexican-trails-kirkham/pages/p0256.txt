# Chapter XVII

[[subtitle]] The Faith of Cholula

NOW and then a mule-car runs from Puebla to Cholula, a rather squalid town, whose interest lies in the bloody pages of the Conquest, and which must, therefore, be seen through the historian’s eyes.

The great pyramid of Cholula has to-day the appearance of a vast mound of earth, and there is little to indicate to the casual observer that it is artificial. Covered with a thick growth of shrubs and trees, as some monuments are incrusted with mosses, it is only in places that one sees its layers of bricks.
