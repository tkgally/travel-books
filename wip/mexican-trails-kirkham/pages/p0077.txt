Little by little the unearthly cold of those stone walls crept into my bones. I pulled a bed-quilt around me and applied myself in that dim light to the historic pages of the Conquest.

Suddenly an inspiration seized me—why not an oil stove? I called the boy, who in due time appeared, the stove in his embrace. He wore a look of amused wonderment as he deposited the cylinder and retreated. The winter of my discontent neared its end in a genial thaw.

So thinking I applied the fateful match. A murky light appeared and glowered through the little window like a single red eye. Rubbing my hands, I tried to imagine it a crackling log, as I resumed my book.
