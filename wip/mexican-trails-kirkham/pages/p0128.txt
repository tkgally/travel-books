# Chapter VIII

[[subtitle]] The Potters’ Village

THE gentlest of all excursions from Cuernavaca is across the barranca, over the old bridge with its massive Roman arches, and up the steep winding road to San Antone. Here is a little village given over to the potter and his clay; one of the memorable places, a spot pervaded by the atmosphere of a distinct craft, stamped and characterised by it. It is an ancient calling, invested with the spirit of the past and associated with times and with lands whence came prophets and seers. One is reminded of Persia, of Omar and Saadi and Hafiz, and of Biblical times.
