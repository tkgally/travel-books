# Chapter XXII

[[subtitle]] Unbeaten Tracks

T<sc>wo</sc> thousand feet lower than Patzcuaro, on the edge of the “hot country,” lies Uruapam. It is at the intersection of two zones; to the east ascends the temperate, while to the west one descends in a day’s ride to the tropics. From Uruapam I proposed to go across country to Colima. The latest map of the section I could find bore the date 1865, but I reflected that probably the villages through which the route lay had not changed meantime, nor built new or better trails.
