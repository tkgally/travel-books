# Chapter XIV

[[subtitle]] Old Tasco

S<sc>omewhere</sc> in the enchanted distance, in the mountains of mother-of-pearl, lies the village of Tasco. Looking from Cuernavaca in their direction, I had often thought of that mysterious place, where José de la Borda took out his millions upon millions, and where he built his beautiful church, and promised myself some day to depart into that ethereal region of light in search of the fabled village which slumbers amid the hills, and of which only charming rumours are afloat. For no “tourista” ever goes to Tasco; it is not even on his map. It hears not whistle of locomotive, nor crack of cochero’s whip.
