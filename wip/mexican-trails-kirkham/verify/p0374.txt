[[center]] IN THE OPEN

[[center]] Intimate Studies and Appreciations of Nature

[[center]] By Stanton Davis Kirkham

[[center]] Frontispiece in Color after the Painting by Louis Agassiz Fuertes. Illustrated with a Series of Original Nature Photographs

A Nature book of such unusual literary charm that it deserves to be ranked well above most of the more homely out-of-door studies of recent years.—<i>New York Times.</i>

Mr. Kirkham evidently knows his ornithology, and with no uncertain pen writes of birds with the sympathy of a poet and a bird-lover.—<i>Frank Chapman, in</i> “<i>Bird Lore.</i>”

No other American naturalist, unless it be Thoreau, has ever quite surpassed this writer in sensitive register of Nature’s myriad moods.—<i>Chicago Illustrated Review.</i>

He loves Nature as much as Thoreau, and he studies her ways as closely as W. H. Gibson did. The essays are delightful.—<i>Buffalo Express.</i>
