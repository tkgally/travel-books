We would ride down the barranca and up the other hill to the town and loiter indefinitely in its quaint streets—for Tasco is as quaint as Le Mont San Michael. Near by is a little red hill, full of tunnels and shafts, now abandoned, but memorable in the annals of Tasco; for from that knoll Borda took his millions—more than sixty, tradition says—and there lie buried in its bowels a hundred and fifty slaves upon whom the rock caved. Some of this very wealth was transmuted and became the marvellous church.
