If after five years, payment is discontinued, his bones are taken from their pigeon-hole and thrown in a heap in the catacomb below, with countless other bones and skulls. He is evicted, as once he may have been evicted from his adobe hut for non-payment of rent, and after that he has no longer even a number. He has returned to the dust who lived so little removed from it all the days of his poor grey life. Another takes his pigeon-hole—an enemy, perhaps—and becomes number 707. But Pablo de Marez cares not.

Up from Guanajuato a host have come to be pigeon-holed and numbered, here to sojourn for a little while, only to be thrown into the bone heap at last and to lie thenceforth with their fathers.
