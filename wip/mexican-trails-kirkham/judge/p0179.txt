# Chapter XI

[[subtitle]] The Pilgrimage

THERE is now in progress the annual pilgrimage to Chalma, which lies in the fastness of the Ajusco, some thirty miles from Cuernavaca. Of all the pictures which here gently draw one out of oneself, this is perhaps the most clearly defined. Guadalupe, Cholula, Ameca, Chalma are so many little Meccas, regarded with deepest reverence and seen through mists of tradition. While Chalma is of only local importance, the Indians come in great numbers from all over the valley and even from as far as Puebla.
