Is not the market-place the heart of every pueblo in Mexico? But who shall take the village pulse?

Little excursions into Wonderland these, for which I daily forsake the music of the fountain, and the seclusion of the patio, returning to its pensive shades with fresh pictures and new substance for dreams.
