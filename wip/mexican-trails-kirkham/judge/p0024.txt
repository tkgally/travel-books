More than one murder has taken place in the good old-fashioned way, for the bandits are not all dead; and more than once there have been rumours of summary justice, meted out in the equally good old-fashioned way of lining up the “bad men” against a wall and shooting them. Even so, the most desolate regions of Mexico are safer to-day than the streets of New York or Chicago.

While some complain of the attitude of the peons to foreigners, I have found it on the whole not only courteous but kindly. They will throw unseemly epithets, it is true, but only once have they thrown stones at me. Compared with that of an Arab, a Turk, or a Chinaman on his own soil, their conduct is exemplary. The enjoyment of travel, so easily marred by even covert hostility, suffers little here on that score; but indeed it would require much to offset the wonderful charm of Mexico, for few countries better repay the traveller.
