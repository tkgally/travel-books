Its hotels, cold and solemn places, bespeak a grave decorum; as do its shops, where long files of clerks stand on guard, peering out into the sunshine like prisoners from their cells.

But who can describe these diamond mornings? So delicious is the air that one is conscious of breathing. Here are we driving in the Paseo as if it were June instead of December—June in the Elysian fields. Chapultepec is in bloom and in song; while above the lovely Valley of Mexico loom the ghostly volcanoes, visions of infinite calm in their ageless, deathless beauty. One cherishes these early hours all the more that the afternoon is less to be depended on, and at this elevation the nights are cold.
