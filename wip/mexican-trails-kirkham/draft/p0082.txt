In the lobby another audience was waiting for the second <i>zarzuela</i>. I sauntered back to the hotel, through the quiet and almost deserted streets. Gendarmes stood at their posts, their lanterns in the middle of the road. The door of the hotel was closed and I had to rap with my cane before the sleepy portero let me in. It was half-past ten, and Mexico slumbered.
