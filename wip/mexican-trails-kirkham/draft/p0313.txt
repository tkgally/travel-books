It was eight o’clock, and the wind had somewhat abated, when they finally appeared on the shores of Iguatzio, and we paddled under the stars in the silent night to Patzcuaro. The return voyage required seven hours instead of three, because of these fickle moods of a mountain lake. Yet it was well worth while. One cannot go hunting Titians in a dugout every day; and it is good to visit a community who have something they hold above money.
