# Chapter XXI

[[subtitle]] A Strange Quest

IT is but two hours by train from Morelia to Patzcuaro, but the transition is great; for Morelia is a beautiful little city of the old world, glistening on the plain, while Patzcuaro—well, Patzcuaro is a sleepy village lost in the Sierra above the mosquito line. It is quiet enough in Morelia, heaven knows—a place in which to dream and indulge your fancies; in Patzcuaro you rusticate.

Not within the memory of the oldest inhabitant has anything happened here—if we except, of course, the advent of the railroad.
