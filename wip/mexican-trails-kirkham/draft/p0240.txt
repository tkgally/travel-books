The Painter continued to produce good stories from his ample supply. I have never reached the bottom of that barrel. Truly a friend changes the face of the world, and if he be a merry man withal, then may you let down the curtain of your mind and mellow and ripen a little in the genial glow and find yourself expanding.

Since I no longer sit solitary, eating grimly like one who undergoes an ordeal, but break bread with my caste, the days pass pleasantly. In odd corners of the old town I am sure to come upon a white umbrella, surrounded by a group of children, who have learned that in return for replenishing the water bottle there will presently be a distribution of dulces.
