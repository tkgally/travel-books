My mozo on this trip was a bold rascal; I am sure he had been a bandit. He wore tight blue trousers, a leather coat, long leggins, and stuck in his red sash was a revolver as big as an old-fashioned horse pistol. When he addressed me I never felt sure whether he was about to demand my money, or to make a casual remark. However he robbed me only of the amount agreed upon and I can recommend him as an honest muleteer.

At Tonila it is customary to spend the night. It is a mere mountain village, but because of the pack-trains and travellers, of peculiar interest.
