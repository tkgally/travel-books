It is a little world of itself, peopled by birds and butterflies, and perhaps by the shades of the departed, as all old gardens are apt to be. Fancy what whisperings there have been here in all the years, while the moonbeams glanced through the dark foliage of the zapotes and danced upon the lake; what confidences within this garden in the days of New Spain and in all the revolutionary years which followed. The walls, having ears and no tongue, guard many a secret.

One haunting memory they hold, for it was here that Carlotta spent much of her time. There are those who still recall the charm of her presence. She was much alone, and these walks under the mangoes have often known the footsteps of the ill-fated empress.
