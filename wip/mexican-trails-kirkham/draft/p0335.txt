A hen and her brood entered and pecked at stray kernels of corn scattered about. Occasionally they darted under the bare feet of the scholars, dodging in and out with great dexterity. But the indifferent boys kept on shouting. It was their way of studying.

At length the driver was ready, the mule car rumbled away, and the teacher could turn his attention to the rabble of boys. The hubbub seemed neither to confuse him nor to disturb his good-nature. As he was about to resume his instruction, a second customer appeared. Again the small cart was produced; again the smiling barber made his excuses, while the uncomfortable victim, facing the disorderly class, resigned himself to the shears.
