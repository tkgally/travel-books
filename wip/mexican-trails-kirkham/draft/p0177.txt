Some suggestion of the Aztec costume remains in the embroidered bands which the boys wear about the head, with long plumes inserted in the back and falling forward, in the predominance of green—the royal Aztec colour—and perhaps in the peculiar head-dress of the girls. The boys wear over their usual white cotton suits a little coat of green or purple and a short skirt of rose-colour or other shade, while the girls have embroidered capes of different hues. But they have laid aside their sandals and are made ridiculous by long pantalettes and antique congress gaiters.
