When summoned to use his martial powers, however, Don Sanchez shows himself equal to the occasion. A recreant pig appeared one day on his beat, and the law, in the person of Sanchez, seized him by his hind legs and started wheel-barrow fashion for the <i>carcel</i>, the pig squealing with terror and much resisting. What! A pig in the Calle Matamoros? Don Sanchez was scandalised. He should be shown forthwith that this was no by-road of Atlacomulco but the Calle Matamoros.
