Shafts of light strike aslant the vast grey interior, and fall on the old onyx pulpits and on the dusky gold of the great altar with its pillars of malachite. Six priests in vestments of violet, of scarlet, and gold, stand before the altar, and boys swing silver incense-burners, the incense rising higher and higher and mingling at last with the shafts of light. The onyx, malachite, and gold, the rich wood carvings of the choir, the old vellum books dimly seen within, lying open on antique stands, the flickering candles in the shadowy distance, and the gorgeous vestments of the priests create one superb harmony of tone as of some marvellous old tapestry.
