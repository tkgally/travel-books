# Chapter XV

[[subtitle]] A Hill Town

IT is a bond I have with old Benito Gonzales in Cuernavaca that his native land—his <i>tierra,</i> as he calls it—so appeals to me. For, as he says, where is there another like it? In Michoacan, in Vera Cruz, in Guerrero are no such people as here; and as for Mexico—<i>Que borrachios!</i>—what drunkards! The people there have become stupid with pulque. Then again in all Mexico where is there such another view? He loves to put down his sombrero for a moment and discourse, needle in hand, enjoying an appreciative listener.
