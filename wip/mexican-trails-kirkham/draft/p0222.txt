No one spoke. Afterwards the Painter remarked that that particular view was spectacular—but he said it very deferentially. The church dominates all; the village merely surrounds and leads up to it; the mountains are a setting for it. It is the matchless centre of that isolated world which exists in the heart of a vast solitude of undulating hills, range upon range. In the background are impregnable cliffs, cyclopean masses of porphyry, towering above, while far below, the stream has cut its way through limestone and shale, forming a tremendous barranca with precipitous walls. Nature is on a titanic scale. Evidences of convulsions and upheavals are everywhere and it was perchance from these terrible birth-throes that Tasco was born into the world.
