# Chapter XII

[[subtitle]] Tepoztlan and Xochicalco

“GRINGO” was brought to my door before daybreak, and I mounted and rode away by the light of the stars. I had ordered a good horse, with an easy gait, to take me to the ruins of Tepoztlan, but found myself astride a beast built on the Navajo plan, with the gait and disposition of an alligator—deliberate, sullen, and full of malice. At a snail’s pace we crept along the Calle Morelos in the darkness, avoiding the occasional lanterns of the gendarmes and assailed by pariah dogs.
