[[cont]]Liquid mud is being poured into these, thus sealing them like the cells of a mud-wasp. It will filter through in fifteen days, they say, and purify the sugar.

“Nothing but earth and water are used, señor; and we make the best sugar in the valley.”

Be that as it may, there seems to be no further process. It is cool and dim here, as in some old wine vault, too dim unfortunately for the camera, so that one may carry away only a mental picture.

As I return along the highway, still following its time-worn ruts, I am as near as need be to the good old swashbuckling days, which in Mexico took on their own peculiar colour and character. The country has its rapidly developing modern side but this is not the road to it.
