The schoolmaster, for it was he, left off his hair-cutting and came forward with an effusive greeting.

“The house is yours, señor,” he said, his face all smiles, as he begged me to excuse him while he finished with the shears. I thought of the long-suffering passengers waiting without, but forgot them again in the interest of the proceedings.

The narrow room was lighted by several large openings, which served as windows. At the extreme end was a low partition of cotton cloth, concealing, perhaps, the domicile of the schoolmaster. On the floor lay a pile of blue powder, like the bluing used by washwomen. A boy was mixing this with his hands, now and again giving it a little pat.
