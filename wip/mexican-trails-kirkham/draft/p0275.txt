You have a hopeless and baffled feeling, as if you should try to make the acquaintance of a community of prairie dogs. They do not understand Spanish and have not yet heard of the Conquest. While the village of Mitla is but twenty-five miles from Oaxaca, it is sundered by an immense gulf of time from the world of to-day. The twentieth century is not due here for a thousand years to come.
