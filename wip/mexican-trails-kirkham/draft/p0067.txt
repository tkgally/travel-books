At length the dough is kneaded, and the woman, weary with her work, sits back and lights a cigarette, while I turn my steps towards México.

Like all Mexican villages, Tacubaya has its innumerable fiestas. Life to the peon is a series of these, the working days being merely interpolated. In describing the usual life of the village one should really choose a fête day. To the Old-World category of the saints has been added a list of Mexicans, and all must be celebrated. The Virgin appears under many titles—she is Our Lady of This and Our Lady of That. The peon reckons by another year than we: his is a saints’ calendar. He is forever either preparing for a saint’s day or recovering from one. He attends the fêtes of the adjoining villages and the adjoining villages come to his.
