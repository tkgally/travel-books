“So you don’t know Quetzalcoatl?”

“No–o, señor.”

“But you know who San Antonio is?”

“Ah—yes.”

“And if you lose anything, he will find it for you?”

“He can.”

“Why do you put that leaf on your face?” She had a green leaf pasted on one temple, a common remedy with the peon.

“That is for my headache.”

I bade Concha good-bye, more than ever impressed with the faith of Cholula.
