The ruin of Xochicalco lies in the opposite direction, and at about an equal distance from Cuernavaca. The road leads towards the lovely Vale of Tempe and its ever elusive mountains, which, on your approach, seem to recede as the opaline mantle lifts itself from the foreground and falls upon remote chains and peaks.

After leaving the hacienda of Temisco, the trail winds over a desolate stretch of country where nothing grows but the prickly pear. Despite the heat of the sun, the air is exhilarating, and those ethereal mountain chains ever beckon you on.
