<verse>
“Venus bella,
Affeiçoada a gente Lusitana,
Por quantas qualidades via nella
Da antigua tam amada sua Romana,
Nos fortes coraçoens, na grande estrella
Que mostraram na terra Tingitana;
E na lingua, na qual, quando imagina,
Com pouco corrupção crê que he a Latina.”
</verse>

<verse>
Fair Venus
Loved the Lusitanian people,
For in their dauntless hearts,
And valorous deeds in Tingitana,
She saw again displayed the spirit
Of her much loved Romans of the olden time;
And in their language, so near akin to that of Rome,
She seemed to hear once more the Latin tongue.
</verse>
