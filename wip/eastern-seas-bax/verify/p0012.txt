Although to the casual observer small results were apparently obtained, the real effect has been to compel the Chinese to acknowledge their responsibility for the conduct of the tribes on the east coast of Formosa, towards shipwrecked people. Hitherto they had always replied to complaints of foreign nations, by stating that they had no control over the savage tribes on the east coast, and that the foreigners must punish them themselves. This had been the case when an American vessel had been wrecked, and the crew murdered at the south end of Formosa; the United States had been obliged to send vessels of war to punish the savage tribe, in consequence of the Chinese expressing their inability to do so.
