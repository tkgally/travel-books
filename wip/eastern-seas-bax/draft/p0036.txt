On the 19th of November the captain of the Chinese Imperial gunboat “Chento,” and myself, started in his gig, with a crew of six Chinese sailors, to examine the water communication in the neighbourhood. We sailed up the river Han, with a strong fair wind and tide, to the town of Cheek-yuen, about thirty miles from Swatow, in six and a half hours.
