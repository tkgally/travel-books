Notwithstanding this prohibition, there are evident signs that the sulphur is sometimes taken by the Chinese by stealth. In the neighbourhood of the springs there are some very pretty groves of parsamum trees around the farm-houses. The parsamum tree bears a luscious red fruit, which is very pleasant when fresh, and also when it is properly dried. A great part of our ship’s company walked out to see the sulphur springs, taking their dinners with them, so as to make a regular pic-nic of it under the trees, and enjoyed themselves much.
