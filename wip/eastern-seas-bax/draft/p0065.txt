A consultation was immediately held, and it was agreed to send deputies to Koxinga, offering to surrender Fort Province rather than to lose all. They went to his camp, then consisting of about 12,000 men, who were besieging Fort Province. They were armed with three different sorts of weapons; the first, of bows and arrows; the second, of scimitars and targets only; and the third, of back-swords and pikes, three or four feet long, with broad pointed irons at the ends. The deputies were conducted into a spacious tent, where they waited till Koxinga was at leisure.
