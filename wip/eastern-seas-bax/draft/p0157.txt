# Chapter V.

[[subtitle]] Search for a Lorcha—Chin-Chew—Arrive at Japan—Accompany the Commander-in-Chief to Kobé and Nagasaki.

O<sc>n</sc> the evening of the 31st of March information arrived that a lorcha belonging to and commanded by an Englishman had been taken possession of by the crew, and that the captain had been murdered. The crew were Chinese, from the city of Chin-Chew. It was therefore supposed that the lorcha had been taken by them to the southward, and either burnt or sold. The “Dwarf” put to sea at once to search the coast, having the Vice-Consul from Pagoda Anchorage on board to act as interpreter with the Chinese officials at the places we intended to call at in our search. We carefully examined all the bays and long inlets, and made enquiries at different towns and villages on the coast until we got to the harbour of Chin-Chew.
