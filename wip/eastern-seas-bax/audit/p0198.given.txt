The streets of the town run at right angles to each other, and have pavcments of wooden planks for foot passengers in order that they may be more easily kept clear of snow in the winter. The houses are made of wood, and fitted with double windows to keep the extreme cold out. Many of the houses were unoccupied, as the officials had already commenced removing to vladivostok to prepare for the arrival of the governor general there the next year. A great deal of the machinery from the dockyard bad also been sent down. The cultivation was confined to a few kitchen gardens belonging to some of the houses, in them we saw potatoes, beans, peas, vegetable marrow, and a few other vegetables, which appeared grow very well.
