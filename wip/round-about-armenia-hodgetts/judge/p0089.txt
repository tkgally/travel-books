Within the century the gradual awakening of Oriental countries has not been without its influence on the Armenians, and we find them constituting a large part of the population of Constantinople, and spreading themselves all over Turkey and Eastern Russia, Persia, and even parts of India.

From time immemorial they have been a remarkable race, but their geographical position has made their country the battlefield of empires. Alternately conquered by Persians and Turks, they have thrown themselves into the arms of Russia in the vain hope of salvation. Gibbon says that their ancient Christian kings or despots, “who arose and fell in the thirteenth century on the confines of Cilicia, were the clients of the Latins, and the vassals of the Turkish Sultan of Iconium.” The Cilicia of those days is the modern Zeithun.
