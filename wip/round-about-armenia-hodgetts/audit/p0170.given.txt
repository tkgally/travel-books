Armenian professors lecturing on scientific branches of study will be found scattered over the entire globe. The Russian minister of education, Delyanoff, is an Armenian.

It may be objected agaiust the Armenians as a race that they have no national athletic games, and cannot, therefore, find their national life that stimulus for activity which has given England her supremacy, and which founded the Olympian games of Greece. How can we expect a nation ground down by taxes to play when they have had to spend their lives in work? Besides, the Armenians are not singular in this respect. The Turks and Persians, the Russians, and in fact all the neighbouring races, are more distinguished for their indolence than their fondness of games.

But when the Armenians, in common with nearly all Orientals, are gamblers—that unfortunately cannot be denied. It is a vice, however, which they share with the majority of the inhabitants of the globe.
