I made friends with this lady, and elicited from her, in the course of conversation, that her daughters were being educated at the expense of the State; her sons also, in reward for certain services her husband had rendered; but she had many grievances, the greatest being the expense of the coronation. Her husband had been compelled to purchase a new uniform, and had spent a lot of money at St. Petersburg.
