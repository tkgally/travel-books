We went on to visit our loyal native friend, who gave us a welcome, took us into his upstairs front verandah and insisted on our drinking a bottle of excellent port wine which he produced; but port wine is not a refreshing beverage in Africa. The house although built in European style exteriorly, was kept and furnished in the interior after the native fashion of dirt and discomfort.

The country round Dix Cove is so densely covered with vegetation that it cannot be a healthy situation. The open sea before it is the only redeeming point. I was really sorry to leave Mr. Hughes there—he looked so delicate.

On the 4th August we arrived at Axim, which is to the westward of Cape Three Points. We towed Captain Hoare's brig, the <i>Alligator</i>, round with us. At Axim I was sorry to find that Captain Shepherd, the Commandant, had left for Cape Coast, very ill. Dr. Johnstone was Acting-Commandant, and the only white person in the place. He joined me as a fellow Commissioner, and our work began here.
