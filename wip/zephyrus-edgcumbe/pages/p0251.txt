It seems strange that such young countries should so soon be threatened with a land question, but the indifference of Governments to a reasonable limitation of the size of properties, and the dog-in-the-manger greed of big capitalists who grasp at large areas of land, in order that they may demand exorbitant prices for their unimproved holdings, is not a practice peculiar to the River Plate.
