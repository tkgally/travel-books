By forwarding them on to Brazil by the line on the Banda Oriental side, all the troubles consequent upon passing through an additional custom-house are avoided, which would not be the case if they were sent by the East Argentine line. Another thing that will operate in favour of the Uruguayan railway is, that the inhabitants of the province of Rio Grande de Sul, and of the Banda Oriental, are to all intents one and the same people. The Banda Oriental was originally part of Brazil, and although it is now a separate country, a close intercourse has been kept up, and a very friendly feeling exists between the people of Uruguay and those dwelling in the neighbouring provinces of Brazil.

The Banda Oriental has been both the cause and the scene of much contention in times past between the Argentine Republic and Brazil.
