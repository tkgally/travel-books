The sewers were cleaned, and every slum and hole and cranny. Refuse of all kinds was destroyed, and severe penalties were attached to any unclean ways. Yellow fever left the city and only gradually returned some years after the occupation was over. Rio, it seems, has taken warning from the repeated visitations of yellow fever, and especially from the wide-spread mortality of 1886, and has so far stirred herself as to be able to present a clean bill of health in this respect during the past season (1887), no doubt assisted therein by the fact that those most prone to the disease have been carried to untimely graves.

This much is necessary to explain the precautions taken in the River Plate against yellow fever, and our consequent relegation to a lazaretto.
