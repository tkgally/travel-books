Monte Video, like Lisbon, lies stretched out on a long hill-side, and presents an imposing appearance when approached by water. To the left of Monte Video, as seen from the sea, rises the Cerro, about 500 feet high, crowned by a lighthouse. From this hill, the ‘green hill,’ the city obtains its name of Monte Video. Though the cupolas and minarets of the churches make a show at a little distance from the city, Monte Video cannot be regarded as a ‘churchy’ place.
