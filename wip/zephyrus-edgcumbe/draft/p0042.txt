[[cont]]visited later. Before we quite reached the top we quitted the ‘bond’ for a carriage drawn by four mules. The crest once turned we were rattled down at a headlong pace, banged round corners anyhow, and bumped up and down on our seats like india-rubber balls. This we found to be quite the usual style of driving, yet accidents seldom happen unless a wheel comes off, which they regard, however, as a trifle.
