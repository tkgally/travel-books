# Chapter V.

[[subtitle]] ‘Bichos.’

W<sc>e</sc> were the only <i>bonâ fide</i> travellers in the hotel at Tijuca. All the other occupants were merchants from Rio, with their wives and families, or well-paid clerks who seemed thoroughly to enjoy life—kindly friendly people, much accustomed to social ways, many of them living permanently, when in Rio, in large comfortable well-appointed boarding-houses.

After dusk the barata moves about with the activity of a gigantic black-beetle, which it closely resembles in form. It is an insect nearly as long and as broad as one’s first finger, and of a dirty brown hue.
