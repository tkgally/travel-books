The Brazilian Empire and the River Plate republics practically refuse to chart their own coasts, on the plea that as they have no mercantile navy they are not concerned to do it.

The result is that mariners still steer by the chart made by Captain Fitzroy (with whom sailed Darwin in the ‘Beagle’) more than fifty years ago, corrected and amended from time to time by the costly process of reported wrecks on uncharted rocks and shoals.
