[[cont]]Here on several days in the week a very good band plays, and all the rank, fashion, and beauty of the neighbourhood meet together, to see and to be seen. The grounds, covering some 840 acres, are beautifully laid out. In the park of Palermo is a small zoological garden, restricted almost entirely to the Argentine fauna. In the centre of the park is a grand avenue of palms. These palms are some sort of date-palm, the blossom of which is very pretty, resembling a gigantic spike of creamy spirea. The fruit is of a pale yellow, oval shape, about the size of a big cob-nut.
