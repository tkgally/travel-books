[[cont]]possessed of one; for after the boats had left, and we were on our way once more, I found, running at large in the rigging, unclaimed, one of the prettiest little marmosets. We took charge of him and furnished him with a comfortable home in our cabin—a chattering little mortal, equally fond of a fresh grape and a blue-bottle fly. Little ‘Waif,’ as we called him, lived happily enough through the summer, but when the November days came round, he became peevish and husky, and at last one evening went to sleep and woke no more.
