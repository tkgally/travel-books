[[cont]]moored in the offing a perilous matter even on a calm day. The difficulties of it have developed in the boatmen a remarkable cat-like nimbleness of hand and foot. The fishermen of this coast go to sea on little rafts called janguardas, consisting of four or five logs braced together, propelled by a triangular snow-white sail poised endways. At a distance these little craft look like mere white pinions, the log deck being entirely hidden by the water.
