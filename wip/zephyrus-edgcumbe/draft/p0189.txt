| | £ |
| One league (6,660 acres) of good land | 3,000 |
| Three thousand sheep at 5<i>s.</i> | 850 |

| Estancia buildings | 300 |
| Twenty horses at 4<i>l.</i> | 80 |

| Three shepherd huts | 90 |
| Corrals (inclosed yards for cattle) | 70 |
| | £5,490 |

An estancia built for 300<i>l.</i> will not be one of a very magnificent kind, but ‘it will serve.’ A friend of mine who is farming prosperously in the Banda Oriental, after having abandoned New Zealand in disgust, told me that in New Zealand he purchased 800 acres from the State, but that to clear them and bring them into a state of cultivation cost him about 15<i>l.</i> an acre, and he said that his was no unusual experience. In the Banda Oriental no clearing is necessary; nature has done everything except put up the fences.
