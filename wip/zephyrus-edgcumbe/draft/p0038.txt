# Chapter III.
[[subtitle]] Rio to Tijuca.

W<sc>e</sc> reached Rio on the nineteenth morning after leaving England. It is impossible for me to picture in words the beauty of the approach to Rio as we steamed in at daybreak through the narrow gateway in the mountains, flushing first deep purple, then crimson and rose-colour, in the streams of light which ascended from the east, harbingers of the full blaze of the tropical sun. Although the entrance to the Bay of Rio is a mile wide, its true width is so dwarfed by the lofty mountains that stand on either side that it looks like a mere gap.
