# Chapter XII.

[[subtitle]] Up Stream.

A<sc>fter</sc> the meal was over which celebrated our deliverance from captivity, we proceeded by the ‘bond’ over a portion of the sixty miles of tramways, which serve the 120,000 inhabitants of the city of Monte Video.
