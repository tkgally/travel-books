As far as my experience goes, hotels either omit to provide mosquito curtains altogether, or provide such as offer no effectual resistance to the mosquito. Satisfactory mosquito curtains are luxuries only met with in private houses. So detestable do these little brutes make themselves, and so merciless are they, that I think it well to mention an easy way of destroying them by a method not generally known, if one may judge from the number of bedrooms one comes across, in which the walls are blotched all over with the dead bodies of the slain, recording many a protracted hour of midnight torture.
