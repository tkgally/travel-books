The first land we sighted of the new world was Fernando Noronha with its rugged peaks; the home of murderers, for hither are sent the cut-throats of Brazil, the Emperor (Dom Pedro II.) having virtually abolished capital punishment by the simple expedient of refusing to sign the warrants of execution; this branch of the executive not being delegated to a minister as in England.
