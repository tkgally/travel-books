There was a grand dispute waging here, between the agents of the company, of which Mr. Perkins was manager, and a German, who was agent to Lord Dundonald. The latter had formerly bought a good deal of the land about here, and owned some of the Pitch Lake; but he claimed the monopoly of shipping pitch at La Brea, which the others disputed, and a wordy war rose high; but Mr. P. had a boat brought up, and he and several of his men loaded it, defying the others to stop them by legal measures. After numerous threats of law on both sides, they calmed down without fighting, and La Brea soon resumed its wonted quiet.

Mrs. E., walking round the village, bought a lot of pods of the vanilla, which grows on a creeping plant in the interior of the forest, and is difficult to get even here. They cost about one dollar a pound, but a pound goes a long way in cooking.
