# Chapter XIII.
[[subtitle]] Trinidad.—Bocas de Monos.—Port of Spain.—Lodging Houses.

N<sc>inety</sc> miles took us to Trinidad, and in the morning we were in sight of the island, and steering straight for the first of the Bocas or mouths which open from the sea into the Gulf of Paria. To the north-east the coast consisted of high, steep promontories; and to the south, beyond the Bocas, the blue mountains of South America rose up in long succession. We entered the Boca de Monos, or Monkey’s Mouth, a narrow but sufficiently safe entrance, though the current being strong makes it sometimes dangerous for sailing vessels. They generally enter at a wider passage, more to the south. There are several Bocas, divided from each other by small steep islands; but probably at one time these islands were all one, and Trinidad was a part of the great American continent.
