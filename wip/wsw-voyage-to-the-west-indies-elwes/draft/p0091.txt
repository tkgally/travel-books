We dined at Spanish Town at Miss Stewart’s, and drove to Kingston in the cool of the evening. This road, for twelve miles, is through a flat swampy country, and appears the very hotbed of malaria and mosquitoes. There was nothing of interest, except some fine specimens of the barren fig-tree, with enormous trunks and buttresses projecting on all sides, and the marsh by the sides of the road was full of tall ferns.
