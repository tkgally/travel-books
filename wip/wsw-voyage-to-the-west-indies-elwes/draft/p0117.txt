The volcanoes of the Andes do not run their lava down in these long slopes, at least, none that I have seen; for they have been forced up through more recent strata, and they are not composed, like the Sandwich Islands, of successive layers of lava, but of mountains of limestone and other late formations tilted up to an enormous height.
