The Indians were intensely interested in the railroad track—the first they had ever seen; and squatted down, rubbing the metals with their fingers. They hung about all the morning, on the look-out for a little pilfering; and when the train started, some went up to Denver by train, and the rest camped up the Fountain, about a mile below Manitou, to hunt in the mountains.
