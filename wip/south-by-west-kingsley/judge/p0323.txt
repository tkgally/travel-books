Alas for one’s dreams of the floating gardens! One object of our ride was to try and find some trace of them. In vain had we asked all our friends: no one could tell us where they were, and all we could discover were oblong patches of garden, with a slimy ditch between each, which shake, it is said, if you jump on them, and—worst of all, they are covered with onions.

We came round to the canal again, higher up, and crossed by the Garita, where the port-dues are taken from the barges. The traffic by this canal from the south and south-west is enormous.

In twelve months, from 1st July 1865 to 30th June 1866, 102,541 tons of goods, paying duty, entered the city by this canal, besides 29,231 head of cattle:—
