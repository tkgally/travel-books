[[cont]] in the first figure, a gentleman, if he has no partner, may slip in and take a <i>paloma</i> (dove), that is, steal a lady from her first partner: but everything is done so quietly, lazily, and gracefully that you hardly seem to move.

After the dancing was over we parted from all the ladies with the regular Mexican salutation,—a stage embrace, one hand on the shoulder, the other round the waist; and a kiss on both cheeks. It took me completely by surprise with strangers, Señora——, the President's daughter, for instance: but it is the correct thing; and must be gone through as a matter of course.
