A great “Fonction” was going on, as it was the 1st of May, the month dedicated to the Virgin, and all her shrines were covered with flowers, and crowded with worshippers. The organ played lively tunes, which sounded more suitable to an opera-house than a church, the choir sang by snatches, and a wheel of bells on the screen was spun round and round, jingling as the Archbishop elevated the Host.
