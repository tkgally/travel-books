There was a piano in the room—old and of the tin-kettle order, it is true: but we managed some music nevertheless. M. G., the Consul, gave us a capital song in Spanish and Apache Indian, and some absurd little Mexican things; and then I started a Volkslied, and our German hosts went on with one after another till dinner, at 12.30.

At dinner we were introduced to Herr D.’s pretty Mexican wife: but unluckily she could speak nothing but her own tongue, so that we ladies could only make eyes at each other.
