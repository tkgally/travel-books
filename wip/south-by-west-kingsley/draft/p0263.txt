[[cont]]Plaza: but, being built of adobe, which is much used here on account of its being safer, because more elastic than stone in case of earthquakes, the authorities have seen fit to paint it skyblue and yellow. So, like the houses, which are painted in the same way, it has a kind of gingerbread look.

Inside the cathedral there are altars to different saints or virgins along the sides, with life-size figures of the special saint by the side of each altar; they were really very fine; all coloured to imitate life, in perfect proportion and well executed.
