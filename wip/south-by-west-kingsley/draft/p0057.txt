In the morning of Wednesday we were woke up at five by the conductor; when we discovered to our surprise that during the night we had climbed up about 2000 feet, and were now at Altoona, near the top of the Alleghanies. It was a misty morning, so that the views were rather spoilt: but over the summit we caught glimpses through the mist and clouds of grand scenery as we wound round the mountain sides. On either hand were pine forests, some black from recent fires, others with a brilliant undergrowth of sumach and dogwood.
