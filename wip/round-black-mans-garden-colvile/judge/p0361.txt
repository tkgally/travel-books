Early next morning we rounded Cape Palmas, a pretty palm-covered promontory, boasting the unusual luxury of a lighthouse, but nevertheless adorned with wrecks; then changing our course to the east, we anchored at noon off Tabu, near enough to the land to get a good whiff of miasmic gases, which reminded me strongly of Madagascar. Tabu has one large European factory on a big black rock overhanging the sea on the left bank of a pretty little creek, beyond which is the native village and its usual background of palms.
