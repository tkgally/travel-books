At last we turned a sharp corner, and found ourselves in the long-sought-for little river, up which, had we arrived only an hour sooner, we should have drifted rapidly on the flood; but now the tide had begun to ebb just as we did not want it, and our tired boatmen had a further struggle up stream, which lasted till two on the following morning,
