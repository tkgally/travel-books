I screamed at intervals for at least a quarter of an hour; when at last, finding, I suppose, that I did not follow, he left the horse, and came to seek me, but when he saw me, it was some minutes before he would come to me. He shouted to me to ‘come on to him, the ass would not hurt me,’ &c., &c. At last, seeing I did not stir, he came nearer, and heard me faintly articulate, ‘Ils m’ont tué.’ ‘Qu’est-ce que c’est donc?’ I held up my bloody hands, and told my tale. He was for leaving me there fainting, and pursuing the young savages, and it was all I could do to persuade him I was not fit to be left. Then it was, ‘Les vilains diables! Who would have thought it? who could have dreamed of such a thing happening? If he could only catch the villains!’ ‘I did not expect this, certainly,’ said I slowly and feebly, ‘but I predicted that some misfortune would happen to me before I reached Andorre if you persisted in leaving me.
