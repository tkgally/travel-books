Her husband was evidently very much in love with her. ‘Ma femme,’ said he to me the day after my arrival, when he introduced <i>sa petite</i> to me, ‘est très-jeune, nous avons été marié deux années, et elle n’a pas encore vingt ans.’ Her father, too, softened his voice and did not shout at her. He was evidently proud of being the father of La Señora Federigo. She had clearly married above her station, and was ‘a credit and an honour to her family.’ And there was the equally pretty, and more amiable looking, single sister unnoticed, as is usual.
