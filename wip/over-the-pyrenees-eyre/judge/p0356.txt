I wish I could describe the beauty of almost all the scenery of this line of railway, so as to set it before my readers, and show them the reaches of fertile valleys, the blue mountains on either side, and the picturesque and peculiarly built towns, with their spires, and citadels, and castellated walls that rose on their rocky sides.
