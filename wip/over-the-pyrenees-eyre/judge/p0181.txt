For instance, we passed a group of peasants reposing on the hill-side; they might have been gipsies for ought I know, but they had no beauty of form or face; it was only the picturesque grouping of the whole set around what was worth an artist’s going to Spain to see. In the centre of the group stood an ass, laden with packages like mine, minus the crate; but upon those packages, as fearlessly and securely as on a bed of down, slept a child some two years old. It was not a pretty-faced child, but I never saw anything so exquisite as its attitude of perfect repose—anything so beautiful as the curves of its delicately moulded limbs, whose foreshortening—its feet were turned towards me—would have been a study for Raphael or Correggio—anything so lovely as the rounded dimpled arms. What a study for an artist about to paint ‘The Flight into Egypt,’ the whole group would have made.
