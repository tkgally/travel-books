I do not wonder the ancients personified countries; if I were a painter, I should depict Spain as a tawny black-browed woman, sitting on a panther.

At Jaen, the soldier and the miserable man got out, and their place was filled by a pretty <i>donna</i> with her little girl; but alas! the fat man, who took up most space, remained, and we went on stewing as in a cauldron, unable to move a limb, without each other's permission; so closely were we crammed in that small <i>rotunda</i>, till nightfall; when we stopped at a <i>venta</i> or inn, to get something to eat. I detest Spanish diligences, but Spanish <i>ventas</i> are greatly preferable to Spanish railway stations.
