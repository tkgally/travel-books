It is my firm conviction that men don’t like sensible women, even if they don’t preach or usurp undue authority. Most men like babies; something they can alternately worry and scold; and pet as their ‘petite.’ It is not the active, sensible, managing woman, who exerts herself for her husband and children’s comfort, notwithstanding Solomon’s sayings about ‘the prudent woman,’ who is most beloved in life, and most regretted after death, however amiable she be; it is the idle, lazy, yea-nay character, for whom everything has to be done, and who, had she been in the lower ranks of life, would have driven her husband to the public-house by her thriftlessness, slatternly house, and screaming, dirty, ill-managed children, that in the higher ranks of life, where she has servants to wait on her; is beloved for her very helplessness.
