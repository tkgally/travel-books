# Preface.

I <sc>have</sc> but a few words to say by way of preface. The favourable reception accorded to ‘A Lady’s Walks in the South of France’ induced my publisher, Mr. Bentley, to suggest my taking a similar tour through Andorre and the Spanish side of the Pyrenees.
