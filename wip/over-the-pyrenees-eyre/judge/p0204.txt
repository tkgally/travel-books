The masses are rising to their true place and rank, as men and women, but have not yet learned how to behave with due respect to others as well as themselves; and are not yet sufficiently educated, or refined, for the position they claim. A hundred years hence I dare say it will not be so.

Godwin calculated long ago, that all the labours performed in the world now, might be equally well performed, if every man laboured four hours a day. We are coming, I doubt not, to a time when there will be no masters, or servants, mistresses, or maids; but each person will look on work as on a duty, and wait upon themselves.
