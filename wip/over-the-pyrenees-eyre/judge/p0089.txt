I have been ten days at Ax, detained by illness, and there have been three benedictions or processions—the Fête Dieu, the St. Jean, and another. The priests encourage them as much as possible, as they amuse the people, gratify their love <i>du spectacle</i>, and increase their power—over the women at least.

On the 26th of June I left Ax with much regret, for I had been ill, and received there the kindness I needed; and it is in itself a most lovely valley, full of picturesque and beautiful walks, and fields of loveliest rare flowers.
