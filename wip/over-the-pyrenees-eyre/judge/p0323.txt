These pendants end (as it appeared to me) in small hollows, like an oyster-shell, and the interior of each of these, as well as the outer sides of each pendant, are painted in brilliant colours, red, blue, and white, and edged with gold. The Moorish Court at the Crystal Palace, beautiful as it is in itself, gives no idea of the effect of these ceilings. Interspersed among the point-lace carvings on the walls, are Moorish inscriptions, quotations from the Koran, heraldic mottoes, and coats of arms.
