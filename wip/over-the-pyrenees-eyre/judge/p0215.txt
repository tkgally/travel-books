It only needed a little paper, paint, and cleanliness, to have made it a very comfortable abode for the passing stranger. No one was up but an old woman. Could I have some chocolate?—that is always to be had in Spain, and it is generally very good, only they make it into a thick paste. ‘Ora, ora,’ was of course the answer, ‘when the mistress comes.’ I waited hours, and no mistress came. I was fainting for food. At last the old woman went to fetch her daughter; some chocolate was brought forth, and I washed a cup and saucer for myself while it was making, and a glass to hold water to dilute it.
