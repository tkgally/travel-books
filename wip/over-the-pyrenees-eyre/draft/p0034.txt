They have had the same disease as the vine—<i>ils sont tout crêvés.</i> In our house we lost 2,000 francs by them this year—<i>figurez-vous</i>, 2,000 francs!’ She spoke as if it were millions, and, as all things are comparative, it probably was millions to her. She told me there are three kinds of worms—the common, the Montenegro, and the Japanese worm. Only the Japanese, which is the hardiest, succeeded this year. I had read that the Japan silk-worm required no attention, and was left to feed on the trees; but she said it needed the same care as the others, at least, <i>dans son pays</i>, for the heavy rains killed it if left out of doors.

What is the reason of the potato disease, and the vine blight, and the silk-worm disease? Are all things wearing out? Is the world growing old and near its end? God grant it may, and a better, and a happier, and fairer world arise from its ashes!
