The Sculpture Gallery I could not see—it was closed; and to have seen it I must have remained some days longer at Madrid, which neither suited my inclination nor my pocket. Hotels and travelling expenses are ruinous in Spain. So when, on the sixth morning, I found my precious manuscripts, and Mr. Ford’s entertaining guide-books had safely arrived—alas! too late for me to profit by the latter—I left Madrid the next day.

I did not see the inside of the royal palace; I take small interest now in stately rooms, gildings, and decorations; but when I read Mr. Ford’s account of the splendid equestrian statue of Philip IV. on his war charger, in the garden, I regretted not having visited it.
