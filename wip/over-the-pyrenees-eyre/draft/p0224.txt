The Spanish women in general wear nothing on their heads but perhaps a lace veil, and altogether dress for the street, as other women in other countries dress for a private party or a ball. They have, of course, a right to dress as they please, but until Spaniards allow a well-conducted, modest, quietly-dressed woman in black, to walk their streets and parades without being mobbed, they cannot lay claim to being a civilised nation.
