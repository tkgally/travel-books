[[fn *]] Having travelled in France for a period of sixteen months, I, of course, knew and complied with the regulations. My dog and my carpet-bag always travelled in the same luggage van, and when we arrived at a station, I always asked for both at the same time. In all my wanderings, never did I lose any baggage, except the two times mentioned in this last tour at Manresa in Spain, and at Cette; and each time through the mistake of an official who did not know his duty. I consider, therefore, that the Lyons Railway Company treated me very scurvily in making me pay for the mistake of their man. I had nothing to pay for the packet lost at Manresa; it was sent on to Madrid free of charge.
