The husband and wife are doing reverence to the Pontiff; and behind them, half in distance, half as it were in air—to mark it is an idea not yet realised—rises a church into which a procession is entering. This dream preceded the erection of Santa Maria la Mayor at Rome, under Pope Liberius, about the year 360.

Another that pleased me greatly was a painting of Philip II. on horseback, by Velasquez; and there were several noble pictures by Murillo, Zurbaro, Alonzo Cano, and other famous artists of the Spanish school, which interested me greatly. Among them a child Christ and Angels, by Murillo; and another by the same artist, of a Virgin standing; Christ appears above her in the air; below her feet, cherubs wave lilies and palm branches.
