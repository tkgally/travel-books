Darker and darker grew the night; and I admired amazingly a glittering light, which I imagined marked the entrance of the salt-mines. We drove on, still on the precipitous banks of the stream, to a part of the road where men were busily employed by the light whose beauty had added so much to the effect of the view, in widening and repairing the road.
