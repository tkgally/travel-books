The Plateria is pre-eminently the jewellers’ street, though there are shops of other kinds, and, as he says, ‘many of the ornaments exhibited in the windows are of very beautiful workmanship, and the large earrings of rubies, emeralds, or a fire-coloured stone, like a cross between a ruby and a cornelian, and set chiefly in silver; are all of elegant antique form.’ Some of them are as long as my forefinger, and must be terrifically heavy to wear; but the smaller ones—stone pendants of various size and form, hanging one from the other so as to form a pattern—are very lovely.
