She knew a little English, as did the captain, for both had spent five months in Glasgow while this vessel was building. I read ‘Glasgow’ on all her machinery. The Spaniards owe everything to England and hate her for her superiority. English blood, English energy and talent, and English treasure, preserved their nationality to them when Spain would otherwise have become a province of France, under the iron grasp of the first Napoleon. English capital and English labour and skill are gradually—for no English energy can make Spanish sloth and Spanish indolence creep faster than at a snail’s pace—covering the country with a network of railways. Liverpool and Manchester supply her with steam-engines, and probably all her best steamers are like the Bayo—English or Glasgow built.
