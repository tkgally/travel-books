At last a man came, dark-visaged, with curling black moustachios, and hawk-like, dark, fierce-eyed face, tall and stout in person, with full white shirt sleeves, puffed out below the cuffs of his black coat, a small turn-down white collar, and black necktie, who politely offered me his arm and conducted me up-stairs. My guide followed, and we explained that I wanted some hard-boiled eggs, bread, wine, and a bed-room. In fact I had eaten nothing but a little bread and two small squares of chocolate all that day, although I had walked at least seven miles of rough mountain road, besides the two hours’ ride from Andorre la Vieja to San Julian. ‘Prao, prao.’ I should have it directly.
