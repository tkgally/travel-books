He went in and had a cup of coffee, a smoke, and a chat with his friends. I remained outside on my mule, and coffee was brought to me, but it was so bad I could hardly drink it. Most coffee is bad in Spain. This coffee was brought in a large cup and saucer, I suppose because I was riding; but the usual custom in Andorre, and Spanish wayside inns, is to drink it out of glass tumblers, such as we use at dinner. My host at Andorre made bitter complaints to me one day of the number of tumblers that got broken, especially in frosty weather. ‘Why do you use them then?’ said I.

‘The peasants will have them; they are larger, you see, and hold more than a coffee-cup.’

I thought it would have been easy to have large coffee-cups, but probably they are not easily attainable in either country, at least in the wild districts.
