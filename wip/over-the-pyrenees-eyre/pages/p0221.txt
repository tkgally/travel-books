‘Are these yours also?’ said the astonished waiter, pointing to the chairs, as he shouldered my bag.

‘Certainly not,’ said I; ‘I know nothing about them.’

He showed me into a clean-looking bed-room, and supplied me with water, and I was thankful to bathe, and change my travel-soiled garments, and then to descend with Keeper to the <i>salon</i> and have some food; after which we started to explore the town.

‘The Rambla,’ says Mr. Ford, ‘was once a streamlet, La Riera den Malla, of the Mall, which bounded the western wall of Barcelona; it is now a gay public walk, with handsome shops on either side, and is, I should think, near two miles long. Here are the principal hotels—that of Las Cuatros Naciones, the Fonda del Grande Oriente, and El Falcone.
