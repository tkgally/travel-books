‘C’est vrai!’ exclaimed the Frenchman, ‘Madame is right; the English always give more than they have promised.’ And he translated the whole into Catalonian to Joan.

‘I have brought her from the Seu,’ said he, reproachfully, ‘for quinze francs.’

‘You bargained to do so, with Señor Federigo; I had nothing to do with making the bargain,’ said I, ‘and you promised him to take every possible care of me. You told him I should be “bien avec vous;” instead, you did all that depended on you, to make me suffer. I will not reward you.’

‘C’est juste!’ exclaimed the Frenchman; and even the Spaniards all joined in reprobating Joan’s behaviour.
