As to the food, I cannot speak so favourably. Spanish cooking, and Spanish meat is detestable: from the dish-water soup, the cold fried-fish, and the bouilli, like skeins of coarse worsted, cut into long pieces; to the measly-looking, coarse pork, also cut into pieces; and served up on a bed of dry <i>chicharas</i> or chickpeas—a large, round, dry pea, as good as our English dried peas would be, boiled whole; which, Mr. Ford says, was the favourite national dish of the Carthaginians: as it seems to be of their descendants, to this day.
