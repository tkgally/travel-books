# Chapter X.
[[subtitle]] Madrid.

I <sc>went</sc> the next morning to the Barcelona railway station to inquire for my parcel. It was not there, of course, and I asked for the telegraph office. Will any English person believe the officials refused to telegraph for it? They were not <i>bien</i> with the Saragossa line, it was not their line; and as I had lost my baggage through the fault of an employé, who ought to have given notice of the change of waggons at Manresa, the telegram contained a complaint, and they felt a certain delicacy, &c. &c. I had better go to the government telegraph office at the Puerto del Sol.
