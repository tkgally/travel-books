[[cont]]Venus; and, as in that, a stray curl or two half escaped, and waved loosely over the delicate, shell-shaped ear. She wore a chemise, with full sleeves gathered above the elbows, and her beautifully moulded arms, and brown but perfect hands, were bare; and I thought how much better that full shift sleeve, often seen in foreign paintings, sets off the arm than any London or Parisian ball dress. Above her shift she wore a double kerchief of dark green muslin and a coloured print skirt. After awhile, she changed places with her husband, who spread a shawl on the bench for the elder child, and tried to get it to sleep; but it was too excited to slumber. I never saw a man so fond of his child; he was always feeding, or giving it drink, or hugging it.

I do like to see a man fond of his wife and children. It is always a proof of true manliness when a man is tender to those weaker than himself; and I grew quite interested in my two neighbours. As to the baby, it was a little darling; and might have been a Queen's, if high birth gave beauty; so delicate were its small features, so exquisite the symmetry of its little frame.
