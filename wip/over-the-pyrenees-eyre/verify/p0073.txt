I have never been able to get a good view of the insect herself, as she works on the side next a wall that I cannot get to, but she appears much like the common wasp, only rather smaller bodied, and with a more pointed tail and narrower wings. I want to take the nest, but am afraid of being stung; and only fancy carrying a nest of wasp larvæ to hatch in one’s bonnet-box, as they would be sure to do <i>en route.</i> To-day the wasp is at work on my side, constructing new cells. She is smaller than the common wasp and darker, her body is black, banded with narrow yellow stripes, her wings are dark brown, her legs yellow, her head larger than the common wasp and black. I like Ax extremely. One is not so covered with white dust when one walks along the road as at Avignon, Nismes, and Foix.
