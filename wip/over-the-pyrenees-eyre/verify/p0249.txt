Broad strings of braid pass from the toe to the instep, betwixt the toe and heel, and crossing at the back of the leg, are tied in front, or at one side. The same sort of sandal is worn by the peasants of Andorre. We were waited on by a pretty young girl.

‘I dare say you would not think our attendant has been three years married,’ said my acquaintance to me, ‘but she has.’

‘I have been told Spanish girls marry very early,’ I answered.

‘Yes,’ said he; ‘she, however, was eighteen when she married, though she looks so young; but when I married, my wife was only fourteen; she was a mother before she was fifteen. She died seven years ago. I have been seven years a widower.’ He said this with a half sigh.

‘That is too young for a girl to marry,’ said I; ‘she must be still too much of a child to be fit to manage a house and children.’
