Chapter I.

The North Wind doth blow—The Doctor—Decline of Originality—Our Scheme of Travel—Preparations for journey to Lapland, and Means of Transport.

The North Wind doth blow,

And we shall have snow.

These were among the first sounds that fell upon my infant ear, and I used to be deeply concerned to hear from my nurse what steps the robin would take to keep himself warm. There was a charm in the words that they have not yet lost, and the experiences of other journeys into the distant North made the prospect of travelling to the home of the North Wind a very attractive one.
