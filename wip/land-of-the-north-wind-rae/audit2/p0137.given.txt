Then we went to keep an appointment with a photographer, whom we bad engaged to photograph Haparanda from Torneå for us, and torneå from Haparanda. The forsworn artist saw us coming and fled, for he had not attempted to take either photograph. We hailed him lustily as he was escaping, and he had to stop and face us. He declared he would take the photographs first thing in the morning, and excused himself by saying that he had no idea of taking a view of Haparanda until we had suggested to him that it would be remunerative: he begged us to spare him for a fortnight more, when he declared the photograph should be in our hands in England. We asked how it was possible to believe him after perjuring himself so violently; but his good-natured little wife came out and we were softened, and told them where to dircct the photographs.

When we came to the hotel the baggage was ready for packing, and we fell to work. Such was our energy that in half an hour cooking apparatus, riding garments, reindeer skins, waterproofs, portmanteaus, and rugs were condensed into an orderly group of seven packages, and ready for transport to the boat.
