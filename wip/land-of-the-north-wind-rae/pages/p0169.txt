[[cont]]the Professor was cutting with my knife some of the dirtiest nails that I ever saw. Walking up to him I said with pleasantness, Perhaps you would like to borrow my nail-brush at the same time, sir? He hardly knew whether I was a good-natured simpleton or intentionally provoking him, but he must have decided that I was the former; for he said, O vell I tenk you sir, I am not much accostomed to broshes.

This little passage filled us with a substantial joy, which lasted until we entered the muddy old Humber, and the Wakefield was moored to the quay in Grimsby dock.
