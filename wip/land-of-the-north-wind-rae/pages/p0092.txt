At the last moment I have been prevailed upon by an immense circle of friends and relatives to withdraw the song and music, and can only express a regret that I may have raised the reader’s hopes too high.

The Doctor and I have been asked whether it is true that the Laplanders bury their money for the sake of security. We are none of those travellers who think it right to express an opinion upon every point that may be raised: we can only say modestly, we do not know. We never found any buried money: we never met anybody who had found any: we never saw a Laplander bury his money: and we never knew a Laplander who had any money to bury.
