The Samoyedes seem to be one of the links required to establish the relation of the Laplanders with the Mongols. The Laplanders are Europeanized Samoyedes, the Samoyedes wanderers of Chinese race.

In this country of theirs, within the province or district of Mezén, was found the mammoth tusk which we saw in Archangel Museum. On the banks of the Lena and Yenisei, in the country of their neighbours the Yakûts and Tûngusi, were those beds and deposits of mammoth tusks the discovery of which contributed to the distinction of the Russian naturalist Pallas. In the frozen ground in places were found entire carcasses of this extinct tropical monster, occasionally with the evidences of hair and wool upon the hides.

The circumstance of these stranded creatures being found, where now the Arctic fox, reindeer, and bear exist, points to three distinct periods of the earth’s existence. The period when tropical animals, and consequently tropical vegetation to support them, existed north of Lat. 60° N. The glacial period that destroyed, and at the same time preserved evidences of, this remarkable animal life. Lastly this comparatively temperate period, in which neither glaciers nor mammoth exist.
