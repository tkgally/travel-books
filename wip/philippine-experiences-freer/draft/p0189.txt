They will freely sacrifice life itself for an abstraction, and at the same time turn aside from the prosaic work of commonplace preparation which would make the sacrifice unnecessary and give form and substance to the abstraction. Their temperament predisposes them to dwell too much on their expectations of the future, and too little on the live, exigent problems of the present.
