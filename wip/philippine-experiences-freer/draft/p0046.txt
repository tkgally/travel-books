[[cont]] the last stage of our journey. Rather an imposing caravan we imagined ourselves—seven Americans, as many Filipinos, and fourteen pack animals (ponies and <i>carabao</i>) to transport our supplies. We camped in the mountains two nights, going over the four-thousand-foot pass in the Caraballo Sur the second morning. We saw no head-hunters. The third day we passed through the Isanay town of Dupax, where we were hospitably entertained at dinner by the <i>presidente.</i> That night we slept in the school-house at Bambang, and the following day made Solano. I had been on the way from Manila five weeks and two days, a journey requiring but six days in fine weather.
