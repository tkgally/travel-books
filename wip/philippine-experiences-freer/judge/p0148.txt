# Chapter X

[[subtitle]] Departure from Solano

[[subtitle]] Orders for Transfer—Departure—Illness—Back to Dupax—Generosity of the <i>Presidente</i>—Lack of Physicians—Their Place Supplied—In Solano Again—Arrival of the Superintendent—The Second Departure—Difficult Travelling—The Return to Manila.

As <sc>the</sc> time passed the schools continued to thrive. Much there was that was crude and imperfect, particularly where the native teachers had no American instructor to counsel and guide them; but we consoled ourselves by reflecting that our schools were much better organized, equipped and taught than any the province had before known. Our division superintendent had promised to make us a visit at the earliest time possible, to which we began to look forward. Dupax, Bambang and Aritao were without American teachers, and an additional American was needed in Solano so that I might be able to visit all of the schools of the province.
