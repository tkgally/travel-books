The <i>presidente</i> and his assistants were weak officials, and failed to do the little they might have done to improve the conditions. But finally the time came for the new election, and the ablest and most progressive man in the town was chosen <i>presidente</i> for the new term of two years. Notwithstanding that the municipality was so poor that the combined salaries of the officials were less than forty <i>pesos</i> (twenty dollars) a month, the new <i>presidente</i> began a campaign of improvement. By means of twenty sacks of rice donated by the government from the relief fund voted by the United States Congress, the large frame <i>presidencia</i> was re-roofed, repaired and divided by a partition, making two rooms of equal size; one of these was for the school, which up to that time had been kept in a dwelling-house, and the other was for use as the town hall.
