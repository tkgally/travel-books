Don Mariano was not gentle with such offending officials when, as occasionally happened, he became cognizant of their abuses or neglect of duty; and woe betide the offender who incurred his displeasure—well might he wish that he had never been born. A scathing rebuke, administered in public more often than not, followed by a lecture, was the punishment for minor offences; and thereafter the governor's eye was upon that official, and he was not likely to offend again soon. If the offence were of greater gravity the reprimand and upbraiding were followed by suspension pending formal investigation and punishment by the provincial board.
