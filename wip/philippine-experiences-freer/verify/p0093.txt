Unfortunately, the quiet people of Quiangan are not the only Igorrotes in the Solano country. There are also tribes of head-hunters, who sometimes descend on a Christian hamlet or a company of travelling <i>Cristianos.</i> Individual Filipinos would not travel in the country alone; only the fearless Americanos ventured to do that; but they were safe, for did they not carry huge revolvers and belts filled with cartridges, and was not each and every one of them a sure shot? Both the wild men and the lawless men in the Philippines have the utmost respect for firearms in American hands, so well did our soldiers do their work.

Not long after our arrival word came that the head-hunters had raided the <i>barrio</i> of Bone, a hamlet belonging to the town of Aritao, about eight miles west of Dupax, and had burned a number of houses and murdered several people. Among others who suffered was the native school-teacher, whose dwelling was destroyed and some of whose family were killed.
