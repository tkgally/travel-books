# Note

The descriptive works on the Philippines most likely to interest the general reader are:

The Philippine Islands, Foreman, Charles Scribner’s Sons, New York.

The Inhabitants of the Philippines, Sawyer, Charles Scribner’s Sons, New York.

The Philippine Islands and Their People, Worcester, The Macmillan Co., New York.

Yesterdays in the Philippines, Stevens, Charles Scribner’s Sons, New York.

Gems of the East, A. H. S. Landor, Harper & Brothers, New York.

The Philippine Islands, Fred W. Atkinson, Ginn & Co., Boston.

Philippine Life in Town and Country, James A. LeRoy, G. P. Putnam’s Sons, New York.
