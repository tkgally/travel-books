[[center]] P<sc>rogramme</sc>

[[center]] D<sc>ecember</sc> T<sc>wenty-seventh</sc>

At 12 o’clock, noon, a general ringing of the bells, and the band will play in the atrium of the parish church.

[[center]] D<sc>ecember</sc> T<sc>wenty-eighth</sc>

At 5 <sc>a.m.</sc> all the bands participating in the festivities will celebrate reveille; general ringing of the bells. At 8 <sc>a.m.</sc>, solemn mass in the parish church, the sacred office to be performed by the young father, curate of this parish; and solemn procession. At 9.30 <sc>a.m.</sc>, inauguration of the theatre with the Iloco comedy, “Moro-Moro.” At 5 <sc>p.m.</sc>, a grand musical concert, in which all the organizations taking part in the <i>fiesta</i> may join. An impartial judge will award a premium of ten <i>pesos</i> to the band giving the best interpretation of the music played. At 7 <sc>p.m.</sc>, theatrical function, a comedy in Gaddan prose.

[[center]] D<sc>ecember</sc> T<sc>wenty-ninth</sc>

At 8 <sc>a.m.</sc>, theatrical function, Gaddan comedy.

During the evenings of the 27th, 28th and 29th both public and private edifices will be illuminated.

Cock-fighting during the three days named.
