[[cont]]man in words like these: “Don Tomás, it has been eleven weeks since I passed this way before, and you have done absolutely nothing to put this bridge in shape. What do you do with your time? Do you sleep all day, or are you so interested in training your game-cocks that you cannot attend to public business? Fie, for shame. Why did you permit your fellow-townsmen to elect you to the honorable office of <i>presidente</i> if you did not intend to fulfil your duties? So far as I can see, your sole interest lies in collecting your monthly salary of fifty <i>pesos;</i> just fifty <i>pesos</i> too much for so lazy an official as you. You have been collecting the material, have you? Where is it, then? So it is still in the woods, as I thought. How many logs have you cut? Only two? Indolent one. Why, man, don’t you know that the <i>presidente</i> of Cuyus-cuyus cut and drew six heavy timbers and thirty planks for the repair of the bridge there in two weeks, and had them in place in a third? If he can do that, why cannot you? It is a pity that you cannot do even your small part in the upbuilding of your town, when the insular government grants you schools and teachers for your children and the province builds your roads. Shame on you, I say. Be alive, man; wake up and do something
