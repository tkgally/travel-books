On the other hand, the little Filipino is eager to learn, from his books and in all other ways. While somewhat indolent constitutionally, he nevertheless applies himself in school, and, when he has learned how to study alone, usually addresses himself to study diligently. If he knows his lesson he is so eager to recite that he uses every means he may to attract his teacher’s glance. He is mentally alert and often consumed with the desire for knowledge;[[fnref *]] moreover, he is now proving himself capable of sustained effort.
