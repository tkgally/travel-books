## Misrepresentative Women, and Other Verses.

[[center]] By HARRY GRAHAM,

[[center]] A<sc>uthor of</sc> ‘R<sc>uthless</sc> R<sc>hymes for</sc> H<sc>eartless</sc> H<sc>omes</sc>,’ ‘B<sc>allads of the</sc> B<sc>oer</sc> W<sc>ar</sc>,’ ‘M<sc>isrepresentative</sc> M<sc>en</sc>,’ ‘F<sc>iscal</sc> B<sc>allads</sc>,’ ‘V<sc>erse and</sc> W<sc>orse</sc>,’ <sc>etc.</sc>

[[center]] <i>Foolscap 4to. With Illustrations by</i> D<sc>an</sc> S<sc>ayre</sc> G<sc>roesbeck</sc>. 5s.

Admirers of Captain Graham’s ingenious and sarcastic verse will welcome this fresh instalment, which contains, among the ‘other verses,’ a number of ‘Poetic Paraphrases’ and ‘Open Letters’ to popular authors.

## The Land of Play.

[[center]] By MRS. GRAHAM WALLAS.

[[center]] <i>Crown 8vo. With Illustrations by</i> G<sc>ilbert</sc> J<sc>ames</sc>. 3s. 6d.

The four stories which make up this delightful children’s book are entitled ‘Luck-Child,’ ‘The Princess and the Ordinary Little Girl,’ ‘Professor Green,’ and ‘A Position of Trust.’

## A Song-Garden for Children.

[[center]] A Collection of Children’s Songs

[[center]] Adapted from the French and German by HARRY GRAHAM <sc>and</sc> ROSA NEWMARCH.

[[center]] The Music Edited and Arranged by NORMAN O’NEILL.

[[center]] <i>Imperial 8vo. Paper.</i> 2s. 6d. net.

[[center]] <i>Cloth, gilt top.</i> 4s. 6d. net.

This is a charming collection of forty-three French and German songs for children translated and adapted by Capt. Graham and Mrs. Newmarch. It includes nine songs arranged by J. Brahms for the children of Robert and Clara Schumann.
