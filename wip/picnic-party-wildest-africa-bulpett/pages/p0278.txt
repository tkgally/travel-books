My Abyssinian syce became suddenly very ill here. Upon my inquiring into his case, I found that on the advice of one of his friends he had allowed the tip of his uvula to be cut off by way of treatment for a cold in his throat. In this state he naturally could not eat the coarse food which was all we had to give him, and as there was no fresh milk to be had, it was probable that he would die. We of course had to carry him for several days’ march, and for some time we were afraid it would be a bad case; but it is wonderful how these people can thrive on nothing, and by the end of the week, he was almost well again.
