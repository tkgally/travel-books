[[cont]] company of Soudanese troops under the command of an Egyptian officer, Riad Effendi by name. These Soudanese had built huts for themselves, and had drawn up military lines just as if they meant to settle down there for the rest of their lives. They had brought their wives with them, and they had poultry, and did not seem to be having at all a bad time of it. There was nothing for them to do except to guard the camp from surprise by the natives.
