Even in this may be seen the likening of the man to heaven, and of the woman to earth; and the custom should teach a woman how necessary it is for her in everything to yield to her husband the first, and to be herself content with the second place.”

The prevailing religion added its weight to these teachings. According to Buddhism, women are greater sinners than men, hardly knowing the difference between truth and falsehood. Only men can enter Nirvana, or become Buddhas. Even the merciful Amitábha can take women to his paradise only by first changing them to men. “A nun of a hundred years’ experience must bow reverently before every monk, even though he be ordained only on this day.” In this life, woman must obey her father in childhood, her husband in wedlock, her son in her widowhood.
