Of these, thirty-six are preaching or studying theology, fifty-eight are teachers, forty-five are students, twenty-one are in business, five are editors, and two are in the diplomatic service. Besides the above-named graduates, more than eighteen hundred young men have been connected with the school for a longer or shorter period. Less than ten students have been graduated from the collegiate department who have not been professing Christians.

Mr. Kozaki, Dr. Neesima’s successor, has already been spoken of. He is of a philosophic bent of mind and of wide scholarship. He is a sympathetic and impressive preacher, and profoundly interested in the religious welfare of the students, the prosperity of the theological department, and the speedy Christianization of Japan.
