Doubtless such sins as lying and licentiousness are found more commonly and in more offensive forms here, and the shame which should accompany them is far less than with us. I could write a dark chapter on this theme were I so disposed. And while other sins, such as anger and quarreling, are less common in Japan than in America, the general state of society is unquestionably lower. But missionary effort has its justification in the fact that men everywhere are sinners and in need of a Saviour, and not in any special degree of corruption and degradation in sin which a particular people may have. The wise missionary will be open-eyed to the good rather than to the evil that is in the people he seeks to help. He will heartily agree with Bishop Potter that the most effectual regenerating power is “that enkindling and transforming temper which forever sees in humanity, not that which is bad and baleful, but that which is lovable and improvable, which can both discern and effectually speak to that nobler longing of the soul which is the indestructible image of its Maker.”
