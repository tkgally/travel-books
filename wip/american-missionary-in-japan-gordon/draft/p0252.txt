Engaged in the coasting trade are 1,407 ships of foreign pattern, with a tonnage of 141,144; and 637,034 vessels of Japanese style.

The progress in education and in the diffusion of knowledge has been equally marked. Twenty years ago there was not in all the Empire a single newspaper published in the vernacular by a Japanese. To-day there are more than seven hundred periodicals, which sent out in 1890 an aggregate of 188,289,728 numbers. Seventeen Tōkyō dailies send out 46,872,000 numbers annually. The number of books published in 1890 was 17,658.
