# Chapter XI.

[[subtitle]] The Growth of a Church.

T<sc>he</sc> history in some detail of the growth of a single local church is illustrative of the method of growth which generally prevails.

One of the earliest students in the Dōshisha school was a native of Kameoka, a town in the corner of Tamba, fifteen miles to the northwest of Kyōto. He became a Christian, and, with the same zeal which he has since shown in the pastorate, on every visit home eagerly told his friends and neighbors of the new life into which he had entered. Among them all, however, but one, a Mr. Murakami, a man somewhat older than himself, was at that time moved by the story. This man frequently thereafter walked to Kyōto to attend Christian services, and was finally received into one of our churches.
