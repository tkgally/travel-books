But after a conference of an hour, it was the unanimous opinion of the committee that he should be baptized at once. We found that he had written three small volumes of Chinese poetry (which Japanese scholars are very fond of) upon Christianity. Some of these poems were said to show very deep spiritual insight. He had had prayer and Scripture reading in his family, and had made it known in the community that he was a Christian. In his examination he said, among other things: “Many object to Christianity because of the cross; but to my mind the cross is the distinctive glory of the religion of Christ.” With one other instance of remarkable guiding toward the light, I will close this chapter.
