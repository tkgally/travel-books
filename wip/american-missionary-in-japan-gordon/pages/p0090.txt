He severs his family connection and strikes for liberty. He is a shorn lamb, and leaving all.”

The subsequent history of nearly every one of these men is such as to justify the hopes to which their faith and patience under persecution gave rise. Of the men who went to Kyōto and continued their education, one (Kanamori) afterward built up a strong church in Okayama, then for a time was acting principal of the Dōshisha, after that becoming pastor of the Banchō church, Tōkyō.[[fnref 1]] Another (Yokoi), after remarkable success as an evangelist and pastor at Imabari, is now pastor of the Hongo church, Tōkyō, and editor of “The Christian,” a weekly religious newspaper, and “The Rikugo Zasshi,” a Christian magazine.
