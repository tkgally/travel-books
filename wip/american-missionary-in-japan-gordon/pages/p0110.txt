Another preacher spoke of the fact that old Japanese armor is too large for the men of the present generation. This proves physical deterioration because of the violation of hygienic laws. On the other hand, the old armor in the Tower of London, being too small for modern Englishmen, proves the reverse. From this, the application to shrunken, deteriorated souls was swift and strong.
