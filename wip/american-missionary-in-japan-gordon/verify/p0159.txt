“Now, children,” said Mr. Ishii, “that happened many years ago in Holland, but the same kind heavenly Father still watches over his children, and I believe He will help us. As many of you as think the same will please go with me, when you have finished your supper, to the little graveyard back of the house, and there we will pray in faith for help.”

Nearly thirty of the children volunteered to go, some of them without tasting even a mouthful of the scanty meal. Mr. Ishii opened the little service of prayer, and, after offering a very urgent petition himself, started, supperless and probably dinnerless, to attend the church prayer-meeting, leaving the children praying in the temple graveyard.
