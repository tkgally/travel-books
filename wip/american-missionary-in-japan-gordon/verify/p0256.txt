# Chapter XX.
[[subtitle]] Christianity and New Japan.

W<sc>hat</sc> relation is there between Christianity and the New Japan described in the preceding chapter?

New Japan is largely a product of Christian influences.

Notice that I do not say that it is the product of Christian influences only. I cordially subscribe to the opinion advanced by Dr. W. E. Griffis in “The Mikado’s Empire,” that there were internal forces at work in Japan, which would have brought about a revolution in the national life, even though outside influences had not come into play. The presence of Commodore Perry’s fleet in Japanese waters but quickened the action of revolutionary forces, which had long been in operation.
