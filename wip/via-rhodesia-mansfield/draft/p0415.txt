[[cont]]I demurred, but the man’s face looked so intelligent, and he had such beautiful artistic hands, I had to consent, and so we sat down at a table outside a café, ordering some ice-cream. And then followed a few minutes full of wonder, for the Indian was a marvel—by palmistry he gave details of the past and foretold the future, parts of which seemed to me most improbable and out of the question; however, all of what he said with regard to the latter came true within six months! And yet he could not possibly have known me, for the town was filled with the passengers from many liners. Perhaps the most marvellous thing about the “fortune” was that this Indian told me practically the same as “Cheiro,” the first celebrated London palmist, and the “Queen of the Gipsies” both told me over ten years ago!
