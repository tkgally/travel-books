When they returned to the boat, just in time not to be left behind, our clever friend discovered that the shawl he actually got was about half the size of the one he saw first, and not worth more than about ten shillings. It is sometimes wrong to want to be too clever.

I enjoyed an excellent lunch in a new restaurant at the upper end of the quay, where, in the open, under an enormous canopy, the statue of Lesseps, the constructor of the canal, can be seen.

Half of the pleasure of living will go if ever the time come when we shall know for certain what happens after death, and to be certain what to-morrow will hold would probably take away half the inducement to live through to-day. And yet, few of us can say that we have not at some time of our lives tried to peep into the unknown future, always telling our friends that of course we don't believe in palmistry or astrology; but it is very amusing to hear.
