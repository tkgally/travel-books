Of course the men could not dress, that was out of the question, as there was only one black coat in Salisbury for many years, and that was the one owned by Dr. Jameson in his dignified position of Administrator. But could a woman dance in blue serge when that serge had had so many weeks’ wear? No, certainly not; and into Mrs. Marshall Hole’s pretty little fair head there crept a scheme. Saying no word of her secret, she bought white limbo (calico) and made herself a dress, draping around the low V some of her husband’s white silk handkerchiefs. The night of the dance arrived, and when the other ladies saw the confection they both exclaimed, “Oh, your boxes have come!”
