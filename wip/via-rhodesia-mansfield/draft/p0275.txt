But something had to be done, so the white man in charge of the district sent for the witch-doctor and told him that unless the body of the old Chief was buried without bloodshed within a fortnight, and a new Chief elected, he, the witch-doctor, would answer for it with his life. History does not relate what spells the witch-doctor used to appease the spirit of the old Chief, but suffice it to say the body in the beeswax case disappeared and has not been seen or heard of since, and a new Chief reigns.
