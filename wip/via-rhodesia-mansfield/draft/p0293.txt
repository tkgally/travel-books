After leaving the open plain I entered more thickly wooded country, and here every minute one saw a buck of some sort or other. I am not very keen on killing things, but I was tired of tinned meat, and even “cuckoos” may pall, so I took aim at a pookoo standing on rising ground about sixty yards away (I think, though I confess to not being a very accurate judge). I fired, but had aimed too low, for the dust flew up; the obliging creature, however, stood quite still, and the second shot spelt death.
