In North-Eastern Rhodesia I saw convicts working in gangs of three and four, chained together. Up-country, white prisoners are found to be very undesirable by the magistrates of remote districts, as it is difficult to feed and keep them, European prison accommodation being either inadequate or entirely lacking. A white man gets passed on from pillar to post, and in fact I was assured that if I only committed a crime I could travel through Africa free of cost, as no one would want me.
