Instead, however, of being disfigured in this way, I was let off unscathed, for the lion apparently had been well fed, and, not liking the look of me, just turned and went away quickly through the grass and was soon lost to view.

Not all lions are man-eaters; only when too old to catch game do lions turn their attention to man-eating, and then they prefer natives to whites. But the unconcern of some natives is amazing.
