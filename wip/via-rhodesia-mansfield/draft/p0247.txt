Truly, in some parts of Rhodesia one could live like a king on an old age pension, that is, if one would live as a peasant and be happy merely in the possession of health. Some people would say, “Yes, but think of the monotony of the life of a native, living and eating, marrying and dying!” Well, many a poor white has more of pain and less of pleasure and sometimes not even enough of the eating—a starved native does not exist.
