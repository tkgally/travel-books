Surely these extracts form a most valuable testimonial to Rhodesia.

In Rhodesia, the Cape land measurement is used, land being surveyed and sold by the morgen. This measurement is slightly different from English measurement, 1000 Cape feet being equal to 1033 English feet, and a morgen of land, consisting of two Cape acres, is therefore equal to 2·11654 English acres.

The short ton of 2000 lbs. is used.
