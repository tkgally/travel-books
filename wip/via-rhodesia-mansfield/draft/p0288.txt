Little “Ugly” was asleep on my lap. I didn’t want to go to bed, for sleep holds dreams, and to awake in an enclosure is to be alone. Out there by the camp fire, who could be lonely with the whole world for company?

Then I sang to my banjo, and all the men stopped their chattering and turned to listen. No, I am not a musician, but there are snatches of songs which take beauty from the surroundings and make harmony of one’s thoughts.

And then one by one the men stretched themselves out and went to sleep, only the fires and I were awake, and it was some time before I crept away to my tent, the cry of hyenas in my ears and in my heart the fear that soon other sounds might come, sounds holding even greater horror than darkness.

Selous has since told me that he loves to be surrounded by the calls of wild animals. I, too, felt a weird, awful fascination, but alas! I must be a coward, for fear was there too, like a damp cold hand on my forehead.
