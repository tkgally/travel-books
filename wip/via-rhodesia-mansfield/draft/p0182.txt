# Chapter XV

[[subtitle]] <i>At Rail Head</i>

[[image: Photograph of men standing on a path beside a building and a railway carriage, with flooded grassland and a small boat beyond]]
[[caption]] Preparing to drag the coach through the floods

THERE appeared in the Press, local and otherwise, the official notification that on account of the height of the floods of the Kafue River no passengers or parcels would be carried beyond this spot until further notice. What was to be done? A wire came stating that my carriers had arrived at Broken Hill and would be ready to take me north as soon as I could get there.
