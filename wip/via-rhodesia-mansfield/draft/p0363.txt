During the whole nine days’ travel from Abercorn to Karonga I met only one white man on the road, and he was Mr. Stuart Wells, Assistant Magistrate at Karonga, who was out on a tour of inspection.

It was not far from the Luangwe River that I suddenly came on a tent with a table standing outside it, on which rested a large tin of Cerebos salt and a tea-pot, and then I knew white blood must be about somewhere, and soon Mr. Stuart Wells put in an appearance. I decided to camp there and proceed to Karonga the next day.
