With regard to the conclusion that I must have been very lonely, let me hasten to say that I don't believe loneliness and life on the veld have any connection with each other. Many have written on the loneliness one experiences in crowds, but I never met a man or woman who had really lived a veld life who considered loneliness a bar to happiness.
