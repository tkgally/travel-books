As I advanced up the hill I quite expected to see a fortified castle at the top, and wished that the Baroness Orczy could have been by my side, adequately to describe the scene.

I don’t know if it was the Postmaster-General or the Civil Commissioner or the Inspector of His Majesty’s Prisons who came to greet me this time, but anyhow a nice white man was waiting at the top of the steps, and informed me that Mr. Averay Jones, the Assistant Magistrate, was engaged with a case in Court, but hoped in the meantime I would take possession of his house.
