Presently Dr. Chisholme returned from church, and I found him a very kind and interesting man, not the uneducated Bible-thumper so many missionaries are. He entertained me most hospitably before I went on my way, and told me many interesting details about the customs of the natives.

Now why could not such a man as this be placed at the head of a mission for poor whites, either at home or abroad?
