In Salisbury what one loses on the cocoanut shies one makes up on the roundabouts; one may complain of the unnecessary space between the public buildings for a busy man or woman, but no one can find fault with the delightful sports grounds, which are quite the finest in Rhodesia, if not in South Africa.
