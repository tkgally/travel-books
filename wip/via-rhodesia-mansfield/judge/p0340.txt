Presently the noise from the village ceased, and I was just thinking that the lion had gone and peace would reign. I must have been dozing when the cry of a wounded animal startled me. I think a poor buck must have been caught at that moment not very far away. I listened. The cry was again repeated, and then silence came until a wild turkey cock started shrieking.
