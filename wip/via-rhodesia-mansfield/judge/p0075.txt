I cannot write a scientific paper on the prospects of farming in Rhodesia, because I am not an expert, but perhaps a few remarks from personal observations may interest.

Rhodesia wants more poultry and dairy farms, especially near the towns. Imported eggs and tinned butter and cheese seem ridiculous in a country where land can almost be had for the asking. Fresh eggs sometimes cost five shillings a dozen, not only in Bulawayo but also in Salisbury and in Umtali. I know of one woman, the wife of a farmer, living not far from the Matoppos, who makes £150 a year by keeping fowls for laying; and I came across another woman near Umtali who had made £18 in a few weeks.
