Never staying nor turning back, he strode proudly to the great gate and demanded admittance; and when the guards challenged him and bade him give his name and errand, he answered: “Zinguza namdogy-yeng Prah”—“it is Zinguza, your lawful lord!”

Then the guards, astonished, bewildered, flung open the great gate, and Zinguza with his slave passed in, looking neither to the right hand nor the left, nor accosting any man, but silently marching up the paved way, in the middle of it, straight to the palace; and all the people fell back before the royal outlaw, and many there were that made, yet did not make, salaam. And so he reached the lower wicket of the palace, but his name had gone before him, and Mentaragyee, the Evil-minded, stopped the way.
