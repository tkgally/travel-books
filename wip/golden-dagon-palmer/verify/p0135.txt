# Chapter XIV.

[[subtitle]] Magnanimous!—The Stockades of Rangoon—The Streets—The Machinery of Boodhism—The Golden Dagon—The Great and Little Bells—Boodh and “Baccy”—The Ingathering—Young Shway-Madoo.
