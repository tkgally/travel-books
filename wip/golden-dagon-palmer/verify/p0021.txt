# Chapter I.
[[subtitle]] Hong-Kong—The Bore—Ay-Chung—Kumpny's Buttons.

W<sc>e</sc> kept lonesome companionship together, my cheroot and I, in the verandah of Brooks's in Hong-Kong.

Up a narrow climbing court, a score or two of yards off the main street, and hard by the gate of the Bishop's Palace, Brooks's was a famous house for reflection and billiards, and a bad (that is, a good) one for fleas—especially poor in prospect.

But it had one evident advantage—it withdrew, as it were, from the town, affording you a respite from your Hong-Kong, and permitting you by snatches to dream that you were out of it: out of its swarms of entomological coolies
