With each “hah!” a vigorous plunge of every paddle into the brine in strictly musical time, and the low, slender craft shot through the water like a feathered shaft, her bow half-hid beneath a pile of foam.

Presently the <i>Tenasserim</i>, which had been hourly expected with dispatches from Calcutta, rounded the point and approached her anchorage under “full power” and with a rapid current. She was not on the Governor’s programme for the day, nor was her “back-water,” which—being a phenomenon for which the Burmese steersmen were wholly unprepared—caught the boats “broadside on” half way between the ship and shore, and throwing them for an instant into ridiculous disorder, and their crews into a panic, capsized them without exception. In a moment every gong was silent, every singer dumb, every dancer paralyzed, every flag struck, every spear lowered, and arms and ammunition speedily “expended,”—as the Company’s pursers say of stores which cannot be accounted for.
