Our repast over, we mounted and rolled homeward, reaching Moulmein at dusk. At the ferry, with many regrets, we parted from our mountainous friends. I embraced “old Injin-Rubber’s” trunk, making him sensible, I doubt not, of the affection I had conceived for him, and which I retain unaltered to this day.
