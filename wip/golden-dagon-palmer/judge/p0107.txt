As we came down we found the Commodore lying, broadside on, at the stockade, the <i>Serpent</i> having got aground higher up the river. There was a hurry, and a rush, and a lively flourish of gold umbrellas on shore, as the <i>Hermes</i> approached with her prize. Next came the order “beat to quarters!” Three minutes afterward, eleven guns from the fort right at the <i>Hermes</i>. Then up went the Commodore’s signal to “engage the enemy,” and the work began. The frigate, of course, opened first—broadside after broadside, a rapid and annihilating fire slap through the works.

The <i>Phlegethon</i> took up a closer position and engaged them at short distance; and the <i>Hermes</i>, which had cast loose from the prize, attacked the lower stockade, where was a small village—half barracks, half police station.
