# Chapter XXIII.

[[subtitle]] The Pagoda Road—Poonghee and Missionary—The Bazaar—Disemboweling the Gods—Burmese Veneration.

R<sc>angoon</sc>, on our return thither, is filled with bustling builders and lively bazaars. The panorama of the Pagoda Road, from the river up, presents some strange encounters and contrasts.
