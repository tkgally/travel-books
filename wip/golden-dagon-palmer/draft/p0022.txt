For a brief and blessed season you had escaped from your last accursed cripple—from the elephantiasis that had chased you round the corner, and the white leprosy that just missed you as you dived through the door of the nine-pin alley and fled up the narrow stairs, putting Brooks and his garrison between you and all the Flowery Kingdom.

Brooks's is eminently a house to get away to; and after the first three days of my foretaste in Hong-Kong of that town's infernal counterpart, I blessed mine inn, as though I were Dives and had suddenly come upon a dewy bower, in among the brimstone, where something could be had to drink.
