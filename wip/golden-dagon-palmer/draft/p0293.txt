That cunning, which I have described to you as being all that was left to me, again filled me with a sense of power and safety, which it is quite impossible to explain to you now. I felt that I could outwit them, that I could seize the thousand doubts by which they were perplexed, and on the strength of which I was sure they would not dare to touch me. They could not know whether I was friend or foe. Why should I be thus helpless and alone, if I were of the number of their victorious enemies? There were Englishmen about the person of their king; their officers of highest rank were foreign; there were Englishmen (so it was afterward reported) in that very town, fighting for its defense.
