Five weeks after that, being in Yangeenchinyah again, I was rewarded by finding him wonderfully improved: the sight of one eye was quite restored, and the other was recovering rapidly. Poor fellow! his gratitude was not very demonstrative; but his delighted wife embraced and kissed my feet, and laughed and cried together, and presently, running off in high excitement, returned—with a basket of rotten eggs. She had saved them for me ever since the morning he could first see the light. Good creature! to her they were not rotten, they were only saved—she perceived them with the nose of her heart. I told her they were very nice, and I hope I thought they were.
