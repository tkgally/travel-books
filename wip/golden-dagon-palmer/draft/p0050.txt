# Chapter IV.

[[subtitle]] Penang—Running Amok.

P<sc>enang</sc>!—Paradise and Peridom attainable by steam! And yet, for all its pools of silver, and its bowers of balm and beauty, and its bird-bells tinkling tunefully, and its orchards of Araboid aromas, and its drowsy palms nodding tipsily over brimmers of spiced ether, and its bamboos rippling where long shadows sail, that Eden also hath its fiend.

While we were there, a Malay ran amok. The fellow—a familiar vagabond who hung about the skirts of the town—had been bambooed for a theft.
