[[cont]] I thought of hallooing to them; but in another moment I was hiding myself under the cover of the boat. I felt that if I but showed just one hair of my head, there would be fifty bullets through me in a flash. They would fire, of course, at any head emerging from that boat.

Good God! how long it was! the poor woman, paralyzed with fear, crouching opposite me, and I, shutting out the light from my eyes, awaiting a horrible death at the hands of my own friends—death after all.

But there was an end of it by-and-by. Whether they heard the recall, or had been ordered to return immediately, I know not; but they went back over the bank, and I could hear the glorious brogue, as they hurrahed and shouted to each other. As the last man disappeared, I ran after him, dragged myself up the bank, and cried: “Help, help, boys!” as loud as I could; some of them returned, thinking, perhaps, that one of the party was hurt.
