# Chapter XVII.

[[subtitle]] Our Miracles—The Fisherman’s Butcha—The Tribute of Rotten Eggs—Blowing-up a Poonghee.

M<sc>any</sc> a poor wounded prisoner whom our surgeons had made whole again before he was set free, had returned to his native village with a wonder-moving story of the skill and kindness of the Inglee doctors; so the fame of our miracles had spread from Rangoon to Prome.

The poonghees practice no surgical arts; the halt Burman must go halt forever, and the poor bazaar girl, struck by a shot from the musket of a dacoit, if she does not die outright, must carry the ball till her time comes. Nevertheless, they believe that however grave the wound, however prostrating the shock, if by any conjuring they can get at the ball, so as to see and feel it and toss it in their hands, they will surely recover.
