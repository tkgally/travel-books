[[cont]] seemed to come from Heaven. I knew that savages set up madmen in their temples and worship them, accepting their ravings as oracles. I knew that if I could make them believe that I was mad, I should not only be protected by them, but regarded as something almost divine.

So I beckoned to them to come to me, dancing as I did so, yelling, shouting, and pulling my hair; taking off my jacket, I threw it down upon the deck of the boat and stamped on it; I capered, I made strange noises, and I sang—all the while beckoning them to approach.

The chief came first of all. He walked toward me very slowly and cautiously, halting every few steps. But I sharply commanded him to come on board, and he came; so we stood together on the deck of the little boat. I laid my hands upon his shoulders as I had placed them upon the shoulders of the two boatmen. He stood perfectly erect.
