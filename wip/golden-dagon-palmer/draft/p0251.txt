In the laws of Meenyoo, which are the Law of Burmah, it is laid down that “the punishment of his crimes, who judges iniquitously and decides falsely, shall be greater than though he had slain one thousand women, one hundred poonghees, or one thousand horses;” and in another place, pious Burmans are advised that “the good wives are of three sorts—the wife that is like unto a sister, the wife that is like unto a friend, and the wife that is like unto a slave; but the best of these is the wife that is like unto a slave.”
