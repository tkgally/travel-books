The <i>Medusa</i> ran on with Captain Tarleton, and, as she entered the passage, showed the signal to close and support her. Just then we discovered four large war-boats crossing over from Bandoola’s bluff. Each boat contained from 100 to 150 men, and had a light gun in the bow.

It appeared that, although Bandoola had exerted himself with creditable activity, we had been too fast for him. As soon as his boats were made out, all steam was crowded on, and, running down upon them, we “pitched in” with grape, canister, and round shot. They gave us their musketry, from the boats and the town, as well as they could, but with no effect. Our fire never slacked for two hours.
