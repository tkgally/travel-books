Then, coffin, staging, elephant and all, they carried Poonghee and set him down inside a bamboo house, erected for the occasion, and hung around with white and yellow flags; and they dashed the walls with petroleum, and laid combustible matter all about the room, on the staging and in the coffin. Then they stood off and fired rockets at the whole; till presently roaring flames burst forth, Poonghee went up to Nieban in the blaze, and everybody else went home.
