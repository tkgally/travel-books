Socially, the women of Burmah enjoy, as I have said before, in some respects the largest liberty. They are not immured, as elsewhere in the East. Among themselves they go and come at will. They interchange visits, and are admitted to the privilege of a friendly intercourse as free as that which their most indulged sisters of the West enjoy.
