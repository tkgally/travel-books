[[cont]]illumination by torches and bon-fires was brilliant, and with a few blue-lights we contributed largely to the splendor of the scene. Altogether, our grand engagement at Pontalong was an uncommonly showy affair.

When we had burned as much powder as we thought it necessary to expend for such a moral impression, we permitted our fire to be silenced, and retreated precipitately, according to arrangement; whereat fresh bursts of shouting, dancing, gun-firing, and general glorifying on shore.

Let us hope that when the Myosugi returned, he treated our friends to the thanks of the city and a public dinner, made heroes of them off-hand, and postponed their decapitation, which was pretty sure to happen one day or another.
