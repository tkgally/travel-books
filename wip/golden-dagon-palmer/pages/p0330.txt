## V. Imperial Pegu.

“P<sc>egu</sc> is a city, strong, and very fair, with walls of stone, and great ditches round about it.

“There are two towns, the old and the new. In the old town are all the merchant strangers, and very many merchants of the country. All the goods are sold in the old town, which is very great, and hath many suburbs round about it; and all the houses are made of canes, which they call bamboo, and be covered with straw. In your house you have a warehouse, or godon, which is made of brick, to put your goods in, for oftentimes they take fire, and burn, in an hour, four or five hundred houses; so that if the godon were not, you should be in danger to have all burnt in a trice.

“In the new town is the king and all his nobility and gentry. It is a city very great and populous, and is made square, and with fair walls, and a great ditch round about it, full of water, with many crocodiles in it.
