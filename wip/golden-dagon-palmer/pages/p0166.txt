Young as the boy was, he displayed much of the savage’s temper under physical suffering, and bit and scratched rather than cried. His mother remained on the deck below in an agony of impatience, but with no doubt or fear. Although we urged her repeatedly to come up to her child, she resolutely refused—“it was not for her to stand above the heads of all the Inglee Rajahs.”
