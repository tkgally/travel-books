The dominions of Russia have been silently and securely extended in Central and Eastern Asia, and it is impossible to converse with an intelligent Russian officer without perceiving the immense advantages these late conquests are acquiring for that Power; and the natural feelings of enmity and alarm of Russia, experienced instinctively by the tribes of Tartars and of Ainos, are easily visible to the traveller, whilst the covert hatred and dread entertained by the Japanese of their encroaching neighbours require more time to elicit.
