A few weeks will bring the result of an advance made so tardily as almost to forbid all hopes of success. Should the talents and ardour of officers and men lose the prize they deserved, it is to be desired that next spring our squadron should be early in the field, to prevent a repetition of the opening scene of the late campaign in the Pacific,—the escape of the enemy’s ships before the arrival of the blockading squadron.
