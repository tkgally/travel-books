The noble vessel resumed her progress westwards after seven or eight hours’ detention, and in five days the lofty mountains of Ceylon were visible on the north-western horizon. Slowly the lower land appeared without our detecting the fragrance our imaginations led us to anticipate as ever breathing from this queen of the Cinnamon Islands. A little after sunset the light glittered over the sea, and we were forced to remain all the long tropical night outside the port it beaconed.
