It is more Siberian than Siberia; and to complete the desolateness of such exile, the only peaceful joy, “unexcised of kings,” which comes to all,—domestic happiness,—is embittered at its springs, as children of European parents, however healthy and pure their blood, born at Aian, are invariably scrofulous. The family of the gentleman who gave me this information was suffering from this calamity, and it seemed to fill up the measure of the regrets with which he looked back on his Fatherland.
