The position was so strong, and might have been rendered so very much stronger, that the doubts of the correctness of the assertion of the shallowness of the northern part of the gulf increased in the minds of some officers, including, I believe, the Commodore. Unless a safer retreat existed to the northwards, the Russians could hardly have found a place where they could have awaited an attack with more confidence, and from which, when beaten, they could have easier retreated, after destroying their vessels.
