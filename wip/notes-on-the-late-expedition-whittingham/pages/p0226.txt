For a long time the Russian negotiator was trifled with; detained at Nagasaki, where,—contrary to the experience gained from our failures at Canton, a city, which in China bears the same relation to European commerce which Nagasaki hold in Japan,—Admiral Puniatin commenced his operations; that course was not agreeable to the old sailor, but it is told of him, that happening to meet Von Siebold in St. Petersburg, immediately prior to his departure for the East, he had requested the advice of this famous voyager in Japan, and was warned in reply to be most cautious, conciliatory, and yielding, and that eventually everything required would be gained.
