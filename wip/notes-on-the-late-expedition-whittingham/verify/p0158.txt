Encounter.—Southwards through Sea of Okhotsk.—Arrive at Aniwa Bay.—Skirt Shores of Sagalien, and Risiri and Rifunsiri Islands.—Conduct and Appearance of Russian Prisoners.—Distance at which Capes were visible.—Pass Matsmai.—Enter Sangar Straits.—Arrive at Hakodadi.

T<sc>he</sc> water is cold in this sea; even at the end of July it was seldom above 40° of Fahrenheit, so that a southerly wind was invariably attended by fog, whilst the chilly northerly breeze kept the temperature of the air much nearer that of the water, and consequently clearer.
