The chief factor subsequently, with the impulse of a generous nature, took every opportunity of expressing his gratitude, and repeated it to the amiable and accomplished French Commodore, upon receiving promises of protection, made in the urbane and delicate terms so natural to our gallant allies.

A higher state of excitement arose on board on the 3rd of August, on a steamer, with a vessel in tow, emerging from the more than usually distant fog. It could only be one of the allied steamers, and the ship towed must be a prize; the latter was soon seen to be a brig. Presently signals were exchanged, and busy rumours stated that the Barracouta, which had left our squadron for Japan on the 29th of July, had taken 280 prisoners.
