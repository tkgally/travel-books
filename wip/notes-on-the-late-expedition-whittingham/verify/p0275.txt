Early on the 8th of October I quitted Shanghai, and, with favourable wind, tide, and current, floated fast down on the yellow muddy river, and “Night with her hundred eyes” glancing so brightly and purely through the clear atmosphere, gleamed on our rapid passage through the romantic and rocky islets which stud the coast.
