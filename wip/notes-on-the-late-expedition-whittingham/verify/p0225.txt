Several citizens of the great Republic have fruitlessly endeavoured to sell American products, or to obtain permission to land and fit up stores; and I should not be surprised to hear that resentment has sprung up in the United States, in consequence of so few of the advantages anticipated from the treaty with Japan having been realised.
