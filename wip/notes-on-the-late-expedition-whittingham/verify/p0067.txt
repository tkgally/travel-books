The merchants are held in great contempt by Japanese authorities, and are kept in as great subjection as the shopkeepers, peasant farmers, and fishermen. They can never aspire to the proud position of wearing two swords, or of being dressed in the silken petticoat; they are, however, so far indulged as to be able to purchase the permission of girding one sword: and if the effects of these tyrannical customs are often as repulsive as they appeared in the cunning and knavish countenance of the merchant we saw, many years of free commerce will pass before this class can attain its proper high functions of gradually introducing the civilisation of the West into the country.
