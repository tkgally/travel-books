I endeavoured to imitate the humorous judge, but being no smoker, and one of those to whom the remark of a witty French commodore applies, who having weighty vices cannot bear the addition of light ones, I failed, to the intense delight and hearty laughter of our elated guests, of whom the doctor was the merriest. Whether he was less used to spirituous liquors than the rest, or that not being treated with respect by his superiors a certain amount of buffoonery was superinduced on the medical character, I could not ascertain: his closely shaven head gleamed over a countenance in which shrewdness, and perhaps the cunning of oppressed talent, were strangely mingled with good humour, and his face peered everywhere in quest of the novelties of the strangers.
