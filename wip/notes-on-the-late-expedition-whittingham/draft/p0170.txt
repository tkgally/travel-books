I was delighted to find that contrary to the severe and somewhat barbarous “orders” and “regulations,” the priest, surgeon, and sick were to be landed and placed under the charge of the chief factor, and that a letter was to be sent to the governor of the province, acquainting him with the fact, and requesting that if any of the men so landed should recover, they should not be permitted to serve against the allies until regularly exchanged. Subsequently I blushed to find that the French code, as far as it respects prisoners, was more civilised and chivalrous, as it expressly requires the speediest practicable release of <i>all non-combatants,</i>—women, priests, and civilians.
