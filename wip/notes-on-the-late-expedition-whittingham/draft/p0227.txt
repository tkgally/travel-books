The presence during the past summer of so many large English frigates and steamships, and the retreat of the Russian squadron into the Amùr, will probably reduce the ascendancy which the proximity of Russian territories and arms, and the astuteness of her diplomacy, had obtained.

We did not drop the anchor in the harbour of Simoda; we only “lay to” for an hour off the bay without communicating with the shore, on which two Europeans were seen hastily approaching the point nearest the frigate. It was conjectured, and it subsequently appeared correctly, that they belonged to the Greta, of the capture of which they were probably ignorant.
