Silently and dully the ships proceeded southwards, a damp veil often hiding them from each other; but a momentary raising up of the fog usually proved how perfect the discipline was which, for so many weeks, had kept the squadron together in unknown and mist-covered seas. On the 16th one of our consorts, lately despatched to a rendezvous to pick up any vessel sent there, was seen at anchor close to a steamer; the usual quick signals passed, and it was ere long known to all that H.
