A light southerly breeze springing up enabled us to weather Cape Sangar by three <sc>p.m.</sc>, and we slowly passed the steep rocky headlands beyond it, despite of the rapid current hurrying past us into the straits. We had been so many hours striving to get round these points, that a pleasant feeling of success was called forth as we lingeringly left them behind us.
