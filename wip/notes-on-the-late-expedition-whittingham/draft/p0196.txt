Self-possession, quietness, and order reigned as paramount as in the salons of the most civilised people: curiosity fled quickly before the sensations such a scene naturally creates, though it led me to stand outside and watch the persons issuing and entering the small bathing-house; and, without being able to pique myself on one and the least of the <i>fortes</i> of the Great Condé, that of detecting the condition of each passer-by, I saw enough to convince me that the bathers were not confined to the lowest or dissolute classes.
