[[cont]]Such a proposition seemed to take the Japanese by surprise, accustomed as they had been to assume the most authoritative control over the movements of British officers in their waters, and they declared such a thing was unheard of and impracticable. No change of mind was caused by their objections, only ample time was allowed them to communicate with the governor and the officers commanding the batteries and forts: the authorities instantly availed themselves of the time given, and their boats were rapidly borne along to the rude cadence of their standing oarsmen, to convey the intelligence to the officials of the nine provinces which they declared would be affected by the change of plan.
