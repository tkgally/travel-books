No sooner, however, had we reached the main street, which runs for a mile parallel and close to the sea, than we were joined by two two-sworded officials, who gradually collected more of their brotherhood.

It was very pleasing to trace in the rosy cheeks and robust figures and merry countenances of the lower orders the signs of a contented life, and to observe the scarcity of cripples and the absence of beggars. There was one unhappy exception to the generally healthy state of the people, which was, that a large proportion seemed to suffer from some complaint in the eyes, and not a few had lost the sight of one eye. This was more common amongst the lower officials, whose reputed disorderly lives may, perhaps, account for it.
