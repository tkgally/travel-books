I was then on the eve of relinquishing the command of the Royal Engineers at Hong Kong; and recollecting the circumstances of the disastrous repulse at Petropaulowski in the preceding autumn, I considered it my duty to offer my services, with the concurrence of the officer commanding the troops, to the admiral. His Excellency stated that he had no idea of attacking any of the Russian ports in the East; and I should have given up all thoughts of joining the squadron, had I not received a kind invitation from Commodore the Hon.
