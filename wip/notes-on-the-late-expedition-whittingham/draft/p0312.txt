The crowded state of hotels and lodging-houses, and the numbers thronging the picturesque streets of Valetta, seemed to testify that this island capital is no sufferer by the war, which hurries so many regiments past it, and new buildings, almost worthy of their vicinity to the noble auberges of the days of the knights, were rising in many places.
