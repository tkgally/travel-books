Still, were the canal practicable for large ships, a momentary naval supremacy in the Mediterranean would allow France to seize Egypt and to threaten our Indian possessions; whilst, if the canal was made only large enough for small vessels, the commercial cities of the Mediterranean would, perhaps, share a trade with us from which they are at present nearly excluded.
