[[cont]]The brig was ordered by signal to examine the enemy’s force. In her usual well-handled style, and to the muttered admiration of the Sibylle’s crew, she approached the outer bay, and off it signalled “a large frigate, three corvettes, a brig, and a steamer,” as the composition of the enemy’s force.

When we arrived within four or five miles the two sailing vessels shortened sail, and the auxiliary steam corvette, which had previously received orders to “get up steam,” was directed by signal to enter the outer harbour and reconnoitre the position of the opposing squadron. She was long getting out of our view, but did not remain long out of sight; for about 2 <sc>p.m.</sc> her yards were again visible, as likewise her signal “that there were six vessels.” She was then recalled: the strong breeze and rapid tide so retarded her return that it was nearly five o’clock when she came alongside the frigate.
