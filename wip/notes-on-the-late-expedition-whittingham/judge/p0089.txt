Our country walk was well timed, as the three following days were so wet that there was some difficulty in getting the shore boats to bring the remaining supplies of fish, vegetables, wood, and water. These necessaries were got on board before the evening of the 6th, when the harbour-master and merchants came to receive payment for the supplies.
