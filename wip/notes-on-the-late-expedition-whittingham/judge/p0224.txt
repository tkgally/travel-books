The heir to the Siogoon is supposed to be favourable to the western powers, and to wish for the speedy introduction of European arts and civilisation. To the latter party, the governor of Hakodadi and several of the most powerful of the feudal princes are presumed to belong, whilst the governors of Nagasaki have hitherto acted as if they adhered to the old national principle of seclusion.

Great difficulties have been made in the carrying out of some of the provisions in the American treaty, which are construed differently by the Japanese and United States officers: the former endeavour to frame articles in such a manner as “to keep the word of promise to the ear and break it to the hope,” the phrases following each other, so that each restricts its predecessor until the residue becomes to them “beautifully less.”
