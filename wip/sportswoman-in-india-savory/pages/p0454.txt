The question is worth asking, why in a country of such natural wealth as India the lower classes should wallow in ignorance, degradation, and poverty? The reasons—to go the root of the matter—are twofold: the climate, and the fertility of the soil. A hot climate incapacitates men for arduous work, and enforces a food which requires little labour. A fertile soil will grow rice and return an average of at least fifty-fold.
