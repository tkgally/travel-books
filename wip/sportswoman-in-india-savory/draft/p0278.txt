Srinagar and our marches through the valleys, though charming, were welcomely exchanged for a more adventurous month, upon which we could look back afterwards, and feel that we had put in a chapter of stirring life, and had played the game a little.

We slept one night at Soper. Who does not know the thrill of feeling with which one starts upon the unknown, those great expectations which are half the battle? The day called us about five—a beautiful morning it was, a westerly wind still blowing strong, but the clouds all blown away to China.
