“A daring leader is a dangerous thing. F. pronounced our best way to lie down the <i>couloir</i>, and taking H.’s place, cut footholes for our descent. It certainly was steep. We were going cautiously, moving one at a time, when suddenly we heard ‘<i>Crack!</i>’ and all our hearts stood still. H., just above me, said quietly, ‘We’re done for!’ The snow had cracked across just above us, at first only a gape of half an inch; but now the crust of the lower half was slowly beginning to slide downwards, and away we went on it.

“‘Stop!’ we all shouted instantaneously, dashing our axes into the underlying ice. They slid over the hard surface fruitlessly. ‘Stop!’ thundered F., again and again hewing at the ice. But there was no stopping. Slowly at first, faster and faster every moment, we flew down the <i>couloir</i> on our avalanche, driving up clouds of snow in front of it. <i>Was this the end?</i>
