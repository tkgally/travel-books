“Yes,” he observed, “your story is indeed a good one, and reminds me of the <i>Kamishimo</i> thief of which you have no doubt heard before this.”

“No, indeed, I cannot say I have, and I would gladly hear it, but that it is so late and I must be going. I thank you for the entertainment. I have enjoyed the benefit of your society for the past year, and hope I may continue to do so.”

“You are very kind, and I am sensible of the honour you have done me in calling on me so quickly. I hope you’ll come and pay me a long visit when the days lengthen.”

“I shall not fail to do so. Good bye. Good bye.”
