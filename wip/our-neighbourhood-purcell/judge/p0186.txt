# A Flute Player.

A conservatism which is remarkable only for its slavish obedience to established custom is an insuperable bar to progress. A stroll through “Our Neighbourhood” on any day of the week would verify this aphorism unless the wanderer happened to meet in his circumambulations with Inos’ke, the flute man. Exceptions, however, after all, prove the rule, and the contrast to the established state of things which he presents only serves to throw into stronger relief the old-fashioned prejudices of the neighbours.

To a community so dunderheaded as ours, a nose to the end of time would simply be a nose. Our grandfathers were content to consider it as such, and who are we that we should endeavour to improve upon our ancestors? It’s no use to represent it as an “organ” or a nasal prominence, for we are no lovers of new-fangled words (or people either for the matter of that), and we wont believe you.
