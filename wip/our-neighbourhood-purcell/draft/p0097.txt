“Ha! ha! ha! Very true, very true! I verily believe the dog at the Okurasho had an ancestor at that meeting, Mr. Tapster. I always thought it was no harm for us poor folks to steal a bit, but for a man in office it’s—detestable,” said the barber, as he tied the knot of a queue, he was at work on, so viciously, that he held his breath over it, and only relieved his mind and his lungs simultaneously as he pronounced the word “detestable.”
