Seizing an opportunity of a pause for breath, however, he proceeded to rate his friend soundly for his misconduct. “There you go again,” he says. “You could not hold your tongue for a couple of hours, but must take advantage of a public place like this to make yourself ridiculous, and disgrace me.”

“What!” returns the Japanese dog in an outraged tone:—“Did you not tell me that I should always bark at thieves?”

“Of course I did, what then?”

“Why, I’ve taken a look round here, and as I see none but thieves, I’ve followed your advice: that’s all!”
