[[cont]]Three thousand six hundred million <i>ri</i> the poor soul is supposed to travel on his way to heaven, but as he visits this earth of ours on the 12th night of the 7th month of every year, it is difficult to conjecture when his journey ends. It is said that this is one of the Buddhist mysteries which true believers must not question. The festival just alluded to is called <i>bon</i>, and is kept by lighting fires of hemp refuse before the doors of such as have dead relatives, and hanging lighted lanterns in the windows to guide the spirits to their quondam homes and back again. Nor is it the only occasion on which they are supposed to revisit their friends; for it is a popular superstition that a butterfly out of season, or flying in an unusual place, is a spirit coming back to hover round his loved ones.
