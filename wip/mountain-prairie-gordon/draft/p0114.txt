It is a common belief among the people of Victoria that there are no mosquitoes in the Province. We found them, however, as active and powerful as we had ever known them in Ontario. If, as is said, it is the female mosquito that stings, this is the only instance in which there appears to be a superabundance of female labour in British Columbia.
