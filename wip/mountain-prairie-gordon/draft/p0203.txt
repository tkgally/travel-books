The rain was falling heavily, and the lightning playing about us, as we pitched camp at the lower end of the Portage, on a plateau about ninety feet above the river. On the opposite side, down near the water’s edge, we saw a solitary light glimmering in the small log-house that is known as Hudson’s Hope. We had passed the Mountains, and had entered on the vast Prairie Region.
