Although our Canadian Pacific line will not pass through it, and although it may not for many years be found necessary to embrace it in our system of railways, yet it is known that a branch line would be perfectly feasible and for the most part easy of construction, extending from the vicinity of Edmonton to Pine River or to Dunvegan. The district must naturally be peopled by immigrants coming from the east, and therefore not until large portions of the country between Manitoba and Edmonton have been cultivated need we look for many settlers on the banks of the Peace; but none who traverse it can doubt that the Province of Unchagah must in due time prove to be a most valuable portion of what is, as yet, the undeveloped interior of our Dominion.
