With tooth and claw the bear tore his flesh, at one stroke taking away his scalp, carrying the right ear with it, at another stripping a large piece from his shoulder, at another rending a piece from his side. Through all this torture poor Moostoos remained conscious, but was motionless as a corpse till the grizzly, apparently thinking that he was dead, moved off, and then the lacerated man dragged himself to the camp. He has never wholly recovered, though it is four years since this happened, but he still hunts with much energy and success. It is hardly possible for him, however, to go nearer to the jaws of death without finding them close on him for ever than he did in his fight with the grizzly.
