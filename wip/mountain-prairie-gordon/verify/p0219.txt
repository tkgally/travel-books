Records of the climate kept at Fort St. John shew that the first snow-fall usually occurs towards the end of October; the average date for the first appearance of ice on the river is about the 7th November, and for the opening of the river about the 20th April, while planting begins early in May, and potato digging about the third week in September. The average depth of snow does not exceed two and a half to three feet; and here, as throughout the district, the horses winter out, finding abundant grass on the neighbouring slopes and plateaux. Major Butler states that when he passed here he encountered the first mosquito of the year on the 20th April, an incident that to many in Ontario may be expressive of the early opening of the season.
