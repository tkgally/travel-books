The constant change of our course revealed every few minutes new intersections of these islands and of the mountain ranges of the mainland, and we could often see three or four lines of distance. Sometimes we were shut in on every side, until it seemed impossible that our ship should ever find its way out. Then the captain would point to an island just ahead, and say our course lay behind it, and sure enough in a few minutes we were “round the corner,” so to speak, and in another lovely reach of sea.
