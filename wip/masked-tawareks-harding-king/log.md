## Decisions
- No title on the cloth cover: `cover.jpg` is the title page (a pencil scribble over “KING”, as on the scan).
- The folding map is missing (torn out; stub at p0425); the Internet Archive's own scan (searchformaskedt0000unse) lacks it too: said in `notes_on_text` (Tom, 2026-11-22: a missing map alone does not hold a book back).
- Divisions: half-title, list of illustrations, the book's title above Chapter I (kind `front`, Chapter I `n: 2`) and the index in the JSON only. Frontispiece p0008-1 on the title page.
- Contents: no printed contents and no chapter titles: editor's titles naming places and subjects (proposed by a Sonnet subagent from the draft text, checked against the opening pages; said in `notes_on_text`).
- The three pages of figures (pp. 326–328, p0416–p0418) are bound inside a sentence of the appendix: `leaf_order` reads p0419 (p. 329) before them (said in `notes_on_text`). The heading split over the opening (“Comparison of Greek and / Tawarek Methods of Writing.”) is one heading on p0416.
- Figures: 41 photographs on 38 plates (21 printed sideways, turned with `--rotate 270`), the Tifinagh inscription p0365 and 11 figures in the appendix, all cropped by Opus subagents and looked at.
- The letters in translation (pp. 148–149, 246–248) are in body type set off by space only: plain paragraphs (the spot-read had made them blockquotes after the draft's one on p0192; unwrapped, lessons “quotations set in the text type”).
- Waivers: `leaves` p0009, p0010 (IA's overall shots of the title opening). `keep_hyphens` p0046 (south-east), p0186 (long-expected).
- p0360 “tbe” in the draft and the corrections pass; printed “the” (found by preflight `misprints`).
- “Tuareg Tuarik” (preface) printed without a comma: left as printed.
- Assumed: period “c. 1901–02” (the spring of 1901 spoken of as past, p0189; no year given).
