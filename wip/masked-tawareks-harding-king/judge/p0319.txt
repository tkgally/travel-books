The discovery of these letters created such a sensation in Algeria that the French Minister at Tangier was instructed by his Government to demand an explanation from the Sultan.

After some negotiation he succeeded in extracting the following curious repudiation, which the Sultan caused to be written by his Secretary for Foreign Affairs:—
