H. visited the arsenal, which is carried on, on a different and less expensive system than that at Foo-Chow, there being only seven or eight Europeans in the whole establishment. He was much pleased with a large frigate they have just built, and much amused to find himself accosted as her future commander. There seemed, in fact, some difficulty in persuading the public that such was not the case. The report no doubt arose from the general belief, that the Chinese have some difficulty in knowing what to do with their vessels after they have built them, and will ultimately require to have a European naval man to teach them.
