## Tennyson’s (Alfred) Works. Author’s Edition.

To be completed in Five Monthly Volumes, commencing May 1.

This Edition will be in crown octavo, printed on superfine paper, with handsome margins, in clear, old-faced type—each volume containing a Frontispiece—and will be elegantly bound, either in cloth gilt, bevelled boards, or in Roxburgh binding, with top edges gilt.

<i>Cloth gilt,</i> 6<i>s. each; half-morocco, Roxburgh style,</i> 7<i>s.</i> 6<i>d. each.</i>
