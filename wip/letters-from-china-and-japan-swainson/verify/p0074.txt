The present plan is that he should sail about the beginning of October, after the Typhoon season is over, and cruise down to Hong-Kong, spending some weeks <i>en route.</i> I have a slight hope that if he remains there long, I may go by steamer and join him; but nothing is yet fixed. Meantime we are making what H. calls my cage, and its outer court (viz., the house and garden) as pretty as we can, and the house is a very cheerful one, which is a point in my favour, during the long solitary days that are before me.

I wish you could see the Anchorage as it is at present. On Sunday when we went to the top of the hill above the church, with our backs to all the ugly buildings and chimneys of the Arsenal, the view of the harbour with about seventy fine ships lying at anchor, and the beautiful broad river and hills beyond was magnificent.
