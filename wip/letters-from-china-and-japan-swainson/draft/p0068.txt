A little fish and vegetables serves as <i>a sauce</i> to give piquancy to the rice, which they hold in a small bowl close to their chin, and shovel into the mouth with chop sticks.

As you perceive, an eastern household is composed entirely of men, excepting the “Amah” or lady’s attendant, who is not mentioned in the foregoing list, for the excellent reason that I have not yet found one. We had some difficulty in making the servants understand that they must knock at “<i>Missisy’s</i>” door, and not walk into my room in their usual unceremonious fashion, but they at last seem to comprehend the propriety of doing so.

One of the most disagreeable customs the Chinese have, is the length to which they allow their nails to grow. In the case of one’s own servants their <i>eagle’s claws</i> are, of course, at once ordered off, and H. finds it quite necessary to pursue the same system with his pupils by having, every now and then, a parade for the cutting of nails.
