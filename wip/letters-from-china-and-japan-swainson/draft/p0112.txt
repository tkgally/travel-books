Among the delicacies of the cool season, I must not forget to mention the small oysters for which Foo-Chow is remarkable, and which are grown in quantities on bamboos in the river.

# Letter XXV.

[[right]] <i>January 10th.</i>

Hearing a sound which somewhat reminded me of my national “<i>pipes</i>,” I looked out of the window and beheld a red and gilt chair preceded by a number of people playing on instruments made of bamboo, and in form resembling a flute, while others carried baskets, lanterns, and an umbrella. “Boy” informs me it was a “<i>gentleman going to catchee wife!</i>”

The <i>catcheeing</i> of a wife is as serious an affair in China as it is elsewhere, and accompanied by many more forms and ceremonies than in most countries, nor is the important matter of a trousseau regarded by the Chinese women with less interest than that of their western sisters.
