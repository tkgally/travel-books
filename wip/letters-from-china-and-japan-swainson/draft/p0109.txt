# Letter XXIV.

[[right]] 1<i>st January</i> 187-.

The year, I believe, was ushered in by our fellow Europeans with much firing of guns and sending up of rockets; but all these demonstrations were lost on the lazy people, who, plunged in the arms of Morpheus, heard none of them.

To-day we have had a great many visitors, it being the fashion here for every one to call or leave cards; which latter custom has been adopted by the Imperial Commissioner and some other Mandarins, from whom we received pretty, bright-coloured bits of paper on which were stamped the characters signifying their name. Of course the Chinese card of <i>His Honour-Sûn</i> was returned in due form.
