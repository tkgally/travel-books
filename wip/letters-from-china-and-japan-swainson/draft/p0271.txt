## Payne (John).

SONGS OF LIFE AND DEATH. Crown 8vo. 5<i>s.</i>

“The art of ballad-writing has long been lost in England, and Mr. Payne may claim to be its restorer. It is a perfect delight to meet with such a ballad as ‘May Margaret’ in the present volume.”—<i>Westminster Review.</i>

## Pelletan (Eugène).

THE DESERT PASTOR, JEAN JAROUSSEAU. Translated from the French. By Colonel E. P. De L’Hoste. With an Engraved Frontispiece. Fcap. 8vo. New Edition. 3<i>s.</i> 6<i>d.</i>

“A touching record of the struggles in the cause of religious liberty of a real man.”—<i>Graphic.</i>

“There is a poetical simplicity and picturesqueness; the noblest heroism; unpretentious religion; pure love; and the spectacle of a household brought up in the fear of the Lord. . . .”—<i>Illustrated London News.</i>

## Penrice (Major J.), B.A.

A DICTIONARY AND GLOSSARY OF THE KOR-AN. With copious Grammatical References and Explanations of the Text. 4to. 21<i>s.</i>

Intended to be of service to the beginner in mastering some of the difficulties of the Kor-an, as an introduction to the study of Arabic literature. Each word will be found under its verbal root. The original meaning of the words is added. Standard books on the same subject have been consulted, and their authority quoted.

“The book is likely to answer its purpose in smoothing a beginner’s road in reading the Kor-ân.”—<i>Academy.</i>
