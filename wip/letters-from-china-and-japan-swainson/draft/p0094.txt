The latter fact is not due to the amount of protection Britain gives her subjects in China, but for reasons some of which I have already mentioned, and others shall be given hereafter. Meantime you are going to have a commentary on what has been said on the subject in Parliament.

In the <i>Times</i> of the 28th September there is a list given of the ships of war stationed at the different ports, in which it is mentioned that the “Severn” is at Foo-Chow; and then there is a kind of postscript stating that the “Banterer” has been sent there, as if intended to denote that the latter was <i>in addition</i> to the other ship. It is impossible to believe that the statement could be made with the wish to deceive; but there is a material difference in the amount of protection the two ships would give—the “Severn” being a large frigate, the other a small gunboat.
