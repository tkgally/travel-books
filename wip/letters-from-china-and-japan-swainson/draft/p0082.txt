Madame D. and I have paid several visits to the Roman Catholic Church to try a very fine harmonium which has lately arrived from France.

Of course she and her compatriots are greatly excited about the war with Prussia, indeed we all are, and I do nothing but read newspapers for days after the mails come in. With these we are very well supplied as our neighbour and we take the <i>Times</i> and the <i>Pall Mall Budget.</i>

We are afraid all this commotion in Europe may turn away attention from the Chinese who are evidently much inclined to get <i>bumptious</i> in the north, in consequence of no decided action being taken regarding the Tien-tsin massacre; but surely in the cause of humanity such a barbarous outrage will not be allowed to pass without punishment.

The people in the north and those here are quite a different race and speak a different dialect, so that no one need be nervous about us. The Imperial Commissioner also is a most despotic individual and keeps them in very good order. He was most indignant about the robbery and did all in his power to discover the offender.
