The women’s dress, in which there is not much variety, you must have often seen in pictures, so I need hardly describe it.

In the original it is picturesque, but must be most uncomfortable from the extreme narrowness of the petticoat, which causes great difficulty in walking; this, added to the loose kind of pattens they wear on their feet, obliges them to shuffle along with that <i>Grecian bend</i> which is said to be the origin of a somewhat similar fashion among nations who ought to know better than to distort their natural gait.

20<i>th.</i>—Behold us at the International Hotel, Yokohama, having just landed under a leaden sky and torrent of rain.

First impressions of this part of Japan decidedly unfavourable!
