The V——s made very good arrangements for my comfort and happiness. Sir H—— is the type of an Englishman. I enjoyed my trip to Claydon very much.

9<i>th.</i>—Went first to the India Office, and then drove to Wimbledon to Sir B. F——s. He was not at home. Saw Lady, Mr., and the Miss F——s. I had a pretty long talk with them. Mr. F—— is a nice young fellow. He is fifteen. Miss C. F—— played the piano and sang some pretty airs. I cannot help remarking here again, that the F——s are good and very hospitable people. Then called on the Persian Ambassador, who was not in the house. Took a drive through the Park and returned home about 6.30 <sc>p.m.</sc>

10<i>th.</i>—Went to Euston Square Station, and left at 10.30 <sc>a.m.</sc> for Langholm. Changed trains twice. Arrived at Langholm about 8.30 <sc>p.m.</sc> In the evening it was very hot. Mr. M—— met us at the distance of twenty miles from Langholm. We are living in the hotel called the “Temperance Hotel.”
