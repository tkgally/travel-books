Then we saw the people dancing quadrilles and Scotch reels. I like latter very much. The ground was nice, green, and soft. I enjoyed this afternoon very much.

14th.—Took a long drive in the afternoon, which I enjoycd very much.

15th.—Went to Longwood to Mrs. B——’s at 4 p.m., where I saw some ladies and gentlemen to whom I had been introduced already. We played the game of croquet twice. I won both times. We enjoyed it very much. We had some grapes and champagne at Mrs. B——’s; who is a nice polite lady.

16th.—Drove to Mr. J——’s moor to shoot grouse. After driving some five or six miles, rode. We got ten brace of grouse. It was hot. Returned at about 7 p.m.

17th.—Called on Mr. M—— in the afternoon, and then took a long drive, which we enjoyed very much.

18th.—Went shooting at about 11 p.m. We got ten brace of grouse. Returned at about 7 p.m. It was cold, and mist and dew were falling.
