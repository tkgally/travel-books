In the evening went to the Queen's Theatre, where the <i>Midsummer Night's Dream</i> was played. The dresses and scenery were something wonderful. Mr. Phelps acted remarkably well.

6<i>th.</i>—Went to the Stereoscopic Company, in Regent Street, and bought some photographic views of London.

7<i>th.</i>—Went to Price's Candle Factory, where we were received by the manager, who showed us how the candles and soap are made. Mr. Child showed us how the night-lights are made. I had never seen the night-lights before. I was very much pleased with the process by which the above things are made. I liked the coloured candles very much. We saw how they are packed also.

In the evening went to the Prince of Wales's Theatre, where we saw <i>M.P.</i> and two other pieces played. I liked <i>M.P.</i> very much; Miss Marie Wilton acted Cecilia's part very well. The theatre is small, but it is pretty, and nicely decorated.

8<i>th.</i>—Went and called on Mr. S—— at 3 <sc>p.m.</sc>, when I saw Mrs. S—— and others. Had some champagne and grapes with them. Mr. and Mrs. S—— are very good, kind, and hospitable persons.
