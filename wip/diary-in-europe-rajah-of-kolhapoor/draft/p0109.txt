16<i>th.</i>—It was rainy to-day.

17<i>th.</i>—Drove to Euston Square station, and left at 7.15 for Dublin. Arrived at Holyhead at 2 <sc>p.m.</sc> Set sail immediately, and arrived at Kingstown at 6.45 <sc>p.m.</sc> The Private Secretary to the Viceroy of Ireland met me at Kingstown. With him went in the train to Dublin, where we arrived at 7 <sc>p.m.</sc> Then in the Viceroy’s carriage drove to the “Gresham Hotel.” I was very sick in crossing the channel, on account of its heaving. The steamer in which we crossed the channel was large and very nice.

18<i>th.</i>—Went in the Viceroy’s carriage to the Viceregal Lodge at 12.30, and saw the Viceroy, who introduced Lady Spencer to me. I was received by one of the Viceroy’s aides-de-camp and private secretary at the entrance. I had a pretty long conversation with Lord Spencer, who is a very kind and courteous man. The Viceregal Lodge is in the Phœnix Park, which is a very large and beautiful park. The lodge is large and well furnished. The Viceroy returned my visit, with his aide-de-camp, at 3.30. The Viceroy’s Private Secretary called on me afterwards, and with him we drove first to Trinity College, where we saw the Provost, the head of the College.
