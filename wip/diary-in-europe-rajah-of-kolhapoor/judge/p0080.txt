Then went with Lady D—— to her house, where I had some peaches. Then we saw the people dancing quadrilles and Scotch reels. I like the latter very much. The ground was nice, green, and soft. I enjoyed this afternoon very much.

14<i>th.</i>—Took a long drive in the afternoon, which I enjoyed very much.

15<i>th.</i>—Went to Longwood to Mrs. B——’s at 4 <sc>p.m.</sc>, where I saw some ladies and gentlemen to whom I had been introduced already. We played the game of croquet twice. I won both times. We enjoyed it very much. We had some grapes and champagne at Mrs. B——’s, who is a nice polite lady.

16<i>th.</i>—Drove to Mr. J——’s moor to shoot grouse. After driving some five or six miles, rode. We got ten brace of grouse. It was hot. Returned at about 7 <sc>p.m.</sc>

17<i>th.</i>—Called on Mr. M—— in the afternoon, and then took a long drive, which we enjoyed very much.

18<i>th.</i>—Went shooting at about 11 <sc>p.m.</sc> We got ten brace of grouse. Returned at about 7 <sc>p.m.</sc> It was cold, and mist and dew were falling.
