It commands a very good view of the neighbouring country. There are small summer-places in these gardens from which also a good view is obtained. Then saw the farm and the keepers’ houses. The castle is a large, old-fashioned building. It dates from very old time. Some portion of it has been built lately. The rooms in it are nicely fitted up. The Duke’s office-room is full of different kinds of arms and armour. It is a very large and good collection of arms. The Duke was very kind and attentive to me. Drove to Fochabers in the Duke’s carriage and left at 4.9 <sc>p.m.</sc> At Forres, I met Mr. Mackintosh, who introduced some gentlemen to me. Then we went in the same carriage to Inverness, where we arrived at 6.30 <sc>p.m.</sc>

<i>29th.</i>—Drove to the Inverness Pier at 6.45 <sc>a.m.</sc>, and embarked on board the steamer <i>Gondolier</i>, in which the arrangements were very nice and comfortable. The steamer was beautifully furnished. The scenery at some parts of the canal was charming and beautiful. Our steamer started at 7 <sc>a.m.</sc>, and arrived at Banavie at 2.30 <sc>p.m.</sc> Thence drove a mile and again embarked on board tho steamer <i>Pioneer</i>, which was not so pretty as the <i>Gondolier</i>. Arrived at Oban at 7.45 <sc>p.m.</sc>, and drove to the “Great Western Hotel” where we stayed the night.
