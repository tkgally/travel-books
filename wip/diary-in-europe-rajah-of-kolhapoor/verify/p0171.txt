2. That a committee, to be appointed, should be requested to make arrangements for raising in this city some memorial of the great esteem in which this virtuous, well-beloved, and educated prince was held by all, and of the deep regret which was felt at his sudden death.

3. That a letter of condolence be addressed to his Highness's mother, and the two Ranees he has left behind him, expressive of the sympathy of the country with them in their unconsolable grief; and deploring his Highness's death, in the prime of youth, as a great national loss.

4. That in order to carry out the wishes of the meeting just expressed, and in order to collect subscriptions, a committee be appointed.
