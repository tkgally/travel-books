In the evening took a drive through Regent Street, Bond Street, and Oxford Street, and saw how they were lit up. I enjoyed my drive very much. I liked the way in which the things were put in the shops. Every part of the things were shown to the public.

11<i>th.</i>—Drove in the afternoon to Wimbledon, to Sir B. F——’s. Saw Lady and the Misses F——, and had a long conversation with them. They gave me a very good reception. I have never seen such kind people as the F——s are. In the evening went to St. James’s Hall for the concert, which was in aid of the German sick and wounded. Several ladies and gentlemen sang and played on different instruments. I liked the German song, “The Watch on the Rhine.” I was very much disappointed at not having heard Madame Otto Goldschmidt.

13<i>th.</i>—Drove to Reilley’s, in Oxford Street, and selected some guns.

Went in the afternoon to a shooting-ground, and shot with the guns which we chose yesterday.

In the evening went to the Globe Theatre, and saw <i>Taming the Shrew</i> played. I liked the play. Miss Alleyne acted well. The theatre is a small but pretty one.

15<i>th.</i>—Drove to the India Office at 3.30 <sc>p.m.</sc> No one being there whom I knew, went and called on Mr. W——, and then took a drive in the park.
