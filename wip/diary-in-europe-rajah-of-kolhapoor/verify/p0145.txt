# II.
[[subtitle]] The Durbar at Poona.

[[center]] <i>(From the Bombay Gazette.)</i>

[[right]] Friday, October 9, 1868.

T<sc>he</sc> first Durbar held by Sir Seymour Fitzgerald took place at Poona on Thursday. The scene of the ceremonial was the handsome hall of the Engineering College, a room well adapted for the purpose, under the circumstances. The day was fine, the roads were crowded with natives in holiday costume, and they evidently enjoyed the tumasha, as they always do.
