<i>3rd.</i> Drove to a photographer’s, where my likeness was taken in different positions. Took a drive on the Thames Embankment. An enormous sum of money has been spent on this embankment. It is a broad way, and there is a good avenue of trees on one side of it.

In the evening went to Drury Lane Theatre, where I saw <i>Amy Robsart</i> played. The scenery and dresses were beautiful. I liked them immensely. I cannot write how pretty these dresses and scenery were. The theatre is very large and handsome.

<i>4th.</i>—Drove in the afternoon to the British Institution. There is a large collection here of paintings drawn by members of the Royal Family and painters. The money which is got by selling pictures goes to aid the German sick and wounded in the war. I bought some pictures painted by the royal painters. Then we went to see the Holborn Viaduct works, which are very large, and of good strong workmanship. We saw the Meat Market on our way. It is an enormously large and fine strong stone building. Returned at about 6 <sc>p.m.</sc> We celebrated our <i>Dussara</i>[[fnref *]] here.
