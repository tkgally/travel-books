Why do again what has been done so well already by Mr. H. L. Whitney, of Honolulu, whose handy little pamphlet will tell all that the traveller needs to know? Miss I. L. Bird has written a charming book of travel in the Hawaiian Archipelago. If I can give my readers half the pleasure she has given me, I shall be satisfied. Better still, if I can leave them wishing that they too could go to Hawaii. Why not? Sixteen days of journeying from Boston or New York will take you there.
