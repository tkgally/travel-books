So singing and sighing we sailed past the setting sun into the night again. And at midnight there was a cry made. We started as if the coming of the Bridegroom were indeed announced. “Look!” cried one, pointing to the heavens; and there, far up among the stars that overhung the southern horizon, we saw a great light.
