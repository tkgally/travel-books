In this very fact they show that they have taken Christianity in its typical form; for where the spirit of Christ is, there always is the spirit of missions. I was in Honolulu when the “Morning Star,” the mission-ship which makes an annual voyage among the stations in the Pacific, arrived there. She brought glad tidings from afar; for there, too, in those lost islands, so far away from the civilized centres of the world, the word of Jesus Christ had taken such hold that the queen of one of the islands—Obodinyeh—had left her pleasant home among the hilly and fruitful lands, and sailed away from her kingdom and her people to carry the news of salvation to the low coral islands further west. Such examples of Christian heroism as this can hardly be surpassed, if they can be equalled, among our own more favored communities; and they show, beyond all cavil, the receptiveness of these people to the Christian spirit.
