Norwegians usually receive good impressions of our country from their emigrating compatriots; many of the seamen and skippers have sailed to and from our shores, and have brought back a slight knowledge of the language and reminiscences of wonderful fruits, grand buildings, gala spectacles, and a general gorgeousness, such as the palaces of Bagdad offered to the dazzled eyes of Aladdin.
