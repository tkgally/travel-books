“The glass goes up,” he replies, not without a deprecating glance around the horizon. He identifies himself with our desires and feels an almost personal responsibility for their fulfilment. We study the heavens like astronomers; we walk the deck with restless steps, and note all signs. The horizon has grown broader as we advance in latitude, and now, at nearly 72°, its sweep appears illimitable. At eleven o'clock the clouds have all melted out of sight, and a vast unbroken dome of pale blue spreads over a pale lavender sea.
