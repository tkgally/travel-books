The solos of the Litany were intoned by a deacon whose <i>basso profundo</i> of incredible power resounded like the notes of a great organ to the remotest end and all through the domes of the vast building. These voices are peculiar to Russia; they are sought for through distant provinces, and receive large remuneration. A very earnest and frequent refrain is “<i>Gospodimi pomilui</i>” (“Lord, have mercy on us”), in which choristers join, and prolong the last syllable like the sigh of an Æolian harp.
