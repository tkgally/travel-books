The climax of all the dazzling and half-barbaric opulence of historic and hereditary souvenirs is found in the Imperial Treasury. From the palace of the Tsars an immense staircase closed by a trellised iron gate leads to this receptacle of gifts from sultans and shahs, tributes of alliance with wild Asiatic chiefs, tokens of commercial traffic,—in short, the assembled Lares and Penates of all centuries past.
