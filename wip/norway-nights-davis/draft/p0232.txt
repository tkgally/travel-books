[[cont]]covered with the very finest enamel of various colors, principally light blue and ruby, laid on with twisted threads of gold that would pass through a needle’s eye. Jewel-caskets, toilet articles, sugar-bowls, spoons, etc., fill one shop exclusively. This fine enamel is rather new, and therefore little known outside of Russia; but even the older fabric on silver gilt is also beautiful, though not quite so delicate. The best shop is that of Ivan Petrovitch Chlebnekoff, on the Nevski Prospekt.

The fur-shops are ruinously attractive, especially the sable, which we had never seen so fine and dark. The London merchants declare that they procure some of the very best qualities; but the Moscow dealers assured us that they never sell those outside of Russia. Sables are always expensive because so small a portion of the little animal is of the coveted dark color. The famous Potemkin had a muff which cost a thousand guineas.
