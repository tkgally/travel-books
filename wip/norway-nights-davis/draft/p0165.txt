We motioned for release, but he shook his head. We waited five impatient minutes, and I then made a second appeal, whispering the word “Dampskib,” and pointing on my watch to the hour of its departure. That immovable custodian only shook his head more obstinately, and gazed fixedly at the preacher. We heard the ship’s bell ring for the passengers’ return, and trembled at the thought of being abandoned to our fate. At last the prayer reached its ultimate note, the key was deliberately turned in the door, we flew to the boat, the boat flew to the steamer, and “home, sweet home” was never more welcome.
