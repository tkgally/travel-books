# Chapter II.

[[subtitle]] Moscow.

W<sc>hen</sc> the Emperor Nicholas decreed a railway between St. Petersburg and Moscow for military convenience, and with pen in hand drew a straight line, which left out all villages and towns <i>en route,</i> he builded better than he knew, or cared, for the benefit of future tourists. A more flat and desolate intervening country would be difficult to find anywhere; even more dreary is it than the desert plateau between Burgos and Madrid. Therefore one loses nothing by taking the night-train to Moscow, which starts about eight o'clock.
