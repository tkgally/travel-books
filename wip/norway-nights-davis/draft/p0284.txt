The bells of Moscow! There may exist such musical intonations elsewhere, but I have never heard them. Every morning at an early hour the bells in the churches near the Slavianski Bazaar lifted their grand voices, not suddenly, in stunning avalanche of sound, but in single successive notes in the same diapason, which filled the air with harmonious pulsations, deep and thrilling as those of a mighty organ. All other bells, even festive bells in other lands, are a jangle and a wrangle forever hereafter,—excepting “Big Ben” of Westminster and St. Mark’s in Venice, which in their melodious resonance are akin to those of Moscow.
