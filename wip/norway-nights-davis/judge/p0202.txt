More attractive, however, are the cottage-interiors and portraits of the Swedish artist Amalia Lindegren, whose peasant-children have the grace and innocence of Arcadia under their homely garb.

The shops of Stockholm are not beguiling; old silver at “Hammei’s,” Dalecarlian costumes and excellent colored photographs of peasants, were our only temptations.
