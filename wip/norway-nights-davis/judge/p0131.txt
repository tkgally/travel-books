This ancient pile stands a little apart, in a well-kept church-yard, among such shrubs and flowers as the climate affords. It is built of a bluish slate which contrasts well with white marble columns in the interior; near the high altar stands a replica of Thorwaldsen’s majestic Christ, presented by the sculptor, the effect of which is very noble as seen from the entrance-door.
