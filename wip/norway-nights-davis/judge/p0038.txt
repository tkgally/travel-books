However, not to plunge unduly into history, Bernadotte, successful adventurer, soldier, and statesman as he was, had wisdom enough to make many popular concessions, and his successors have proved acceptable rulers. But it is evident even to a passing traveller that the old jealousies are not quite effaced between the united kingdoms.
