We went to the ambitiously-named Victoria, which overlooks the lake. Its bare floors and deficiency of toilet requisites suggested “roughing it,” but through our emphatic gestures and our few invaluable Norse words the bewildered landlady contrived to understand enough of our needs to make us comfortable. After our excellent supper of brook trout and wheat pancakes, or “pankagen,” which are a specialty of Norway, the captain of the <i>Kong Oscar</i> walked up from his boat to escort us to a neighboring waterfall which for the honor of his country he was fain to exhibit.
