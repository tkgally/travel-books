We embarked for Christiania on the fine Danish steamer <i>Melchior;</i> but as it came from Stettin with full complement of passengers, we were unable to obtain private sleeping-cabins, and it became a serious question whether we should not be obliged to sit up all night. Happily, the captain’s kindness, added to a slight knowledge of English and German, came to the rescue, and he ordered the large deck-saloon to be placed at our disposal after 10 <sc>p.m.</sc> This was the first of a series of gratuitous courtesies which we received from the chief officers of ten different steamers in northern waters; for which we hold them in grateful remembrance.
