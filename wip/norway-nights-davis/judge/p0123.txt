We sat on deck in view of friendly islands, and soon entered the extensive fiord of Trondhjem. <i>En passant</i> it may be said that all these fiord steamers provide an abundance of good food and good sleeping-cabins—which latter, however, are few in number and ought to be engaged at least two or three days in advance by telegram or letter to the main offices. There is a curious custom of asking for families what they call a “moderation” price, which means that a man and his wife, or a brother and sister, pay for only one and a half tickets. This explains the title of a pleasant little book called “One and a Half in Norway.”
