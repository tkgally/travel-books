[[cont]]ract, the first station of interest was Kirkestuen, which boasts of a quaint little church containing some stiff Byzantine-like painted figures of Christ with angels, an almost solitary instance of such adornment in Norway. While we waited at the station to rest, a jolly old party, with great “breadth of beam” and a round face wreathed in smiles, stood still to be sketched, much to the delight of his family. I have no doubt he was the landlord; and, in compliment to the Byzantine angels, he took an attitude as nearly as possible like theirs, rigid, ascetic, with pendent arms and monumental legs, as ludicrous a combination as could be imagined.

At the unpronounceably-named station of Skjaesggestad the dark red spire of another antique church is the only prominent object.
