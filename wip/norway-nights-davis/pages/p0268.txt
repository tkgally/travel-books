As we approached Moscow we noticed officers in dashing uniforms; long-bearded merchants; Tartars or Armenians; Russian peasants in long caftans and long hair; women with bright-colored handkerchiefs tied at the back of their heads; a little better class sparkling with chains, bracelets, and all sorts of showy jewelry. No one hastens, for there are three warning bells, and no confusion prevails.
