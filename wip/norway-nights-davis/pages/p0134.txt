“Give yourselves no anxiety, ladies,” said this most gracious of ship-masters; “if there is no other cabin, I will give you mine.”

We assured him we would gladly indemnify him for the sacrifice,—his cabin was the largest and best on the steamer,—but he would not listen to this proposal, and said he had given it away many a time. Happily the necessity was avoided, as no new passengers appeared at the last moment. I will say <i>en passant</i> that it is best to secure tickets two or three weeks, at least, in advance from the main office at Bergen; not knowing this, we had bought ours at Christiania when all the best cabins assigned to that office had already been disposed of.
