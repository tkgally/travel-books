One day two great eagles flew near the <i>Capella</i>, quite formidable enough to justify the stories of their audacity in attacking oxen and other large animals: by the device of dipping their wings in the waves, then in the sand, they blind their prey by flapping against the eyes, when it loses in pain the power of resistance. The lemming, a rodent larger than the water-rat, is a dreaded devastator of wheat-fields, and, notwithstanding the agency of owls and hawks in destroying them, they were formerly made the subject of solemn exorcism by a <i>Lemming Litany</i> in the churches.
