It was the hour for morning Mass in the principal chapel, and we obtained places near the choir, which was composed entirely of nuns. Their voices had neither sweetness nor power, and they were very unattractive women, sallow, dark, and of ascetic pallor. Perhaps their hideous veils were partly responsible; few faces could stand the test of thick black serge, standing high and rigid on the head like an iron crown, and falling in long folds over a dress of the same funereal character.
