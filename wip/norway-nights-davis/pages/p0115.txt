Emerging from the Geiranger, the boat crossed to Hellesylt, the village where we intended to pass the night. We made our way up a rough, stony path to as primitive an inn as can well be seen, to find it so crowded (“crowd” implying about two dozen people) that we gladly returned to the steamer, which the captain had previously warned us would be more comfortable. He received us with a friendly laugh and with Spanish hospitality: “I <i>thought</i> you would come back, ladies!
