We received ready admission into one of their <i>Gammer</i>, or huts, through an inconveniently low entrance, which was closed with a reindeer-skin. It was not more than eight feet in diameter, built of birch-bark supported on saplings, and filled in with earth; the walls inside hung with deer-skins, wooden bowls, cheese, dried meat, guns; and in one corner was a chest, probably for holiday clothing.
