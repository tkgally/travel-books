In one corner is a camp bedstead, on a table a few modest toilet appurtenances, a hand-mirror in a green morocco frame, brushes and comb of the simplest sort, an ordinary pocket-handkerchief, a worn and faded uniform. Very unostentatious also are the living-rooms in a small palace built for him in his youth, but occasionally occupied by the present imperial family.
