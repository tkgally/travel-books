Then followed compliments to the “orator of the day,” the ship, and the officers; and when we all went on deck to sip our <i>café noir</i>, and to watch the waning and waxing of our last nocturne in northern waters, a final resolution was proposed, namely, “Never, never to sail on any ocean but the Arctic, or in any ship but the <i>Capella</i>, Iveson, master.”

Our tour ended practically when we landed at Trondhjem; and it was not without a tinge of regret that we obeyed the imperious mandate of the locomotive, and found ourselves, after one night’s ride through charming scenery, at Christiania, <i>en route</i> for Sweden.
