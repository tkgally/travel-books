One of the sitting-rooms in this ancient house was dazzling to behold: a bright blue dado half-way up, and the remainder, together with the rafters, bright pale-yellow! The pantries and closets were exhibited with housewifely pride, as well as an enormous key with most complicated wards which hung in the entrance-hall, souvenir of a past habitation. The boys of ten and twelve years who drove us to the next station were sons and heirs to this primitive wealth—frank, good-humored little chaps who amused themselves and us by repeating a few English words which seemed to them intensely funny. Their laughter was contagious, and we arrived in merry mood at 11 o'clock at Dombaas, our station for the night.
