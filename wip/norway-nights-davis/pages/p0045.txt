Modern collectors have swept away the greater part of these interesting relics; in a short time there will be scarcely an old spoon left outside of museums. Hitherto the peasants emigrating to America have often sold their silver heirlooms; but they never part with them if they can help it. Among these <i>curios</i> is one group that brings a shudder—the girdle and knives used in the duel which prevailed among the lower classes until about sixty years ago. The combatants began by driving their knives into a piece of wood; that portion of each blade not buried in the wood was bound around with strips of leather, leaving for use only the part which the wielder had been able to stab into the wood.
