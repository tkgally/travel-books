# Chapter X.

[[subtitle]] Sweden.

[[subtitle]] (<i>Par Parenthèse.</i>)

S<sc>weden</sc> is to most travellers a parenthesis between Norway and Russia, comprised of Stockholm and the Dalsland and Götha canals. The latter form so pleasing a feature that it is certainly unwise to fly by rail to the capital without allowing an added thirty hours for the sake of seeing these celebrated water-routes which are justly the pride of the country.
