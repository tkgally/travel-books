The room of Russian pictures is very interesting, because national in design, and novel in subject; the artists receive much encouragement from the government, and when they indicate talent are sent off with pensions to study in Paris and Rome. It was unfortunate for us that several private galleries were closed for the summer, especially in Moscow, and we thus missed seeing some of the most celebrated Russian creations.

The numismatic collection, which is extremely rich and valuable, contains rare coins from Greece and from all the ancient provinces of Russia, many of them earlier than the period of dies, being merely bits of metal chopped from the mass.
