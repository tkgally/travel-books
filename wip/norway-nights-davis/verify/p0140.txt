Next appears on our fast-changing panorama the vast glacier of the Svartis, hundreds of square miles in area, which covers a plateau four thousand feet high and sends several of its jagged spurs quite down to the sea.

Soon after the captain summons us with the electric words, “Now, ladies and gentlemen, we are entering the Arctic Circle;” and, executing a gay little <i>pas-seul</i> on the centre of the deck, he stretches a rope firmly across to represent the line that divides us from the zone we leave behind. “You must be on duty till two o’clock,” he adds; “no one is allowed to go to bed: there is too much to see; and coffee will be served on deck at twelve.” For the first time we were far enough northward to see the sun at that mystic hour.
