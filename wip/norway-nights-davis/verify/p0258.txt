There is a little cottage of two rooms on the Neva which Peter occupied before the erection of the house alluded to above. The small room where he ate and slept is now a chapel, completely lined with pictures of saints in gilded shrines, the most important one being a repulsive image of the Saviour, which accompanied Peter wherever he went.
