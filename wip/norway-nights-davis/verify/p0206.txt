# Chapter XI.

[[subtitle]] Finland.

[[subtitle]] (<i>An Episode.</i>)

A<sc>s</sc> we steamed, about 6 o’clock <sc>p.m.</sc>, out of the harbor where sits on her throne of islands the Queen of the Baltic, the fair city presented a lovely picture: spires, roofs, and the conspicuous royal palace gleamed in gold; white suburban villas made points of light in wooded headlands; broad masses of trees formed contrasting shadows; half-furled sails and tall masts sentinelled the shining waters. Floating onwards, the glittering details gradually concentrated into a point of unity which soon was lost to sight on the misty horizon.
