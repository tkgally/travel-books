Another writer speaks of the abundance and variety of mosses and lichens clothing the rocks with rich colors, and adds that after one passes the crest of the Fjeld and descends to the station of Kongsvold one finds it filled with Scandinavian botanists, who are attracted by the great variety of Alpine flora. He says: “It is comical to see detachments of professors and students sallying forth in the early morning with their gayly-colored tin cases slung around them. In the afternoon they return laden with floral treasures which they spread before the house on masses of blotting-paper, kept from flying away by large stones.
