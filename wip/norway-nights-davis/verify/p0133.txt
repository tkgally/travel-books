# Chapter VI.

[[subtitle]] The Arctic Ocean.

T<sc>he</sc> first tourist steamer of the season had sailed June 21st; the <i>Capella</i>, of three days later, considered even better, lay in the harbor awaiting its freight of forty passengers. It had good cabins, a good <i>cuisine</i>, and excellent officers. Captain Iveson, a handsome, comfortable-looking man, won our hearts by his first act of courtesy. The only two cabins which we were able to secure two weeks previously proved uncomfortably small for a party of three, and with dismay in our hearts we applied to him for another.
