The <i>quantum sufficit</i> in Scandinavia is somewhat less than elsewhere on the Continent, and is indicated in a general way in the guide-books. Mr. Bennett is especially serviceable to ladies travelling without a courier, and they can very well dispense with one on ordinary routes if they will take the trouble to learn a little Norsk. A few lessons only are necessary for pronunciation, and Bennett’s Phrase-book is more helpful than those books generally are, being more ingeniously arranged. If a guide is preferred, a man in Christiania named Aak may be highly recommended.
