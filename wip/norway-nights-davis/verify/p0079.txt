On and on runs the exhilarating river until it pauses to sleep in a small lake at Bredevaugen, but wakens again when reinforced by two torrents which are utilized to turn several saw-mills. These ordinarily uncouth structures do not mar the scenery in Norway, because the roofs are covered with green turf, and the pine logs lying about are harmonious with gray and yellow lichens.
