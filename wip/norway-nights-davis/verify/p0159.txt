# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] Hammerfest.

I<sc>t</sc> would be wearisome to my readers to trace too accurately our course around the countless islands, fiords, promontories, and cliffs; nor would I trench on the provinces of the guide-books. Now on the right, now on the left, the rapid variety appealed to our attention, and gave no time for reading or for the diversions of an ordinary sea-voyage. Sleep seemed almost culpable during that single day of one hundred and eighty hours, from the midnight of our leaving Trondhjem to the noon of our return; watches were the relics of a past and fettered existence, “Good-night” an irony.
