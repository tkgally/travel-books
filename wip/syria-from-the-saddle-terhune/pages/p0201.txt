THE tents had become soaked through during our rainy night at Jenin, and by some accident—probably a misunderstanding between the canteen-mule and his burden—the train was some hours behind us. Night had already fallen, so David sent our horses on to the khan under Serkeese’s charge, and took me to the house of the Protestant missionary at Nablous.
