Whatever distortions they have met, or will meet, have been and always will be at the hands of Christians.

“Night is the only time to see these things properly,” said Mr. Wallace as we started for home. “In the daytime one is confronted with such a maze of facts, traditions, present realities, and lepers, that every sacred association is lost, and we look on it all as on some ordinary scene from history.”
