Riding on, we came to the ancient site of Samaria, three-quarters of the way up a hill. At the top of this hill once stood Ahab's ivory palace, and in the plain beneath the Syrian army had encamped when Samaria was besieged. Down the road by which we climbed the hill, had crept the four lepers who, starved in Samaria, had resolved to throw themselves on the mercy of their enemies. Here and there on the hill-side I saw a bit of pillar or a broken sarcophagus
