John and his cooking outfit took possession of the kitchen and prepared my meals as usual. There were no other guests at the time, so I had the entire hotel, fully six rooms, at my disposal; and, beyond the ordinary Eastern modicum of fleas and mosquitoes, I was made quite comfortable.
