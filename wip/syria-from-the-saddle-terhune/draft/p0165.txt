# Chapter XVII.

[[subtitle]] Story of a Lordling's Scrape—Bedouin Bandit—Cana in Galilee.

[[image: Decorated initial letter H beside a sketch of a rider on a camel among rocks and bushes]]

“He was the son of an English lord,” began David, “and was making a tour of Palestine after he left Oxford. When we came to the Sea of Galilee, he saw the wild ducks and wanted to shoot some. I hired a man from Tiberias to row him, and they started after a flock of ducks that was swimming about half a mile from shore. The Englishman had a double-barreled gun loaded with heavy shot;—double B, I think the size was. Some ducks rose in front of the boat, and the Englishman called to his rower to stoop down while he fired at them over his head. The man did as he was told; but after he heard the gun go off he raised his head again, forgetting there was a second barrel. Just as he rose, the second barrel was fired and half of the poor fellow's head was blown off. I saw him afterward. He was not good to look at. The Englishman went almost crazy.
