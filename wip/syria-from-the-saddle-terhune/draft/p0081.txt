I felt my insignificance by comparison as I followed David’s glorified form down the stairway and into the street, where a crowd of loungers was gathered to see us off. The horses awaited us, saddled, the light of coming battle shining in Massoud’s big eyes. Ayoub and the hotel proprietor wished me <i>bon voyage</i>, then, turning to David, kissed him on both cheeks, the regulation Oriental greeting of farewell between friends. I was sorry to leave the Hotel Dimitri, for they had given me excellent fare and service. Such accommodations are not to be despised in a half-barbarous land.
