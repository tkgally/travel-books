# Chapter XVI.
[[subtitle]] Capernaum—Tiberias.

[[image: drawing of a man in a head-cloth seated on a horse]]

DAVID gave orders in the morning for our camp to be struck and carried ahead to the other side of Tiberias, while he and I should ride to Capernaum and come back by a shorter route. Capernaum lies a few miles to the north, and to reach it we skirted the shore, passing a stream and a ruined Roman aqueduct on the way. A modern house stands a quarter-mile from Bethsaida, in which lives a priest who built it that he might spend his last days in Galilee.
