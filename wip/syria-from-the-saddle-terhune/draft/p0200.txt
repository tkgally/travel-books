[[cont]] blue ribbon of the Mediterranean. A hazy light from the setting sun filled all the valley as we rode into it. A stone bridge spanned the brook, and green banks ran down on either side to the very edge of the water. Bridge, brook, and grass combined to form a picture that looked like a section of Devonshire scenery. Crossing the brook and riding along the highroad for a mile, we reached the town of Nablous just as darkness closed in.
