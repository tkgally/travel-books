# Chapter VIII.
[[subtitle]] Across Country—Camp Life.

[[image: Decorated initial W with a man kneeling at a fire before a tent]]

WE started on a road that ran along the Abana, and traveled southwest.

This afternoon’s ride was the last experience we were to have with regular highways for several weeks. I did not know this, or I should not have grumbled so much at the occasional hollows and boulders that blocked our passage. For, compared to the paths and the so-called roads we were to traverse before reaching Jerusalem, this was a veritable <i>descensus Averni.</i>

Plans have been made for building a regular carriage road from Damascus to Jerusalem; but at present, like another much-talked-of thoroughfare, it is paved only with good intentions.
