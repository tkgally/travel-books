A little before sunset we neared the village of Katana, where Paul is believed to have seen the great Light. This location is, like Nimrod’s grave, traditional, but as Katana has always been the last stopping-place between Jerusalem and Damascus, and is on the Roman route between the two cities, there may be some truth in the story.
