# IV.
[[subtitle]] Bay of Rio

We were up by break of day to see the entrance to the bay of Rio. The scene that burst upon the view was grand, solemn and imposing. A chain of wild, dark-coloured mountains formed the coast line; ahead of us the land receded, discovering, as we approached, two rocky islets, one of them crowned by a light house. Presently we began to descry houses perched here and there among the crescent of hills. Even the rays of the rising sun could not light up the sombre outlines of the towering summits of Gabia, Tijuca, Corcovado and Sugar Loaf. It was a panorama that seen for the first time seemed as though Nature could never frame anything more wayward or picturesque.
