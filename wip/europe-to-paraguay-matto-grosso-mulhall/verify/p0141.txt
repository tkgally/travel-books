# XX.

[[subtitle]] Return to Buenos Ayres

Very early in the morning we hurried to the “Jauru” steamer, which carried us only too soon out of sight of Asuncion, en route for flat and uninteresting Buenos Ayres. Again skirting the Gran Chaco, even closer than before. This enormous tract of country covers 200,000 square miles, and extends from the frontier of Bolivia to the province of Santa Fé, along the western bank of the rivers Paraguay and Paraná. It is peopled by wandering tribes of Indians, the principal of which, besides those I have already mentioned, are the Mocovis, Abipones, Tobas, Ocolas and Matacos.
