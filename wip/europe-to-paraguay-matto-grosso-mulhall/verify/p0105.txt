Before again starting my husband warned me of the difficulties we should encounter, and gave me my choice to return or to proceed. We were both too much interested in our journey to choose the former, and having left our heavy luggage at Curumbá embarked for Cuyaba. On the mole alongside the launch, we saw some men skinning a huge alligator; just as they had half completed the process the creature plunged into the water; he must have fallen 10 feet. I should like to have seen how his friends received him.
