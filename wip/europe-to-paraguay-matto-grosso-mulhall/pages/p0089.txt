# XIV.
[[subtitle]] Excursion to Paraguari

We made an excursion to the interior of Paraguay, to the little village of Paraguari, formerly one of the Jesuit Missions. We left Asuncion by train at 8 a.m. and our first halt was at Trinidad, where there is a handsome church in which are interred the bones of Carlos Antonio Lopez, commonly known as Lopez I. Here we had to obtain water for the engine, and half-a-dozen-boys were occupied for half-an-hour in filling kerosene tins and conveying the water from an adjacent brook. This railway has completely fallen to decay; it was constructed in the most workmanlike manner by the English engineers in the time of Lopez; at present it has to stop every few miles to cut timber for fuel or procure water from a rivulet.
