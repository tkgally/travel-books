# Chapter XI.

[[subtitle]] The Missionary Outlook.

A <sc>few</sc> years ago, in journeying along the highways of Japan, the traveller would see at the entrance of every village and near the cross-roads a wooden edict-board hanging where every passer-by might read it, upon which was written in large characters, “The evil sect called Christian is strictly prohibited.” This law No. 3 was suspended by the side of other laws against stealing, murder, and insurrection, and, like them, was formerly punishable with imprisonment and even death.
