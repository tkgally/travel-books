The men have their heads shaved at the top, in the old-fashioned way. The Samourai have the family crests upon their clothing.

Class distinctions are slowly breaking down in Japan with the incoming of western civilization. The Samourai no longer monopolizes the military power, for the government have called the common people to be soldiers, and the proud Samourai have been forced to labor honestly with their own hands.

It was my good fortune to witness a mock battle in the presence of the Mikado, showing the skill and discipline of the sturdy soldiers who now compose the new army. The battle took place in the suburbs of Tokio, between fourteen thousand Japanese troops. It commemorated a peaceful victory in diplomacy, which Japan had recently gained over China, in the adjustment of the Formosa question, which had long threatened war.
