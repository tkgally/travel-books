# Chapter X.
[[subtitle]] A Trip to Kioto.

K<sc>ioto</sc> is the “sacred city” of Japan. Until a few years ago it was considered the spiritual capital, where his Mysteriousness the Mikado resided, whose august person was solemnly veiled from even the gaze of his own subjects. The idea of a foreigner from the outside world ever gaining admittance to the sacred city would have horrified the good Japanese of the olden time; nevertheless, wonderful things are happening in our day, and changes have come to pass which would have paralyzed the ancient court; so that I really went to Kioto and sojourned among its most sacred temples as comfortably as though I were rusticating on the beautiful banks of the Hudson.
