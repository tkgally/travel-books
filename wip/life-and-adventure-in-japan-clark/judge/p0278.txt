This weather-beaten board travelled eight thousand miles, by itself, and is perhaps the only one which ever left the country. After it had gone the local governor who had given it to me tried to get it back again; but I replied that Japan had no further use for the law, and that I had sent it to America for safe preservation!

The missionary work, which was slow and difficult at the outset, has received a new impulse within the past few years, and much good has been accomplished at the five open ports, and in some districts of the interior which Christian men have been able to reach. As the provinces have not yet been freely opened to foreign intercourse, however, most of the missionary interests still centre about Yokohama, and here many of the missionaries reside.
