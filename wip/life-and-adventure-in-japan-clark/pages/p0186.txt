[[cont]]rules and regulations governing the students were very strict.

In the picture of the gateway the name “Kaisei Gakko” is seen on the right pillar, in Chinese characters; my friend Dr. Veeder, the professor of physics, is standing in the centre of the picture with a few of his students around him; some of them are in regulation dress, and others in native costume.

The other view which is given of the Kaisei Gakko presents the front of the building as it appeared the day that the Mikado visited and opened the institution.
