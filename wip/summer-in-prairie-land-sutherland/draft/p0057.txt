In the course of time other reservations will be set apart for the Bloods and Sarcees, but the two above mentioned are already located. In the meantime, McLeod is a good point from which to do preliminary work, as many Indians are almost constantly to be found around the post; but as soon as they begin to settle on their Reserves, the missionary must locate himself among them. The Blackfoot Nation comprises the Blackfeet proper, the Bloods, Piegans and Sarcees, all speaking dialects of the same tongue, and readily understanding one another.

In the evening we prepared to bid good-bye to Fort McLeod. Bro. and Sister McLean accompanied us to the landing place, and watched us as we crossed the stream. Those who travel together over these uninhabited plains, sharing mutual toils and dangers, form strong friendships, and there was a feeling of deep sadness in my heart at this first break in our party. I could not but think of the isolation, the discouragements, the trials in store for those we were leaving to grapple single-handed with the darkness and ignorance and immorality of surrounding heathenism; and as I saw Bro.
