# XIII.
[[subtitle]] Boating Down the Saskatchewan.

ON Monday, August 23rd, we prepared to bid farewell to our friends at Victoria. It took some time to get our effects carried down the bank to the river, and stowed away in the boat. As McDougall must now leave us to return to his work at Morely, and the rest of us knew nothing of the route toward Winnipeg, a guide became indispensable. After some enquiry I engaged an old H. B. freighter and steersman—Sam Favel, to wit—who proved a valuable help on the journey. Having several children at Red River, he was the more willing to go, and for his board, and a moderate compensation, agreed to make the through trip. At a quarter-past two everything was in readiness, and our friends came down to the bank to see us off. Our party had been steadily thinning out at various points, and now we were to part from Bro. McDougall, upon whom we had relied in every emergency
