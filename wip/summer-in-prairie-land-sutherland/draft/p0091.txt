He was about to christen one baby Enoch, but discovered just in time that it wasn’t that kind of a baby, and that its parents desired to call it Flora; so Flora it was baptized. These varied services had well filled up a busy day, and we returned to the mission house somewhat wearied in body, but thankful for the experience of a Sunday at Morley.
