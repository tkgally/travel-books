The Mission premises at Whitefish Lake consist of the house in which the Missionary resides, and a building used for a school-house and church. The latter building had become rather dilapidated, and two or three years ago the Indians made preparations to build a new church. The walls of a building were erected, but they have been unable to finish it, and help from some quarter will be indispensable. The buildings occupy a site on a rise of ground near the south end of the lake, some three or four hundred yards from the shore. The lake itself is about twelve miles long by four miles wide, and contains an abundant supply of whitefish of superior quality. Several small fields adjoining the Mission are well cultivated, and the garden gave very satisfactory evidence of the fertility of the soil. About half of the latter enclosure was sown with grain, which at the time of my visit was heading out.
