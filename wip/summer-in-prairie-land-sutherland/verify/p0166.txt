# XV.
[[subtitle]] Prince Albert to Touchwood Hills

THE morning of Monday, September 6th, was bright and beautiful. We were up betimes to make arrangements for a start; but, as usual, a great many “last things” had to be done, so it was half-past nine before we were ready for the road. The river journey was ended, and we must again take to the plains; and as one team could not transport both passengers and baggage, some additional outfit had to be procured. After some enquiry I purchased, from a member of the police force, a native “bichon” pony, for $55. In Mr. Deacon’s yard stood a new cart, which a young man had brought when coming to the country and now wanted to sell. We soon came to terms at a moderate figure. A set of cart harness cost me some $7 more, and this completed the necessary outfit.
