# Chapter XVII.

[[subtitle]] Spiritual Relations—Carrizal—Vuelta Grande—The River Motagna—Hot Springs—Llano Grande—A Sugar Plantation—Mountains of Choacus—An Invalid Carriage—San Geronimo—Salama—Flowering Yuccas—Mountains of Quilila—Santa Rosa—Valley of Purula—Cabildo.

A ROAD is such a familiar object that the effect it gives to a landscape is often overlooked, but I think that just as a castle on a hill or a cottage on the hill-side enhances the natural beauty of a scene, so does a road render more attractive the country through which it winds.
