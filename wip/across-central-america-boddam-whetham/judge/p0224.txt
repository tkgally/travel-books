Descending by a deep cutting in the wooded hills, we crossed a brawling stream and ascended by a paved road to the green table-land. Orange, mango, coffee, sapote trees and an abundance of foliage adorned the gardens in the outskirts of the town; passing through these we wended our way through the district of Candelaria, leaving the hill of Carmen on our right and in a very short time drew rein in the patio of the Gran Hotel.

That evening instead of eggs and tortillas, I indulged in a repast which an elegant Californian mining phrase would describe as “a hunky old spread, with all the express French frills.”
