Yet in looking them over, it has been rather urgently suggested by kind and perhaps partial friends that they contain many things that would interest Sabbath-school children, and might serve to give some minds the same impulses that so early stirred my own.

With the consent of the little folks to whom they were written,—the most of them my grandchildren,—I have gathered up some of those letters, and in precisely the simple and familiar style in which they were written to my own dear little ones, I send them out to the children of our Sabbath schools and Christian families.

When I was quite a little child I was taken to New York, and while there accompanied my brother to a great Fair of the American Institute, held in what was then a very fashionable place, called Niblo’s Garden.
