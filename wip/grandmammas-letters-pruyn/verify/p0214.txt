We had the funeral in the school-house, and there were more than a hundred people and children present. Our school-children sang “Around the throne of God in heaven,” and after the address and prayer, the whole congregation joined in singing in Japanese, “Nearer, my God, to Thee.”

It is so pleasant to feel that by all these circumstances our dear children are being taught some good lessons. I think little Kittie’s death will be the means by which a good many will learn of a better life in heaven, and that we need not be afraid to die, if we only love and trust Jesus.
