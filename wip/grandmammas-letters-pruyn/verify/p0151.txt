I have a very sweet little story for you to-day, and I know you will be glad to read it, for it shows you how God is making this a real Christian home for the dear children who come to it.

One dear little girl in our “Home,” who united with the foreign church, has been developing quickly and sweetly in her Christian character. For a long time I have been in the habit of giving the children a short text to learn each day, and repeat at the breakfast-table. Minnie sleeps in a room adjoining mine, and always hears the little ones say their morning prayers. I used to hear them talking and repeating their verses, and did not notice anything special.
