There is a platform at one end, and on it a table with a Bible, hymn-book, and bell, and also a couple of large straw chairs. By the side of the platform stands a small organ, that would seem a very poor one, I suppose, in Albany, but it serves our purpose very well till we can get a better one.

About forty come together now, but the number increases so fast that, by the time you read this, there may be a great many more. Some are quite small, and others quite grown-up persons.
