I am going to tell you something more about the children in Japan, for I want you to know all you can of them, and I think you will like it as well as anything I can write. I do believe there are more children here, for the size of the country and the number of grown-up people, than there are anywhere else in the world.

It is very fortunate for those who live here that they are so good-natured and quiet, for it would be dreadful if they were as noisy and quarrelsome as some children I have seen. Some persons say they are so good because their parents allow them to do just as they choose.
