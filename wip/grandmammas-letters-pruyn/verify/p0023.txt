# Grandmamma’s Letters from Japan.

# I.

[[right]] T<sc>welve</sc> H<sc>undred</sc> M<sc>iles at</sc> S<sc>ea</sc>, June 6, 1871.

T<sc>o my dear</sc> L<sc>ittle</sc> F<sc>olks at</sc> H<sc>ome</sc>,—M<sc>ary</sc>, B<sc>ertie, and</sc> K<sc>ittie</sc>:

I just wish you could look at me as I sit here in this beautiful cabin of the good ship Japan, and feel with me the pure, fresh sea-air, as it blows so cool and delightful over me; or if you could go with me out on the deck, and look over the great Pacific Ocean, on which we are sailing, how you would wonder!
