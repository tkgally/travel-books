# XIII.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, N<sc>ov</sc>. 10, 1872.

M<sc>y dear</sc> L<sc>ittle</sc> F<sc>olks at</sc> H<sc>ome</sc>:

I suppose you have heard, through my letters to the big people, all about our moving into a nice, large house, and how happy we all feel, because now we can take more into our family, and be so much more comfortable: so I will not tell you anything more about that. But there is something in all our gladness that has made us feel very bad; it was one of the hardest things for me that has happened since I came to Japan. I know, you too will feel sorry when I tell you we have sent away from our home all the little boys who were with us.
