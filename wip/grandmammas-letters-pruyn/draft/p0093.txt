Do you not think we ought to try and teach them better, and help them learn about our dear Father in heaven, and our kind, loving Saviour, who went about when He was in this world, healing all kinds of disease and sickness; and that He is just the same now, able and willing to do all that they desire, if they will only believe in Him, and pray to Him, instead of praying to their hideous wooden idols.

Another thing which makes me so sorry, and of which I believe I have told you before—<i>they never sing.</i>
