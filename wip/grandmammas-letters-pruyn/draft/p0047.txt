But I must tell you something of the dear little children who live in our home with us. We have five; and although they knew nothing of the dear Saviour, and had never been taught to pray, or sing His praise, yet I am sure it would delight your hearts, as it does mine, to hear them now. I think you will feel as I do, that we have cause to praise God, that in less than three months these dear children have learned to sing, “There is a happy land,” “Jesus loves me;” can join in asking a blessing at the table, and repeat every morning and evening a little prayer we wrote for them, besides trying to say, “Our Father who art in heaven.”
