O, it makes me so sorry, and I would so love to tell them of our dear Father in heaven, who has given us the Sabbath for our happiness! but they speak such a strange language, I cannot talk with them, or make them understand anything about it. Some good men are working very hard to put the Bible into the Japanese language, and when that is done, they can read of Jesus, and what God, the true God, would have them do.

There are so many little children here, and, O, I do pray that before they grow up, the Bible will be all ready for them, for I want them to become Christian men and women, and not idolaters as their parents are!
