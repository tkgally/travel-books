I want now to tell you a little story about one of our dear girls—the little Annie of whom I have so often written.

I went to the breakfast-table one morning, as I generally do, to wait upon the children, and hear their Bible verses, which they always repeat at that time, when I found that Annie was not there. I asked why she was absent, and was told she was sick; so I went immediately to her room, but found, upon questioning her, that she only had a little stomach-ache, and was staying in bed, more because she felt (as little folks often do, you know) a little lazy and sleepy, and did not want to get up so early.
