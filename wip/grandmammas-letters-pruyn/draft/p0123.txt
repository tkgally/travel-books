Yet we try to be patient. We always feel so thankful, after it is over, that no person has been hurt; and it gives us occasion to prove our trust in God. David said in one of his sweet psalms, “Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea;” and it is always very sweet for me to repeat those words, and to feel that God is <i>our</i> refuge.

One night we were all quietly sleeping, when there came one of the severest shocks I have ever felt. Our children are so used to them that they do not often get frightened; but it was very startling to be fairly shaken out of a sound sleep, and some of them began to scream and run wildly about; this frightened others, and soon there was a terrible commotion.
