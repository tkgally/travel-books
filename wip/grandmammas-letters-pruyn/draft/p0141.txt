The men who performed on the instruments showed considerable skill, and it was a great gratification to me to see their ancient and curious mode of performance.

I was more interested, however, in the part of the entertainment which showed how the ladies of the higher classes in Japan are educated, and what their ideas of musical performances are.

There were five ladies; one the wife of one of Mr. C——’s interpreters, a bright, sprightly little thing, not more than fifteen or sixteen years old, and the others all plain, middle-aged women.
