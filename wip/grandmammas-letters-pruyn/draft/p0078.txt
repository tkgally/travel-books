[[cont]]so easily the thoughts that are in my heart. Do you ever thank God for your little, common, every-day comforts? Do you not think it pleases Him when we try to see His love and tender care for us in all the good things we have, whether they are great or small?

But I am going to tell you now about a great religious festival that is going on among the Chinese and Japanese here. There are a good many Chinese in this place, and it seems this festival is kept by the people of this country and those of China at the same time, although their religion is not exactly the same.
