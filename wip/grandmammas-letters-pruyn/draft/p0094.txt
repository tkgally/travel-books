You know that we ladies in this house, and all the missionaries indeed, want to teach them these precious truths that make our own dear little folks at home so much wiser and happier than they are. Do you not think this is right? And will you not do all you can to help us?

A little black-and-white messenger came all the way across the big sea, and told me that Bertie had said, “Why can’t grandma tell the heathen children about Jesus without my pennies?” Is that so, Bertie? Did you say just that? Well, so I could tell them; only you must remember they speak a different language from the one we use, and they could not understand me if I tried ever so hard to talk with them.
