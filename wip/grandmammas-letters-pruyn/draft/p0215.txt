Are you not glad that there is a Home where these poor heathen people and children can hear the “good news”?

And now I want to tell you and Mary, and Bertie too, for I think he worked hard for the Fair, as well as the girls, that the money you sent me I used to buy two little bedsteads, and the beds and pillows for them.

I wish I could draw a picture of the room in which they stand. There were four little beds in it, but we needed more very badly, so now we have them, six in all, and two little girls sleep in each one. They stand in a square, right in the middle of the room, three on one side, and three on the other, with the heads together. This seems a queer way, perhaps, to you, but it gives better air, we think, to all the little sleepers, and it is easier to get around the room.
