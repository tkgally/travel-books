Yet I think that I must leave that also, and in some future letter give you some account of the fruits of this Christian life, particularly of the work some of our Christian girls are doing in visiting, reading the Bible, and praying among their people.

Just now I must tell you something that has pleased us all very much, concerning a little girl supported by a kind lady in America. I am sure you will all be especially glad to hear that O’Sono is such a bright and good girl, and that we have the greatest pleasure in witnessing her rapid improvement, and her quick, bright, and happy manners.
