The Lake of Hakone is like a diamond with the richest emerald setting. A lovely sheet of water, with grand old mountains rising all around it, covered with thick, rich grass and evergreens.

There are many places up there that I would like to tell you about. The fine old temples away up on the sides of the mountains; the immense stone images along the shores of the lake; the wild and desolate region called O’Jin-Oku, or the “Great Hell,” because it is literally a place of “fire and brimstone,” the smoke coming out in small jets all over the place, and the boiling-hot sulphur water pouring out of the rocks in many places, while the noise made by the roaring of the internal fires is equal to a steam-engine.
