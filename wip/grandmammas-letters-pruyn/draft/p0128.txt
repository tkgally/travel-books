Around the flag-pole we had beautiful twigs and bushes twined for some distance from the ground, and then some pretty little evergreens set out all about it, so that it looked as if it stood in a miniature forest.

Then we set a long table at one side, and on it we had all the flowers, vines, and wreaths that we could get or contrive. We had bread, with nice jam spread on it (the Japanese never eat butter), crackers, cakes of many kinds, oranges, nuts, and a good many varieties of candy, which the native bakers make.
