They had brought there several large baskets, very much like the crates crockery comes packed in. On the ground, in various places, they spread large straw mats. Then they put up in rows, all around the grounds, little sticks about a foot and a half high, which I think was <i>punk</i>, such as you light fire-crackers with, for they lighted them all, and they burned very slowly for many hours; or they might have been incense sticks, for they placed one at the head and one at the foot of every grave.
