You know the Japanese, like all heathen nations, are without the Bible, which gives the only true account of the creation of this world; and they must get their knowledge from tradition, or information handed down from one to another. This kind of information is not very reliable, for it is so easy for different persons to change things as they repeat them.

How much more safely we can depend on our account of the beginning of the world, for you know “holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost,” and “all Scripture was given by inspiration of God,” and so we are sure there is no mistake or change in our history.
