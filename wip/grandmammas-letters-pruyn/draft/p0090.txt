The little children are all carried on the back, instead of in the arms, and so the larger children are made to carry the little babies strapped on their backs, as the easiest and safest way for them to take care of them; and as they come towards you, you can see only the head of the baby over the shoulder of the other child, and thus it seems as if it had two heads; and I have a good many times, until I became used to it, been quite startled by seeing such an odd sight.
