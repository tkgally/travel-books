And it is the most touching thing to me sometimes, when I am praying, to feel her little hand working itself into mine, or softly laid against my cheek.

And this little creature, so apt, so affectionate, was, five months ago, a poor, neglected little waif, floating about among the most degraded heathen. Do you not love her, my dear little Kittie, and are you not glad she has now such a pleasant home, and some one to tell her of Jesus and his love?
