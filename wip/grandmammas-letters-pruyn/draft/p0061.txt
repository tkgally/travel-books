But this is a very long letter, and now I must close. Only I want to tell you that nothing pleases me so much as to hear that you pray for me every day, and that God will make this school the place where many will come to learn the truth, and forsake their foolish customs. I am sure God is already answering your prayers, and that is the reason why we have such a happy home, and so much to praise Him for.
