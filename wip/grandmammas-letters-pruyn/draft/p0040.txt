[[cont]] they have what they call “Matsuris,” or Festival days, and then every one is expected to go to their own temple, give a little money, and say some prayers; besides this, all the temples have a good many priests, who live in them, and are always ready to say prayers for any who come there. So that there is no time when there are not priests and people in the temple. No one is allowed to go in with shoes on their feet, so we had to sit on the outside steps and take ours off.

But my letter is getting so long that I must not tell you any more, except a short story about “Georgie,” one of our little boys. He is a very quiet, gentle little fellow, eight years old.
