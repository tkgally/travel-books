[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, Feb. 26, 1872.

M<sc>y dear little</sc> F<sc>olks at</sc> H<sc>ome</sc>:

As mamma and papa are away off in Georgia, I suppose I must consider <i>you</i> the family now, and send you a very big letter. . . .
