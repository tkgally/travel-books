[[cont]] We always keep a hanging light burning in the hall, and in a moment or two every woman and child was gathered there.

We took the poor little scared creatures in our arms, and, hushing their fears as well as we could, we tried to tell them of the loving care of the great God who can take up in His hand all these thousands of islands “as a very little thing,” and yet never forgets even one of His little helpless children.

How sweet it is to trust in Him, and to <i>know</i> that we are always safe in His hands!...
