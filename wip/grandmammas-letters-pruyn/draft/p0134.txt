Mr. C——’s house is built on one corner of the outermost wall of the moat, and from it I can look in one direction far off over the city; in the other, over the successive moats and intervening grounds, I see those wild and grand old ruins.

But about the temple. We started in our <i>jin-ri-ki-sha;</i> but no sooner were we outside of the wall of the moat, than the people began to see that some strange being was among them; for you must know I was the first foreign lady who had ever been in that city.
