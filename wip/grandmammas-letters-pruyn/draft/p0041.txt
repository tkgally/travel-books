Georgie’s father died, and left him, with his little brother, who is also with us, to the care of their heathen mother. She is very glad to have them here, and seems quite proud to have them learn so readily; but still she wants them to worship her gods, and never loses an opportunity to teach them to do so. A short time ago she came here and brought some of those little pieces of paper of which I have written. She told Georgie he must go to the temple to pray, and use them there.
