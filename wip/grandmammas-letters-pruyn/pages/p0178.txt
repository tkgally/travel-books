God has been so very good to us in this Mission Home, and has given us so soon to see many of our dear children taught to love and pray to Jesus, and it is such a real delight to all the ladies who are living in the house to have these dear young girls and little children right here in our own Home, and day by day to see them so well, so comfortable, so happy, that it always seems as though no one could understand it all, unless they could come here and see for themselves.
