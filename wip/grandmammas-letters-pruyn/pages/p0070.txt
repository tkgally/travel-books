[[cont]]for a while I could not walk, but at last I went on, and waded the rest of the way, and finally got safely to the dry land, where all the rest were standing laughing at me. I learned a good lesson by it, and hereafter I do not mean to set myself up to be wiser and better than every one else, but just do as others do, and make the best of it.

When we came on shore, a crowd of dirty, curious people, including children, gathered about us; and you will not wonder they did so, when I tell you that I was the first white or foreign woman who had ever been in that part of the country.
