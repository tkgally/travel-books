Among these, too, there are piled up, as only the Japanese know how to do, common, rough stones, that are covered with beautiful little vines and plants, growing out of the crevices, filled with earth. Some of these are very curiously arranged, and all look so pretty among the trees and bushes, that I often wish the gardeners in America knew how to use rough stones as the Japanese do.

All this makes a very pleasant place to play, and our children do not need to go away from home for pleasure; but as we like to do all we can to make them happy, we thought we would give them a tea-party out on the lawn, and let them have a good frolic among the trees.

We had a great tall bamboo pole set up in the middle of the grass circle, and by cords and pulleys raised our beautiful flag, with its stars and stripes, to the top
