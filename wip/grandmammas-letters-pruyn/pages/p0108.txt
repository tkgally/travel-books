<verse>
“Jehovah sits upon the clouds,
And governs all the sky;
He rolls the thunder round the globe,
And bids the lightning fly.”
</verse>

Ever since that night I have always known that God is in the storm as well as in the sunshine, and I have often thanked Him that it is so sweet, and makes me feel so safe, to believe He is <i>my</i> Father, and able to take care of me in every place. Do you feel so? And are you glad to know that God is everywhere, and in all things?

And now we will come back to our story. The storm lasted so long that we were obliged to stay up on the mountain all night, for which I was not sorry, though we had neither bedding nor food of our kind there.
