I could only surmise, from what I know of heathen superstitions, that the food was designed to supply the wants of the dead in the other world, and the papers burned were prayers for their happiness. I rather think the whole thing was intended for the benefit of those who are in the other world, and is done every year to prove their affection and reverence for their departed friends.

The ceremony of burning the papers has been repeated three times during the week.
