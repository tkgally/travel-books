I cannot tell how true this is, but it is a little odd to find these people doing exactly as we have always been accustomed to do in this respect, and it is very pleasant and natural for me, I can assure you.

The houses here are so small that the people live about as much in the streets as they do in-doors; and at this time, as you go about the town, everything looks gay and lively. Each one is dressed in his or her best clothes; and, indeed, every one who expects to have them at all during the year, has <i>new</i> ones; for these poor folks do not generally get anything new except at this season.
