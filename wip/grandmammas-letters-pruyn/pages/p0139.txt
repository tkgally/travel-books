They were delighted to see me when they found I knew their son so well; and when I expressed, as mere politeness, my admiration of a beautiful rose-tree, which was growing beside the door, the father ran away, and in a moment came back with a large knife, and before I could imagine what he was going to do, he cut it right off close to the ground, and, with its great clusters of magnificent flowers, presented it to me, with a most profound bow. When I tell you that roses are just beginning to be cultivated in Japan, and the people prize them very highly, you will understand what an expression of politeness and pleasure this was.
