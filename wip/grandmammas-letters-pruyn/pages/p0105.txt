These were something very nice, according to their ideas, for, as wool has never been grown or used in Japan, it is only very lately that they have seen any kind of woollen goods. Some foreign merchants have brought a large number of blankets, of all colors, and the Japanese think them wonderful. Any person who is rich enough to buy some of these is very proud to have them, and so our friend was willing to gratify his pride, and make us comfortable, as he thought, by letting us use his new blankets.

We tried the beds, but we found them very hard, notwithstanding the blankets, and concluded the mats on the floor were easier than the doors.
