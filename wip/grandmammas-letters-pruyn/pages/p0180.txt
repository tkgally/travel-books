I shall put in this letter a picture[[fnref *]] which I want you all to look at carefully, and then you will know just how the outside of our school looks, and you will be better able to fancy all the bright, happy faces that fill it day by day. There are only a few figures in the photograph, for we could not very well get all our family in; but there are enough to show you how these children and young ladies look, and enable you to judge how very good God has been in giving us such a nice school-house and such a pleasant home.
