The missionaries and Christian people here, feel that it is very important to be prepared for this liberty, and hence we desire that there be many more here to study the language.

I must not forget to tell you, that within the last few weeks a prayer-meeting has been commenced in our house, by a few natives who come here on Sabbath evenings, at first to learn to sing; and that now for the first time in the history of this ancient empire, native Japanese pray together, and exhort one another, and that too with native women present.
