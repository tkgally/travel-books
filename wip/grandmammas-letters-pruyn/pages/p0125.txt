# XV.

[[right]] Y<sc>okohama</sc>, April 20, 1873.

D<sc>ear</sc> M<sc>ary</sc>:

I know you will let Bertie and Kittie feel that this letter is partly theirs; but sometimes I like to send one just directed to one of you, so that you can feel more as if I was thinking of, and talking to you all by yourself. And then it gives you the opportunity to be generous, and let others share your happiness.
