Then, the wind blew very hard, and some of us, myself among the number, were so sea-sick that we had to lie right down in the bottom of the boat, among the ropes and boards, and, O, we did feel so bad, we thought we never could get up to go on shore when the boat reached the other side!

All that passed away, however, as soon as the boat became still; but what do you think we found when we stopped sailing? Why, that we were nearly a mile from the land!
