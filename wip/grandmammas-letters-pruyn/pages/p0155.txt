There were many wise and curious looks from the little ones. Many little heads were peering around every time the door of a certain room was opened, and many surmises, if a piece of wrapping-paper or a bundle was seen. You see that little Japanese girls are exactly like our American children in such things, and although they were never obtrusive or selfish, yet they were very curious.
