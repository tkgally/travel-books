I wonder how many little Christian children in America would have acted just as our Annie did. Do you not think <i>you</i> would have been in too great a hurry to get to the breakfast-table, when you knew the rest were almost finished, to stop for praying? Or do you not suppose you would have been startled by a stranger coming in, and would have jumped up off your knees?

I hope dear Annie’s example will help you, my dear children, and that prayer for you, as for her, will be something so sweet, and so sacred, that nothing can hinder you in your devotions.
