They say their emperor is descended directly from the gods, and call him “The Teuno,” which means “The son of heaven;” and they used to think their people were the only ones who were truly wise, and theirs the only country that was beloved and favored by the gods. This was what made them such a proud people, and so unwilling to let foreigners come among them.

The history says, anciently heaven and earth were not separated.
