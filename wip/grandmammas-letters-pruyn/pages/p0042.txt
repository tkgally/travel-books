You cannot think how these little things rejoice my heart. I think they ought to make the friends at home feel that the bread they are casting upon these waters is found without waiting “many days.” And this is some of the “good news” I told you I was going to write. I hope you will thank God for all He is doing; and that He may keep and bless my dear children away off in America, is the constant prayer of your
