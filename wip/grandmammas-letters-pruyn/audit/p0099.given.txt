I think you do; and so I need not explain it to you; but you do not know what a wonderful thing it is to have some of these heathen people meet every day in one place, and listen to the reading of God’s holy word, and then join in His worship. And you do nor know, perhaps, this is the very first time, and the first house in which this has ever been attempted in this country, although it is more than two thousand years old. And this is what makes it so rcmarkable, and gives us such cause for joy.
