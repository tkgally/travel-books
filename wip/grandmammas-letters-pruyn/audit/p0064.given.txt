I wish you could see hear her now, as I often do, when she comes down into the dining-room, early in the morning, and sits there waiting for the other children and the breakfast. She likes to get up early, and is generally the first one to come down stairs. There she will sit in her little chair; so contented and happy, and sing one hymn after another, “Jesus loves me,” “Christ is born the Lord of glory,” “There is a happy land,” and parts of several others; and then when the breakfast is over, she claims a cushion
