The lot on which this house stands, like all the lots on this bluff, is of very irregular shape, and the back part of if runs down a steep, almost perpendicular hill. Adjoining the very lowest part of it is a Chinese burying-ground, and one day they had some very strange ceremonies there. There was no one to explain them to us, and so I cannot tell you what they mean, and it was all so funny that I would have felt like laughing at it, only it made me realize how ignoraut and blind the poor creatures are, and I felt, since it was a part of their superstitious religion, I ought rather to pity them.
