Christmas and New Year’s Day, which we had the good fortune to spend at sea, and in this magnificent steamer, were kept up right merrily; our dining-saloon and the sitting-room were tastefully decorated with the flags of all nationalities and evergreens, and the good old chief steward provided sumptuous dinners on these two occasions; in fact, we had a most charming passage throughout our voyage on the Pacific Ocean, and enjoyed the trip immensely. I cannot forget Christmas and New Year’s Day which we had the good fortune to spend at sea, as I have said above. On the voyage we had three deaths among the steerage passengers, and in accordance with Chinese customs, and with the agreement made by the agents of the steamers, their bodies were not buried at sea as usual, but were embalmed, and packed in cases to be landed at Hong Kong.
