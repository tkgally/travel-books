After having spent two days in Manchester, I took leave of my friends, and left for Liverpool, which place I reached after an hour's run. I drove up to the Quays and Docks, and saw the principal buildings, and left next day, by Pullman Palace Car, for London, <i>viâ</i> Derby, and thus completed my Scotch Tour.
