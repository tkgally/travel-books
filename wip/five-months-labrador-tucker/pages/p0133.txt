It is also a daily occurrence among them to take by force the wives of others whom they may happen to fancy; and all that is necessary to decide the claim, is to vanquish the former husband in wrestling. On these occasions, the by-standers never attempt to interfere; nor will one brother even offer to assist another, except by giving his advice aloud, which being equally heard, may be equally followed by both the parties engaged. When one of them falls, or yields, the other is entitled to carry off the woman, who was the cause of the contention. It is a common custom among them to exchange wives for a time, as one of the strongest ties of friendship between two families; and in case of the death of either husband, the other considers himself bound to support the children of the deceased.
