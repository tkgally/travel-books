An excellent champagne supper was nightly provided “gratis” for all comers, and everything was conducted in the most quiet and orderly manner. Faro was the game at which most money changed hands, though the amount which was staked on the cards was almost nothing in comparison with the enormous sums won and lost at similar establishments in our own country during the race meetings at Newmarket, Windsor, and Brighton.

Though gambling is equally contrary to law in both countries, the hell-keepers appear as free from police interference in the one as in the other.
