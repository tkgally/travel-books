But can she ever hold together when these vast uninhabited regions become thickly populated with various races of Anglo-Saxon, Indian, Negro, and Chinese origin? If she does, then America will indeed become powerful enough to “whip all creation.”

The war, which broke out in 1846, between Mexico and the United States, resulted in the cession of California and New Mexico to the latter in 1848; and when in January of that year gold was first discovered in the American river, near the present city of Sacramento, a tide of immigration from both east and west set in, causing California to be what it now is—one of the most prosperous States in the Union.
