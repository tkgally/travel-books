Farther on we passed the station called Ogden City, which, it has been decided, is to be the terminus or junction of the Union Pacific and the Central Pacific Railways, as well as of the Salt Lake City and Montana roads. Ogden is but forty miles from the Mormon capital, and is the nearest point to which the main line of the Great Inter-Oceanic Railway will pass. Darkness had again closed in before we reached the long steep canôn which leads into the Valley of Salt Lake, and it was not until midnight that we drew up at the Townsend House in the famous city of the Saints.
