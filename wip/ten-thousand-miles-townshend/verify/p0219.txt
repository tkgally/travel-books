Next day we went over some of the principal public buildings, including the court-house and prison, where about a dozen Gentile horse-thieves were confined. An unfortunate lunatic, whose only covering was a blanket thrown round his shoulders, kept moaning “I want to be free, I want to be free.” We were then shown over the cellars belonging to the corporation, where we tasted some of the wine of the country, which was sweet and not unpalatable. Brigham Young encourages the cultivation of the grape, and is now endeavouring to promote the culture of the silk-worm, in order that the Mormons may themselves manufacture silk in course of time. Cotton is grown in Southern Utah, which has water carriage to the Gulf of California by the navigable waters of the Colorado river.
