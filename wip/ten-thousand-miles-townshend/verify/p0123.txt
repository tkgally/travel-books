The fate of those who fell alive into the hands of the enemy was almost too horrible to relate. Some had their entrails pulled out and fastened to a tree, round which they were driven until their whole interior was wound round it. Others were hoisted up on sharp stakes and left to die a lingering death, while many were skinned alive and mutilated.[[fnref *]]
