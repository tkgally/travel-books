# Appendix.

[[subtitle]] The following is a list of the animals hunted and shot by us in America.

The American buffalo or bison (<i>Bos americanus</i>), killed on the plains west of the Missouri.

The elk or wapiti (<i>Cervus canadensis</i>), killed in the Rocky Mountains.

The Virginian deer (<i>Cervus virginianus</i>), killed in the forests of the Adirondack region.

The white-tailed deer (<i>Cervus lucernus</i>), killed on the plains west of Missouri river.

The black-tailed deer (<i>Cervus macrotis</i>), killed on the plains and in Rocky Mountains.

The prong-horn antelope (<i>Antilocapra americana</i>), killed on the plains and in the Rocky Mountains.

The big-horn or mountain sheep (<i>Ovis montana</i>), killed in the Rocky Mountains.

The jackass rabbit or hare (<i>Lepus callotis</i>), killed in the Rocky Mountains.

The sage rabbit (<i>Lepus artemisia</i>), killed in the Rocky Mountains.

The black mink (<i>Putorius nigrescens</i>), killed in the Adirondack region.

The grey wolf (<i>Canis occidentalis</i>), killed on the plains west of the Missouri.

The prairie wolf or coyote (<i>Canis latrans</i>), killed on the plains and in the Rocky Mountains.
