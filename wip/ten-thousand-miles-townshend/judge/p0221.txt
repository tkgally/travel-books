We asked our Heavenly Father to show us the truth, and we fasted and prayed that if God had a true church on earth He would send us a messenger. About this time Parley P. Pratt called on me with a letter of introduction from a merchant of my acquaintance. I thought my friend had imposed upon me a little in sending a man of this persuasion to me. I, however, received him courteously, as I was bound to do. We talked for three hours or upwards, and he bound me as close to the Scriptures as I desired, proving everything he said therefrom. I afterwards wrote down eight sermons that he preached, in order that I might compare them with the word of God. I found nothing contrary. I then examined the book of Mormon and the prophecies concerning it. That was also correct. I read the book of ‘Doctrines and Covenants,’ and found nothing unscriptural there.
