The first thing to be done, after choosing the camping ground, was to find and cut down the trees best suited for making tent poles. Some young pine trees we found best adapted for our purpose, and on the poles with which these supplied us we fixed up our tents, one for ourselves and one for the guides. The branches which we cut off were laid down to form a flooring for our tent, and a waterproof rug laid over this served for our bed. It takes some time to get accustomed to the hardness of a bed on the ground; before we became quite used to it our bones had an uncomfortable habit of aching, not at all conducive to sleep. After the first night or two, however, being willing and even anxious to adapt ourselves to circumstances, we slept as sound as if in a soft bed at home.
