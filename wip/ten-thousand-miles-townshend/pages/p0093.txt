The number of cadets is usually about two hundred and fifty. Returning thence by river to New York, we had a delightful run down stream of two hours and a half, in which short time the splendid North River steamers perform the distance of fifty-one miles.

The next few days were chiefly occupied in getting the stores, ammunition, letters of introduction, and other necessaries for our hunting in the Far West.
