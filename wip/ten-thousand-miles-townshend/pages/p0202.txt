About 3 <sc>a.m.</sc> the coach was stopped, and our driver informed us that he “guessed we had better walk,” as there was a swamp to cross in which the coach would probably stick or upset. So out we got, and waded through the bog, sinking up to our knees in mud and snow. The six horses and coach plunged in after us; but, besides the poor animals, there emerged only the front wheels of the vehicle, the body and hind wheels remaining firmly stuck in the mud. We had, consequently, to wade back into the swamp and unload the coach before we could extricate it, a fearfully cold operation, in which we spent the best part of two hours. At last, however, our first night of misery in a stage-coach came to an end, and about 8 <sc>a.m.</sc> on Saturday, the 7th, we reached the military outpost of Fort Bridger, on the borders of Wyoming and Utah.
