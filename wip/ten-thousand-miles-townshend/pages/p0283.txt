# Chapter XVIII.

[[subtitle]] Start for England—Wreck of the Rhone—Tortola—Glorious Sunset in a Calm—A Heavy Gale in Mid-Atlantic—Sunday before Christmas—Drowned Out—Loose Sails, Bulwarks and Life-boat—Christmas Evening—A Vessel Vainly Endeavours to Signal—Reach Plymouth Sound—Fearful Weather in the Channel—Reach Southampton.

R<sc>epairing</sc> on board at 11 <sc>a.m.</sc> on Monday, 14th, we soon afterwards steamed away from our berth, where we lay between two intercolonial West Indian mail steamers, which had transferred their cargoes to us, and were about to return to the various islands between which they ply.

In a few hours we passed the island close to which the ill-fated <i>Rhone</i> perished, with most of her passengers, in the earthquake of last year.
