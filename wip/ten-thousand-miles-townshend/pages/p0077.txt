[[cont]]and Baltimore. Shortly after leaving Rochester, one of the passengers, who had for some time been sucking away at a whisky bottle, became very uproarious. The check-string which communicates with the engine-driver was immediately pulled by the conductor, and on the train coming to a standstill, the drunken man was turned out of the cars on to the track, where he was left, the train again proceeding on its way. At Williamsport, which we reached about 10 <sc>p.m.</sc>, a sleeping car was attached to the train, in which, by the additional payment of four dollars, we secured a compartment containing two berths, where we got as much sleep as the jolting of the train over the horribly laid track would allow.
