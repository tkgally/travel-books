Chapter VIII.

The Indian Trail—Dog Creek—Gambling—Measurement of Time—Indian Ponies—The Red Man’s Arms—Deserted Wigwams—Prairie Dog Villages—Cayotes, on Prairie Wolves—False Alarm—Rattlesnakes—Turkey Creek—Indian Songs and Dances—The Indian Pipe—The Red Man’s Heaven—Invitation Visit a Pawnee Village—Indian Morality—Respect for Old Age.

Wednesday, 21st. The morning broke with a thick, cold fog, through which we marched, following an Indian trail four years old, utterly invisible to us, but easily made out by our Indian guides, whose power of following at full gallop a trail, of which we could not make out the faintest sign, was marvellous.
