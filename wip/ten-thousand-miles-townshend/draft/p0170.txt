Though large herds of antelope covered the hill sides, they were so shy that our day’s bag only consisted of two of them, and a few wild duck. Every night that we passed in the Rocky Mountains the cold was so intense that water froze into a solid lump when left inside the tent, and every river, pond, and swamp was covered with ice in the morning. The days were, however, beautiful, with a cloudless sky and hot sun.

Saturday 31st, we started at 6.30 <sc>a.m.</sc> and forded the river, following up the course of the stream about five miles. We then struck off to the right and commenced the ascent of the great ridge of the Rocky Mountains, which divides Wyoming from Colorado.
