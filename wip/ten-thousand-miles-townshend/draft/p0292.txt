## Fish.

In the lakes of the Saranac region and Adirondack Mountains we caught the small river trout, great lake trout, cat-fish, pike, and pickerell. In the Rocky Mountains and Humbolt river, Nevada, I saw a species of black-spotted trout, caught by the Indians, but I believe unknown in Eastern America or Europe.

## Reptiles.

The only reptiles we met with were rattlesnakes, water-snakes, land-turtles, and frogs of every variety.

The above Latin names are taken from the published reports of explorations and surveys made by order of the United States government in 1853—56.
