Having left our luggage at the railway station we proceeded to the Grand Hotel, where we were soon in the full enjoyment of breakfast, musquitoes, and a temperature of 110 degrees Fahr. in the shade.

Panama is a curious old town. Its towers and crumbling walls are green with damp, and overgrown with the rankest tropical vegetation. The population, numbering about 12,000, is composed chiefly of negroes, the little black children running about the streets stark naked. The climate is said to be one of the most unwholesome in the world, and the few white inhabitants of the town have a worn, sallow appearance, very suggestive of yellow fever and ague. Situated at the foot of Mount Anchon, on a peninsula stretching out into the fine Bay of Panama, it has the advantage of being a free port, and does a fair amount of trade in Chinese goods, cigars, and Panama hats.
