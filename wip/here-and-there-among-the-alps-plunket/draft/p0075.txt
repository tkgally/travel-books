Whence come all the people who fill the innumerable hotels of Switzerland? and what became of them before these hotels were built for their accommodation? It is no use in trying to solve these mighty problems, to answer glibly, ‘Oh, they all stayed at home,’ because, if this were the case, then the various quarters from which they come would now be empty, whereas it is the experience of every traveller who moves much about the world, that each place at home or abroad to which he returns after some time of absence, he finds to have become larger and fuller than when he last visited it. I have never as yet heard these questions answered in a satisfactory manner.
