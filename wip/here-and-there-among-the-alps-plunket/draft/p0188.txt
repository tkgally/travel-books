There is with ladies, even for those who attain a certain degree above the average capacity of climbing, a great drawback to many of the most interesting ascents. I allude to the impossibility of making some of these excursions in one day, and the necessity of sleeping in a cave or small hut, containing but one room. Of course in this matter every lady must be the best judge for herself, whether or not she will submit to this amount of roughing. Of these places I only speak by hearsay, as we have never spent a night in one of them, but from the description given of them they must be the very reverse of agreeable, and it is a question whether the pleasantness of the following day will counterbalance the unpleasantness of the preceding night.

For the comfort of those who, like ourselves, shrink from such rough quarters, it is good to know that they need not be debarred from all great ascents by this difficulty.
