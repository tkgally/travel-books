# Among the Alps.

# Introduction.

I<sc>n</sc> these days of Alpine adventure, when so many of our countrymen, braving the dangers and hardships of the higher regions, ascend one mountain after another, till scarce a European peak remains untrodden by British feet, the literature of the Alps has attained a degree of importance unheard of, or unthought of some twenty years ago.
