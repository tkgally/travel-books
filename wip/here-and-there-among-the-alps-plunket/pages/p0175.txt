Before taking leave of any of my kind readers who may have followed us thus far in our wanderings, I wish to add a short chapter to this little book, for the benefit of those who, like ourselves, spend their summers among the mountains, in the hope that a few plain directions contained in it may be of use to them, and that the knowledge we gained from that rather hard teacher, experience, may be communicated to them in gentler fashion, and without the severe lessons with which its acquisition was at times enforced on us.
