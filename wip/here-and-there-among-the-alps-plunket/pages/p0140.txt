# Chapter IX.

[[subtitle]] <i>The Schwarzkopf and the Steinerne Meer.</i>

J<sc>udging</sc> by the accommodation at Heiligenblut, our hopes of Fuscherbad had not soared high; we were therefore most agreeably surprised by the superiority of this latter place. It can scarcely be called a village, but is rather a small group of hotels surrounding a little chapel, very prettily situated in the midst of green meadows and fine trees, through which pleasant paths have been made for the benefit of the patients who frequent the place. At the nearest of these houses we stopped, and found it, though unpretending, to be exceedingly clean and comfortable, and the owners very civil and friendly. It belongs to a very old man, who in his day was, we were told, one of the best guides of the district.
