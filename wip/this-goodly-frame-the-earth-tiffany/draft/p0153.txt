Thus so hollow a shell as the Chinese Empire nowhere else exists; while none the less bodily and in latent mental capacity the Chinese are one of the most powerful races on the globe,—far the superiors of the Japanese in solidity of mind, in business capacity, in potential depth of thought and persistence of will, in almost everything but artistic sensibility.[[fnref 1]]

[[fn 1]] And yet a conflict between China and Japan has turned out like a fight between an ox and a hornet, in which the hornet, able to get in everywhere and the ox nowhere, the big, helpless bovine runs bellowing across the plain. So much will stereotyped routine and too protracted addiction to Yao and Shun do with a mighty people.
