But she, at the very threshold of ghastly death, the fatal torch of Yama before her, calmly ascended the funeral pile, and, lying down by the side of her husband, with one hand under his head and another on his breast, was heard to call in a half-suppressed voice, ‘Hari! Hari!’ (Krishna! Krishna!),—a sign of her firm belief in the reality of eternal beatitude. . . . A great shout of exultation then arose from the surrounding spectators, till both the dead and living bodies were converted into a handful of dust and ashes.”

In submitting this heroic and pathetic story and simply leaving it to make its own impression, I can only humbly trust I shall not be subjected to the suspicion of being in secret a perfidious advocate of Sati, or Hindu widow-burning.
