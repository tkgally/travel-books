The brute in his nature laid to rest, again would the profound thinker resort to his aerial suspension, and resume the thread of broken-contemplation. Oh, that Robert Browning, with his deep psychical insight, could have seen him! There was material there for a profounder poem than “Caliban on Setebos.” Browning’s Caliban had no outreaching, prophetic element in him. In this Caliban it was impossible not to feel it working,—an elemental, slowly differentiating, secular force!
