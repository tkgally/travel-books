## II.

In the description given in the last chapter of Thi’s tomb at Memphis allusion was made to the Egyptian doctrine of the double. Now just as surely as in Dr. Edward E. Hale’s instructive story, “My Double and How He Undid Me,” its unhappy writer was brought to grief by not fully taking in the exact nature of his own double, so equally will every embryo student of early Egyptian conceptions of spirit-life find himself “undone” if he does not take in the exact nature of the Egyptian’s double. The double is fundamental, as much a part of the man and his belongings as are his own or their own shadows in the sunshine.
