This tribal solution appeared to me the more unsatisfactory in that the handsomer and more intelligent blacks are not isolated individuals, but are closely led up to by others, and seem indeed to present the perfected type, to which the entire mass is tending. Even granting that this could be wholly accounted for by marriage of negroes of finer with those of inferior strain (I am speaking only of unmixed blacks), it still remains that the proclivity of the entire race is strongly progressive. Were it otherwise, such marriages might just as well have resulted in the degradation of the higher characteristics.

Another fact which seems to me to bear upon the marked capacity of the African race for development under favourable influences, climatic and other, is the apparently disproportionate advance made by the men in comparison with the women. If the finer specimens of Negro were all traceable to differences in the original stock, such examples should be found as often among one sex as the other. If on the other hand one of the chief factors in the evolution of the higher tendencies of the inferior race, is contact with the superior, the slower rate of advance observable among negro women has its <i>primâ facie</i> justification in the circumstance of their lives being led much further apart from such influences.
