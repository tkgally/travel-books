# Leaf V.

TWO tiers of verandah or balcony give a pleasing aspect to the hotel at which we are staying. When we alighted on our first arrival the guests were sitting in little groups about the door, and regarded us with that wholly unsmiling scrutiny characteristic of Americans. While our baggage was being conveyed to our room (that corner one which I have since heard they call the Bridal Chamber), I took a vacant rocking-chair, and began to look about me. The momentary lull which our advent among this company had caused, was broken by a voice from the road beneath the lower verandah, calling up to some one seated a little in the rear of me. It was a man’s voice, and, judging from the faint despondent tone, a weak and a weary one.

“Anna, I have lost my keys.”

“Have you so?” was responded.

“I saw them last on my drawers, in my room—just behind you.”
