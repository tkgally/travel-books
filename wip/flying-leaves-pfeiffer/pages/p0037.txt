Having sat out the last course of Turkish hospitality, we took our leave of these unhappy women, upon whom time seemed to hang with the weight of lead. At the gate we passed their black guardian, a monster who to civilized eyes looked almost fabulous. As he gazed down upon us from his vast height, with a grotesque arrogance, his swollen pride yet ineffectual to smooth out the wrinkles of his blasted youth, we thought of all the treasure of wasted womanhood still existing in the needy overworked world, and we said to ourselves, “How long?”
