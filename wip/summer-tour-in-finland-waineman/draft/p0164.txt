[[cont]] captain became furiously excited, and began shouting orders in a fluent mixture of Finnish, Swedish, and Russian. But no language could avert our doom—we had struck on a sand-hill in the middle of the open water, and were firmly imbedded thereon! Had it been a rock, my tour in Finland would have been brought to an abrupt conclusion.

All's well that ends well! After strenuous efforts on the part of the crew and ourselves, with the help of long poles, we at last glided out into the open waters again.

After this we had no further adventures. At last we were landed safely at Sordavala, thoroughly chilled and famished, and our ladies very much subdued, just as the clocks were striking midnight.
