They are very unlike the Burmans in looks and character, but are equally unlike the natives of India from the other side of the Bay of Bengal.

There are a very clannish people. Sometimes, nearly all the able-bodied men from one village come to Burma to obtain employment, and, whenever possible, they try to keep all together on one ship. This, occasionally, leads to awkwardness or confusion. The head man of the crew is called the Serang, and, when he, for any reason, is dismissed or transferred to another ship, the whole of the crew usually follow him. If there is friction between the Captain and the crew over any real or fancied grievance, the Serang may, and probably <i>will</i>, leave the ship, when she is on the point of sailing, and take all his men along with him.
