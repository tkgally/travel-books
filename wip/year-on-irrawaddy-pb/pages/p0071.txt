# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] Sunset and Searchlight.

Of course the sun has set every evening of the trip, but the fact has not been specially recorded before this, and, although the use of the searchlight has been mentioned casually once or twice, neither event has received the consideration it deserves—so says the Skipper. Let me try to make amends.

In Burma, when the sun sinks below the horizon, there is a rapid change from day to night. So sudden and unexpected is it to one accustomed to Northern twilights that it leaves one almost breathless. There is no
