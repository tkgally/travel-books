Each night on the upward journey we anchored after sunset, but, as it happened, the day’s run never ended near any town, and no other Flotilla boat found an anchorage near us, so that, until Pakkoko was reached, we had no other company than our own, and that of the Chief-Engineer occasionally.

At Pakkoko our peaceful existence came to an end. The “Falam” had to be handed over to one of the Commanders on the Chindwin River, the steamer he vacated had to be taken up to Myingyan to go on the ferry run, and another ship had to be brought down to take its place.
