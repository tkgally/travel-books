I shall never forget the face of one young secunnie, who was rebuked in a humiliating fashion, after sending us zig-zagging up the river like a sailing-yacht against a head-wind. The Skipper saw that there was a stretch of clear water ahead, so he said to the purri-wallah,

“<i>Bawarchi bolao</i>” (Call the cook).

The cook came along, and the Skipper told him to take the wheel and hold it steady. This he did, and the ship ploughed forward on a straight course. The shamed and disgraced secunnie stood by, digging his knuckles into his eyes, and blubbering aloud. But he learned his lesson, and that was the main thing.
