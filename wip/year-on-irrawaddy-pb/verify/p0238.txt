I have never heard anyone mention this pagoda, or seen any account of it in a book, but to me it was far more interesting and beautiful than anything else I saw at Mingoon.

The noted pagoda at Mingoon,—the one which was intended to be the largest in the world and which was abandoned before it was one third finished, and was afterwards cracked across by an earthquake,—did not attract me half so much as this nameless one. Still it would have been a neglect of duty to visit Mingoon and not see this pagoda, so from our point of vantage, we noted its position, and, descending, made our way towards it.

Only the plinth of this was finished—a huge, cubical structure of brick rising to a considerable height before beginning to narrow in terraces towards the top.
