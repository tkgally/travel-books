# Chapter XIII
[[subtitle]] An Evening at Kyoukyai.

Though the usual trip for an oil boat is to Nyounghla and back, we sometimes get a little variety. Occasionally there is cargo to discharge at some little village above Nyounghla, or there are bags of ground-nuts or beans to be loaded at Thingin.

One afternoon in May, we arrive at Kyoukyai with a cargo of iron pipes to be discharged. The day has been hot, but there is a promise of coolness in the air, and the prospect ashore looks inviting. On board there is nothing but dust and heat, noise and confusion. Gangs of perspiring coolies are chanting an irritating strain as they roll the clattering pipes along the iron deck of the flat. The Chief Officer and Serang are shouting orders, the engineers are hammering at a damaged float in the wheel, and steam is hissing away both fore and aft. So we decide to go ashore for a stroll.
