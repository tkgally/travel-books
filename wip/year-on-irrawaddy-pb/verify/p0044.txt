At the Pilot station above Henzedah we pick up another Pilot. He is waiting on the bank with his bundle and umbrella beside him and when he hears the steamer’s whistle he jumps into a sampan and makes for the ship. There are three men in the sampan and when they are near enough a Kalassi waiting on the flat heaves them a line which they catch and make fast. Suddenly the sampan capsizes and there are three men struggling in the river! Away goes the cutter and picks them up, three draggled, foolish, sorry looking objects. They confess afterwards that they made the line fast amidships and naturally the boat swung across the current and turned turtle. They are funny people! After fifteen or twenty years’ experience on the river they are still capable of doing these amazingly stupid things.
