[[cont]]papers fluttering and whirling around his head like snowflakes in a blizzard. The unfortunate member of the crew who is told off to help him has several unhappy moments. The work proceeds after this fashion:—

Skipper. “<i>Martol lao!</i>” (<i>Bring a hammer!</i>)

The man brings a plane.

Skipper. “<i>Martol</i> you jungle-wallah! <i>Martol! Martol!</i>”

The man brings a saw.

Skipper (cresendo and staccato) “Oh! you pagal! Martol! Martol! MARTOL!”

He imitates the action of hammering and the man interpreting it rightly, at last brings a hammer.

Then follows an interval while we pass another steamer.

A little later on:—

Skipper. “<i>Iss-crew-drill-lao!</i>”

(Not knowing the Hindustani, he mangles the English in order to make it more intelligible to the poor bemazed Chittagonian.)

The man, hopelessly at sea and not daring to confess it, tries his luck with a brace-bit.

Skipper. “Oh you idiot, you fool! <i>Tumhara bat nai-junta? Iss-crew-drill lao!</i>”
