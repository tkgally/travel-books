Of course there are butlers, <i>and</i> butlers. Some of them are absolute treasures, but this variety is only to be found on the mail or cargo-steamers,—never on an oil-boat. Lucky is the commander who can get “Jumbo” (so named because of his elephantine proportions) or one of his numerous well-trained relatives to undertake the messing of the ship. “Jumbo” is an institution. He is almost as well known in Further India as is the immortal Gunga Din in India proper. One can walk on board, half an hour before dinner, and say to him, without any qualms.

“Jumbo there will be six extra to dinner to-night.”

“Very good sir!” says he, and disappears below.

Punctually to the minute the dinner is served—plenty for everybody, and everything done to perfection. Jumbo, the incarnation of corpulent dignity, stands silently by to see that all goes well.
