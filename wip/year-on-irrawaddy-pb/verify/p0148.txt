He does this purposely, and hops away with an agonised expression, holding the injured knee in both his hands. This causes peals of laughter. It is not difficult to make a Burman laugh, and, when he does, he does it heartily.

When we go on we are followed by all the children in the village—a quiet subdued little crowd, all eyes and ears, but evidently hoping for more fun.

Back once more aboard the ship, we amuse ourselves by throwing pice amongst the youngsters, and watching them delving and scrambling in the loose sand to find them. There is a plenty of excitement, but no snatching or fighting. The elder ones see that the babes get fair play.
