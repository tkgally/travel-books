Not long after this, we meet an oil steamer which has evidently come to grief, for water is flowing over the decks of the flats, and they are crowded with men. She has struck a rock and is struggling down to Rangoon with all pumps working. We stop to lend her our pulsometer. A little later on the Skipper becomes very interested in something he sees ahead. He studies it through his binoculars and then turns his telescope on the object. The secunnie also notices something unusual, for he hazards the opinion,

“<i>Jaharge lak gia Sahib.</i>”

“There’s a B. O. C. launch aground over there,” says the Skipper, “I shall have a shot at pulling her off.”
