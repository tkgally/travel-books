# Part II.

[[subtitle]] Varied Experiences Month by Month.

# Chapter XV.

[[subtitle]] Fogs and Heat.

Having described one trip in detail, it is not my intention to give a minute and particular account of each of the others “<i>ad nauseam.</i>” During the course of one year we went up to Nyounghla and back probably twenty to twenty-five times, and, though the round trip is, roughly speaking, about a thousand miles in length, one soon gets to know the river, and there is naturally a great deal of monotony in the life.

Our chief interest was the weather.
