Needless to say I came in for a good deal of chaff at dinner time when the mate recounted my adventures. The Commander who had previously been teasing me about Suffragette tendencies possessed himself of my menu card when my attention was elsewhere and next time I looked at it I saw inscribed thereon:—

<verse>
‘<i>Our Suffragettes are very brave, and stern their moral code,</i>’
‘<i>But you should see them skip and scream when meeting with a toad:</i>’
‘<i>To principles they will adhere though they should go to jail,</i>’
‘<i>But any beetle frog or mouse will make their stout hearts quail.</i>’
</verse>

With which appropriate epitaph, I close the chapter.
