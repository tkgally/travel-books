It would be a wise thing for travellers in the quite uncivilised portions of the great forest, where fresh provisions and even fruits are difficult to obtain, to carry a supply of lime juice. The cure I have alluded to is said to be efficacious, and it certainly had a good effect on one of our men. It consists of the application of gunpowder and hot capsicum per rectum.

As the invalid provided a substitute, we started forward again only one less in numbers, but minus the charm (?) of female society, and bid “adios” to our Indian friends with <i>shug rato gama</i> [[fnref *]] to some of them who at the same time were setting out for Iquitos and the Huallaga, <i>viâ</i> the Pastassa, for salt and poison.

[[fn *]] A mixture of Quichua and Spanish in common use; equivalent to “Hasta luego,” or the American “So long!”
