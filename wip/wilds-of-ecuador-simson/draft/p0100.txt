Our host came out and formally visited us, presenting another large plantain leaf filled with fresh uncooked fish—all very small trout and other diminutive fry, which we prepared like whitebait for dinner, with sweet potatoes and plantains.

What a grand and festive spread was this, with the addition of a fowl, after our miserable and scanty fare of the past three or four weeks! I ate so much—shame be upon me for having to make the confession—that I could hardly stand or speak for a short time after we had dined!
