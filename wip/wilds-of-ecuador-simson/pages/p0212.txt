# Chapter XV.
[[subtitle]] The Aguarico to the Amazons.

C<sc>ontinuing</sc> our long canoe journey, we spent a night at a spot called Santa Maria, where a massacre of Piojés by the Auhishiris had also taken place; and from there another long day’s paddle, and an hour the following morning, brought us to Paula Cocha, which we intended exploring. We settled on a sandbank, opposite the entrance of the Cocha, or lake, on an island called Auhishiri Island—for behind it is the mouth of the Auhishiri river—and unloaded the lightest of our canoes.
