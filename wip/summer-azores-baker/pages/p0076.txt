We reached the floor of the crater in an hour and a quarter. It was covered with mints and tansy. The pond, which from above had appeared like stagnant water, was the outlet of a clear brook, the surface being covered with a network of the leaves of some aquatic plant. Gold and silver fishes darted among the roots. At some time or other, the crater lakes have been stocked with these fish. There are no native fish in the islands, and no snakes or reptiles of any kind. A few small pond-lilies bloomed sweetly near the shore. It was a relief to find life and beauty in place of stagnation and decay. Gazing upward, it was an awful sensation to see the clouds pouring in over the edge of the abyss like a cataract, and rolling above us like billows of the sea.
