[[cont]]travels; she likes the <i>burro</i>, and enjoys the <i>buena vista</i>.

The <i>senhora Portuguese</i> does nothing of all this. She reads nothing; she sits at home and fans herself; she “<i>valsa, valsa, sempre valsa</i>,” and cares for nothing but “<i>dança, dança, sempre dança</i>.” And, fanning himself violently with his hat, Anton waltzed down the road to show us how she did it.
