They are mystified; then they shout with delight, the “<i>senhora sabe Portuguese.</i>” We don’t let them know we don’t <i>sabe</i> for awhile. Then, regardless of moods and tenses, and other grammatical trifles, we patch up a mongrel sentence out of our little Latin, Italian, and French. In nine cases out of ten, they understand and answer, so that we get the substance of what they say. Thus, by sheer audacity we really get a good deal out of the language.
