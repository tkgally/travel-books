# The Caldeira.

[[center]] <i>Tuesday, Aug. 5.</i>

NO one should miss seeing the Caldeira, the crater, <i>par excellence</i>, of Fayal. It is reached by a gradual ascent of nine miles from Horta, which is easily made on a donkey or in a hammock. The hammock, fastened at either end to a long bamboo pole, is thus borne on the shoulders of two men. We were wakened at four in the morning by our escort, who chattered and smoked incessantly, while waiting for us to breakfast. They carry small bags of tobacco in their pockets, with innumerable little squares of corn-husks. The latter they wet with the lips, and roll up the tobacco in them into cigarettes as they need them.
