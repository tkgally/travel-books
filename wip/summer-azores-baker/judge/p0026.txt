“There wasn’t a wink of sleep on board that night. The next morning I put the flag at half-mast. Everybody was solemn as death. ——’s wet hat lay on the hatchway. They all thought he was dead.

“The captain come off to see what was the matter. I told him the story,—how we heard the splash, got out the boats, and picked up ——’s hat. Right in the midst of it the officers from Rodrigues come aboard to claim their man. We told ’em the story, and showed ’em the wet hat. They never offered to search the vessel. They see how bad the men felt; and they believed it all, and pulled off.

“Late that afternoon we got our water all aboard, and bore away to sea. I waited till we was almost out o’ sight o’ land: then I says to the captain, ‘I guess I’ll go below and get a cigar.’ I went, and hauled the step away; and there was ——, all in a heap. I can see that fellow’s face right before me now, white as chalk, eyes as black as night. He looked like a wild man.

“‘What now?’ says he, trembling all over.
