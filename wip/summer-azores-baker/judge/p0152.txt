# Sight-Seeing.

[[center]] <i>Saturday, Sept. 13.</i>

WE go out early for a tour through the shops. They abound in inlaid work, displaying to the best advantage the beautiful cabinet woods from which the island takes its name. The basis of all the inlaid work is the Til wood, which is found only on this island and the Canaries. It resembles rosewood in color and in capacity for fine polish.

We found embroideries equalling those of the French, both in quality and cost; baskets, grass mattings, and wicker furniture for a mere song. Monkeys, with parrots and other gay birds from the African coast, were exposed for sale at the shop-doors.
