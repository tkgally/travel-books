# San Miguel and Its Port.

[[center]] <i>Monday, Aug. 18.</i>

S<sc>unrise</sc> of the next morning finds the steamer anchored off Ponta Delgada, the seaport of San Miguel (St. Michael’s). Although this island is the largest and finest of the Azores, the first view of it from the sea is disappointing. Innumerable little conical hills extend in a monotonous, scalloped ridge behind the city. Externally Ponta Delgada resembles Horta. Its commerce is mostly with England, while that of Horta is with America. It has more wealth than Horta, and some fine residences surrounded by superb gardens of world-wide fame. There is a small English colony of pleasant people, and an English church.
