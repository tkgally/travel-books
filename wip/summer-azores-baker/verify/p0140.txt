# In Quarantine off Funchal.

[[center]] <i>Tuesday, Sept. 9.</i>

A GUARD is put on board. We are ordered to hoist a yellow flag, and to anchor off far from the town on quarantine ground. We feel like lepers, and pretty mad lepers at that.

An English steamer from the Gaboon steams in ahead of us. In an hour or two she will sail, and take letters. We fall to writing as a vent for our misery. It is exasperating to sit here seasick, within a stone’s-throw of the town, see-sawing on the tide, and drifting lazily round on our anchor-chains as the wind takes us, and see the other steamer discharge her cargo and passengers.
