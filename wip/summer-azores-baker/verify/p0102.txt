# The Furnas.—Grená and the Caldeiras.

[[center]] <i>Monday, Aug. 18.</i>

T<sc>he</sc> bottom reached, our tongues are loosed. There is certainly a charming novelty in the idea of dwelling for a while in an extinct crater. Not so extinct, however, but that it still quivers with half-suppressed convulsions of internal rage, and mutters sullen premonitions of future outburst. Not so alive, either, but that man has pitched his habitation all over its surface, and cornfields wave upon its slopes, and the yam and the sweet potato flourish, cheated into a tropical luxuriance by its subterranean fires, and watered by the spray of its boiling brooks.
