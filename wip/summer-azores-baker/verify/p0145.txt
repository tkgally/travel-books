# Ashore in Madeira.

[[center]] <i>Friday, Sept. 12.</i>

WE pulled for the shore in one of the queer little boats, followed by a rickety old tub full of naked youngsters who clamored for silver. We threw small silver coins as far away from their boat as we could into deep water. They dived like lightning flashes from their boat side, and, seizing the coin before it touched bottom, shouted its value as they rose to the surface, puffing and blowing the water from their mouths.

The surf ran high as we drew near the shelving beach. Three or four men stripped up their linen trousers to the hips, and, plunging into the tide, seized our stern, and fastened a rope to it, the other end of which was attached to two bullocks on shore. With goads and cries the bullocks were then urged ahead, and drew us, boat and all, high up on the shingly beach. So we finally got ashore at Madeira.
