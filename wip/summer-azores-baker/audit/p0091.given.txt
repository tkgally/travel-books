A Peep at San jorge, Graciosa, and Terceira.

Thursday, Aug. 14.

AN excellent line of Portuguese steamers affords fortnightly communication between the Azores and Lisbon. Letters from America are received at the islands by this line; in from twenty to thirty days from the time mailing in New York or Boston. Taking passage in these steamers at any of the islands except Pico, which is regarded as but a suburb of Fayal, one may visit the others of the group.

We embarked at Horta at midnight of Thursday, Aug. 14. Silent and depressed, as is natural to those who go from the known to the unknown, we pushed out into the darkness. The bay was calm. Jupiter left a shining wake upon the waters as be went his way in the heavens. Our boat ploughed a furrow of phosphoresccnce in the sea. Stars fell from our dripping oars.
