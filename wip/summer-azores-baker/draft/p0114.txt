When the family is too numerous to stow away in the two beds, others are made up under them, and trundled out at night. A loft is also made in the peak of the roof for the big boys, by swinging a floor of boards half across the living-room, above the other beds. Often one may see the men of the family taking here their noonday rest while below

<blockquote>
“The wife, solacing with song her tedious labor, runs through the webs with her shrill sounding shuttle.”
</blockquote>

Wandering from house to house in the valley of the Furnas, we easily forget we are living in the middle of the nineteenth century, so primitive are the occupations of the people.

Some wove the web
