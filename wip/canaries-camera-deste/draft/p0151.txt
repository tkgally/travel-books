[[cont]] mortified, when travelling on the newly-opened Port Soudan railway, at seeing numbers of birds perched on the telegraph wires—all with their backs to the train, as though it was beneath notice; but these are merely the impertinences of children compared with the lofty disdain of the camel, which is alone among animals in its unaffected contempt for humans.

The Arabs have a saying to account for it: the Moslem knows only ninety-nine attributes of Allah—but the camel knows the hundredth, <i>and he will not tell.</i>
