They are said to have been picked up on the beach after the defeat of the midnight landing party, when most of the boats were sunk by gunfire from the forts, or by the surf of the dangerous coast. As Nelson stepped upon the jetty his arm was shattered by a cannon-ball, and it is said that the first occasion on which he wrote with his left hand was in signing a letter of thanks to the Spanish commandant for the courtesy that had been shown to his beaten men.

Till within recent years the flags were openly displayed in the church, but since the day when an over-zealous English middy carried them off to his ship—only to be commanded sternly to return them immediately with profuse apologies—they have been hung up out of harm’s way.

Some people spend the whole winter at Santa Cruz, and I have often wondered how they manage to amuse themselves there, but that is only because we did not happen to find the place attractive. You will find that Santa Cruz, in return, is equally unable to imagine how people employ their time at Orotava. It is not fair to judge of a place from a few days’ stay, for we have always found that occupation develops by degrees as one settles down, but I think we got a little tired of daily walking down into the town and taking a carriage up again, and felt that we should be glad to get away into the country.
