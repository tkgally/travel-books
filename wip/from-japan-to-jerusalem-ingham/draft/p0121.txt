One conversation held with the Chinese owner and head of the steamship line we are now travelling by is worth mentioning. He said, ‘I have two sons in England, one is just finishing at St. Thomas’s Hospital after having obtained a Cambridge degree, the other is at Birmingham University studying engineering. When young men of this type return to China and find place and power they will gradually replace us older men, and China will then go ahead very fast, but you cannot expect any great changes until then.’ This sounds eminently reasonable, and all that one has seen goes to show that any faster pace would be unwise.
