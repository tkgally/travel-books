The case slipped from my hand as I was putting it in my pocket; in picking it up, the head-waiter noticed a coronet which happens to be engraved upon it, and at once received the impression that he had entertained a Russian Princess unawares.

But feeling that it was not fair to interrupt the market any longer, and cause, indeed, a stagnation of the town’s whole business, I got in beside my hunting-sack, saddle and saddle-bag, and told the man to go on, remarking roomily “To the mountains!”
