But I was roused from my reflections by the coming of mine host, a civil Pole, and the mob pressed closer to hear what he had to say.

It seemed there was a room, only one room vacant, and though to him it would have been a rapture to receive the most noble Herrschaft, his conscience required that he should impart to her one thing—the mattress on the bed was filled with <i>goat’s hair!</i>

<i>Nu!</i> Every one in the town, the other Herrschaft, would be honoured in the tarrying of the by him for ever to be respected noble lady, but—his best rooms, his two superb rooms, were occupied by mining engineers, and it was with heart’s sorrow that he confided that the mattress upon the bed in the third remaining room <i>was filled with goat’s hair!</i>

For myself I had not, and have never had, any case against goat’s hair, but I observed immediately from the tone in which the excellent creature spoke, that it was something to be avoided; so I nodded intelligently, tuned my voice to his own mysterious but regretful key, and having decided to exploit the doctor’s, we parted with tears in our eyes.
