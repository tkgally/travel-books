The party amused itself by endeavouring to alarm me about the journey, its length, its hardships, and so on. The way was, it seemed, absolutely <i>set</i> with bears, otherwise lonely to the heart's desire. With the happy confidence of complete ignorance, I smiled upon them, and they bid me good-night, with all their affectionate civility, a little before ten o'clock, for we had to be up by four next morning.
