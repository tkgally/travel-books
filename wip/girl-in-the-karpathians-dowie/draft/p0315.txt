It would, perhaps, seem to suggest a measure of conceit on my part, and certainly it was a bold move; but, whatever my shortcomings (and these have not been hidden under a bushel), an undue sense of my importance is not conspicuously one of them, for, whatever the last words of these readers might be—and, down the wind, I seem to catch echo of some of them—I do not for a moment flatter myself that they would bid me “write often”!
