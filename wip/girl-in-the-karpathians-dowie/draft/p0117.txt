[[cont]] I’m only asking. Of course, I know what I want; but really I am not quite clear as to the general desideratum. The Ruthenian who gets drunk at least has the grace to permit a like indulgence to his wife and daughters—which is justice, at any rate; but then they all go down the hill together.

I took this problem up a pine-hill with me and spent some hours among its mazes. On first coming out in those little sunny grass glades which surprise one now and again within the woodland, on first looking round upon the hot, gay standing flowers, with blue and green dragon-flies, and any choice of butterflies chasing among them, my thought was only one of irritation against the stupid human race that seems so persistently to mismanage and misuse every opportunity, every good gift, and every perfect gift.

It was the season of the myriad unknown beetle, and he was powerful in his masses, and astounding in his variations. He invaded everything. When he is in his zenith, then it is the heyday of the summer as well.
