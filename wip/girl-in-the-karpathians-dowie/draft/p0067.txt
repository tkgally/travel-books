They are square, built round with rough hewn logs; an immense crane, made of a pine-tree and very nicely adjusted, dumps the wooden jugs under the water, holding them firmly by one ear—much like the bathing women of my youth; it also lifts them up and swings them deftly to the edge.

But with the warmer weather my blue basin fell into disuse, and in the early morning, or about eleven, or previous to coffee at four, or in the evening, or the night, or indeed any time, I went straying over the ragged river-bed barefoot to the weir. The approach to the river on my side was flat; on the further, the hills rose. The sheer bank that overlooked the stream was sweet with wild strawberries, and I would swim across in a slanting line, buffeted by the current, and risking the queer whirlpools, to climb up with immense difficulty and eat all that were ripe.
