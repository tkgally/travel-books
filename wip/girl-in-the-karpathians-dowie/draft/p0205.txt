It is in that short hour that a flea snatches the brief moments of repose necessary to furnish it with strength for the campaign of twenty-three long ones. This is authoritative. I know it! I have proved it, and the fact is recorded in my life's blood. I opened the window, and stood by it looking out upon a dim, calm, grey world—a world without a flea-bite on all her rugged surface!

O but I was tired! I put my fingers in my eyes, and they were like jelly-fishes that the ebb-tide has forgotten—soft and squashy—ugh!
