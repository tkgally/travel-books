# Cassell's Blue Library.

Cassell's “Blue Library” is the name given to a new series of novels issued by the Cassell Publishing Company. Well-known American, English, and Continental authors will be represented, and none but books of high literary merit and of permanent value will be admitted to this series.

The volumes are of 12mo size, and are bound in <i>Blue Cloth</i>, simply but artistically decorated with a design in silver and gold.
