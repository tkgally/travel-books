Having said “good afternoon,” I would go to my outhouse to read or eat wild strawberries. In the evening a solitary walk preceded supper, and toward midnight, when the village was in bed, I bathed in the black hole below the bridge, with only a moonbeam for companion.

Such was the dull and pleasant pattern of my early days in Mikuliczyn.
