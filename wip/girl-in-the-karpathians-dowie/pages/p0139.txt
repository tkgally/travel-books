On my second last night a circus appeared in the precincts of the village, and a thin, unduly agile, large-eyed child started up with the suddenness of a Corsican twin in my room, and showered leaflets upon which statements as to the pleasure and satisfaction in the performance expressed by the crowned heads of Europe were modestly set forth. I decided to go: if I had time, and some one else would be answerable for my support, I would do little else save go to circuses.

The occasion proved very interesting. All the village and large parts of several other villages were going. The postmaster hoped diffidently that he should be permitted the felicity of introducing to me the lady—the third lady—he had honoured with his choice.
