I pressed cigarettes upon them, pushed the dear, pattering, loose-limbed calf out of the way, and sat down by a window, which I opened. Soon the room was blue with tobacco smoke, and through this I saw the poor artist in sheepskins sitting on the bench covered with a blanket, his little table beside him heaped with brass eyelets; wools, red, orange, green, and yellow; and snips of pinked-out red and green morocco, which he applied to the beautifully dressed skin upon which he was working. He could not have been more than six-and-twenty, and yet there he was confined to the house, an invalid, a wreck, and married! The brother was not so far gone, though sickly and scrofulous in the extreme. He used to bring me eggs, painted and decorated in self-made colours, and covered with very quaint designs. The sisters made the embroidery for the tops of sleeves upon the linen gowns, and all had charming artistic tastes, and gentle, winning natures.
