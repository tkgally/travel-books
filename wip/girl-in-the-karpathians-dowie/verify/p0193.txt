O that little lighted room, with the mob of silly unknown saints’ faces upon its walls, the row of carved spoons in the rack, the dried flowers taken from the church on a fête-day hanging above the crucifixes and crossed pistols; the table, solid, in two kinds of wood and “Blinder’s” pride, half-decked with its red cover, and bearing the bowl of hot milk, not an hour from the cow, but still further warmed to within a minute of seething point in a big iron pot; the dish of kolesha, steaming, turned in a stiff square lump from the pan; the plate of hard-boiled eggs; finally, the whispered “Proszę” of the woman!
