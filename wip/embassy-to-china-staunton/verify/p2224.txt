“The dimensions of the parapets, embrasures, and loopholes, are as in the first tower.

“The embrasures or ports in each of the rooms, and the recesses for those of the second story, are all arched.

“The coins of the doors, windows, ports, embrasures, and many of the salient angles and staircases in the towers, as well as the broad bases or stone foundations of the towers and intervening wall, are of a strong grey granite, with little mixture of mica in it.

“The rest of those buildings consist of bricks of a bluish colour. They are laid in laminæ of a brick thick each; forming, as it were, so many distinct walls as there are bricks in thickness.
