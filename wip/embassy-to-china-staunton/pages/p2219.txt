“The bottom of the loopholes is on a level with the terrepleine of the wall; and from thence they are sloped downwards, so as to discover an enemy within a few yards of the basis of the wall. It will perhaps be thought, that this position is much better calculated for the use of fire arms, than for that of bows and arrows.
