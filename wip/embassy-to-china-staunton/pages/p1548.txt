By employing a part of his leisure during the present season in sailing to Japan, with a view to sound the disposition of that court towards a mercantile connection with the English nation, his Excellency might be enabled to determine about going there, agreeably to his instructions, in case there should be a reasonable prospect of public benefit from such a measure; or if Sir Erasmus Gower should find that people persevering in their usual system of exclusion, or that, otherwise, no material advantage was to be derived from any intercourse with them, the information of that commander might save the expence of the Embassy’s continuing longer in Asia, for the purpose of such an expedition.
