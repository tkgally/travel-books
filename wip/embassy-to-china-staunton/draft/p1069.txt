This event retarded, but did not prevent, the prosecution of the measure originally intended. Fresh circumstances occurred to press its execution. A more comprehensive view, also, was taken of the subject; and considerations of humanity and philosophy were superadded to those of policy and commerce. Among the transactions which give lustre to the present reign, some of the most memorable were the voyages undertaken under the immediate auspices of the Sovereign, whereby the boundaries of science were enlarged, and the globe was circumnavigated, without the incentives of gain or conquest. But advantages were obtained more durable, and more worthy of elevated minds.
