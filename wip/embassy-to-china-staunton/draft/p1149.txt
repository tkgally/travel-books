Before these travellers got to the seaport of Orotava, they passed through the city, or upper town, of the same name, neatly built of stone, on an irregular surface. They took the dimensions of a remarkable dragon’s-blood tree growing near it; to which tree any of the same kind in Madeira, tho there thought large, were, comparatively, but striplings: its trunk measured, at the height of ten feet from the ground, thirty-six feet in girth; at the height of fifteen feet, this trunk divided itself into about a dozen branches, sprouting regularly, as from a centre, in an oblique direction, upwards, like the subdivisions of an umbelliferous plant, all of equal dimensions, and producing, at their extremities only, thick and spongy leaves, resembling, but much smaller than, those of the common aloe.
