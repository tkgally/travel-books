The taxes were severely felt, particularly in the interior provinces, where the carriage and transit duties increased the price of every article so enormously, that a bottle of port wine, for instance, cost ten shillings sterling to the consumer.

The rising spirit of the people, little patient of the hardships imposed upon them by the mother country, led, not long since, to a conspiracy at Minas Geraes, remarkable, and indeed formidable, as some of the principal officers of the government there, both laity and clergy, entered into it. It appears that the troops sent from Portugal were seldom afterwards called home. The civil officers, except the viceroy, were allowed also to be stationary. These persons, tho natives generally of Portugal, soon changed their original affection for the mother country into an attachment to that, where they were likely to spend their days; and were sometimes tempted to sacrifice to their own, the interest of their employers.
