In Bangar the English had formerly a factory; and in the city of Borneo there were still supposed to be some British subjects, either constantly residing there, or occasionally trading to it. Nothing would be more desirable, or more consistent with the general object of the mission, than any fair and peaceable endeavour to spread the use of British manufactures throughout every part of Asia, from whence any valuable return might be made to Europe, which was eminently the case of Borneo. The jealousy of the Dutch traders might be in the way in some parts of that extensive country; but others might be found where there was less a likelihood of interfering with them.
