It was determined, therefore, to continue the voyage to Chu-san, where there was a greater likelihood of effectual assistance. The distance was short, the season favourable; and in the former passage it had been found, that “in no part of the world was the sea so clear of danger as from Chu-san to the river of Tien-sing.”
