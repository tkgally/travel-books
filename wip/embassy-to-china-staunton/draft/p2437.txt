Profane, but classic writers, mention the use of oil and salt, as common in the sacrifices of the ancient Europeans. Virgil describes Æneas as pouring oil on the slaughtered victims,

<verse>
“Oleum fundens ardentibus extis;”
</verse>

<verse>

</verse>

and Horace, the

<verse>
