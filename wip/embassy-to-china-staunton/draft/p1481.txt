During her absence the deputation mentioned at Chu-san, and another from the governor of the province, to the Embassador, had been on board the Lion. Presents of provisions were brought by both; and invitations given for his Excellency and his suite to partake of entertainments on shore, which he declined; alleging the necessity of the immediate prosecution of his voyage towards the Emperor's court.
