Fresh provisions were served daily to the Lion’s crew; the decks and beams washed with vinegar, and an allowance of it given to the men; fires made to air the ship; and the ventilators kept constantly at work. It was likewise found advantageous to send the invalids and convalescents from the ships ashore, to take air and exercise every day; the same was done at Angeree point, situated to the southward of point Nicholas, where the Dutch had a small battery of four guns, near a Malay village. Here indigo was manufactured from the leaf, growing in the neighbourhood. A bar at the mouth of the river at Angeree prevents the freedom of its current, and the lee of the indigo vats, thrown into it, must affect its good qualities; which circumstances render it inferior, as a watering place, to Nicholas bay.

The lowest order of the people on the coast did not appear to be obliged to perpetual labour, for the mere preservation of their existence; but had leisure, means, and disposition for amusements of different kinds. One of the Malay entertainments consists in the display of the various attitudes and postures of the same actor under different masks. By dint of exertions and of long practice, he had acquired such a power over the exterior muscles of his body, as to give to each an independent voluntary motion.
