The solitude was lovely, and as I dozed off I listened to the twittering of a few birds, while far away I could just hear the sleepy tinkle of a bullock-bell. There is no sound as sleepy as that slow tinkling when heard far off—<i>clangle, clangle, clangle,</i> and then silence, and again the sleepy noise as the beast moves slowly along munching the new grass.

It must have been some hours before I awoke, for the sun was getting low when I sprang up, collected my things, swung my swag over my back, and idled slowly along the banks of the river. The day was heavy, and I felt uncommonly tired in spite of my long sleep, so I looked with some longing for that clump of bush. I must have walked a good three miles before I caught sight of it.
