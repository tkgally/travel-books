“Now,” said Bones, “we ought to get at least five replies to that advertisement, which means we will have five pounds five to carry us on for a few weeks.”

In spite of all my protestations that I knew no more about elocution than he did about common sense, he took no notice, his only remark being:

“The advertisement is already in the paper, and to-morrow we will call for the answers.”

I noticed that Beans was keenly interested, and was licking his lips, whilst a new light shone in his eyes.

“By Jove!” he exclaimed, “if we can only get about a dozen pupils we can start a theatrical company and take them round the colony.”

I wondered if he was one of the stage-struck gentlemen. Suddenly he added:

“I say, Bones, now that you have settled us, what are you going to do yourself?”

“Don’t ask rude questions,” said Bones; “I will no doubt find something suitable to my taste in time.”
