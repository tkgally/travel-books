To look down and let the eye follow the rugged sides, all blue as if lit by some invisible light, and yet to have no idea what strange cause in nature is responsible for it; then to move on to less beautiful but more extraordinary pools where boiling water is rushing up from some hidden spring and gurgling as it reaches the top. The hot air steams up and the moonlight cuts through it and makes it dance like a mist. Around are drooping palms and great fern-trees whose shadows are cast in strange shapes, and the steam half hides them from your view. As we gazed at these weird things my companion began to shiver and held my arm a little tighter. Presently she became nervous, and suggested we should go back.
