If this hadn’t happened I should have been clean out of it, because they have prejudices, and, if I had been compelled to work in the town, the chances are I should have remained a working man, for no one in Christchurch would speak to a ’bus-driver or a carter, though they would in Auckland. Bones made several good friends there when he was a tram-conductor, and he kept the job for a whole month—which I believe was the longest term he spent at manual labour—because he liked being made a fuss of by the charming passengers. In Christchurch he would have been scorned, in spite of his aristocratic appearance and his navy-blue serge suit.

There are many interesting places round Christchurch, all worth going to see, and New Brighton is one of them. As its name implies, it is a seaside resort; it only lies a few miles out from the town, and there is more sand there than in any place I have seen; in fact, if you sit down on the beach when a high wind is blowing you will very soon be buried.
