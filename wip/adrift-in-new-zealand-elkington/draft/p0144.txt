I have started life a dozen times at least, and I know heaps of others who make a hobby of it. It is like going for a ramble in a new country without having the faintest idea what you are going to see, only knowing that everything will be new to you. It’s Nansenising. Change is the best pick-me-up in the world, and the man who goes the same walk every day at the same time, and sees the same people, is, in my opinion, on his death-bed; the life that was in him must be oozing out of him from sheer lack of stimulant, for when interest ceases death begins. And I am sure you can’t die as long as you keep on moving. I have seen people beginning to die years before they actually did; first they drop this little interest, then that, and so on, till all their interests are gone, and then, heigh-ho! it is time they went, for the world has no more use for them.
