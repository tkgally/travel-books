They are the curse of the country, and if I were the Premier I would class them as undesirables and keep them away. If I were at home and had a son I particularly disliked and wanted to ruin, I would send him out here and give him a remittance, and if that didn’t finish him I would believe I had misjudged him.”

“Here, sit down, old chap!” cried the languid one, “and try a cigarette. You are spoiling the music. You are so damned energetic—you don’t rest. Take example by the Governor there; you see he’s fast asleep.”

I glanced across in the direction in which my friend was looking, and saw an elderly man wearing a soft hat and a dark tweed suit of clothes. He was not asleep.

“Where is his house?” I asked.
