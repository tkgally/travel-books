I remember at the time I thought his philosophy was absurd, but since then I must confess that I have found his principle an excellent one, and in the various callings, professions, and trades which I have sampled I have invariably adopted it. What was also more to the point just then, it allowed me plenty of time to see things, and, when weighed in the balance, what are a few pounds a week more or less compared with the delight of seeing life and enjoying the endless beauties of this little world of ours?

No one need fear he will starve in New Zealand provided he does not let opportunities slip, and any one can make a living there if he does not mind working, be he a mechanic, a tradesman, or even a public schoolboy.

Halstead never worked in the afternoon unless it was thrust upon him. He knew dozens of good people, and spent his time after lunch in playing cricket and tennis, watching races, going to picnics, or reading at the library.
