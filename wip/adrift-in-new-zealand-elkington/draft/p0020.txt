“Not much.” The answer came sharply, and my companion relapsed into silence, whilst I returned to my contemplation of the changing coast-line. Suddenly I heard him speak again. “Some take to drink,” he said, “others go on the gum-fields—and then commit suicide.”

“You are a cheery person,” I remarked. “Perhaps you would not mind giving me an idea as to which of these lines you think I'll follow?”

The old man glanced at me with a stony stare.

“Take to drink first, and then——” but before he could finish his sentence the bell rang for breakfast, and in his eagerness for that meal he seemed to forget all about me, and hurried below.

Had I not been young and keen his prophecy might have damped my ardour for a new life amid new scenes, but as it was I laughed, and was soon making short work of a basin of weak porridge.
