After this work came the branding of the calves, which was a thankless job; a calf is small, but he can kick and struggle most fearfully, and when the hot iron gets on to his back and the smoke gets into your eyes the job becomes an uninteresting one; but still, it has to be done, so you do it and make merry over the task.

As soon as this is over the sheep begin their little escapades, and they require far more attention, as they not only keep you on the watch all day, before they lamb, but for a week or two after, and then the little ones have to have their waggling tails cut and their ears marked. There may be about 70,000 of them, so you can reckon on having a good many thousand to handle each day. And how they smell! This is a beastly job, and I never met any man who liked it.
