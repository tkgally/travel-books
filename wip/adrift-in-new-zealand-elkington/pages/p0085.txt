# Chapter IV

[[subtitle]] Auckland’s show places—Mount Eden—Some hints to librarians—The Museum, the Domain—Some newspapers and some experiences, and the reiteration of a well-proved fact.

T<sc>he</sc> chief show places in Auckland are the Museum, the Library, and Mount Eden, and of course the harbour. But if you are anxious to start with a good impression of the town, walk, drive, or cycle to the top of Mount Eden as soon as ever you can, as you are certain to be asked if you have been there, and if you have not, then say you have, or you will have to listen to indifferent descriptions of the beauty of the view from its summit. Mount Eden lies at the back of the town, and—dare I say it?—looks like an ordinary hill; it is of no great height, and, to the unsuspecting, not worth going up; but, when once on its crest, or, properly speaking, the edge of its crater, a finer view you could not wish to see.
