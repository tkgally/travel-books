A strange sensation of peace came over me. The imps of the bush went farther and farther away, and the harsh sounds became fainter and fainter. If this was death, I thought, how sweet it was!

It must have been hours afterwards, for the sun was well up in the heavens, when I came back to consciousness. At first a strange feeling of uncertainty possessed me, and I could not collect my thoughts. I wondered, in a vague way, where I was.

Little by little a feeling of unrest came over me, and I began to realise my position. The events of the past night crowded before me, till I remembered everything.

My hands were red with blood, and I became aware of a dull pain in my head. My hair was all clotted with blood, and the broken bough of a tree lay by my side; its branches half over my body.

The whole ghastly position was before me: I was lost in the bush. Weak, sore, and feeble, I rose slowly to my feet, and crawled to the little stream
