# Chapter XIV
[[subtitle]] Was it Fate?—The gum-fields and life story of a piece of gum—Aristocrats and the simple life—Diggers <i>[[corr verus|versus]]</i> Bushmen, and a brutal scene.

A <sc>great</sc> many people in this world consider that Fate is the ruling spirit of their lives, and if, by chance, they make fools of themselves, it is Fate they blame; others consider that Luck, good or bad, as the case may be, is their master; but who Mr. Fate is, or whence comes Mr. Luck, no one has ever told me, and I am glad I never had their tasks to perform. Fancy keeping in touch with a few hundred million different human beings! Nice job that.

Now, when I told Miss Frazer, during a chat over a delicious cup of tea, that I was going to a little township, about fifteen miles north of Auckland, she said “Ah!” and lifted her eyebrows, and the only answer I could find was:

“Well?”
