# Chapter XVI

[[subtitle]] More about the bush—The pleasures of a fire—I return to Auckland—What a boom is like—The end of the run.

I<sc>nside</sc> this long wooden shanty there were four men seated round a blazing log fire, and one of them was the “missing mate” for whom our friend had been looking. When we entered we were called to the fireside and asked for our story, which we told. They were a bright lot of fellows, all strapping great men, who looked as if they were made for heavy work. Two were Australians, our rescuer being of that nationality—as I had already guessed from his peculiar language—and the other three were New Zealanders.
