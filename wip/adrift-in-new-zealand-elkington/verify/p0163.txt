Another reason why it is tolerated and has to be continued, in spite of the agitation amongst squatters, is that there is at present no other way for the real workers to find out where employment is wanted, or, on the other hand, for settlers to get men if they need them. So the system continues, and the wicked flourish in order that the good may not suffer, which is not always the case—at least so I am told by our vicar.

But to return to where I left off in the last chapter. My companion, knowing the run of the ropes, said he would lead the way and do the necessary talking, to which proposal I agreed promptly.
