I have seen Auckland under many different conditions, but never in such a wild state of excitement as it was then. People were hurrying along the streets with eager faces; men were standing in groups discussing mines in excited tones, and the whole place had an air of business that had never shown itself before. Stocks and shares were on the lips of every one, and an old money-lender I knew told me he had never lent so much on pianos all the time he had been in Auckland as during the last few weeks of the boom.

Auckland was bitten, and every one was up to the hilt in shares.

The Stock Exchange, which at any other time looked like a deserted market-place, was a scene of the liveliest interest. Every available space in the town was let, and nearly every room in every hotel was occupied, and every one appeared to be gloriously prosperous.

I soon threw myself headlong into the swim, and in a few days was as mad and as happy as the rest.
