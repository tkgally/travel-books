“Mr. Shoemaker is a trained traveller. He has the time and the means to travel in luxury and he has developed his qualities as a keen observer by travels in various parts of the world. Hence he possesses in a very high degree the descriptive quality. He also sprinkles his pages with a certain descriptive humour, and endows them with a certain personal interest and charm that add to the attractiveness of his travel records. For the rest Mr. Shoemaker judiciously studs his pages with legend and historic incident; describes the charm of the old chateau; and completes the attractiveness of his book by the extensive and excellent series of photographs with which he illustrates a delightful tour.”—<i>The Guardian.</i>
