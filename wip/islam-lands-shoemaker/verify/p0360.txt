What a tour it has been, and it is but half over. What a gorgeous panorama has unrolled itself since we sailed from Marseilles four months ago. Beginning with a glimpse of the wrecked cities of Messina and Reggio as our ship steamed past them; then on through stately Egypt, and far south until the sadness and the brilliance of the Sudan surrounded us, giving place to the glittering Red Sea and gay city of the Caliphs; and then the joyous motor tour in Tunisia and Algeria, when Carthage, Tunis, Kärawân, Dougga, Timgad the magnificent, and fairy Biskra, with its Garden of Allah, succeeded each other and were followed by the grandeur of the Chabet Gorge, the African Corniche, and the beautiful Kabylia Mountains until Algiers was reached and passed and this beautiful run to Oran ended all. How it unrolls itself to our mind's eye as our ship leaves port and we realise that our journey is over.
