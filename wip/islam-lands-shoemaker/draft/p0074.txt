How profound the peace and repose of the city to-night! I feel tempted to mount yonder white camel asleep under a mimosa tree and so wander off into the town, anywhere, everywhere, and I could do so in safety, where but a few years ago my life would not have been worth purchase, might cross the river, and all alone, save for the camel and the moon, enter the very house of the Khalifa to be greeted with, “Have a whiskey and soda, old man.” All the grisly horrors of ten years are over and done with, at least while England stays here, and I know of no more profoundly peaceful scene than this spread out below me in the moonlight to-night.
