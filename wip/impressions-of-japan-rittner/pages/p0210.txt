These are the so-called blessings of modern civilisation. Japan, which is less civilised, is a prosperous country; it thrives on its art and manufactures; it has sufficient produce to satisfy the nation and even export the surplus; and why? because civilisation has not yet had time to prey on the country to such an extent as to destroy everything. To be out of work is an expression unheard of there. If the towns are stocked with people the surplus will go into the fields and till them, or into the rice field or tea plantations, anywhere so long as they can find work to do. They have no trades unions to fix the prices of their wages, no strikes—the natural consequence—to throw men and women out of work and on the charity of others. They work, and in that work they find enjoyment and are happy.
