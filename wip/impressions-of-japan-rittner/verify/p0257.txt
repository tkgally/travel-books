He built many temples all over the country, and some of the most celebrated ones. Though many sects of Buddhism were introduced into Japan, many others have sprung up since; but the new ones never entirely superseded the old, and consequently the Tendai and Shingon sects still survive. Though the sects may differ as regards worship, forms of ritual, and temple decorations, the doctrine of Nirvâna remains the same in all, and is the height to which all Buddhists aspire.
