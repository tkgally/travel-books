Lake Biwa was formed in the night of the winter season, <sc>b.c.</sc> 251, and in the same night the mountain Fujiyama arose from nothingness, thus equalising the forces of nature; so says the legend. It has the shape of a harp: Biwa. It is 38 miles long, and its greatest breadth—up north—is 13 miles. It is of surpassing beauty, and contains the eight famous views, called by the Japanese “Omi-Hakkei.” These are:

1.—The autumn moon from Ishiyama.

2.—The evening glow at Seta.

3.—The clear sky breeze at Awazu.

4.—Boats returning from Yabase,

5.—The sound of the evening bell at Miidera.

6.—Rain by night at Karasaki.

7.—Evening snow on Mount Hira.

8.—Wild geese alighting at Katata.
