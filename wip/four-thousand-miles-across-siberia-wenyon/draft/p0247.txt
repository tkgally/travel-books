There are over a hundred tanneries, and Tiumen leather is found in all parts of the country; but a considerable number of the hides prepared here are used in the town itself, which turns out every year not less than seventy thousand pairs of top-boots and three hundred thousand pairs of leather gloves.

But to emigrants and travellers Tiumen is chiefly interesting as the first place of embarkation on the navigable rivers of Siberia, and the eastern terminus of the railway across the Ural Mountains. The station is two miles distant from the town, and is an imposing building of red brick, much too large for the requirements of present traffic, which is limited to one arrival and one departure every twenty-four hours.

Our train left a little before midnight, and it took us, even by railway, two nights and a day to reach the Kama River, on the western side of the range. It was tantalizing to commence this journey in the dark—being impatient for a look at the mighty range of mountains which has been the scene of so many romantic stories.
