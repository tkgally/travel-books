## Numbers.

### Cardinal Numbers.

One, <i>Ichi</i>.

Two, <i>Ni</i>.

Three, <i>San</i>.

Four, <i>Shi</i>.

Five, <i>Go</i>.

Six, <i>Roku</i>.

Seven, <i>Shchi</i>.

Eight, <i>Hachi</i>.

Nine, <i>Ku</i>.

Ten, <i>Jiu</i>.

Eleven, <i>Jiu ichi</i>.

Twelve, <i>Jiu ni</i>.

Thirteen, <i>Jiu san</i>.

And so on to nineteen.

Twenty, <i>Ni jiu</i>.

Twenty-one, <i>Ni jiu ichi</i>.

Thirty, <i>San jiu</i>.

Forty, <i>Shi jiu</i>.

And so on to ninety.

Hundred, <i>Hyaku</i>.

One hundred, <i>Ippiaku</i>.

Two hundred, <i>Ni hyaku</i>.

Thousand, <i>Sen</i>.

One thousand, <i>Issen</i>.

Two thousand, <i>Ni sen</i>.

Ten thousand, <i>Man</i>.

Hundred thousand, <i>Jiu man</i>.

Million, <i>Hyaku man</i>.

Ten million, <i>Sen man</i>.

Thirty-eight million, <i>San-sen happyaku man</i>.

Billion, <i>Cho</i>.

### Multiplicative Numbers.

Once, <i>Ichido</i>.

Twice, <i>Ni do</i>.

Three times, <i>San do</i>.

Four times, <i>Yo tabi</i>.

Five times, <i>Go tabi</i>.

Six times, <i>Roku tabi</i>.

Seven times, <i>Shchi tabi</i>.

Eight times, <i>Hachi tabi</i>.

Nine times, <i>Ku tabi</i>.

Ten times, <i>Jittabi</i>.

Double, <i>Bai</i> or <i>Nibai</i>.

Triple, <i>Sam bai</i>.

### Months.

D<sc>ate</sc> (month and day), <i>gappi</i>.

January, <i>Sho gatsu</i>.

February, <i>Ni</i> “

March, <i>San</i> “

April, <i>Shi</i> “

May, <i>Go gatsu</i>.

June, <i>Roku</i> “

July, <i>Shchi</i> “

August, <i>Hachi</i> “

September, <i>Ku gatsu</i>.

October, <i>Jiu</i> “

November, <i>Jin ichi</i> “

December, <i>Jiu ni</i> “

### Days of the Week.

Sunday, <i>Nichi yobi</i>.

Monday, <i>Gatsu</i> or <i>Getsu yobi</i>.

Tuesday, <i>Ka yobi</i>.

Wednesday, <i>Sui yobi</i>.

Thursday, <i>Moku yobi</i>.

Friday, <i>Kin yobi</i>.

Saturday, <i>Do yobi</i>.
