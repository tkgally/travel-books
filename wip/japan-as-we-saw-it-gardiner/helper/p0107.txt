[[image: jinrikisha with passenger and puller on a road winding through a dense bamboo forest]]

Among the pleasant one-day trips is that to Mogi <i>(Moh-jee)</i>. With two coolies to a jinrikisha, we ascend the mountain side back of the town by the densely populated road passing the Bronze Horse Temple.

Before reaching the summit, from the turnings of the highway we get picturesque views of the town beneath and behind us, the expanse of vision being increased as we ascend and come upon each successive turn, until entering a deep cut near the top.
