## Hours.

<i>Ichi-ji</i>, one o’clock.

<i>Ni-ji</i>, two o’clock.

<i>San-ji jip-pun</i>, ten minutes past three.

<i>Yo-ji jiu-go-fun</i>, fifteen minutes past four.

<i>Jiu-ji han</i>, half past ten.

<i>Jiu-ichi-ji shi-jiu-go-fun</i>, fifteen minutes to eleven.

<i>Jiu-ni-ji jiu-go-fun mae</i>, fifteen minutes to twelve.

<i>Han-ji kan</i>, half an hour.

<i>Jiu-go-fun</i>, a quarter of an hour.
