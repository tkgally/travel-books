# Nikko.

FROM our hotel in Tokio jinrikishas convey us in thirty-five minutes to Uyeno station, from whence trains of the Nippon Railway leave for Nikko at 8.50 and 11.35 a.m., covering the intervening ninety-one miles in five hours and five minutes (fare $2.78). While waiting in the station entrance while Shimid procured tickets, a police officer demanded our passport, a performance to be repeated by others many times in the future.
