[[image: a rider on a dark horse crossing a grassy hillside, with fir trees on the skyline]]
[[caption]] On a Good Trail

Alexander said that the trail to Stewart’s Lake was open and the ground good, and that we might march fast; but we were still weak and did not relish the idea, and were very much pleased when he volunteered to accompany us, bringing some horses of his own in order that we might ride. As soon as we had got a sufficient quantity of provisions we left the fort, and travelled gaily along the trail, driving the pack-horses. We were still very unwell, so that riding was by no means a pleasure for the first three days, and the poor Doctor suffered tortures, and bumped breathless upon an air-cushion.
