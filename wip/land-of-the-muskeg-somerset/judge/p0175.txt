It is exceedingly difficult to get a shot at these animals, as they are extraordinarily wary. Having found the ‘lodge,’ the hunter chooses a convenient spot on the bank, and sits with his rifle on his knee waiting for the beaver to appear. Two, and sometimes three, hours may pass before the beavers move; then they come out and swim near the bank,
