The nights were clear and frosty, but when the rain held off we slept in the open under our mosquito-nets, as we found this much more pleasant than huddling together in a tent. We could not march fast, as the horses were heavily laden and out of condition, so that it was not until the third day that we crossed the Rat River and started into the poplar forest.
