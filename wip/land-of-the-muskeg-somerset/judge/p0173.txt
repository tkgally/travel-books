[[cont]]failure we gave up hunting in the bush as a useless waste of time, and moved on towards the more open mountains, where we hoped to have a better chance of success. By this time we had finished all the meat (in the course of three days), and had returned to our diet of bread and fat, and so were using up our flour far more quickly than we should. One day Daukhan managed to kill two small beavers, but these did not last us long, so he turned his attention to the river again, in the hope of discovering another dam. In many places we found open paths, about three feet wide, in the woods leading down to the water, which had been made by the beavers as they dragged the logs towards the river. Daukhan said he had watched them many times, and that they would sometimes carry logs more than sixteen feet long for a mile
