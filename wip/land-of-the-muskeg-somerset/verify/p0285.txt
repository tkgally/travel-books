On the morning of our departure from the fort we found our canoe-men in high spirits, for they had been gambling the greater part of the night, and had won heavily. The unfortunate natives of the place came down to see us off, and told sadly how one had lost a fiddle, another a shirt, another a frying-pan, and so on. Towards afternoon we Cottonwood Cañon, and halted by the bank to inspect it. I have passed over the details of the shooting of the Fort George Rapids, but the Cottonwood made so deep an impression on me that I must attempt to describe it.
