## The Pioneer Series.

12mo, cloth, 3s. net; or, paper covers, 2s. 6d. net.

<i>The Athenæum.</i>—“If this series keeps up to the present high level of interest, novel readers will have fresh cause for gratitude to Mr. Heinemann.”

<i>The Daily Telegraph.</i>—“Mr. Heinemann's genial nursery of up-to-date romance.”

<i>The Observer.</i>—“The smart Pioneer Series.”

<i>The Manchester Courier.</i>—“The Pioneer Series promises to be as original as many other of Mr. Heinemann's ventures.”

JOANNA TRAILL, SPINSTER. By <sc>Annie E. Holdsworth.</sc>

<i>The Observer.</i>—“Every word tells that it is the work of a true woman, who has thought deeply and lovingly on a most painful subject. . . . The picture is a beautiful one, which it would be well for many women to ponder over. In her claim for wider sympathy, a higher understanding of right and wrong, and her noble picture of woman helping woman, the authoress has done a good work.”

GEORGE MANDEVILLE'S HUSBAND. By <sc>C. E. Raimond.</sc>

<i>The Spectator.</i>—“This very clever and terse story. . . . Mr. Raimond is undoubtedly an artist of great power. He certainly understands women's distinctive graciousness and ungraciousness as few women of the advanced type appear to understand it.”

<i>The Pall Mall.</i>—“Clever, biting, and irresistible.”

THE WINGS OF ICARUS. By <sc>Laurence Alma Tadema.</sc>

<i>The Daily Telegraph.</i>—“An intensely pathetic tale of passionate love and ineffable self-sacrifice. . . . Nothing has been more impressively told in the pages of modern fiction than the <i>dénouement</i> of this sad but deeply fascinating story.”

THE GREEN CARNATION. By <sc>R. S. Hichens.</sc>

<i>The World.</i>—“‘The Green Carnation’ is brimful of good things, and exceedingly clever. It is much more original, really, than its title implies. The character sketches are admirable and are probably drawn from the life.”

<i>The Observer.</i>—“The book is a classic of its kind.”

AN ALTAR OF EARTH. By <sc>Thymol Monk.</sc>

<i>The Speaker.</i>—“It is not merely clever, but pathetic and natural.”

A STREET IN SUBURBIA. By <sc>E. W. Pugh.</sc>

THE NEW MOON. By <sc>C. E. Raimond.</sc>
