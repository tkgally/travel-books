We were in Edmonton a full week, and though most hospitably welcomed in that flourishing sentinel town, it is to be said that our plans were received with derision. What folly to go to certain misery and failure, when by staying where we were we could get the finest wild-fowl shooting in the world! Then there were any number of black bears and, if I remember rightly, deer, moose, and a whispered rumour of buffalo, and all in a neighbourhood teeming with comfort. It is always so in my experience in a frontier town.
