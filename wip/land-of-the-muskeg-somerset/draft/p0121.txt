Wherever we camped we were always much annoyed by ants, which swarm throughout the whole country, so that even when protected by a net from mosquitoes and other flying insects, one’s bed was always invaded by hosts of creeping things. Without mosquito-nets life would be unendurable, and a man once stricken down by illness would most likely be worried to death. Even the moose and deer in the country are often killed by bull-dog flies and mosquitoes, their blood being sucked away until they succumb through exhaustion.
