But when he began to grow old his son had usurped his place as chief, and when he died the old man was too feeble to take up the reins of government again, and so authority had died out in the tribe, and the great man had fallen upon the charity of the Hudson’s Bay Company. When we saw him he was entirely blind, and quite awful to look upon. He was in his wheel-barrow going up to his meal at the fort.
