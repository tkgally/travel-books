On the following morning we began packing the horses. Now packing is an art that may not be learned in a short time. Pollen and I had had a considerable amount of practice both in the southern Rockies and in California. Round, Daukhan, and John were experts. For my part I confess that I had acquired but a slight knowledge of the art, as I have never been either strong or heavy enough to get the requisite pull on the lash-rope. And so it happened that the task of packing fell to the other four, whilst Ramsey, the Doctor and I busied ourselves with the other duties of camp. And there was much to be done.
