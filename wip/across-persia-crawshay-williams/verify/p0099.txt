The irony, too, how pathetic!—that proud King trampling underfoot the Emperor of the great Roman world, and dumbly watching the ruins of his former glory growing ever more desolate, the sons of his great people always descending the scale of mankind, his pomp and power growing perpetually more remote and more unreal.
