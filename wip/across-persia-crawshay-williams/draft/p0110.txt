Leopards were more likely than ghosts, but neither seemed sufficiently probable to keep me from going in, so I laughed at their fears and went on. After some murmuring, they consented to follow me, and when, in the centre of the great subterranean hall which I found within, I fired off a flashlight in order to obtain a photograph,
