It was hard to leave, just realizing what an infinitesimal morsel I had seen, and what volumes could be read from those great stone pages of the past. But the sun was sinking: I had only time for a visit to the north tomb, with its two sepulchres and rounded roof; for one last look at the splendid vista of steps, rising in a gathering beauty, to be crowned at the summit by the vision of those grim beasts that guard the wonderful terraces, and then I looked my last on Persepolis.

<verse>
‘I saw the solitary Ringdove there,
And “Coo, coo, coo,” she cried; and “Coo, coo, coo.”’
</verse>

The lines haunted my brain as the tall pillars waned and faded, until at length the hill-side hid them from my sight.
