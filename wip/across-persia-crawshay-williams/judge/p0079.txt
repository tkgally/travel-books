I did not shoot my partridge; it disappeared up the mountains and into the dusk; but I was rewarded for my chase by a magnificent view of the broad sea-like plain below me, fading dimly away to the real sea into which, as I looked, sank the red rim of the sun.

Daliki lay below, clad in a blue haze, and through the dark <i>meidan</i> was drawn the silver thread of my beloved river.
