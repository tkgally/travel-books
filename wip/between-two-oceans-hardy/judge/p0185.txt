‘He lived in fine style when he had his wives here with him,’ they said. He has two wives, who are now in China. He has also a history. Some three years ago a reward was offered in China Town for his life, he having given some offence to some unknown person in authority. Such things are not uncommon. We saw one day in a Joss-house an inscription, which, being translated to us, was a promise of the favour of the great Joss to any person or persons who should remove from this life two given individuals who had the misfortune of being obnoxious to Joss. Sam Lee was in the way, and a reward was offered for his being put out of the way. He very sensibly did not wait to be violently removed, but took himself out of the way, and returned to China with his wives.

He came back to San Francisco in time, having, we may presume, received some intimation that he was no longer an obstacle to be got rid of.
