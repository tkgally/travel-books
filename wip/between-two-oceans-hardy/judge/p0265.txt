The next day our faces fell when we greeted him. He appeared in a ‘boiled shirt’ (Western for a linen front) and white collar and cuffs. He had also been to a barber’s and got himself ‘shaven and shorn.’ As a civilized gentleman, he was now presentable; but, alas! for the ideal frontiersman of yesterday, he was picturesque no more.
