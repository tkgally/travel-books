At every station the train-boys boarded the car with ‘Latest dispatches;’ every traveller had his evening paper; we had ours; every eye sought eagerly the latest item:

[[center]] <i>Special Dispatch from Delaware.</i>

‘President’s train just passed. President doing well. He has asked to have the speed of the train increased.’

[[center]] <i>Latest from Philadelphia.</i>

‘Arrived safely at Philadelphia. Pulse 102.’

And I think not a heart but brightened with sympathetic gladness as we read,

‘He bears the transfer remarkably well.’

‘Will he live?’ was then the question in every mouth, and none knew surely what was the answer the next few days would give.
