# Californian Sketches.

## I.—In ‘The Golden City.’

A<sc>rriving</sc> in San Francisco by the Overland Route impresses us as a coming back to the world again after a lapse into the wilderness. For, in leaving the wonderful Phœnix City of Chicago for the West, it seemed that we left the world behind; in Omaha, our next stage, we felt as if we had got to the outside fringe of civilisation and cultivation, and wondered vaguely what could be beyond? into what wild exile were we about to plunge?

Beyond Omaha come four long days and nights of speeding across the seemingly limitless desolation of the prairies—the barren uplands of the Rocky Mountains—the wide waste of the sage-brush and alkali desert.
