I was sitting in the Japanese sandals which the train boy had provided, and they were very comfortable for the feet. I am bringing a pair home. I saw the lotus flower in bloom on the way here. I wish you could see the wonderful wistaria vines I have seen in Japan—some of them over one hundred years old, and simply enormous. A. C. H. came over this morning to help me pack. I leave to-morrow for Northern Japan to visit Sendai.
