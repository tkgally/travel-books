After the flag dance; the girls sat down and smoked their pipes contentedly. It was not so dreadful. The pipe only held as much tobacco as you could balance on the end of the nail your little finger. They got just two puffs out of it, I believe. It is about like their innocent tea. It is amusing to see how people spend money in the shops here. They just can’t help it. The shop agents hover around the hotel, and follow people to their rooms, show them pretty things, and coax them into buying. I told the englishman about the bamboo-shop. He went there and came back loaded down. I think he left fifteen or twenty dollars at the shop before he got through. I eat regularly here four poached eggs, along with steak or chicken, for breakfast, which shows my appetite is good. But then I am workiug. Just think of the appetite I have developed in writing these letters!
