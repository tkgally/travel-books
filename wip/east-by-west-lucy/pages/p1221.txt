It simply came to this, that the God of Happiness, of his bounty, and incited by many prayers and offerings, had crowned the pleasure and excitement of the day by dropping in among the counter attractions of the booths three ladies in strange garb, and the most must be made of the opportunity. The women gathered about and stared with undisguised curiosity. They furtively felt the material of dresses and cloaks, and were particularly struck by the arrangements of the back hair. Their general impression appeared to be one of good-humoured astonishment, not unmingled with pity for unfortunate persons of their sex who, either from necessity or choice, thus attired themselves.

By slow degrees we reached the temple steps, and stood under the shadow of its overhanging roof. Before the temple is a red wooden structure of two stories, designed as an entrance gate. A number of large sandals were hung up before images of the Two Heavenly Kings.
