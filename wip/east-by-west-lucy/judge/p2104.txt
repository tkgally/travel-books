[[cont]]Shintoism and Buddhism. In 1881, of the Shinto Kiyodoshiyoku, or Shinto preachers, the number of male preachers is 17,756, and that of female preachers 95. Of the number of disciples, males are 1299, females 3. Of the Buddhist Kiyodoshiyoku, or Buddhist preachers, males are 75,144, females 1131; and of the number of their disciples, males are 19,664, females 1347. The lower classes of people are generally believers in Buddhism.

<i>Annual Income and Expenditure.</i>—The income for the fiscal year ending in June, 1881, was 63,320,565 yen, and the expenditure of the same period 63,170,893 yen. The estimated income for the year ending in June, 1882, was 68,573,995 yen, and that for the year ending in June, 1883, 66,814,122 yen.
