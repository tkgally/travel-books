[[cont]]Let them alone. They have toiled much and long in an alien land, bearing the insults and often the cuffs of a race they despise. Now they have made their little heap of money, and are going back to spend it with their families and with the sweet certainty that their bones shall rest in their own land. There will be labour again when the voyage is over and they land in Hongkong. In the meanwhile, let them

<verse>
Muse and brood and live again in memory,
With those old faces of their infancy,
Heap’d over with a mound of grass,
Two handfuls of white dust shut in an urn of brass.
</verse>
