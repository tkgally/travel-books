Among the sources of revenue, the most important is the land tax. Ten years ago the source of revenue had been land tax alone. Although the land tax has gradually been reduced through the land tax reform, and the system of imposing other kinds of taxes has been introduced, still at present land tax amounts to 57/100 of the total revenue.
