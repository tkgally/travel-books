Bombay was delighted. From a commonplace town it was growing into a beautiful city, compared with which Paris under the wand of Baron Hausmann would have to take second rank. Then the bills began to come in, and there followed a period of consternation, broken by a blast of indignation. The popular ædile became the execrated spoiler. He stood gallantly to his post for some time, asserting the inviolability of his office; but the wrath of the taxpayer prevailed, and finally the once autocratic Commissioner was smuggled out of Bombay something after the fashion in which Sir John Falstaff escaped from Dame Quickly’s in the hour of peril.
