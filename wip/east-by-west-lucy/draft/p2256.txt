One morning, in spite of all precaution, a shot found its way through one of the blocked-up windows, and a deep hole low down on the opposite wall shows where it landed. No one was hurt, but one lady died of fright.

Food and water were brought to the prisoners through a secret underground passage communicating with the Tykhana. Like the house of Dr. Frayrer, this carries proof of the fury of the mutineers when they leaped over the mud walls of the entrenchment and entered the silent and deserted Residency. Having no English men and women to slash and hack, they turned with impotent fury upon the very stone staircases of the Tykhana and broke them down.

Here is the guard-room, next door to the fatal breakfast-room, where six soldiers were buried alive in the ruins created by a shell. Here is the tower, honeycombed with cannon-shot, on the top of which day after day some gallant officer volunteered to stand, telescope in hand, and report the movements of the mutineers.
