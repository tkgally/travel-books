[[cont]] all very well to say the babies must be carried out—but where to begin? To make a wholesale raid upon them would have had as much appreciable effect as attempting to empty the Serpentine with a bucket. Accordingly, in spite of the high authority invoked, the babies, with the exception of the few prematurely choked, remained and wailed, their united voices frequently drowning that of the President of the Apostles, and throughout the whole of his address and of others that followed prevented people beyond the middle of the hall hearing a single consecutive sentence.
