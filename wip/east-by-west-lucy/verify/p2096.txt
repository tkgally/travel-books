# Chapter VI.

[[subtitle]] The New Empire in the West.

D<sc>uring</sc> my stay in Japan, I had the advantage of many interesting conversations, both with Mr. Inouyé and Mr. Ito, on the condition and prospects of this interesting country. In order to enable me to acquire full and accurate knowledge on the subject, the Foreign Minister laid all the departments of the State under contribution, and I received from each statements which contain the latest and most accurate statistics of the trade, commerce, and general condition of the empire. I have thrown them together in the present chapter.

<i>Imperial Family and Government.</i>—The dynasty of the empire of Japan was established by the Emperor Jimmu, in 660 <sc>b.c.</sc>, and to the throne have succeeded, from generation to generation, the same dynasty without interruption up to the present time.
