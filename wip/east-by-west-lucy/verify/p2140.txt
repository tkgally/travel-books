The policemen wear a modification of their British brethren’s uniform, one detail being that it is apparently optional with them to wear stockings. Some do and some do not. A pretty sharp contrast passed me in High Street. A tricycle came along, and on it was seated a grave and reverend signor, in yellow turban, white jacket, red shirt, a paper umbrella, and bare brown legs.

In spite of tricycles, High Streets, water-pipes, lamp-posts, and police in uniform, Singapore is intensely tropical. The atmosphere is something that one never looks through elsewhere. The figures that throng its sunny streets are all tropical. Europeans dress in white duck suits with straw hats and umbrellas. The native men dress in as little as possible. The Chinese come out in cool costumes of white, or of that rich blue, the making of which and transference to calico stuffs seems to be one of their secrets.
