There hang over the stream graceful trees, unknown in England, with delicate foliage like maidenhair fern, of every shade of colour, from deepest gold to daintiest green. Through the gorge, winding at every few yards, the train glides along at a pleasant driving pace, giving time to enjoy all the beauty spread abroad. Nearly always we have the river, for which and the track there is just room enough in the cañon. All through the long morning the crisp mountain air is full of sunshine, and even when the sun goes down and the moon and the stars come out over the plains there is a deep blue sky framing the ever varying picture.

At midnight on the far horizon towards which we were speeding a new and startling light flamed forth. It was too low to be a constellation, and out of the way of the aurora borealis. As we drew nearer it spread in extent, and the smoke about it began to form a cloud. It looked like a burning city; but it was only a stubble field, and this was one of the peaceful processes of Western agriculture. In this happy land straw is not worth the trouble of reaping.
