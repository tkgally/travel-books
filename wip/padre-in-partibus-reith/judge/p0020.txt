The letters that are following were originally published in the <i>Singapore Free Press</i>, between March and June 1896, and were all written at or near the places described, so that the impressions came hot from the branding. A few trifling changes have been made in the text, and some explanatory notes added; otherwise the letters appear as first printed.

E<sc>dinburgh</sc>, <i>January</i>, 1897.
