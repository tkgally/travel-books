[[cont]]It was larger and higher than the average Balinese hut, and had more wood and less mud about it. It was shaded almost to darkness by great trees that grew beside it. We were greeted by two pariah dogs, and an old man, who was evidently impressed by the fact that strangers had come to see the place where the clever but eccentric <i>orang Blanda</i>[[fnref *]] had resided so long.

From Bali to Lombok is a voyage of six or eight hours. We left Boeleling on the evening of February 27th, so as to be at Ampenan by daylight next morning. Along the Bali shore as we left, we saw the flash of innumerable Chinese crackers, and the brilliance of the stars was temporarily eclipsed by sheaves of rockets that rose from the streets of Boeleling. A few hours later we were watching Nature’s fireworks, with deeper interest, for as we coasted along Bali, making for the Lombok Straits, the clouds in the south were ruddy with the reflected glare of an invisible crater on the island.[[fnref †]]
