Signs of the scourge that yearly devastates the banks of the Menam were apparent in the bottles of cholera-mixture standing on desk and toilet-table. It is the way of the European to be prepared for the worst, though it seems going to meet the trouble half way. Still with the cholera raging violently around and not confining itself to Asiatics, it is almost impossible to keep a calm and equable mind, and quite impossible to restrain a feeling of alarm at the slightest touch of <i>sakit prut.</i>[[fnref *]]
