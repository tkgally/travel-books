Seventy-five guilders, or six guineas, ought to cover the expenses of the whole trip. I strongly recommend English tourists in his part of the world to consult Mr. Henderson, of Messrs. Henderson & Co., who has a large English store in sourabaya. He has a good knowledge of Eastern Java, and is able to give the best advice as to exeursions and routes. I hope he will forgive my mentioning his name in this connection, but it is the only return I can make for the valuable advice and assistance I received from him. One last piece of advice—Take as little baggage as possible, and don't forget to put some warm clothing in it.

Sourabaya,

April 3rd, 1896.
