Mexico, as it now is, forms the north part of Central America; on its north lie Texas, New Mexico, Arizona, Upper California; on its west is the Pacific Ocean; on its east the Gulf of Mexico; on the south it is bounded by Guatemala and British Honduras.

Its area is about seven hundred and forty-four thousand square miles; its population between nine and ten millions, about one-half being pure Indians, the remainder Spanish and other European races, half-breeds, negroes, and others.

About half the country is within the limits of the temperate zone, the other between the tropics; about its centre is that vast plateau, the table land, in parts from six to eight thousand feet above the sea; along this plain, which is formed by their expansion, runs from north-west to south-east, a lofty chain of mountains, part of the great range of the Andes,
