Here you can buy anything, from a brougham to a diamond ring; from a piano to a dressing-case. Money is advanced upon anything and everything, and great bargains are to be picked up occasionally. We were politely shown about the building. As most likely to be interesting, some of the most costly sets of jewels were taken from their strong places and shown to us. Their time for sale had not yet come; some of the sets were very handsome, and had been pledged for many thousand dollars, shown by a ticket annexed to them; they were all old fashioned.

My party one day, without me, went to Guadalupe, and during the journey one of them, L——, had her pocket picked, the only theft we have suffered from as yet. She wore an open pocket, on what Jack would call the starboard quarter of her dress, a pocket borrowed in pattern from the watch pocket of the old four-post bedstead. Into this, when she entered the street car for Guadalupe, was stuffed a pocket-book loaded with seven silver dollars, a pair of gloves, and a handkerchief.
