These gentlemen remain on board to dinner, and the consequence to me is a very pleasing two hours’ conversation with them.

Speaking of the ruins of ancient buildings in Mexico, Dr. T—— tells us that there lives in Vera Cruz a Mexican gentleman who has made the antiquities of his country his especial study; that he has deciphered the inscriptions found at Palenque, Uxmal, and other places; that he has discovered that the Aztec calendar stone built into the cathedral at Mexico is only calculated and intended for half the year, and that he found the stone for the other half in the woods near Tuxpan, imbedded in the earth; that he has many curious manuscripts and other relics connected with the ancient history of the country, and if I land to-morrow an hour’s talk with him may be attainable.
