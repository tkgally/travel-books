# Letter No. 5.

[[right]] H<sc>otel</sc> I<sc>turbide</sc>, C<sc>ity of</sc> M<sc>exico</sc>,

[[right]] 7th March.

ON Tuesday last we had an expedition on the canal, a party from the American Consulate accompanying us. Driving up the paseo de la Viga, and passing a square wherein stood a bust of Guatemozin (there is none of Montezuma anywhere), we reached a bridge crossing the canal which runs into, or rather out of Lake Chalco, and getting into a large flat bottomed boat, with comfortable seats and an awning, were quietly poled along by a couple of Indians for some miles, to a village called Ixtacalco, where was a very old church built by Cortés.
