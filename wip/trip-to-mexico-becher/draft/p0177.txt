To make obligatory education effective, penalties and rewards have been enacted, “the first consisting in the privation of certain offices and rights in regard to the fathers of families, who fail to comply with the law, the second limited to exemption from active service in the National Guard, and from the payment of any taxes arising therefrom.”

We have admired the alameda and its rows of cocoa-trees, and the Indian laurels in the gardens of the plaza, and bought a lot of chocolate, famously good here, to take home with us.

The rest of the party have “retired,” as our American cousins call going to bed, long since; and scribbling far into the night, I acknowledge to myself that the city of the “True Cross,” or this its hotel, or both, are hot and stuffy.
