I have omitted to tell you that opals are plentiful here and very cheap; the best we have seen for sale have been those brought to the hotel; many of them are large, but none are extraordinary otherwise.

We are very comfortable here and shall leave the Iturbide with regret; its restaurant and its cookery are excellent; the <i>chef</i>, a Frenchman, always honours us by bringing in what he thinks the best dish himself, and everybody in the house is attentive and obliging.

To-morrow, we leave for Puebla.
