[[cont]]Tuxpan; and on Friday morning, all going well, we are to be at Vera Cruz.

The Gulf is as calm as can be desired, and nearly as blue as the Bay of Naples in its best mood. We like our ship, our captain, our purser, <i>almost</i> like the whole opera troupe, who appear everywhere, but we <i>rather</i> wish they didn’t smoke <i>quite</i> so much and hadn’t <i>quite</i> so many dogs.

Have they heard, we wonder, of that troupe travelling in Mexico not many years since who were robbed and stripped, and whose prima donna perched on a rock, was made to sing her best for the amusement of their robbers.

We have just passed large patches of muddy water in a space of about two miles in this deep bright blue sea, which cause some speculation among the passengers; one, whose nationality is settled at once, suggests that the big fishes, &c., are holding a democratic meeting, far down below; the captain says when he passed here last, there were patches of petroleum on the water; my conclusion is, volcanic disturbances.
