A fire in the evening would be very nice to have, for we are, so to speak, in a well—a deep cellar, walled up with high mountains; but no fire can be had: there is no fire-place.

England has no representative in Mexico, and the United States minister is absent; but Mrs. F—— shows us much hospitality and kindness, enables us to see something of the English-speaking society, and is especially good to T——, who, but for this, would know much less of Mexican manners, customs, and shops.

My letter is more than long enough. Addio!
