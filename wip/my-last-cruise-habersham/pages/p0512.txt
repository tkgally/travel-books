And so we returned on board with our wearied limbs and disabled boat, and left old Bruin to drag his battered hull to some quiet corner, there to stuff his wounds with leaves and growl through the long and feverish night.

The next day was unfortunately beautifully clear,—“just the weather for surveying;” and so we continued our work and left our wounded foe to whiten upon the mountain’s side, or drag through heavy weeks and months of slow recovery.
