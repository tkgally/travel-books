Some of them were half flying, half swimming through the mud, weeds, and water of the field; others striking out like good fellows across a little creek that separated them from their home. All seemed anxious to arrive first; and, as they gained the boat’s side, they tumbled in, heels over head, without the least apparent regard to life or limb. I watched, with the pilot, to see one of them slapped; but, to his evident chagrin, there seemed to be no ‘last duck’ that time.”

So much for Southern China and ducks. That same night we anchored in Hong-Kong, and began to count the hours that were yet to pass before our departure for more northern latitudes.
