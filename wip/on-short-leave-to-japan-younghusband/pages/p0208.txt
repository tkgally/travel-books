A high garden wall was the mild Mikado’s only defence. Where might is right, and, despite arbitration companies and peace societies, big battalions will in all probability rule the world to the end, it is not difficult to imagine how low the power of the Mikado must have sunk, before the daily growing evidence of the physical might of his vassal. As dissimilar as the Mikado was from the Shogun, so unlike were his followers to those of the military dictator. Here mild and peaceful nobles, with lineage of untold antiquity, but whose inclinations lay in the direction of the cloister rather than the clash of arms, took the place of the stalwart daimios, to whom war was as the breath of life. The latter appeared mere <i>parvenus</i>, hired cut-throats of the Shogun, to the ancient nobility.
