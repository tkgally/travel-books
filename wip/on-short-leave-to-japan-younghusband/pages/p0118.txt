At the appointed moment the little fellow, with childish inconsequence, trotted up and presented the dagger to the old noble, who was now kneeling stripped to the waist. As laid down by rule, he stabbed himself below the waist on the left side and drew the knife across. But here the dramatic effect to us was spoilt, for, instead of doing the deed in silence, as the brave fellow above, he must needs take this highly inopportune and uncomfortable moment to make a long speech. We had noted him all along as being a very garrulous and talkative old man, but with nine and a half inches of steel in his interior he fairly surpassed himself. Most people in the audience would have given twenty cents to see him pass away quietly—we would have given a dollar gladly. At last he finished, and the little fellow the <i>kaishaku</i> raised his sword to give the death-blow.
