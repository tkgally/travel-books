For ten cents the lady kindly consented to perform, and we came to the conclusion that it was ten cents very easily earned. The dance consisted of walking slowly and demurely to the front of the stage, shaking a large rattle three or four times with one hand, and then a fan three or four times with the other. Then right-about turn, and a demure retirement to the back of the shed. The same simple manœuvre is executed over and over again, as long as one cares to stop and see it. When all other trades fail, I am thinking of setting up as a sacred dancer myself.
