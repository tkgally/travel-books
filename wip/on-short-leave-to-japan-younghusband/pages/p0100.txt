This is Binzuru. Now, Binzuru was a gentleman who, though he had taken the vow of chastity, so far forgot himself as to pass a remark on the beauty of one of the lady votaries. For this highly reprehensible conduct, it was ordered by Buddha that he should be placed outside the chancel; but, by way of tempering the wind to the shorn lamb, to him was ascribed the useful attribute of having the automatic power of curing all diseases. The true believer has, for instance, only to rub Binzuru's jaw, and then his own, to be immediately cured of the toothache. As we stood by we saw him in constant use: some had head-aches, some back-aches, some leg-aches, some stomach-aches, and some aches all over.
