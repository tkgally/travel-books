[[cont]]Manilla cigars at their best and cheapest. They vary from one and a half dollars to six dollars a hundred. An excellent cigar can be had at the rate of three dollars a hundred. It seems impossible to get a good Manilla cigar in England, or in India; yet here they are, of the finest quality, and simply pining for purchasers. I have heard it gravely stated that there is opium in Manilla cigars, and that consequently they give you a headache. As my friend the tobacconist remarked, ‘they would be worth a shilling a-piece if there was opium in them: opium is as dear as gold.’

We were much tempted to continue our journey to Yokohama by the <i>Empress of China</i>, one of the magnificent new ships running in connection with the Canadian Pacific Railway; but unfortunately the return journey would not fit in, so we decided for the Messageries Maritimes line. We chanced across the <i>Caledonien</i>, a very large and comfortable ship with only six first-class passengers on board.
