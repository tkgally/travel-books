We all had an ugly few minutes, and then a sharp-eyed sailor discovered that it was the keel of a capsized vessel of some sort drifting across our stern. Universal thanksgiving, and more breakfast. A cyclone generally lasts for twelve hours or so, and then moves off elsewhere; or rather it is moving all the time, thus—

[[image: diagram of a series of overlapping circular loops with arrows, showing a cyclone's rotating and drifting path]]

round and round, and yet drifting all the time. Our cyclone, however, lasted three days and three nights, with pouring, driving rain the whole time.
