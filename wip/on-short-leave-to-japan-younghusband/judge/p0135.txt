Again, a lady who had been at the hotel before wrote asking about rooms. Out comes the <i>Polite Writer.</i> Here we are—the very thing! ‘From a Gentleman to a Lady whom he has met only once.’ Met once before—most appropriate! Needless to say, the letter is a highly erotic effusion ending: ‘Pleading your beauty and grace of person, and charms of manner and mind, as my excuse for venturing to write to you, I remain, Madam, etc.’ As may be imagined, this made a very telling and effective ending to the reply about the rooms.
