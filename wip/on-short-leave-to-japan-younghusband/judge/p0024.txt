This was the <i>William Tell</i>, the captain told us—one of those unlucky ships which is always getting into trouble. She was comparatively new, but had started badly. During her trial trip in the English Channel, she rolled so badly that the crew, being convinced that she would capsize, deserted her, and she was abandoned to her fate. But, later, a pilot-boat came across her, and the men, boarding the deserted vessel, brought her safely to harbour: and what was more, claimed salvage, and got it! She has probably done her last voyage now, anyhow.
