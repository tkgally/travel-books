Everything and anything that belongs to you, down to that pudding-faced freckly boy, is angelic. Everything and anything that belongs to himself is, for conversational purposes, debased to the lowest possible comparative stratum.

However, this has nothing on earth to do with Japan. Let us get back to Kobé. Motomachi is the name of the principal street in the Japanese town, in which are to be found all the best shops for buying curios and odds-and-ends. Not one of the least of the merits of these dealers is that they do not in the least resent your buying nothing. They show all they have with pleasure, and merely add to their parting salutation, ‘Please come again.’ For those who are making any lengthened stay in Japan it is of course better to wait till experience has taught them what are the best things to buy, and what are the proper prices to pay
