As the launch put off, a wild chorus of the English ‘Good-bye’ went up from the wharf, and was responded to from on board in the same language; yet I doubt if there was a single Englishman amongst them. One sometimes wishes that the more rabid Radicals—those whose aim and object in life would appear to be to dismember the great empire which has been raised by the genius of their forefathers—could go about the world and live about the world for a year or two. They would without a shadow of doubt return to England confirmed Conservatives on all imperial questions. It is not till we leave Europe behind, and get out into the wider world, that we begin to appreciate the immense factor that the British nation is in the prosperity of the world.
