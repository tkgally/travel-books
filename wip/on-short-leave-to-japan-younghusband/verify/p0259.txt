A ‘battalion’ of cavalry consists of three squadrons, each 160 strong. The horses cost on an average about fifty dollars each. Officers’ chargers are supplied and fed by the State. A first-class horse will cost as much as 400 dollars. In the school I rode a very handsome little chestnut, fourteen hands, well bred, and very like an Arab. He came from America, and belonged, I believe, to the general. His price was put at 2000 dollars! He would be worth about Rs.1000 in India, which leaves a margin of about Rs.3000 a-head profit to any one who cares to send across a batch of Arabs. The rough-riders in this school were long, thin fellows, quite the cut for the business, and dressed like English grooms in their stable gear.
