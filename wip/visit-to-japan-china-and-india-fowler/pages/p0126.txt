<verse>
[[cont]]Who drive a loathsome traffic, gauge and span,
And buy the muscles and the bones of man?
The tender ties of father, husband, friend,
All bonds of nature in that moment end;
And each endures, while yet he draws his breath,
A stroke as fatal as the scythe of death.”
</verse>

England has made many sacrifices for the suppression of the slave-trade, and for the abolition of slavery; but the time has not yet arrived when her efforts can be permitted to terminate.
