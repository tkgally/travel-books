Though tedious, I thought myself as well off as if I had been at the sea-side. It seems to me quite as pleasant to be enjoying the sea air in a good ship as to be counting the waves at Brighton. At length our long voyage drew to an end. On Sunday the 31st, after service, the distance was posted up as 143 to Yokohama. Several land-birds appeared, driven out by the wind, and settled on the vessel. It blew hard at night, and we were glad that, under the care of a protecting Providence, we were near our destination, and had not to encounter a gale far from land.
