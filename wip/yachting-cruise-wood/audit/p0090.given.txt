I don’t know where they got the idea from, but the result was a mixture of a London footman and a barrel-organ monkey. No one could have appeared to advantage in such a costume; but it was cruel and unfeeling to ask an old man over seventy years of age, who has one leg smaller than the other, from a spear wound, to make such an antic of himself.

In the evening, I rowed on shore with Captain Chapman of the “Dido,” and found the old king sitting in front of his house on a fallcn tree, with a fathom of cloth round his loins, smoking clay pipe, in which costume he appeared far more at his ease, and much more of a king.

All this week my crew had been very busy cleaning the vessel thoroughly. Owing to the unpleasant smells on board, I had all the ballast taken out, thoroughly cleaned, and put back again.

On the 22nd I sailed for Vanna Balavu, an island or the eastern side of the group, and after a tedious passage of five days; for we were much delayed by calm weather, we anchored in Loma Loma Harbor
