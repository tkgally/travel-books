At night the volcano on the island of Ambrym was in violent eruption, and though it was at least sixty miles away, the air was filled with fine ashes, and everything on deck was covered with a grit resembling gunpowder. It penetrated into the cabins; eyes, nose, and mouth were choked with it, causing terrible irritation.

Fortunately the weather was very fine, and in the direction of the volcano the whole heaven was lit up with a wild lurid glare, that might have been mistaken for an aurora, only that at times it glowed with greater brilliancy.

The following day we sailed into a bay on the south-east side of Mallicolo, discovered by Captain Cook, and called by him Port Sandwich.

It was curious to see the natives behaving in exactly the same way as they did when the great explorer visited them.
