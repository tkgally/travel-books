# A Yachting Cruise in the South Seas — C. F. Wood (1875)

IA: yachtingcruisein00wood (Getty Research Institute; 500 ppi; 262 leaves incl. IA's hidden colour cards p0000, p0261). Another scan: ayachtingcruise00woodgoog (Oxford/Google).
Eligibility: re-checked 2026-11-21 (Opus subagent): no reading text (Online Books Page none; Wikisource mentions only; NZETC / Early New Zealand Books none; Faded Page none; Gutenberg by web search and the 2026-11-20 catalogue check; a HathiTrust machine-OCR dump at distantreader.org is not a reading edition); reprints only print-on-demand.
