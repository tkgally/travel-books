It follows, then, that the system of getting labour which we have endeavoured to regulate in Fiji, exists here, free from all restraint, but for the occasional visit of a man-of-war. I must however do the planters the justice to say, that all the labourers I saw looked fat, happy, and contented; in fact, much improved by transplantation from their own savage islands.

The natives of Vaté had little in the way of trade to offer. The presence of the white man has already had its effect upon them. They will be rapidly reduced to the condition of paupers; all that they can produce being without doubt squandered at the grog shop which is attached to one of the planters’ residences. This is much to be regretted, for the Vatese really are a remarkably fine race.
