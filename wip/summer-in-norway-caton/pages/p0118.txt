So soon as the anchor was dropped, we were surrounded by little boats all anxious for fares. It was announced that the steamer would remain some hours here, so nearly all went on shore. The streets were long, straight and clean, and the houses looked neat and comfortable. I observed a good many children in the streets, from the infant in its little carriage to boys and girls a dozen years old. I directly made a raid upon a candy shop and filled my pockets. I soon proved that the children of the far north have tastes similar to our own, but they were decidedly more courteous and well-behaved than those to be met with in any American town.
