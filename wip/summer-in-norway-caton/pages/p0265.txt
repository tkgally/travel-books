Since the tide had come up, our boat had been brought around into the creek, so we did not have to be packed on board, but took the boat without great difficulty. So soon as we got out of the creek and fairly into the fjord, we found a nice little breeze, and fair, so our sails were set and we sped along at a dancing rate.

What is more charming than a quick run in a sail-boat in still waters? Here the fjord was so narrow that there was no room for the breeze to ruffle the water seriously. We had a delightful run home, where we arrived in good time for dinner, for which our excursion had given us excellent appetites. This was one of the pleasantest as well as most instructive excursions I made during our stay at Tromso.
