I ate my dinner in silent disappointment, while the rest of the party, I really think, were more jolly than ever, and seemed to think it rather funny that I had made such a signal failure as a cook as well as an educator.

This is the first station on the north side of the Dovre Fjeld. The broad table-land on the flattened top of a mountain is called <i>troislette</i>, while the mountain itself is called <i>fjeld</i>. So Dovre Fjeld means Dover Mountain, which comprehends also the troislette or table-land of the top. This table on top of the Dovre Fjeld is about twenty-five miles across by the road which we traveled. It is by no means a level plateau, but is, to say the least, hilly or heavily rolling.

Although Kongsvold is, rightly considered, on the fjeld, we were by no means at the top or table.
