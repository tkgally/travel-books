Frantic gestures with hands and feet were according to no fixed rules, but by a dexterous movement she got upon the rock, from which she got to dry land without wet feet or serious trouble. Of course we managed to get a hearty laugh from the incident, and as we thought we laughed in English we hoped the young Norwegian would not understand it, but he did, and I may here say that the first thing in English a foreigner can understand is a good hearty laugh, especially if it is supposed to be at his expense.
