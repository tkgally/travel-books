Ethnologists have invented for this ancient people a name, which of course is a new name, of which the ancients never heard; but as they must have a name for the purpose of designation, no doubt the selection has been made for good reasons. They call them <i>Ugrians.</i> This embraces many families, some of which reach nearly to Behring’s Strait on the east, and probably they might, without great effort, bring our Esquimaux into the same great division of the human family. By what name they should be called, or whether they preceded the age of bronze or not, I do not now propose to discuss; but I have said this much merely to show that it is conceded on all hands that they are by far the oldest people of Europe.
