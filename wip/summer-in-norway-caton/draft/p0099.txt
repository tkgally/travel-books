Two donkey-engines were placed fore and aft to hoist heavy articles from the boats and lower them into the hold. During all the time I was on board Norwegian steamers, I never saw but one article injured, and that was a heavy chest, from which the rope slipped, and which fell into the hold. At this the mate gave an exhibition of loud talking, but whether it was Norske profanity I could only judge from the accent and the wag of the head. From these I judged it to be such.

I would cordially recommend to American railroad superintendents to employ Scandinavian baggagemen, if they can procure them, before they have become so demoralized as to think it a commendable feat to smash a trunk and scatter a lady’s wardrobe all about the platform, and then chuckle over the exhibition.
