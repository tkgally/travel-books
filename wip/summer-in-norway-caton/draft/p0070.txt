Faggots reinforced his missionary arguments, and the sword severed the ligaments which bound his subjects to the heathen gods. But his reign lasted only five years, which was too short a time to establish the faith of men whose conversion had been enforced; and when the pressure of his mighty hand was withdrawn, they relapsed to the old religion and did penance to their heathen gods.

Had Olaf adhered to the monogamic teachings of Christianity, and been content with a single wife, he might have reigned long enough to have allowed the new faith to have taken root, but he must marry another wife and go and claim her dower at the point of the lance.
