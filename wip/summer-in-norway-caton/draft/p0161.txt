[[cont]] pulverized bones, or of the antlers of the deer, strings of the claws or teeth of certain animals worn around the neck, are believed to be wonderful remedial agencies for certain diseases, or to have most potent effects to ward off diseases or dangers, and we may understand that a poultice of the finely-chopped flesh of animals may in many cases be a very good application, without attributing to it any supernatural virtues. A string of the claws of the glutton is believed to be an effectual remedy for vertigo or flightiness, and the oil of the same animal is used effectually for the cure of cutaneous diseases. To drink the warm blood of certain animals is supposed to give strength and courage.
