[[cont]] we skirted along numerous islands which lay on our right. Oh, how we longed to get inside, where we should have still water and a quiet sleep. At last the door was pointed out through which we should enter and find the long wished for calm. This we entered in the dusk of the evening, between eleven and twelve o’clock, and then we glided along in waters as still as if in a canal.

We had not been in the habit of retiring before dark, and so we sat upon the deck, wrapped in cloaks and blankets, waiting for darkness to come; but it came not. At midnight we stopped at a flourishing town of one thousand inhabitants, named Aalesund, but still it was only dusk.
