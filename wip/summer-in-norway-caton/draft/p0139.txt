# Chapter X.

[[subtitle]] H<sc>ammerfest; its commerce and oil factories; cod-fishing; the sei; the fishermen; religion and education; hostility to</sc> C<sc>atholics; confirmation and suffrage; general education; the</sc> L<sc>apps; history and language; nomadic or mountain</sc> L<sc>apps; social distinctions; family relations, dress, physical qualities; an insane bridegroom; domestic virtues;</sc> L<sc>app women; use of tobacco; coast</sc> L<sc>apps; religious character; fanatical crusade; superstitious charms.</sc>

H<sc>ammerfest</sc> is a town of about two thousand people, and is situated at the head of a large land-locked bay and is surrounded by high mountains. Immediately behind it the face of the mountain is nearly vertical. For a third of a mile the foot of the mountain is so near the shores of the bay that there is only room for a single street. At the north end of that a valley opens, through which a considerable creek comes rushing down. This valley widens out farther back, presenting a very pretty country beyond.
