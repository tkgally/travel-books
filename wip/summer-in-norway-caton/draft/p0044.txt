“But,” said I, “would they not bite at live bait?”

“Oh, yes,” replied the disciple of Izaak, “no doubt I could fill the boat in a short time by tempting them with live bait, but in doing so I should take no satisfaction; indeed, I should feel myself disgraced, and would never show myself at the club again.”

“Of course,” said I, “you would not adopt that as a mode of fishing, but in the cause of science, surely it would be permissible.
