# Chapter V.

[[subtitle]] <sc>The great cathedral; Olaf the Holy; miraculous spring; kings crowned at Trondhjem; present dynasty; union between Norway and Sweden; population; steamship lines; variety of language spoken; environs of the city.</sc>

I<sc>f</sc> a contemplation of the city of Trondhjem has betrayed us into an episode, a visit to the great cathedral, which is by far the most interesting object in the city, and is intimately connected with the introduction of Christianity into Norway, invites, if not requires, another digression relating to the events connected with its origin, for without these we merely look upon a pile of dilapidated stone and mortar, some parts going to decay while others are being renewed, and its statuary is crumbling away under the breath of time, and many of its sacred ornaments betray the footsteps of the march of ages.
