Well, now I have had my growl at the most disreputable thing I saw during the entire journey, so I will dismiss those railroad managers, I hope for ever.

We have now got through with our Recollections of Norway, which was the extent of my undertaking when I commenced these jottings; but as I do not like to end with a growl an account of a journey filled with so many pleasing memories, we will take a rapid flight over the rest of the trip, just enough to indicate the route, and to get us as far away as possible from Laxa, the very name of which I dislike above all things.

We arrived in Stockholm before noon, and found quarters in the new Grand Hotel, which was first opened to travelers that day. All things considered, this we found the best hotel I have ever patronized in Europe. Rooms, furniture, table and service, were unexceptionable, and in a magnificent structure.
