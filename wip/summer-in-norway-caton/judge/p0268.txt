A kindly feeling seems to pervade the whole community, which a little propensity to what we call <i>tattling</i> does not seem to destroy. Truthfulness and integrity in their ordinary intercourse seem to be ingrain.

Four days sufficed to <i>do</i> Tromso and its environs, and so we packed our trunks and bid farewell, probably for the last time, to the arctic city, got into the little boat in the evening and pulled away to the steamer <i>Trondhjem</i>, which had arrived the morning before, bound south. I say in the evening, but it was so only in name, or rather hour, for the sun was still shining down upon us in full vigor all night long, so that so far as light can serve to make the day, it was all day—there was neither evening nor morning, but day always. I confess that this unchanging light—this continual brightness at all times the same, except dimmed by clouds, had already become monotonous.
