The great trouble I had to encounter in the pursuit of facts, was the want of careful observations by those who had such great facilities for observing. They never dreamed that this operation had any effect upon the growth of the antlers, and so any peculiarity of growth would not be attributed to that cause, and as it was a matter of not the least earthly moment whether the antler dropped off or broke off near the head, they would take no notice of the fact whether its disappearance was produced one way or the other. It was only in such facts as affected their interests directly that their observations became reliable and valuable.
