What were those people like and who are their descendants? Where are the records they have left, and in what condition did they live? Ancient relics here are scarce, and the paleontologist finds scant record to tell him of the past.

The islands, however, which we this day passed were not all mountains. The views were diversified with lower lands and tamer scenery. On quite a number the surface was comparatively low and level and covered with trees and shrubbery, almost out of keeping with the usual scenery in arctic Norway.
