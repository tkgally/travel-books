An expert, say a costermonger, would answer “for a crupper.” No such thing. From the beginning was Egypt and the Nile. The Nile never wanted a boat, nor Egypt an ass; and the tail was given to help the one into the other. See how quickly it is done! First his forelegs are lifted in, then a man from above in the boat, gives a single haul at the tail, and in goes the donkey like a shot.
