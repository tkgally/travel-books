We had, therefore, made some time before a list of the things we should, as we thought, certainly require, calculated the quantities probably necessary, and had ordered them to be sent from England per Peninsular and Oriental steamer. This list we now supplemented by purchases at Alexandria; and we give the outfit in detail, for the benefit of any reader who may start, as we did, on the Nile without a dragoman.
