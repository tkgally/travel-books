Sunday we attended an uninteresting service in St. Andrews Cathedral (not yet completed) and listened to a poor sermon by one of the canons.

I cannot close without a mention of the Bougainvillea vines which are of two beautiful colors, red and purple, and grow to enormous size, some large enough to bury three whole trees. Hibiscus is so easy to cultivate that it is used as a hedge and flowers profusely. Poinsettia and Night-blooming-cirius are so plentiful as to be a feature. The Royal Poinciana were just starting to flower.

Two things are left for next trip—the excursion, 15 hours each way in a boat, to the famous volcano Kilanea on the island of Hawaii, and learning to ride a surf-board.
