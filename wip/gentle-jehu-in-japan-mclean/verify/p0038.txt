The Moana was most attractive, with a lovely garden, and charmingly located on the edge of the water and within sight of the surf-riders’ favorite haunt, so we hated to return to the town. But rumors kept reaching us of the hard luck that our fellow passengers on the Korea were experiencing and it seemed wise to make sure of what we <i>could</i> have. The Young is a very citified hotel, but the table is good and there is a pretty palm-garden on the roof. Incidentally it is free from mosquitoes, which the other is not. Our one room proved to be a suite of bath, bedroom and parlor in which we could be quite comfortable except that the former occupants were still in possession and remained until five o’clock in the afternoon, when they departed on the Korea.
