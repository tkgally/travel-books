[[subtitle]] The Capital

[[right]] <i>April fifth.</i>

[[right]] I<sc>mperial</sc> H<sc>otel</sc>.

TOKYO seemed so very Europeanized after the other Japanese towns we had been visiting that our first glimpse of it struck us with the chill of disappointment. The streets were too wide, automobiles too numerous, trolleys too noisy and too fast, and there were too many brick and stone buildings. Our eyes had become so unaccustomed to the signs and symbols of modern civilization that the shock of meeting them all again dazzled us so as to at first obliterate the picturesqueness and the beauty of the things really Japanese. We soon found, however, that they were all there and that it was only a question of knowing where to look. Though I cannot claim for Tokyo the charm of the old capital, still it has many beauty spots and many interests peculiarly its own.
