Our pursuing angel was not bothered by a change of hotel. She ran us to earth before lunch or at least found Marie at the hotel and left word that she would be back again directly when she took us for a beautiful trolley ride. She also dined with us that evening and enthusiastically accepted an invitation to motor around the island with us next day.

Although the roads were in some places far from good, and we had many slight showers, called “liquid sunshine,” not hard enough or of sufficient duration to do much damage, it was without exception the most beautiful automobile ride I have ever taken anywhere. The scenery was as varied as it was beautiful.
