# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] Tôkyô (<i>continued</i>).

I <sc>had</sc> not been long in the Hotel Métropole when I was invited by the kind clergy of Bishop Edward Bickersteth’s Mission in Azabu to put up at their house, of which the Rev. Armine F. King is warden. In fact, an invitation had been sent to me as soon as my arrival at Yokohama became known at the Clergy House, but the letter did not reach my quarters there till after I had set out for the Hakone Hills, and did not find me till I arrived at the port again on the eve of my leaving the country.
