As I was with much discomfort squatting down in front of the raised lacquered tray which served as a table, and with no little difficulty taking my meal, it dawned upon the little landlady that a chair and a table would be just the things for me, and, motioning to me to follow her, she conducted me to a room below which had not only those two Western luxuries, but sundry others besides. In fact, I found myself, to my agreeable surprise, in a room which, but for the paper <i>shoji</i>, would be entirely European. Why she had not thought of it before is one of those things which puzzled me in my dealings with the natives.
