E. Fripp.

T<sc>he</sc> favourite summer resort of the Kobe residents is Arima, some nine miles from the settlement, “as the stork flies,” but more like sixteen by road. It is fourteen hundred feet above the level of the sea. It is famous for its bamboo basket industry, and for its medicinal springs, which Hideyoshi is said to have used, and which are regarded as a panacea for rheumatism, and the various forms of skin disease
