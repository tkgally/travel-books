But the commissariat department on Japanese railways has yet to be developed, and the European traveller who goes a long distance is obliged, if he has not become reconciled to Japanese food, to take his own along with him.

The fares are on the general basis of three <i>sen</i> per mile for first class, two for second class, and one for third class.

Luggage is checked as in America, first-class passengers being allowed 100 pounds free, and second class passengers 60 pounds, but it need hardly be said that, among such a kind-hearted people, a slight excess is taken no account of. Officiousness is not a characteristic of Japanese officials.
