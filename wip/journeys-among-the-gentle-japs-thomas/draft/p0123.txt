His numerous retainers (<i>samurai</i>) were thus disbanded, and became Rônins—that is, “wave-men,” tossed about, like a wave of the sea, leading a wandering and unsettled life, without ostensible means of existence. Forty-seven of them, with one Oishi Kuranosuké at their head, determined to avenge their liege lord’s fate by compassing the death of Kôtsuké no Suké. They cautiously laid their murderous plans, but Kôtsuké no Suké was so jealously guarded against the danger which was suspected that there seemed no immediate prospect to the conspirators of success to their scheme. In order to throw their enemy off his guard, they dispersed and betook themselves to different employments. Their ringleader began to lead a dissolute life at Kyôto, and ultimately became so degraded that Kôtsuké no Suké, who kept a watch upon his movements, ceased to fear any danger from the old retainers of Takumi no Kami.
