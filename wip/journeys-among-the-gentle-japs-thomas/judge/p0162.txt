One went so far as to snatch my copy of <i>Murray</i> out of my hand, and pretended to read it to the intense amusement of her friends. It was the first act of rudeness on the part of a native that I had experienced. I have been wondering since if she knew that <i>Murray</i> did not give them a very good name. Perhaps it is scarcely likely. After the pretence of being able to read Igerisu (English), the rude little <i>mousmé</i> proceeded to examine and comment upon the sketches of gods and goddesses in the book, the names of which she gave in almost every instance correctly. The hideousness of Emma-o, of Fudo, and of the Seven Gods of Luck, seemed to cause her some amusement, as it does Europeans. In that respect probably she did not differ from less “forward” members of her race. The Japs wear their religion very lightly. They take neither life nor religion <i>au grand serieux.</i>
