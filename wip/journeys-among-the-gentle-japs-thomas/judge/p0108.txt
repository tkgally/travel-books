[[cont]]Within are a second moat and wall, immediately enclosing the private grounds and palace of the Mikado. Miss Bacon, who was privileged to see, not only the grand reception-room, throne-room, and dining-room, but also the private apartments of the Emperor and Empress, describes the palace (in her interesting little volume on “Japanese Girls and Women”) as being in Japanese style, but with various foreign additions. The larger rooms are furnished after the luxurious manner of European palaces. The private apartments are in Japanese style, and very simple. The floors are matted, not carpeted, and the Emperor and Empress repose on them <i>à la japonaise,</i> in preference to using chairs. Their Imperial Majesties have adopted many European customs, but in their private life they still prefer the national ways.
