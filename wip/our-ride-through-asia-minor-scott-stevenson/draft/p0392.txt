I remember, indeed, feeling like a bit of elastic—I would fall and stumble at every step, but at once recover myself. At one time I know Andrew insisted on lifting me on to my horse; but the agony of the moment was too great to bear, and I screamed to be taken down—so he let me go on my own way. He solemnly affirms, and won't retract the statement, that I kept on saying: “I hate you, Andrew. I hate the trees. I hate Nahli. I hate everything. I hate everybody. And I hate myself,” over and over for more than an hour.
