[[cont]]for a start. We first saw, however, that our poor driver was attended to. He had been lifted on to a donkey by two of the natives, and we sent him and his horses back to the village. He revived enough before starting to accept the present we put in his hand to make up for the damage done to his araba; and we eased his mind by the promise to send a zaptieh from the nearest town, with an order to make the people help him in every way.
