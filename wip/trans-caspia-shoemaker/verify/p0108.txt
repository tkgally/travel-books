# Chapter X.

[[subtitle]] Bokhara, “The Noble.”

<verse>
“Where are the kings and where the rest
Of those who once this world possessed.”
</verse>

[[right]] June 8, 1894.

T<sc>he</sc> name of this ancient metropolis is derived from the Sanskrit “Vihara,” a monastery, showing that even across the Kara Kum desert the shadow of Buddha had fallen and rested on this far northern city. Bokhara stands in a veritable paradise compared to the desert, and all the valley around it shows the exuberant richness of long cultivation. Here again are trees of some size, and the multitude of fruit trees show what a feast the traveler may expect next month.
