The devotion of the serpent was because of the kindness it had received from the empress during her life; and “kindness” to-day will reap as great a reward, though it may take another form. Perhaps, however, it was simply a small act of atonement toward the human race from the descendants of that other serpent in another eastern garden famous in history.

The legends in these eastern cities remain unchanged and unchangeable and are told to the traveler of to-day as they were told to Schuyler in ’73, and to all those who came before him to this beautiful city of Samarkand. This seems a day of graves, but up to this present moment they have all been such old, old graves that they have excited no feeling save curiosity. Now, however, it is to be somewhat different.
