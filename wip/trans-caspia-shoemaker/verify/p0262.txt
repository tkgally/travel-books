But does she do so for their own good; does she hold any good feeling toward them, or toward any other of her conquered provinces? Is it not simply for the greater military glory of the “Czar?” For the “Czar” is Russia and Russia is the “Czar”—all the other seventy millions of human beings are mere ciphers, though this is the nineteenth century. Look at the treatment of Poland. It will answer the question I have asked. Austria and Germany make but little difference in the government of their portions of that dismembered kingdom from that which they accord to other sections of their county. But with Russia! If a man is a Pole, he is accorded much the same treatment that “Jean val Jean,” in Hugo’s great work, received from the police of France.
