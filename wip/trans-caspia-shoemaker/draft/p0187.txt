One of the ladies spoke excellent English, affording me a great treat, as, aside from B., I have not heard my own tongue in weeks. You must know French when you visit this portion of the Far East, and even that language is of small use. There is a good deal of it here, because of the presence of the army, but in Samarkand there were but three persons who could speak it. So Russian, in Turkistan, you must use if you do not know Sart or Persian. To my mind, there is nothing to justify the traveler in coming to Tashkend. There is absolutely nothing worth seeing.
