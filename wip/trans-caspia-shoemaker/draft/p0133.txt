# Chapter XI.

[[subtitle]] Samarkand.

<verse>
“Look around thee now on Samarkand!—
Is she not queen of earth? her pride
Above all cities? in her hand
Their destinies? in all beside
Of glory which the world hath known
Stands she not nobly and alone?
Falling—her veriest stepping-stone
Shall form the pedestal of a throne.
And who her sovereign? Timour—he
Whom the astonished people saw
Striding o’er empires haughtily,
A diadem’d outlaw!”
</verse>

<verse>
“For one touch of her hand,
I would give Bokhara, I would give Samarkand.”
</verse>

[[right]] S<sc>amarkand</sc>, June 9, 1894.

I AM asked from home whether that last is mere poetic license, and whether Samarkand is not one of those places that anybody would give away.
