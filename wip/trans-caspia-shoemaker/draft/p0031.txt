The “Islands” are, of course, closed, most of the bridges over the Neva being taken away until the ice goes out in April. There are but few cafés in the town. The Opera and French theater form the stock of play-houses, to get into either of which you must be “booked” weeks ahead. Day commences at 11 <sc>a.m.</sc> and ends at 2 <sc>p.m.</sc>, when darkness and the snows come down over Russia. The rich who live here make life gay, but God keep the poor! One can not but contrast these wretched peoples with the happy, free-from-care darkies of our Southern States. There is never any music or banjo playing here. One never sees a dancing bear surrounded by dancing people. It is all sodden wretchedness for the poor of Russia.
