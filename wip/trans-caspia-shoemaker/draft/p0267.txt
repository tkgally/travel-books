With those things properly cooked—and what guide at home can not cook?—one can get on very well. But after a long and hot day’s ride, to know that nothing save the eternal mutton and tea await one, is more than most well men can endure for a protracted period. You will ask why I do not use the stores I possess. I do so wherever I can, but what are they? A bag of Sart bread that an ax will not cut, and which must be soaked in hot water in order to be used at all—yet it is all that you can procure, and you must bring that from Osh; then some boxes of semi-sweet crackers, a cake of soup, some gingersnaps, a few cans of sardines and venison—all of which are most ancient, to say the least. You can also purchase chocolates and sweetmeats, but one can not live on such things.
