I confess I dread the passage of the “Sable Noir,” but it will be made in a good railway carriage, where I can take proper care of myself. One can not but feel a regret that these rippling waters, that dance so merrily onward, will find their end, not in the ocean, but in the horrid sands of the Black Desert, which all the waters of the globe could not moisten or fertilize.
