[[cont]]less than an hour he arrived at my hotel and withdrew every thing. “His wife had promised the tarantass;” specials “were not necessary.” In other words, like most of his nation, he was all promise and no performance—lip service. I speedily secured the services of the hotel keeper and started out to buy a tarantass, which I succeeded in doing very shortly. Of course, the Sart, knowing my necessity, made me pay for it, but the very high charge of seventy-five roubles ($37.50) had to be endured. Two hours were needed to put it in condition, but by 5 <sc>p. m.</sc> I was rattling through the streets of Marghilan, having bade farewell to the little policeman who spoke such good English and desired so greatly to come with me. Early in life he had ran away to sea, and, landing in Halifax, learned our tongue, a fact which makes him constantly an object of suspicion to his governor. I telegraphed to this governor of Marghilan from Osh, asking if I might take the youth through to Srinagar with me. The result was, to me, a curt refusal, and to him, a threatened arrest.
