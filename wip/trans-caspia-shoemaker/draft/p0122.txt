Human life is nothing in the Orient; and when one reaches China, one finds the condemned going to execution with a feeling more of curiosity than of fear, of which they seem utterly devoid. Barbarous tortures still exist in Bokhara. A criminal is beaten with sticks, stabbed with knives, has his eyelids cut off and eyes gouged out, is dragged at the tails of horses, and finally either tossed from this great minaret or quartered and then thrown to the dogs.
