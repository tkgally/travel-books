Jorneoff’s baggage train was immense, and I warrant that he travels with all luxury. No simple rice night after night, because he can not eat mutton eternally. Nor would there have been for me, had I in any way understood the resources, or lack thereof, of the land. On another trip things would be very different. I have roughed it for weeks in our Rockies and elsewhere, and certainly think I can get on with as little as most men; but there are few who can exist, as the Kirghiz do, on tea and tough mutton.
