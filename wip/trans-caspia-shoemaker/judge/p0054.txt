Down at last into the valley of Tiflis! To our amazement, the heat is not great, and the valley reminds us of many in France. Hedges of primrose, poppies, and corn flowers, roses and cherries; yet with all its resemblance to France, there is a certain indescribable something that recalls the “Land of the Vulture,” and we should not be surprised to see the minarets of Cairo rise from the valley before us.
