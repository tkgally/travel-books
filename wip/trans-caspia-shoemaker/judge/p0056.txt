# Chapter IV.

[[subtitle]] Tiflis.

IT is a vast comfort after the inferior hotels of Russia—inferior not so much in the structure as in the management—to land at the very clean and delightful Hotel De Londres here. Mine host and his mother are Germans; hence the cleanliness. It gives one a homelike feeling to be so welcomed, convinces one that after all the traveler “finds his warmest welcome in an inn.” How that quotation recalls the splendors of the Ponce de Leon, and the senseless, useless life one leads therein. But let us come back six thousand miles or more to Tiflis, the last few miles of which have been very dusty and we descend from our carriage a sight to behold, and in no very good humor. Cross?
