But, for the matter of that, it is not so much better for a native born Russian. He will, it is true, receive advancement before all others, up to a certain point; but each rank cowers before the one above it, and all tremble before the Czar; and by the “Czar” I do not mean the man, but the office, to which Alexander III is as great a slave as any of his empire. Seventy millions of automatons, who think and move, and have almost their being, through the will of one man. What comparisons one of another nation—knowing India—is forced to make as he journeys through Turkistan!
