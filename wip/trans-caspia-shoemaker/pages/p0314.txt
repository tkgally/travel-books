[[cont]]beyond the minarets of St. Sophia and Sultan Achmet, rises a vast jumble of roofs—rises that great desire of Russia’s Czar—Stamboul.

<verse>
“The Bosphorus shall be free;
It shall make room for me,
And the gates of its water-streets
Be unbarred before my fleets.
I say it: the great white Czar.”
