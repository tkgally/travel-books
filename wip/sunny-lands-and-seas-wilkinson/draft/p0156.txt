During the more severe epidemics of earthquakes, the people camp out in the streets. On the preceding page is a rough sketch of the Cathedral tower, which, having withstood the shocks of centuries, succumbed to the last one.

The Spanish administration of Manila they say is disgraceful; and the Governor nothing more nor less than a robber. Our friends here will not even let us post our letters, for fear that they should be destroyed for the sake of the stamps.
