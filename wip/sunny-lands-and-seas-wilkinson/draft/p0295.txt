Contemplate the state of these poor natives at the end of this fortnight’s orgie—this whirl of riot and dissipation. Is it not enough to account for the alarming decrease in the population? To what else can it be due? hunted up and down, as they incessantly are, by packs of their ever-vigilant foes, what can they do but <i>die</i>—even the very toughest of them? What ordinary flesh and blood could survive it? We all wonder what becomes of their bodies, for we never see their graves. Do they shrivel up and dissipate themselves into their calm summer air, or do they rush in frantic despair to where ‘the balmy zephyrs stir the dreamy blue;’ and in a fit of delirium drown themselves in their lazy murmuring ocean, seeking elsewhere a rest which they have been unable to get in the land of their forefathers?
