We had a terrible fright here last night, but, happily, are both still alive to tell the tale. Our hotel is a little way out of the town, and in the dead of night we were startled from our sleep by the most piercing shrieks and wailings, apparently close at hand. Simultaneously we called to one another, and simultaneously we gasped out, ‘Good heavens! what was that?’ Murder or mutiny must be at its bloody work again, and there is nothing between us and it, but a gossamer-like reed blind. Oh for my trusty revolver now! which foolishly I had left at the bottom of a trunk on board, thinking I should not be likely to want it. How I cursed my folly! How I swore I would be strapped to it, if ever the light of another day blessed us! There they are, those piteous shrieks again, and nearer still; doubtless another poor victim going in agony to his long home! What a terrible suspense this is! then came the quickly-following thoughts of how terrible it is to die so young, so far from home! how useless a life, how much better in the future if we were only spared <i>this</i> time!
