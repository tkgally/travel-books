[[cont]]Taj, the glory and the pride of India! We have long had visions of it, and we have delighted to conjure up its image, unsubstantial, towering silently in the sky like a mass of golden evening cloud, which flings back to the setting sun its lavish colours, and in soft murmuring answers of gold bids farewell to the god of day! Our hearts beat quicker as we think that by to-morrow we shall have actually seen it! It is like the first meeting with the beauteous woman who is, you know, to enslave one by her soft witchery and resistless charms. May we be not disappointed!
