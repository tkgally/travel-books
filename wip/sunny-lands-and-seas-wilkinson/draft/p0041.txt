In the evening we heard the Governor’s band, a most excellent one, and well led. There was nearly a full moon shining, and a great number of people were enjoying the music and the deliciously cool evening after a blistering day. We heard much nice music, including a new waltz by Waldteufel, ‘Toujours Fidèle,’ a soft dreamy waltz which seems very popular. Music must be the food of love, for we were enraptured with everything, and especially with the solemn splendour of these Eastern nights, so vast, so quiet and still, so full of peace and rest. Music never sounded sweeter; and it was being torn back to earth when it ceased, and the cares of the day came stealing upon us again with the thought of bed, mosquitoes, and other earthly discomforts.
