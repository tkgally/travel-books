# Chapter XIII.

[[subtitle]] The Land of the Rising Sun.

[[subtitle]] Endurance of Jinrickishaw Coolies.—Their Short Lives.—The Japanese ‘Big Paul.’—Scenery on the Road to Nara.—Tea-house.—‘Tiffin.’—Dai-Butzu.—A Primitive Hostelry.—Japanese Servants.—Dinner.—Cuttle-fish.—Native Candles.—Curios.—Midnight Visits.—Our Ablutions.—The Road to Osaka.—Unbidden Guests.—A Remedy.—Fish.—A Bicycle.—Osaka, the Venice of Japan.—The Mint.—The Castle.—The Fast Young Man of Japan.—Back to the <i>Ceylon.</i>—Curio Dealers and their Failings.

[[right]] March 16, 1882.

T<sc>his</sc> morning at eight we started in jinrickishaws for the ancient city of Nara (thirty miles distant), and if by chance the eye of an athlete happens to light upon these lines, let him mark them. I had two little Japanese coolies for my team, the more bulky De Bosco, three; but besides ourselves to pull, they had our heavy ulsters, rugs, and weighty Gladstone-bags.
