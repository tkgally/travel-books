Some Satsuma china De Bosco bought he was first asked 270 dollars for; but on his offering 150 and Mr. Hughes telling the people we were leaving immediately in the <i>Ceylon</i>, the offer was reluctantly, and with a much-injured air, accepted.

Mr. Hughes further told us it would take many weeks before they would recover their senses, and trade again as reasonable beings. After the <i>Wanderer</i> had been there, they were just the same, and a mere boy, the guide of a well-known and lavish Liverpool gentleman, was paid nearly a thousand dollars’ commission from the different shops at which his master had made his purchases.
