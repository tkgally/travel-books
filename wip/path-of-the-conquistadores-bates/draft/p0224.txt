Next morning we go, through woods with no underbrush and only the hanging vines to trouble, to the lagoon behind the camp, and paddle around to get some shooting. It is a good percentage bag. We fire four shots and bring back a monkey, a hawk, a dove, and a white crane. There are no aigrettes on the crane, which is a male. The aigrettes are generally secured from the females in the breeding season, for the birds are wild and can be approached only at that time. Venezuela has passed a law that these white cranes are not to be shot, and the aigrette-hunters are supposed to pick up the feathers on the water.
