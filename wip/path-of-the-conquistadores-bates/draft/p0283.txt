The Commandante of Bolivar, El Señor Coronel Pilar Para, invites you to go shooting with him along a lagoon across the river. Wood-pigeon are the objective. It is a real test of shooting to get these birds in the instant before they dive down into the brush. The difficulties of wing shooting, however, do not trouble El Señor Coronel. He sneaks up to a distance of about ten yards and lets them have it sitting. Then he turns around and grins, while a boy goes into the brush for the mangled remains. Twelve pigeons and two parrots are the bag before it gets too hot for comfort and we return.
