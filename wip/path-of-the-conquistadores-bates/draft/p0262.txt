You remark incidentally that you have seen the river and hope to see something of the interior. Rather to your surprise he says at once, “I will arrange it,” and adds the Castilian formula to the effect that his house is yours: “Haga Usted lo mismo que si estuviera in su casa.” You pay your respects and presently leave.

Passing up the street, you step in upon Senor José Aquatella, who takes you to the Club Union Commercial. He orders two lemonades, and you sit together on the broad portico overlooking the Calle de Orinoco and the river.
