Cedar chests, palm-fibre baskets, and bundles of many kinds are on their way ’tween decks, where a large family of Trinidad negroes have camped. Five porters carry the cumbersome equipment of the English tourist—saddle, top-hat-case and all—to the cabin indicated by the buxom Trinidad negress who is stewardess, and seems to manage all the men.
