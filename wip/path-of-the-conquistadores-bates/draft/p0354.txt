Mulattos are fairly common, and Zambos, negro and Indian half-breeds, less so. There is every possible permutation of these race mixtures.

The English engineer between the courses tells the tale of the Callao Mine.

“About 1860 some Yankees from California came here and began to pan gold. They founded the Callao Mine, and a French company was organized and bought it. After a few years’ work the company became insolvent, and, to eke along, paid tradesmen script redeemable in shares. A thousand pesos’ worth of shares would be bought then for 50 pesos.
