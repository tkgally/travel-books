Suddenly there is a chorus of cries, stamping of feet and rattling of ropes. The boom swings over in a jibe. The throat halyards of the lateen sail part, and it comes down with a bang, knocking one of the crew into the river. The night is pitch dark; confusion of the pit reigns. After you have been walked over, the dog stepped on, and everything bedevilled generally, things are fixed up and we go on again, the castaway climbing back complacently.

With malicious frequency now the boom swings across, and you find your head in the scuppers, your feet high up to windward, and have to crawl around.
