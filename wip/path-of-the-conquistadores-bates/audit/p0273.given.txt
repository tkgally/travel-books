“This city; you know, was one of the last that was held by the Spaniards during the War of independence. All the monks from round about and the wealthy landowners and the officials fled to it. Some brought their possessions, and when Bolivar entered the city, buried them here. A tenant in one of my father’s houses up the street found a treasnre and left the country a rich man. All these houses were built by the Spaniards and have walls 3 feet thick, with secret closets and floors.

“Many buried money in the country. Eight million pesos’ worth of gold is said to have been interred at the old monastery of San Seraphine.
