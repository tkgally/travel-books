[[cont]]us back to Mayaro at about noon, after long stretches of wading, for the tide nearly catches us under the cliffs. A long run in the automobile brings us to the celebrated Asphalt Lake. The straggling village at its edge is an extraordinary spectacle. Not a house but is twisted out of plumb. The land is the source of never-ending litigation, because the slowly shifting currents of the pitch bottom in a few years move yards and gardens on to other men’s property, distort boundaries into every possible shape, carry landmarks a hundred yards away. Some natives are doing a little desultory digging here before the territory of the Asphalt Company begins. A green bamboo across the road marks its boundary.
