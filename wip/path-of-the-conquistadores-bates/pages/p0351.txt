Then I had to go to a hat-store which had the stamp-selling concession and buy a one-bolivar stamp. I took this to the Custom House, and one man pressed a rubber marker on the paper and another signed it. I knew intuitively that the stamp should have been cancelled and I pointed this out. The second official said I was right, they had happened to miss it. He opened a penknife and stabbed the stamp. Then I took the document to still another place, and the Commandante stamped it anew and wrote his name upon it.”
