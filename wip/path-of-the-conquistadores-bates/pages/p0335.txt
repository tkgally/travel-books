[[cont]]The other is very difficult and dangerous, but if there are any <i>tigres</i> you get them. The first method is to ride around the savanna with dogs and try to run on a trail. The dogs chase the <i>tigre</i> up a tree, and you station yourself about 10 feet away and shoot it in the eye. The second way is to go into the <i>montaña</i> to their dens. If jaguars are in the neighbourhood they will be there. You build a fire and stand in front of the hole. They come out on the jump and you have to shoot quick. “<i>Esta los llanos</i>”—he points to the level savanna, “<i>y esa el monte,</i>” he points to the jungle-covered hill above.

We enter the <i>montaña.</i> The peon, machete in hand, leads the way to cut the vines and creepers where they are impassable. In a few minutes you are fighting your way up the hill through the worst jungle you have ever had to face, bar none.
