## Gallant Little Wales

[[center]] By

[[center]] JEANNETTE MARKS

[[center]] <i>Author of “Through Welsh Doorways,” etc.</i>

[[image: engraving of a ruined castle tower overgrown with ivy]]

This is a most attractive book, both for the holiday buyer and for the traveler. It deals with the most picturesque regions of Wales, as well as with some of the more interesting aspects of Welsh life of the present day, while there are other chapters dealing with such entertaining episodes as Dr. Johnson’s tour in Wales. The illustrations, from old prints in the possession of the author, are well chosen and add materially to the interest and value of the book.

[[center]] $1.25 <i>net.</i> Postage extra.

## The Gateway of Scotland

[[center]] By A. G. BRADLEY

[[center]] <i>Author of “In the March and Borderland of Wales”</i>

A picturesque and informing account of those charming parts of England, Midlothian, Lammermoor and the Merse, which constitute, as the author implies in the title, the gateway of Scotland. The book is lavishly illustrated with more than two score line drawings and eight full pages in color, making one of the most attractive holiday travel books of the season.

[[center]] $4.00 <i>net.</i> Postage extra.
