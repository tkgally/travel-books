To foreigners the leader generally promises concessions and so gets money. With a force and some cash he marches to the capital. Matos had sixteen thousand men against Castro’s six thousand. I was with Castro that day in the steeple of the Church of Ascencion. Matos’ men none of them wanted to die. Castro said that morning, ‘I win,’ and his regular soldiers went through the insurgents like a mad bull.
