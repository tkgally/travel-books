Everybody laughs at the Major, and then we pour him a drink of Scotch to cheer him up.

The talk drifts to the indentured coolies. The engineer has studied their social system while in India. “All here are of the lower castes—sudras,” he says, “and each goes down one degree by leaving India. It will take many payments to the priests when they return to procure redemption.”

“Many of them don’t return at all,” comments Robertson. “I have a lawsuit with a time-expired coolie freeholder about a road. They are the worst people for going to law you ever saw.”
