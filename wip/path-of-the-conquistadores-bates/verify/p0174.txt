[[cont]]we steer dead south. All of a sudden the boat turns completely around and faces Trinidad. Joe is at the wheel.

“What are you doing there?” howls Fitzgerald. “Drop the wheel!” He takes it himself. We have not gone a hundred yards before the boat does the same thing again. The tiller is helpless. Some whirlpool has swung the boat about bodily, though only a little swirl on the surface shows the whirlpool’s location. No harm done, but it jerks one’s nerves a little.

The wind freshens measurably. White caps are on the waves. Gulls fly by, shrieking hoarsely, or poise alongside. The wind is still astern.

Up ahead now looms a solitary rock, the Sentinel—“El Soldado.” Sharp and menacing it stands. We steer to seaward of it, as we are making for one of the eastern outlets of the river and the wind is favouring us.
