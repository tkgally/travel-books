# The Path of the Conquistadores

# I

[[subtitle]] The Conquistadores

SIX battered caravels were slowly nearing the coast of South America. Their planking, warped and parched by weeks of sailing beneath the torrid sun, showed gaping seams. Long strings of weeds trailed from their sides. They were in momentary danger of sinking from their leaks. None had more than one cask of water.

On the narrow poop-deck of the largest vessel stood a tall, gaunt, lonely figure. His long white hair hung lankly down.
