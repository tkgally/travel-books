A passenger for Pedernales, at one of the mouths of the river, is bound to go to Ciudad Bolivar without touching foot to ground, pass the customs, and then come back. To disobey means arrest, jail, fines, and endless trouble to the diplomatic representatives of whichsoever foreign Government has to dig the culprit out. But the officer of a vessel is a bird of another colour. It is not only his pleasure but his duty to land and present his papers and his compliments to the Commandantes and other officials on the way up. And what Commandante is such a particularist in the law of Caracas as to prevent his <i>amigos,</i> once landed, from taking a stroll or getting a shot at some alligators? <i>Voyez vous?</i>

Many prominent citizens of Venezuela are in the Consulate of Port of Spain.
