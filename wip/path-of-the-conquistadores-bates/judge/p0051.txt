In 1690 Levassor de la Touche, and in 1716 Blackbeard Tench the pirate, attacked Trinidad. Small wonder that in 1773 only 162 male adult whites were recorded as living on the island.

A French resident of Grenada, M. de Saint-Laurent, became, in 1778, the real founder of Trinidad. So impressed was he with its fertility that he bought a large area of land, drew up a Bill of Rights, or Cedula, got it approved by Spain in 1783, and secured the appointment of an excellent Governor, Don José Maria Chacon.
