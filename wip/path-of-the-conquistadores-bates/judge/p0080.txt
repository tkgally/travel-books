# II

[[subtitle]] Trinidad

THE green slopes of Tobago, where the shipwreck of the real Alexander Selkirk inspired the “Robinson Crusoe” of Defoe, have been left behind in the dark mists of the Caribbean. Ahead lies a shadowy range of mountain peaks, growing every moment more clear as the dawn lights up their densely wooded sides and outlines the trees that crown their crests.
