“Now when I came to San Felix, I went into the inn there, and I saw that same Turk eating dinner. When he saw me he went to his room without finishing. ‘That is queer,’ I thought, and waited for him to come out. I then said to the landlord, ‘Go tell the Turk I want to see him.’ The Turk told the <i>posadero,</i> ‘I am sick and cannot come.’

“So I went to the door and said, ‘Open, or I shoot you through the door.” He did not open, so I kicked in the door and arrested him. ‘You murdered that woman and girl,’ I said. ‘Confess, or I shoot.’ So he confessed.
