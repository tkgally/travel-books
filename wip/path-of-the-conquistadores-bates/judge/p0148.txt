“Then there is no trouble about going up the Orinoco and into the interior?”

“Never a bit,” says Fitzgerald. “The Venezuelans are the real goods—dead game sports and no limit.”

“That settles it,” you remark. “I am going to Ciudad Bolivar to-morrow on the ‘Delta.’”

Fitzgerald thinks a moment and sizes you up with a sidelong glance. “Say, I’m off for there myself to-morrow on my launch; come along with me.”

You sweep a scrutinizing glance over him in turn; thinking a moment, too, you recall Jefferson’s shrug wherein he shook off all responsibility. Then you accept.

“Done,” and on it you shake.
