At dawn you are definitely awakened by Alvarado. You find everything packed but your hammock. One of Señor Bermudez’s daughters has a cup of black coffee ready, and you are very soon in the saddle.

“Adios, señor,” the ladies call to you. Alvarado and Señor Bermudez touch hands to shoulders and our cavalcade starts.

“We will breakfast at San José,” says Alvarado.

The llanos become more rolling as we advance and the rocky thickets more numerous. The grass seems better.
