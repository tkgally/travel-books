It was very impressive, the contrast between the place as we saw it and the Shushan of the Bible, the capital of a vast empire at the height of its power, where the Great King feasted his princes and nobles in a palace splendid with silver and gold, and “showed the riches of his glorious kingdom.” I shall always remember it as it was that night, the lonely mound surrounded by the desert, a great hazy full moon rising slowly over it, and no sound but the occasional cry of the jackals, or the wail of a plover flitting about us like a departed soul.
