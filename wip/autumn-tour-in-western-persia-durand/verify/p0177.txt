# Chapter XII

A<sc>ll</sc> the 15th of November we stayed in Dizful, resting our animals and making arrangements for collecting supplies. We were told that for the next ten days’ march through the mountains we could get nothing whatever, and that we must take grain and bread, and even chopped straw for our animals. This meant a considerable addition to our number of mules, for in ten days a mule eats a large part of the grain and straw it carries. We set our people to work on this, and meanwhile, as they said it would take at least two days, we decided to make an expedition to Susa, the ancient Shushan, where the French are excavating.
