Seeing that this was not good for trade the cheery captain took to returning good for evil in the shape of fruit for stones, and very soon he found the vessel hailed with shouts of delight. Now you would think it was the one thing for which the villagers live.

In the same way Captain Norton had tamed his passengers. At first they used to storm the ship and take possession, absolutely refusing to pay any passage money, but they are now orderly enough, though Sayyads, descendants of the Prophet, and others still at times expect to be carried about for nothing.

Well, we groped our way up the ditch, the Ab i Gargar, until the afternoon, the <i>Shushan</i> running her nose into or nearly into a mud bank occasionally, while little brown torrents rushed indignantly at us from either side as if we had no business there.
