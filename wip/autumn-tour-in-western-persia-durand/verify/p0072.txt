Persia is a wonderful country for fruit. Grapes, peaches, pears, apples, everything seems to grow naturally. But little is done to improve the trees, and one never gets anything like an English hothouse peach or English grapes.

So we went on to Ispahan, where we arrived on the 12th of October. A couple of marches out we had been met by the Prince Governor’s head man, the Fatih ul Mulk, a very polite and agreeable gentleman, who brought out some carriages for us, and gave us altogether the most kindly and hospitable reception. The Zil es Sultan, or Shadow of the King, is the brother of the Shah and governor of the province.
