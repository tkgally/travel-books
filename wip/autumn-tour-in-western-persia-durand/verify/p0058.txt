In this district, Shimran, the Shah and the Persian nobles and the Foreign Legations generally spend the summer, or a part of it. The late Shah used to leave Tehran about the end of May, when the heat began, and move slowly up, a mile or two at a time, from one of his beautiful garden houses to another, until finally in the end of July he would march away over the mountains to a cool place on the north side of the range, where patches of snow lie all the year round. With him went several hundreds of people, and innumerable horses and mules, so that the camp was of imposing size.
