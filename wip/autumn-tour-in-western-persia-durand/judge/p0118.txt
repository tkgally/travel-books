That night was unpleasant. We got to a very nice little camp, near a place called Shalíl, but in the evening while we were at dinner, heavy rain came on, and we were flooded out of our Shamiána, which collapsed. All night the deluge continued, and the roar of the stream just below us and the drumming of the rain on the tiny Kabul tents combined to make an almost deafening noise. To make matters worse, one of our servants managed to get into a quarrel with a Bakhtiari. He was foolish enough to strike or threaten to strike one of these wild highlanders, who immediately drew his knife and tried to stab him.
