It was pitched on turf, actual turf—quite flat—probably the old bed of the stream which now lay a hundred feet below. Looking down over the edge, one had to climb a little way down to see it, was a beautiful big pool, overhung by rocks—deep green water and pebbly shallows, with fish swimming about them. Between us and the pool we noticed a curious carved platform, with long rows of cup-shaped markings. My husband climbed down and examined the rocks, and came to the conclusion that the markings were not real “cup marks” but comparatively recent, and made possibly by the tents of the Lurs. They were curiously regular and systematic.
