Finally, we wished to return to Tehran by way of Luristan, the country of the Feili Lur tribes, and to see whether there was any chance of opening up another trade route, often talked of, from the Karun to the North. Incidentally, we wanted also to visit the ruins of Susa, “Shushan the palace” of the Scriptures; and there was, I think, some idea of possibly getting a lion or two in the Arab country hard by, where lions still exist.

It will be seen from the accompanying map that this march involved crossing and recrossing the great mountain range which stretches from the Turkish frontier in a south-easterly direction towards the Persian Gulf. As this range runs up in points to a height of 13,000 feet, and there are no made roads, the travelling is rough.
