(3) <i>Agriculture.</i>—The following numbers are taken from the <i>C.N.</i>:—

| Nature of Crop. | Area Sown in 1895. Hectares. | Area Sown in 1908. Hectares. | Area Sown in 1908. Acres (Approximate). |
| Wheat | 1,600,000 | 4,854,086 | 12,000,000 |
| Maize [[fnref 1]] | 800,000 | 1,940,884 | 4,800,000 |
| Linseed | 300,000 | 1,266,825 | 3,130,000 |
| Oats | — | 386,261 | 955,000 |
| Barley | — | 93,689 | 231,500 |
| Vines [[fnref 1 again]] | — | 122,457 | 303,000 |
| Fruit trees | — | 59,190 | 146,000 |
| Alfalfa | 712,006 | 4,656,707 | 11,500,000 |
| Rye-grass, &c. | — | 2,072,169 | 5,110,000 |

The above gives a total of about 38,000,000 acres as under cultivation; while, taking a sort of average of the various statements made, it seems probable that about 300,000,000 acres are capable of cultivation. The errors can hardly be so great as to invalidate the conclusion that Argentina is yet in its infancy as regards agricultural development.

[[fn 1]] To illustrate the difficulty of feeling confidence in the numbers quoted, I may mention that the <i>S.Y.B.</i> gives 7,345,500 (not 4,800,000) acres as under maize; while the <i>A.Y.B.</i> gives 39,000 hectares, and not 122,457, as under vine-culture, and for about the same date, 1908.
