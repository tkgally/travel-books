## <i>Porters and Guides.</i>

I was told that at least two men who can use an ice-axe and climb a mountain can be found at Puente del Inca or thereabouts. Enquiries might be made about them.

## <i>Clothing, &c.</i>

It would be out of place to attempt to give here a list of things required for camping out in mountains generally. I am confining myself to some hints that seem to me to be useful for the Andes in particular. And as regards clothing, I would say that any intending climber will get some idea of the cold if he reads Sir Martin Conway’s book about Aconcagua. Even at 12,000 feet only, and inside a tent, one had to close up every crack round the neck; and I was cold even with a Jäger sleeping-bag inside a heavy one that Dr. Helbling lent me.
