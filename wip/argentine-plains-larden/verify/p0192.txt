May it not be the case that man with his herds of cattle has disturbed the balance, and that this is but a time of transition? When all the land is occupied, will not locusts be kept down and the balance restored?” I am not suggesting that locusts are a blessing; only that we may be witnessing a striking case of a balance disturbed by man.

Why not, indeed, regard the Pampas as the feeding-ground and the locusts as the natural wild animals feeding on it—as bison elsewhere? But I am out of my depth here; I believe I have accidentally got into teleology, when I intended only to give the actual history of a locust invasion!
