# Chapter XII

[[subtitle]] To the Glacier Region at the Head of the Rio del Plomo Valley—Up the Valley of the Rio de las Toscas (or Cajon de Estoca)—Back to Puente del Inca

I<sc>t</sc> was an easy day for us, this descent from our Tupungato camp to the mouth of the Rio de las Taguas; and the cañons that we had crossed before did not seem quite so formidable the second time, though twice a mule fell. We reached the Rio de las Taguas about 4.30 p.m.; and, turning up this valley, camped near the mouth of it at 5 p.m. on the (true) right-hand side of the river. Next day (February 17th) we set off at 10.15 a.m. Dr. Helbling soon stayed behind to take some survey measurements, while I went on with Evaristo. The negrito had gone back to Puente del Inca for supplies. About 12 o'clock we came to a warm saline spring, the water of which had deposited a huge fan-like sheet of calcium carbonate that ended on the main valley-river.
