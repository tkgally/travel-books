# Pe-Che-Li.

I<sc>t</sc> is a great shock to the moral system, that plunge from the fair, green shores of Japan, to the swampy banks of the Peiho, of which I can just discern the outline, as we anchor in the midst of the fleet.

The sky is mud-coloured, the sea is mud-coloured, the land is mud-coloured, and, as our gun-boat approaches nearer, we can make out two mud-coloured forts, commanding the entrance of a mud-coloured river.
