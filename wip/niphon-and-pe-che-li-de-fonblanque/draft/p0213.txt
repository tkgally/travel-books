Did I actually, in the streets of that strange Yedo, meet the princely hawking-party, the quaint Daimio’s procession, the swaggering band of two-sworded Yaconins, and the group of moosmes bathing at their doors?

Was I ever at Ojee, smoking drowsily under the shadow of camelias, and accepting of fragrant tea from a kneeling damsel—or did I dream it all?

Already my recollections of beautiful Japan fade into mist, like unsubstantial visions; and its scenes come back upon my memory, as does a familiar old tune, heard in days gone by, from a voice hushed for ever long ago!
