[[cont]]the destruction of a miserable tyrant’s palace, and the exaction of a few thousand pounds, are but poor compensation for such lives as have been lost to the country. That the authors and perpetrators of outrages, the full extent of which has never yet been divulged, are known, and might have been brought to justice, admits of no doubt. The cowardly murderer of poor Brabazon lives, and holds, to this day, a high command under his Imperial master. Not one of all who instigated or executed tortures and death upon their helpless victims has been brought to justice, although to have hanged such wretches from the highest turret of the walls of Pekin would have done more towards teaching the Chinese Government humanity than all the verbose diplomatic notes or edifying sermons that could be written or preached.
