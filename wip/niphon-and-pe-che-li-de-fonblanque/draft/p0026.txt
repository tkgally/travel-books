One of them, Lieutenant Habersham, is the author of an excellent work on Japan,[[fnref *]] which he visited while attached to Commodore Perry's expedition, and which so completely won his heart, that on our arrival he resigned his commission, and established himself as a merchant at Yohuhama. If intelligence and energy can make a man's fortune in Japan, he ought to become a millionaire. Another has recently acquired fame as the Commander of the Confederate steamer <i>Nashville.</i>

We sailed from Hong Kong on the 31st December, and I assisted in drinking out the old year under the shadow of the stars and stripes. On the 8th January we passed a group of volcanic islands, some of them in full eruption. I asked one of the sailors who had been employed on a survey in these seas what the volcanoes were called.
