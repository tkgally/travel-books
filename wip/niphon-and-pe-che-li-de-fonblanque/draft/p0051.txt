As the Dutch interpreter delivers this message, Muri-Yama, who is evidently perplexed at its nature, becomes more attentive; at the word “Parde,” he interrupts the speaker by asking, “Did you say horses?” Between each sentence he blows upon his closed fingers, and utters a subdued “<i>Hein, Hein,</i>” and at its conclusion, nods in token of comprehension, and proceeds in the usual manner to convey the message to his Chief.
