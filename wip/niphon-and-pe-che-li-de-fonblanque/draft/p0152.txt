[[cont]] Arrived at the temple, the ladies performed their devotions with the most perfect propriety of demeanour, passed their hands reverently over the wooden image of their particular deity, prayed for success and prosperity in their <i>métier</i>, and returned to the Gankeroo, quits with the past and hopeful for the future, in the happy, calm frame of mind of a <i>dévote</i> leaving the confessional.

Riding on the Nagasaki road the other day, I met with another striking illustration of the entire absence of modesty which astonishes Europeans on their first visits to Japan. A little urchin, seated at the door of a neat and substantial dwelling-house, gave the alarm of the approach of a Tojin, and as I passed I had the pleasure of seeing the whole family rush into the street to gaze at me.
