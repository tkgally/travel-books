When the Gorogio, or Grand Council of State, determined, by a considerable majority, to entertain Lord Elgin’s demands for the conclusion of a treaty of commerce, one of the principal Daimios arose from his seat, and, half drawing his sword from its scabbard, exclaimed,—“Let traitors be false to the laws of their country and the traditions of their ancestors; I, for one, while I have life, will resist the admission of the detested foreigner!” and so saying, he left Yedo, accompanied by his retainers, and, secluding himself in his remote dominions, became and remains, one of the leaders of a large and powerful party of anti-Europeans.
