Why cannot he hold his stupid tongue, and let us dream on, as we people the spot with dear absent forms and faces, that now, alas! and for many a weary day to come, we can see only in our dreams? For Ojee is a long way from Pimlico, and my road to it lies through Pekin!

And as we lie there, at peace with all the world and happy in our reveries, the clash of arms and the groans of strong men in their agony are heard in the streets of Yedo, and its pavements are red with blood.
