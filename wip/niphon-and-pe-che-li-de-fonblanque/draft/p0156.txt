I have been informed, on excellent authority, no less than that of a married man, that henpecked husbands are by no means rare in Japan.

Of the higher ranks of women I cannot speak, never, I grieve to say, having had the honour of forming their acquaintance; but I am led to believe that the rights of woman, within proper limits, of course, are perfectly understood and admitted by men of rank.[[fnref *]]

I think I am also safe in asserting that, whatever may have been a woman's antecedents, breaches of chastity on the part of wives, are very rare, which is not surprising when we bear in mind that the penalty of a <i>faux pas</i> is death to the offenders and irremediable disgrace to those connected with them.
