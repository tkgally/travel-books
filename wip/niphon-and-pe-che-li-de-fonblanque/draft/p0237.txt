Our allies were, as a rule, represented in even a more ridiculous form than ourselves, and I believe, on more than one occasion, resented these caricatures rather seriously, as a national insult or a base reflection upon the glorious army of France; but our men were less susceptible, and heartily enjoyed the contemplation of red-coated monsters with their trousers buttoned behind, and carrying their firelocks with the left hand over the right shoulder.
