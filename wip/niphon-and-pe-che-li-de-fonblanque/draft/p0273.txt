Can such things be under a Government professing even a semblance of justice and humanity? The ruffian Yeh is reported to have executed no less than 70,000 men during the term of his vice-royalty; and the cruelties commonly committed upon offenders at Canton, while our troops occupied the city, were so outrageous, that the English general, upon his own authority, interfered to put a stop to them, or, at any rate, to their perpetration in public.

[[fn cont]] not only tolerated, but licensed by the Government. It is impossible to dwell upon so repulsive a subject, but if ever a land has, by its vices, merited the blighting curse of the Almighty, that land is China.
