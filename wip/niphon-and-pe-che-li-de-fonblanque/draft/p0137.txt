Occasionally, we met with the savage scowl of a flushed Daimio’s follower, and, two or three times, angry cries of “Tojin!” and “Baca!” fell upon our ears; but the demeanour of the great mass was, if not friendly, certainly inoffensive. I do not know how far we had to thank our escort for this.

One of the great sights of Osakusa are the waxwork and carved wooden figures illustrative of Japanese character.
