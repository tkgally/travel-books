They appear to have adopted all our words of command and technical terms, and it was curious to hear our naval phraseology rendered, as “Shoven sie off,” “Bring mir das logbuch,” “Pullen sie weg,” &c.

The good Commodore was all courtesy and kindness; his officers were high-bred and accomplished men, and, probably from the nature of their career, less stiff-necked and opinionated than Prussians generally are; the men were sturdy, ruddy-cheeked, active fellows, and there was a general air throughout the ship of smartness, cleanliness, and regularity, which we are apt to believe peculiar to our own service.
