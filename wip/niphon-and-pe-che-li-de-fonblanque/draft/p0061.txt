Kanagawa was originally intended to be the European trading port of Yedo; owing, however, to its obvious inferiority, for purposes of commerce, to Yokuhama—a village about three miles to the south-west—the latter became the resort of the merchants, while the Consuls alone took up their residence in temples[[fnref †]] allotted to them in Kanagawa, which is prettily situated on the high and richly-wooded banks of the harbour, while Yokuhama is little more than a swamp recently reclaimed from the sea.
