Here and there along the banks, and more particularly at the narrower passages, batteries may be seen, half masked by trees and shrubs. They are right to hide their black muzzles; what business have the implements of hideous war in these fairy scenes?

We passed the night in the loveliest possible little harbour. Although we might almost have jumped ashore on either side, it took us some time to find water sufficiently shallow to anchor in. There were populous villages all around us, and we could see the people wandering to and fro, or collecting in chattering groups, after their day's toil.
