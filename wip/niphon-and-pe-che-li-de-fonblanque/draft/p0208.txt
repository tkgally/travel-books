Unfortunately, the rainy season, which is, I believe, a very long and frequently-recurring one here, had set in; and I was unable, during my short stay, to visit the environs of Nagasaki, which, by all accounts, are very beautiful.

The Russians appear to be quite at home here. They had no less than four gunboats in the bay, and had established a small naval colony on the shore opposite to Nagasaki.
