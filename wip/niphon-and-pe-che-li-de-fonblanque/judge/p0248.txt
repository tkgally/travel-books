Fokee nods his head, with a grin, and “wilos” accordingly, not without a certain instinctive knowledge of what is expected of him; while the Briton says to his comrade,—

“They are a poor ignorant lot, them Fokees, aint they, Bill?” whereunto Bill responds with an assenting sigh of pity and a proud sense of intellectual superiority.
