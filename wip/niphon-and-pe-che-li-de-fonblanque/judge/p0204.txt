28<i>th October.</i>—Yesterday morning we saw the last of the Sowonada Seas, and skirted along the coast, which may boast its beauties too, though, I fear, our late feast has made us fastidious. To day we passed Hirado, the great coal depôt of Nagasaki, and, a few hours later, anchored, having reached the intermediate stage of our voyage.
