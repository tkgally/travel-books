The egg-shell and jewelled china, so much and so justly admired, is all of modern manufacture, and the demand for it within the last two or three years has, I am told, raised its cost by nearly two hundred per cent. I have seen some fine specimens of Miaco ware, an opaque composition with a rough and unglazed surface; but none of these Japanese manufactures that I have met with equal the better specimens of old porcelain, which I subsequently saw in the north of China.
