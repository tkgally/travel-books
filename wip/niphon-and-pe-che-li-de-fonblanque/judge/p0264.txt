Prices rise gradually in China, as you travel north. In Hong Kong an English shilling’s-worth costs you a dollar; in Shanghae, a tael (6<i>s.</i> 8<i>d.</i>); and in Tien-tsin about ten shillings, without reference to vulgar fractions.
