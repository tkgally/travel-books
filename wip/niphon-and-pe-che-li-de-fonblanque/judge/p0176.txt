One of the Vice-Governors of Yedo, Matabé, the interpreter to the Legation, and several Yaconins, formed our escort, and norrimon-bearers, bettos, coolies, servants, and followers, together with a troop of pack-horses, swelled our <i>cortège</i> to the dimensions of a small invading army.

Mr. Alcock, although he had of necessity asked the Japanese Government to make arrangements for our comfort and security <i>en route</i>, had stipulated that he should be as little as possible embarrassed by the presence of officials, as he wished to travel <i>niebon</i>.
