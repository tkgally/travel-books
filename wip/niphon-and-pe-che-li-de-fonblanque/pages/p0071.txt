If you give him kobangs,[[fnref *]] there is not an article of your jewellery—ring, studs, watch-chain, or locket—which he will not manufacture at an almost nominal charge for workmanship. Given a pigskin, and he will even make you a hunting-saddle, which it requires some examination to distinguish from a veritable Peat.
