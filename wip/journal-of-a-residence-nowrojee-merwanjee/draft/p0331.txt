The middle basin is 250 by 200 feet, having an entrance of 49 feet, and the north basin 282 feet 6 inches, by 200 feet six inches, with an entrance of 102 feet. And here is also a frigate dock 207 feet long, 75 feet broad, 19 feet 9 inches deep, with an entrance 57 feet wide. Some idea may be formed of the magnitude of the works carried on in altering and enlarging the dock yard, when it is stated that for making the foundation alone, ninety-nine thousand piles were driven. One of the most curious things to be seen in Sheerness Dock Yard, is the very extraordinary model of the yard upon a very large scale. We have mentioned that where Sheerness Yard now stands, the sea used to flow over; we have told that it is constructed upon a muddy ooze which had no firm bottom, having quicksands beneath, and there never were perhaps greater obstacles to be overcome than in getting a foundation.
