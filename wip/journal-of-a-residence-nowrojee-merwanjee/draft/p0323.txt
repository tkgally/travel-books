[[cont]]and descended a narrow and almost precipitous hill to the village of Aylesford. Here there is a bridge over the Medway. The parish church is upon an eminence just above the village, and the quiet, home view from this spot, would make a lovely picture at high water; for, at low tide, when the water has receded, this village loses much of its beauty. In this quiet church lay the remains of several of the family of Lord Grey and of the Colepeppers; they were some centuries since active partakers in the wars of their days, and their armour and weapons are some of them hanging now over their tombs. What a contrast! here in the quiet retirement of a peaceful little nook lay those whose lances were stained with blood. Here awaiting their final judgment lay the mighty men of arms, who in their life times led on to slaughter and to devastation.
