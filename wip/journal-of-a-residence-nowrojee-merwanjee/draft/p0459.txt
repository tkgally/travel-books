We however had a gentleman waiting our arrival who immediately called a carriage for us, and we all were driven to Carrick’s Royal Hotel in George’s Square, where we remained the whole time we were at Glasgow, it is a fine place, and much attention was paid by the proprietor to us. It being Sunday we found every shop closed, and it appears to us that the Scotch were very particular in keeping the Sabbath. The next morning we were favoured with a call from Mr. Wright, who was requested by our friends at Liverpool, to pay us attention, he was accompanied by his son Mr. John Wright who had been to Bombay and knew our friends very well there, his brother is now a partner in the firm of Messrs. Nicol and Co. of Bombay and he himself is interested in the well known firm of John Fleming and Co. of Glasgow.
