# Chapter XIX.
[[subtitle]] Chatham Dock Yard.

W<sc>e</sc> paid a visit to the Queen’s Dock Yard at Chatham, accompanied by our kind friend John Seppings, Esq., who introduced us to Mr. Stone, the then master shipwright, and Messrs. Moore and Laire, the assistant master shipwrights. We went from London Bridge by a steam boat to Gravesend for two shillings each, and from Gravesend to Chatham in a very easy van as they called it, for two shillings each. The ride from London to Chatham by the coach is very beautiful; the view from Shooters’ Hill and from Dartford Hill, is very fine, and the view from Northfleet to Gravesend of the Thames, with the never failing supply of ships and vessels sailing thereon, is most pleasing. In all England we are told there is not a prettier ride than from Gravesend to Chatham.
