Large docks are now constructing in this yard, and a great many improvements have been made of late, so that there is very little doubt from its proximity to the seat of government, and its situation on the banks of the Thames, that this arsenal in course of a few years will become one of the first importance.

The buildings and the storehouses are very large, and all the officers have suitable residences within the walls of the establishments.
