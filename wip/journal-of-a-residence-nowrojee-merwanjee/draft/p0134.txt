There is also a large and handsomely fitted-up refreshment room attached to this house, where refreshments, such as pastry, fruit, and the favorite beverages of the seasons, are to be procured. The visitors, at least many of them, resort to this place in the intervals of the different acts. The expenses of this establishment must be enormous, as they pay the singers many thousands a year, at least the principal ones, and some of the best dancers are paid very largely; and there are such beautiful performers on the musical instruments, that they and the swarm of dancers must cost much money. The boxes are, most of them, subscribed for the season; but boxes and stalls may be engaged by going to booksellers' at the west end of London, at fourteen shillings and sixpence, pit tickets are eight shillings and sixpence each, and the admission to the gallery is five shillings.
