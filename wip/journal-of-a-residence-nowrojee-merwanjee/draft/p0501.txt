<verse>
[[cont]] Though short your sojourn in our land,
And soon, as friends, we part,
Long, long will friendship hold her hand
Still cherish’d in the heart.

You kindly say ‘departure hence,
Will cause you pain and grief!’
Then think of <i>home</i>—a charm from whence,
We <i>all</i> obtain relief.

God prosper you, where’er you roam,
Be with you on the main,
Conduct you safely to your <i>home!</i>
To meet dear friends again.

But say not, ’though the mighty main,
Our <i>homes</i> in time may sever,
That we shall <i>never</i> meet again,
Say rather—<i>soon for ever!</i>

There is a land of endless joy,
Where <i>sorrows</i> never dwell,
Where friendship lasts without alloy,
And <i>all</i> shall say, <i>’tis well.</i>

To that fair land, my valued friends,
May we our course pursue,
And meeting <i>there</i>, when this life ends,
A friendship pure, renew.”
</verse>

## A Consoling Reflection.

<verse>
“What though on earth we meet no more,
And seas our homes may sever,
Still let us hope when life is o’er,
<i>To meet in Heaven for ever.</i>”
</verse>
