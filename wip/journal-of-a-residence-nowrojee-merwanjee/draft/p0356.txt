The ceiling is very richly gilt in various pleasing and ornamental devices, and on both sides of the corridor are placed a variety of ornamental and curious things, elegant cabinets, tables, chairs and such other things, ancient and modern, so distributed as to afford a varied and pleasing picture to the eye; here are some hundreds of busts in marble of members of the Royal Family, foreign princes, English noblemen, heroes who fought battles and other distinguished persons living and dead, almost all of which were the productions of the best sculptors.

The corridor is divided into two parts by a pair of folding doors, which when opened the whole length, with the splendid furniture give it an air of grandeur and magnificence not to be surpassed.
