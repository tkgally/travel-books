Very odd things are sometimes mixed upon tomb stones; sometimes they represent the living as lamenting the dead, at other times the dead is as it were speaking from the grave. And in Chatham church yard was a stone with this upon it:—“A man had buried two wives,” after stating the name and age of the first, was the following:—“The Lord gave, and the Lord hath taken away, blessed be the name of the Lord.” In a few years his second wife died, and following her name and age—was “I called upon the Lord and he heard me, and delivered me out of all my troubles.”
