Having, by the 15th of October, procured letters of introduction from our friends in London, the following was the day we fixed upon for leaving London by the Southampton railway; it was too late in the season, winter had already set in, the country had lost its beauty in a great degree, and the days were getting shorter; however, from the unavoidable circumstances which detained us, we hoped to enjoy our tour as much as we could, and to collect as much information as time would permit us. We proceeded to the railway station on the morning of the 16th, at ten o'clock, and took our seats. The fare from London to Southampton for the first class is twenty shillings for ladies and gentlemen, and thirteen shillings for servants.
