They give employment to several hundreds of persons, including cashiers, clerks, and porters. The bank is an immense pile of buildings in Threadneedle Street, and is nearly opposite to the Mansion House, at the bottom of Cheapside, and facing the spot where the new Royal Exchange is building for the meeting together of merchants for the transaction of their daily business. The exterior walls of the Bank measure three hundred and sixty-five feet on the southern side; four hundred and forty feet on the western side; four hundred and ten feet on the northern side; and two hundred and forty-five feet on the eastern side. The charter was granted to the Bank of England, in consequence of the first proprietors lending £1,200,000. to the government.
