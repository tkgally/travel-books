He says: “The government of the British possessions on the continent of Asia is vested in two powers with co-ordinate authority; viz., the East India Company and a Ministerial Board, termed His Majesty’s Commissioners for the Affairs of India, the latter being devised by Mr. Pitt, as a check upon the political proceedings of the former. A few words will be necessary to explain this complex authority.
