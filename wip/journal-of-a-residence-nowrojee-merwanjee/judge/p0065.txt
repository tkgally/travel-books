# Chapter IV.

[[subtitle]] Introduction and Reception at the East India House.

S<sc>hortly</sc> after our arrival in England, we were taken by our friend, Mr. George Forbes, to the East India House in Leadenhall Street, and we had the honor of being introduced to the then Chairman of the Honorable East India Company, Sir James Law Lushington, and the Deputy Chairman, Richard Jenkins, Esq. (now Sir Richard Jenkins). We were received with great condescension by them, and were assured of our receiving every encouragement during the prosecution of our studies in Great Britain.
