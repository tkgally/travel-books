The effect in looking down from this gallcry upon the several things in constant motion, is quite enchanting, and we do not hesitate to say, that if we had seen nothing else in England besides the Adelaide Gallery and the Polytechnic Institution, we should have thought ourselves amply repaid for our voyage from india to England.

There can be nothing conceived more interesting to persons like ourselves, who having from an early age been taught to believe that next to our duty of thankfulness and praise to our God and Creator, that it is the duty of every man to do all that he can to make all mankind happy; we were early instructed that the man who devoted this energies to the works of science and of art deserved well of his fellow men.
