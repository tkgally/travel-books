Many first-rate men, including Canning, Lord Dudley and Ward, Lord Francis Egerton, &c. have written for this work, and the articles in the Quarterly are quite as masterly written as those in the Edinburgh. £100. and upwards are not unfrequently paid for each of the articles in the Quarterly, the circulation of which is said to be about ten thousand. They both of them continue most strongly to advocate their political opinions, and when any strong political question is agitating the public mind, under the pretence of reviewing some little pamphlet upon the question, they say all that can be said for or against the measure.
