# Chapter XXIV.
[[subtitle]] Vauxhall Gardens.

W<sc>e</sc> paid an evening visit, during our residence in England, to this delightful place of amusement. They are on the Surrey side of the water, not far from Vauxhall Bridge, and we were amply repaid for the trouble we took to visit this place.
