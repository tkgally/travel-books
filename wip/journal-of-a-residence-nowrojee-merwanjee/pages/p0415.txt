# Chapter XXXI.

[[subtitle]] Tour to the Naval Arsenals and Principal Seaports.

I<sc>t</sc> was suggested by many of our friends that we should, before quitting England, visit some of the manufacturing towns, and the Royal Naval Arsenals, which have, for years, been the pride of the English people; the noble specimens of human ingenuity and of naval architecture that are here produced are, and have been, the chief means of establishing the maritime supremacy of the British over every other nation on the surface of the globe.
