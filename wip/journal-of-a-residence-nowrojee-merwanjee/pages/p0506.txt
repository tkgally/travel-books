In Maidstone church yard, the county town of Kent, is the following:—

<verse>
“The grave has eloquence, its lectures teach
In silence, louder than divines ean preach,
Hear what it says, ye sons of folly, hear,
It speaks to you, lend an attentive ear.”
</verse>

### On Two Children in Essex.

<verse>
“Two sweeter babes you never did see,
Than God Almighty sent to me,
They were surprised by Ague fits,
And here they lie as dead as nits.”
</verse>

<verse>
“Reader, if patience, meekness, faith, and truth,
Have charms for age, or influence for youth,
Pause on <i>this</i> spot, <i>here</i> drop one heartfelt tear,
<i>Here</i> learn to die in hope, or live in holy fear.”
</verse>
