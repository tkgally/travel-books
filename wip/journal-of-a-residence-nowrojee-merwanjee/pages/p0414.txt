We are finally of opinion that by giving due encouragement to such literary productions our countrymen in a few years will perceive the benefit that would result from it, and that the progress they will have made in literature in a short period will exceed their most sanguine expectations.

In conclusion we sincerely congratulate the editor in having been the first to have projected and carried into effect this praiseworthy object, it reflects the highest credit on him and we trust that he will long live to have the satisfaction of seeing the beneficial result of his exertions, and of observing others imitate his example.
