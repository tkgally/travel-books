# Preface.

T<sc>his</sc> book was written against time. On the 19th of January I left Port Said for England, on the 30th of January I landed at Plymouth, and on the 15th of February I started for the Soudan campaign, leaving my manuscript in the hands of a friend, who not only volunteered to undertake all the business transactions with my publishers, but that most ungrateful of all tasks, the correction of another man’s “proofs.”
