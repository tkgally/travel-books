In vain they waited for their only holy man to die; he strangely enough seemed to take no particular interest in what every one else acknowledged was a disgrace to the parish, and lived on as contentedly as if the whole place was studded with tombs. At last a patriotic young Afghan felt that it was to be stood no longer, and, creeping up behind that holy man, with a long knife he dealt him a deadly blow between the shoulders, and piled a heap of stones over his body, to the great content and comfort of his fellow-citizens.
