As would be expected, the upper portions of these <i>talus</i> are covered with the larger fragments which are deposited when the force of the torrent first begins to abate, the middle portion by smaller fragments, and the lower parts by the comparatively small pebbles which are all that the now enfeebled current can roll along. The <i>débris</i> slopes once passed, the muddy torrent finds itself on the flat silt bed of the valley, and as it meanders sluggishly along towards the sea, it loses the power of carrying with it even the light particles of mud which are all that it now retains of its spoil from the mountain-side; and so year by year fresh rocks are rolled from the ravines on to the tops of the <i>talus</i>, and fresh mud deposited in the bed of the valley. What goes on now above water in the Wady el Arabah, went on, on a larger scale,[[fnref 1]] in the gulf whose place it has taken.
