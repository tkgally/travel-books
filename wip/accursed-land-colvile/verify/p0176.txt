As I explained in my general description of the valley, its sides in its lower part are marked by long, gentle slopes of <i>débris</i>, the centre of the valley being a nearly flat bed of mud; a section of it, therefore, say five miles from Akabah, would be something in this form:—

At Mekreh Hadîd the central mudflat has disappeared, and we have nothing but two stony slopes coming to a point in the centre of the valley thus:—

[[image: Cross-section diagram of the valley showing two stony slopes meeting at a point, lettered A, B, C]]
[[caption]] Sea-level.

It is rather a remarkable point, to which Peacocke drew my attention, that the line of demarcation between these slopes is very clearly marked by the nature of the stones of which they are composed.
