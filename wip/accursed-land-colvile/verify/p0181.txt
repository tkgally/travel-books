It was not a pleasant walk; the sun was intensely hot, and after the recent rain the mud was very deep. The surface had just dried and was covered with a thin incrustation of salt, through which, at each step, one broke and sank into the slush below. There was not a dry spot anywhere to sit down on, and if one stood still one only sank deeper and deeper into the mud. In the middle of this lively place I spread out my coat to receive the aneroids, and prepared myself to pass a very miserable hour, waiting for Cash. Now my experience through life has been that when one has made up one's mind to be bored, in nine cases out of ten one enjoys oneself immensely; and whenever one thinks that one is going to spend a particularly pleasant evening, the chances are greatly in favour of one's wishing oneself safely home in bed before it is half over.
