I had expected that the men might be a little uncivil at first, but they were all perfectly respectful, standing up when I neared the fire, and not sitting down again till I asked them to do so. For some time I looked in vain for Salman, and it was not till one of the men had lifted up the corner of a bundle of rags which lay near the fire, and disclosed the patient, that I discovered his whereabouts. With very great care his arms were bared, and I was shown that a piece of skin about the size of a threepenny-bit had been knocked off his elbow. I said it was a dreadful wound, and applied a wet bandage to it. Then, amid dreadful groans from the patient, his back was bared, and I found a good bruise on the right side, well clear of the spine.
