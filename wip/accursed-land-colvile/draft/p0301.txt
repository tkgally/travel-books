It was across one of these valleys that my route lay this afternoon. In the foreground there was gravel, in the middle distance gravel, and in the distance chalk. What a distance that gravel stretched! After a two-hours’ ride the chalk ridge which I had seen from our halting-place seemed scarcely a mile nearer. At sunset I caught up the baggage-train, and as I did not know the pass through the ridge, pulled up and travelled with them till we reached its summit. It was now pitch-dark, and through the darkness I saw two lights to the westward; the one steady and some height above the ground, the other moving about upon the plain. The steady light, said the men, came from a window in the Nakh’l fortress; so whispering another <i>emshi</i> to my dromedary, I shot ahead once more.
