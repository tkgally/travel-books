Here on the eastern side of the valley the same still holds good, and springing out of the granite mountains in the midst of which stands Mount Hor we see a long series of gravel slopes inclining towards the west. On the western side, however, exactly the reverse takes place. The soft chalk of the western range, breaking up under the action of water, instead of accumulating at the mouth of the wadys, is worn away by these torrents, and instead of raised mounds we find deep cuttings. Thus here, as at Rishi, owing to the great accumulation of <i>débris</i> on the eastern side of the valley, the lowest line is to be found, not in any part of the original gulf, but on the slopes of the mountains which formed its sides.
