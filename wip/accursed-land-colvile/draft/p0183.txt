There is a reverse to every medal, and the refraction which had called up this strange and beautiful revival became an abominable nuisance when the arrival of Cash recalled me to the business of the day. What was water and what was land? was there any water at all? and if it was all land, which was the highest and which the lowest part of it? were questions which in this dancing, delusive atmosphere it was utterly impossible to answer until one had trudged for many a weary mile over the soft mud.
