On the west a gradual slope of clay covered with small fragments of limestone, rose for a couple of miles, and on the east the ground, of a similar nature, cut up by numerous small water-courses, gradually ascended in a series of undulations towards the limestone ridge of Rishi. On the north-west was the Wady Lehyana flowing between the chalk and limestone ranges, and ascending in the south-westerly direction to the Tih, while towards the south it followed the extreme western border of the Arabah. Straight to the south lay the broad expanse of the Arabah itself, bounded on the east by a bold granite range, above which towered the double top of Jabal Haroum (Mount Hor), and on the west by the low chalk-hills which here formed the boundary of the Tih plateau.
