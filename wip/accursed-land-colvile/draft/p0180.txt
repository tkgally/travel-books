Here I jumped off my dromedary, and stepped off on foot across the mud in a north-easterly direction, leaving him standing on the brink, unable to follow me on his dromedary, and not liking to leave the beasts to their fate. I left him evidently lost in speculation as to what atrocity I should commit next, and whether he would ever see me again, and completely of one mind with his countryman who exclaimed, “Truly there are many devils, but the greatest of all devils is a Frank in a round hat.”[[fnref 1]]
