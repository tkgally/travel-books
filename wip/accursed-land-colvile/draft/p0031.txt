[[cont]] myself in a place where <i>Tändstickor</i> match-boxes were more valued than half-crowns, and even in Morocco many people who would not say thank-you for a sovereign, would do a good deal for a sixpenny tobacco pouch. Watches, clocks, purses, tobacco pouches, and knives, and musical boxes, I had found most useful in Morocco, and thinking that the Bedwin would probably like much the same class of things I bought a number of these articles, but, as it turned out, with the exception of the knives and tobacco pouches, they were not much appreciated. As far as my experience goes, the Bedwin only care for three things: money, weapons, and tobacco; and they seem to appreciate all three simply with regard to quantity. Three five-franc pieces are more acceptable than a sovereign, a big pistol which will not go off is more prized than a small one which will, and a pound of anything which will burn in a pipe, than an ounce of the best tobacco which one can buy.
