Ten miles beyond the marsh of El Ta’abah and twenty-eight miles from Akabah, we came abreast of a very remarkable hill called Nawibi; its shape at once attracts attention, rising as it does in three distinct steps like the summit of a huge step pyramid. A closer examination shows that while the base of the mountain is granite like the rest of the range, its summit is undoubtedly sandstone, at an elevation far above that of the corresponding strata in the western range, and further, that, whereas the stratified rocks on the west dip to the north-west, the strata on this mountain dip to the eastward.
