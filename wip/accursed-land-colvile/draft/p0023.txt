In 1882 events occurred which proved how easily that route might be stopped, while the fast-increasing traffic, and consequent increasing blocks, combined with the rapacity of the French company, were beginning to disgust British shipowners. Under these circumstances it was proposed to make a second canal through the isthmus, with British capital, and under British control. Political reasons, however, interfering with the scheme, it had to be abandoned.[[fnref 1]]

Just then somebody remembered Captain Allen and his long-forgotten scheme for a canal through the Dead Sea, and the Palestine Channel Syndicate was formed for the purpose of ascertaining whether it was practicable, and I had the honour of being chosen to carry out the investigation.
