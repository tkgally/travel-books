As I had ten miles to go before getting to my ground, I settled to ride there, but as usual there was a hitch about the camels, and when it was time to start Cash’s was not ready, and as he said he would just as soon walk for a bit, I started off, leaving word for the other camel to be sent on at once. I do not know whether any one ever attempted to follow me, but if they did they never succeeded in catching me up, and when I got to my ground there was no one but Mohammoud to take care of the one I had been riding.
