My thermometer was hung on the shady side of the camel for ready reference whenever the aneroid had to be consulted, which fact I was unfortunately led to forget by the peculiarly exasperating conduct of my camel, who this morning seemed determined to prove that if she was forced to go at the breakneck pace of two and a quarter miles an hour there was nothing in the contract which compelled her to go straight. On my right wrist was hung a favourite reminiscence of South Africa, in the shape of a “sjambok,” a piece of raw giraffe hide, and this I applied, with the best intentions possible, to what I thought was her unprotected hide. Unfortunately the thermometer intervened, and, as is often the case, the innocent suffered for the faults of the guilty.
