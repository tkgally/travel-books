# Chapter IV.

[[subtitle]] Sinai—An opposition mountain—<i>Embarras de richesses</i>—A misnomer—Ain el Arkhdar—Seductive oasis—Longitude in time—Mabrukka—Another casualty—An unknown friend—End of the stern chase—The Wady el Ain—The power of steam—A well-conducted establishment.
