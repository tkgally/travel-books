I had got the latitude of Akabah by an altitude of the pole-star, and it had agreed with my observation up the valley, but I wanted, if possible, to get the corroborative evidence of the sun, and this was my last chance; so, much to the disgust of every one, I made a six-hours halt on the beach, and although I did not get a meridian altitude, I got three at equal intervals near the meridian, which answered the same purpose.

Leaving the head of the gulf at its north-western extremity, we began our ascent on to the Tih plateau up the dry bed of the Wady el Haserat, a broad torrent which receives the drainage of all the neighbouring hills.
