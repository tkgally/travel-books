But no one, I am bound to say, thought much of the ram grievance. “Now, that was only the home-walker”—<i>i.e.</i>, the tame sheep, who often helps to manage the others—they remark. However, every one bustles about; some to meet Miss Menzies—some to fetch up our wraps, which are soon shaken down in a large airy church, very preferable to the ram's room.
