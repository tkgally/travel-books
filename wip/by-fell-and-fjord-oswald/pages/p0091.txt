# Chapter VIII.
[[subtitle]] Nordtunga—Borgasyslu—Holtvördsheide—The Northern Coast.

[[image: a young woman in profile wearing a long veil, beside a decorated initial T]]

THE wind died away in the night, and when I woke a bright sunshine poured through the little square windows on either side of the humble altar, which, however, was duly railed off from the rest of the building. The church was about forty feet long by fifteen wide, built of single overlapping planks like a shed, and it was very cold, as the damp of the piled-up churchyard outside had rotted the lower boards. But it was ecclesiastical in plan, with pews to the west, and a pulpit in front of the chancel. The hinges and bolts of the door, of well-wrought iron, were the only handsome things in it, and probably they had descended from the richer days when the islanders gathered wealth on the high seas.
