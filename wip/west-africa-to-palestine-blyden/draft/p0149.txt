On Saturday evening, August 18th, Dr. Bliss invited me to accompany him to witness the festivities of a native wedding. We went first to the house of the bride; for among the people of this country the bride and bridegroom celebrate their nuptials in different houses, and sometimes in different and distant villages, for several days before they come together, and before the marriage ceremony is performed. We found the bride surrounded by her female relatives and friends. She sat adorned in rich and costly attire, with her eyes closed. She wore suspended from her hair more than twenty pieces of gold coin, about the size of an English sovereign—the gift of her intended.
