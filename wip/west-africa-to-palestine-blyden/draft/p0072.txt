On this occasion, however, my depression of spirits did not arise so much from what I was then suffering physically as from the influence exerted upon me by the point of view from which I was disposed to contemplate future events. “Come to this side,” said a Maltese guide to me in the splendid church of St. John at Malta, who was showing me a magnificent painting by Rubens of “John the Baptist in prison,” but which I did not seem to appreciate—“Come this way, and you will have a better view.” From the point where I was standing I saw nothing but a dark, dingy ground. A slight change of place revealed a rich and most beautiful picture. Is it not thus in the actual world? Does not everything depend upon the standpoint which we occupy? and is not the slightest move often sufficient to make all discord seem perfect harmony?
