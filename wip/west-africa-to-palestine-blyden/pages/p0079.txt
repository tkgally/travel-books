As soon as the lamps were lighted, the Parsee would go to the side of the ship to pay his adoration to the mighty waters, or, as he explained to me, to the Supreme Being, through water and fire—the two most powerful elements by which He has chosen to manifest Himself. at their devotions without thinking what a rebuke they furnished to professing Christians, upon whom the true light shineth, with many of whom you may associate for weeks without knowing that they have any belief at all. Of the four Europeans who occupied the cabin with me, there was but one who, on retiring to rest at night, would give any sign of an upward aspiration, and then the manner in which he would express himself left it doubtful whether he attached any meaning whatever to his words.
