As the intercourse between you and the important people of this country becomes more intimate, so, we hope, the economic relation between the United States and Japan will become closer in the future.</i>

<i>Your Excellencies, ladies and gentlemen, before sitting down, I ask you to join me in drinking the health of Mr. and Mrs. Schiff and their party.</i>

<i>March 29th. The Nobles’ Club.</i>

In responding, I tell some appropriate anecdote, and then becoming serious I sound a note of advice against the danger of overburdening the country with new commitments, dwelling particularly upon the fact that Japan has no more valuable asset than the high credit it has again attained in the markets of the world and which should be scrupulously guarded. Again the evening passes most pleasantly, and we find ourselves at our hotel discussing the nice impressions we have received.
