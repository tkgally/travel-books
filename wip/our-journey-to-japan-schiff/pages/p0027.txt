Indeed, one can hardly imagine, without having seen it with one’s own eyes, the fascination of this perfect paradise, the soothing effect of its climate, and the joy called forth through a nature so abundant in its richest gifts. Our forenoon automobile ride ends at the Moana Hotel, a modern hostelry, situated near the sea, which latter throws its waves upon the beach in the most perfect colors of blue, white, and green. At the Moana Hotel Mr. Kishi has provided luncheon for the entire party, in an airy dining pavilion; we much enjoy the meal, and during its progress we have an opportunity to see something of the winter population of Honolulu, which largely consists of Americans who have come to the island to escape the severe American climate and to enjoy instead the island’s eternal Spring weather.
