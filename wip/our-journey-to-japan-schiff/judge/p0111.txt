The Baroness is an attractive Japanese lady, who speaks some English and makes a charming hostess. We meet there Mr. Kato and his wife—the latter being a sister of Baron Iwasaki. Mr. Kato was formerly Ambassador to England, and lately Minister of Foreign Affairs, having resigned about a month ago because of his strong opposition to the Railway Nationalization Bill, a Government measure. I am seated between the hostess and Mrs. Kato, Mother between the host and Mr. Kato, and we much enjoy conversation with these well-informed people. After luncheon we are shown through the conservatory and its annex, the latter containing a choice collection of rare Chinese porcelain.
