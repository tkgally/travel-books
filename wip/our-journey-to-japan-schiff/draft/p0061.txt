But in speaking of the factors conducing to the success of the loans, there is one more aspect which must be specially emphasized. I mean the prompt decision of Mr. Schiff, who happened to be in London when the negotiations for the first six per cent loan were going on, not only opened the way for having the loan issued in America, but the American participation at the very opportune moment enhanced the general popularity of Japanese securities and had beneficial effects upon all the successive issues. Moreover, Mr. Schiff rendered me valuable assistance in opening the German negotiations for Japanese loans. It was my good fortune to become thus acquainted with Mr. Schiff, and having always felt it a pleasant duty to deal with him, I warmly appreciate his personality as an important factor in bringing about the success of the loans.</i>
