<i>Saturday, June 2d.</i>—This we decide to make a day of perfect rest. We take a short walk in the morning and a somewhat longer one in the afternoon. Toward evening Otto Schiff, who several weeks ago had come from England to visit Canada on business matters, arrives to meet us and to join us on our journey East; he brings us welcome news from New York, which he passed through some three weeks ago. The evening passes in conversation with the newcomer, and, as is now the habit, a game of bridge is indulged in before we retire.

<i>Sunday, June 3d.</i>—Our stay at peaceful Banff is at an end. Much recreated by the two days' idyllic rest, we return to our cars at about ten o'clock in the morning and have them attached to the “Imperial Limited” en route for Montreal. Shortly after leaving Banff we pass out of the mountain region and emerge upon the great plain, which extends some fifteen hundred miles to Lake Superior.
