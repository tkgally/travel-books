[[cont]]These gardens have a show of flowers which for richness and variety of colour, can probably not be equalled elsewhere in Japan. Horticulture in this country is a curious art; the fruit trees are laden with blossom, but bear a fruit which is not worth eating, and which no amount of grafting ever improves; neither do roses ever repay the trouble which is expended on their cultivation; but the Japanese have such a variety of other beautiful flowers that they can afford to record a few failures.
