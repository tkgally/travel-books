A thick overcoat, a warm rug, a water-proof and an umbrella, and good stout boots for those who are fond of walking are essential; whilst gentlemen who hope to be received at any of the Court Garden Parties should take a top hat and a frock-coat, as this “order of dress” is compulsory on these occasions; but invitations to the palace which are issued through the legations at Tokio are limited to those foreigners who have been previously presented at their own Court at home.
