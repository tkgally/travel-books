From five o’clock in the afternoon until seven, a military band, the leader of which is an Italian and the players from Manilla, discourses music which is good enough as far as choice of pieces goes, but is quite middling as regards the quality of the performance.

But to return to the Parsees, in whom I had become much interested, although I had up to that time not made the acquaintance of any of them. I had asked whether they have a place of worship here, for I wanted to visit one of their temples, but was told there is none in Shanghai. I had also been told that they religiously abstain from the use of tobacco, considering it a profanation of fire. After that I watched them carefully, but never saw one of them with either a cigar or a cigarette.
