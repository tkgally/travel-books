One advantage of travelling by water instead of on land is that you save the expense of a linen duster. Unfortunately, however, while you escape the dust, you cannot, unless you go by sailing vessel, so easily avoid the cinders. One got lodged in my eye and gave me much pain for several days, until I called on the ship’s doctor for relief. With the aid of an instrument, he removed it, but the eye had become so inflamed that it required treatment for some days afterward.
