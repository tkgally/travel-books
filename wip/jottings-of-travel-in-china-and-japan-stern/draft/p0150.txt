Although the <i>jin-ricsha</i> and the Chinese barrow are much used here, there seems to be a large supply of horses. The Chinese seem much given to driving out of an afternoon, and you can meet hundreds of traps and rigs of all kinds on the road to or from the Bubbling Spring tea gardens. When high dignitaries go about they use the sedan chair, which is carried by four coolies. Should it be a <i>taotai</i>, for instance, who takes an airing, his chair will be preceded and followed by a crowd of retainers, his exalted rank requiring this.
