Yesterday being very stormy, we had what is known as regular Nagasaki weather. Indeed, the sailors jokingly tell us that it rains here about eight days out of the seven. We have had “leaky” weather ever since our arrival. Notwithstanding the rain, I went ashore yesterday in a <i>sampan</i>, and visited the shops of some of the workers in tortoise shell, a branch of industry for which Nagasaki has quite a reputation. After an hour thus spent, I was glad to return to the steamer and to remain there for the rest of the day.

This morning the weather promised to mend, and I went ashore again, looked in at some porcelain and curio shops, and visited two bazars, making a few purchases in several of them.
