# VIII. A Japanese Restaurant.

[[right]] <i>Yokohama, May 25, 1887.</i>

I arose at a late hour this morning, having had a somewhat uncomfortable night. Nothing more than a cold shiver now and then, that caused me to fear that I had a touch of malaria. Having kept well thus far, I had no notion of succumbing to the climate, and so began the day with a dose of quinine. When I got down stairs it was half past eight o’clock. At eleven o’clock, I took the train for Tokio, where I had an appointment with Mr. Ngato Shiraishi, Professor of Engineering at the Imperial College of Japan, and also with Mr. R. Masujima, President of the Law School and one of the leading lawyers of Japan.
