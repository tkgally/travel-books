# XIII. <sc>Kobé–Nagasaki.</sc>

[[right]] <i>On board the S. S. Satsuma-Maru,</i>

[[right]] <i>Nagasaki, June 4, 1887.</i>

We left Yokohama on the 31st of May, at noon, in the S. S. <i>Satsuma-Maru,</i> Captain Conner commanding. The steamer is, I understand, the smallest in the fleet, and is owned by the Jananese company known as the <i>Nippon Yusen Kaisha.</i> This organization controls a fleet of one hundred or more steam vessels. A few of the older ones formerly belonged to the Pacific Mail S. S. Company; the larger portion were built on the Clyde.
