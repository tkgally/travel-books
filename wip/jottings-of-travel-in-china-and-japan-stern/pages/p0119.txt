[[cont]]at Shimonosaki, where we remained about an hour or more, and at Nagasski, where we dropped anchor at six o’clock A.M. (about an hour ago). We shall leave here this evening or sometime to-morrow, sailing across the China Sea, direct for Shanghai, which point we hope to reach late on Monday or early on Tuesday next.

Among our passengers was an intelligent and agreeable Scotchman who came to Yokohama some eighteen years ago, at the call of the Japanese government, and who was in its employ some eight years, superintending the erection of light-houses. He knows the coast thoroughly, and had much that was interesting to tell us about various points we passed, as well as about Japan and the Japanese. I was very sorry to part company with him when we reached here, for this is his journey’s end.
