After leaving the Shinbashi Station at Tokio I rode for about half an hour in a <i>jin-ricsha</i>. For the greater part of the way our route lay through a quarter of the city that was quite new to me and finer than any portion of Tokio I had yet seen. I was told afterward that it is the most aristocratic section of the city. When I reached the restaurant, a sort of private club-house, where we were to be entertained, I found that it was on a hill overlooking a beautiful little valley and surrounded with fine trees and shrubbery. The place and its surroundings were so picturesque that I could only wish for a chance to see it all in fine weather. On our way thither I got the impression that we were going into a section devoted to semi-detached suburban villas, such as we have on the outskirts of our cities.
