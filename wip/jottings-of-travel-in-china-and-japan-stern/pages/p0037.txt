The city is quite hilly and is crossed in different directions by cable cars, which are run here far more satisfactorily than in Philadelphia or Chicago. Whenever you get to the top of one of the hills, you have charming views of the beautiful Bay and the heights beyond. I shall spare you a description of the Park, the Cliff House, and the Seal Rocks, but shall make up for this by sending you photographs of those points of interest.

The H. family received me with open arms. They have made me so welcome, and have been so kind in their unremitting efforts to make my stay here a pleasant one, that I shall hate to say good-bye to them, for it will seem like leaving home a second time.
