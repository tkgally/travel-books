Two thoughts force themselves on the observer: First, that this ignoring of the native, so consistent with the policy of the foreigner in China, goes far to explain the want of sympathy between the two sections of the population and furnishes one reason for the slow growth of Western ideas and culture in this empire. Second, that the Chinese are a peaceful, docile, order-loving people is evident from the manner in which they submit to this order of things.
