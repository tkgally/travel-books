Fires usually do great damage in such towns as this, and thus the excitement is easily accounted for. I would have been sorry to miss seeing the crowd, which was interesting because of the varied elements of which it was composed.

I have been at Tokio three times, have visited several Buddhist temples, have seen no end of Japanese curios, beautiful pottery, fine lacquered ware, superb bronzes and delicate carvings. Although I shall never become learned in those things, the terms Satsuma, Kaga, Owari, Hizen, etc., have more of a meaning for me than ever before. The curio shops of Yokohama are better worth visiting than those of Tokio.
