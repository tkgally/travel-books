The real enjoyment is to see whole streets of them; to see the tailor, the butcher, the sandal-maker and the fish merchant plying their various trades; to see the wilderness of babies, for they are met with at every turn, of all sorts and sizes and in appalling profusion; and then, again, to pass through the same streets after nightfall, when colored paper lanterns are hung up in front of every booth.

The last letter I received from home reached me on the day I left San Francisco, April 26th. If at Shanghai I can intercept any letters for me that may be on their way to Pekin, I shall try to do so.
