# XI. Treaty Revision, Etc.

In certain circles the closely related topics of extra-territoriality and treaty-revision are frequently canvassed. Both Japanese and foreigners find much to object to under existing conditions; but, while both sides ask for added privileges, they do not find it so easy to concede what is wanted of them. Perhaps this is hardly just to the position of the Japanese, for they are only asking back what, because of their weakness, they surrendered to the great powers. At present legal causes in which foreigners are interested are conducted before the consular courts, and, as a consequence, there are as many different jurisdictions as there are nationalities represented here.
