Great quantities of rice are shipped from here, and the fine harbor affords ample room for a large amount of shipping. French, German, English and Japanese merchant steamers are now lying at anchor here, and one large Russian frigate is on the dry dock, which is said to be the largest this side of Suez.

It would amuse you to see how they load vessels with coal. The barges or lighters are rowed up alongside of the vessel that is to receive the coal. Men are down in the hold busily filling the baskets, which are handed along from one helper to the other, until they reach the vessel. These helpers form a line, and do not stand more than two feet apart.
