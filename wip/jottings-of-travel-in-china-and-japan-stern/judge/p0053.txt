The servants are Japanese, the steward is a Chinaman who speaks excellent French, the clerk in charge of the office is Portuguese, the <i>comprador</i> is of course a Chinaman and, if I remember rightly, the night watchman is a Wallachian.

As it has been deemed advisable to defer going to China until we have called on some of the Japanese gentlemen to whom we bear letters of introduction, we have postponed our departure for Shanghai for at least a week. Our original plan was to go to-day.

To-morrow, we hope to visit Tokio, the capital, and, from what I have heard, we are likely to find the old city even more interesting than Yokohama.
