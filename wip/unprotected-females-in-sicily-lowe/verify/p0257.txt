In a remote corner of the church stood a trunk, with brass nails, in which, towards evening, the deceased Secretary would be removed to a vault by monks, his own servants following to take possession of the key.

The other churches were full of people <i>adoring the Magi:</i> and no wonder, when their Eastern highnesses were so brilliantly clad and situated. In the most conspicuous part of the sacred edifice, mounds were raised to imitate the hills of nature, covered with trees, and traversed by roads, up which were climbing tiny figures of men, women, and children, towards one point, where cows were grazing; there, lay a baby in the arms of a young female leaning on a manger, and crowned witb a miniature diadem. She was receiving the obeisances of three gorgeous potentates, whose followers bore heaps of presents. The effect was curious as it was pretty; some of the <i>Presepi</i>, as these clerical twelfth-cakes are called, being more tasteful than others.
