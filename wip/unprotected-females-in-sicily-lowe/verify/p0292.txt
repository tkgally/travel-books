# Chapter XXIV.

[[subtitle]] Passing Through Rome and Florence.

[[subtitle]] Coral and Lava—Mola di Gaeta—A Theft at Terracina—Buffaloes and Their Masters—Entering the Eternal City—The Holy Week—Delights of the Campagna—A Fortnight at Civita Vecchia—Florence in Spring—A Last Adventure—Flowers, and Farewell.

W<sc>ith</sc> a basket of branching coral, and green lavas from the brilliant stream left by the last eruption, we entered an excellent vetturino carriage by appointment, at the Naples railway-station, and set off for Rome.

Mola di Gaeta’s round nose lying on the sea was the first prominent object. The women with heads of clustering curls, were graceful as the slopes and gardens around. The king’s residence is a fortress.
