I will put down some of the fixed expenses, the others entirely depend upon whether terms are arranged beforehand or not; for instance, at a provincial town, acquaintances who succeeded to the same apartments we had had for eight tari a-day, paid fifteen ducati for them. The Sicilians, celebrated diplomatists in all ages, fancy they may as well profit by the lavishness of those to whom money is no object. In Palermo carriages are two tari an hour; for the environs a little more. At the palace the royal porter, priest, and gentlemen who showed their respective departments, were all pleased with a trifle; and a tari may be considered to go as far as a franc.

The most beautiful drive in the neighbourhood is to Monreale, on the summit of a hill, up whose side climbs an excellent road, bordered with cacti, each prickly leaf crowned with a gorgeous red flower in summer, in autumn with the oval fruit called figs of Barbary, deliciously refreshing, but guarded with minute prickles, dreadful to the ungloved hand which steals one over the wall.
