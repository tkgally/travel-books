[[cont]]not let us alight till he found out an inn prepared for travellers. He was a long time absent, and the worthy magistrate was busy plying us with cups of coffee, to keep our eyes open. At length he returned, having been told by sundry night-caps at the windows of three inns, “that they were full,” which he said, was mere laziness: at the fourth he remained till they had furnished an empty room on purpose. He made the postilions drive to the door, and acknowledging things did look rather wretched, threw his cloak on the floor, saying: “There is at least a carpet,” proving that Walter Raleighs are not extinct. He then continued his journey to Naples; and as forty hours’ travelling had left us but few Sybarite feelings, we soon slept soundly in the really clean beds.
