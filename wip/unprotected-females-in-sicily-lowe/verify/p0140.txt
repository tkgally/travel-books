We thought the man would have claimed the whole sum under pretence of being willing to wait another day, but he compounded for six ducats, and went down. For do you think, reader, I had relinquished or was at all doubtful about the ascending project? no, no, more determined than ever! and persuaded mamma to wait till the weather cleared up.

Though it poured for five days running, we never spent a pleasanter time, going out between the storms, and thoroughly exploring the characteristics of Ætna; indeed, a residence on the mountain is necessary to fully appreciate its charms; but before saying more about them, let me finish telling you of the proper preparations for conquering them all.
