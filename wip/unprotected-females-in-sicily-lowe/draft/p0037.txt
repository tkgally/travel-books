The interior is mouldering away; two ancient paintings on wood, inscribed 1184, are its venerable curiosities; one representing the Madonna inscribing a manuscript by an open window, through which is seen the eagle, forsaking his flight to the sun, and soaring down to her superior brightness. In the sixth century, one of the monks of this chapel became Pope Agatone at Rome.
