Before starting, coffee in doll’s cups, with minute spoons, was brought up; the charge for it was one tari, for each bed two tari, and with a present to the ragged maid-servant, made exactly the amount the other travellers paid, minus their supper, which was three tari; that added gives a fair idea of expenses in the interior of Sicily, which are not much more extravagant than in Norway.

The peasants came out prepared for bitter cold, clad from head to foot in shaggy sheep-skin, which made us ask them “if they had been banished to Siberia?” but after a few hours the penetrating mistral wind became almost benumbing inside the carriage, and our woollens were all required for snugness.
