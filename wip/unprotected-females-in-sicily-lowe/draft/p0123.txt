The merchant, despite his manufactory, kept a shop, the distinction between wholesale and retail being very confused in Sicily. Mounting to his apartment on the first floor, we found the door barricaded, as if the entrance of a fortress; and there being no bell, our knuckles were sorely tried in making an impression on its thick outside. The sole result was a screaming inside, like a hag in a passion, and a fearful bang of a distant door. We stood in despair on the stairs, till a being likely to help us should come past; at length a man who knew something of the inmates, by making a peculiar noise, got one of them to the door; and after a long parley through the creaks as to what we were, which he found difficult to explain, being completely mystified himself, the iron hinges groaned backwards, and we were let in by an elderly female acting Duenna to the merchant's pretty young wife, who in her zeal kept her mistress barricaded up during the husband's
