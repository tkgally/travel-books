He eyed the gold in a basilical manner, begged me to give him a piece, “as he had never seen one before;” would not be bolted out at my return, but knelt down on the ground to lace my boots—I almost wish I had kicked him, for the moment my back was turned three piastres were slipped out of the bag! I looked again—he had sneaked off, and to our astonishment we found the gentlemen only knew him by sight as officiating at one of the churches! There was no time to be lost, so we said nothing to them of the theft till we were fairly off; they were disgusted, but not so astonished as one would fancy; and afterwards, when I have mentioned the occurrence to priests of the upper classes, they have generally answered, “But there are such miserable creatures in our lower orders,” or else, “All kinds enter the Church now-a-days.” The traveller will generally find a priest or two hovering about the passages, seeing what he can pick up, and one is always waiting at the door with a money-box covered with figures consuming in flames.
