The last few spare minutes we peeped in at an old Antiquary’s, who paints cupids, and had some awfully valuable cameos;—then gave one distant look by moonlight at the ruined glories standing in calm melancholy beneath the flight of time, who, touched to the heart, folds one wing as he sweeps across them, lest his ruthless rushing should bring annihilation . . . . . Farewell, grand calmness . . . . . we must be back to the other end of Europe!

Storm held the skies all the next day with valiant roar of thunder, hail, and rain; the great mal-aria plain seemed to enjoy the sensation; its peasants were collected in the towns of San Catajo and Serra di Falco, playing cards in the caffés, as the Palermo fishermen do on the Marina, and coming out in the few intervals of lurid light to fill the piazzas with groups of cloaked and hooded figures.
