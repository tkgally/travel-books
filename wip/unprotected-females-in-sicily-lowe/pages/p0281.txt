They had not much conversation, no one at Paolo receiving any education beyond what nature gives; spelling-books being reserved for the secretaries of the upper classes. The landlord’s son, the little monk I mentioned, was an embryo Franciscan, having been “promised” on his recovery from some dangerous infantile illness. I begged his father not to force him into a cell against his will; he replied that he had no idea of doing so; “but it is your wish, Cico dear?”

“Yes, Tata,” answered the interesting child.

The next day the weather was fine, and we had been assured the Vapore would arrive, yet the hours wore away without it appearing; at length our <i>entourage</i> confessed it never came on Sundays, the custom-house and public offices being closed everywhere.
