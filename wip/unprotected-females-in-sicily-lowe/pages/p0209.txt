So we returned to Giarra, and were still more aggravated by the uncivilisation of the poor hostess, who actually asked, “Will you have a room for four?” It was not till various sleeping victims had been sacrificed on the brick floor, that the irritation of the party was soothed. Next morning before daybreak we were roused up again; but alas! by this unfortunate fit of brute obstinacy, were to forego the pleasure of stopping at ancient Taurominium, now Taormina, and climbing that blue promontory we saw when sailing from Syracuse; as one of the party was obliged to be in Messina in the evening. Of course we thought we had missed “the finest thing in Sicily.”
