Seeing an antique coach of monstrous size, attended by antique servants, and into which entered an unlimited number of gentlemen in the Spanish dress and frill, we found it to be, on inquiry, the conveyance of the Senate (certainly large enough to hold the whole assemblage), and that it was then proceeding with them to the weekly Saturday ceremony of unveiling the statue of the Madonna at the Ave Maria. This was the moment chosen for the outbreak of the last revolution, the soldiers firing off their muskets and calling <i>Viva Maria!</i> their cry throughout the war. Poor things, their desperate struggles would have been successful, but for the Neapolitan troops which held the fort, and fired shells continually on their Sicilian brethren; so that the hatred between them is doubled since then, and King Bomba sits all the more securely on his throne from knowing he has not to dread the strength of unity among his victims. The Messinese and Catanese speak also very bitterly of the English, and on the slightest hint of our government sympathising with them
