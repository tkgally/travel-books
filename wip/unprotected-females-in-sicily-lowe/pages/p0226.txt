We sat on a balcony all night like other people, watching this spectacle, the crowds circulating, and the mountaineers or <i>Ceramedde,</i> who come with their pipes to play to their “<i>caro Bambino,</i>” and had done so in front of every image of its Virgin mother for nine days before its birth. The Intendente’s house set forth a beautiful display of lights, which became blue, red, orange, and green in succession: fireworks and crackers were let off in the streets, then the dawn sent us all to bed, fortunate not to be blown up in a dream by an explosion like the Catanian one.
