The line of tottering houses called a street, was filled by the funeral procession in pointed masks with holes for the eyes, and as they were following an infant corpse, were also robed in white trimmed with blue; over which, on account of the weather, they had grotesquely placed projecting wide-awake hats and “policemen” capes. Their torches lighted us to the best inn, very Sicilian in appearance, but already full. There was another which could only accommodate us next door to the cocks and hens, there being but a slight partition between them and the head of our roosting-places. Such a chanticleering as began at daybreak would have laid Hamlet’s or any other ghost for ever!
