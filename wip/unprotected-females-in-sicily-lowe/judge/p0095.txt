# Chapter VIII.

[[subtitle]] Modern Girgenti and Sulphur Mines.

[[subtitle]] Liveliness of Africa’s Neighbours—A Lettiga—Descent into a Sulphur Mine—The Cathedral—Change in the Town since Byron’s Days—The Temples at Night—Volcano of Mud—Something about a Priest.

D<sc>on</sc> A<sc>rtemisio</sc> and Don Pasquale, introduced by letters from our Caltanisetta friends, came with the dawn to reply in person, offering homage and services, and commencing them by sending in a delicious tribute of fish for breakfast, ready cooked. The Sicilians seem to rise as early as the Norwegians, for get up when we might, they were always beforehand with us, and had to be sent about something useful for several hours.
