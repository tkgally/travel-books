The rising moon now silvers all these forms.... The carriage stops,—a figure very like Pluto comes with a light, says, “Signorine, you must come inside now.” It is the good wizard, Don Salvatore, who makes us enter the cavern of the vehicle.

We had already shared some of our Marsala with him, and shown a wish to be civil, so that not only did he say he would not smoke, but also, when the troublesome postilions came and disturbed our mythological musings by asking for their “buona mano,” which has now become a regular tax of five bajocchi per stage for each traveller, besides the price of the place, four piastres, Don Salvatore offered to pay it for us during the entire journey, settling the amount on arriving at Catania, so that we were left to muse and dream tranquilly, and only once disturbed by an awful rolling over the paved bed of a river near
