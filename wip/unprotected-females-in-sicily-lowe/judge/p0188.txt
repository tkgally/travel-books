# Chapter XIV.

[[subtitle]] Syracuse.

[[subtitle]] The Cathedral Temple of Minerva—The Harbours—The Catacombs of San Giovanne—The Altar of Jupiter—The Greek Theatre and Roman Amphitheatre—The Ear of Dionysius—State Prisons—The Fountain of Arethusa—Silver Santa Lucia and Her Ceremonies.

B<sc>efore</sc> breakfast there is time to look at the neighbouring cathedral, which has stood 2,500 years as a temple for the worshippers of the protectress of the Syracusans, beginning with Minerva, and ending with Santa Lucia. The spaces between the massive pillars which had formed the open temple of the ancient goddess, are now filled up with plaster outside; inside a second row of modern columns were being covered with crimson hangings and tinsel flowers, in honour of the modern one, who stood upon the altar. The edifice was full of carpenters; a priest, and a young school in priestly dress were directing their movements, and we were told to return in the evening.
