# Chapter VI.
[[subtitle]] A Provincial Town.
[[subtitle]] Difficulties and Despair—Revival and Salvation—A Hero Appears—More Romance—Style of Place—The Clubs—Exiles—Jealousy—Arrangements of a Courier for his Victims—Curious Peasantry—Twenty-four O’Clock.

C<sc>altanisetta</sc> was now but ten miles distant, and reached at dusk. The coachman found us a nice apartment in a house called “Il Giardino verde” (the green garden), near the large piazza. Our truly good-natured travelling companions continued the journey to their native town on mules, the road ceasing here; that large section of country to the south-east being left to the mercy of bridle paths.

All our provisions were exhausted; and being much fatigued, I gave the old servant of the house some money to buy eggs, or any thing she could get.
