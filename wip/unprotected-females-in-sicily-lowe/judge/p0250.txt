The Signore had never yet tasted infused Souchong, and I am sorry to say, put the very first mouthful of the mixture out; they were in the highest spirits, delighted with the adventure, and possessed presence of mind enough for a court. Tiriolo, like many other villages, had no inn; thus the country still yields specimens of that old-fashioned virtue, hospitality, in some places almost reduced to a fossil state.

At the succeeding post, a fat commissary of police wanted a seat to the next town, to see about some crime or other; and as the Corriere would not have his worship inside with him, telling us privately because he took bribes, our sentinel was obliged to relinquish his seat on the box, upon receiving his written order.
