After dinner, many Florentine ladies drove out to pay their evening visit, and joined in a large round game at billiards. At the moment of leaving, the nightingales were singing so sweetly in the aromatic shrubs, their chorus would almost chase sleep away.

Now all the majestic mountain avenues for leaving Italy lie open before us,—we choose the Splugen, cross Como’s lake, and arrive at the first châlet, as nature is withdrawing her drugget of snow, and letting her summer carpeting appear. The flowers, immediately taking the hint, are springing up rapidly on all sides: the gentian, of deep Mediterranean blue, and the forget-me-not, pale as the northern sky, grow together at their country’s frontier; culling a little sprig of the latter for you, kind reader, I leave you in their right pleasant company.
