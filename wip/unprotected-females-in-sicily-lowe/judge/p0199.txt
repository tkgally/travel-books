<verse>
“O Maria!
Quando naceste voi, nacque bellezza:
* * * * *
La Neve vi donò la sua bianchezza;
La Rosa vi donò l' suo bel golore;
La Maddalena le sue bionde treccie;
Cupidò v' insegnò tirar i cori!”

“O Maria!
Beauty and thee were born together:
* * * * *
Snow-flakes lent thy skin soft fairness;
The Rose threw its blush o'er thy cheek;
The Magdalen gave thee her golden tresses;
Cupid taught thee to steal hearts!”
</verse>

Next morning I was looking from the Belvedere at the gigantic appearance of the steamer midst the seven small fishing-boats of the harbour, and watching the rich decorations which were being spread upon all the houses where the saintly procession was to pass, when Ætna's white top nodded through the clouds. I instantly returned her bow, and flew to persuade Mamma to go immediately on board the steamer.
