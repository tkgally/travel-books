Yet they had no right to be warm; for if the heat of Africa breathe over Ætna’s snows in winter, can the imagination even,—that ardent burner!—glow to the slightest idea of what Sicilian climate must be in summer, when they declared it was much hotter? All the national fire of character became at once comprehensible to us, and even infectious; as stifling heat spreads languor and exhausted indifference around, so do the directly darting rays stir up the soul to rage, and maddening the blood, make it impel the body on to opposition. No, heat! you shall not vanquish! Though, unexpected, determined, and ferocious, you find us without even the shade of a tiny umbrella against your attacks! That hour’s toil up to the column was inexpressibly painful.
