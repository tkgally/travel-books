# Second Excursion.

[[right]] British Consulate, Hakodate,

[[right]] July 24, 1860.

M<sc>y dear</sc> S<sc>ir</sc>,

I finished my last voyage on the 19th ultimo. On Wednesday the 27th I started again, on a second cruise, having still Her Majesty's horses at my disposal.

There was no chance of a ship. We had been, in vain, waiting for one for the horses; and so, leaving orders for an express to follow me, should a ship arrive, I left with Mr. Cowan and M. l'Abbé Mermet.

Up to the lake described in my last letter, the route was the same. From that spot we went northerly some few miles, but the scenery we passed through was, if possible, more delicious than that of the lake. Certainly this time we had not the society of ladies, and we were still unarmed.

I had beforehand told the Governor that I was intending this cruise, and he made no objection to it.
