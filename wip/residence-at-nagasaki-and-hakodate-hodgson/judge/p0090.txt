## Third Excursion.

[[right]] British Consulate, Hakodate,

[[right]] August 13, 1860.

M<sc>y dear</sc> S<sc>ir</sc>,

As I hope that my two letters describing my voyages of the 16th and 24th ult. may, more or less, have interested you, I will now give you a short account of my third voyage.

Before all, you must remember that I am unarmed, except the guns which my Japanese carry to shoot or frighten bears, and go back to Hakodate, if so directed or requested by the governors, to whom I announce my departure generally the day before.
