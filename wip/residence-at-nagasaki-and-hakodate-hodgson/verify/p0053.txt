# Chapter II.

[[subtitle]] Interviews with the Governor of Nagasaki.

H<sc>aving</sc> given a short account of a conference with Japanese officials at Nagasaki, and a correct, but very cursory sketch of the curious money system I found on my arrival, I will now slightly touch on my interviews with the Governor; and after mentioning a few of the natural products of Kiūsĭū, and its commerce, leap on board Her Majesty's ship “Highflyer,” and steer for the port I know most of, touching at Kanagowa and Yedo, for a little food for paper. Others will describe Nagasaki, and Niphon, better than I can; but as the system of government is the same over all Japan, I may have gleaned, in my brief notes, as much information as many, from the officers, doctors, and priests.
