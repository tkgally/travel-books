Our hut was very tiny, consisting of two rooms, one about eight feet square, over the lake and running stream; this was for the ladies, child, and maid. The other room, somewhat longer, was drawing-room, dining-room, and bed-room for the gentlemen.

But, with the exception of the village at the Botanical Gardens and the small town where we slept, we had seen no trace of civilisation; a few charcoal-burners only were met with, and I could not help thinking it was time to open to Christians this unsealed volume of love.

Not a human being on the road, and yet so near two large cities as Matsumai and Hakodate!

In the night-time we were desired to fire guns (which my Japanese servants carried for the purpose) to frighten away the bears.
