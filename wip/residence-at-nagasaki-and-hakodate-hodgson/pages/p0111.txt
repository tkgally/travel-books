The garden of the Legation is charming: it is the property of the priests who still occupy part of the Temple; and the avenue from the landing-place up to the house is broad, neat, and lined with a handsome avenue of trees and lovely borders, full of flowers, on each side.

The lake was in front of a large verandah, abutting from the dining-room, drawing-room, and private apartments of Mr. Alcock. Over the lake, to a quiet, shady, yet very magnificent “bosquet,” was a narrow but elegant sylvan bridge—and from the extremity of the wood was a lovely view of the sea and suburbs of Yedo. The oak flourished in this British home. All the officers of the “Highflyer,” who could obtain leave, came and visited us, and a merry group we formed; for we all become friends very soon in these distant lands, and throw off the cold ceremony (so suitable to cold climates and frigid zones) when cast on our own resources for distraction or amusement.
