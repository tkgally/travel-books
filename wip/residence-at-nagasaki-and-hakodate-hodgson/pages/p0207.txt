The Tycoon is the temporal Emperor. The imperial domains can furnish 100,000 warriors; the Gōrrogĭō, or Council of State, is under his orders; the priests and doctors, nominally under the protection of the Mikado and the Dăīrĭ, are virtually and effectively also under the dominion of the Tycoon. The Daimios are independent princes or nobles; that is, when they are able to assert their independence—there lies the question.

There are, it is said, some 260 nobles in all the Japanese Isles. Of course some are far more wealthy than others, and, therefore, of more or less importance, according to their property and its products. There are princes, or Daimios, worth 1,000,000<i>l</i>. (one million of pounds sterling) per annum, and others not worth 5000<i>l</i>. a year. It is, therefore, the policy of the Tycoon and his council so to conciliate the powerful princes, that he may not only have a greater numerical preponderance on his side, but also the more substantial and wealthy party.
