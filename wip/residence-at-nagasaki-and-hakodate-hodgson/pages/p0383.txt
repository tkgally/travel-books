While this sheet is in the press we have received part of the 4th volume of the Proceedings of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, containing notices of the Cryptogamous plants gathered in the late American expeditions to Japan, the <i>Fungi</i> by Messrs. Berkeley and Curtis, the <i>Musci</i> by Messrs. Sullivant and Lesquereux, and the <i>Algæ</i> by Dr. Harvey. The species mentioned in these papers we have here added, although they must be a very fragmentary portion of the cryptogamic flora of the island. In the list of Mosses those found in the Loo Choo Islands, and also those gathered by the American expedition on the mainland, are incorporated. No account of the Lichens has as yet appeared:—
