The dancing-girls or singing-girls are to be found in every city or town, and may be hired for any party, but they are never insulted. They are, moreover, well paid, and accompany themselves on guitars and tom-toms. I never saw any other musical instrument in Japan. These singing-girls will come in parties of three to your house and perform all they know either of singing or dancing, not much to the edification or enchantment of the European, but immensely to the delight of the enthusiastic Japanese.

There is much real family affection in these islands: old age and old people are respected, and children are spoilt and petted. The only one of the family connection in the domestic circle to be pitied is the wife. She may be mistress of the house and honoured as her husband's lawful wife, but she is not the sole proprietor of his affections; she has to associate with many others who share his affections, and to live in daily and continual intercourse with them.
