## Fourth Excursion.

[[right]] British Consulate, Hakodate, August 27, 1860.

M<sc>y dear</sc> S<sc>ir</sc>,

I must now send you some account of my fourth and, probably, my last journey inland, which in many respects has been the most interesting.

I started with Mrs. Hodgson, Madame Albrecht, and my daughter, accompanied by M. de Nasimoff, an officer attached to the Russian consulate. It was our intention to proceed easterly to Cape Yezan (on the map), and return by the volcano mentioned in my first excursion.

We left at 8 <sc>a.m.</sc> on the 16th, and, after arranging all at Kamida, proceeded to the sulphur springs, crossing a charming little river with dwarf oak to its banks.
