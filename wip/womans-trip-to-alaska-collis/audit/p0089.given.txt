Chapter VI.

DAYLIGHT was tinting the landscape when we resumed our voyage, and; as I had resolved at the start that I would only sleep when I could not see, I was up and walking deck before six o’clock, filled with a sense of pity and regret, to use no harsher term, for those who still remained in bed. Your cup of coffee and biscuit are always ready for you at the tap of your electric bell, so that no excuse about “not liking to get up early because you have to wait so long for breakfast” avails you one particle. It you do not prefer what I am looking and wondering at to the comfort of sleep (which is an excellent thing in its way), by all means remain in bed, so that the few who are enjoying the effect of the sunrize may have plenty of elbow-room. Let us see who are those who are around our little group of three, sbaring with us the pleasant breeze and balmy sun-rays of this exquisite June morning.
