The stone may be recognized, should you have any difficulty in finding it, by the names of tourists and inscriptions cut all over it in all the tongues of the Tower of Babel.

And now; quite by accident, I had perhaps the most interesting experience of my whole trip,—certainly one that has made an everlasting imprefsion upon my mind; an object lesson which often and often will set me thinking, a subject which would require a volume to do if approximate justice. The joyous shouting of half hundred boys, some of them dashing across the road in

pursuit of a foot-ball; well-clothed, well-fed boys; healthy, vigorous, intelligent boys; Indians, half-breeds, Muscovites, and a few Americans. What did it mean? From where had they so suddenly come? From school.
