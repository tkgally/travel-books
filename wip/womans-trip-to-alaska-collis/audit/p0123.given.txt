Of this custom I saw a good deal at Wrangell, though I did not see there what was very conspicuous in Sitka, namely, the use of the powder embellishment; in which the Sitkan maidens are very proficient, handling the subject with a delicacy of touch which was quite remarkable, save that the brown-berry tones of the throat, neck, arms, and hands remained in strong contrast to the pearly features.

(Kodak’d by Miss M. D. Beach.)
