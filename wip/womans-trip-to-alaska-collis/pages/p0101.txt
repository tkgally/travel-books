It was evident that the place was entirely without horses and vehicles of any kind, for the principal street—if street it may be called—was grown a foot high with grass, and was chiefly used as a place to store canoes and firewood; there evidently had existed at some distant period a plank side-walk, which ran along the entire front of the village, but time had played such havoc with it that the people now walked in the street to avoid it. It seemed to me as though there was not energy enough in the whole place to light a fire on a cold day.
