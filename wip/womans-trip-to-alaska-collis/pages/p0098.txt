# Chapter VII.

[[image: decorated initial T with a carved animal figure, totem-like, among foliage]]

THE fourth day out from Tacoma (June 5th) we found ourselves, when we came from our state-rooms lying at the wharf at Fort Wrangell, the United States war vessel, <i>Pinta</i>, being very near us, and the small boats of both ships plying to and fro exchanging civilities.
