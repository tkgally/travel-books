We arrived, on Tuesday morning, May 21st, at the city of Tacoma. And just here let me say that, much as I wanted to arrive at the port from which sailed the vessel that was to carry me to Alaska, it was with sincere regret that I left the comforts and luxuries of travel which I had experienced in that train from the Mississippi River to the Pacific Ocean.
