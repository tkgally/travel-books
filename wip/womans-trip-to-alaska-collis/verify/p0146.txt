But I and those ladies and gentlemen who accompanied me through the rancherie and the schools at Sitka can vouch for the fact that it is only half a mile from savage, uncivilized ignorance, superstition, filth, and immorality, to education, deportment, thrift, domestic felicity, and all human happiness. Thank God I had seen “<i>le revers de la medaille.</i>” To have gone back to my comfortable home in New York and to the embraces of my bright, healthy, intelligent children, feeling that these poor little wretches at Sitka were to remain outcasts during the brief time that disease and degradation should permit them to exist on earth, would have been a great sorrow. Thanks to the Presbyterian Board of Home Missions and to Mrs. Elliott F. Shepard, the reverse is a great joy.
