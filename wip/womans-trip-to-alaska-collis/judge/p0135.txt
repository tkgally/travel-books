While this was going on I availed myself of the confusion incident to the crowding around the Archbishop to take a look at the interior treasures and decorations of the church, which are a great surprise measured by the impression made by the fading colors and apparent neglect of the exterior. There are two altars in the transepts, the larger one being shut off excepting during the service by two golden bronze doors, each exquisitely ornamented by solid silver images of the patron saints, the doors themselves being of fretted and open work and most effective pattern.
