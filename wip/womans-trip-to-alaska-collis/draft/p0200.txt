Our return trip was so arranged as to pass in the daytime many exquisite marvels of scenery, which we had lost in the night coming up, among them the Wrangell Narrows and Clarence Straits, whose unfathomable, still waters mirrored the rocks, which buttressed the innumerable fiords upon each side of us into all kinds of shapes, shades, and angles, presenting a kaleidoscopic land- and water-scape the whole livelong day, all of which, as I think back, comes to me like the fantasy of a delightful dream. In one deep canyon, where you could almost shake hands with a friend on shore, there soared aloft a veritable American eagle, floating from side to side and encircling us with the sweep of his graceful motion for many miles until we reached the open
