A long blow of the whistle; an interval of fifteen ] minutes and then another short blow; a shout from the Captain instructing the men on the wharf to cast off the ropes; a signal to the engine-room ; a turn of the engine, and we were sailing up the picturesque waters of Puget Sound. The day was beautiful ; it v could not have been better adapted to the use we were making of it if it had been provided specially for us; in fact during the whole fortnight that we lived on the Queen we had the most charming weather—bright sunhight and cloudless skies—excepting the day of our arrival in Glacier Bay, where it rained for a few hours.
