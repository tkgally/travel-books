It is full of beautiful islands, some of them rising so precipitously from the sea that there is no foothold on them for man; others sloping down so gracefully to the water and dressed in such gorgeous colors that you want immediately to buy one, and build a house on it at the crest of the lawn. If you look at them as links of a continuous chain, you perceive that they are simply spurs of the Olympic Mountains partially submerged by the ocean, and that if by some convulsion of nature the water receded, the steamer would find itself stranded at the base of a deep canyon and surrounded on all sides by a range of mountains.
