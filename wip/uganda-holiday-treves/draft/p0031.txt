They chuckled to think of the small white shoes and the trim silk stockings on roads of which they knew, but felt a lump rising in their throats as they pictured the gallant little maid when months of an African sun had sapped the beauty of her cheek and had drawn a shadow across the merry grey eyes, and when, it may be, the tiny lace handkerchief would be crumpled up in a fevered hand and the head that tossed on a hot pillow would look strangely old.
