A hair-brush applied without caution presents after use a blood-red appearance. These phenomena are none the less alarming when it is explained that the colour is due to oxide of iron in the earth. The explanation may be chemically correct, but it conveys little solace to the white man made red; while, at the same time, it will be understood that a self-esteeming lady without a mirror on this part of the journey is in danger of losing her reason.
