# Chapter VIII.

[[subtitle]] The No Dance—Kanagawa—Straw Work—Cemetery and Cremation—Courage Test—Okuma San Letter.

T<sc>he</sc> morning found us again in company with our Japanese friends, speculating upon what would be the order of the day, and wondering if it could equal the celebration of yesterday.

We had not long to wait before the shouts of the boys and girls were heard down the avenue, as they rushed on ahead proclaiming the advent of something new and pleasant. Soon there swept into view a monstrous car, drawn by four bullocks, on which was erected in evergreens a huge <i>torii</i> or gateway, with many banners suspended from it which bore the inscription in Japanese characters, “Long live the Emperor,” and above, on the cross piece of the <i>torii</i>, were the words, “In Celebration of the Promulgation of the Constitution.”
