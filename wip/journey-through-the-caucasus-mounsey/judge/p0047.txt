[[cont]]Colchian linen) might be grown to any extent. Imeritian women have long been celebrated for their beauty. Jason was no greenhorn, but a man who had travelled far and seen much, and therefore we may presume that Medea’s charms were not inconsiderable; great too were the prices paid in the marts of Stamboul for the fair slaves of Colchis, and the present generation does not belie its repute, though the wintry season and several inches of snow in the streets greatly curtailed my opportunities of obtaining ocular demonstration of the fact. The climate is good: snow falls exceptionally, and, though the summer heats are great, they are free from the feverish elements which prevail in Mingrelia and along the eastern coast of the Euxine. Kutäis therefore may have an “Avenir.”
