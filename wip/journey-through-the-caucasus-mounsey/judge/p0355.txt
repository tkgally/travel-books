# Appendix.

## Routes.

### I.

[[center]] P<sc>oti to</sc> T<sc>iflis</sc>.

Total distance, 360 versts (240 miles).

Principal Stations:—

Maran.

Kutäis.

Quiril.

Belagor.

Suram.

Gori.

Railway is opened to Quiril and will soon be completed to Tiflis.
