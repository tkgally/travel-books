A mile further on and we arrived at a narrow opening in the hills, through which we looked down on the plain of Shiraz and across its now green corn-fields to snow-capped mountains on its southern side. A short descent ensued, and we then entered a broad straight avenue about a mile long, and bounded on either side by gardens filled with cypresses, pomegranate-trees and roses. At its end was the gate of the city, and we thence made our way to the house of my hospitable friend the English telegraph officer of the district.
