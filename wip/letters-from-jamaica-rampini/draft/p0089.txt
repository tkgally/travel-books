Songs are sung little mournful in their character; as an example we may give the following:—

<verse>
“Me len’ him my canoe,
Him broke my paddle,
(<i>Chorus.</i>) John Joe, widdle waddle.

Me len’ my fish-pot,
Him tief my net,
John Joe, widdle waddle.

Me len’ him my harpoon,
Him tief my line,
John Joe, widdle waddle.

John Joe no hab
None hat ’pon him head.
John Joe, widdle waddle.

John Joe no hab
No shirt ’pon him back,
John Joe, widdle waddle.

If I catch John Joe
I will broke him neck,
John Joe, widdle waddle.”[[fnref 1]]
</verse>
