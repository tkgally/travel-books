<i>W.</i>—Of course we do.

<i>P.</i>—Remember you ’pon you’ oath, sa!

<i>W.</i> (to prisoner)—Ax me questions, sa!

<i>P.</i>—George Edwards! Last week, Monday, was you coming from you’ ground?

<i>W.</i>—To be sure, sa!

<i>P.</i>—What side you’ ground is?

<i>W.</i>—At Content, in this parish?

<i>P.</i>—What kind of ground it is?

<i>W.</i>—Hi! Capital cultivation, sa! It stand well.

<i>P.</i>—No, sa! It lazy-man ground, sa! I have witness to prove dat—I have witness to prove dat.

<i>W.</i> (horrified)—My king!

<i>C. P. S.</i> (to prisoner)—Go on, sir! go on, sir!

<i>P.</i> (solemnly)—Missa Edwards! Is you a Christian?

<i>W.</i>—Hi! That hab nothing to do with the question. Ax me questions, sa!

<i>P.</i>—Answer me the question. Remember you ’pon you’ oath, sa!

<i>First Magistrate</i> (to prisoner)—Go on, sir!

<i>P.</i> (with an air of offended dignity)—Did I call you thief?

<i>W.</i>—To be sure you did.

<i>P.</i> (vehemently)—I deny the question! I deny the question! I deny the question!

<i>W.</i> (indignantly)—Don’t cross-cut (interrupt) me, sa!

<i>C. P. S.</i> (to prisoner)—Never mind. Go on with your examination.

<i>P.</i>—Wasn’ you in a beastly state of stopsication?

<i>W.</i>—My father! What is this?
