## IV.

M<sc>y dearest dear</sc>,—It is with a perplexing heart of anxiety that I take up my pen to address you this time, having propos marriage to you. I am now anxious to know the full intention of your mind, accompany with parent. On my side, let it please you to believe me that I am desireous to oblige you in whatever thought or ways that you lik. if you cannot stop up this way, but rather to be in Santicross [Santa Cruz], I am very willing to do so.
