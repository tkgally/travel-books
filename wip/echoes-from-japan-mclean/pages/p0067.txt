That which was a mere curiosity at first, now gave place to a real interest, and an increasing desire to learn more and more. I proceeded then to the several Epistles, and went on, and on, with a humble spirit, to be taught the truth, and nothing but the truth. The whole of the New Testament was thus read through in a short space of time. Then I began with the Old Testament. But it was not necessary to finish the whole of it before God opened my eyes to the solemn truth. “Look unto Me, and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth.” I looked up unto Jesus, and in Jesus, and in none else, I found my personal Saviour, and I thanked God and rejoiced with joy unspeakable and full of glory. In Jesus Christ I have discovered the true Son of God, who came down upon the earth to live, even as a Man of sorrows and acquainted with grief, and to die a death of shame, in order to restore lost sinners to His, your, and my Father. Verily, “He was wounded for our transgressions, He was bruised for our iniquities.” Oh, what shall we do to thank Him for this wonderful work of self-sacrifice and love? Believe on Him, brethren!
