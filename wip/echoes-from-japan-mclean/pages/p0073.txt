The work spread from ship to ship, and not only did the captains of vessels permit me to have Bible readings on board, but the men were allowed special leave to visit me at my own house.

When a sailor breaks his leave, he has to pay the penalty by being denied the privilege of coming ashore for a given time, and under certain circumstances, the transgression may lead to his confinement as prisoner. In those days there was but little of this among a certain “set.” Under the sobering and solemnizing power of the Holy Spirit, they were true and faithful in the carrying out of their respective obligations, and with a purer and stronger motive than formerly, fulfilled them to God, and not unto men. Ot this their officers took knowledge, and attributed it to the “influence brought to bear upon them on shore.”
