Again, in the same house, I invited little children to come to me every Sunday morning for half an hour to sing hymns and learn one passage of Scripture. It was astonishing how those little mites picked up the hymn and tune, and repeated after me such verses as “Suffer the little children to come unto Me,” “God so loved the World,” etc., until they were learned. This unpretending start was suddenly put a stop to by cholera having broken out in our neighbourhood. My seven charges were called, <i>pro tem.</i>, to their homes, and I was advised to take a house in the foreign settlement, where we resumed our operations after the desolating disease had subsided.
