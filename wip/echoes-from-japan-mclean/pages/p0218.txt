Its face is said to express supreme indifference to all that is going on in the lower world, or having reached the nirvana. We then proceeded to the Ni-gatsu-do. Lighted candles abounded, and the hum of voices, as the priests and people intoned and bowed in the presence of their respective idols, was very painful. Binzuru, the god-of-healing, had his face and other parts of his body nearly rubbed off by the frequent rubbings of devotees. Whatever portion of the body is afflicted, they rub the corresponding part of the idol’s person, and, by such means, they expect healing. Stalls and shops abound here, where there are charms and every variety of priestly invention calculated to appeal to the religious sentiment of the worshippers who go and come by thousands.
