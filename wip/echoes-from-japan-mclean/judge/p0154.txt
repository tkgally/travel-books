## The Moon Beholding

takes place in the autumn, when the gazers stand, if possible, on the bank of a river, or on a bridge, in order to see it reflected in the clear and still water. Pictures of real merit are often produced from the reflection of the moon in the waters. They also like to look at it through the trees.

## The Creator.

There are many in Japan now as well as in the past, who ascribe glory to their <i>ideal</i> Creator for the lavish way in which he has beautified their country with his good things.

## The Book of Books.

The Japanese are not only ready to move forward with the tide of European methods, but also for the reception and spread of the Gospel. The sacred Scriptures are printed whole and in parts, and freely circulated, as well as a large variety of other Christian literature. Many reverently regard the Bible as the “Book of Books,” and it is touching to see a devout Japanese handle the sacred volume.
