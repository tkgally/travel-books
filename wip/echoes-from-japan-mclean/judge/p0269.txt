[[cont]]It being holiday season, and my pupils absent for six weeks, there is no <i>human</i> prospect of making the two ends meet. But the “<i>Mighty Man of Wealth</i>” is above such extremities, and to-day he says:—“<i>Blessed be thou of the Lord, my daughter.</i>” He seems to be educating me into a life of trust, sometimes through trial, and sometimes through glad surprises—“handfuls of purpose,” which he lets fall by the way. Few know the heavenly art of giving “<i>as unto the Lord Himself,</i>” consequently, the giver and the receiver come short of blessing.

I have not been writing home at all about the glorious work, nor even speaking of it, lest Heaven's sweet bloom should get rubbed off. But there is a time to <i>speak</i>, and a time to keep <i>silence</i>, for all that.

Cheering letters from the Thalians; they are at Shanghai. They say that Mr. S. D. C. Douglas is sent of God to that place, he is preaching Jesus, in the power of the Holy Ghost, in the cathedral and theatre. The Lord laid it on my heart to invite Mr. Douglas to visit the ports of Japan. We are praying daily that he may be brought to us, full of the only power and qualification which God can own and bless.
