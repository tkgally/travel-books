I have seen gaudily dressed Eastern women, and chastely attired ladies, but in this instance I saw nothing but what a better judge of dress and its combinations than I, termed <i>superb.</i> No powder or rouge was seen on their fair complexions on this occasion. After having partaken of a very choice dinner, which was cooked and served in French style, the young ladies handed us a few mementos of our visit. It was getting late, and we bade each other farewell We were shown into our Jinrikishas and taken to our destination, which was on that night only a mile or two off. That God may be the rewarder of such singular acts of disinterested kindness is an oft-breathed prayer. <i>They</i> saw that we meant much more than we could express, and <i>we</i> saw from their bright, open countenances, that they understood and felt that true kindness looks for no reward, save the privilege of seeing that it is appreciated.
