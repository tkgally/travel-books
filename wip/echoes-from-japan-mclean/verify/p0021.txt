See, how the sea of heads bends simultaneously in one direction, as if swearing (by the Emperor Jimmu—founder of the Mikado’s dynasty) to him who sits before them, that henceforth they and their retainers will bow to his will, as becometh filial sons and loyal subjects! Hitherto, they have been petty kings over their own domains; but, henceforth, they seem to say, “<i>We shall be servants to our Lord the Tenno</i>” (Mikado). Another profound bow, and all their richly decorated swords (emblems of distinction and power) are as good as surrendered. Hark! They unanimously and reverently groan out a final assent to what they believe to be the voice of the throne (or heaven), and they retire, bearing the name of Kwazoku—flowery family—in lieu of the more illustrious title, viz., <i>Daimio</i> (lit., great name).
