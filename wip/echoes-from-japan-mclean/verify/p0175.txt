T<sc>ook</sc> M<sc>e in</sc>! means personal and mutual intercourse! So that they cannot help <i>feeling</i> and <i>seeing</i> that God is among us.

By inviting them to luncheon, dinner, or to spend the Sunday with us, and taking them to bright Gospel services and Bible readings, where they can see heart and soul put into the teaching, preaching, and worship of the true God, great good may be done.

Indeed, many things will suggest themselves to soul-winners. Our love for the heathen in this age of progress (when Easterns come to our shores by thousands) may be gauged and tested by God, by the efforts we are making to win them to Him on the spot. We have already lost glorious opportunities. Numbers of Oriental students, who have spent years in our midst, have returned without having been spoken to once about <i>Jesus and His love.</i> As already stated, they cannot help seeing the efforts we are making in sending missionaries to their people, but our apathy towards those of them who are here is apt to be misunderstood.
