## The Carp Ascending the Waterfall.

[[center]] (<sc>see cover.</sc>)

“Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life.”—1 Tim. vi. 12.

It is a common sight to see, on Japanese works of art, and in picture-books, a carp trying to swim against a strong current or waterfall. This allegorical picture has a very interesting history, and is derived from a Chinese story. In some part of China there is a strong current, called Rio-mon, or Dragon’s Gate. This stream is looked upon as sacred; so that, if any fish succeeds in scaling it, it becomes a dragon.[[fnref *]] The passage is very difficult, it being rocky and steep, and every fish except the carp fails in the attempt. Very often, at the birth of a son, a carp is sent to the parents as a present.
