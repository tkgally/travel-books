[[cont]]God has satisfied my longings, with His own presence, far beyond my expectations. The half cannot be told.

I teach my European pupils in the morning. The Japanese come in the afternoon. Their determination to learn is unexampled. History and Natural Philosophy are studied by some. They are wearing European hats for the first time in their lives, evidently, for frequently on going away they forget to take them with them, and think nothing of leaving them in my charge till next day.

H.M.S. <i>Cadmus</i> has arrived. Some of the Christian sailors belonging to her called on me, bringing with them a note of introduction from my sister. They began to sing and pray at once, and were like “giants filled with new wine.” Some of our English neighbours raised a storm, objecting to our unconventional worship. They sent out their servants with tin kettles and gongs, but “God stopped the mouth of the Lion,” and gave us rest.

My little bungalow is full of melody, and my health is much improved. I can see clearly that God meant me to come to this favoured land, to give the blue-jackets a welcome. Would that all Englishmen were such a credit to the British flag as they are! True Christianity makes them contented, loyal subjects.
