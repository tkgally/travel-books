[[cont]]workers. This ship carries a chaplain. He has written me a kind letter, thanking me for kindness shown to his men.

Went early in the morning to U.S. <i>Alert</i>. Had a full meeting. . . . . Very rough on the water. Visited other vessels also.

Went on board the <i>Egeria;</i> she, like the <i>Frolic</i>, is favoured with a Christian captain, who heartily appreciates the interest taken in his men.
