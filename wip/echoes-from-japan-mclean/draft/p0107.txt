The following incident may be worth relating:—

## An Early Call.

The pupil, to whom I refer on page <pref>43</pref>, having heard of the newly-organised Church, felt that he must join it; but on learning that, if baptized by Mr. ——, he must consider himself a member of <i>his</i> Church, and not feel at liberty to identify himself with any other, he felt bewildered. This was too mysterious a problem to the guileless inquirer. His large-hearted uncle, who took the deepest interest in that which was a matter of profound concern to his nephew, proposed that they should go early the next morning to Yokohama, and see me and get the matter cleared up; for it was not for a moment suspected that I could have any difficulty in answering their questions. They travelled about eighteen miles, and were announced about nine o’clock, when they at once poured forth their troubles with reference to the public rite of baptism.
