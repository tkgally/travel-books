There were some who seemed overtaken by the adversary, drawn aside from following the Lord; yet I firmly believe the Lord began a good work in them; and we know what He once sets His hands to, He will accomplish (Phil. i. 6). So we may hope the work begun at Yokohama may go on till the coming of the Lord. Since last I saw you, till the present moment, the Lord has been leading me along very tenderly and graciously. I must speak well of Him, and testify to the blessed fact that His compassions fail not, they are new every morning; great is His faithfulness. In all my wanderings and departures from Him, His grace has restored me; and in all my unfaithfulness He has proved Himself the unchanging one, the God of my salvation, and my rock of defence, and still continues to be gracious; for all the way by which He has led us tends only to increase our confidence in Him.
