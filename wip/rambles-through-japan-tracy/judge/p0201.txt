The cascade by which the goddess appeared has been transformed into a fountain for the ablutions of pilgrims. Three stone gutters, slightly projecting beyond the edge of the rock over which the stream once fell, conduct the water into as many currents and cause it to fall into a square stone basin, ten or twelve feet below. Directly under each stream is a small square block of stone, perhaps eighteen inches in diameter; upon these pilgrims stand and repeat their prayers, the act being meritorious in proportion to the time which the devotee can endure the water dashing over head and body. Here I saw a spectacle privileged, I fancy, to few travellers in Japan. Looking down a long flight of stone steps leading from the platform connecting the two temple buildings, I saw a number of nude figures bathing under this cascade.
