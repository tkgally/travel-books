I stated my business in a jumble of Japanese words which they were good enough to comprehend. Was the price too much? Neither would commit himself on this point, but one was ordered by his superior to accompany me back to the inn, and the result was a charge of one yen, instead of one and a half. Mounting upon my pony, I expressed duly my thanks to the young official, who seemed on his part delighted to have had the opportunity of doing something.

The road up the pass is at an easy grade, the bed well constructed, and the windings in and out of picturesque ravines and round about jutting crests are as fine and as numerous as one could wish. The long procession of pack-horses and foot travellers, outlined against the sky far above us; the lengthening train below us, slowly marching in and out of the curving passage we had made half an hour or an hour before; Saka-moto, with its black-roofed houses and simple narrow street spread out like a map;—all this was novel as experience, and beautiful as a view. As we ascended still higher the scenery becomes more and more impressive. In respect to beauty it far surpasses the two Swiss passes I have crossed.
