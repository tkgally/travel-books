Punaluu society seemed to be in a state of primitive simplicity. After walking up to the little hotel through a path between lily-ponds bordered by rushes, no proprietor was apparent. Everything was open, all on one floor, doors and windows hospitably wide, beds carefully made, and not a soul in sight. So we took possession, and after a time an excellent Chinaman appeared who officially turned the house over to the guests and their peaceful slumbers.
