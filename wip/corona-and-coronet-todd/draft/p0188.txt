Then the yachts parted too far for sound of word, while Tantalus and Punch Bowl and fair Diamond Head grew indistinct—yet more misty with atmospheric distance, and finally disappeared in gathering twilight. With full hearts we said <i>aloha</i> to these beautiful islands, already like home to each of us, with their friendly faces, their pathetic music, their gentle language like running water, their unsolved problems, and their brooding charm.
